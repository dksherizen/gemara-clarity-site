Chagigah 17a

חגיגה י״ז ע״א

Topic

סמיכה and offerings on יום טוב, the Sunday 'day of slaughter' after שבועות, and the seven make-up days of שבועות

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier discussion: רב אשי shows that the ban on סמיכה on יום טוב does not prove that leaning on an animal's sides is forbidden, because the place where סמיכה is done counts as the animal's back. Then a new משנה begins. בית שמאי and בית הלל argue about which offerings may be brought on יום טוב and whether one may lean on them. They also argue about whether the 'day of slaughter' moves to Sunday when שבועות falls on Friday, and they agree that it does when שבועות falls on שבת. On that Sunday the כהן גדול wore regular clothes and people could eulogize and fast, so that no one would think those who say שבועות is always after שבת were right. The גמרא then learns from a פסוק that שבועות has seven days of תשלומין, like פסח. It asks why we don't compare שבועות to סוכות and give it eight days, and it settles on seven using the rule תפשת מרובה לא תפשת. Next it explains that the mention of סוכות in the פסוק teaches that סוכות, like פסח, requires לינה. The amud ends by asking where we learn that פסח itself requires לינה.

Opening lines

רַב אָשֵׁי אָמַר:

Rav Ashi said:

אֲפִילּוּ תֵּימָא צְדָדִין מוּתָּרִין,

Even if you say that the sides are permitted,

כָּל דְּבַהֲדֵי גַּבַּהּ — כְּגַבַּהּ דָּמֵי.

anything that is next to its back is considered like its back.

The steps (13)

  1. Resolutionרב אשי: what is near the back counts as the back
  2. Statementמשנה: which offerings on יום טוב, and leaning on them
  3. Statementשבועות on Friday: is the day of slaughter moved to Sunday?
  4. StatementBoth agree: if שבועות is on שבת, slaughter is on Sunday
  5. StatementNo festive clothes on that Sunday, so as not to support the wrong view
  6. Statementשבועות gets seven make-up days, like פסח
  7. ChallengeWhy not compare שבועות to סוכות and give it eight days?
  8. Resolutionשמיני עצרת is a separate holiday
  9. ChallengeFor make-up days, שמיני עצרת does count with סוכות
  10. ResolutionWhen unsure, take the smaller number
  11. ChallengeThen why does the פסוק mention סוכות?
  12. Resolutionסוכות is compared to פסח for staying overnight
  13. QuestionWhere do we learn that פסח requires staying overnight?
