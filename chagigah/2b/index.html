Topic

The end of the חצי עבד וחצי בן חורין debate, and which kind of חרש is exempt from ראייה and from שמחה.

Overview

This amud finishes the debate about a person who is half slave and half free. בית שמאי point out that he cannot marry anyone, even though the world was created to be filled with people. So the master is forced to free him, and the slave writes a document owing his master half his value. בית הלל agree to this. Then the גמרא goes back to our משנה's words 'except for a חרש, שוטה and קטן.' Because the חרש is listed with people who lack understanding, he must be someone who neither hears nor speaks. A person who only cannot hear, or only cannot speak, counts as fully capable. A ברייתא seems to say such a person is exempt, so רבינא (or רבא) explains that our משנה is missing words. Such a person is exempt from ראייה but still obligated in שמחה. A full חרש, a שוטה and a קטן are exempt from both. A ברייתא supporting this begins at the end of the amud and finishes on the next amud.

Opening lines

תִּקַּנְתֶּם אֶת רַבּוֹ,

You have fixed things for his master,

וְאֶת עַצְמוֹ לֹא תִּקַּנְתֶּם.

but for himself you have not fixed things.

לִישָּׂא שִׁפְחָה אֵינוֹ יָכוֹל,

To marry a maidservant he cannot,

בַּת חוֹרִין אֵינוֹ יָכוֹל.

and a free woman he cannot.
