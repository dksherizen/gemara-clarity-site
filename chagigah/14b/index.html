Topic

The stories of expounding מעשה מרכבה, the four who entered the פרדס, and questions put to בן זומא

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier discussion: people stayed trustworthy in Torah matters even when ירושלים fell, but not in business. The גמרא then returns to מעשה מרכבה with a ברייתא. רבי אלעזר בן ערך presents his own understanding before רבן יוחנן בן זכאי. Fire comes down, the trees sing, an angel confirms his words, and his rebbe praises him warmly. A second story follows: רבי יהושע expounds, clouds and a rainbow appear in midsummer, and angels gather. רבן יוחנן בן זכאי praises his students and tells of a dream inviting them to a special place in Heaven. The גמרא then asks why a list of three lecturers leaves out רבי אלעזר בן ערך, and explains that the list counts only a chain of lecturers. Next comes the famous ברייתא of four who entered the פרדס, where only רבי עקיבא came out safely. Finally, people bring questions to בן זומא. The second question, about a pregnant בתולה marrying a כהן גדול, continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הָא בְּדִבְרֵי תוֹרָה,

This one is about words of Torah,

הָא בְּמַשָּׂא וּמַתָּן.

that one is about business dealings.

בְּדִבְרֵי תוֹרָה — הֲווֹ,

In words of Torah, there were [trustworthy people];

בְּמַשָּׂא וּמַתָּן — לָא הֲווֹ.

in business dealings, there were not.
