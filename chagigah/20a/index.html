Chagigah 20a

חגיגה כ׳ ע״א

Open Chagigah 20a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Purity levels and guarding: whether חולין kept at the purity of קודש count as קודש, and when a lapse of attention (היסח הדעת) makes a careful person's items impure

Overview

The גמרא first asks whether ordinary food kept at the purity level of קודש counts like קודש. A proof from the silence of the משנה is pushed aside, but the case of יוחנן בן גודגדא shows that it does. Next come rulings that a careful person's clothes become impure through a lapse of attention: a shawl handed back by a friend, and clothes switched by mistake, including a case where רבי עקיבא ruled switched bathhouse clothes impure. רבי אושעיא challenges this with a ברייתא about a barrel guarded as wine that turned out to be oil, which stays pure. To explain that ברייתא, רבי ירמיה says a person can guard an item from impurity but not from being disqualified, and a ברייתא about a basket and a shovel shows that such half-guarding exists. רבה בר אבוה adds stories where רבי ישמעאל praised the rule that intent to guard is what keeps an item pure. The גמרא explains that in the switched-clothes cases the owner truly stopped paying attention. It then asks why a shawl held by a friend should be impure, and רבי יוחנן answers that people do not guard what someone else is holding. The amud ends by questioning that assumption, and the proof comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

מִדְּלָא קָתָנֵי בְּהוּ מַעֲלָה.

From the fact that it does not teach a higher level for them.

וְדִלְמָא הַאי דְּלָא קָתָנֵי בְּהוּ מַעֲלָה,

But perhaps the reason it does not teach a higher level for them

דְּאִי דָּמוּ לִתְרוּמָה — הָא תָּנֵי תְּרוּמָה,

is that if they are like תרומה, it already taught תרומה,

וְאִי דָּמוּ לְחוּלִּין — הָא תָּנֵי לְחוּלִּין.

and if they are like ordinary food, it already taught ordinary food.
