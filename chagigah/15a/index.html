Chagigah 15a

חגיגה ט״ו ע״א

Topic

בן זומא and the upper and lower waters, and how אחר (אלישע בן אבויה) left the Torah and refused רבי מאיר's pleas to return

Overview

The amud opens in the middle of a question to בן זומא: may a pregnant woman who still seems to be a בתולה marry a כהן גדול? He answers that שמואל's rare case does not matter, but she may have conceived in a bath. The גמרא defends that answer against another statement of שמואל. Next, a ברייתא tells how רבי יהושע found בן זומא lost in thought about the gap between the upper and lower waters, and said he was 'still outside.' The גמרא shows the flaw in בן זומא's proof and lists opinions on how wide that gap is. Then the גמרא turns to אחר, אלישע בן אבויה. He saw the angel מיטטרון sitting in heaven, wrongly thought there might be two powers, heard a בת קול shut him out from תשובה, and left the Torah. Several stories follow in which his student רבי מאיר keeps begging him to return, and אחר keeps answering that the heavenly voice excluded him. The amud ends as רבי מאיר brings him from one place of learning to another to hear children's verses, and the story continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

יָכוֹל אֲנִי לִבְעוֹל

I am able to have relations

כַּמָּה בְּעִילוֹת בְּלֹא דָּם.

several times without blood.

אוֹ דִלְמָא דִּשְׁמוּאֵל לָא שְׁכִיחָא?

Or perhaps a case like שמואל's is not common?

אֲמַר לְהוּ: דִּשְׁמוּאֵל לָא שְׁכִיחַ,

He said to them: a case like שמואל's is not common,
