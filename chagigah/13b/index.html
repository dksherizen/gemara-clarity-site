Topic

The מרכבה vision: its creatures, angels and wings, contradictions between פסוקים, and the fiery river

Overview

This amud keeps explaining the vision of the Divine Chariot (מרכבה) in ספר יחזקאל. It explains the word חשמל as fiery creatures that speak, or creatures that are silent when God speaks. It describes how the חיות run and return like flickering fire. רב says the stormy wind went to make נבוכדנצר ruler of the world, so ישראל would not be shamed by a lowly nation. A ברייתא names the angel סנדלפון, who ties crowns that rise to God by themselves. רבא says ישעיהו saw the same vision as יחזקאל but described it like a city dweller who is used to the king. The גמרא then settles several contradictions between פסוקים: the ox face that became a כרוב, six wings versus four, and the number of angels. Several of these are answered by the destruction of the בית המקדש, which reduced things in heaven too. Finally it discusses the fiery river, נהר דינור: it comes from the sweat of the חיות and pours onto the wicked, or onto 974 generations that were never created. That last teaching continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

חַיּוֹת אֵשׁ מְמַלְּלוֹת.

Speaking creatures of fire.

בְּמַתְנִיתָא תָּנָא:

It was taught in a baraita:

עִתִּים חָשׁוֹת, עִתִּים מְמַלְּלוֹת.

At times they are silent, at times they speak.

בְּשָׁעָה שֶׁהַדִּיבּוּר יוֹצֵא

When the divine speech goes out
