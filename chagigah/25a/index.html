Topic

Trusting an עם הארץ about the purity of wine and oil: the גליל versus יהודה for קודש, and the pressing season for תרומה

Overview

The amud opens with the end of the משנה: an עם הארץ is trusted about mixed jugs of wine and oil during the pressing season and for seventy days before it. The גמרא asks why the משנה trusts people in יהודה but not in the גליל for wine and oil of קודש. ריש לקיש answers that a strip of כותים land lies between the two regions, and passing through it makes the produce טמא. The גמרא tests this answer three times. A closed box does not help, because the משנה follows רבי, who holds that an אהל זרוק is not a tent. A sealed clay jug does not help, because קודש is not protected by a צמיד פתיל. The חברים of the גליל who made their produce pure simply stored it until אליהו comes. Next the גמרא asks how the משנה can trust an עם הארץ with תרומה during the pressing season when another teaching does not trust him. רב נחמן answers that the משנה is about people who press in season and the other teaching is about late pressers. רב יוסף's answer, that the other teaching is about the גליל, is rejected from a ברייתא. Finally, רב ששת is asked whether a כהן who wrongly accepted a barrel after the season may keep it until the next season, and he begins to answer from a משנה that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הַמְדוּמָּעוֹת — נֶאֱמָנִין עֲלֵיהֶם

That are mixed: they are trusted about them

בִּשְׁעַת הַגִּיתּוֹת וְהַבַּדִּים,

during the time of the winepresses and the olive presses,

וְקוֹדֶם לַגִּיתּוֹת שִׁבְעִים יוֹם.

and seventy days before the winepresses.

גְּמָ׳ בִּיהוּדָה אִין,

GEMARA: In Judea, yes,

The steps (22)

  1. StatementEnd of the משנה: trust about mixed jugs during the pressing season
  2. QuestionWhy יהודה and not the גליל?
  3. Responseריש לקיש: a strip of כותים land separates them
  4. ChallengeWhy not carry it in a closed box?
  5. ResolutionThe משנה follows רבי: a moving tent is not a tent
  6. ProofThe ברייתא showing רבי's view
  7. ChallengeWhy not use a sealed clay jug?
  8. Resolutionקודש is not protected by a sealed lid
  9. ChallengeA ברייתא suggests only חטאת water is unprotected
  10. RejectionThe ברייתא is contrasting water not yet made holy
  11. ChallengeBut חברים made pure wine and oil in the גליל
  12. ResolutionThey set it aside until אליהו comes
  13. StatementThe משנה: trusted on תרומה during the pressing season
  14. ChallengeContradiction: leave one basket for a poor כהן
  15. ResolutionEarly pressers versus late pressers
  16. StatementAn example of late pressers
  17. ResolutionAnother answer: the strict teaching is about the גליל
  18. ChallengeThe גליל is the same as יהודה for תרומה
  19. ConclusionBack to the first answer
  20. StatementAfter the pressing season: do not accept the barrel
