Topic

The מצוה of ראיון (appearing in the בית המקדש on the רגלים): does it have a שיעור, and what exactly is the ראיון?

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the משנה in פאה that lists things with no fixed measure, including the ראיון, the מצוה of appearing in the מקדש on the רגלים. רבי יוחנן explains that from the Torah the ראיון has no minimum and no maximum, and the minimum amounts come from the חכמים. Next, the גמרא asks what the ראיון is. רבי יוחנן says it is showing up in the עזרה, and ריש לקיש says it is showing up with a קרבן. At first the גמרא explains that their dispute is about someone who comes on the later days of the רגל without a קרבן. ריש לקיש attacks רבי יוחנן three times from sources that forbid coming 'empty-handed', and each time רבי יוחנן answers that they speak only of the main day. Then רבי יוחנן challenges ריש לקיש from 'just as I come for free, so may you'. The גמרא accepts this challenge and reframes the dispute: everyone allows coming without a קרבן, and they argue only about how many offerings one may bring. A challenge from 'הֹקַר רַגְלְךָ' is answered by saying that the פסוק is about חטאות and אשמות, with support from רבי לוי and a ברייתא. Finally, רב יוסף begins to explain the ברייתא's rule against appearing 'in halves'.
