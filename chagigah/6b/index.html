Chagigah 6b

חגיגה ו׳ ע״ב

Learn Chagigah 6b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Chagigah 6b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Was the עולה at הר סיני an עולת ראייה or the עולת תמיד? Which תנאים hold each view, why רבי ישמעאל is removed from the list, and רב חסדא's question about which animals were brought.

Overview

The גמרא asks which תנאים hold that the עולה brought at הר סיני was an עולת ראייה, and which hold it was the עולת תמיד. For רבי ישמעאל, the details of the מצוות came only later in the אהל מועד. So it first seems he cannot hold the offering was the תמיד, since the same offering would have gone from not needing הפשט וניתוח to needing them. רבי אלעזר says the laws of the תמיד were said at סיני but it was not brought there. רבי עקיבא says it was brought and never stopped, and he explains the פסוק in עמוס as meaning that only שבט לוי brought it. On the other side, the גמרא brings בית הלל, רבי עקיבא, and רבי יוסי הגלילי, whose ברייתא shows that only the חגיגה existed before the דיבור. Then the גמרא notes that רבי יוסי הגלילי holds the desert תמיד really did change from no הפשט וניתוח to needing them, so רבי ישמעאל is taken off the list. רב חסדא asks whether the desert עולות were sheep or bulls. That would matter for the trope of the פסוק or for a vow, and the question ends in תיקו. Finally, the גמרא begins quoting a משנה in פאה about things that have no set measure.

Opening lines

וּפְרָטוֹת בְּאֹהֶל מוֹעֵד.

and the details in the Tent of Meeting.

וְרַבִּי עֲקִיבָא אוֹמֵר:

And Rabbi Akiva says:

כְּלָלוֹת וּפְרָטוֹת נֶאֶמְרוּ בְּסִינַי,

General rules and details were said at Sinai,

וְנִשְׁנוּ בְּאֹהֶל מוֹעֵד,

and were repeated in the Tent of Meeting,
