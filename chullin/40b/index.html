Chullin 40b

חולין מ׳ ע״ב

Open Chullin 40b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Can one act of שחיטה for עבודה זרה break three prohibitions at once, and can a person forbid an animal that is not his?

Overview

The גמרא continues testing רב הונא's ruling that cutting one סימן of an animal for עבודה זרה makes it forbidden. It tests the ruling against a ברייתא: one who slaughters a חטאת on שבת, outside the עזרה, for עבודה זרה brings three חטאות. The problem is that once the animal is forbidden, finishing the cut is like 'chopping dirt' and should not count as שחוטי חוץ. רב פפא first places the case in a bird חטאת. The גמרא objects that עולא, whom רב הונא follows, says even a tiny act forbids the animal. A second answer, that the person said he worships only at the end of the שחיטה, fails because then the ברייתא could have spoken of any קרבן. מר זוטרא in the name of רב פפא gives the final answer: a bird whose windpipe was already half cut, so one tiny cut makes all three prohibitions happen at once. רב פפא then notes that the difficulty comes from two details in רב הונא's ruling: 'one סימן' and 'his fellow's animal.' Owning a חטאת for כפרה does not make it his own. Finally, רב נחמן, רב עמרם and רב יצחק rule that a person cannot forbid what is not his, and the גמרא begins a challenge to them from the same ברייתא, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מְחַתֵּךְ בְּעָפָר הוּא!

He is just chopping in dirt!

אָמַר רַב פָּפָּא:

Rav Pappa said:

הָכָא בְּחַטַּאת הָעוֹף עָסְקִינַן,

Here we are dealing with a bird sin offering,

דְּכוּלְּהוּ בַּהֲדֵי הֲדָדֵי קָאָתֵי.

where all of them come at the same time.
