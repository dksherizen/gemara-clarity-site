Chullin 111a

חולין קי״א ע״א

Topic

Liver and blood: cooking liver with other meat, draining its blood, and roasting liver or udder above other meat

Overview

The גמרא finishes an earlier question by saying the other food was forbidden because of the liver's fat. It then asks a new question: does the liver's blood make other meat cooked with it forbidden? רבי זריקא brings a story where rabbis ate a windpipe cooked with the liver, but רב אשי rejects the proof, so the question has no answer from that story. A side point follows: רבא shows that vinegar used to scald liver cannot be forbidden. Next, רב נחמן insists that his guest eat cooked liver, and the גמרא shows that תנאים already argued about liver cooked with other meat. A story at a שבת meal teaches how to cut liver so the blood in its vessels drains out, and the גמרא adds that spleen, which is just fat, needs no cutting. Then come two opposite versions of a rule about roasting liver or udder above other meat, and מרימר gives the final הלכה. The amud ends as a new story about רב אשי at his father-in-law's house begins, and it continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וּמִשּׁוּם שַׁמְנוּנִיתָא.

And it is because of the fat.

מִשּׁוּם דְּמָא, מַאי?

Because of blood, what is the law?

When he again went up,

The steps (17)

  1. ConclusionThe earlier case is forbidden because of fat
  2. QuestionWhat about the liver's blood alone?
  3. Responseרבי זריקא answers from a meal he ate
  4. Rejectionרב אשי says the story proves nothing
  5. Statementרב פפא thinks the scalding vinegar becomes forbidden
  6. Rejectionרבא: then the liver would be forbidden too
  7. Statementרב בר שבא refuses cooked liver
  8. StatementThe question about liver is a dispute between תנאים
  9. StatementA special meal in honor of שבת
  10. ChallengeA liver with a blood-filled vessel
  11. ResponseHow to prepare liver so the blood drains
  12. StatementThe cutting rule applies to liver, not spleen
  13. ProofSupport from שמואל's spleen dish
  14. StatementLiver above meat is permitted, udder above meat is forbidden
  15. Statementרב דימי teaches the opposite
  16. Conclusionמרימר's final ruling
  17. Statementרב אשי visits his father-in-law's house
