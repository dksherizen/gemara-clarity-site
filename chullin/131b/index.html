Topic

Chullin 131b: the poor-gifts and the owner's rights, whether לוים must give the priestly gifts, and whether a כהנת may receive them

Overview

The גמרא first finishes a ברייתא about the gifts for the poor. It brings the פסוקים for the gifts of a grain field (לקט, שכחה, פאה) and of a tree (שכחה, פאה). The owner has no טובת הנאה in these gifts, meaning he cannot choose who gets them, because the תורה says to 'leave' them. מעשר עני is different: the תורה says to 'give' it, so the owner may choose. Even a poor owner must give these gifts, and בית דין takes them from him if he does not. The גמרא then asks whether לוים count as 'the people' who must give the זרוע, לחיים and קבה to a כהן, which רב was unsure about. Several ברייתות are brought and pushed off, until one seems to refute רב outright. רב answers that this is a מחלוקת of תנאים, and he is unsure which one to follow. מרימר rules like רב and like רב חסדא. Finally, רבא challenges עולא for giving the gifts to a כהנת, and עולא's reply continues on the next amud.
