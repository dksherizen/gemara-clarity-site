Topic

Who may slaughter: the impure slaughterer's knife, and the readings of the משנה by אביי (כותי under supervision), רבא (a Jew going in and out), and רב אשי (ישראל משומד)

Overview

This amud starts in the middle of a challenge. A person who is impure from a מת is an אב הטומאה, so he should make his metal knife impure ('חרב הרי הוא כחלל'), and the knife would then make the meat impure. The גמרא gives two answers. In the first, his impurity came from a שרץ, which cannot pass to the knife. In the second, he slaughtered with a reed stalk, which cannot become impure. Then אביי reads the משנה as being about a כותי: he may slaughter from the start only when a Jew stands over him. If he slaughtered without that, he is given a כזית of the meat to see whether he eats it. רבא challenges this and proves from a משנה about guarding wine that a Jew going in and out is enough from the start. So he gives his own reading of the משנה. Both readings run into the same question: what case do the words 'וכולן ששחטו' refer to? Both end with קשיא. Finally, רב אשי reads 'הכל שוחטין' as including a ישראל משומד who eats נבילות לתיאבון, in line with a ruling of רבא that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

חֶרֶב הֲרֵי הוּא כֶּחָלָל,

A sword is like a corpse,

אַב הַטּוּמְאָה הוּא,

he is a primary source of impurity,

לְטַמְּיֵיהּ לְסַכִּין,

so let him make the knife impure,

וַאֲזַל סַכִּין וְטַמִּיתֵיהּ לְבָשָׂר!

and the knife goes and makes the meat impure!
