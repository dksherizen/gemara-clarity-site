Chullin 7a

חולין ז׳ ע״א

Learn Chullin 7a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Chullin 7a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

רבי's permission of בית שאן and the challenges to it, the respect owed to a תלמיד חכם's new הלכה, and the story of רבי פנחס בן יאיר and his animal.

Overview

This amud finishes רבי's answer to his family about permitting the produce of בית שאן. Just as earlier kings left חזקיהו room to make his mark, רבי's ancestors left him room to make his. From this the גמרא learns that a תלמיד חכם who says a new הלכה must not be pushed aside. It gives three wordings of this lesson, each with its own source. Then יהודה בריה דרבי שמעון בן פזי asks how בית שאן could be outside ארץ ישראל. The answer is that עולי בבל never conquered it, and רבי holds that the first holiness of the land was only for its time. Next, רבי ירמיה raises several doubts about the proof from רבי מאיר eating a leaf there without tithing, and רבי זירא answers each one. The phrase 'animals of the righteous' leads to the story of רבי פנחס בן יאיר, for whom the river גינאי split. The amud ends as his donkey refuses barley at an inn, and the story continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא מָקוֹם הִנִּיחוּ לוֹ אֲבוֹתָיו

Rather, his ancestors left him a place

לְהִתְגַּדֵּר בּוֹ,

through which to make his mark;

אַף אֲנִי מָקוֹם הִנִּיחוּ לִי אֲבוֹתַי

I too, my ancestors left me a place

לְהִתְגַּדֵּר בּוֹ.

through which to make my mark.
