Topic

Fat and mucus as seals for holes, the corrected rulings on טרפה and אבל, comparing holes (מקיפין), and a hole in the rectum

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a question on רב נחמן's rule that only permitted fat (בר חמצא) seals a hole. The גמרא answers by splitting the קיבה into a curved 'bow' side and a straight 'bowstring' side. It gives two versions of which side is in dispute. It then shows how the people of ארץ ישראל ate this fat after removing a handful, with stories about רבי אמי and רבי חנינא. Next comes a ברייתא in which רבן שמעון בן גמליאל says mucus (ליחה) sealing a hole in the intestines keeps the animal כשרה. A report in the name of רבי יוחנן says the הלכה follows him on טרפה, and follows רבי שמעון on a late-arriving mourner (אבל). A scholar checks the report with רבי אבא and learns that the טרפה part was backwards. The final ruling is that the animal is a טרפה, while in אבל we follow the lenient רבי שמעון, as שמואל taught. The amud then teaches that a doubtful hole may be compared with a new hole (מקיפין) in the intestines, the lung and the windpipe, with limits on what may be compared. It closes with זעירי's rule that a hole in the חלחולת is kosher where the hips hold it, and רבא's objection to quoting this in רב נחמן's name, which continues on the next amud.
