Topic

Partnerships with a Jew or a גוי, and whether they are obligated in ראשית הגז, תרומה, חלה, פאה, בכורה and מזוזה

Overview

The גמרא finishes the debate over whether רבי אלעאי may learn the law of ראשית הגז from תרומה. The רבנן say the repeated word ראשית separates the two. רבי אלעאי answers that the repeat only marks their different kinds of holiness, and the וי"ו joins them back together. A second answer for the רבנן is that even in תרומה a partnership with a גוי is obligated. A ברייתא proves this: רבי and רבן שמעון בן גמליאל argue only about ברירה. Another explanation has רבי אלעאי learn both exemptions from the word צאנך, because in both cases the sheep is not his alone. The רבנן answer that only a גוי partner exempts, since a גוי has no obligation. רבא then shows that רבי אלעאי agrees partners are obligated in תרומה, חלה, פאה, בכורה and מזוזה, because each has a plural word in the Torah. The singular words either exclude a partnership with a גוי, teach the amount of dough for חלה, or, for ביתך, are needed for a teaching of רבה that continues on the next amud.
