Topic

Two פסוקים about הכשר, אביי's answer that some הכשר is only מדרבנן, and the Torah source for חיבת הקודש

Overview

The גמרא first asks whether one of two פסוקים about הכשר comes to exclude holy food (חיבת הקודש). It answers that one פסוק is for טומאת מת and one is for טומאת שרץ. Both are needed, because each kind of טומאה is stricter in its own way. Next, רב יוסף and רבי זירא challenge רבי אלעזר from cases where food becomes susceptible without water: a slaughtered animal, and grapes harvested for the winepress. אביי answers both: the חכמים treated these like הכשר by water, only מדרבנן. רב יוסף objects that this answer would also settle ריש לקיש's question about dry holy flour. אביי replies that ריש לקיש was asking about burning, which needs טומאה מדאורייתא. This shows that ריש לקיש holds חיבת הקודש is דאורייתא, so the גמרא searches for its source. A proof from the start of ויקרא 7:19 fails, because the animal may have been wet from a river. The end of the פסוק, which includes wood and frankincense, proves that holiness alone makes things able to become טמא.

Opening lines

לָאו לְמַעוֹטֵי חִיבַּת הַקֹּדֶשׁ?

Is it not to exclude items made susceptible through regard for sanctity?

לָא: חַד בְּטוּמְאַת מֵת

No: one is about impurity of a corpse,

וְחַד בְּטוּמְאַת שֶׁרֶץ.

and one is about impurity of a creeping animal.

וּצְרִיכִי, דְּאִי אַשְׁמְעִינַן טוּמְאַת מֵת –

And they are needed, for if it had taught us impurity of a corpse,
