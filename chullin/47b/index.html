Chullin 47b

חולין מ״ז ע״ב

Learn Chullin 47b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Chullin 47b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Lung colors, textures, and tests that decide whether an animal is a טרפה, and whether damage inside the lung counts as a missing piece.

Overview

This amud finishes a ruling about a defect on the back of the lung, then goes through many lung conditions. רפרם rules that a lung 'like a chip of wood' is a טרפה, and several opinions explain what that means. רבא says a lung dark like eye shadow is כשרה but black like ink is a טרפה, because black is red that went bad. Green and red lungs are כשרה, proven from two stories of רבי נתן about babies whose ברית מילה was delayed until they recovered. רב כהנא and רב סמא add more appearances: liver-like and leek-green are כשרה, while meat-like and yellowish shades are a טרפה. רבינא and רב יוסף then give hands-on tests for a sealed spot and for a whistling lung, using a feather, saliva, or lukewarm water. Finally, a lung that turned to liquid, or wasted away inside, is כשרה if the outer skin and airway tubes are whole. רבי אבא's challenge from the משנה is answered with the view of רבי שמעון. A new ruling begins at the very end and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֲבָל אַגַּבַּהּ,

But if it is on its back,

אֲפִילּוּ כְּטַרְפָּא דְאָסָא – טְרֵפָה.

even as small as a myrtle leaf – it is a tereifa.

אָמַר רַפְרָם:

Rafram says:

הַאי רֵיאָה דְּדָמְיָא לְאוּפְתָּא – טְרֵפָה.

This lung that is similar to a chip of wood – it is a tereifa.
