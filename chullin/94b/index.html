Topic

Which kind of town (announcing non-kosher sales or not) the ברייתא is set in, how the announcement is worded, and why letting someone mislead himself is not deception

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא about sending meat to a גוי and then asks what kind of town it describes. In some towns, Jewish butchers announce every time they sell non-kosher meat to a גוי. In other towns, they don't. The first and last clauses of the ברייתא seem to fit only a town that does not announce. The middle clause, which worries that a גוי will resell נבלות to a Jew, seems to fit only a town that does announce. אביי accepts that the clauses are set in different kinds of town. רבא puts them all in an announcing town and splits them by whether an announcement was actually made. רב אשי puts them all in a non-announcing town and explains the middle clause as a גזירה, in case a Jew sees the sale. The גמרא then gives the wording of the announcement, 'meat has fallen to the soldiers,' and explains that this is not deceiving the גוים, because they deceive themselves. A story about מר זוטרא, רבא and רב ספרא supports this idea. The amud ends as a new story about a butcher begins, and it continues on the next amud.
