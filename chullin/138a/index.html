Topic

ראשית הגז: proving that a מנה of forty סלעים exists, how the five-סלע measure is weighed, and the פסוק source for the 'small garment' measure (the אבנט).

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a line from the previous page about a weight of sixty סלעים. It then proves that a תנא really speaks of a מנה worth forty סלעים, using רבי אליעזר בן יעקב's words about a torn leather flask. Next the גמרא explains the משנה's measure for ראשית הגז. The five סלעים are weighed after washing, but the כהן does the washing, not the owner. The 'small garment' measure comes from 'לעמד לשרת': the gift must be enough for an אבנט. The אבנט is chosen over the bigger מעיל because we take the smaller amount. The wool כיפה does not count because 'הוא ובניו' requires a garment shared by all כהנים, and every כהן wears something called a 'belt.' Then רב חסדא and רבי נתן בר הושעיא disagree about someone who shears each sheep and then sells it. Last, the גמרא asks which תנא says the obligation follows a seller who kept some wool. רב חסדא's answer, רבי יהודה's ruling on פאה, is rejected by רבא. רבא brings instead the משנה about buying a cow's innards that contain the priestly gifts.
