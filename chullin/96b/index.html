Chullin 96b

חולין צ״ו ע״ב

Open Chullin 96b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

How רבנן and רבי יהודה read the פסוק of גיד הנשה, and the משנה about a גיד הנשה cooked or roasted with other food

Overview

The amud first finishes the dispute about eating a small גיד הנשה. רבי יהודה requires a כזית because the פסוק says 'eating', while רבנן use that word to teach that one כזית out of a big nerve is enough. Going back and forth, the גמרא shows how each side uses 'על כף הירך' and 'הירך' differently. It ends that part by answering that the word כף is written twice, so it can both exclude birds and teach שמואל's rule. Then a new משנה teaches that a thigh cooked with its גיד הנשה is forbidden if the nerve gives off taste, measured like meat flavoring a turnip. The same rule applies to a forbidden piece cooked with other pieces: if it is recognized we go by taste, if not all the pieces are forbidden, and the broth always goes by taste. In the גמרא, שמואל limits the משנה to cooking, saying a thigh roasted with the nerve may be peeled and eaten up to the nerve. The גמרא challenges him from רב הונא, who forbids a kid roasted with its חלב even at the tip of its ear, and the answer comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְרַבִּי יְהוּדָה – אֲכִילָה כְּתִיבָה בֵּיהּ,

And Rabbi Yehuda? 'Eating' is written regarding it,

וְרַבָּנַן – הַהִיא אֲכִילָה

And the Rabbis? That word 'eating' is needed

דְּכִי אִית בֵּיהּ אַרְבָּעָה וַחֲמִשָּׁה זֵיתִים

to teach that when it contains four or five olive-bulks

וַאֲכַל חַד כְּזַיִת מִיחַיַּיב.

and he ate one olive-bulk, he is liable.

The steps (13)

  1. Statementרבי יהודה's source: the Torah says 'eating'
  2. ResponseHow רבנן use the word 'eating'
  3. Responseרבי יהודה's source for eating part of the nerve
  4. Responseרבנן use 'upon the spoon' for שמואל's rule
  5. Responseרבי יהודה: the whole thigh is included
  6. Responseרבנן: 'the thigh' excludes the outer nerve
  7. ChallengeThe word כף is already needed to exclude a bird
  8. ResolutionThe word כף is written twice
  9. StatementA thigh cooked with its גיד הנשה
  10. StatementA גיד הנשה cooked with other sinews
  11. StatementThe same rule for נבלה and non-kosher fish
  12. Statementשמואל: roasting does not spread the taste of the nerve
  13. ChallengeChallenge from רב הונא: a kid roasted with its fat is fully forbidden
