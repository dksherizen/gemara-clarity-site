Chullin 43a

חולין מ״ג ע״א

Learn Chullin 43a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Chullin 43a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The categories of טרפות, רבי יוחנן's rulings on the gallbladder, liver and gizzard, and holes in the layers of the gullet and gizzard

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier count of the טרפות in the משנה. עולא then teaches that all טרפות fit into eight basic types told to משה at סיני, and חייא בר רבא says the type called נקובה has eight cases. He explains that the perforated gallbladder is only the view of רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה. Next comes a series of rulings from רבי יוחנן. The הלכה follows רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה on the gallbladder, since איוב survived only by a miracle. On a removed liver, a כזית must remain, and the גמרא shows that his students report his view differently. Two more rulings follow: a pierced gallbladder sealed by the liver is kosher, and a pierced gizzard with its inner lining whole is kosher. The גמרא then discusses organs with two layers, including the gullet's red outer layer and white inner layer, and holes in both layers that do not line up. Finally רבה teaches that a scab over a hole in the gullet does not count as a covering, and that the gullet must be checked from the inside. The amud ends by asking when that last rule makes a practical difference, and the answer comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא הָנָךְ תַּרְתֵּי דְּאַפֵּקְתְּ,

Rather, those two that you removed,

לָא תַּפֵּיק.

do not remove.

אָמַר עוּלָּא:

Ulla said:

שְׁמֹנָה מִינֵי טְרֵפוֹת

Eight types of tereifot

The steps (27)

  5. ResolutionWhy not nine? The gallbladder is one sage's view

  7. StatementMemory aid for the coming statements

  9. ChallengeThe colleagues challenge from Job's gall poured out
  10. Resolutionרבי יוסי answers that a miracle is different
  11. Statementרבי יוחנן rules that an olive-bulk of liver must remain

  13. ResolutionTwo אמוראים disagree about רבי יוחנן's view

  23. Challengeרב אשי: it should be the other way around
  24. ConclusionThe correct report of מר זוטרא matches רב אשי
  25. StatementA scab over a wound in the gullet is not a real membrane
  26. StatementThe gullet must be checked from the inside
