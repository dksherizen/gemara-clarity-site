Topic

The צומת הגידין (its strands and how much cutting makes a טרפה), and a broken leg bone: where the break is, and how much skin and flesh must still cover it

Overview

The גמרא finishes defining the צומת הגידין, the bundle of sinews in the thigh. In an animal it has three strands, one thick and two thin. Two versions disagree on whether cutting the thick one or the two thin ones makes the animal a טרפה. In birds there are sixteen strands, and cutting even one makes it a טרפה. רב and שמואל then argue over how much must be cut, and the גמרא gives two versions of their exchange, compared with רבנאי's report of שמואל. Next comes the משנה's broken leg bone. רב says a break above the ארכובה with most of the flesh gone makes the animal a טרפה. שמואל says only the limb is forbidden. After a back-and-forth, the ruling from ארץ ישראל follows רב, and we learn that שמואל himself took back his view. The amud ends with a bone that broke and came out of the body. The גמרא discusses how much must stay covered and whether skin counts like flesh. It then starts a case of soft sinews before רבה, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מָר בַּר רַב אָשֵׁי אָמַר:

Mar bar Rav Ashi said:

כֵּיוָן דְּזִיגִי –

Once they look clear,

אַף עַל גַּב דְּלָא חִוָּורֵי.

even though they are not white.
