Chullin 16b

חולין ט״ז ע״ב

Learn Chullin 16b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Chullin 16b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Attached blades, a fixed knife and pressing, reed-stalk rules, and the משנה's 'הכל שוחטין' and 'לעולם שוחטין'

Overview

The גמרא asks whether a blade that was detached and later attached counts as attached for שחיטה. Several proofs from a ברייתא are tried and all are pushed aside: the flint is in a cave wall that was always attached, and a knife stuck in a wall never becomes part of the wall. שמואל then limits the knife-in-the-wall ruling to a knife above the neck, because of the worry of pressing (דרסה). רב זביד and רב פפא each defend שמואל against a ברייתא that seems to allow both positions. Next comes a list of five things you may not do with a reed stalk, and the גמרא narrows or explains several of them. Finally the גמרא explains the משנה's words 'הכל שוחטין' (every animal, even a bird, needs שחיטה) and 'לעולם שוחטין'. רבה ties the second phrase to רבי ישמעאל's view about בשר תאוה staying permitted even after the exile. רב יוסף begins objecting to this, and his objection continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

תָּלוּשׁ וּלְבַסּוֹף חִבְּרוֹ

Detached and in the end he attached it

לְעִנְיַן שְׁחִיטָה מַאי?

with regard to slaughter, what is the law?

תָּא שְׁמַע:

Come and hear:

הָיָה צוֹר יוֹצֵא מִן הַכּוֹתֶל,

If there was a flint sticking out of the wall,
