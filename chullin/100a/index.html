Chullin 100a

חולין ק׳ ע״א

Open Chullin 100a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Why a בריה and a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד are not cancelled out, and the debate between רבה בר בר חנה and רב over a forbidden piece that gives flavor to kosher pieces.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier question. The גיד הנשה is not cancelled out because it is a בריה, a distinct thing of its own. The גמרא then turns to the משנה's next case, a piece of נבלה mixed with kosher pieces, and asks why the kosher majority doesn't cancel it out. It answers that a piece of meat fit to serve to honored guests is too important to be cancelled. Next, the גמרא explains why the משנה needed to teach both the גיד and the piece, since each has a reason the other lacks. A new discussion follows. רבה בר בר חנה rules that a forbidden piece forbids the pot only if its flavor reaches everything. רב is stricter: a kosher piece that absorbs the flavor becomes נבלה and forbids all the pieces of its kind. רב ספרא challenges רב, and אביי answers that the forbidden piece was removed early. רבא then begins a second answer, which continues on the next amud.
