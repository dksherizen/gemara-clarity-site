Topic

The גיד הנשה of a non-kosher animal and of a נבלה, and when one prohibition can take hold on another (איסור כולל, איסור חמור), proven from a טמא who eats impure קדשים.

Overview

The amud opens with the end of a ברייתא: רבי שמעון exempts someone who eats the גיד הנשה of a non-kosher animal. The גמרא challenges this with a מה נפשך argument. רבא answers that sinews have no meat-taste, and a פסוק limits the גיד prohibition to animals whose meat is permitted. Next, רב יהודה quotes רב about the גיד of a נבלה: רבי מאיר says the eater is liable twice, and the חכמים say only once. The חכמים agree, though, that the גיד of an עולה or a שור הנסקל brings two punishments. רבא says this split view belongs to רבי יוסי הגלילי, and he proves it from a משנה about a טמא who eats impure קדשים. He explains that they argue only when the meat became impure first: the חכמים use איסור כולל (מגו), and רבי יוסי הגלילי rejects it. Finally the גמרא asks why the stricter טומאת הגוף does not take hold anyway. רב אשי answers that טומאת בשר may be the stricter one, since impure meat can never be purified in a מקוה.
