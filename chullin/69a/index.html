Topic

How far the mother's שחיטה reaches for her עובר: the boundary of a fetus, a limb that came out, the offspring and milk of such an animal, and the פסוק that permits the fetus

Overview

The גמרא first tries to prove something from the משנה's closing כלל, but pushes the proof off: the extra words teach that a קלוט still inside a cow is permitted, even for רבי שמעון. רב חנניא then asks whether the עזרה counts as the boundary for a fetus of קדשים whose leg came out. אביי answers that a fetus's boundary is always its mother. אילפא asks about a leg that came out between the two סימנים, and רבא uses a קל וחומר to rule that the leg is טהור from נבלה. רבי ירמיה asks about the offspring of such a fetus. After several reframings, the גמרא's real question is whether that animal's milk may be drunk, and it ends in תיקו. Finally, the גמרא looks for the פסוק that permits pieces cut from a fetus. It drops 'בהמה בבהמה' because of the laws of תמורה and uses 'וכל בהמה', limited by 'אותה'. It then raises a challenge from רבי יוחנן's ruling about a dove-shaped fetus, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

זֶה הַכְּלָל: דָּבָר שֶׁגּוּפָהּ – אָסוּר,

This is the principle: something that is its body is forbidden,

וְשֶׁאֵינָהּ גּוּפָהּ – מוּתָּר.

and something that is not its body is permitted.

שֶׁאֵין גּוּפָהּ לְאֵתוֹיֵי מַאי?

'That is not its body' — what does it come to include?

לָאו לְאֵתוֹיֵי כְּהַאי גַּוְנָא?

Is it not to include a case like this?
