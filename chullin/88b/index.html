Topic

כיסוי הדם: which materials may cover the blood, and רבא's teachings on אברהם's "dust and ashes"

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא about כיסוי הדם. The word "וכסהו" includes fine materials like sawdust, lime and crushed brick, and the word "בעפר" leaves out coarse materials, metal filings and flour. Anything that is a type of earth is valid. רב מרי explains why we do not read the פסוק as a כלל ופרט, which would allow only real earth. רב נחמן בר רב חסדא teaches that the material must be something seeds can grow in. רבא calls that rule nonsense, but רב נחמן בר יצחק defends it from a ברייתא: a traveler in the desert grinds a gold coin instead of using sand, and a traveler on a ship burns his garment for ash. Gold counts because a פסוק calls it עפר, and ash counts because of בית הלל's proof in their argument with בית שמאי. A ברייתא then adds more valid materials. Finally, רבא teaches that אברהם's words "I am dust and ashes" earned his children אפר פרה and עפר סוטה. His second teaching about אברהם begins at the end of the amud and continues on the next amud.

לְבָנָה וּמְגוּפָה שֶׁכְּתָשָׁן?
