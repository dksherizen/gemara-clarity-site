Topic

Whether רבי יהודה is sure which thigh's גיד הנשה is forbidden, his sources for the right thigh, and aggadot about יעקב's wrestling

Overview

רבי יהודה holds that גיד הנשה is forbidden in only one thigh. The גמרא asks whether he is sure it is the right thigh or unsure which one. רב אשי and רבינא push away a proof from the burning of sinews of a קרבן פסח: the burning could be for the fat, or for the outer nerve, which is only forbidden מדרבנן. A proof from a משנה about lashes uses the rule that רבי יהודה holds an התראת ספק does not count. That proof is turned aside, because another report of רבי יהודה, learned from the case of leftover פסח meat, says it does count. A ברייתא about two nerves from two thighs of two animals then proves that רבי יהודה is sure. His giving only forty lashes is explained by one nerve being smaller than a כזית. The גמרא gives several sources for the right thigh: the word 'הַיָּרֵךְ', and pictures of how the מלאך wrestled with יעקב. The רבנן say the מלאך came from behind and struck both thighs. The amud ends with aggadic teachings about the name גיד הנשה, יוסף's brothers, יעקב staying behind for small jars, and the rule that a תלמיד חכם should not go out alone at night. The source for that last rule continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

רַב אָשֵׁי אָמַר:

Rav Ashi said:

לֹא נִצְרְכָה אֶלָּא לְשׇׁמְנוֹ,

It is needed only for its fat,

דְּתַנְיָא: שׇׁמְנוֹ מוּתָּר,

as it is taught: its fat is permitted,

וְיִשְׂרָאֵל קְדוֹשִׁים נָהֲגוּ בּוֹ אִיסּוּר.

but the Jewish people are holy and treated it as forbidden.
