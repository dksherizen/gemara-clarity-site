Chullin 20b

חולין כ׳ ע״ב

Open Chullin 20b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

עיקור סימנין in a bird, זעירי's ruling on a broken neck bone, and the מצוה of הבדלה in a bird עולה

Overview

This amud deals with three questions about bird שחיטה and מליקה. First, it asks how far רמי בר יחזקאל's rule, that עיקור סימנין does not disqualify a bird, reaches. One view ties the rule to the opinion that bird שחיטה is only מדברי סופרים. רב אשי argues the reverse: under the rabbinic view, bird שחיטה copies animal שחיטה completely, so עיקור should apply. רבין בר קיסי limits the rule a different way, to מליקה only, and the גמרא admits this disagrees with שמואל. Next, זעירי rules that a broken neck bone with most of the flesh makes an animal or bird a נבלה. רב חסדא tries to prove this from a bird pinched with a knife, but the proof is rejected, because pinching with a knife is not slaughter at all, whether due to מחליד or דורס. Finally, רבא challenges זעירי: if a broken neck makes a נבלה, why does the כהן keep pinching a bird that is already dead? This leads to a discussion of the מצוה of הבדלה in a bird עולה, which ends with the rule that whatever is part of שחיטה is part of הבדלה.

Opening lines

אֲבָל לְמַאן דְּאָמַר

But according to the one who says

יֵשׁ שְׁחִיטָה לָעוֹף מִן הַתּוֹרָה,

there is slaughter for a bird from the Torah,

יֵשׁ עִיקּוּר.

there is ripping (it disqualifies).

אֲמַר לֵיהּ: אַדְּרַבָּה,

He said to him: On the contrary,

  2. RejectionThe opposite makes more sense for the Torah-law view

  6. ResolutionAnswer: this view disagrees with שמואל
