Chullin 86a

חולין פ״ו ע״א

Learn Chullin 86a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Chullin 86a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

How רבי told רבי חייא to kill a bird without needing כיסוי הדם, and the משנה about שחיטה by a חרש, שוטה, or קטן, with רבי מאיר's reason for being lenient

Overview

The amud begins by finishing the story of רבי חייא's flax. רבי told him to kill a bird for its blood in a way that would not need כיסוי הדם. The גמרא explains why רב דימי's version ('צא טרוף') and רבין's version ('צא נחור') each mention only one method: each teaches a different חידוש in a 'לא מיבעיא' style. Next the גמרא asks how moths could harm רבי חייא's flax at all, since flax was protected in his merit. It answers that a צדיק's merit can help the world but not himself, like רבי חנינא בן דוסא. Then a new משנה rules on שחיטה done by a חרש, שוטה, or קטן, for כיסוי הדם and for אותו ואת בנו. The גמרא concludes that the חכמים argue with רבי מאיר on both laws. It then asks why רבי מאיר is lenient: רבי אמי says it is because most of their actions are done wrong. The גמרא challenges why a majority is needed, since רבי מאיר worries even about a minority joined to a חזקה, and it begins proving this from the משנה of a child found next to dough. That proof continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לָא מִיבַּעְיָא קָאָמַר:

He is speaking in the style of 'it is not necessary':

לָא מִיבַּעְיָא ״צֵא נְחוֹר״

It is not necessary to say 'Go out and tear loose'

דְּלָאו שְׁחִיטָה הִיא כְּלָל,

since that is not slaughter at all,

אֲבָל ״צֵא טְרוֹף״ אֵימָא

but with 'Go out and make it a טרפה', I might say

The steps (23)

  2. QuestionWhy does רבין's version not say 'Go and make it a טרפה'?

  4. ResolutionWhy רבין's version says 'tear loose': tearing is not a bird's שחיטה

  20. ResponseWhy they are presumed to fail: most of their actions are done wrong
