Chullin 34b

חולין ל״ד ע״ב

Learn Chullin 34b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Chullin 34b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Eating food with third-degree impurity (שלישי): when it makes a person's body impure for קודש or unfit for תרומה

Overview

The amud opens with the last words of an earlier ruling: being careful at the level of תרומה still counts as impure when it comes to קודש. רבי זירא then reports, through a chain of sages, a ruling of רבי ינאי. If someone eats a שלישי of חולין that were handled with the purity of קודש, his body becomes a שני for קודש. רבי זירא challenges this from a משנה that mentions only the purity of תרומה. רבי אסי answers that the משנה uses a 'לא מיבעיא' style, and the גמרא finally explains that רבי אסי and רבה בר בר חנה disagree about רבי יוחנן's view. Next, עולא rules that eating a שלישי of חולין handled with the purity of תרומה disqualifies a person from eating תרומה. The גמרא shows why עולא's rule was needed even though the משנה seems to hint at it. Finally, רב המנונא challenges עולא from a משנה that allows a שלישי to be eaten in a stew mixed with תרומה. עולא begins to answer by setting the stew case aside, and his reason continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

טוּמְאָה הִיא אֵצֶל הֶקְדֵּשׁ.

is impurity with regard to sacred food.

אָמַר רַבִּי זֵירָא אָמַר רַבִּי אַסִּי

Rabbi Zeira said that Rabbi Asi said

אָמַר רַבִּי יוֹחָנָן אָמַר רַבִּי יַנַּאי:

that Rabbi Yochanan said that Rabbi Yannai said:

הָאוֹכֵל שְׁלִישִׁי שֶׁל חוּלִּין

One who eats a third-degree item of non-sacred food

The steps (11)

  1. ConclusionEnd of the previous ruling: the purity level of תרומה counts as impure for קודש
  2. StatementEating a third-degree item of חולין handled with קודש purity makes the body a second-degree for קודש
  3. ChallengeA teaching mentions only the purity of תרומה, implying not the purity of קודש
  4. ResolutionThe teaching uses a 'no need to say' style
  5. ChallengeA quote from רבי יהושע seems to limit the rule to תרומה
  6. ResolutionTwo אמוראים disagree about רבי יוחנן's view
  7. Statementעולא: Eating a שלישי disqualifies a person from תרומה
  8. ChallengeWhy did עולא need to say this? The משנה already teaches it
  9. ResolutionWithout עולא, we might have read the משנה differently
  10. ChallengeThe stew with תרומה spices challenges עולא
  11. Resolutionעולא sets the stew case aside
