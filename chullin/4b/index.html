Chullin 4b

חולין ד׳ ע״ב

Open Chullin 4b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Trusting a sinner's food and שחיטה: the חמץ proof, the ברייתא of הכל שוחטין, and רב ענן's proof from יהושפט at אחאב's feast.

Overview

The גמרא asks how far we can trust a sinner about food. It first uses a ברייתא about חמץ held by sinners after פסח, which is permitted 'because they exchange' it. From this it proves that even a sinner will not leave permitted food to eat forbidden food. It then tries to support רבא from a ברייתא that says everyone may slaughter, even a ערל and a ישראל משומד. The proof is pushed off: the משומד there is someone who worships idols, following רב ענן in the name of שמואל, who allows eating from such a person's שחיטה. רב ענן's source is that the righteous king יהושפט ate at the feast of the idol-worshipping king אחאב. The גמרא tests this proof step by step. It asks whether יהושפט really ate, and whether he only drank. It then asks who did the slaughtering: עובדיה, the seven thousand, or אחאב's servants. The amud ends with one more possibility, that each king's men slaughtered for their own side, and the answer comes on the next amud.
