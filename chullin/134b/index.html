Chullin 134b

חולין קל״ד ע״ב

Open Chullin 134b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Gifts to the poor and to the כהן: when the owner must deliver them, and the exact borders of the foreleg, thigh and jaw

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a list of four doubtful cases where a גר is exempt, because each one is only about money owed to a כהן. רבין then gives his own version of ריש לקיש's contradiction about standing grain. Next, רב ששת rules that when no poor people come, the owner may keep the לקט, since it is for the poor and not for birds and bats. The גמרא challenges this from תרומה and מתנות כהונה, which the owner must make sure reach a כהן. It concludes that those gifts are different because the Torah uses the word 'giving' about them. The extra word תעזב is then explained as teaching about a vineyard made הפקר and harvested. A story about רבי אמי taking a sack of coins gets two answers: he took it for the poor, or an important person is different. Finally a new משנה defines the foreleg, thigh and jaw. The גמרא explains why the foreleg is the right one, what the jaw and maw include, and how the jaw's limit fits a ברייתא, and the chapter הזרוע והלחיים ends.

Opening lines

רֵאשִׁית הַגֵּז וְהַמַּתָּנוֹת,

The first shearing and the gifts,

וּפִדְיוֹן הַבֵּן,

and the redemption of the firstborn son,

וּפִדְיוֹן פֶּטֶר חֲמוֹר – לִפְטוּר.

and the redemption of a firstborn donkey: to exempt.

כִּי אֲתָא רָבִין, אָמַר:
