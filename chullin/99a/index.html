Topic

The נזיר's foreleg as a חידוש for nullifying the flavor of קדשים, and אביי's challenge to the one-hundred rule from the משנה of split beans and lentils

Overview

The amud begins with the cooked foreleg (זרוע בשלה) of the נזיר's ram, which is קדשים, yet its flavor does not forbid the rest of the ram. The גמרא asks whether we can learn from this that the flavor of קדשים is always nullified. It answers no: the פסוק about חטאת teaches that food absorbing its flavor takes on its status. The נזיר case is a חידוש, and we do not learn general rules from a חידוש. We still learn the 100 or 60 measure from it, because that is only a stringency: by Torah law a simple majority would be enough. רבינא gives another reading of the case: it comes to permit the place of the cut (מקום חתך). Then רב דימי repeats the rule that forbidden food is nullified only in one hundred. אביי challenges him from a משנה about תרומה split beans cooked with lentils, where taste alone decides, and argues that the amount that works there must be sixty, not one hundred.

Opening lines

וְלִיגְמַר מִינֵּיהּ?

And let us learn from it?

גַּלִּי רַחֲמָנָא גַּבֵּי חַטָּאת:

The Merciful One revealed regarding a sin offering:

״כֹּל אֲשֶׁר יִגַּע בִּבְשָׂרָהּ יִקְדָּשׁ״,

"Whatever shall touch its flesh shall become holy,"

לִהְיוֹת כָּמוֹהָ –

to be like it –
