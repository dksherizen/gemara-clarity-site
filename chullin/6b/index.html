Chullin 6b

חולין ו׳ ע״ב

Open Chullin 6b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Do we worry that an עם הארץ will secretly swap food? Two challenges, from the innkeeper and from the women grinding grain, are each answered by showing a special motive in that case.

Overview

Just before this, the גמרא explained that a mother-in-law is suspected of swapping food only because of a special motive. Now it asks whether we ever worry about swapping in general. It brings a משנה about an innkeeper suspected of swapping, and answers that she has a special motive: she wants the Torah student to eat her hot food. Next it brings a ברייתא about the wife of a חבר grinding grain with the wife of an עם הארץ, where רבי שמעון בן אלעזר says the עם הארץ's wife even hands out grain. The גמרא reasons that someone who steals would surely swap. רב יוסף answers that she too feels justified: 'the ox eats from its threshing.' The גמרא then turns to a story about רבי מאיר eating a vegetable leaf in בית שאן, which led רבי to permit all of בית שאן. When רבי's family objects that he is overturning their ancestors' practice, רבי begins his answer with the פסוק about חזקיהו breaking the copper snake. He asks why the righteous kings אסא and יהושפט had not already destroyed it, and his answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וּלְעָלְמָא לָא חָיְישִׁינַן?

And in general, are we not concerned?

וְהָתְנַן:

But didn't we learn in a mishna:

הַנּוֹתֵן לַפּוּנְדָּקִית שֶׁלּוֹ –

One who gives to his innkeeper

מְעַשֵּׂר אֵת שֶׁהוּא נוֹתֵן לָהּ

tithes what he gives her
