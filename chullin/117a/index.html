Topic

מעילה on חלב of קדשים but not on blood, and the rule that nothing has מעילה after its מצוה is done

Overview

The amud opens at the end of a משנה comparing חלב (forbidden fat) and דם (blood). חלב of a קרבן carries מעילה, פיגול, נותר and טמא, but blood applies to more kinds of creatures. The גמרא asks how we know the חלב of a שלמים carries מעילה. רבי ינאי learns it from a reverse היקש to the פר כהן משיח. When רבי חנינא asks why רבי's source "כל חלב לה׳" is not enough, אביי shows that each פסוק adds something: one brings in the kidneys and יותרת, the other brings in the sheep's אליה. This raises the question of why the אליה is not forbidden to eat, and the גמרא settles on רב זביד's answer: forbidden חלב is only fat found in ox, sheep and goat alike. Next the גמרא gives three sources that blood has no מעילה. רבי יוחנן's source rests on the rule that nothing has מעילה once its מצוה is done. The גמרא defends that rule against the case of תרומת הדשן by calling it one of "two verses that come as one". When that answer is questioned according to the view that such pairs do teach, the reply begins at the end of the amud and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מוֹעֲלִין בּוֹ,

One is liable for misuse of consecrated property with it,

וְחַיָּיבִין עָלָיו מִשּׁוּם פִּיגּוּל

and one is liable for it because of piggul

וְנוֹתָר וְטָמֵא,

and notar and eating while impure,

מַה שֶּׁאֵין כֵּן בַּדָּם.

which is not so with blood.
