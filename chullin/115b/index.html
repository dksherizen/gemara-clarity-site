Chullin 115b

חולין קט״ו ע״ב

Learn Chullin 115b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Chullin 115b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The sources for the eating and benefit prohibitions of בשר בחלב, and the rules for refuting a קל וחומר and a במה הצד.

Overview

This amud asks where we learn that בשר בחלב is forbidden to eat and forbidden for הנאה. רבי יוחנן challenges ריש לקיש: רבי already learned the eating ban from the extra ״לא תאכלנו״. ריש לקיש answers that his own source adds the ban on benefit, and the גמרא shows that רבי learns benefit from a גזירה שוה of קדוש and קדש. Then come more sources: דבי רבי אליעזר learns it from selling a נבלה, and דבי רבי ישמעאל learns it from the three times the תורה writes ״לא תבשל גדי״. איסי בן יהודה learns eating from טרפה and benefit from a קל וחומר from ערלה, which the גמרא defends using חמץ and כלאי הכרם. The גמרא then asks why איסי needs a גזירה שוה at all, and why it needed כלאי הכרם instead of a במה הצד. This leads רב אשי and רב מרדכי into a back-and-forth about how each kind of proof may be refuted. The amud ends as רב מרדכי begins a new version of ריש לקיש's teaching, which continues on the next amud.

Do you say the verse is speaking about meat in milk,

The steps (25)

  1. ChallengeWhy invent a new source when רבי already taught one?
  2. ProofHow רבי knows the פסוק is about meat and milk
  3. Resolutionרבי's source covers eating, but not benefit
  4. QuestionWhere does רבי learn the benefit prohibition?
  5. Responseרבי learns it from the words קדוש and קדש
  6. StatementThe school of רבי אליעזר: you may sell a נבלה, but not after cooking it in milk
  7. StatementThe school of רבי ישמעאל: three פסוקים for three prohibitions
  8. Statementאיסי בן יהודה: eating בשר בחלב is forbidden, learned from טרפה
  9. Questionאיסי בן יהודה asks: where do we learn the benefit prohibition?
  10. ResponseA קל וחומר from ערלה proves benefit is forbidden
  11. Rejectionערלה is special: it was never permitted
  12. Proofחמץ בפסח shows that an item once permitted can still be forbidden for benefit
  13. Rejectionחמץ is special: it carries כרת
  14. Proofכלאי הכרם has no כרת but is still forbidden for benefit
  15. ChallengeWhy not learn the eating prohibition from ערלה too?
  16. ResolutionA logical argument for eating could be broken, so a verse-based link is needed
  17. ChallengeWhy not combine ערלה and חמץ instead of using כלאי הכרם?
  18. Resolutionרב אשי: נבלה would break the combined proof
  19. Rejectionרב מרדכי: a במה הצד cannot be broken by an outside case
  20. ChallengeThen use a בְּמָה הַצַּד after all
  21. ResolutionBoth sources grow from the ground
  22. Challengeכלאי הכרם grows from the ground too
  23. ResolutionDifferent rules for refuting different proofs
  24. ChallengeRefute all three sources together
  25. Resolutionרב מרדכי introduces a different version of ריש לקיש's teaching
