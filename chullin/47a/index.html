Topic

Checking the ריאה for טרפות: cysts, the normal five lobes, an extra lobe, and the 'little rose lobe' (עינוניתא דוורדא)

Overview

רבא gives two rules about the lung. First, two cysts next to each other make the animal a טרפה. A single cyst that only looks like two is tested by piercing it with a thorn. Second, a healthy lung has five lobes, three on the right and two on the left. רבא says a missing, extra, or switched lobe makes the animal a טרפה. In a story, מרימר rules that the הלכה does not follow רבא about an extra lobe. The גמרא limits this to an extra lobe that sits in line with the others; one in between the sides is a טרפה. Then רב הונא מר בר אויא tells רב אשי that field animals commonly have an in-between 'little rose lobe', which is כשרה, and the גמרא begins to limit that to a lobe on the inside face of the lung.

Opening lines

וְאָמַר רָבָא:

And Rava said:

הָנֵי תַּרְתֵּי בּוּעֵי דִּסְמִיכָן לַהֲדָדֵי,

These two cysts that are next to each other,

לֵית לְהוּ בְּדִיקוּתָא.

have no checking.

חֲדָא וּמִתְחַזְיָא כְּתַרְתֵּי –

One that looks like two –

The steps (8)

  1. StatementTwo cysts side by side, and one cyst that looks like two
  2. StatementThe normal lung has five lobes
  3. StatementA lung with an extra lobe is brought to מרימר

  5. StatementThe leniency applies only when the extra lobe is in line with the others
  6. StatementAn in-between lobe comes before רב אשי
