Chullin 74a

חולין ע״ד ע״א

Learn Chullin 74a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Chullin 74a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The hanging limb and the rule that מיתה makes it count as fallen off but שחיטה does not; how שחיטה works for a fetus (בן שמונה, בן תשעה, בן פקועה)

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier point: a hanging limb of a properly שחוט animal is forbidden only by a rabbinic מצוה to keep away. רב יוסף then quotes רב that eating a hanging limb brings מלקות, while another Sage quotes רב the opposite way. רב יוסף explains that both quotes are right: מיתה makes the limb count as fallen off (ניפול), so it is אבר מן החי, but שחיטה does not. רבא finds the source for this in the extra word בְּמֹתָם, which is written twice in the פסוקים about שרצים. רב חסדא and רבה then disagree about whether the מחלוקת in the משנה about a fetus's limb covers a dead fetus too. Next, the גמרא settles a clash between the משנה and a ברייתא about a live בן שמונה, and shows how the ברייתא learns that a slaughtered טרפה is טהור. רב הושעיא asks whether slaughtering a live nine-month fetus inside its mother works, and רבא pushes away the proof brought for it. Finally, a new משנה rules on a fetus found inside a slaughtered animal: רבי מאיר says a live nine-month fetus needs its own שחיטה, while the חכמים say its mother's שחיטה is enough.

Opening lines

אֵין בָּהֶן אֶלָּא

There is nothing with them except

מִצְוַת פְּרוֹשׁ בִּלְבָד.

a מצוה to separate oneself, only.

Rav Yosef sat before Rav Huna,

וְיָתֵיב וְקָאָמַר:

and he sat and was saying:
