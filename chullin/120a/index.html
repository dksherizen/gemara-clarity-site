Topic

Items that join with meat for טומאת אוכלין, and when drinking counts as eating

Overview

The גמרא works through our משנה's list of things that join with meat to reach the size needed for טומאת אוכלין. ריש לקיש says only true protectors like bone count, so hair does not. רבי יוחנן says hair is also a שומר because it goes through the hide to the meat. When רב אחא asks how תפילין parchment could then be valid, the גמרא answers that a hole the ink covers is not a hole. רבי יוחנן's proof from a hair across from meat is answered by saying the hair is a handle (יד), as a hair among many hairs. The גמרא then defines רוטב as congealed fat and קיפה as spices, since those are not food on their own. A side discussion shows that drinking melted חלב, dissolved חמץ, or a liquefied bird נבלה still counts, because the word נפש includes one who drinks. The גמרא proves that all three mentions of נפש are needed. Last, it explains why a separate word is needed to forbid the liquids of שרצים: without it, the דיו rule would require a כזית.
