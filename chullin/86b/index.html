Chullin 86b

חולין פ״ו ע״ב

Learn Chullin 86b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Chullin 86b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Why רבי מאיר needs a רוב, which of רבי's rulings is final, and the rules of covering the blood of many animals (כיסוי הדם)

Overview

The amud begins by finishing a challenge to רבי אמי. If רבי מאיר joins a מיעוט to a חזקה in the dough case, why does slaughter by a חרש, שוטה וקטן need a רוב? The answer is that being lenient about a ספק טומאה does not allow being lenient about a ספק איסור. Next, the גמרא asks which of רבי's two rulings, one like רבי מאיר and one like the חכמים, came later. A story about רבי אמי, רבי זירא and רבי אבא shows the question was asked in the בית מדרש but not answered. רבי אלעזר's message to בבל, which mentions only the ruling like רבי מאיר, proves that ruling was the later one. A new משנה teaches that one covering of blood is enough for many חיות and birds slaughtered in one place, while רבי יהודה says to cover the חיה's blood before slaughtering the bird. The גמרא explains how each side reads the words "או" and "דמו" in the פסוק. Finally, רבי חנינא says רבי יהודה agrees that only one ברכה is said, and the גמרא challenges this from a story about רב's students and the cup of wine.

Opening lines

סְמוֹךְ מִיעוּטָא לַחֲזָקָה

Join the minority to the presumption

וְאִיתְּרַע לֵיהּ רוּבָּא.

and the majority is weakened.

אִם אָמְרוּ סְפֵק טוּמְאָה לְטַהֵר,

If they said that in a case of doubtful impurity we rule it pure,

יֹאמְרוּ סְפֵק אִיסּוּר לְהַתִּיר?

will they say that in a case of doubtful prohibition we rule it permitted?
