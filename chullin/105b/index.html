Chullin 105b

חולין ק״ה ע״ב

Learn Chullin 105b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Chullin 105b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Hand-washing at a meal and מים אחרונים, then אביי's list of customs whose true reasons are health, poverty, and protection from מזיקין

Overview

This amud finishes רבי ינאי's rule about hot water for washing hands. In a second version his rule applies to the final washing (מים אחרונים), and from it the גמרא infers that even scalding water is allowed for the first washing. רב נחמן teaches that the middle washing is optional, except between a cooked dish and cheese, where it is required. רב יהודה בריה דרבי חייא explains that מים אחרונים are a חובה because of dangerous מלח סדומית, which can blind the eyes. Then comes a long series of אביי's teachings: 'At first I thought the reason for this custom was simple, but my teacher told me the real reason.' These cover not washing onto the ground, not taking things off the table while someone drinks, gathering crumbs, avoiding foam, eating from a tied bundle, vegetables that fell from the table, sitting under a gutter, and pouring a little water out of a pitcher. Stories support these teachings: the angel of poverty and the careful man, a witch on a boat, the demon under the gutter who had to pay for a wine barrel, and a demon-servant in רב פפא's house. That last story continues on the next amud.

  6. StatementHow rare the dangerous salt is

  9. StatementWhy final washing water is not poured on the ground
  10. StatementWhy nothing is taken off the table while someone drinks
  11. StatementThree limits on the table rule
  12. Statementמר בר רב אשי was strict even with spice tools
  13. StatementGathering crumbs protects against poverty
  14. StatementThe story of the man the angel of poverty could not catch
  15. StatementNot drinking the foam prevents illness
  16. StatementEvery simple way of getting rid of foam causes harm
  17. QuestionSo what should a person do with the foam?
  18. ResponseSink the foam into the drink
  19. StatementCures for illness from foam
  20. ConclusionPoverty follows the poor
  21. StatementVegetables from a tied bundle: witchcraft, not gluttony
  22. ProofThe witch who could not harm the sages
  23. StatementVegetables that fell from the table: bad breath, not disgust
  24. StatementNot sitting under a gutter: demons, not dirty water

  29. StatementThe demon asks for a payment date
  30. QuestionThe demon is late: why didn't you come on time?
  31. ResponseDemons may only take ownerless things
  32. StatementPouring water from a pitcher is because of bad waters
  33. ProofA demon-servant in רב פפא's house comes back late from the river
