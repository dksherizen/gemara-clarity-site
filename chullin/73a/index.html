Chullin 73a

חולין ע״ג ע״א

Open Chullin 73a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Treating joined parts as separate, and how far the mother's שחיטה reaches: the fetus's limb in the debate between רבי מאיר and the חכמים, and a limb hanging from the animal

Overview

This amud opens with the end of רבינא's rule: something that is going to be cut off is treated as already cut. That is why a fetus's foreleg and the rest of the fetus count as two separate pieces touching. The גמרא first suggests this follows only רבי מאיר, who says a too-long handle needs to be immersed only up to its final length. It then answers that even the רבנן agree that joined pieces of food count as separate pieces touching. After explaining why the משנה says 'he cut it off,' the גמרא turns to the חכמים's view. They hold that a slaughtered טרפה makes מוקדשין impure, and that the mother's שחיטה saves the fetus's limb from נבלה impurity, just as שחיטה saves a טרפה. רבא shows that the ברייתא recording the debate with רבי מאיר is missing lines, and a second ברייתא confirms the full order of the argument. ריש לקיש and רבי יוחנן then disagree about whether the same מחלוקת covers a limb hanging from the animal itself. רבי יוסי ברבי חנינא explains רבי יוחנן's distinction: a fetus's limb can go back into the womb, but a hanging limb can never be reattached. The amud ends with a challenge to רבי יוחנן from רבי מאיר's words in the ברייתא, and the discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

כְּחָתוּךְ דָּמֵי.

is considered as though it were cut.

כְּמַאן? כְּרַבִּי מֵאִיר,

In accordance with whom? In accordance with Rabbi Meir,

דִּתְנַן: כָּל יְדוֹת הַכֵּלִים

as we learned: all handles of vessels

שֶׁהֵן אֲרוּכּוֹת וְעָתִיד לְקׇצְצָן –

that are long and he plans to cut them down –
