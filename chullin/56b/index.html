Chullin 56b

חולין נ״ו ע״ב

Learn Chullin 56b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Chullin 56b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Birds that fell into fire or were injured: color changes in the innards, the crop, and intestines that came out

Overview

This amud opens by finishing the previous discussion: a bird's brain membrane is checked with a wheat straw, and רב שיזבי rules that our geese are treated like water birds. Next comes the משנה's case of a bird that fell into fire. Any amount of greening of the innards makes it a טרפה, and a liver that turned green facing the intestines is a טרפה because it shows the intestines were singed. Two stories teach that the color rule is about change: green is bad only in the naturally red organs (gizzard, heart, liver), and naturally green organs that turned red are also a טרפה. Boiling can reveal whether a color change was only smoke or a real burn, but the גמרא rejects רב אשי's demand to always boil, since we do not presume a defect. A trampled or slammed bird must be checked. A new משנה then lists injuries that leave a bird kosher, and the גמרא discusses רבי's view on a removed crop (ending in תיקו) and the 'roof' of the crop, which is treated like the ושט. Finally, intestines that came out are kosher only if not put back in the wrong order, based on 'הוא עשך ויכוננך', and a story about a man whose intestines came out begins and continues on the next amud.
