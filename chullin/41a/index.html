Chullin 41a

חולין מ״א ע״א

Topic

Can a person forbid what is not his through עבודה זרה (גוי versus Jew versus משומד), and a new משנה on where one may not slaughter

Overview

Earlier on the daf, רב הונא ruled that someone who slaughters another person's animal for עבודה זרה makes it forbidden. רב נחמן, רב עמרם and רב יצחק disagreed, because אין אדם אוסר דבר שאינו שלו: a person cannot forbid something that is not his. The amud first explains that a person's own חטאת counts as his, because he gets atonement through it. It then answers two challenges from משניות by saying the person causing the harm was a partner. Next the גמרא shows that the same argument appears among the תנאים, in a ברייתא about a גוי pouring a Jew's wine as a libation. רב נחמן and his colleagues then give a new approach that fits even the stricter תנא: only a גוי forbids another's property, because a Jew who does this just wants to upset the owner. The two משניות are now explained as speaking about a ישראל משומד. רב אשי adds that a Jew who accepts a warning and goes ahead counts as the greatest משומד. Finally, a new משנה begins, listing places where one may not let the blood of a שחיטה flow, such as seas, rivers, vessels and a hole in the ground. Its reason for the marketplace rule continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֲבָל זֶבַח לָא.

But an animal offering, no.

וְאִי אֵין אָדָם אוֹסֵר

And if a person does not render forbidden

דָּבָר שֶׁאֵינוֹ שֶׁלּוֹ,

an item that is not his,

מַאי אִרְיָא חַטַּאת הָעוֹף?

why specifically a bird sin offering?
