Topic

Where on the windpipe a valid שחיטה may be done, and how cuts partly out of place (מוגרמת) are judged

Overview

The גמרא rules that a שחיטה from the שיפוי כובע (the slope of the thyroid cartilage) and below is valid. רב חנן בר רב קטינא challenges רב נחמן, who ruled this way, because the ruling seems to match neither רבנן nor רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה. רב נחמן answers that he relies on a received teaching, not on either side's reasoning. Next, רבי יהושע בן לוי sets out three levels of leniency for a cut that slipped too high (מוגרמת): רבנן, then רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה, then רבי חנינא בן אנטיגנוס. The הלכה follows רבי חנינא בן אנטיגנוס, as רב נחמן did. The גמרא then asks exactly which cut of thirds רבנן and רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה argue about: two-thirds properly and then one-third out of place, or the reverse. רב הונא and רב חסדא disagree, and the גמרא brings two versions of their debate, including a challenge to the proof from the משנה 'רובו של אחד כמוהו'. Last, the גמרא discusses three-part cuts, with רב הונא and רב יהודה reporting opposite rulings in the name of רב. רב חסדא tells רב הונא not to take back his ruling, and the reason comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְהִלְכְתָא מִשִּׁיפּוּי כּוֹבַע וּלְמַטָּה – כְּשֵׁרָה,

And the halacha is: from the incline of the thyroid cartilage and below, it is valid,

וְהַיְינוּ דְּשַׁיַּיר בְּחִיטֵּי.

and that is where he left over some of the arytenoid cartilage.

רַב נַחְמָן אַכְשַׁר

Rav Nachman declared valid

מִשִּׁיפּוּי כּוֹבַע וּלְמַטָּה,

a slaughter from the incline of the thyroid cartilage and below.
