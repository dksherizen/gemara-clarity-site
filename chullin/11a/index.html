Chullin 11a

חולין י״א ע״א

Learn Chullin 11a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Chullin 11a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Where do we learn to follow a majority we cannot see (רובא דליתא קמן)? A series of proofs from animals that are never checked for being טריפה

Overview

The amud opens with the last line of an earlier discussion: a row of men reports to the כהן that a נגע in a house has stayed the same. That is how רב אחא בר יעקב explains the ברייתא, so for him it is no proof that we rely on חזקה. Then a new topic begins: where do we learn to follow the majority (זיל בתר רובא)? The גמרא is surprised at first, since the פסוק ״אחרי רבים להטות״ says it outright. It explains that a majority we can see and count, like nine shops or the votes of a סנהדרין, is clearly covered. The real question is about a majority we cannot see, like assuming a קטן and קטנה will grow up normally. A series of sages then try to prove it from animals that the תורה says are used without being checked inside, so they might be טריפה. The proofs come from the head of an עולה, the bones of the קרבן פסח, and the אליה of a שלמים, and each one is rejected because the animal could be checked after all. More proofs follow from the עגלה ערופה and the פרה אדומה, each defended against the objection that a טריפה would not matter. The amud ends in the middle of a new proof from the two goats of יום כיפור, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

כְּגוֹן דְּקָיְימִי דָּרָא דְּגַבְרֵי

For example, where a row of men stands

וְאָמְרִי: כִּדְקָאֵי קָאֵי.

and they say: as it stood, it stands.

מְנַָא הָא מִילְּתָא

From where is this matter

דַאֲמוּר רַבָּנַן: זִיל בָּתַר רוּבָּא?

that the Rabbis said: go after the majority?

The steps (17)

  1. ResolutionA row of men passes word about the mark to the כהן
  2. QuestionWhere do we learn to follow the majority?
  3. ResponseSurprise: a פסוק says it outright
  4. ResolutionA majority we can see is not the question
  5. QuestionThe real question: a majority we cannot see
  6. ProofProof from the head of a burnt offering
  7. RejectionMaybe the head is split and checked
  8. ProofProof from the bones of the קרבן פסח
  9. RejectionThe brain of the פסח could be checked by burning through the skull
  10. ProofProof from the tail of a שלמים, which must be offered whole
  11. ResolutionThe tail is cut at a spot where a torn spinal cord matters
  12. RejectionThe tail could be split and checked while staying joined
  13. ProofProof from the heifer whose neck is broken
  14. ResolutionA tereifa would matter for this heifer
  15. ProofProof from the red heifer
  16. ResolutionThe red heifer is called a sin offering
  17. ProofA proof from the two goats of יום כיפור
