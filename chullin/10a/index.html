Topic

Doubt and danger: uncovered liquids, and a knife found notched after שחיטה (רב הונא vs. רב חסדא)

Overview

This amud opens at the end of a discussion about a ברייתא on vessels whose covering changed while the owner was away. Creeping creatures uncover vessels but do not cover them, so the two cases are treated differently. If the vessel was found just as it was left, there is no טומאה at all. Exposed water, though, is forbidden even in a case of doubt, and the גמרא concludes חמירא סכנתא מאיסורא: danger is treated more strictly than a prohibition. It then quotes a משנה in תרומות: water, wine and milk become forbidden when left uncovered long enough for a snake to come out, drink, and go back to its hole. Next comes a מחלוקת between רב הונא and רב חסדא about a knife found notched after שחיטה and then used to break bones. רב חסדא rules the animal כשרה, because a ספק does not override a ודאי. רבא challenges this from a case of טבילה, and through a series of back-and-forth answers the גמרא concludes that the knife was flawed, not the animal. Finally, a ברייתא saying 'any doubt in שחיטה is invalid' is explained as covering doubts about pausing or pressing, not the notched knife.
