Topic

The fetus (עובר) and the mother's שחיטה: whether a foreleg that left the womb stays forbidden, as רב rules, and the גמרא's challenges to him from the משנה and a ברייתא.

Overview

The משנה teaches that the mother's שחיטה normally permits the fetus inside her. If the fetus only put out its foreleg and pulled it back, it is still permitted. If it put out its head, it counts as born and needs its own שחיטה. Pieces cut from the fetus inside the womb are permitted, but pieces cut from the mother's own organs stay forbidden. רב adds that the foreleg that came out is itself forbidden, learning from the פסוק of טרפה that meat which leaves its boundary becomes forbidden. The גמרא challenges רב from the משנה's words 'brought it back'. Along the way it shows that the head clause teaches nothing new: בכורות covers a person, and the placenta משנה covers an animal. The גמרא defends רב by saying 'brought it back' teaches a rule about the spot of the cut. It then brings a ברייתא with several cases about a foreleg that came out, and that challenge continues on the next amud.
