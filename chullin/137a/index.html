Topic

ראשית הגז: why it is learned from מעשר בהמה, why it applies only to sheep, רבי יוסי's view on shearing and harvesting, and how many sheep obligate it

Overview

The גמרא finishes explaining why רבי שמעון compares ראשית הגז to מעשר בהמה rather than בכור: an ordinary animal is best learned from an ordinary animal. It then looks for the source that ראשית הגז applies only to sheep. After a word-link of 'shearing' is challenged, the גמרא settles on רבי יהושע בן לוי's teaching that the wool must be fit for the service of the כהנים. The word-link is then used to teach that sheep with hard wool are exempt. Two ברייתות disagree about wool that comes off when sheep are washed, and the גמרא explains them as רבנן versus רבי יוסי, who requires real shearing or harvesting. It then proves that רבי יוסי agrees when another method is the normal way, using לקט and onion beds. Finally, the גמרא discusses how many sheep obligate ראשית הגז: two, five, or four. רבי strongly backs רבי יוסי's four, and the גמרא challenges him with the rule that a third, in-between opinion does not decide.

Opening lines

בִּקְדוּשָּׁה, וּמְכִירָה –

In consecration, and sale –

וְהָנָךְ נְפִישִׁין!

Ordinary from ordinary is preferable to him.

The first shearing does not apply
