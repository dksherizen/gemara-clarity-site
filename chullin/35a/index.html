Chullin 35a

חולין ל״ה ע״א

Learn Chullin 35a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Chullin 35a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Whether eating food with שלישי טומאה makes a person impure for תרומה and קודש: the rulings of עולא, רבי יונתן and רב יצחק בר שמואל בר מרתא, רמי בר חמא's challenge, and the proof from מדרס.

Overview

This amud opens with the last words of an earlier explanation: a mixture does not need the purity of תרומה, because the eater never takes in a כזית of the תרומה within כדי אכילת פרס. Then רבי יונתן quotes רבי: someone who eats actual תרומה that is a שלישי may not eat תרומה, but may still touch it. The גמרא explains why this ruling and the earlier ruling of עולא are both needed. Next, רב יצחק בר שמואל בר מרתא rules that eating a שלישי of חולין prepared with the purity of קודש does not stop a person from eating קודש, because only real קודש creates a רביעי. רמי בר חמא challenges him from a משנה. That משנה says eating a שלישי makes a person a שני for קודש, even though its case must be about חולין prepared like תרומה. רב יצחק answers that the purity of תרומה already counts as impurity when it comes to קודש. The גמרא supports this from the משנה about clothing, where each group's clothing is מדרס for the group above it. רבא starts to reject that proof by saying מדרס is a special case, and his explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דְּלֵיכָּא כְּזַיִת

As there is not an olive-bulk

בִּכְדֵי אֲכִילַת פְּרָס.

within the time of eating a half-loaf.

אָמַר רַבִּי יוֹנָתָן אָמַר רַבִּי:

Rabbi Yonatan said that Rabbi said:

הָאוֹכֵל שְׁלִישִׁי שֶׁל תְּרוּמָה עַצְמָהּ –

One who eats a third-degree item of actual teruma –

The steps (8)

  1. ConclusionClosing reason: not a כזית within the time of כדי אכילת פרס
  2. StatementEating a שלישי of real תרומה: no eating תרומה, but touching is allowed
  3. ResolutionWhy we need both עולא and רבי יונתן
  4. StatementEating a שלישי of חולין prepared with קודש purity: may still eat קודש
  5. Challengeרמי בר חמא challenges from a case of חולין prepared like תרומה
  6. ResolutionThe purity of תרומה counts as impurity for קודש
  7. ProofA source from the clothing of different groups
  8. Rejectionרבא says מדרס is a special case
