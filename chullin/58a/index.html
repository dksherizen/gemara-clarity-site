Topic

Eggs and offspring of a טרפה: two versions of אמימר's ruling (זה וזה גורם, or whether a טרפה can reproduce), and how long a doubtful טרפה stays forbidden

Overview

אמימר rules that eggs already inside a hen when she became a טרפה are forbidden. Eggs formed later come from a forbidden hen and a kosher rooster, so they are permitted under זה וזה גורם מותר. The גמרא defends this ruling from a משנה that forbids a טרפה's egg, and from the dispute between רבי אליעזר and רבי יהושע about bringing a טרפה's offspring on the מזבח. In this version they argue about זה וזה גורם. They argue about the מזבח because showing the strength of a lenient view is preferred. This is רב אחא's version, which follows רב אחא בר יעקב that a טרפה can produce offspring. רבינא holds that a טרפה cannot lay eggs or become pregnant. So he teaches אמימר's ruling differently: the first eggs of a doubtful טרפה are permitted only if the bird lays again. In his version the משנה is read as 'finished,' and the offspring dispute is about whether a fetus is part of its mother. The גמרא then rules that a doubtful טרפה is cleared once a male lives twelve months or a female gives birth. It also brings רב הונא's rule that a boneless creature cannot live twelve months, which רב פפא starts to apply to wormy melons, a point that continues on the next amud.
