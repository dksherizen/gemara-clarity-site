Topic

The טריפות of a bird, and how to check whether a weasel's bite on the head pierced the membrane of the brain

Overview

This amud opens with the last line of a ברייתא: the skin of the בית הבושת counts like meat for פיגול and כרת. Then a new משנה lists the injuries that make a bird a טריפה, such as a pierced gullet, a cut windpipe, a weasel's blow to the head, and singed innards that turned green. A crushed bird that lives a full 24 hours is kosher. The גמרא begins with the head injury. רב, שמואל and לוי teach that you press the brain from inside, and if it bulges out through the skull the bird is a טריפה. זעירי first says a weasel bite cannot be checked at all because its teeth are thin and crooked, but later he takes this back. He reports that רבי שמעון בן לקיש allows checking by hand but not with a nail, while רבי יוחנן allows even a nail. The same argument is traced to רבי יהודה and רבי נחמיה. Three times a teaching of לוי, that a broken skull makes a bird טריפה even when the membrane is whole, is answered the same way: it refers only to a water bird, whose membrane is soft. The amud ends with practical ways different sages checked the membrane, and the list continues on the next amud.
