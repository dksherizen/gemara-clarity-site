Topic

Limits on קל וחומר: the פרה אדומה and עגלה ערופה, and what disqualifies כהנים and לוים

Overview

The גמרא finishes explaining why a פרה אדומה cannot be done with עריפה. At first it says the word 'חוקה' blocks any קל וחומר. It then challenges this from יום הכפורים, which is also called a חוקה, yet still needed a special פסוק to block a קל וחומר. So the גמרא gives a new answer: the word 'הערופה' limits עריפה to the עגלה alone. Another פסוק, 'וערפו', shows the עגלה cannot be done with שחיטה. A new משנה then says some things disqualify כהנים but not לוים, and the reverse. A ברייתא explains that מומין disqualify כהנים, while age disqualifies לוים, and it learns both rules from 'זאת אשר ללוים'. It adds that the age limit applied only while the משכן was carried, and that a לוי trained from 25 and served from 30. From those five years of training it learns a lesson for students, and רבי יוסי says the test period is three years. The amud ends with רבי יוסי asking for the פסוק showing that in שילה and the בית המקדש only a failing voice disqualifies a לוי; the answer comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

אָמַר קְרָא ״וְשָׁחַט״ וְחוּקָּה:

The verse says 'and he shall slaughter,' and it says 'statute':

בִּשְׁחִיטָה אִין, בַּעֲרִיפָה לָא.

with slaughter, yes; with breaking the neck, no.

וְכֹל הֵיכָא דִּכְתִיב בֵּיהּ חוּקָּה,

And anywhere that 'statute' is written about it,

לָא דָּרְשִׁינַן קַל וָחוֹמֶר?

do we not derive a קל וחומר?
