Topic

How a handle (יד) and a protective covering (שומר) count for טומאה, starting from the hide of a נבלה, and the sources for each rule.

Overview

This amud is about how parts attached to food or to a נבלה count for טומאה. It opens by finishing a ברייתא about the hide of a נבלה. רבא explains that the hide does not join with the meat to make a כזית, but when a full כזית of meat is on it, the hide acts as a handle (יד), so touching it makes a person טמא. The גמרא then quotes a משנה with three levels. A יד passes on טומאה but does not count toward the measure. A שומר (protective covering) passes on טומאה and also counts toward the measure. Something that is neither has no part in טומאה. The word ״לכם״ in the פסוקים teaches that handles count, and a קל וחומר teaches that a שומר does too, which leaves the שומר פסוק free to teach joining. Through a long back-and-forth about which handle פסוק is extra, including the rule of דיו, the גמרא settles on three sources. A יד brings טומאה in, a יד carries טומאה out, and a שומר joins with the food.
