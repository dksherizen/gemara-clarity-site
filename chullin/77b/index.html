Topic

Cooked donkey hide and טומאה; the placenta and the worry about another fetus; firstborn and קדשים placentas; דרכי האמורי

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a challenge to רבי יצחק נפחא's question about cooked donkey hide. Both טומאת אוכלין and טומאת נבלות seem to be already settled by ברייתות, so the question looks unnecessary. The גמרא answers that the question was about טומאת אוכלין, and donkey hide is different because it is disgusting. Next, on a שליא that partly came out, רבי אלעזר and רבי יוחנן disagree about when we worry that it belonged to another fetus. After a challenge from רבי ירמיה, the מחלוקת is reworded: רבי אלעזר is the strict one and requires the placenta to be attached to the fetus found inside. A ברייתא about a woman who miscarries supports him. The גמרא then explains why a first-born placenta may be thrown to the dogs: females plus the נדמה make possible בכור males a minority. With קדשים, however, most young are holy, so the placenta must be buried. Finally, אביי and רבא teach that a practice with a real healing purpose is not דרכי האמורי. The גמרא begins to challenge this with a ברייתא about painting a tree red, and the challenge continues on the next amud.
