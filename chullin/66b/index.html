Chullin 66b

חולין ס״ו ע״ב

Learn Chullin 66b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Chullin 66b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Why the תורה lists fins as well as scales, and which finless water creatures are permitted

Overview

This amud first finishes a list of permitted fish. It then quotes a משנה: every fish with קשקשת (scales) also has סנפיר (fins), but not the other way around. The גמרא asks why the תורה mentions fins at all if scales are enough. A פסוק about scale armor already defines קשקשת, so רבי אבהו answers that the extra word is there to make the תורה great and glorious. A ברייתא then explains why the תורה says both 'eat' and 'do not eat': a person who eats a non-kosher fish breaks both an עשה and a לא תעשה. The ברייתא also learns that tiny finless water creatures are permitted. They are allowed in vessels, and the words 'of all that are in the waters' allow them even in pits, ditches and caves. The גמרא then works through the פסוק using the rules of כלל ופרט and כלל ופרט וכלל. The amud ends with רבינא starting a rule from ארץ ישראל about two general statements next to each other, and that rule finishes on the next amud.
