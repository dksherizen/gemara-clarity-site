Topic

The גיד הנשה on the מזבח, exaggerated language in the words of the חכמים, and רבי יהודה's view that only one thigh is forbidden

Overview

The גמרא finishes the debate between רבי and the רבנן on whether the גיד הנשה of a קרבן goes on the מזבח. רבי says an attached גיד הנשה is burned, just like חלב and דם. The רבנן say only what a Jew may eat is offered, and חלב and דם are special because the Torah commands offering them. רב הונא rules that the גיד הנשה of an עולה is removed and put on the תפוח, the ash pile. He defends this against רב חסדא and a ברייתא, and another ברייתא supports him. Mentioning the תפוח leads to a side discussion about exaggerated language in the Torah, the נביאים and the חכמים, including שמואל's three examples: the תפוח, the golden גפן and the פרוכת. The גמרא then says our משנה, which forbids the גיד in both thighs, does not follow רבי יהודה, who forbids only one thigh. It asks whether רבי יהודה is sure it is the right thigh or only leaning that way. A proof from a משנה about the קרבן פסח is offered, but רב איקא בר חנינא rejects it by explaining the case as one where the two גידים got mixed up.

וְרַבָּנַן, ״מִמַּשְׁקֵה יִשְׂרָאֵל״ –

And the Rabbis say: 'From the drink of Israel'
