Topic

Who must give the מתנות כהונה, how they are enforced, divided and eaten, and which כהנים may receive them.

Overview

The amud opens with רב אושעיא finishing an earlier discussion about a בכור that a כהן already received and later sold to a ישראל after it got a blemish. The משנה says that one who slaughters for a כהן or a גוי is exempt from the gifts, and רבא learns from its wording and from a פסוק that the duty falls on the butcher, even a כהן butcher. In a story, רבי טבלא advises a poor כהן to partner with Israelite butchers. רב נחמן still obligates them, because a כהן butcher is exempt only for his first weeks, and not at all once the shop is set up. רב חסדא bans a כהן who withholds the gifts, and the גמרא explains that the twenty-two-year ban on the butchers of הוצל teaches that we may fine them without warning. Next come rules on splitting the gifts between כהנים and on רבי יוחנן's view that the meat is like טבל, which the הלכה rejects. The amud ends with רב חסדא's rules that the gifts are eaten roasted with mustard, and that a כהן must be expert in the twenty-four gifts. That last rule is rejected by a ברייתא about a כהן who does not accept the עבודה, and the ברייתא's list of services continues on the next amud.
