Chullin 54a

חולין נ״ד ע״א

Learn Chullin 54a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Chullin 54a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Clawing (דרוסה) and how to check for it, רב's ruling on a dislocated windpipe, why we do not add to the טריפות, and the new משנה of כשרות

Overview

The amud opens by settling a question: a דרוסה (predator's clawing) on either the וושט or the קנה makes the animal a טריפה at any size, because the venom keeps burning. Two stories follow. In each, scholars say a clawed animal only needs checking near the intestines, and someone swears that רב taught the check runs from the skull to the thigh. When ריש לקיש says he does not know who רב is, רבי יוחנן reminds him how great רב was. ריש לקיש then praises רב and quotes his ruling that a windpipe found loose after שחיטה is כשרה. רבי יוחנן says to test it by comparing cuts, and רב נחמן limits רב's rule to a case where the שוחט did not hold the סימנים. The גמרא explains that the משנה's זה הכלל adds seven more טריפות. Two hunter stories show that we do not add new טריפות, since medicine could heal those wounds. A new משנה then lists injuries that leave an animal כשרה. The גמרא opens with רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש arguing over which list is complete, and starts testing their views on רב מתנא's case of a dislocated hip, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וֶושֶׁט נְקוּבָתוֹ בְּמַשֶּׁהוּ,

The gullet: its perforation is with any amount,

דְּרוּסָתוֹ בְּמַשֶּׁהוּ.

its clawing is with any amount.

קָנֶה נְקוּבָתוֹ בִּכְאִיסָּר,

The windpipe: its perforation is at the size of an issar,

דְּרוּסָתוֹ בְּכַמָּה?

its clawing, at how much?
