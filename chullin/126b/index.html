Topic

Partial corpse-טומאות, the 'can it be touched' rule for sealed bones and eggs, and which mice count as a טמא שרץ

Overview

The amud opens with three things from a dead body that are טמא: a full ladle of רקב, a bone the size of a barley grain, and the גולל and דופק. Each of these spreads טומאה in only two of the three usual ways (מגע, משא, אהל), and each one is missing a different way. Next the גמרא turns to the thigh bone of a נבלה or שרץ. A ברייתא teaches that a sealed bone does not make you טמא by touch, but a pierced one does, because the rule is that what can be touched is טמא. רבי זירא and רב פפא challenge this rule with an animal in its skin and a kidney in its fat. Both are answered: neither is really sealed. רב אושעיא then rules that a bone someone only planned to pierce already counts as open. A new משנה teaches about a שרץ egg and a mouse that is half flesh and half earth. The גמרא brings פסוקים for these rules, gives two versions of רבי יהושע בן לוי's condition on the mouse, and starts a ברייתא showing that a sea mouse is not טמא. That ברייתא continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שָׁלֹשׁ טוּמְאוֹת פּוֹרְשׁוֹת מִן הַמֵּת,

Three impurities come out from a corpse,

שְׁתַּיִם בְּכׇל אַחַת

two (ways of making impure) are in each one,

וּשְׁלִישִׁית אֵין בָּהֶן,

and the third is not in them,

וְאֵלּוּ הֵן: מְלֹא תַרְוָוד רָקָב,

and these are they: a full ladle of corpse-dust,
