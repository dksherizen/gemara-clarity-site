Chullin 26b

חולין כ״ו ע״ב

Open Chullin 26b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Purifying תמד through השקה, then three משניות of pairs that never overlap: מכר and קנס, מיאון and חליצה, and the שופר blast and הבדלה

Overview

This amud finishes a set of short rules and closes the first פרק of חולין. It opens with תמד, a weak drink made by soaking grape residue in water: before it ferments, it can be purified through השקה, touching the water of a מקוה. רבא limits this to water that was pure at first, but רב גביהה shows that the water and residue are mixed in both cases, so השקה works either way. Next come three short משניות, each with a pair of things that never happen together. The first is a father's right to sell his daughter (מכר) and the קנס fine. The second is מיאון and חליצה. For both of these pairs, רב says the משנה is only רבי מאיר's view, and חכמים let the two overlap. The third pair is the שופר blast before a holy day and הבדלה. The גמרא explains how the שופר is blown on a Friday יום טוב and where the special הבדלה wording 'בין קודש לקודש' is said. It rules against רב ששת בריה דרב אידי and against רבי דוסא, and רבי זירא explains why הבדלה lists the full set of distinctions even after a weekday יום טוב.

Opening lines

מַשִּׁיקוֹ בַּמַּיִם,

one brings it into contact with water,

מִשֶּׁהֶחְמִיץ – אֵין מַשִּׁיקוֹ בְּמַיִם.

once it has fermented, one does not bring it into contact with water.

אָמַר רָבָא:

Rava said:

לֹא שָׁנוּ אֶלָּא שֶׁתִּמְּדוֹ

They taught this only where one made the temed
