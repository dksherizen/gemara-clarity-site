Topic

רבא's rule on salting with forbidden food, שמואל's laws of salting meat, and the משניות on בשר בחלב

Overview

The amud opens by asking why רבא needed his own source to forbid kosher meat salted with טריפה meat, when שמואל already taught that salted food is like boiling food. The answer is that שמואל's rule might cover only blood, while רבא teaches that juices and gravy also forbid. The גמרא then tests רבא against a ברייתא about a kosher fish salted with a non-kosher fish, and each challenge is answered by reading the ברייתא's wording carefully. Next comes a memory sign for three teachings of שמואל: meat must be salted and rinsed well, salted meat must sit on a vessel with holes, and breaking an animal's neck before it dies traps blood in the meat. Along the way the גמרא adds the order of rinsing and salting, which inner parts are not assumed to have blood, and that several pieces may be salted together. The question of whether neck-broken meat is forbidden even for the owner ends in תיקו. A new משנה teaches that putting chicken and cheese on one table breaks no Torah law, since עוף in milk is only rabbinic. Another משנה sets out which meat and milk fall under בשר בחלב, with the views of רבי עקיבא and רבי יוסי הגלילי. The גמרא then begins looking for the source, with רבי אלעזר's proof from 'the kid of the goats' continuing on the next amud.
