Chullin 60a

חולין ס׳ ע״א

Topic

Stories of רבי יהושע בן חנניה and the קיסר, רב יהודה's teachings about bulls and אדם הראשון's bull, and creation made complete

Overview

The amud opens with stories of רבי יהושע בן חנניה answering the Roman קיסר and his daughter. The קיסר cannot look at the sun, so he certainly cannot see the שכינה. His feast for God is swept away by wind and rain, which are only God's 'cleaning staff.' The קיסר's daughter mocks God as a carpenter and asks for a distaff. She is struck with צרעת and receives the distaff Rome gave every leper, and God does not take it back. The גמרא then brings teachings of רב יהודה about bulls: what a normal bull looks like, and how the bull of אדם הראשון had one horn and had its horns appear before its hooves. This supports רבי יהושע בן לוי, who says all of creation was made fully grown, with full understanding. רבי חנינא בר פפא adds that the grasses reasoned on their own to grow by their kinds, and the angel of the world praised ה׳. The amud ends as רבינא begins a question about grafting two grasses together, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

חָזֵינָא לֵיהּ.

I want to see Him.

אֲזַל אוֹקְמֵיהּ לַהֲדֵי יוֹמָא

He went and stood him facing the sun

בִּתְקוּפַת תַּמּוּז,

in the season of Tammuz.

אֲמַר לֵיהּ: אִיסְתַּכַּל בֵּיהּ.

He said to him: Look at it.
