Chullin 52a

חולין נ״ב ע״א

Open Chullin 52a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Birds that fall (surfaces and the glue-board), and when uprooted or broken ribs make an animal טרפה

Overview

The גמרא finishes its list of surfaces a falling bird might land on. If the surface slips away under the bird, there is no worry of רסוק אברים; if it stays firm, there is. Next comes a bird stuck to a glue-board (דבוק). The two versions of the רב אשי and אמימר dispute lead to the ruling: two wings stuck is forbidden, one wing stuck is permitted. The גמרא then turns to the משנה's case of broken ribs. Twelve ribs make a majority, and זעירי and רבי יוחנן add limits on which breaks count. בן זכאי and רבי יוחנן argue over whether uprooted ribs need most of one side or most of both sides. רב rules that a rib uprooted with part of its vertebra is a טרפה, and that losing two opposite ribs of one vertebra is like an animal split in half (גיסטרא). A long back-and-forth explains these rulings and the way his students worded their question. The amud ends with שמואל's three cases of טרפה and a challenge to his rib ruling, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

חוֹל הַדַּק – לָא חָיְישִׁינַן;

Fine sand: we are not concerned;

חוֹל הַגַּס – חָיְישִׁינַן;

coarse sand: we are concerned;

אֲבַק דְּרָכִים – חָיְישִׁינַן;

dust of the roads: we are concerned;

תִּיבְנָא וַעֲבִיד בִּזְגָּא – חָיְישִׁינַן;

straw that was made into a bundle: we are concerned;
