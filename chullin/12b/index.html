Chullin 12b

חולין י״ב ע״ב

Learn Chullin 12b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Chullin 12b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

רבי's ruling about animals found slaughtered, the שחיטה of a חרש, שוטה, and קטן, whether שחיטה needs כוונה, and whether a קטן has legally meaningful מחשבה

Overview

The גמרא first finishes explaining רבי's statement about the argument between רבי יהודה and רבי חנינא בנו של רבי יוסי הגלילי. Each sage agrees with the other in one case: everyone follows רבי יהודה for a trash heap in the marketplace, and everyone follows רבי חנינא for a trash heap in the house. Next, the גמרא goes back to the משנה about a חרש, שוטה, and קטן. From the words 'שמא יקלקלו' (lest they ruin, in the future), רבא learns that we do not give them animals to slaughter לכתחילה. The משנה adds that if others watched them, their שחיטה is כשרה. The גמרא asks who holds that שחיטה needs no כוונה. רבא answers that it is רבי נתן, based on a ברייתא about a thrown knife, and the one who taught that ברייתא ruled הלכה כרבי נתן. The גמרא explains how a thrown knife could move back and forth: it bounced off the wall and came back. Finally, רבי יוחנן asks whether a קטן's מחשבה counts, and רבי אמי objects that a משנה about shells hollowed out by children already settles it. That משנה continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הָכִי קָאָמַר:

This is what he is saying:

נִרְאִין דִּבְרֵי רַבִּי יְהוּדָה

The words of Rabbi Yehuda appear correct

לְרַבִּי חֲנִינָא בְּנוֹ שֶׁל רַבִּי יוֹסֵי הַגְּלִילִי

to Rabbi Chanina, son of Rabbi Yosei HaGelili,

בְּאַשְׁפָּה שֶׁבַּשּׁוּק,

in the case of a trash heap that is in the marketplace,

The steps (12)

  1. ResolutionHow each opinion 'appears correct' to the other
  2. StatementThe משנה: a חרש, שוטה, and קטן are left out of slaughtering
  3. StatementLearning from the future tense: do not hand them animals to begin with
  4. StatementThe משנה: if others watch them, their שחיטה is valid
  5. QuestionWho holds that שחיטה needs no intention?
  6. ResponseIt is רבי נתן, as shown by the case of the thrown knife
  7. ConclusionThe one who taught the ברייתא rules like רבי נתן
  8. ChallengeA thrown knife does not move back and forth
  9. ResolutionThe knife went and came back in the normal way
  10. QuestionDoes a child's thought count?
  11. ChallengeWhy ask about thought if the משנה already answered it?
  12. ProofThe משנה about shells that children hollowed out
