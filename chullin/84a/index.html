Chullin 84a

חולין פ״ד ע״א

Open Chullin 84a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

No כיסוי הדם for consecrated birds or for a בהמה, and the תורה's lessons of דרך ארץ about eating meat and spending wisely

Overview

The amud opens by explaining why there is no כיסוי הדם for a bird consecrated for בדק הבית. It cannot be redeemed after slaughter, because redemption needs העמדה והערכה. רב יוסף shows that this follows רבי, who takes one point from רבי מאיר and one from רבי שמעון. A second answer uses the פסוק ״וְשָׁפַךְ וְכִסָּה״: covering applies only when nothing comes between pouring and covering. The same answer works for קדשי מזבח too. מר בר רב אשי learns the same exemption from ״חַיָּה אוֹ עוֹף״. רבא then answers יעקב מינאה that a בהמה's blood is poured 'like water' and needs no covering, and the גמרא proves from the מקוה פסוק that blood still cannot purify. Next, ברייתות learn lessons of דרך ארץ about meat: eat it with effort and real appetite, from your own animals, and never use up everything you own. רבי אלעזר בן עזריה gives a food-spending scale, and רב, רבי יוחנן and רב נחמן discuss it. The amud closes with money lessons from a פסוק in משלי and opens a statement by רבי יוחנן that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְלִיפְרְקִינְהוּ וְלִיכַסִּינְהוּ?

But let him redeem them and cover them!

בָּעֵינַן הַעֲמָדָה וְהַעֲרָכָה.

We require setting and valuating.

וּכְמַאן?

And in accordance with whom?

אִי כְּרַבִּי מֵאִיר, דְּאָמַר:

If like Rabbi Meir, who says:

  35. StatementYour own food comes first
