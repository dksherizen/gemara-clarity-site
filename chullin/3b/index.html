Topic

Explaining 'הכל שוחטין' and 'וכולן ששחטו': רבינא's readings, why each אמורא rejected the others, and the שחיטה of a כותי

Overview

The גמרא finishes testing רב אשי's reading of the opening משנה of חולין. In that reading, 'הכל שוחטין' includes a ישראל משומד whose knife is checked for him, while a חרש, שוטה, or קטן can never slaughter. The משנה's next line, 'וכולן ששחטו ואחרים רואין אותן', fits no case in this reading, so the גמרא calls it a קשיא. רבינא then offers a new reading in two versions. In the first, 'הכל' means experts in the laws of שחיטה. In the second, it means people established not to faint. In both versions, 'וכולן' refers to someone who cannot be tested or asked afterward, so only the fact that others watched him makes his שחיטה valid. The גמרא then sums up why each אמורא (אביי, רבא, רב אשי, רבה בר עולא, רבינא) did not accept the others' explanations. Finally, a ברייתא teaches that a כותי's שחיטה is permitted when a Jew watches him, or if he eats a כזית of the meat himself. It then begins a similar case that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בּוֹדֵק סַכִּין וְנוֹתֵן לוֹ,

One checks a knife and gives it to him,

וּמוּתָּר לֶאֱכוֹל מִשְּׁחִיטָתוֹ.

and it is permitted to eat from his slaughter.

אֲבָל לֹא בָּדַק וְנָתַן לוֹ –

But if one did not check it and give it to him –

לֹא יִשְׁחוֹט,

he should not slaughter,
