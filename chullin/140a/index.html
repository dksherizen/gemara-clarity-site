Chullin 140a

חולין ק״מ ע״א

Learn Chullin 140a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Chullin 140a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Whether צפור means only kosher birds, what 'טהורות' excludes in the מצורע's birds, and שילוח הקן when the eggs or chicks are not fit to eat

Overview

The גמרא defends רבי יצחק's rule that the תורה's word צפור means only a kosher bird. A פסוק in דניאל is turned aside, because non-kosher birds are only called 'birds of the heavens.' 'כל צפור טהורה' is explained as pointing to a kosher bird that is still forbidden: the slaughtered bird of a מצורע, whose eating breaks both an עשה and a לא תעשה. Next come two proofs from the מצורע's 'two living kosher birds.' 'Living' is read as having whole limbs. 'Kosher' cannot be about a טריפה, because the teaching of the school of רבי ישמעאל already excludes that. So four אמוראים give the word a new job: רב נחמן בר יצחק says birds of an עיר הנדחת, רבא says reusing a bird before it is sent away, רב פפא says birds traded for idol birds, and רבינא says a bird that killed a person. Then the גמרא returns to the משנה: a kosher bird sitting on non-kosher eggs is exempt from שילוח הקן because of רב כהנא's rule, 'take for yourself, and not for your dogs.' Finally, it asks why a טריפה mother is not compared to טריפה chicks; the discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

״וּבְעַנְפוֹהִי יְדוּרָן צִפְּרֵי שְׁמַיָּא״

'And in its branches will dwell the birds of the heavens'

– ״צִפְּרֵי שְׁמַיָּא״ אִיקְּרוֹ,

They are called 'birds of the heavens,'

״צִפְּרֵי״ סְתָמָא לָא אִיקְּרוֹ.

but they are not called plain 'birds.'

תָּא שְׁמַע: ״כׇּל צִפּוֹר טְהוֹרָה״,

Come and hear: 'Every kosher bird [you may eat]',
