Topic

Half-כזית pieces of נבלה on a hide or a wood chip: when touching or carrying them makes a person טמא

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier discussion: רבי יוחנן's statement is about a thin layer of flesh (מרודד) stuck to a נבלה hide. Next the גמרא turns to the משנה's case of two half-כזית pieces on a hide, where רבי ישמעאל says the hide makes a person טמא by carrying but not by touching. בר פדא says this applies only to touching the outside, because touching the two pieces one after the other from the inside adds up. רבי יוחנן says separate touches never add up, and he links רבי ישמעאל to רבי דוסא בן הרכינס, who says separate coverings of an אהל do not add up. רב עוקבא בר חמא challenges בר פדא from a ברייתא, and רבא answers that רבי עקיבא requires touching to be possible from every side. Then רב אויא סבא asks whether, for רבי ישמעאל, a sealed thigh bone makes impure by carrying, and רבה בר רב הונא dodges the question because he is tired. עולא rules that two half-pieces stuck on a wood chip do not make impure by carrying, because the כזית must be carried all at once by itself. The גמרא defends him with the thin-layer answer and shows that רבי אליעזר in a ברייתא holds his view. Finally, a new משנה begins about the thigh bone of a human corpse.
