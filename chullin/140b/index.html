Topic

שילוח הקן: unfit fledglings, unresolved questions, the male קורא, and a mother hovering or perched above the nest

Overview

The amud continues the laws of שילוח הקן. It first shows that the mother bird is not judged like the fledglings, and אביי rereads a ברייתא so it fits רב כהנא's rule that טרפה fledglings are exempt. Next come several questions: רב הושעיא asks about partly slaughtered fledglings, רבי ירמיה asks about things lying between the mother and the eggs, and רבי זירא asks about a bird sitting on another kosher species' eggs. The first two end in תיקו, and אביי's attempted proof for רבי זירא's question is rejected. The גמרא then explains the משנה's מחלוקת about a male קורא. רבי אליעזר learns from the word 'brooding' that it must be sent away, and רבי אלעזר limits the argument to the male קורא alone. A new משנה teaches that a hovering mother counts if her wings touch the nest, that even one egg is enough, and that unfertilized eggs and chicks that can fly are excluded. Finally the גמרא discusses רב's ruling about a bird perched on tree branches above the nest, in two versions of a debate over a ברייתא. The second version ends with a challenge that continues on the next amud.
