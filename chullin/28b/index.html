Topic

Checking a doubtful duck, רבי יהודה's rule about the ורידין, and whether exactly half of a סימן counts as a majority

Overview

This amud opens by finishing a practical problem. רבא has a duck with a bloody neck, and he needs to know whether its קנה was already cut or its ושט has a hole. The גמרא rejects checking it after שחיטה and also rejects checking it before. רב יוסף, רבא's son, solves the problem: check the קנה, slaughter through the קנה alone, then turn the ושט inside out and check it. From this the גמרא learns that in a bird, 'one סימן' can be either one. Next, רב חסדא says רבי יהודה requires cutting the ורידין only in a bird, so that the blood drains. Several sources seem to make the veins part of the שחיטה itself. Each one is reread to mean puncturing them during the שחיטה, while the blood is still warm. An elder adds that the veins may even be punctured with a thorn, and a ברייתא supports רב חסדא. Finally, רב and רב כהנא argue whether exactly half of a סימן counts as a majority, and the גמרא tests רב's view with proofs that continue onto the next amud.
