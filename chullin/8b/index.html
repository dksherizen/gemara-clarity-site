Chullin 8b

חולין ח׳ ע״ב

Open Chullin 8b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Residue from knives and forbidden fats: knives of עבודה זרה and גוים, cleaning a טריפה knife, and keeping meat apart from חלבים

Overview

This amud finishes רב נחמן's case of slaughtering with a knife of עבודה זרה. The גמרא explains that the knife is forbidden but has no residue on it. That works if the knife was only used to cut a branch for the idol, or if it is an old knife heated white-hot. Next, רב says that after slaughtering with a knife of גוים you must peel the meat, and רבה בר בר חנה says rinsing is enough. The גמרא gives two versions of why they argue: the slaughter spot is hot but the סימנים are busy pushing out blood, or the spot is cold but the knife's pressure pushes residue in. רב אחא and רבינא then argue about cleaning a knife used on a טריפה, and the הלכה is that cold water, or even wiping it with a cloth, is enough. רב rules that a butcher needs separate knives and separate rinsing water for meat and חלבים, because of a גזירה, and the requirement itself keeps them from getting mixed up. Finally, אמימר warns not to lay fatty flanks on top of other meat. A new challenge to that rule begins at the end and continues on the next amud.
