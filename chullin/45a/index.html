Chullin 45a

חולין מ״ה ע״א

Learn Chullin 45a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Chullin 45a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Windpipe damage measures, the valid area of the neck for שחיטה, and the brain membranes

Overview

This amud opens with the last line of a story, where a sage says that people who honor him are really honoring themselves. Then the גמרא turns to damage in the windpipe (קנה). Holes that remove real material add up toward the size of an איסר coin. Tiny sieve-like holes that remove almost nothing add up toward a majority of the windpipe's circumference, like a cut. For a bird, whose windpipe is too small for an איסר, the damaged piece is folded over the opening, and if it covers most of it the bird is טרפה. The גמרא also rules on a door-like flap and on a crack along the length. On the crack, רבי יוחנן says any tiny whole bit at the top and bottom is enough. Next it marks the area of the neck valid for שחיטה, which is the stretch the animal extends naturally while grazing, and it defines the חזה. Finally it discusses a hole in the brain's membranes and where the brain ends and the חוט השדרה begins. That last answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אִתְיַיקּוֹרֵי הוּא דְּמִתְיַיקְּרוּ בִּי.

It is an honor for them that they honor me.

אָמַר רַב יְהוּדָה אָמַר רַב:

Rav Yehuda said that Rav said:

נִיקְּבָה כְּנָפָה –

If it was pierced like a sieve,

מִצְטָרְפִים לְרוּבָּא.

they join together to make up a majority.
