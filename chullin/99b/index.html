Chullin 99b

חולין צ״ט ע״ב

Topic

Cancelling forbidden items in mixtures: תרומה leaven, fish ציר, and the flavor of the גיד הנשה

Overview

The amud opens in the middle of a debate between אביי and רב דימי about a משנה in ערלה: how much plain dough is needed to cancel out תרומה leaven that fell into it. רב דימי reads the רישא as one hundred and one and the סיפא as one hundred. אביי then asks why strong leaven is not cancelled in one hundred and one. רב דימי is silent, and אביי suggests that שאור is different because its leavening power is so strong. רב דימי supports this with רבי יוסי ברבי חנינא's teaching that not all measures are equal: fish ציר needs close to two hundred. The גמרא explains that רבי יהודה lets ציר be cancelled at all because it is only 'sweat'. The גמרא then returns to the משנה of גיד הנשה. רב הונא explains how its flavor is measured, and the גמרא notes a תנא who holds that sinews give no flavor. Stories about רבי חנינא and רבי אמי follow, and the הלכה is set that sinews give no flavor. The amud ends with a new challenge: why isn't a cooked גיד הנשה cancelled by the majority?
