Chullin 107a

חולין ק״ז ע״א

Learn Chullin 107a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Chullin 107a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Rules of נטילת ידים: whole-day stipulation, channel water, valid vessels, the רביעית, and eating with a cloth

Overview

The amud opens by finishing רבי אבינא's advice to people short of water: wash once in the morning and make a condition that it covers the whole day. Two versions argue over whether this works only in a שעת הדחק, which decides whether he agrees with רב. רב פפא then rules on washing from a channel filled by buckets. A bucket with a hole joins the channel to the river, so hands may be dipped there, but the bucket itself is useless for pouring. רבא requires a רביעית of water. The גמרא settles two statements of his by saying two people may share water that started as a full רביעית. A second version of אמימר's answer claims the amount does not matter, and the גמרא rejects it, while sages are shown making vessels that hold a רביעית. רבא and a ברייתא rule that a prepared barrel stopper or wineskin is a valid vessel, but a sack or basket is not. Finally the גמרא asks whether one may eat holding food in a cloth instead of washing. A proof from רבי צדוק is rejected, and a new proof from שמואל and רב begins, continuing on the next amud.

Opening lines

פַּקְתָּא דַּעֲרָבוֹת:

[To the people of] the valley of Aravot:

כְּגוֹן אַתּוּן דְּלָא שְׁכִיחִי לְכוּ מַיָּא,

people such as you, for whom water is not readily found,

מְשׁוּ יְדַיְיכוּ מִצַּפְרָא

wash your hands in the morning

וְאַתְנוֹ עֲלַיְיהוּ לְכוּלֵּאּ יוֹמָא.

and make a condition on them for the whole day.
