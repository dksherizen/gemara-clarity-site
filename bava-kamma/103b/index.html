Bava Kamma 103b

בבא קמא ק״ג ע״ב

Learn Bava Kamma 103b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Bava Kamma 103b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Whose view is the משנה's rule that only a robber who swore falsely must make sure the real owner gets paid: רבי עקיבא or רבי טרפון, in light of their מחלוקת about robbing one of five people?

Overview

The amud opens with the end of the משנה. A robber who swears falsely about the extra חומש must add a new חומש on top of it, again and again, until the amount drops below a פרוטה. The same rule of קרן, חומש and אשם applies to a פקדון and the other cases in the פסוק. The גמרא notes that the משנה makes the robber chase the victim only if he swore. It asks whose view that is, using the מחלוקת of רבי טרפון and רבי עקיבא about someone who robbed one of five people. First it answers that the משנה follows רבי עקיבא, who is strict only after an oath. It explains רבי טרפון's leniency through the enactment of paying the בית דין. רב הונא בר יהודה and רבא knock down this answer, with a ברייתא about a buyer and a story about a חסיד. So the גמרא switches: the משנה follows רבי טרפון, who agrees to be strict after an oath because of the פסוק, while רבי עקיבא is strict even without an oath as a קנס. Finally the גמרא asks why the משנה needs the oath at all, since רבי טרפון is strict whenever the robber admits on his own. It begins quoting a ברייתא that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הֲרֵי זֶה מְשַׁלֵּם חוֹמֶשׁ עַל חוֹמֶשׁ,

this one pays a fifth on top of a fifth,

עַד שֶׁיִּתְמַעֵט הַקֶּרֶן מִשָּׁוֶה פְּרוּטָה.

until the principal is reduced to less than the value of a perutah.

וְכֵן בְּפִקָּדוֹן, שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר:

And so too with a deposit, as it is stated:

״אוֹ בְּפִקָּדוֹן אוֹ בִתְשׂוּמֶת יָד

"with regard to a deposit, or with regard to a pledge,

  7. ResolutionWhy רבי טרפון is lenient even after an oath
