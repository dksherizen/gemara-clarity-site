Topic

What the lists of damage categories of the משנה, רבי אושעיא and רבי חייא include and leave out, and the משנה's comparisons between the אבות

Overview

The גמרא keeps asking why certain payments are missing from רבי אושעיא's list of damage categories. עדים זוממין are left off because he follows רבי עקיבא, for whom their payment is a קנס. An ox that hurts a person is not split off, because even רבי עקיבא says it is paid only from the ox's body. The אונס, מפתה and מוציא שם רע, and the מטמא, מדמע and מנסך, are each either already covered by an existing category or are a קנס, which the list does not include. The גמרא then asks what רבי חייא's number of twenty-four leaves out. It answers the מפגל, because the list does not deal with קדשים, and the מוסר, because his damage is only through speech. The מוציא שם רע and עדים זוממין stay on the list, because one's speech comes with an action and the other's speech is called an action by the תורה. רבי אבהו explains that 'אבות' in the long lists means everything is paid from מיטב, learned from four shared words. Finally the גמרא begins explaining the משנה's comparisons between שור, מבעה and the living categories, and that explanation continues on the next amud.

עֵדִים זוֹמְמִין,

Conspiring witnesses,

דְּמָמוֹנָא הוּא, לִיתְנֵי!

which is a money payment, let him teach it!

סָבַר לַהּ כְּרַבִּי עֲקִיבָא,

He holds like Rabbi Akiva,

דְּאָמַר: אֵין מְשַׁלְּמִין עַל פִּי עַצְמָן.

who says: they do not pay based on their own admission.
