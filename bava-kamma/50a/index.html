Bava Kamma 50a

בבא קמא נ׳ ע״א

Open Bava Kamma 50a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Liability for a בור according to רבה and רב יוסף, נחוניא the well-digger, and God's exact judgment of צדיקים

Overview

The amud continues the argument between רבי ישמעאל and רבי עקיבא about who is liable for a בור. According to רבה, they argue about a pit on one's own property: רבי עקיבא reads "בעל הבור" as a pit that has an owner, while רבי ישמעאל reads it as the one who made the hazard. רב יוסף disagrees. He says everyone holds a person liable for a pit on private property, and the real argument is about a pit in רשות הרבים. There, רבי ישמעאל learns liability from the words "opens" and "digs", while רבי עקיבא says both words are needed for other lessons. Two ברייתות are brought to challenge the readings, and each time the גמרא shows how both רבה and רב יוסף can explain them. Next, a ברייתא teaches that someone who digs a well and hands it over to the public is exempt, and נחוניא the well-digger is praised for doing exactly that. The amud ends with stories and lessons about how exactly הקדוש ברוך הוא judges צדיקים, and a new teaching on a פסוק that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בְּבוֹר בִּרְשׁוּתוֹ –

[they argue] about a pit on his own property –

רַבִּי עֲקִיבָא סָבַר:

Rabbi Akiva holds:

בּוֹר בִּרְשׁוּתוֹ נָמֵי חַיָּיב,

a pit on his own property also makes him liable,

דִּכְתִיב: ״בַּעַל הַבּוֹר״ –

as it is written: "The owner of the pit" –
