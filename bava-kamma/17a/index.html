Bava Kamma 17a

בבא קמא י״ז ע״א

Open Bava Kamma 17a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

חזקיה's funeral honor, the reward for תורה and kindness, and the opening משנה on רגל

Overview

This amud finishes the discussion of how חזקיה מלך יהודה was honored at his death. רבי יהודה says 36,000 men walked before him with bared shoulders. רבי נחמיה objects that אחאב got the same honor. He says instead that they laid a ספר תורה on the bier and said 'this man kept what is written in it.' The גמרא asks that we do this today too, and gives several answers. The last answer, from רבי יוחנן, is that only for חזקיה did they say 'he taught' the תורה. Next, רבי שמעון בן יוחאי teaches that whoever is busy with תורה and גמילות חסדים earns the portion of two tribes, יוסף and יששכר. Then the first פרק ends with a הדרן. The second פרק opens with a משנה: an animal walking normally and breaking things is רגל and pays full damage, while kicking, pebbles, a second broken vessel, and a chicken's string or hopping pay half.

Opening lines

וְחַד אָמַר שִׁבְעָה.

And the other one said: seven.

וְאָמְרִי לַהּ: שְׁלֹשִׁים.

And some say: thirty.

תָּנוּ רַבָּנַן:

The Rabbis taught:

״וְכָבוֹד עָשׂוּ לוֹ בְמוֹתוֹ״

"And they gave him honor in his death"
