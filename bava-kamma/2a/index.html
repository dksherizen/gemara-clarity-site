Bava Kamma 2a

בבא קמא ב׳ ע״א

Open Bava Kamma 2a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The four אבות נזיקין, and how אבות and תולדות relate in נזיקין, שבת, and טומאה.

Overview

This amud opens מסכת בבא קמא with a משנה listing the four אבות נזיקין: שור, בור, מבעה, and הבער. The משנה shows that each has a feature the others lack, so no one category could be learned from another. It then names what all four share: damage is normal for them, and you must guard them. When they cause damage, the one responsible pays with מיטב הארץ. The גמרא notes that the word אבות implies there are תולדות. It asks whether those תולדות follow the same law as their אבות. To explore this, it looks at שבת. There a תולדה brings the same חטאת and סקילה as its אב, and the labels matter only for counting how many חטאות are owed. For רבי אליעזר, the names only reflect whether a labor was important in the משכן. The גמרא then begins quoting the משנה that lists the אבות of טומאה, and that discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳ <big><strong>אַרְבָּעָה</big></strong> אֲבוֹת נְזִיקִין:

MISHNA: There are four primary categories of damage:

הַשּׁוֹר, וְהַבּוֹר,

the Ox, and the Pit,

וְהַמַּבְעֶה, וְהַהֶבְעֵר.

and the Maveh, and the Fire.

לֹא הֲרֵי הַשּׁוֹר כַּהֲרֵי הַמַּבְעֶה,

The nature of the Ox is not like the nature of the Maveh,

The steps (12)

  1. StatementThe four main categories of damage
  2. StatementOx and Maveh differ, and both differ from Fire
  3. StatementThe moving damagers differ from the Pit
  4. ConclusionWhat all four share, and the obligation to pay
  5. QuestionIf there are primary categories, are their subcategories treated the same?
  6. StatementBringing in the example of שבת, which also has primary categories
  7. StatementIn שבת, subcategories carry the same punishment as primary categories
  8. QuestionSo what is the difference between a primary category and a subcategory?
  9. ResponseThe difference shows up when counting how many sin-offerings are owed
  10. QuestionWhy call it אב and תולדה according to רבי אליעזר?
  11. ResponseAn אב was an important labor in the משכן
  12. StatementThe משנה that lists the אבות of טומאה
