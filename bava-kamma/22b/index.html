Bava Kamma 22b

Topic

The machlokes between רבי יוחנן (אשו משום חציו, his fire is like his arrows) and ריש לקיש (אשו משום ממונו, his fire is like his property), tested against the משנה of the burning גדיש with a tied עבד or גדי.

Overview

This עמוד tests the מחלוקת between רבי יוחנן, who holds that a person's fire is like his arrows (אשו משום חציו), and ריש לקיש, who holds that it is like his property (אשו משום ממונו). First the גמרא finishes rejecting a proof from the camel-and-lamp case, which depends only on who was careless. Next it brings a משנה: someone who burns a stack with a tied slave is completely exempt. That seems to fit only the arrows view. ריש לקיש answers that he lit the slave's own body, which is murder under every view. The גמרא then explains that this משנה still teaches something new, because the goat and the slave had different owners. A second proof comes from a משנה that exempts someone who sends fire with a חרש, שוטה וקטן. ריש לקיש, quoting חזקיה, limits that משנה to a coal the חרש fanned, while handing over a flame makes the sender liable. רבי יוחנן says even a flame is exempt, unless the sender hands over wood, chips and a lamp. Finally רבא shows that a פסוק supports רבי יוחנן, and he begins quoting a ברייתא that continues on the next עמוד.
