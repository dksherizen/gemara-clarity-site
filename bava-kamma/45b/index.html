Topic

Bailees' liability for an ox, and how much guarding exempts the owner of a תם or a מועד: the views of רבי מאיר, רבי יהודה, רבי אליעזר and רבי אליעזר בן יעקב.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a question about a ברייתא on bailees of a goring ox: whose view does it follow? It cannot fully match רבי מאיר or רבי יהודה. רב הונא בר חיננא says it follows רבי אליעזר on guarding and רבי יהודה on the שוכר. אביי says it follows רבי מאיר, in רבה בר אבוה's version where the two opinions about the שוכר are switched. רבי אלעזר then rules that a שומר חנם pays if the ox damages others but not if it is injured. רבא explains that the guard knew the ox was a gorer and took responsibility only for protecting others. A new משנה follows with three views on an ox that was tied or locked in properly and still did damage: רבי מאיר says both תם and מועד are liable, רבי יהודה says only the תם is liable, and רבי אליעזר says only slaughter counts as guarding. The גמרא explains how רבי מאיר and רבי יהודה read the פסוקים, including the rule that a ריבוי after a ריבוי comes to limit. It also brings רבי אליעזר בן יעקב, who says lesser guarding is enough for both. Finally, רב אדא בר אהבה says רבי יהודה exempts only the מועד half, and the גמרא uses רב's rule about the right and left horns to show that רב disagrees with him.

דְּאָמַר: שׂוֹכֵר כְּשׁוֹמֵר חִנָּם דָּמֵי,

let it teach: 'except for an unpaid guard and the renter!'
