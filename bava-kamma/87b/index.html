Topic

Who receives the injury payments when a father or others injure a daughter or son: רב's view that the Torah gave the father only שבח נעורים, and the challenges to it.

Overview

רב rules that when someone injures a young daughter, the payment goes to her, because the Torah gave the father only שבח נעורים. רבי אלעזר objects from the עבד עברי and from a ברייתא that says others who injure a minor daughter pay her father. Both objections are answered: the father gets only שבת (lost work time), since her earnings are his. The גמרא then finds two ברייתות that disagree about a father who injures his own adult son. It settles them by asking whether the children are supported at his table. Defending that answer, it explains that a self-supporting daughter keeps what she needs for food and only the surplus goes to her father. It also explains that a supporting father lets his children keep injury money from outsiders, unlike a strict father who claims even that. Next the גמרא defines סגולה as a ספר תורה or a fruit-bearing date palm. Finally it brings ריש לקיש and רבי יוחנן arguing over the same question and begins to challenge רבי יוחנן's view about a mere wound, a challenge that continues on the next amud.
