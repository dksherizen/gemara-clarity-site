Topic

The אבות and תולדות of damage by an ox: קרן, its sub-types, and the sources for שן and רגל

Overview

The גמרא finishes its example from טומאה, where the תולדות are weaker than their אבות, and asks how it works in damages. רב פפא answers that in damages some תולדות are like their אבות and some are not. A ברייתא lists the three אבות of an ox: קרן, שן and רגל. The גמרא proves from two פסוקים that 'goring' means using a horn, and explains that the second פסוק shows the difference between תם and מועד applies to an attached horn too. It then lists the תולדות of קרן: pushing, biting, crouching and kicking. Along the way it learns from the words יגח and יגף that an ox מועד for people is מועד for animals, but not the other way around. Since the תולדות of קרן are fully like קרן, רב פפא must have meant שן and רגל. The גמרא begins bringing the פסוקים for רגל and שן, and the second proof continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

וּטְמֵא מֵת.

and one who is impure from a dead body.

תּוֹלְדוֹתֵיהֶן לָאו כַּיּוֹצֵא בָּהֶן;

Their offshoots are not like them;

דְּאִילּוּ אָב – מְטַמֵּא אָדָם וְכֵלִים,

for a primary source makes a person and vessels impure,

וְאִילּוּ תּוֹלָדוֹת – אוֹכָלִין וּמַשְׁקִין מְטַמֵּא,

while offshoots make food and drink impure,
