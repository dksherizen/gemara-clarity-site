Topic

רבי יהודה's exemption of a סומא, the משנה on who pays for injuring a person, and who receives payment for injuring a young daughter

Overview

The amud begins by finishing רבי יהודה's view on a סומא. He exempts a blind person from all civil laws, learned from the פסוק about the killer and the גואל הדם. A second ברייתא has him exempt a סומא from all מצוות, and רב שישא בריה דרב אידי links מצוות to משפטים through another פסוק. רב יוסף, who was blind, first wanted the הלכה to follow רבי יהודה. After hearing רבי חנינא say that one who is commanded and does is greater, he wanted the opposite. Then a new משנה teaches that a person who injures pays all five payments plus דמי ולדות, while an ox's owner pays only נזק. It goes through special cases: parents, יום הכפורים and שבת, Hebrew and Canaanite slaves, the חרש, שוטה וקטן, and the slave and married woman. The גמרא opens with רבי אלעזר asking רב who receives the payment when someone injures another man's young daughter. The question's two sides are laid out, and רב's answer comes on the next amud.
