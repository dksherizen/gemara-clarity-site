Bava Kamma 40a

בבא קמא מ׳ ע״א

Learn Bava Kamma 40a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Bava Kamma 40a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Responsibility for goring oxen held by others: guardians of a חרש, שוטה, or קטן, the nature and amount of כופר, and a borrower whose ox turns out to be a מועד.

Overview

This amud covers three topics. First, it finishes discussing רבי יעקב's ruling about the ox of a חרש, שוטה, or קטן. The ruling could be about a תם, which would teach that בית דין appoints an אפוטרופוס. רבא's reading as a מועד is defended because it packs two ideas into one case. רבינא then says the real argument is whether a change of control (רשות משנה) turns a מועד back into a תם. Next, a ברייתא says stewards do not pay כופר. רב חסדא and רב פפא debate whether כופר is atonement or a money payment, and whose value sets the amount. Then רב אחא בר יעקב asks רב נחמן two hard questions about כופר: how partners pay it, and whether בית דין takes collateral for it. רב נחמן cannot answer. Last, a ברייתא about a borrowed ox that turns out to be a מועד leads to a back-and-forth about why the borrower still pays half. That debate continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אִי לְרַבִּי יְהוּדָה –

If according to Rabbi Yehuda —

דַּעֲבַד לֵיהּ שְׁמִירָה פְּחוּתָה,

it is a case where he gave it reduced guarding,

וְלָא עֲבַד לֵיהּ שְׁמִירָה מְעוּלָּה.

and did not give it superior guarding.

אִי לְרַבִּי אֱלִיעֶזֶר בֶּן יַעֲקֹב –

If according to Rabbi Eliezer ben Yaakov —
