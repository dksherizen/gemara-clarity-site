Bava Kamma 76b

בבא קמא ע״ו ע״ב

Topic

A thief who slaughters a stolen קדשים animal: can the שחיטה count as the act that 'permits' the animal, when really זריקה or פדיה is what permits it?

Overview

The גמרא is explaining how a thief can owe the fourfold or fivefold payment for slaughtering a stolen consecrated animal. After explanations given in the name of רבי יוחנן (slaughter inside the מקדש), ריש לקיש gives his own case: slaughtering blemished consecrated animals outside. רבי אלעזר objects to both. Inside, it is the זריקה of the blood that permits the meat. For a blemished animal, it is the פדיה that permits it. So the slaughter alone does not seem to permit anything. The גמרא answers that רבי אלעזר forgot רבי שמעון's rule: what is ready to be sprinkled counts as sprinkled, and what is ready to be redeemed counts as redeemed. It proves the sprinkling half from a ברייתא about נותר. Meat left overnight after the blood was fit to be sprinkled can get טומאת אוכלין, even though the blood was never actually sprinkled. The amud ends as the גמרא begins a second ברייתא of רבי שמעון to prove the redemption half, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְרֵישׁ לָקִישׁ אָמַר:

And Reish Lakish said:

בְּשׁוֹחֵט בַּעֲלֵי מוּמִין בַּחוּץ.

It is a case where he slaughters blemished animals outside.

תָּהֵי בַּהּ רַבִּי אֶלְעָזָר:

Rabbi Elazar wondered about this:

לְרַבִּי יוֹחָנָן – שְׁחִיטָה מַתֶּרֶת?!

According to Rabbi Yochanan, does the slaughter permit it?!

The steps (12)

  1. Statementריש לקיש: the case is slaughtering blemished animals outside
  2. Challengeרבי אלעזר's challenge to רבי יוחנן: sprinkling is what permits
  3. Challengeרבי אלעזר's challenge to ריש לקיש: redemption is what permits
  4. Resolutionרבי אלעזר forgot רבי שמעון's rule: what is ready is treated as done
  5. ProofSource for the rule: a ברייתא of רבי שמעון about leftover meat
  6. ProofThe ברייתא explains: before the sprinkling or after it
  7. Statement'Before sprinkling' really means before the blood was fit to be sprinkled
  8. QuestionWhen could meat stay overnight before the blood was even fit?
  9. ResponseSlaughtered right before sunset
  10. ResponseThe case of meat left overnight after the blood was fit for sprinkling
  11. ConclusionBlood ready to be sprinkled counts as sprinkled
  12. ProofSecond claim: an animal ready to be redeemed counts as redeemed
