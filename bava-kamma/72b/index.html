Topic

Slaughtering חולין in the עזרה and the four-or-five payment; עדים זוממין in theft cases; when an עד זומם becomes disqualified

Overview

The amud opens in the middle of an argument over whether the ban on benefiting from חולין שנשחטו בעזרה is from the Torah or only דרבנן. Someone tried to prove it is only דרבנן from our משנה, which makes a thief who slaughtered in the עזרה pay four or five. רב אחא בריה דרבא pushes back, but רב אשי rejects his answer because "וּטְבָחוֹ" requires a complete slaughter. רב אשי then answers in the name of רב גמדא, quoting רבא: the thief began cutting the סימנים outside and finished inside, so the animal becomes forbidden only as the slaughter is completed. A new משנה follows about עדים זוממין who testified that someone stole an animal and then slaughtered or sold it. It explains who pays what when one pair or two pairs of witnesses are proven false, and why the whole case falls if a theft witness is false. The גמרא then brings a dispute: אביי says an עד זומם is disqualified going backward, from when he testified, and רבא says only from when he is caught. A second version says רבא really agrees with אביי, and his different reason continues on the next amud.
