Topic

How הצד השוה works (including רבינא's falling wall), the word חב, and the dispute over paying damages from 'the best land'

Overview

This amud first finishes the discussion of what the words הַצַּד הַשָּׁוֶה in the opening משנה come to add. Through a back-and-forth between שור and בור, the גמרא shows that each one answers the other's weakness, so a case can be learned from what they share. רבינא then applies the same method to a wall or tree that falls into the street after בית דין told the owner to take it down. Next the גמרא explains why the משנה uses the short word חב instead of חייב: the תנא was from ירושלים and spoke briefly. It then brings a ברייתא where רבי ישמעאל and רבי עקיבא argue about whose 'best land' the פסוק means when a damager pays. After one explanation is rejected by רבא, רב אחא בר יעקב explains that they argue about whether land quality is measured by the damaged person's land or the damager's, and the גמרא shows how each reads the פסוק. Finally the גמרא asks what רבי עקיבא's קל וחומר for הקדש refers to. It settles on a vow to give money to the בית המקדש and begins to challenge that answer, a challenge that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שׁוֹר יוֹכִיחַ!

Ox will prove it!

מָה לְשׁוֹר

What about an ox?

It is different, because its way is to go and do damage.

Pit will prove it!
