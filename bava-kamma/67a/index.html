Topic

When does a thief own a stolen item? שינוי השם (the beam, the water duct, הקדש and תרומה), the פסוק source for שינוי קונה, and עולא on יאוש

Overview

This amud asks when a thief becomes the owner of what he stole. It starts with a stolen beam built into a building. The ruling there needs תקנת השבים to let the thief pay instead of returning the beam, which suggests the name change alone did not count. רב יוסף answers that a beam keeps its name even inside a house, and he proves it from a ברייתא on a פסוק in יחזקאל. רבי זירא answers instead that a name change which can undo itself does not count. The גמרא then tests whether a lasting name change counts, using a log hollowed into a water duct for a מקוה. It defends the rule by noting that drawn water is only a rabbinic law and that the log was never a כלי while detached. A ברייתא saying a thief's הקדש, תרומה and מעשר are valid is explained by the name change those acts create. Next come two opposite versions of רב חסדא in the name of רבי יונתן on whether the פסוק "והשיב את הגזילה אשר גזל" teaches that a change makes the thief the owner. Finally, עולא begins to prove from מלאכי that יאוש alone does not give the thief ownership, and his proof continues on the next amud.
