Topic

Who pays when others contributed to the damage, and why the ניזק keeps the carcass and bears its drop in value

Overview

The גמרא is looking for cases where other people helped cause damage, yet one person pays for all of it. Adding wood to a fire does not work as an example: either his liability is obvious, or he did nothing. רב פפא brings the ברייתא of a bench that breaks under a sixth person. After several tries, the גמרא explains it as a case where the bench broke the moment he leaned on the others, which teaches כחו כגופו דמי. The ten sticks case is left off the list, either because the list deals only with money or because the list would not follow a lone opinion against the רבנן. Next, the משנה's word 'תשלומי' teaches that the ניזק keeps the נבילה and the מזיק pays only the loss. Three פסוקים are brought as sources for this, and a צריכותא shows why each one is needed. רב כהנא asks why the תורה needs to give the carcass to the ניזק at all, and the answer is פחת נבילה, the carcass's drop in value. The amud ends by asking whether פחת נבילה is a dispute between תנאים, and that discussion continues on the next amud.

אִי דִּבְלָאו אִיהוּ לָא אָזְלָא – פְּשִׁיטָא!
