Bava Kamma 92a

בבא קמא צ״ב ע״א

Learn Bava Kamma 92a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Bava Kamma 92a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The end of the rule about cutting down trees (בל תשחית) with two stories about palms growing among vines, then the start of the משנה teaching that paying damages is not enough: the one who caused harm must also ask forgiveness, and the victim should not be cruel.

Overview

This עמוד finishes the rule about cutting down trees. The word ״רק״ teaches that value matters, and שמואל and רב חסדא each had date palms cut down to protect more valuable grapevines. Then a new משנה teaches that someone who injures another person must ask forgiveness even after paying, and that a victim who refuses to forgive is cruel. Both lessons come from the story of אברהם and אבימלך. The משנה also rules on permission to cause damage. Injury to the body is always paid for. Damage to property is free only with a clear exemption. Permission given by a third person never helps. The גמרא then explains פסוקים from the אבימלך story, including the lesson that a בן נח is punished for failing to learn. Finally, רבא asks רבה בר מרי for sources of several well-known sayings, and the last one continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

יָכוֹל אֲפִילּוּ מְעוּלֶּה בְּדָמִים?

One might think: even if it is worth more money?

תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר: ״רַק״.

The verse teaches: "Only."

שְׁמוּאֵל אַיְיתִי לֵיהּ אֲרִיסֵיהּ תַּמְרֵי.

Shmuel's sharecropper brought him dates.

אָכֵיל, טָעֵים בְּהוּ טַעְמָא דְחַמְרָא.

He ate, and tasted in them the taste of wine.

  9. StatementNo one can permit damage to someone else
  10. StatementMoney pays for embarrassment, but hurt needs forgiveness
