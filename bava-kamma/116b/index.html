Bava Kamma 116b

בבא קמא קט״ז ע״ב

Topic

How a group shares costs and rescued property, and a robber's liability when thugs seize a stolen field

Overview

The amud opens at the end of a challenge. A worker sent with food to a sick person is paid even if the sick person died or recovered, so a rescuer who failed should also be paid. רבי answers that the worker finished his job and the rescuer did not. Next come ברייתות about groups sharing costs. A ransom paid to bandits is split by money, a guide's wages may also be split per person, and cargo thrown off a sinking ship is shared by weight. Donkey drivers and sailors may agree to replace each other's losses, but not losses caused by carelessness. Another ברייתא says a traveler who saves a caravan's goods from bandits saves them for everyone, unless he announced he was saving them for himself. רמי בר חמא, רבא and רב אשי each explain why his words matter. Then a new משנה teaches that a robber whose stolen field is seized by thugs owes nothing in a regional disaster, but must replace it if the loss came because of him. The גמרא explains the wording of the משנה and tells of a man who showed the ריש גלותא's wheat to thugs and was made to pay by רב נחמן. רב יוסף's question about why it matters whether that payment was law or a fine continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לְהָבִיא כְּרוּב וְדוּרְמַסְקָנִין לַחוֹלֶה,

to bring cabbage and plums to a sick person,

וְהָלַךְ וּמְצָאוֹ שֶׁמֵּת אוֹ שֶׁהִבְרִיא –

and he went and found that he had died or recovered –

נוֹתֵן לוֹ שְׂכָרוֹ מִשָּׁלֵם!

he gives him his wage in full!

אֲמַר לֵיהּ: מִי דָּמֵי?!

He said to him: Are they comparable?!

The steps (28)

  1. ChallengeThe worker sent with food for a sick person still gets full pay
  2. ResolutionThe cases are different because the worker finished his job
  3. StatementA ransom paid to bandits is split by how much money each person has

  5. StatementDonkey drivers may agree to replace a lost donkey
  6. QuestionIsn't the last rule obvious?

  8. StatementCargo thrown off a ship in a storm is shared by weight
  9. StatementSailors may agree to replace a lost ship
  10. QuestionIsn't the sailing rule obvious?
  11. ResponseThe safe distance changes by season
  12. StatementRescuing a caravan from bandits
  13. ChallengeHow can both halves of the ruling be true?
