Bava Kamma 110b

בבא קמא ק״י ע״ב

Open Bava Kamma 110b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Laws of גזל הגר paid to the כהנים: which משמר receives it, trading shares, heirs versus gift-receivers, and אביי's rule that the money brings half the כפרה.

Overview

This amud deals with גזל הגר: money stolen from a convert who died with no heirs, which must be paid to the כהנים. It first finishes a question of רבא. What if the amount is too small for each כהן of the big משמר of יהויריב, but the thief gives it to the smaller משמר of ידעיה during יהויריב's week? The question ends as a תיקו. Next רבא rules that כהנים may not trade one גזל הגר payment for another, because the תורה calls it an אשם. Then the גמרא asks whether the כהנים get this money as heirs or as gift-receivers, and whether that changes the law of מעשר בהמה on animals they receive. A ברייתא listing the twenty-four מתנות כהונה includes גזל הגר and calls it a gift, so the כהנים are gift-receivers. Finally, אביי learns from the משנה that the money alone brings half the כפרה. The גמרא tests his reasoning with a חטאת, an אשם, and a יבמה tied to a brother with boils. The answer to the יבמה case continues on the next amud.
