Bava Kamma 18a

בבא קמא י״ח ע״א

Learn Bava Kamma 18a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Bava Kamma 18a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Indirect damage by an animal (צרורות): whether we judge by the first action, and whether the half payment is collected from the animal's body or from the owner's best land.

Overview

This amud deals with damage an animal causes indirectly, called צרורות. First the גמרא tests a ברייתא about chickens that pecked a bucket's rope until the bucket fell and broke. It asks whether this proves that we judge damage by the first action (בתר מעיקרא). It tries reading the ברייתא as the opinion of סומכוס, but the end of the ברייתא and רב אשי's open question rule that out. It then reads the ברייתא as the opinion of the רבנן. רב ביבי בר אביי pushes the proof away: the chicken's force carried the bucket the whole way. Next רבא asks a new question: is the half payment for צרורות collected only from the animal's body (מגופו), or from the owner's best land (מעלייה)? The גמרא tries to answer from a ברייתא about a hopping chicken and from a משנה about a dog that carried a loaf with a burning coal and set a stack of grain on fire. Each proof is challenged, and the reading that רבי אלעזר follows סומכוס and רבי יהודה is itself being questioned by רב סמא as the amud ends.

Opening lines

בְּהִתִּיז צְרוֹרוֹת,

It is where it kicked up pebbles,

וּבִפְלוּגְתָּא דְּסוֹמְכוֹס וְרַבָּנַן קָמִיפַּלְגִי.

and they disagree about the dispute of Sumchus and the Rabbis.

תָּא שְׁמַע:

Come and hear:

תַּרְנְגוֹלִין שֶׁהָיוּ מְחַטְּטִין בְּחֶבֶל דְּלִי,

Chickens that were pecking at the rope of a bucket,
