Bava Kamma 74b

בבא קמא ע״ד ע״ב

Learn Bava Kamma 74b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Bava Kamma 74b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

A slave freed for an eye or a tooth, הכחשה and הזמה of witnesses, and a new משנה on when a thief pays ארבעה וחמשה, including admitting to a קנס

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a question about a slave: if his master blinded his eye and knocked out his tooth, why not free him once for both? אביי answers from the פסוק that freedom is 'תחת עינו', for one injury only. רב אידי בר אבין then tries to prove from a משנה that הכחשה is the start of הזמה. The proof is pushed off, because the witnesses may have been made זוממין on the slaughter first. The גמרא shows this was already argued by רבי יוחנן and רבי אלעזר. It proves that רבי אלעזר says such witnesses are not killed. אביי then explains that רבי אלעזר's lashes apply when the 'murdered' man walks in alive. A new משנה lists cases where a thief pays כפל but not ארבעה וחמשה. The גמרא learns from it that a thief who admits can still be made to pay by later witnesses, which goes against רב הונא. רב חסדא challenges רב הונא from the story of רבן גמליאל and his slave טבי, and the debate continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

נִיפּוֹק בְּעֵינוֹ;

let him go free on account of his eye;

הִפִּיל אֶת שִׁינּוֹ – נִיפּוֹק בְּשִׁינּוֹ;

if he knocked out his tooth – let him go free on account of his tooth;

סִימֵּא אֶת עֵינוֹ וְהִפִּיל אֶת שִׁינּוֹ –

if he blinded his eye and knocked out his tooth –

נִיפּוֹק בְּעֵינוֹ וְשִׁינּוֹ!

let him go free on account of his eye and his tooth!
