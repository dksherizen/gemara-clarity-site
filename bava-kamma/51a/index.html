Bava Kamma 51a

בבא קמא נ״א ע״א

Topic

How deep a deadly pit must be, and who is liable when two people dig or own one pit

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a debate between רבא and רב נחמן about whether a fall into a pit under ten טפחים can kill. רב נחמן first explains that a small pit has enough הבל to injure but not to kill. רבא then challenges him from the height of the בית הסקילה, which the גמרא explains as giving a merciful death (מיתה יפה). רבא's second challenge comes from the ten טפחים of מעקה. After a back-and-forth about what counts as a בית, the גמרא gives רב נחמן's real reason: an ox's belly adds four טפחים, so it really falls ten. A new משנה then rules that when two partners each pass their pit without covering it, the second is liable. The גמרא asks how two people can share a pit, and רבי יוחנן answers that they removed the last clump together. The גמרא then explains the מחלוקת between רבי and the רבנן about one digger finishing another's pit, and the sources for each side. The amud ends with two ברייתות that seem to contradict each other about several people working on one pit; the answer comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

לָא, מִשּׁוּם דְּלֵית בֵּיהּ הַבְלָא.

No, it is because there are no fumes in it.

אִי הָכִי, הוּזַּק בּוֹ חַיָּיב?

If so, why is it taught: if it was injured in it, he is liable?

הָא לֵית בֵּיהּ הַבְלָא!

But there are no fumes in it!

אֲמַר לֵיהּ: אֵין הֶבֶל לְמִיתָה,

He said to him: There are no fumes for death,

  30. Resolutionרבי says both words in the פסוק are already needed

  39. ResolutionThe verse itself shows it is speaking about the one who caused death
  40. StatementThree diggers each add ten handbreadths: all are liable
