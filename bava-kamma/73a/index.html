Topic

Whether an עד זומם is disqualified למפרע: the reasons for רבא, the הלכה like אביי, a משנה that challenges it, and a parallel argument between תנאים

Overview

אביי holds that an עד זומם is disqualified למפרע, from the time of his false testimony. רבא holds he is disqualified only from now on. The גמרא finishes the second reason given for רבא, the loss to buyers, and shows two cases where this reason and the חידוש reason give different results. רב פפא ruled like רבא in practice, but רב אשי rules like אביי. This is one of the six cases, remembered as יע״ל קג״ם, where the הלכה follows אביי. A משנה about witnesses to theft and slaughter seems to challenge אביי. After one answer is rejected, the גמרא explains the משנה as witnesses who testified about both at once. The גמרא then tries to match אביי and רבא to an argument between רבי יוסי and the רבנן in a ברייתא. It rejects that match and instead places their argument on whether תוך כדי דיבור כדיבור דמי, a point that continues on the next amud.

מִשּׁוּם פְּסֵידָא דְלָקוֹחוֹת.

Because of the loss to the buyers.

and two others against the other one.
