Topic

The meaning of מבעה, and how the lists of main damages from the משנה, רבי אושעיא (thirteen) and רבי חייא (twenty-four) fit together.

Overview

This amud first finishes an earlier answer for רב: the משנה teaches that a person is responsible to guard his own body. Next, רב מרי suggests that מבעה means damage by water, and רב זביד suggests it means fire. The גמרא rejects both ideas using the grammar of a פסוק and the wording of the משנה. Then רבי אושעיא counts thirteen main categories of damage: four kinds of watchmen, five payments for injuring a person, and the four of our משנה. The גמרא explains why our משנה and רבי אושעיא list things differently. It does this by splitting 'person' into two kinds and by telling apart direct damage from damage that happens on its own. Finally, רבי חייא counts twenty-four categories, adding fines and theft. The גמרא explains that רבי אושעיא leaves out fines, and that רבי חייא separates money a person received in a permitted way from money he took in a forbidden way.

Opening lines

הָכָא נָמֵי, תְּנִי:

Here too, teach:

אָדָם – שְׁמִירַת גּוּפוֹ עָלָיו.

A person – the guarding of his body is upon him.

מַתְקֵיף לַהּ רַב מָרִי,

Rav Mari challenged it:

וְאֵימָא ״מַבְעֶה״ זֶה הַמַּיִם,

But say that 'Maveh' is water,
