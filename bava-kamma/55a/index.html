Topic

Why "טוב" is only in the second דברות, good signs in dreams, and כלאים among birds and sea creatures

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a question: why does the word "טוב" appear in the second דברות but not the first? רבי תנחום בר חנילאי answers that the first לוחות were going to break, and רב אשי explains that if "good" had been written on them, good would have been cut off from ישראל. Next, the גמרא brings dream teachings. A ט in a dream is a good sign, and after several rejected reasons the גמרא explains that the first ט in the תורה is in the word טוב. Seeing the word הספד written in a dream is also a good sign, because its letters hint to חסו and פדאוהו. The גמרא then returns to the משנה's rule that כלאים applies to wild animals and birds. Rooster, peacock and pheasant are כלאים even though they are raised together, and domestic and wild geese are כלאים because of real differences in how they reproduce. ריש לקיש teaches that crossbreeding sea creatures earns מלקות, learned from the word למינהו. רחבה asks whether driving a goat and a fish together is forbidden, and the גמרא rejects רבינא's comparison to seeds sown across the border, without deciding the question. The פרק שור שנגח את הפרה then ends.
