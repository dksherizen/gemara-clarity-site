Bava Kamma 3b

בבא קמא ג׳ ע״ב

Learn Bava Kamma 3b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Bava Kamma 3b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Finding which תולדה רב פפא meant is unlike its אב (צרורות of רגל), and the רב and שמואל dispute over the meaning of מבעה

Overview

רב פפא taught that some of the main damage categories (אבות) have sub-types (תולדות) that are not like them. The גמרא searches for which category he meant. Obstacles in the street turn out to be just like בור or שור. The תולדות of מבעה and of אש also turn out to be just like their אב, so neither can be his example. The answer is רגל: צרורות, pebbles kicked up by a walking animal, pay only חצי נזק by a tradition, yet they still count as a תולדה of רגל. For רב פפא this is because they are paid from the best property. For רבא, who was unsure about that, it is because they are exempt in רשות הרבים. The גמרא then asks what the משנה's word מבעה means. רב says אדם and שמואל says שן, and each brings a פסוק and a grammar argument. In the end, רב reasons that שור already covers all ox damage, while רב יהודה explains that for שמואל, שור means the horn and מבעה means the tooth, which enjoys the damage it does.

Opening lines

וְאִי דְּלָא אַפְקְרִינְהוּ –

And if he did not declare them ownerless –

אִי לִשְׁמוּאֵל, דְּאָמַר:

if according to Shmuel, who says:

כּוּלָּם מִבּוֹרוֹ לָמַדְנוּ,

we learned all of them from his pit,

הַיְינוּ בּוֹר;

this is the same as Pit;
