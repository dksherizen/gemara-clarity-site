Bava Kamma 111a

בבא קמא קי״א ע״א

Topic

Splitting the robber's payment and אשם between priestly shifts, the order of payment before אשם, and why the חומש does not hold back atonement

Overview

The amud opens with the last line of an earlier discussion: a woman is content with any marriage, as ריש לקיש's saying shows. Then the גמרא turns to a robber (גזלן) who gave his payment and his אשם to two different priestly shifts, יהויריב and ידעיה. רבא explains that both items were given during יהויריב's week. רבי יהודה fines ידעיה for taking money out of turn, so the money joins the אשם. The רבנן fine יהויריב for taking the אשם before the money, so the אשם joins the money. Several ברייתות of רבי then apply רבי יהודה's view to special cases. In one, the first אשם was already offered and יהויריב keeps its hide. In others, one shift or both let their week end without asking for the item. רבא then shows from the words 'with which he will atone' that the money must come before the אשם. Finally, a ברייתא about מעילה, together with a two-way comparison to גזל הגר, teaches that the ram and the קרן are both required for atonement but the חומש is not. With that, the פרק הגוזל עצים ends.

Opening lines

דְּמֵינָח נִיחָא לַהּ בְּכֹל דְּהוּ –

that it is satisfactory for her with any arrangement at all

כְּרֵישׁ לָקִישׁ, דְּאָמַר רֵישׁ לָקִישׁ:

in line with Reish Lakish, as Reish Lakish said:

טָב לְמֵיתַב טַן דּוּ,

It is better to sit as two bodies

מִלְּמֵיתַב אַרְמְלוּ.

than to sit as a widow.
