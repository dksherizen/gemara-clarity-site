Bava Kamma 17b

בבא קמא י״ז ע״ב

Learn Bava Kamma 17b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Bava Kamma 17b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Repetitions in the משנה about רגל and שן, the מחלוקת over pebbles (צרורות), and when indirect damage counts as the animal's body

Overview

The גמרא first asks why our משנה seems to repeat itself, saying both 'the foot is מועדת' and 'the animal is מועדת'. רבא answers that the first line teaches the main category (אב) and the second teaches its offshoots (תולדות). רב אשי explains a similar repetition in the eating section: one line is about a חיה and the other about a בהמה. The גמרא then discusses why the משנה lists its cases in this order. Next, a ברייתא lists all the ways a walking animal causes full damage. סומכוס holds that pebbles (צרורות) kicked up by an animal also pay full damage, while the רבנן say half, and several ברייתות about chickens show the same מחלוקת. רבא explains that the רבנן agree an animal's force is like its body, but the half payment for pebbles is a הלכה learned by tradition. He also compares damages to the laws of a זב, which teaches that a cart pulled by a calf counts as the animal's body. Finally, רבא asks whether a vessel that an animal stepped on, and that rolled away and broke, counts as body damage or as pebbles. The גמרא's attempts to answer this continue onto the next amud.

Opening lines

גְּמָ׳ אֲמַר לֵיהּ רָבִינָא לְרָבָא:

GEMARA: Ravina said to Rava:

הַיְינוּ רֶגֶל הַיְינוּ בְּהֵמָה!

Foot is the same as animal!

אֲמַר לֵיהּ: תְּנָא אָבוֹת,

He said to him: He taught the primary categories,

וְקָתָנֵי תּוֹלָדוֹת.

and then he teaches the subcategories.
