Bava Kamma 101b

בבא קמא ק״א ע״ב

Learn Bava Kamma 101b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Bava Kamma 101b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

רבא's two contradictions: whether color (חזותא) counts as a real substance, and whether non-food growth like wood has קדושת שביעית.

Overview

The גמרא brings two contradictions raised by רבא. First, a משנה about ערלה says a garment dyed with ערלה peels must be burned, which shows that color (חזותא) counts as a real substance. But a משנה about corpse blood counts blood in a garment only if a רביעית washes out, so the stain itself seems to count for nothing. רב כהנא answers that the blood case is a special leniency, because דם תבוסה is impure only מדרבנן. Second, a משנה gives dye plants קדושת שביעית, which suggests even inedible growth like wood has the sanctity. A ברייתא says leaves gathered for fuel have none. רבא answers from the word 'לאכלה': the sanctity applies only to things whose benefit comes while they are used up, which excludes wood burned for its coals. The גמרא objects that wood burned for warmth does give benefit while it burns, and רבא's reply continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

רָבָא רָמֵי, תְּנַן:

Rava raised a contradiction: we learned:

בֶּגֶד שֶׁצְּבָעוֹ בִּקְלִיפֵּי עׇרְלָה – יִדָּלֵק;

A garment that one dyed with peels of orla must be burned;

אַלְמָא חֲזוּתָא מִילְּתָא הִיא.

apparently, appearance is a significant matter.

וּרְמִינְהִי: רְבִיעִית דָּם שֶׁנִּבְלְעָה בַּבַּיִת –

And raise a contradiction: a quarter-log of blood that was absorbed in a house —
