Bava Kamma 66a

בבא קמא ס״ו ע״א

Open Bava Kamma 66a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

How a thief acquires a stolen item: שינוי קונה and its sources, how the fines are valued, and whether יאוש acquires.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the discussion of אתנן. It shows how בית שמאי and בית הלל read the words "הם" and "גם" in the פסוק, and the word "גם" stays a difficulty for בית הלל. The גמרא then says their argument is only about whether שינוי קונה. Everyone agrees that כפל and ארבעה וחמשה are paid based on the item as it was when stolen. This seems to refute רב, who values those fines at the time of trial. רבא answers by separating a physical change in the animal from a change in its market price. Next, רבה shows that שינוי קונה comes from the פסוק "אשר גזל" and from a משנה. One משנה is about wood made into vessels; the other is about first-shearing wool that was dyed before it was given to the כהן, which makes the owner exempt. רבה then says the owner's יאוש also lets the thief acquire, but he is unsure whether this is דאורייתא, like a found lost item, or only דרבנן because of תקנת השבים. רב יוסף disagrees and holds that יאוש does not acquire at all. He then begins challenging רבה from a משנה about stolen חמץ after פסח, and the challenge continues on the next amud.
