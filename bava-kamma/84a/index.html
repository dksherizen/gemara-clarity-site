Topic

Proofs that "עין תחת עין" means money, and court cases about appraising injuries

Overview

The גמרא keeps testing proofs that "עין תחת עין" means money and not a real eye. It rejects the proofs of רבי דוסתאי בן יהודה and רבי שמעון בן יוחי. Equality is about the sight taken, and a case where the punishment is impossible can simply be exempt. The schools of רבי ישמעאל and רבי חייא learn money from extra phrases in the פסוקים. The proofs of אביי (risk of death), רב זביד (pain) and רב פפא (healing) are each pushed away. רב אשי finally learns it from the word "תחת" in "תחת אשר ענה", which matches as both a person and a damage case. Even רבי אליעזר's "ממש" turns out to mean money, measured by the attacker's own value. Two court stories follow, about a donkey and an ox that injured children. They show that an animal's owner pays only נזק, measured by a slave appraisal. רבא rules that injuries to people are not collected in בבל, and the גמרא begins asking why on the next amud.
