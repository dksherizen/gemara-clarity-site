Bava Kamma 85b

בבא קמא פ״ה ע״ב

Learn Bava Kamma 85b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Bava Kamma 85b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The injurer's payments: sources for paying צער and ריפוי along with נזק, cases of each payment without נזק, how שבת is measured, and רבא's and רבה's questions about combined and temporary injuries.

Overview

This amud covers what a person who injures someone must pay. First the גמרא finishes showing that the extra wording 'פצע תחת פצע' teaches two things: an injurer is liable even בשוגג or באונס, and he pays צער along with נזק. Next, רב פפא in the name of רבא learns from 'ורפא ירפא' that ריפוי is also paid along with נזק. The גמרא defends this against two challenges by pointing to the exact wording of the פסוק. It then gives examples where צער, ריפוי, שבת and בושת are each owed with no נזק at all. A ברייתא explains why measuring שבת as a 'watchman of cucumbers' is fair: the better jobs were already paid for in the value of the limb. רבא matches each injury to the job the victim can still do, and says that deafness requires paying the victim's whole value. רבא then asks whether separate injuries that end in deafness are paid one by one, and the question ends in תיקו. Finally רבה asks about a weakened hand that will heal, and a proof from a משנה begins that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לְרַבּוֹת שׁוֹגֵג כְּמֵזִיד

To include one who acts by accident like one who acts on purpose

וְאוֹנֶס כְּרָצוֹן!

and one forced by circumstances like one acting willingly!

אִם כֵּן, נִכְתּוֹב קְרָא:

If so, let the verse write:

״פֶּצַע בְּפָצַע״;

'A wound for a wound';
