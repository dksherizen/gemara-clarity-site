Bava Kamma 51b

בבא קמא נ״א ע״ב

Open Bava Kamma 51b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Shared responsibility for a dangerous pit: deadly fumes, partners handing over a בור, ברירה, and acquiring a בור with its bucket

Overview

This amud is about a dangerous pit (בור) that more than one person helped make, and about who is responsible for it. First the גמרא tries to explain two ברייתות about a pit that several people worked on, using the מחלוקת between רבי and רבנן. רב זביד shows that both ברייתות can follow one view, with the idea of deadly fumes (הבל) explaining the plastering case. רבא then adds that building up a pit's rim to ten טפחים is the same as digging. He also asks what happens if the second person undoes his addition, and that question stays a תיקו. Next come rulings about how water and width affect a pit's deadly fumes, including two more תיקו questions and רב אשי's answer about a pit that was widened. The גמרא then asks when a partner who leaves an uncovered בור stops being responsible. It connects that question to the idea of ברירה, which also explains partners who made a נדר about their shared חצר. Finally, רבי אלעזר teaches that handing over the bucket lets a buyer acquire a בור through חזקה. רבי יהושע בן לוי then begins a ruling about selling a house, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לֵימָא הָא רַבִּי,

Shall we say this one is Rabbi,

וְהָא רַבָּנַן?

and that one is the Rabbis?

אָמַר רַב זְבִיד:

Rav Zevid said:

הָא וְהָא רַבָּנַן;

This one and that one are both the Rabbis;

  4. ResolutionThe plastering created the deadly fumes

  19. ResolutionAnother version of רב אשי: It depends on which side the animal fell

  22. ResponseTwo answers: when he leaves it in use, or when he hands over his bucket

  27. StatementSelling a pit: the buyer acquires once the seller hands over his bucket
  28. ChallengeWhy should the bucket matter? Money or חזקה should do the acquiring
  29. ResolutionIt is חזקה, and the bucket counts as saying 'לך חזק וקני'
