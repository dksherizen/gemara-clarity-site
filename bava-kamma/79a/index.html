Bava Kamma 79a

בבא קמא ע״ט ע״א

Topic

What counts as 'selling' a stolen animal, when a theft is complete (lifting, taking out, or pulling), and whether חכמים required משיכה for שומרים.

Overview

The ברייתא ends by listing ways of passing on a stolen animal that count as 'selling' it, such as giving it to הקדש, trading it, or giving it as a gift. In each of these cases the thief pays four or five times. The גמרא explains that the ברייתא teaches two new points. First, here יש שליח לדבר עבירה, because the פסוק joins slaughtering with selling, and selling always involves another person. Second, giving the animal to הקדש counts as a sale. A new משנה then teaches that the fine applies only if the theft was completed: lifting the animal, or getting it out of the owner's property, completes it, but pulling it inside the property does not. The same rule applies when the thief hands the animal to a creditor or a שומר who moves it. אמימר asks whether חכמים required משיכה for שומרים. After a back-and-forth with רב יימר and רב אשי, the גמרא proves from the משנה that they did, and backs this up with רבי אלעזר and a ברייתא. The amud ends as the גמרא begins asking what kind of rental that ברייתא means.

Opening lines

גָּנַב וְהִקְדִּישׁ;

If he stole and consecrated it;

גָּנַב וְהִקִּיף;

stole and sold it on credit;

גָּנַב וְהֶחְלִיף;

stole and exchanged it;

גָּנַב וְנָתַן בְּמַתָּנָה;

stole and gave it as a gift;

The steps (19)

  1. StatementList of transfers that count as selling a stolen animal
  2. QuestionWhat is the ברייתא teaching?
  3. ResponseThe first part teaches that agency works here for a sin
  4. QuestionWhy is this case an exception?
  5. ResponseSlaughtering is compared to selling
  6. ResponseThe last part teaches that הקדש counts as a sale
  7. StatementWhere the theft and slaughter happen decides the fine
  8. StatementThe animal dies while being pulled out
  9. StatementThe thief hands the stolen animal to someone else
  10. Questionאמימר asks whether a watchman's responsibility needs pulling
  11. Proofרב יימר brings a proof from the משנה
  12. RejectionMaybe the one pulling was a thief
  13. ChallengeBut the beginning of the משנה already taught this
  14. ResolutionTwo cases: stealing from the owner and from a שומר
  15. Rejectionרב אשי: there is no difference, so the proof stands
  16. ConclusionThe proof stands: the one pulling is the שומר
  17. Proofרבי אלעזר says the same thing
  18. ProofA ברייתא teaches it too, and adds a rule about renting
