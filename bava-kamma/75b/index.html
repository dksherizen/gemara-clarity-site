Topic

מודה בקנס ואחר כך באו עדים: when a thief's admission exempts him from the fine, and the מחלוקת of סומכוס and the רבנן

Overview

This amud asks whether a thief who admits to an act that carries a fine is freed from that fine, even if witnesses come afterward. First the גמרא reads the משנה closely. An admission that adds no payment of קרן counts only like 'one witness,' but a real admission that makes him owe the קרן exempts him from the fine. Next it brings a ברייתא about a thief who saw witnesses coming and admitted the theft. It tries to prove from its wording that admitting to slaughter is not a real הודאה. That proof is pushed off, because the fine of four or five is one unit, and once the כפל part falls away, the whole fine falls away. Then the גמרא asks whether this question is a מחלוקת between the רבנן and סומכוס in another ברייתא about witnesses proven to be זוממין. One explanation says they argue about whether admitting to slaughter counts. רב אחא בריה דרב איקא says instead that they argue about testimony that cannot be made זוממין, and the גמרא limits the usual rule against such testimony. Finally the גמרא asks why the false witnesses should pay a full כפל, and רבי אלעזר in the name of רב begins to correct the wording of the ברייתא; the corrected wording continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא לָאו הָא קָא מַשְׁמַע לַן:

Rather, is it not that this is what it teaches us:

וְטָבַח עַל פִּי עֵד אֶחָד

and slaughtered based on one witness

אוֹ עַל פִּי עַצְמוֹ –

or based on his own admission
