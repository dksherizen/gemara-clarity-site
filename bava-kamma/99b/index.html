Bava Kamma 99b

בבא קמא צ״ט ע״ב

Topic

Craftsmen who ruin their work: betrothing with wages, the expert butcher, and the money changer's mistake

Overview

The amud opens with רבא's explanation of the argument between רבי מאיר and the רבנן about a craftsman who betroths a woman with his wages. Everyone agrees that a wage is a debt and that betrothing with a debt fails. The argument is only about a case where he also added a jewel of his own: is her mind on the jewel or on the loan? The גמרא then shows that the same split appears in a three-way ברייתא of תנאים. Next, שמואל rules that an expert butcher who ruins the שחיטה must pay, even when he works for free, and explains that he follows רבי מאיר, who holds that one who stumbles is פושע. רבי יוחנן's rulings are reconciled by splitting the cases: a paid butcher pays, and an unpaid expert is exempt. After a story about רב and a badly slaughtered animal, the גמרא turns to a money changer who approves a bad coin. Only a top expert who erred on a brand-new coin is exempt. רבי חייא paid such a woman anyway, acting לפנים משורת הדין, and רב יוסף's source for this begins at the end of the amud and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

רָבָא אָמַר:

Rava said:

דְּכוּלֵּי עָלְמָא יֶשְׁנָהּ לִשְׂכִירוּת

Everyone agrees that wages are owed

מִתְּחִילָּה וְעַד סוֹף,

from the beginning to the end,

וּדְכוּלֵּי עָלְמָא הַמְקַדֵּשׁ בְּמִלְוָה

and everyone agrees that one who betroths with a loan,
