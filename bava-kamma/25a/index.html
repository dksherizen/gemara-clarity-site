Bava Kamma 25a

בבא קמא כ״ה ע״א

Topic

The rule of דיו: רבי טרפון vs. the חכמים on קרן in רשות הניזק, and when a קל וחומר may be limited

Overview

This amud opens in the middle of the משנה. רבי טרפון builds a קל וחומר from רגל to prove that קרן in רשות הניזק pays נזק שלם. The חכמים answer with דיו: a conclusion can't be stronger than its source, so it stays חצי נזק. The גמרא asks how רבי טרפון could reject דיו, since a ברייתא proves it from the Torah with מרים's seven days. It answers that he rejects דיו only where it would leave the קל וחומר teaching nothing. The רבנן and רבי טרפון then argue over the פסוקים about מרים, each reading them to fit his view. רב פפא challenges אביי with a ברייתא about a זב's semen that seems to ignore דיו. אביי shows that דיו there would also cancel the קל וחומר completely. The גמרא then asks which תנא holds that this semen is טמא by carrying, rules out רבי אליעזר and רבי יהושע, and begins quoting a משנה in כלים that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לֹא אָדוּן קֶרֶן מִקֶּרֶן,

I will not derive Goring from Goring;

אֲנִי אָדוּן קֶרֶן מֵרֶגֶל –

I will derive Goring from Trampling:

וּמָה בְּמָקוֹם שֶׁהֵקֵל

And if in a place where [the Torah] was lenient

עַל הַשֵּׁן וְעַל הָרֶגֶל,

about Eating and Trampling,
