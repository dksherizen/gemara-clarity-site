Topic

גזל הגר: who receives the payment, including a female convert and a כהן who is himself the robber

Overview

The גמרא first challenges רבא's explanation, which seemed to let a robber forgive a debt to himself. If that were so, the Torah's law of giving גזל הגר to the כהנים could never happen. רבא answers that the law applies when the robber admits only after the convert has died, because then ה׳ acquires the debt and gives it to the כהנים. רבינא asks whether robbing a female convert is included. A ברייתא shows that she is, and that the word "איש" teaches that only an adult convert must be checked for heirs. Another ברייתא teaches that the payment goes to the כהנים of the משמר on duty. If the robber is himself a כהן, he cannot keep the payment; it is shared among all the כהנים. The גמרא tries to explain this as a case of a כהן טמא, rejects that answer, and instead learns it through a גזירה שוה from a redeemed שדה אחוזה. Finally, a ברייתא teaches that a כהן may offer his own קרבנות any time, and he keeps their service rights and hide even if he is a בעל מום.

מִכְּלָל דְּרַבִּי יוֹסֵי הַגְּלִילִי סָבַר:

By inference, does it follow that Rabbi Yosei HaGelili holds:

אֲפִילּוּ לְנַפְשֵׁיהּ נָמֵי מָצֵי מָחֵיל?!

that he can forgive it even to himself?!

אֶלָּא גֶּזֶל הַגֵּר

about which the Merciful One says it must be given to the priests,
