Bava Kamma 61a

בבא קמא ס״א ע״א

Topic

The end of דוד's question, and the משנה about a fire that crosses a fence, a public road, or a river

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the discussion of what דוד asked during the war with the פלשתים. The opinion that he asked about טמון באש is tested against the field verses, "he would not drink it," and "he poured it out to ה׳," and it answers each challenge. Then a new משנה teaches that if a fire crosses a fence four אמות high, a public road, or a river, the one who lit it is פטור. רב פפא settles a ברייתא that seems to disagree: the two sources count toward four אמות from opposite directions. רבא and רב פפא add that in a thorny field the four אמות are measured from the tops of the thorns. רב and שמואל argue about whether the public-road exemption is about a high flame or a low flame, and a ברייתא supports רב. The גמרא says the public-road rule follows רבי אליעזר. רב and שמואל also disagree whether "river" means a real river or a water channel, which matters when it is dry. Finally, a משנה about פאה leads to two definitions of שלולית, and that discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא לְמַאן דְּאָמַר

But according to the one who says

טָמוּן בָּאֵשׁ קָמִיבַּעְיָא לֵיהּ,

that he was asking about a hidden item in a fire,

מַאי אִיבְּעִי לֵיהּ קְרָאֵי?

why does he need the verses?

אָמַר לָךְ:

He could say to you:
