Bava Kamma 81b

בבא קמא פ״א ע״ב

Learn Bava Kamma 81b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Bava Kamma 81b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

יהושע's ten conditions for the land of ישראל: what each one allows, and why the count is exactly ten

Overview

This amud continues the list of conditions that יהושע set when the tribes received ארץ ישראל. These conditions are public rights that limit what a private owner can do with his land. They include not taking cuttings from the sunny side of a tree, letting the town use a brand-new spring for free, and fishing in the Sea of Tiberias without blocking boats. Other conditions allow relieving oneself behind a fence, walking on field paths, stepping off a rough road, cutting a way out of a vineyard when lost, and burying a מת מצוה where it is found. Along the way the גמרא tells stories about שמואל, רבי and רבי יהודה בן נקוסא stepping off the road. It also explains how the Torah duty of השבת גופו differs from יהושע's added permission to cut through vines. Then the גמרא checks the count of ten. Walking on private paths turns out to be שלמה's teaching, based on a verse in משלי. The rules of רבי יהודה and רבי ישמעאל are not counted, because the list includes only conditions all the חכמים accept.

Opening lines

וְלֹא מִמְּקוֹם שֶׁהוּא רוֹאֶה אֶת הַחַמָּה;

but not from a place that faces the sun,

שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: ״וּמִמֶּגֶד תְּבוּאֹת שָׁמֶשׁ״.

as it is stated: 'And for the precious things of the fruits of the sun.'

וּמַעְיָן הַיּוֹצֵא תְּחִילָּה –

And a spring that emerges for the first time:

בְּנֵי הָעִיר מִסְתַּפְּקִין מִמֶּנּוּ.

the people of the city take supplies from it.
