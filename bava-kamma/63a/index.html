Topic

Which stolen things carry כפל: birds, the word כל as a ריבוי, and what the listed details exclude

Overview

The גמרא asks whether a thief who steals birds must pay כפל. It first tries to leave birds out, because each animal in the פסוק seems to be read on its own. It then shows that שור and חמור together would already cover all animals, so the extra word שה must come to include birds. The word כל in ״על כל דבר פשע״ is a ריבוי, so even non-kosher birds are included. The גמרא challenges this from the מעשר שני פסוק, where a ברייתא reads כל as part of a כלל ופרט וכלל. It gives two answers. First, בכל is a כלל while plain כל is a ריבוי. Second, the כל of כפל is special, because the section already has its own כלל ופרט וכלל, so the extra phrase must be a ריבוי. Finally, the גמרא explains the listed details: they exclude land, slaves, documents and items that are not clearly defined. ״על כל אבדה״ teaches רבי יוחנן's rule about someone who falsely claims a thief stole a lost item, and that rule continues on the next amud.
