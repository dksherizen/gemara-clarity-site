Bava Kamma 94a

בבא קמא צ״ד ע״א

Learn Bava Kamma 94a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Bava Kamma 94a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Does a שינוי (change) cancel an item's status? בית שמאי and בית הלל on a changed אתנן, אביי's list of תנאים, רבא's rejections, and שמואל's ruling about a stolen animal that got weaker

Overview

The גמרא finishes a ברייתא in which בית שמאי forbid a changed אתנן for the מזבח and בית הלל permit it. Each side reads a different word in the same פסוק: 'גם' includes changed forms, and 'הם' excludes them. The word 'גם' stays a קשיא against בית הלל. Next the גמרא finds the sources for the other תנאים on אביי's list who hold that a שינוי does not change an item's status: רבי אליעזר בן יעקב (a ברכה on bread baked from stolen wheat), רבי שמעון בן אלעזר (a robber's improvements, explained by רב ששת and by תקנת השבים), and רבי ישמעאל (פאה given even from dough). רב פפא asks why so many תנאים would follow בית שמאי, and אביי answers that on this point even בית הלל agree. רבא then rejects every proof, showing that each case has its own special reason. רבי יונתן's question and the extra word 'תעזב' back up רבא's point about רבי ישמעאל. Finally, שמואל rules like רבי שמעון בן אלעזר. The גמרא settles the clash with שמואל's other ruling according to רבא, and for אביי it begins quoting a different version of שמואל's words, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דְּמֵאַסְפּוֹרַק:

of Asporak:

בֵּית שַׁמַּאי אוֹסְרִין,

Beit Shammai prohibit,

וּבֵית הִלֵּל מַתִּירִין.

and Beit Hillel permit.

מַאי טַעְמֵיהּ דְּבֵית שַׁמַּאי?

What is the reason of Beit Shammai?
