Bava Kamma 79b

בבא קמא ע״ט ע״ב

Open Bava Kamma 79b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

גנב vs. גזלן and the ארבעה וחמשה payments; the משנה on animals one may not raise

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier discussion: a rental that comes with a שטר must be a rental of land, since movables are not acquired with a document. Next comes רבי אלעזר's ruling that a thief who hid in the woods and then slaughtered or sold an animal pays ארבעה וחמשה. The גמרא explains that he moved the animal with a stick, and that because he was hiding, he counts as a גנב even though people saw him. רבי אבהו and רבי יוחנן then each give an example of a real גזלן: בניהו בן יהוידע, or the בעלי שכם who robbed from ambush. רבן יוחנן בן זכאי explains that the Torah is stricter with a גנב because he fears people more than הקב״ה, and a parable of two feasts supports this. Two sages then give reasons why an ox pays five and a sheep pays four. A new משנה lists animals that may not be raised: small animals in settled parts of ארץ ישראל, chickens in ירושלים or by כהנים, pigs anywhere, unchained dogs, and pigeon traps near settled areas. The גמרא brings ברייתות that explain where small animals are allowed, why large animals were never banned, and the short thirty-day allowance before a רגל.

Opening lines

שְׂכִירוּת דְּמִטַּלְטְלִין,

A rental of movable property?

מִטַּלְטְלִי בְּנֵי שְׁטָרָא נִינְהוּ?!

Is movable property subject to a deed?!

אָמַר רַב חִסְדָּא:

Rav Chisda said:

שְׂכִירוּת דְּקַרְקַע.

A rental of land.

The steps (30)

  1. ChallengeMovables are not acquired with a document
  2. ResolutionIt is a rental of land
  3. StatementA hidden thief who slaughters or sells pays four or five times
  4. ChallengeBut he never pulled the animal
  5. ResolutionHe drove it with a stick
  6. ChallengeIf people saw him, isn't he a robber?
  7. ResolutionHiding from people makes him a thief
  8. QuestionThen what is a robber?
  9. ResponseA robber is like בניהו בן יהוידע
  10. Responseרבי יוחנן's example: the בעלי שכם
  11. QuestionWhy didn't רבי אבהו use the example of the בעלי שכם?
  12. Responseרבי אבהו: people who hide are not robbers
  13. Resolutionרבי יוחנן: they hid only so travelers wouldn't run away
  14. QuestionWhy is the Torah stricter with a thief than a robber?
  15. ResponseThe thief fears people more than God
  16. StatementThe thief acts as if God doesn't see or hear
  17. ProofThree verses about people who think God doesn't see
  18. ProofThe parable of the two feasts
  19. StatementAn ox pays five because work matters
  20. StatementA sheep pays less because of human dignity
  21. StatementNew משנה: where sheep and goats may be raised
  22. StatementNo chickens in Jerusalem, and no chickens for priests
  23. StatementNo pigs anywhere, and dogs only on a chain
  24. StatementPigeon traps must be far from settled areas
  25. StatementA ברייתא on where small animals may be raised
  26. StatementA second ברייתא names specific wilderness areas
  27. StatementLarge animals were never forbidden, since the public could not keep such a decree
  28. StatementWhy large animals were not included in the decree
  29. StatementKeeping small animals for thirty days before a festival or a son's wedding feast
  30. StatementOnce the festival passes, the animal may not be kept any longer
