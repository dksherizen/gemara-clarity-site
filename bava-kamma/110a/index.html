Topic

A כהן who cannot fully serve hands off his קרבן; paying back what was stolen from a גר to the כהנים

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a ברייתא about a כהן with his own קרבן. If he is old or sick, he picks any כהן to offer it, but the עבודה and the hide go to the אנשי משמר. רב פפא explains that he can serve only with great effort. That still counts as עבודה, but eating that way is אכילה גסה, which counts for nothing. רב ששת and רב אשי give similar rules, one for a טמא כהן with a קרבן ציבור and one for a כהן גדול who is an אונן. The גמרא questions each rule and answers. Then a new משנה teaches about robbing a גר who dies with no heirs: the קרן and חומש go to the כהנים, and the קרן must be paid before the אשם is brought. The גמרא brings ברייתות proving that the word 'אשם' in the פסוק means the stolen קרן. It is not the ram, not the חומש, and not כפל. Finally רבא gives rules for paying גזל הגר: not at night, not in halves, and at least a פרוטה for every כהן. He then begins a question that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְאִם הָיָה זָקֵן אוֹ חוֹלֶה –

And if he was old or sick –

נוֹתְנָהּ לְכׇל כֹּהֵן שֶׁיִּרְצֶה,

he gives it to any kohen he wishes,

וַעֲבוֹדָתָהּ וְעוֹרָהּ לְאַנְשֵׁי מִשְׁמָר.

and its service and its hide go to the members of the priestly watch.

הַאי זָקֵן אוֹ חוֹלֶה, הֵיכִי דָמֵי?

This old or sick one, what is the case?
