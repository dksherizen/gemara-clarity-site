Topic

Whether selling and slaughtering are linked for the four-or-five payment (רבי יוחנן vs. ריש לקיש), and why a stolen כלאים animal is included

Overview

This amud finishes ריש לקיש's explanation of רבי שמעון: an animal that is ready to be redeemed is treated as if it were already redeemed. The גמרא then asks why ריש לקיש did not accept רבי יוחנן's approach. It answers with the פסוק 'וּטְבָחוֹ וּמְכָרוֹ', which ties slaughtering to selling. The two אמוראים follow the same views in another argument, about a thief who sells a stolen טריפה according to רבי שמעון. רבי יוחנן challenges ריש לקיש from a ברייתא, and after some back-and-forth over how the ברייתא is worded, ריש לקיש is left with a קשיא. Then the גמרא asks why a thief pays four or five for a כלאים animal when the theft פסוק says שה. רבא explains that the word או includes כלאים in the theft פסוק but leaves it out in the קדשים פסוק, depending on which animals each פסוק lists.

on top of its pyre.
