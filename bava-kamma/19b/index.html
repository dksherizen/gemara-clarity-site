Bava Kamma 19b

בבא קמא י״ט ע״ב

Open Bava Kamma 19b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Pebbles and tail-swinging, the chicken and the string, and what counts as normal eating (שן)

Overview

This amud covers several topics in animal damages. First, the גמרא finishes a debate about pebbles kicked by an animal (צרורות). It asks whether רבי יוחנן's rule that חצי נזק is paid in every domain covers the public domain, and answers either that רבי זירא retracts or that רבי יוחנן meant only קרן. Next, two sages ask about an animal that does damage by swinging its tail. The answer is that this is normal behavior, unless the swinging is excessive. רב עינא's question about an animal swinging its organ ends in תיקו. The גמרא then explains the משנה about a string on a chicken's leg. It concludes that the משנה is a case where the chicken flung the string. It also shows that רב הונא's rule about an ownerless string tied by a person is a rolling בור. A new משנה on שן teaches that an animal eating food fit for it pays full damage in the damaged person's property. In the public domain the owner pays only for the benefit, and the משנה uses examples from the square and the store. The גמרא's discussion of that משנה explains what counts as normal eating, including רב פפא's cat and dates and רב יהודה's case of the donkey, the bread and the basket.

Opening lines

וְהָאָמַר רַבִּי יוֹחָנָן:

But doesn't Rabbi Yoḥanan say:

אֵין חֲצִי נֶזֶק חָלוּק

Half-damage is not divided

לֹא לִרְשׁוּת הַיָּחִיד

neither for the private domain

וְלֹא לִרְשׁוּת הָרַבִּים.

nor for the public domain.

The steps (37)

  2. Resolutionרבי יוחנן meant pebbles that landed on private property

  4. Resolutionרבי זירא takes back his earlier view
