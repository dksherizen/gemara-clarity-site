Bava Kamma 115a

בבא קמא קט״ו ע״א

Open Bava Kamma 115a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Who pays whom when a stolen item is sold and the thief is identified, and where תקנת השוק applies

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה's case of an owner who finds his stolen things with someone else. רב אשי explains that a rumor of theft in the city settles that they were stolen, not sold. Next comes a מחלוקת between רב and רבי יוחנן about a thief who sold an item and was later identified: does the owner claim from the thief or from the buyer? רב יוסף says they do not really disagree, and אביי pushes back. The גמרא then gives three explanations of what they argue about, ending with רב פפא's view that the argument is about תקנת השוק, the Sages' rule protecting buyers. The גמרא then maps out where תקנת השוק applies: pledges, sales and overpriced sales are covered, but paying off an old debt or credit tab is not. Two stories apply these rules: אבימי's stolen cloak, where the הלכה follows רב כהן, and a stolen scroll sold twice, where רבא makes the owner refund the last buyer in full. Finally a new משנה begins about a man who pours out his own wine to save another man's honey.
