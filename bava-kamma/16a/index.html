Topic

How to read the משנה of תם and מועד, crouching on vessels, and identifying the ברדלס

Overview

The גמרא first tries to say the whole משנה follows רבי טרפון. It does this by explaining one line as a shared courtyard, which counts as the injured party's courtyard for שן but as public property for קרן. רב זביד rejects this, because רבי טרפון would make even unusual eating pay in full. So the גמרא reads the משנה as following the רבנן, with missing words filled in. רבינא objects using a later משנה and gives his own fuller reading: five acts start as תם and can become מועד; שן ורגל are always מועד; damage on the injured party's property is a מחלוקת between רבי טרפון and the רבנן; and certain wild animals are also מועד. A ברייתא matches his reading exactly. Next, רבי אלעזר discusses whether crouching on small vessels counts as normal behavior, and the גמרא gives several versions of that discussion. Finally, the גמרא identifies the ברדלס as the female hyena and the צבוע of רבי מאיר as the male. It then starts to look again at רבי מאיר's addition.

Opening lines

כּוּלַּהּ רַבִּי טַרְפוֹן הִיא;

The whole [משנה] is Rabbi Tarfon;

רֵישָׁא – בְּחָצֵר הַמְיוּחֶדֶת לְפֵירוֹת לְאֶחָד מֵהֶן,

the first part is in a courtyard designated for produce for one of them,

וְלָזֶה וְלָזֶה לִשְׁוָרִים;

and for this one and that one for oxen;

דִּלְגַבֵּי שֵׁן הָוְיָא לַהּ חֲצַר הַנִּיזָּק.

so regarding Tooth, it is the injured party's courtyard.
