Bava Kamma 28b

בבא קמא כ״ח ע״ב

Topic

Damage from a broken כד and objects in רשות הרבים: בור's exemptions, הפקר, and liability for אונס

Overview

The amud opens with רב's ruling about a כד that broke in רשות הרבים. The owner pays for the clothes of someone who slips, but not for the person's injury, because the ground hurt him. שמואל holds the reverse. Objects left in the street are learned from בור, and בור is exempt for vessels, so the owner pays for the person but not for the clothes. רב answers that only an object made הפקר is like a בור. רב אושעיא then brings a ברייתא that seems to trouble both of them. The גמרא shows that each can explain it his own way: רב by limiting it to הפקר objects, and שמואל by tying the flask case to רבי יהודה. רבי אלעזר's two versions about a flask breaking on a stone are linked to רבי נתן's rule. Finally the גמרא asks what רבי יהודה in the משנה means by 'with intent.' רבה's answer leads to a challenge that the תורה exempts an אנוס. The ברייתא brought to answer that challenge continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֲבָל הוּא עַצְמוֹ – פָּטוּר;

But [for] the person himself — [the owner is] exempt;

קַרְקַע עוֹלָם הִזִּיקַתּוּ.

the ground of the world injured him.

כִּי אַמְרִיתַהּ קַמֵּיהּ דִּשְׁמוּאֵל,

When I said it before Shmuel,

אָמַר לִי:

he said to me:
