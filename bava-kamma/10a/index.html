Bava Kamma 10a

בבא קמא י׳ ע״א

Learn Bava Kamma 10a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Bava Kamma 10a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Comparing the categories of damage (שור, אש, בור) and their missing stringencies, and the משנה's rule about one who makes possible part of the damage.

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא that compares the main categories of damage, showing that שור and אש, and then בור and אש, are each stricter than the other in some way. The גמרא then asks why the ברייתא did not also say that a שור pays for broken כלים while a בור does not. One answer says the ברייתא follows רבי יהודה, who holds a בור liable for כלים. That fails because of the ברייתא's own fire clause, which the גמרא first reads as being about כלים. The גמרא gives two ways out. Either the ברייתא follows the רבנן and simply left some cases out (תנא ושייר), or 'something not fit for fire' means scorching a field, which fits רבי יהודה. רב אשי then challenges the רבי יהודה reading, and the attempt to defend it is rejected. Next the גמרא turns to the משנה's line about making possible part of the damage. A ברייתא explains it with a pit dug to nine טפחים and finished to ten, and רב פפא shows this fits even רבי's view. Finally, רבי זירא and רב ששת suggest other examples, the five guards and adding wood to a fire. The גמרא rejects the first, and its look at the second continues on the next amud.

חוֹמֶר בְּשׁוֹר מִבְּאֵשׁ,

that for the ox, one pays a ransom,
