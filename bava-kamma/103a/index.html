Bava Kamma 103a

בבא קמא ק״ג ע״א

Topic

Buying in another's name, רב כהנא's flax and ריבית, and the new משנה on returning stolen money after a false oath

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא about someone who buys a field in another person's name. The seller does not have to write the real buyer a second שטר unless the buyer made it a condition (על מנת). The גמרא asks why each half of the ברייתא is needed and gives a 'you might have thought' answer for each. Then comes a story: רב כהנא paid in advance for flax, the price went up, and the seller sold it. רב tells him he may take the money only if the flax was sold as רב כהנא's. The גמרא first wonders whether this follows only בני מערבא, then rejects that idea. It explains instead that this was only a trust deal (אמנה), and רב allows trust deals in produce but not in money. Finally a new משנה begins. A robber who swore falsely must bring the payment to the victim himself, even as far as מדי, if at least a פרוטה of the קרן is still owed. The amud ends as the משנה starts a new case about a false oath over the חומש, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֵין כּוֹפִין אֶת הַמּוֹכֵר

The seller is not compelled

לִמְכּוֹר זִימְנָא אַחֲרִיתִי.

to sell another time.

וְאִם אָמַר לוֹ: ״עַל מְנָת״ –

But if he said to him: 'on condition'

כּוֹפִין אֶת הַמּוֹכֵר לִמְכּוֹר.

the seller is compelled to sell.

The steps (14)

  1. StatementEnd of the ruling: no second sale, unless there was a condition
  2. ChallengeIsn't the first ruling obvious?

  8. QuestionDoes רב's ruling follow only the people of the West?
