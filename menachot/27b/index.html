Topic

How strict are the sprinklings (הזאות) of the פרה אדומה compared with the inner sprinklings and the מצורע's oil? Two ברייתות seem to contradict each other, and רב חסדא settles it as a מחלוקת between רבי יהודה and רבנן.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier point: if one placement of blood is missing, the service does not count. A ברייתא then says the פרה אדומה's sprinklings are invalid if done שלא לשמן or not aimed toward the אהל מועד. For the inner sprinklings and the מצורע's sprinklings, only the wrong intent ruins them. A second ברייתא says aim does not matter for the פרה either. רב חסדא answers that the two ברייתות follow רבי יהודה and רבנן. To show their dispute, the גמרא brings a ברייתא about a pure person who goes too far into the היכל: for רבנן, death begins inside the פרוכת, while for רבי יהודה it begins only before the כפורת. Each side reads ויקרא 16:2 so that the other side's reading would leave a phrase extra. The argument ends up turning on whether the word 'אל' is exact. רבי יהודה reads it as exact, so for him the פרה's sprinkling 'אל נכח' needs exact aim. רבנן read it as not exact. רב יוסף then challenges רבי יהודה from the בית שני, which had no כפורת, and רבה בר עולא answers him. Finally, רבא begins a different answer: both ברייתות follow רבנן. His explanation continues on the next amud.
