Topic

How to read 'לא יצק' and 'פתים מרובות' in the משנה, and whether a זר may pour the oil: the רבנן versus רבי שמעון

Overview

The גמרא tries to prove from an earlier discussion about mixing (בלילה) that 'לא יצק' in our משנה means the oil was not poured at all. That proof is pushed away: 'לא יצק' can still mean a זר poured it. Next the גמרא explains the משנה's line about breaking a מנחה into 'פתים מרובות'. It gives two answers: either the pieces were too many and too small, or they were truly large and count as neither loaves nor proper pieces. Then the גמרא asks whether our משנה, which lets a זר pour, goes against רבי שמעון. He lists pouring among the jobs that belong to the כהנים. רב נחמן offers two ways to make them agree, but רבא knocks down both. So the גמרא concludes that our משנה does not follow רבי שמעון. It then shows the רבנן's source: the כהן's job begins only at קמיצה. רבי שמעון's reply begins at the end of the amud and continues on the next one.

וְהָתְנַן: שִׁשִּׁים נִבְלָלִין,

But didn't we learn: sixty can be mixed,

and we discussed it:

if they cannot be mixed, what of it?
