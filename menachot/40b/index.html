Topic

The decree against סדין בציצית, which garments need ציצית, and 'תעשה ולא מן העשוי'

Overview

The amud opens by searching for the reason the חכמים forbade ציצית with wool תכלת on a linen garment. A קל וחומר knocks down an earlier reason. רבא then explains the worry: a torn linen garment might be sewn with linen thread near the corner, and that thread later used as ציצית, which is invalid because of 'תעשה ולא מן העשוי'. רב זירא adds a second worry about a nighttime garment. רבא rules that a garment made of cloth and leather follows its main body, while רב אחאי follows the corner. רב הונא rules that ציצית tied onto a three-cornered garment before it became four-cornered are פסול. The גמרא tests this against the early חסידים and against רבי זירא's case of tying ציצית onto a garment that already has them. רבא and רב פפא argue over that second challenge. Finally שמואל rules that תכלת is not subject to כלאים even on an exempt garment, and the גמרא begins asking which garment counts as exempt. That discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

חָמֵץ בְּפֶסַח וְיוֹם הַכִּפּוּרִים

Leavened bread on Passover and Yom Kippur

דְּכָרֵת – סָמְכִינַן אַדִּיסְקֵי,

which carry karet, we rely on letters;

הָכָא דַּעֲשֵׂה בְּעָלְמָא

here, which is merely a positive commandment,

לֹא כׇּל שֶׁכֵּן!

is it not all the more so!
