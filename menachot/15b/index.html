Topic

Combined פיגול intent on an offering and its bread, other versions of רבי אלעזר's question, and רבי מאיר's rule that a זבח makes its נסכים and the מצורע's oil פיגול

Overview

Earlier on the daf, רב ruled that if someone slaughters an offering intending to eat a כזית the next day, half from the offering and half from its bread, the bread becomes פיגול but the offering does not. The גמרא asks why we don't use a קל וחומר to keep the bread from becoming פיגול too. It tests this against a ברייתא about seeds planted in another person's vineyard, where the חכמים forbade the seeds but permitted the vines. It then rejects that comparison: the vineyard ruling was only a rabbinic penalty on the wrongdoer, but פיגול is a Torah law. Next the גמרא brings other versions of רבי אלעזר's question. One version is about the two כבשים of שבועות and their שתי הלחם. In it רבי אלעזר explains that his doubt is only about the bread, and the same קל וחומר discussion is repeated. The גמרא also explains how this version relates to the תודה version, based on the waving that ties the כבשים to their bread. A third version, from רבי אבא זוטי, asks whether 'its fellow' means the other lamb or the bread; רב's proof from a משנה is pushed aside. Finally a new משנה teaches, in the name of רבי מאיר, that a זבח can make its נסכים פיגול but not the reverse. The גמרא explains this with ברייתות about נסכים and about the מצורע's לוג of oil. In both, רבא says that רבי מאיר holds they become fixed to the offering at the שחיטה, like the לחמי תודה.
