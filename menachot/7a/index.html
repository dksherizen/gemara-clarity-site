Topic

How a returned קומץ avoids being sanctified by a כלי שרת, and whether a קומץ may be taken from a vessel resting on the ground

Overview

The amud opens with a קשיא: when the כהן puts the קומץ back into the vessel holding the מנחה, that כלי שרת should sanctify it and make it פסול. רבי יוחנן answers that כלי שרת sanctify only with intent (מדעת). The גמרא then explains that vessels do not sanctify פסולין to be offered, but they do sanctify them so that they become disqualified. רב עמרם gives a different answer: the קומץ was put back on top of the flour and never entered the vessel. After two more rounds, his case becomes a level-full bowl where the handful slides into the dent by itself, as if a monkey put it there. רבי ירמיה asks why the גמרא did not simply say the vessel was on the ground. This opens the question of whether a קומץ may be taken from a vessel resting on the ground. רבי זירא tells the story of אבימי, who always answered רב נחמן that another כהן lifts the vessel, and he defends that answer against a משנה. Finally the question is put to רב ששת, who points to how the לחם הפנים and its bowls are changed on שבת, and his proof continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְכִי מַהְדַּר לֵיהּ לְקוֹמֶץ לְדוּכְתֵּיהּ,

And when he returns the handful to its place,

(תִּקְדּוֹשׁ) [לִקְדּוֹשׁ] וְלִפְסוֹל.

it should become sanctified and become disqualified.

אָמַר רַבִּי יוֹחָנָן, זֹאת אוֹמֶרֶת:

Rabbi Yoḥanan said: That is to say:

כְּלֵי שָׁרֵת אֵין מְקַדְּשִׁין

service vessels do not sanctify
