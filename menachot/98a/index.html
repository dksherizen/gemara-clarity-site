Topic

Measurements of the מזבח and the אמה, respect for kings, the arrangement of the לחם הפנים, and the direction of the ארון and its poles

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a point about the מזבח: its קרנות are measured with the small five-טפח אמה. Adding up the מזבח in טפחים (58 total, 29 half, 23 from the top down to the סובב) shows the סובב is six טפחים above the middle. That fits a ברייתא, and the wording of the פסוק supports it too. The גמרא then sets the medium אמה at six טפחים, proving it from the שלחן. It also explains why the מקדש kept two longer measuring rods in שושן הבירה: so workers would never take הקדש money they had not earned. This leads to why שושן הבירה was pictured on the eastern gate, to the rule of respecting the king, and to the healing leaves of the future river. Next comes a ברייתא showing that every word about the לחם הפנים is needed to teach two rows of six. רבי rules that with rows of seven the top loaf is ignored, because he reads 'על' as 'next to'. Finally, a ברייתא teaches that the ארון lay across the width of the בית, which we know from its poles, and begins explaining how the poles were 'seen' yet 'not seen outside'. That answer comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

לָא שְׁנָא הָכִי

It makes no difference this way

וְלָא שְׁנָא הָכִי.

and it makes no difference that way.

מִזְבֵּחַ כַּמָּה הָוֵי?

How much is the altar?

חַמְשִׁין וּתְמָנְיָא.

Fifty-eight.
