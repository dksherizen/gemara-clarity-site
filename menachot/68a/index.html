Topic

Why רבי יהודה does not fear eating חדש before the עומר, and when חדש becomes permitted

Overview

The amud opens by explaining why רבי יהודה is not afraid that someone handling new grain (חדש) before the עומר will eat it, even though he worries about this with חמץ. The first answer is that harvesting, grinding and sifting are done in unusual ways, which remind the person. A בית השלחין, where normal harvesting is allowed, knocks that answer down. אביי then says people keep away from חדש all year, but not from חמץ. רבא accepts this for רבי יהודה, and adds that the רבנן do not worry about חמץ because a person searching for it in order to burn it will not eat it. רב אשי suggests that only parched flour was prepared, but the גמרא rejects his answer as a mistake. A new משנה teaches that the עומר permits חדש, and that after the חורבן רבן יוחנן בן זכאי forbade the whole day, which רבי יהודה says is Torah law. In the גמרא, רב and שמואל say dawn permits חדש only when there is no בית המקדש. רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש say dawn permits it always, and they read every source that ties the permission to the עומר as 'למצוה', the ideal way.
