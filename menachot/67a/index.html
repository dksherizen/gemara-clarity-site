Topic

Which kinds of kneading (גלגול) exempt dough from חלה: kneading while the dough is הקדש, and kneading while it belongs to a גוי

Overview

The גמרא carries the rules about smoothing grain (מירוח) over to kneading dough (גלגול) for חלה. A משנה proves that dough kneaded while it was הקדש stays exempt from חלה even after it is redeemed. רבא then asks about dough kneaded while a גוי owned it, and a משנה about a גר shows that it is exempt. The גמרא asks whose view that משנה follows. It might be everyone's, because only the מעשרות verses have an extra ״דגנך״. Or it might be only רבי יוסי and רבי שמעון, since רבי מאיר and רבי יהודה could learn חלה from מעשרות through ״ראשית״ ״ראשית״. רבא wishes to see the answer in a dream, and then rules that גלגול גוי follows מירוח גוי for each opinion. רב פפא and רבינא challenge him from ברייתות that obligate a גוי's תרומה but exempt his חלה. The גמרא answers that the obligation on the גוי's grain there is only מדרבנן, a גזירה because of wealthy people.

Opening lines

גִּלְגּוּל הֶקְדֵּשׁ פּוֹטֵר,

Kneading of consecrated dough exempts it,

דִּתְנַן: הִקְדִּישָׁהּ עִיסָּתָהּ עַד שֶׁלֹּא גִּלְגְּלָה

As we learned: If she consecrated her dough before she kneaded it

וּפְדָאַתָּה – חַיֶּיבֶת,

and she redeemed it, she is obligated;

מִשֶּׁגִּלְגְּלָה וּפְדָאַתָּה – חַיֶּיבֶת,

if she consecrated it after she kneaded it and redeemed it, she is obligated;

The steps (17)

  1. StatementKneading while consecrated exempts dough from חלה
  2. ProofProof from a משנה about consecrated dough
  3. Questionרבא asks about dough kneaded by a גוי
  4. ResponseA משנה about a גר answers the question
  5. QuestionWhose opinion is this משנה?
  6. ResponseFirst possibility: everyone agrees, even רבי מאיר and רבי יהודה
  7. ResolutionWhy מעשרות is different: the extra ״דגנך״
  8. ResolutionTwo restrictions in a row include even a גוי
  9. ResolutionBy חלה, both mentions of 'your dough' are already used
  10. ResponseOr maybe the משנה follows only רבי יוסי and רבי שמעון
  11. Statementרבא wishes to see the answer in a dream
  12. Conclusionרבא: גלגול follows מירוח
  13. Challengeרב פפא quotes a ברייתא about a גוי's firstborn donkey and חלה
  14. Challengeרב פפא's point: here is a תנא who splits the two cases
  15. Challengeרבינא brings a second ברייתא about חלה in the Land and תרומה outside it
  16. Challengeרבינא's point: again a תנא splits smoothing and kneading
  17. ResolutionThe grain obligation is only rabbinic, a decree because of wealthy people
