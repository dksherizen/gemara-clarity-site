Topic

The anointed measuring vessels, and how the lamps of the מנורה were built and cleaned

Overview

First the גמרא explains why the רביעית and חצי לוג measuring vessels had to be anointed as holy כלי שרת. The uses the משנה lists for them did not seem to need a holy vessel. רבי חייא answers that the רביעית measured oil for the חביתי כהן גדול. רבי's son רבי שמעון answers that the חצי לוג measured oil for each lamp of the מנורה, and רבי praises them both. Next, רבי teaches that a lamp that went out is cleaned and relit. רבי ירמיה proves that it gets the full original amount of oil, and another teaching backs him up. Then רב ששת says the lamps were one piece with the מנורה but could be tipped for cleaning. The גמרא connects this to a מחלוקת among תנאים about whether the lamps came from the same ככר of gold as the מנורה, and to how they read the פסוק. The amud ends as the גמרא explains why 'all these vessels' is needed, even though the Torah already says the tools are gold.

Opening lines

חוּץ הוּא,

it is [done] outside,

וְאִי נָזִיר –

and if [it was for] a nazirite —

לֶחֶם נָזִיר בִּשְׁחִיטַת אַיִל

the bread of a nazirite, through the slaughter of the ram

הוּא דְּקָדֵישׁ.

is it consecrated.
