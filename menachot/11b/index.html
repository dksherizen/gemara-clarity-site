Menachos 11b

מנחות י״א ע״ב

Topic

How much frankincense (לבונה) must remain to be burned, when extra frankincense disqualifies, and the start of the משנה on wrong intentions during the קמיצה.

Overview

The גמרא begins by settling a contradiction in רבי שמעון's view about frankincense (לבונה) that shrank. It gives two answers: either the ברייתא really says 'a pinch', or one ruling is about frankincense with a מנחה and the other is about frankincense on its own. רבי יצחק בר יוסף, quoting רבי יוחנן, then lists three opinions on how much must be left to burn: the full קומץ (רבי מאיר), two pinches (רבי יהודה), or one pinch (רבי שמעון). All three come from the same פסוק. He adds that the argument is only about frankincense brought with a מנחה. Frankincense brought by itself, and the frankincense in the בזיכין of the לחם הפנים, must stay complete according to everyone, though רבי אמי and רבי יצחק נפחא argue about frankincense brought by itself. Next, רמי בר חמא explains that too much frankincense disqualifies only when a whole second קומץ was set aside. He rules that extra frankincense becomes fixed with the offering only at the קמיצה, or at the actual removal of the בזיכין. A new משנה then begins about wrong intentions during the קמיצה. Planning to eat or burn the offering outside makes it פסול without כרת, and the list of 'next day' intentions continues onto the next amud.

Opening lines

וְהָתַנְיָא (רַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן אוֹמֵר):

But isn't it taught (Rabbi Shimon says):

קוֹמֶץ לְבוֹנָה שֶׁחָסַר כׇּל שֶׁהוּא – פָּסוּל.

A handful of frankincense that is missing any amount at all is disqualified.

תְּנִי:

Teach it this way:

קוֹרֶט לְבוֹנָה שֶׁחָסַר כׇּל שֶׁהוּא – פָּסוּל.

A pinch of frankincense that is missing any amount at all is disqualified.
