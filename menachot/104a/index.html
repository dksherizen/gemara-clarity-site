Topic

Is the blood of a נבילה (an animal that died without proper שחיטה) טמא? The question is resolved through the ruling of רבי יהודה. Then a new משנה begins about how much wine one may pledge as a נסכים offering.

Overview

The amud opens as רבי ביבי explains that his mind is too unsettled to rule on whether the blood of a נבילה is טמא. רב יוסף answers that רבי יהודה, the decisor of the נשיא's household, ruled by his own tradition. That tradition says בית הלל, unusually the stricter side here, hold that such blood is טמא. רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה adds that this applies only to a רביעית, since that much blood can harden into a כזית. A new משנה then teaches that one may pledge 3, 4, 6, or more לוג of wine for נסכים, but not 1, 2, or 5. The גמרא asks whether there is a קבע for נסכים, using the case of someone who brings 5 לוג. אביי's proof from the six collection horns is rejected. רבא proves from a ברייתא that there is no קבע, and רב אשי's challenge from the משנה is answered. Finally אביי begins a new question: if there is a קבע, what is the law for eleven לוג? That question continues on the next amud.
