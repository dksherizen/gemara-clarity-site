Topic

How a vessel joins (צירוף כלי) separated parts of a מנחה, for טומאה and for קמיצה

Overview

This amud deals with the rule that a כלי joins (מצרף) everything inside it for קדשים. An עשרון of flour was split into two halves and placed in one bowl without the halves touching. רב כהנא rules that a טבול יום who touches one half disqualifies both, because the משנה says the vessel is 'מצרף', not 'מחבר'. He adds two limits: a vessel only joins what needs the vessel, and only a כלי חרס passes on טומאה through its airspace. רב כהנא then asks whether this joining is strong enough, from the Torah, to let a קומץ taken from one half count for the other. Two proofs are brought, one from a משנה about mixed מנחות and one from a ברייתא on 'וְהֵרִים מִמֶּנּוּ'. Both are pushed off, because each could be about parts that still touch somewhere, so the question stays unresolved. רבי ירמיה asks whether a vessel's joining and a water connection can work together, and his question ends in תיקו. Finally, רבא asks whether an already-impure half is 'full' of טומאה (שבע לו טומאה). אביי starts to challenge that idea from a משנה about an impure sheet, and his proof continues on the next amud.
