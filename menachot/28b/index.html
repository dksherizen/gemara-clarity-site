Topic

משה's trumpets, the materials of the מנורה, and the מנורה's measurements and decorations

Overview

The גמרא finishes a ברייתא: every כלי that משה made stayed valid for later generations, except the חצוצרות. To find out why, it looks at the word 'לך'. That word alone cannot be the reason, since the ארון also says 'לך'. The answer is that the trumpets have 'לך' written twice. Next comes a ברייתא about what the מנורה may be made from. רב פפא and רב יוסף disagree about what רבי and רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה argue over: cheap metals, or wood. רב יוסף proves from two more ברייתות that any metal is fine and only wood is disputed. Along the way we learn that one may not copy the מקדש's seven-branched מנורה. Then שמואל, quoting an elder, gives the מנורה's full blueprint: 18 טפחים tall, with 22 גביעים, 11 כפתורים and 9 פרחים, all of them required. The גמרא begins checking these numbers against the פסוקים, and the discussion continues on the next amud.
