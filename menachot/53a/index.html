Topic

The source that a מנחה must be מצה and guarded from חמץ, followed by the story of רבי פרידא and רבי עזרא

Overview

רבי אמי quotes פסוקים showing that a מנחה should be made as מצה. רבי פרידא says he wants proof that this is לעכב, meaning the מנחה is invalid without it. רבי אמי answers from 'לא תאפה חמץ'. Then רב חסדא (שיאור), רב נחמן בר יצחק (boiled dough) and רבינא (maybe it is only a לאו) each show that the פסוק could mean something else. So the גמרא brings a ברייתא instead: the word 'תהיה' in 'מצה תהיה' makes מצה a firm requirement. רבי פרידא then asks for the source that מנחה dough must be guarded from becoming חמץ. רבי אמי reads the same word 'תהיה' as 'החייה', and the גמרא explains that the extra wording teaches both lessons. A story follows: רבי פרידא says Torah, not lineage, makes a guest worthy, and he lets in רבי עזרא. The two then trade דרשות on the words אדיר and ידיד, and the ידיד דרשה continues on the next amud.
