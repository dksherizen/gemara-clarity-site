Topic

Which offerings need נסכים: one animal for a vowed עולה, the תודה, the 'not already obligated' rule, separate נסכים for every animal, and the מצורע's offerings.

Overview

This amud continues the ברייתא about נסכים (the flour, oil and wine that come with certain offerings) and the words of the פסוקים in במדבר 15. First, the גמרא explains how רבי יונתן and רבי יאשיה each know that a person who vowed an עולה may bring just one animal, either cattle or sheep. Next, it explains why a תודה needs its own source for נסכים: we might have thought its four kinds of bread take their place. Then it shows that the word 'עולה' narrows a כלל ופרט וכלל. The result is that a קרבן needs נסכים only if the person was not already obligated to bring it, so cases like ולדות קדשים and תמורה are included, while בכור, מעשר and פסח are not. After that, the גמרא goes through each extra 'או' in the passage, and each one teaches that two offerings brought together never share one set of נסכים, in any combination. Finally, it begins the source for the משנה's rule that a מצורע's חטאת and אשם need נסכים, and raises a challenge that is answered on the next amud.

Opening lines

הוֹאִיל וּכְתִיב ״וּמִן הַצֹּאן״,

Since it is written: 'And of the flock,'

כְּמַאן דִּכְתִיב ״יַחְדָּו״ דָּמֵי.

it is as though 'together' were written.

וּלְרַבִּי יֹאשִׁיָּה, דְּאָמַר:

And according to Rabbi Yoshiya, who said:

אַף עַל גַּב דְּלָא כְּתִיב ״יַחְדָּו״

even though 'together' is not written,
