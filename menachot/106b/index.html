Topic

Explaining the רבי/רבנן dispute about a doubtful large מנחה vow, then the minimum amounts for pledges of wood, לבונה and metals

Overview

The amud opens by asking why רבי and the רבנן disagree about a person who is unsure how big a מנחה he vowed. One problem is raised first: burning an extra handful might break בל תקטירו. It is answered with רבי אליעזר's rule that something may be burned לשום עצים, as fuel. רב אחא's idea that the dispute is about רבי אליעזר's rule is rejected by רב אשי. רבא then explains the dispute through the oil argument between רבי אליעזר בן יעקב and the רבנן. רב אשי explains it as the question of owing a small offering and bringing a big one, and the גמרא shows why that argument must be taught in two places. After a סימן, a new משנה gives the minimum for pledges of wood, לבונה, gold, silver and copper, and lists five cases measured by a קומץ. The גמרא finds sources for wood and לבונה pledges. It then rules that a pledge 'for the מזבח' means לבונה, and begins testing that rule against the עולה and the bird עולה. That discussion continues on the next amud.
