Topic

When do the loaves and their offering become bound (זיקה)? Does שחיטה or תנופה create the bond, and why a תודה stays valid when its bread is ruined.

Overview

This amud picks up in the middle of רבי יוחנן's teaching about the שתי הלחם and the sheep of שבועות. Once the loaves and the sheep are bound together, losing one makes the other unfit, and the bond is made by the שחיטה. עולא reports a question from ארץ ישראל: does the earlier תנופה also create the bond? A proof from רבי יוחנן's words is pushed aside, and the question ends with תיקו. רבי יהודה בר חנינא tries again from where the verse 'קדש יהיו לה׳ לכהן' is placed, but רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע shows that the verse describes the items from the start, so it proves nothing. Next, the גמרא challenges רבי יוחנן with a ברייתא about a תודה. That ברייתא says that when a loaf becomes broken, goes outside ירושלים, or becomes טמא after the שחיטה, the animal is still offered and eaten. The גמרא answers that a תודה is different: the Torah calls it שלמים, so it can be brought without bread. At the end, רבי ירמיה assumes that תנופה does create a bond and begins asking what happens if the loaves are lost; his question continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שֶׁאִם הוּזְקְקוּ זֶה לָזֶה

that if they became bound to each other

שֶׁמְּעַכְּבִין זֶה אֶת זֶה.

that they hold each other back.

(וְאֵיזֶה הוּא) [וְאֵיזוֹ הִיא] זִיקָּה שֶׁלָּהֶן –

And what is their bond?

שְׁחִיטָה.

Slaughter.

  13. ChallengeThe ברייתא's last case: a loaf that became impure
