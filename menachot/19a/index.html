Topic

רבי שמעון's source for a כהן pouring the oil, and רב's rule that תורה and חוקה make a step לעכב

Overview

The גמרא first looks for רבי שמעון's source for requiring a כהן to pour the oil on a מנחה. Reading 'הכהנים' in both directions fails, because אביי shows that רבי שמעון reads a פסוק only forward. So the גמרא says his source is the ו in 'וְהֵבִיאָהּ', which links back to the pouring. It then explains why that ו does not make שחיטה need a כהן: שחיטה is linked to סמיכה. A קל וחומר from זריקה and a פסוק about יום הכפורים show that the owner does not have to slaughter either. Next comes a new topic: רב's rule that the words תורה and חוקה make a step לעכב. Using the cases of נזיר, תודה, מצורע, יום הכפורים and the other קרבנות, the גמרא arrives at this reading: חוקה alone is enough, but תורה alone is not. It then tests this rule against מנחה and לחם הפנים, and begins explaining why מנחה is different from the words 'מגרשה ומשמנה'. That explanation continues on the next amud.
