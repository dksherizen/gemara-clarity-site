Menachos 44b

מנחות מ״ד ע״ב

Topic

Which parts of the קרבנות hold each other back: the מנחה and נסכים, the blood placements, and the animals of the מוספין. Also, the order of the מנחה and the נסכים.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the earlier discussion of תפילין with a פסוק from ישעיהו about life and healing. A new משנה then teaches that the מנחה (flour and oil) and the נסכים (wine) do not hold each other back. It also teaches that the blood placements on the outer מזבח do not hold each other back. The גמרא brings a ברייתא where the רבנן and רבי disagree about the order of the מנחה and the נסכים. After trying several פסוקים, the גמרא concludes that everyone agrees the מנחה comes first when the two are brought with a זבח. They argue only when the מנחה and נסכים come by themselves. There, רבי puts the נסכים first because the שירה is sung over them. A ברייתא then shows from a פסוק in דברים that one blood placement on the outer מזבח is enough for כפרה. A new משנה teaches that the different animals of the מוספין do not hold each other back, and רבי שמעון prefers one bull with its נסכים over many bulls without them. The גמרא explains that this משנה means the מוספין of ראש חודש and שבועות in ספר במדבר, not those of סוכות, because the תורה says 'כַּמִּשְׁפָּט' about סוכות.

Opening lines

״ה׳ עֲלֵיהֶם יִחְיוּ

"The Lord is upon them, they will live,

וּלְכׇל בָּהֶן חַיֵּי רוּחִי

and altogether therein is the life of my spirit;

וְתַחֲלִימֵנִי וְהַחֲיֵנִי״.

and have me recover, and make me to live."

מַתְנִי׳ הַסּוֹלֶת וְהַשֶּׁמֶן

MISHNA: The fine flour and the oil
