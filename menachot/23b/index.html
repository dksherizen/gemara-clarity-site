Topic

When is מין במינו בטל according to רבי יהודה, and do we check the מבטל (רב חסדא) or the בטל (רבי חנינא)?

Overview

The גמרא wants to know which opinion stands behind רב חסדא and רבי חנינא, who argue about mixtures of נבילה and שחוטה. It bases them on רבי חייא's teaching that נבילה and שחוטה are בטל in each other. It then reads רבי יהודה this way: מין במינו is not בטל only when one item can actually become like the other. רב חסדא checks whether the larger item (the מבטל) can become like the smaller one. רבי חנינא checks whether the smaller item (the בטל) can become like the larger one. The גמרא tests both views against several cases in the משנה about mixed מנחות, קומץ, and שירים. Each time, it answers with רבי זירא's rule: since הקטרה is said about both the קומץ and the שירים, they do not cancel each other. A ברייתא about seasoned מצה is also deflected: it speaks of a mixture that is mostly מצה. A new story then begins, in which רב כהנא hears the sons of רבי חייא discussing a divided עשרון; that discussion continues on the next amud.
