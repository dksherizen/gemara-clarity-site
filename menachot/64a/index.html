Topic

How much work may be done on שבת when it is permitted: the עומר's three סאה, an extra slaughtered offering, intention versus action, and picking figs for a sick person.

Overview

Our משנה has רבי ישמעאל saying the עומר came from five סאה of barley on a weekday but only three on שבת, while the חכמים say three in both cases. The גמרא looks for another sage who shares רבי ישמעאל's idea. It tries רבי ישמעאל בנו של רבי יוחנן בן ברוקא, then רבי חנינא סגן הכהנים (one reaper on שבת), then רבי יוסי (witnesses of a clearly seen new moon do not travel on שבת). Each comparison rests on the idea כיון דאפשר לא טרחינן: if less work will do, we don't do extra on שבת. Each comparison is pushed away, because the cases differ. Next comes a ruling about someone who slaughtered two communal חטאות on שבת when only one was needed. A contradiction in רבה's words is resolved. רבינא then asks whether we judge by a person's intention or by what actually happened, and רב אשי answers with two versions based on the case of a net that caught fish and a drowning child. The amud ends with רבה's ruling that everyone who picks figs for a sick person on שבת is exempt. רבא then asks whether to pick two figs on two stems or three on one stem, and the גמרא begins to answer that we bring the three.
