Topic

The אשם מצורע and its נסכים, then the משנה about measuring vessels in the מקדש and whether their overflow is holy

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the reason behind רבי יוחנן's ruling. A burnt offering spoiled for its original purpose can still become a voluntary עולה. There is no voluntary אשם, so an אשם מצורע slaughtered שלא לשמו keeps its identity and still needs נסכים. A ברייתא backs him up. A new משנה then teaches that the measuring vessels in the מקדש were used heaped, except the כהן גדול's. It also teaches that overflow from liquid measures is holy, while overflow from dry measures is not, and רבי עקיבא and רבי יוסי each give a different reason. The גמרא explains that the משנה follows רבי מאיר by reading 'all measures' as 'all measurings.' It then shows that the three views of the overflow rule differ over where each vessel was anointed. When the גמרא asks why overflow becomes holy without intent, רב says כלי שרת make things holy even without intent. רבינא answers instead that it is a rabbinic גזירה, and he defends that answer against רבי זירא's challenge from the לחם הפנים. Finally the גמרא starts defining מותר נסכים from a משנה in שקלים, and that discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אִי לָא חֲזוּ לְעוֹלַת חוֹבָה

If they are not fit for an obligatory burnt offering

קׇרְבִי לְעוֹלַת נְדָבָה,

they are offered as a voluntary burnt offering,

אֶלָּא הָכָא,

but here,

אִי לָא מוֹקְמֵית לֵיהּ בְּמִילְּתֵיהּ –

if you do not keep it in its original status,
