Topic

The מנורה's decorations, height, gold and heavenly design; then the rules of letters in a מזוזה

Overview

The גמרא finishes counting the decorations of the מנורה: 22 גביעים, 11 כפתורים, and 9 פרחים, with the ninth flower coming from a פסוק in במדבר. רב says the מנורה is nine טפחים tall. When challenged from the three-step stone the כהן used, he explains that he meant only from the branches up. Next, 'מכלות זהב' is explained to mean that שלמה's ten מנורות used up all his pure gold, refined from a thousand כיכרים down to one each. The גמרא then asks why the מנורה is called 'טהורה.' Since a metal vessel can obviously become טמא, the word must teach that its design came from Heaven. This leads to teachings about fiery models shown to משה, גבריאל showing the מנורה, and things ה׳ pointed out with His finger. Finally the גמרא returns to the משנה about the מזוזה: even one letter not surrounded by blank parchment makes it unfit. It then discusses a hole in the letter ה״י and begins a story about אגרא that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְתַמְנֵי סְרֵי דְּקָנִים,

and the eighteen of the branches,

הָא עֶשְׂרִין וְתַרְתֵּין.

this makes twenty-two.

כַּפְתּוֹרִין נָמֵי אַחַד עָשָׂר כַּפְתּוֹרִין:

The knobs too: eleven knobs:

תְּרֵי דִּידַהּ, וְשִׁשָּׁה דְּקָנִים,

two of its own, and six of the branches,
