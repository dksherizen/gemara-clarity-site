Topic

Offerings that come להכשיר: why the עומר and the אשם נזיר and אשם מצורע are פסול שלא לשמן, and רבי ירמיה's distinction between מכפרים and מכשירים

Overview

The amud opens at the end of a statement about offerings that have a special job. It says a מנחת העומר whose קמיצה was done שלא לשמה is פסולה. It also says an אשם נזיר or אשם מצורע slaughtered שלא לשמן is פסול, because they came להכשיר and failed. The גמרא asks why our משנה does not list the עומר among its exceptions. It answers that the משנה deals only with individual, independent מנחות that have no fixed date. Next, the גמרא asks why the opening משנה of זבחים does not list these two guilt offerings. It answers that other kinds of אשם are כשר, so the משנה could not list 'אשם' as one blanket exception. The גמרא then asks why making fit should differ from atoning. רבי ירמיה answers that the Torah separates מכפרים from מכשירים: only מכפרים can be brought after the owner's death, as shown by the woman after childbirth. רבי יהודה בריה דרבי שמעון בן פזי objects from a משנה where a dead נזיר's עולה and שלמים are still brought. רב פפא answers that רבי ירמיה meant only a fixed הכשר, and the נזיר's offerings are a non-fixed הכשר. The explanation of why the נזיר's offerings count as non-fixed continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וַאֲשַׁם מְצוֹרָע

And the guilt offering of a leper,

שֶׁשְּׁחָטָן שֶׁלֹּא לִשְׁמָן – פְּסוּלִין,

that one slaughtered them not for their sake, they are disqualified,

הוֹאִיל וּבָאוּ לְהַכְשִׁיר

since they came to render fit,

וְלֹא הִכְשִׁירוּ.

and they did not render fit.
