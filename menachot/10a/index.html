Topic

The מצורע's oil and the rules for when a עבודה must be done with the right hand

Overview

This amud deals with the purification of a מצורע and then with the question of when a עבודה must be done with the right hand. First the גמרא explains why the תורה names the thumb and toe for the oil: one mention allows the sides (צדדין), and the other rules out the 'sides of the sides' (צידי צדדין). It then shows that both 'on the blood of the אשם' and 'on the place of the blood of the אשם' are needed. The oil goes on that spot even if the blood was wiped off, and blood that is still there is not a חציצה. רבא says the word 'right' for the oil is extra, so it teaches three גזירות שוות: right hand for קמיצה, right foot for חליצה, and right ear for רציעה. The extra 'left' teaches that the כהן's right hand is invalid for holding the oil. The rest of the repeated words come with a פרשה that is repeated for its one new detail. Then ריש לקיש teaches that 'finger' and 'כהונה' mean the right hand. רבא explains that either word is enough. After אביי's challenge, the גמרא limits this to an act that holds back כפרה. For רבי שמעון, 'finger' works alone but 'כהונה' needs 'finger', and the mention of a כהן teaches that he must serve in his priestly state.

Opening lines

(דְּהָא כְּתִב ״עַל דַּם הָאָשָׁם״!),

After all, it is written: "On the blood of the guilt offering"!

חַד לְהַכְשִׁיר צְדָדִין,

One is to make the sides valid,

וְחַד לִפְסוֹל צִידֵּי צְדָדִין.

and one is to make the sides of the sides invalid.

״עַל דַּם הָאָשָׁם״,

"On the blood of the guilt offering"
