Menachos 8b

מנחות ח׳ ע״ב

Topic

Whether the parts of a מנחה are sanctified separately in a כלי שרת (רבי חנינא, רב and שמואל), and whether a קמיצה or a slaughter done inside the היכל is valid.

Overview

This amud opens with the end of רבי חנינא's view: the flour, oil and לבונה of a מנחה become holy in a כלי שרת only together. The גמרא asks why, according to him, the עשרון and the לוג were anointed. It answers that they serve offerings with only one ingredient: the מנחת חוטא and the oil of a מצורע. Next, the גמרא shows from שמואל's comment on a משנה about blood bowls that שמואל agrees with רב: one part of a מנחה can become holy without the other. The גמרא then examines רבי אלעזר's ruling that a קמיצה done in the היכל is valid. It defends this ruling against a challenge from the פסוק 'וקמץ משם', explaining that the פסוק only teaches that the קמיצה may be done anywhere in the עזרה. Finally, it turns to רבי יוחנן's ruling that שלמים slaughtered in the היכל are valid. It begins a challenge from a ברייתא about כהנים eating קדשים in the היכל during a siege, and this challenge continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לֹא זוֹ קְדוֹשָׁה בְּלֹא זוֹ

This one is not sanctified without that one

וְלֹא זוֹ קְדוֹשָׁה בְּלֹא זוֹ.

and that one is not sanctified without this one.

וּלְרַבִּי חֲנִינָא,

And according to Rabbi Chanina,

עִשָּׂרוֹן לָמָּה נִמְשַׁח?

why was the tenth-measure anointed?

The steps (22)

  1. Statementרבי חנינא: neither part is sanctified without the other
  2. ChallengeWhy was the flour measure anointed?
  3. ResolutionFor the sinner's meal offering
  4. ChallengeWhy was the oil measure anointed?
  5. ResolutionFor the leper's log of oil
  6. Proofשמואל agrees with רב, based on a משנה
  7. StatementBlood bowls sanctify dry things too
  8. ChallengeBut a מנחה with oil is wet
  9. ResolutionThe פסוק teaches about the לבונה
  10. ConclusionSo שמואל holds one part can become holy without the other
  11. ResolutionCompared to blood, a מנחה counts as dry
  12. StatementA handful taken inside the Sanctuary is valid
  13. ChallengeA ברייתא says the handful is taken where a non-כהן may stand
  14. ResolutionThe verse comes to include the whole courtyard, not to exclude the Sanctuary
  15. ChallengeA burnt offering is special because it is fully burned
  16. ResolutionLearn it from a sin offering instead
  17. ChallengeA sin offering is special because it atones for כרת sins
  18. ResolutionLearn it from a guilt offering instead
  19. ChallengeAll of them are blood offerings, so none can be compared to a מנחה
  20. ResolutionThe פסוק stops us from limiting קמיצה to the southwest corner
  21. StatementPeace offerings slaughtered inside the Sanctuary are valid
  22. ChallengeA teaching about priests eating inside the Sanctuary during a siege
