Topic

Why salting is indispensable despite רב's repetition rule, and which items on the מזבח need salt

Overview

רב taught that a rite is indispensable (מעכב) only when the תורה repeats it. The גמרא challenged him from a ברייתא: salt is never repeated, yet the ברייתא treats it as indispensable because it is called a 'ברית'. רב יוסף answers that רב follows our משנה, which says 'לא מלח – כשר'. אביי argues that the משנה only means a זר did the salting. רב יוסף replies that a זר could never approach the מזבח. A second answer says the word 'ברית' counts as if the פסוק had repeated it. The גמרא then asks whether the פסוק 'וכל קרבן מנחתך במלח תמלח' is itself a repetition. It answers that every word of that פסוק is needed by a ברייתא to teach which items need salt: anything burned on the מזבח with wood, but not wood itself, not blood, and not the part of the מנחה the כהנים eat. The גמרא then works through that ברייתא using כלל ופרט וכלל, and asks why blood, rather than the limbs of an עולה, is the item left out. It ends by showing that the limbs share six features with a מנחה (א״ש בנטמ״א).
