Topic

The laws of תפילין straps and knots, and when to say the ברכה

Overview

This amud gives a practical guide to תפילין straps and knots. It opens with a second way to tell 'new' תפילין from 'old' ones. Then it rules that a torn רצועה may not be knotted, because וקשרתם means a complete binding. For sewing with hidden stitches, רב אשי says to follow common practice. רב פפא's view that leftover strap pieces are kosher is rejected: תכלת and אזוב are only תשמישי מצוה, but תפילין are תשמישי קדושה. So straps must have a minimum length, measured up to the index finger. The גמרא then teaches that the תפילין knot is a הלכה למשה מסיני. Its nice side must face outward, and it must sit high and toward the front. Two פסוקים are linked to the head תפילין and to the knot that ה׳ showed משה. Finally, the ברכה on תפילין is said after putting them on but before tying them, so it still comes before the מצוה.

Opening lines

וְאִי נָמֵי כֹּל הֵיכִי

Alternatively, in any case

דְּכִי מִיתְּלֵי בֵּיהּ בְּמִתְנָא

where, when one hangs it by the strap,

אַתְיֵיהּ אַבָּתְרֵיהּ –

it comes along after it,

חַדְתָּתָא, וְאִידָּךְ – עַתִּיקָתָא.

those are new; and the other – old.
