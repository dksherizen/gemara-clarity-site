Menachos 85a

מנחות פ״ה ע״א

Topic

Settling רבי יוחנן's view on lower-quality ביכורים, the story of עפריים, and a new משנה on growing and checking the best grain for מנחות

Overview

The גמרא first asks how רבי יוחנן fits with a ברייתא that seems to go against him. It answers that this is a dispute among תנאים, and רבי יוחנן follows the other side. It then returns to the משנה's rule that מנחות come only from the best produce, and tells a short story in which פרעה's magicians mock משה with a saying about עפריים, a town famous for its grain. A new משנה follows. It says grain for מנחות should not come from fertilized, irrigated, or tree-filled fields, though after the fact it is כשר. It also explains how to grow top-quality grain, and how the גזבר checks the flour for powder and worms. The גמרא asks whether the farmer plows again in the second year, and brings a ברייתא of רבי יוסי that says 'חורש ושונה'. It then pushes back on that proof, since the משנה never mentions plowing twice. The discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא לְרַבִּי יוֹחָנָן,

But according to Rabbi Yoḥanan,

קַשְׁיָא?

it is difficult?

תַּנָּאֵי הִיא, דְּתַנְיָא:

It is a dispute of tanna'im, as it is taught:

שֶׁבְּגַג וְשֶׁבְּחוּרְבָּה –

Produce that grew on a roof or that grew in a ruin –
