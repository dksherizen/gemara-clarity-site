Topic

How תפילין are built: the compartments, the parchments, switching head and arm תפילין, and the order of the פרשיות

Overview

This amud is all about how תפילין are built. First, רבי ישמעאל and רבי עקיבא give two sources for why the head-תפילין have four compartments. Next, a ברייתא teaches from 'זכרון' (one memorial) that the four פרשיות go on four parchments inside compartments made from one hide, and it discusses spacing, separating threads and visible grooves. A second ברייתא says the arm-תפילין are written on one parchment. רבי יהודה and רבי יוסי argue about whether four separate parchments must be attached, and רבא concludes that רבי יהודה changed his mind. The גמרא then asks how head-תפילין may be turned into arm-תפילין, since we do not lower קדושה. It answers that this is allowed only with new תפילין, or with a condition made from the start. Finally, a ברייתא sets the order of the four פרשיות, אביי settles a contradiction about right and left, and רב rules that switching the order makes the תפילין פסול. אביי's limit on that ruling continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

תָּנוּ רַבָּנַן:

The Sages taught:

״לְטֹטֶפֶת״ ״לְטֹטֶפֶת״ ״לְטוֹטָפוֹת״ –

'For totefet,' 'for totefet,' 'for totafot' —

הֲרֵי כָּאן אַרְבַּע,

here there are four,

דִּבְרֵי רַבִּי יִשְׁמָעֵאל.

these are the words of Rabbi Yishmael.
