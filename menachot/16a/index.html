Topic

פיגול intent during only part of the מתיר, and whether a כהן's first intention carries over to later acts (רב against שמואל)

Overview

This amud deals with offerings that become permitted through two things together, like the קומץ and the לבונה of a מנחה. In the משנה, רבי מאיר holds that פיגול intent during just one of them brings כרת. חכמים say there is no כרת unless the intent covers the entire מתיר. They agree when there is only one מתיר, and the same argument applies to the two כבשים of שבועות and the two בזיכין of לחם הפנים. In the גמרא, רב says the argument is only when the intent came last. If the intent was during the קומץ, everyone agrees it is פיגול, because a person acts on his first intention. שמואל says they argue either way. The גמרא challenges רב twice from ברייתות. The first one, about the קומץ, is answered by רב חנינא: two different כהנים did the two parts. The second ברייתא, about blood placed inside on יום הכיפורים, is answered by רבא and then by רב אשי. Each explains that the כהן repeated his intention, which shows it was not carrying over on its own.

The steps (18)

  2. StatementThe Sages agree when the handful is the only permitting factor

  5. Statementרב limits the dispute to silence first, then intent
  6. Statementשמואל says the dispute applies in both orders

  8. ChallengeThe challenge spelled out: intent first, silence second, and they still argue

  13. ProofThe ברייתא continues: inner-altar blood placements
  14. ProofThe point of the proof: they argue even with intent in just one set
  15. RejectionThe two-priests answer may not work here
  16. Resolutionרבא: he repeated the intention in the third set
