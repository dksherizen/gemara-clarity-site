Topic

Why the חביתין (the כהן גדול's daily griddle-cake offering) may be prepared on שבת, and how much oil it needs

Overview

This amud asks why the חביתין, the כהן גדול's daily griddle-cake offering, may be prepared on שבת. One answer is that they are like the מנחה of the תמידין. רבא gives another reason: they need a holy vessel, so baking them a day early would make them פסול through לינה. A ברייתא then asks how much oil the חביתין need. It tries comparing them to the מנחת נסכים (three לוגין) and to the מנחת נדבה (one לוג), and each comparison is equally strong. רבי ישמעאל בנו של רבי יוחנן בן ברוקה settles it from the word תמיד: three לוגין per עשרון. רבי שמעון reaches the same amount by comparing the חביתין to the sheep's מנחה, since both use one עשרון of flour. The גמרא then asks why the ברייתא seems to contradict itself. אביי, רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע and רבה each explain who is speaking in it, and רבה's explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הֲרֵי הִיא כְּמִנְחַת תְּמִידִין.

It is like the meal offering of the daily offerings.

רָבָא אָמַר:

Rava said:

״עַל מַחֲבַת״ –

"On a griddle" –

מְלַמֵּד שֶׁטְּעוּנָה כְּלִי,

teaches that it requires a vessel,
