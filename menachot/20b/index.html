Menachos 20b

מנחות כ׳ ע״ב

Topic

Which altar items need salt? Why the limbs, and not blood, are compared to the מנחה, and how the ברייתא about wood and blood fits with רבי's view that wood needs salt.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier debate about why the limbs of a burnt offering, and not blood, are compared to the מנחה for the salt requirement. The גמרא first argues that blood looks like a better match. The answer is that the limbs share more features with the מנחה. Then the גמרא returns to a line in the ברייתא: 'I would think even wood and blood, which are called a קרבן, need salt.' It asks who holds that wood is called a קרבן, and the answer is רבי. But רבי holds that wood does need salt, as another ברייתא proves, and רבא and רב פפא add that for רבי wood even needs קמיצה and other wood to burn on. So the גמרא removes 'wood' from the ברייתא. That fix raises a new question: what does the פסוק's limiting word exclude, if blood is already excluded by 'מעל מנחתך'? The answer comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

אַדְּרַבָּה: דָּם הֲוָה לֵיהּ לְרַבּוֹיֵי,

On the contrary: blood should have been included,

שֶׁכֵּן מַתִּיר כְּמוֹתָהּ,

since it permits, like it does,

נִפְסָל בִּשְׁקִיעַת הַחַמָּה כְּמוֹתָהּ!

and it becomes disqualified at sunset, like it does!

הָנָךְ נְפִישָׁן.

Those are more numerous.
