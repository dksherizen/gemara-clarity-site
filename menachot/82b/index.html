Topic

Learning that פסח comes only from חולין: the debate of רבי אליעזר and רבי עקיבא, the rule that a היקש cannot be refuted, and the lessons drawn from ״זֹאת הַתּוֹרָה לָעֹלָה...״.

Overview

The משנה rules that a קרבן פסח comes only from חולין (ordinary money), not from מעשר שני money. רבי אליעזר learned this from פסח מצרים, and רבי עקיבא objected on two levels: we cannot learn the possible from the impossible, and even if we could, פסח מצרים had no blood or אימורין on a מזבח. The גמרא explains that each sage argued both on his own view and on the other's. רבי אליעזר answers from פסח מדבר on his own view, and from the פסוק ״וְעָבַדְתָּ״ on רבי עקיבא's view. רב ששת learns from this that a היקש cannot be refuted by reasoning. The study hall asks how something learned by היקש can teach by another היקש, and the answer is that all of פסח is one subject. The גמרא then asks for רבי עקיבא's own source, which is the פסוק ״זֹאת הַתּוֹרָה לָעֹלָה וְלַמִּנְחָה...״ that links all the offerings. From עולה it learns that every offering needs a knife, which is known from the עקידה. From מנחה it learns that the offerings are eaten only by male כהנים, and the testing of which offering this teaches continues on the next amud.

The steps (22)

  1. Challengeרבי עקיבא: the later פסח is different from the פסח in מצרים
  2. Resolutionרבי אליעזר: the פסוק makes the later פסח like the one in מצרים
  3. ChallengeThe גמרא asks: if רבי עקיבא held his first objection, why did he drop it?
  4. ChallengeAnd if he took it back, פסח מדבר disproves his second objection
  5. Resolutionרבי עקיבא's second objection was said according to רבי אליעזר's view
  6. ChallengeWhy didn't רבי אליעזר point to פסח מדבר?
  7. Resolutionרבי אליעזר also answered on two levels
  8. ChallengeWhy not refute the comparison even now?

  11. ResolutionAll of פסח is one subject

  15. QuestionWhich utensil is meant?

  17. ResponseThe utensil is a knife

  20. StatementFrom מנחה: all the offerings are eaten only by male כהנים
