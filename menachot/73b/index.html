Topic

Which offerings a גוי may bring: the מחלוקת between רבי יוסי הגלילי and רבי עקיבא, and the source for רבי שמעון's rule about a כהן's מנחת חוטא.

Overview

This amud opens with the rule that a גוי's שלמים are offered as עולות and burned completely. The גמרא supports the rule with logic (a גוי's heart is fully for Heaven) and with the פסוק "אשר יקריבו לה׳ לעולה". רבא answers a challenge from a ברייתא by explaining that it is talking about שלמים a גוי brought for a Jew's atonement. רבי יוחנן answers a second challenge, from a גוי's מנחה, by showing that the two sources follow a מחלוקת. רבי יוסי הגלילי lets a גוי bring almost any offering except a נזיר's. רבי עקיבא allows only an עולה. The גמרא then shows that sources about a גוי's נסכים can fit either view, because רבי עקיבא's עולה includes everything that goes with it. Finally the גמרא turns to רבי שמעון's rule about a כהן's מנחת חוטא. A ברייתא gives the first reading of the פסוק, and רבי שמעון begins his own reading, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שַׁלְמֵי הַגּוֹי – עוֹלוֹת,

Peace offerings of a gentile are burnt offerings,
