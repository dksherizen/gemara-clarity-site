Topic

רבי שמעון's unresolved view on a קומץ taken for a זבח; the sources for why מנחת חוטא and מנחת קנאות are invalid שלא לשמה, and why an אשם is not; and רב's rule about the מנחת העומר.

Overview

This amud opens in the middle of רב הושעיא's question to רב אסי: what would רבי שמעון say if the קומץ of a מנחה was taken with the intent of an animal offering? רב אסי answers that no one knows רבי שמעון's real reasoning. The גמרא explains that the earlier explanations of רבה, רבא and רב אשי had each been challenged, so none could settle the question. Next the גמרא returns to the משנה and asks why מנחת קנאות is invalid שלא לשמה. רב נחמן first links it to a חטאת through the shared word עון. After a back-and-forth about whether the אשם would then be invalid too, the גמרא limits that link to leftover money (מותר נדבה). The real source for both מנחת חוטא and מנחת קנאות turns out to be the word ״היא״, as with a חטאת. The ״הוא״ written about an אשם teaches something else: an אשם stays an אשם until בית דין sends it out to graze. Finally רב rules that a מנחת העומר done שלא לשמה is פסולה, because it came to permit the new grain and failed, and he begins to apply this to the אשם נזיר, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מָה לִי אָמַר רַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן?

What should I understand that Rabbi Shimon says?

טַעְמָא דְּרַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן

Is the reason of Rabbi Shimon

מִשּׁוּם מַחְשָׁבָה דְּמִינַּכְרָא

because an intention that is recognizably false

לָא פָּסְלָה,

does not disqualify?
