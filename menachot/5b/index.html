Topic

ריש לקיש's view that dawn permits the new grain, רבא on a עומר offered with wrong intent, and why a פסוק is needed to exclude a טרפה from the מזבח.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing ריש לקיש's rule that dawn of the sixteenth of ניסן permits the new grain (חדש). The גמרא shows that he never said this outright. It is inferred from his ruling that new-grain offerings brought on the sixteenth, even before the עומר, are כשר. רבא then rules that a מנחת העומר whose handful was taken שלא לשמה is fully כשר, because wrong intent only works with a fit person, a fit item and a fit place. A ברייתא next learns from the extra words 'מן הבקר' that a טרפה may not be offered. It also tests whether a קל וחומר could teach this instead, going back and forth with חלב and דם and with מליקה. The גמרא asks which refutation the ברייתא had in mind. רב suggests the עומר, ריש לקיש the קטורת, מר בריה דרבינא שבת, and רב אדא בר אבא כלאים. Each one is rejected, mostly because those cases are themselves a מצוה and offering a טרפה is not. The rejection of רב אדא בר אבא's כלאים proof continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הֵאִיר מִזְרָח מַתִּיר.

The illumination of the eastern horizon permits [the new crop].

וְהָא דְּרֵישׁ לָקִישׁ

And this [statement] of Reish Lakish

לָאו בְּפֵירוּשׁ אִיתְּמַר,

was not stated explicitly;

אֶלָּא מִכְּלָלָא אִיתְּמַר,

rather, it was stated by inference,
