Topic

Quality rules for wine for נסכים, the end of פרק כל קרבנות הציבור, and the start of the new פרק on the measuring vessels of the מקדש

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה about choosing wine for נסכים. The wine is taken from the middle of the barrel, and the גזבר knocks with a reed to stop the flow when scum appears. רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה says wine with white scum is פסול. The גמרא resolves a contradiction about sweet wine. רבינא combines the clauses into one rule, and רב אשי says sun sweetness is fine but fruit sweetness is not. It then looks for רבי's source for avoiding aged wine and settles on רבא's 'כי יתאדם'. Short ברייתות explain worked vineyards, medium casks, and why the גזבר knocks instead of speaking: speech harms wine. רבי יוחנן's question about lashes for consecrating unfit wine ends in תיקו. After a list of the best animals for קרבנות and an aggadah about the watchmen on ירושלים's walls, the פרק ends. A new פרק opens about the dry measuring vessels in the מקדש, and רבי מאיר's source for two עשרון vessels, one heaped and one leveled.

Opening lines

הַקְּמָחִין,

[not from the top, because of] the flour-like scum,

וְלֹא מִשּׁוּלֶיהָ – מִפְּנֵי הַשְּׁמָרִים,

and not from its bottom – because of the dregs,

אֶלָּא מֵבִיא מִשְּׁלִישָׁהּ מֵאֶמְצָעָהּ.

rather, one brings from its middle third.

כֵּיצַד הוּא בּוֹדֵק?

How does he check?
