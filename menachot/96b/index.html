Topic

The frame of the שולחן, and whether and why the שולחן can become טמא: the flat-board rule, being carried full and empty, and its gold covering.

Overview

The גמרא asks whether the frame of the שולחן raised the bread higher than an earlier calculation allowed. It gives two answers. One opinion holds that the frame was below the tabletop. Even according to the opinion that the frame was on top, it slanted outward and the bread sat inside it. A ברייתא shows that רבי יוסי and the חכמים really argued about where the frame was. From the view that the frame was below, רבי יוחנן learns that a flat board that can be turned over can become טמא. The גמרא then asks how the שולחן can become טמא at all, since a wooden vessel that stays in one place cannot. It answers with ריש לקיש's teaching: the שולחן was lifted with the bread on it to show the עולי רגלים the miracle of the לחם הפנים staying hot all week. Finally the גמרא asks why the gold covering alone would not make the שולחן able to become טמא, and the discussion of fixed and loose coverings continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

וְהָאִיכָּא מִסְגַּרְתּוֹ!

But there is its frame!

כְּמַאן דְּאָמַר מִסְגַּרְתּוֹ לְמַטָּה הָיְתָה,

It follows the one who said its frame was below,

וּלְמַאן דְּאָמַר מִסְגַּרְתּוֹ לְמַעְלָה הָיְתָה –

And according to the one who said its frame was above –

פַּרְקוֹדֵי הֲוָה מְפַרְקְדָא,

it was tilted, slanting outward,
