Topic

Why the משנה names מנחת חוטא, and רב's ruling that a קומץ taken by a זר is put back, following בן בתירא

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the question of why the משנה names מנחת חוטא separately. The answer is that even according to רבי שמעון, who keeps a sinner's offering plain, an unfit person's קמיצה makes it פסול, and this משנה follows his view. Next, רב rules that if a זר took the קומץ, it is put back into the מנחה. After failed attempts to fit this with the רבנן of the משנה, the גמרא says רב follows בן בתירא. It then tests what new point רב adds, discussing the left hand on יום הכפורים, a זר doing שחיטה, the פרה אדומה, נגעים, and a בָּמָה. A ברייתא shows that בן בתירא was already lenient for every disqualification. So the גמרא says רב's real point is that the קומץ goes back even after it was sanctified in a כלי שרת. A second version says the opposite: it goes back only before it was sanctified. רב נחמן questions the logic of the תנאים who make the כלי the cutoff, and then explains it himself. A new challenge to his explanation begins and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שֶׁלֹּא יְהֵא חוֹטֵא נִשְׂכָּר,

so that the sinner will not profit.

וּמִפְּנֵי מָה אֵינָהּ טְעוּנָה?

And for what reason does it not require [them]?

שֶׁלֹּא יְהֵא קׇרְבָּנוֹ מְהוּדָּר.

So that his offering will not be of superior quality.

סָלְקָא דַּעְתָּךְ אָמֵינָא:

It might enter your mind to say:
