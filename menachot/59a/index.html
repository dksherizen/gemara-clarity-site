Topic

Which מנחות need oil and frankincense, and the ברייתא's word-by-word sources from the פסוק of the עומר

Overview

This amud opens a משנה that sorts the מנחות (meal offerings) into four groups based on two ingredients, oil (שמן) and frankincense (לבונה). Most מנחות need both. The מנחת נסכים needs only oil, the לחם הפנים needs only frankincense, and the שתי הלחם, the מנחת חוטא and the מנחת קנאות need neither. The גמרא begins with רב פפא's rule that wherever the משנה says 'ten,' all ten loaves must be of one kind, against רבי שמעון. A ברייתא then reads the פסוק about the עומר word by word. 'עליה' keeps oil off the לחם הפנים and frankincense off the מנחת נסכים, even though a קל וחומר would have suggested otherwise. 'מנחה' adds the מנחת שמיני to frankincense, and 'היא' leaves out the שתי הלחם. The גמרא then asks why each word points to that offering and not to the מנחת כהנים. It answers by comparing features with the עומר, and when the comparison is a tie, the word 'נפש' decides. The amud ends with an אדרבה challenge about the שתי הלחם that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳

MISHNA:

יֵשׁ טְעוּנוֹת שֶׁמֶן וּלְבוֹנָה,

There are those that require oil and frankincense,

שֶׁמֶן וְלֹא לְבוֹנָה,

oil but not frankincense,

לְבוֹנָה וְלֹא שֶׁמֶן,

frankincense but not oil,
