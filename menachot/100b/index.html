Topic

The end of the לחם הפנים question and the day counts for eating שתי הלחם and לחם הפנים; then the new chapter on redeeming מנחות and נסכים before and after a כלי

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a question about לחם הפנים left on the שולחן into Friday night: shouldn't the שולחן consecrate it and then make it disqualified? רבא answers that the כהן took the loaves off before nightfall. מר זוטרא (or רב אשי) answers that bread arranged the wrong way is like bread arranged by a monkey, so the שולחן never consecrates it. A new משנה then teaches that שתי הלחם are eaten two or three days after baking, and לחם הפנים nine to eleven days after baking. The reason is that baking them does not override שבת or יום טוב, though רבן שמעון בן גמליאל holds that it does override יום טוב. רבינא uses this to prove that, according to the view that forbids them, נדרים ונדבות are unfit on יום טוב by Torah law. The chapter שתי הלחם ends, and a new chapter begins: טמא מנחות and נסכים can be redeemed before a כלי makes them holy, but not after. שמואל says even pure ones can be redeemed before a כלי. The גמרא explains why the משנה still had to state that items made holy in a כלי can't be redeemed, since a בעל מום is called 'טמא'. That discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

כִּי מָטֵי לֵילְיָא דְּבֵי שִׁימְשֵׁי

When the night arrives, the twilight [of Shabbat eve],

לִיקְדּוֹשׁ וְלִיפְּסוֹל?

let it be consecrated and then disqualified!

אָמַר רָבָא: בְּשֶׁקָּדַם וְסִילֵּק.

Rava says: [It is] where he removed [it] beforehand.

מָר זוּטְרָא, וְאִיתֵּימָא רַב אָשֵׁי, אָמַר:

Mar Zutra, and some say Rav Ashi, said:

The steps (18)

  1. ChallengeShouldn't the loaves become consecrated at twilight and then disqualified?
  2. Resolutionרבא: the כהן removed the loaves before nightfall
  3. ResolutionBread arranged the wrong way is like bread arranged by a monkey
  4. StatementNew משנה: שתי הלחם are eaten two or three days after baking
  5. Statementלחם הפנים is eaten nine to eleven days after baking
  6. StatementAfter the two days of ראש השנה, the bread is eaten on the eleventh day
  7. StatementBaking the breads does not override שבת or יום טוב
  8. Statementרבן שמעון בן גמליאל: it overrides יום טוב but not the fast day
  9. Statementרבינא: נדרים ונדבות are unfit on יום טוב even from the Torah
  10. ProofThe proof: even the required שתי הלחם do not override יום טוב
  11. ConclusionHadran — Closing the chapter שתי הלחם
  12. StatementNew chapter: redeeming טמא מנחות and נסכים
  13. StatementBirds, wood, frankincense and vessels can never be redeemed
  14. Statementשמואל: even טהור מנחות can be redeemed before a כלי
  15. ChallengeBut the משנה says they became impure
  16. Resolution"Impure" was mentioned only to match the second half
  17. ChallengeIsn't it obvious that items holy in a כלי can't be redeemed?
  18. ResolutionIt was needed, because a בעל מום is called טמא
