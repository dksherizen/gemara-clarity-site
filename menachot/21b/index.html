Topic

Salt in the מקדש: מעילה, the כהנים' use of communal salt, and בן בוכרי on the half-shekel

Overview

The amud opens with a ברייתא: salt already on a limb of a קרבן is subject to מעילה, but salt sitting on the כבש or on top of the מזבח is not. רב מתנה brings a פסוק in יחזקאל as the source. A משנה in שקלים says בית דין let the כהנים benefit from the salt and wood, and שמואל limits this to 'their קרבנות', not eating. The גמרא first reads this as salting their own offerings. It challenges that reading, since salt was given even to cure the hides of קדשים. It then reads שמואל as allowing salt for eating קדשים but not for חולין. רבינא supports the second reading: a ברייתא proves salt comes from communal supplies for every ישראל, so the כהנים would need no special rule just to salt their offerings. רב מרדכי answers in the name of רב שישא that the rule is needed according to בן בוכרי, who holds that כהנים do not have to give the half-shekel. This answer revives the first reading. The גמרא quotes the משנה about בן בוכרי and explains how a כהן who pays hands his coin over to the community. It then begins to explain the mistaken idea the salt rule corrects, and that explanation continues on the next amud.
