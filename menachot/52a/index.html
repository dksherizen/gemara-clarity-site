Topic

מעילה on the red heifer's ashes, funding communal sin-offerings, and the חביתין after the כהן גדול dies

Overview

The גמרא finishes a ברייתא saying that by Torah law there is no מעילה on the ashes of the פרה אדומה. רב אשי explains that the חכמים first added מעילה because people disrespected the ashes, then removed it when people stopped sprinkling in doubtful cases. Next, רבי יהודה and רבי שמעון argue over whether the פר העלם דבר של ציבור and the שעירי עבודה זרה are paid for by a new collection or from the תרומת הלשכה. A ברייתא from רבה זוטי shows that רבי שמעון is the one who says they come from the תרומת הלשכה. The גמרא then returns to the משנה. רבי יוחנן asks whether, after the כהן גדול dies, the חביתין is brought as a full portion in the afternoon too or is cancelled. רבא's proof from the eighth כהן is rejected by רבי ירמיה, and רבא then brings a proof from a פסוק. A ברייתא settles it: a full portion in the morning and a full portion in the afternoon. Finally, אבא יוסי בן דוסתאי and the רבנן argue over how much לבונה goes with the חביתין, and רבי יוחנן begins a new question about a כהן גדול who died, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בְּאֶפְרָהּ אֵין מוֹעֲלִין.

With its ashes, one is not liable for misuse.

אָמַר רַב אָשֵׁי:

Rav Ashi said:

שְׁתֵּי תַּקָּנוֹת הֲוַאי,

There were two ordinances.

דְּאוֹרָיְיתָא – בָּהּ מוֹעֲלִין,

By Torah law, with it one is liable for misuse,
