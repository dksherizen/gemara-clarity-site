Topic

Vowing a תודה and its loaves from different kinds of money (חולין versus מעשר שני), and רב הונא's rule that someone who vows only 'loaves of a תודה' must bring the whole offering

Overview

The משנה lists ways to vow a קרבן תודה and its loaves using ordinary money (חולין) or מעשר שני money. If the animal is from חולין and the loaves from מעשר, both must come from חולין. If the animal is from מעשר, he may bring it as he said. Any מעשר used for the loaves must be מעשר שני coins, not מעשר שני wheat. רב הונא rules that vowing 'loaves of a תודה' obligates the whole offering. The גמרא defends him against challenges from the משנה and from a ברייתא that forces someone who vowed 'a תודה without loaves' to bring both. The גמרא then asks why that vow is not simply cancelled as a mistake. חזקיה says the ברייתא follows בית שמאי's rule of going by the first words. רבי יוחנן says it can fit בית הלל too, and אביי and רבא explain the פסוק 'שמור ושמעת'. Finally רב נחמן and רב חסדא allow loaves made from wheat bought with מעשר שני money. רבי זירא disagrees and begins laying out the reasoning on both sides, which continues on the next amud.
