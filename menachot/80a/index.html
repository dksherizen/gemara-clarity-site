Topic

When a תודה needs its לחם: רבי יוחנן's rulings on replacements and offspring, שמואל's comparison to the חטאת, and how a תודה relates to its לחם.

Overview

A letter from רב חנינא quoted רבי יוחנן: one kind of תודה needs לחם if it is brought before the owner's כפרה, but not after. The גמרא rules out the replacement of a voluntary תודה and the offspring of a voluntary תודה. It settles on the offspring of an obligatory תודה. The lesson is that אדם מתכפר בשבח הקדש, and an explicit statement of רבי יוחנן confirms all three rules. Next, שמואל ties the לחם of a תודה to whether a חטאת in the same situation dies or grazes. After challenges from רב עמרם and others, the גמרא concludes that שמואל follows רבי שמעון and said only the 'dies' half of the rule. In doing so he rejects רבי יוחנן's idea of gaining כפרה through שבח הקדש. Finally, רבי אבא teaches that lost לחם is replaced but a lost תודה is not, because the לחם exists for the תודה. רבא then begins a case about money set aside for a תודה, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא אַחֲלִיפֵי תּוֹדָה נְדָבָה,

Rather, [perhaps he meant] the replacement of a voluntary thanks offering

בֵּין לִפְנֵי כַפָּרָה

whether before atonement

בֵּין לְאַחַר כַּפָּרָה

or after atonement

טְעוּנוֹת לֶחֶם –

they require loaves
