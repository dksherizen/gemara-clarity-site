Topic

Testing תכלת, buying ציצית, and who is obligated in ציצית

Overview

This amud finishes the גמרא's discussion of how to test whether blue wool was dyed with real תכלת. It brings two tests: soaking the wool overnight, and baking it in hard barley dough. The ברייתא's claim that תכלת 'has no test' is explained as meaning only that you cannot tell whether the wool was dyed as a trial dip. When תכלת from מר ממשכי failed one test and passed the other, רב אחאי showed that the two tests work together in order, and a message from ארץ ישראל confirmed this. The גמרא then discusses buying a טלית with ציצית from the market, and why a Jew may not sell one to a non-Jew. It explains רב יהודה's practice of putting ציצית on his wife's garment and making a ברכה every morning. Finally, it brings a ברייתא on who is obligated in ציצית. It explains why כהנים are included, and shows רבי שמעון's source for exempting women: a night garment is exempt, so ציצית is time-bound. The amud ends as the גמרא begins to ask how the רבנן read that פסוק, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בֶּן אַרְבָּעִים יוֹם,

forty days old,

וְתָרֵי לַהּ בְּגַוַּויְיהוּ

and he soaks it in them

מֵאוּרְתָּא וְעַד לְצַפְרָא.

from evening until morning.

אִיפְּרֵד חֲזוּתַיהּ – פְּסוּלָה,

If its color faded, it is unfit;
