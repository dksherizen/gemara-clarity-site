Topic

Where the arm and head תפילין are placed, and whether the four ציציות are one מצוה or four

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a question about a בכור born with two heads: since the תורה counts פדיון הבכור 'per skull,' this case is an exception. The גמרא then asks how we know the arm תפילין go on the upper arm. It gives three sources: a comparison to the head תפילין, 'a sign for you' (רבי אליעזר), and 'opposite the heart' (רבי יצחק). אמימר explains that 'a sign for you' only fixes the place, so visible תפילין are fine. Next, a ברייתא learns that the head תפילין go on the upper, hairy part of the head, from the איסור of a bald spot for the dead. רבי יהודה reaches the same result through the laws of צרעת. The גמרא then turns to the משנה on ציצית: are the four ציציות one מצוה or four? It offers three practical differences and rules that cutting, tying or sewing a corner does not remove the obligation. The final הלכה is not like רבי ישמעאל, as the story of מר בר רב אשי's torn ציצית shows. That story raises a challenge from כבוד הבריות, and רב בר שבא's answer begins here and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

״אַךְ״ – חִלֵּק,

"Yet" makes a distinction,

שָׁאנֵי הָכָא

Here it is different,

דִּבְגוּלְגּוֹלֶת תְּלָא רַחֲמָנָא.

since the Merciful One made it depend on the skull.

אָמַר מָר: ״יָדְךָ״ זוֹ קִיבּוֹרֶת,

The master said: "Your arm" is the bicep.
