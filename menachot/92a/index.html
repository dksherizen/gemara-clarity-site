Topic

Which קרבנות need סמיכה, and whom the שעיר המשתלח atones for

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a question about נסכים: how much wine goes with a ewe? The answer comes from a משנה in שקלים, and a ewe gets the same amount as a lamb. Then a new משנה teaches that communal offerings usually have no סמיכה, except the פר העלם דבר and the שעיר המשתלח. רבי שמעון adds the goat for עבודה זרה. Private offerings do need סמיכה, except בכור, מעשר and פסח, and an heir acts as the owner. In the גמרא, רבי יהודה and רבי שמעון argue over which offering is the second of the two communal סמיכות that tradition requires. Their argument turns on whether the שעיר המשתלח atones for the כהנים too. רבי יהודה says it does, so the כהן גדול counts as an owner. רבי שמעון says the כהנים are atoned through their own פר. Finally, two ברייתות about סמיכה on the goats of עבודה זרה seem to contradict each other in רבי שמעון's name, and that contradiction continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

נִסְכֵי רְחֵלָה בְּכַמָּה?

The libations of a ewe, how much are they?

וּפָשְׁטִינָא לֵיהּ מִמַּתְנִיתִין:

And I resolved it for him from a Mishnah:

גְּדִי מְשַׁמֵּשׁ נִסְכֵי צֹאן

'Kid' serves for the libations of flock animals

שֶׁל גְּדוֹלִים וְשֶׁל קְטַנִּים,

of big ones and of small ones,

  18. ResolutionThey are equal only in getting atonement, each in its own way
  19. ConclusionSummary of the two opinions on atonement for כהנים
  20. ProofA משנה that teaches רבי יהודה's view
  21. ProofThe same משנה teaches רבי שמעון's view
  22. StatementOnly the communal bull needs סמיכה, not the goats for עבודה זרה
  23. Statementרבי שמעון: the goats need סמיכה, but by the כהן גדול
  24. ChallengeA second ברייתא about the live goat seems to clash
