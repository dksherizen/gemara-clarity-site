Topic

The ארון's poles and carriers, and how שלמה's ten שולחנות and מנורות were placed in the היכל.

Overview

The ברייתא finishes explaining that the poles of the ארון pressed against the פרוכת and made it bulge, without ever being seen. רבי יהודה proves the poles ran along the ארון's width: four carriers, two on each side, could not walk in only one and a half אמות. A ברייתא then teaches that שלמה made ten שולחנות and ten מנורות, arranged five on each side of משה's original vessel, so all the tables stayed in the north and all the מנורות in the south. Two ברייתות that seem to disagree about how far inside the היכל these vessels stood turn out to mean the same spot, 20 אמות from the entrance. רבי says the tables lay east-west, learning from the מנורה, while רבי אלעזר ברבי שמעון says north-south, learning from the ארון and holding that even the מנורה stood north-south. The גמרא then asks how ten tables could fit across the twenty-אמה width of the היכל. The answer is that the tables stood in two rows, not one.

Opening lines

דּוֹחֲקִין וּבוֹלְטִין בַּפָּרוֹכֶת,

They press and bulge out against the Curtain,

וְדוֹמִין כְּמִין שְׁנֵי דַּדֵּי אִשָּׁה,

and they look like the two breasts of a woman,

שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: ״צְרוֹר הַמֹּר דּוֹדִי לִי

as it is said: "A bundle of myrrh is my beloved to me,

בֵּין שָׁדַי יָלִין״.

he lies between my breasts."
