Topic

Three answers (רבא, רב אשי, and רבה) to the clash between רבי שמעון's rulings on a מנחה brought for the wrong purpose

Overview

This amud gives three answers to a clash between two ברייתות of רבי שמעון. One ברייתא says a מנחה brought for the wrong purpose still counts for its owner, and the other says it does not. רבא says intent for a different מנחה is fine, because 'וזאת תורת המנחה' makes all מנחות one law, but intent for a זבח is not. The גמרא then asks whether the same logic would make a חטאת slaughtered for another חטאת valid, and brings different views on that. רב אשי answers that naming only a different pan does no harm, but naming a different kind of מנחה does. רב אחא בריה דרבא challenges him with a dry מנחה brought for the sake of a mixed one, and רב אשי defends his answer. The גמרא then explains why each אמורא rejected the other answers. It ends by starting רב הושעיא's question about a מנחה brought for the sake of a זבח, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וּמַאי זְבָחִים?

And what are 'slaughtered offerings'?

רוֹב זְבָחִים.

Most slaughtered offerings.

רָבָא אָמַר, לָא קַשְׁיָא:

Rava said: It is not difficult:

כָּאן – בְּקוֹמֵץ מִנְחָה לְשׁוּם מִנְחָה,

Here, it is where one takes the handful of a meal offering for the sake of a meal offering,
