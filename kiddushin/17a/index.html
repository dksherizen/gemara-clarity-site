Topic

The עבד עברי who ran away or got sick, and the amount and type of his הענקה (parting gift)

Overview

This amud starts by finishing a ברייתא about an עבד עברי: a slave who got sick still goes free in the seventh year. רב ששת explains that a runaway slave set free by the יובל still loses his הענקה (parting gift). The גמרא then compares two ברייתות on sick slaves. One who can still do light work like sewing never makes up time, but one who could not work for four or more years must make up the time. Next a ברייתא gives three opinions on the size of the הענקה: רבי מאיר says fifteen סלעים, רבי יהודה says thirty, and רבי שמעון says fifty. The גמרא traces each amount to a גזירה שוה: ריקם from בכור, נתינה from the gored slave, and נתינה from ערכין. It also defends each choice against the other sources. Then it shows that the words צאן, גורן, יקב teach what kind of thing may be given: something 'blessed' that grows. רבי שמעון leaves out money and רבי אליעזר בן יעקב leaves out mules. The amud ends by asking why 'winepress' is needed, and the answer comes on the next amud.
