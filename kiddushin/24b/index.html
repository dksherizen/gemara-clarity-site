Topic

Which injuries free an עבד כנעני, whether he needs a גט שחרור, and the details of indirect, partial and accidental injuries

Overview

This amud keeps working out which injuries free an עבד כנעני. The גמרא explains why the תורה names both a tooth and an eye. It then reads the פסוק as a כלל ופרט וכלל with a ריבוי, so any visible injury that does not heal frees the slave, even if the limb still works. A ברייתא then brings a מחלוקת about whether the freed slave also needs a גט שחרור. רבי שמעון learns from a divorced wife that a document is needed, and רבי מאיר reads ״לחפשי ישלחנו״ to mean he is free at once. Next come cases: a blow near the eye or ear, which leads רב אשי to explain that a person hurt by noise really frightened himself. Then the גמרא discusses a weakened eye or loosened tooth, and a doctor master who blinds his slave while treating him, where רבן שמעון בן גמליאל requires intent to destroy. רב ששת rules that removing an already-blind eye frees the slave, with support from the law of bird קרבנות. A new ruling from רב begins at the end and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שֵׁן דְּחָלָב,

even a baby tooth [would free him],

כְּתַב רַחֲמָנָא ״עַיִן״.

so the Merciful One wrote 'eye.'

וְאִי כְּתַב רַחֲמָנָא ״עַיִן״

And if the Merciful One had written only 'eye,'

הֲוָה אָמֵינָא מָה עַיִן שֶׁנִּבְרָא עִמּוֹ

I would have said: just as an eye was created with him,
