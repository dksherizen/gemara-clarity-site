Kiddushin 7a

קידושין ז׳ ע״א

Topic

קידושין through third parties (ערב and עבד כנעני), a woman who gives the money herself, and betrothing 'half' a person

Overview

This עמוד starts by finishing three cases of קידושין where the money does not pass directly from the man to the woman. In the first case, the woman tells the man to pay someone else. She is betrothed, as we learn from an ערב (guarantor), who binds himself without getting anything. In the second case, someone else pays so she is betrothed to a different man. That man is the husband even though he paid nothing, as we learn from an עבד כנעני who goes free with someone else's money. In the third case, the woman has the man pay a third person and is betrothed to that third person. Here both sources are needed, and the גמרא shows that each one covers the weak spot of the other. רבא then asks about a woman who gives the man money herself. מר זוטרא answers that with an אדם חשוב she is betrothed, because the pleasure of his accepting her gift is what she receives. The גמרא explains why the guarantor and slave rules had to be stated both for קידושין and for money matters. Finally, רבא rules that 'be betrothed to half of me' works but 'half of you is betrothed to me' does not. The גמרא compares this to רבי יוחנן's case of an animal owned by two partners, and says three lessons come from it, which are listed on the next עמוד.
