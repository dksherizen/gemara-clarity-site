Topic

Where does the תורה teach שליחות for תרומה, גירושין and קדשים, and what do the words 'גם אתם' teach?

Overview

The גמרא finishes a משנה showing that a שליח can separate תרומה, and asks for the source. Learning it from גירושין fails, because גירושין is חול, so the פסוק 'גם אתם' is brought to include a שליח. Next comes a משנה showing that a שליח can slaughter a קרבן פסח for a group. The source for that is רבי יהושע בן קרחה's famous rule, שלוחו של אדם כמותו. No single case can teach another, because each has its own special feature. When the גמרא tries learning one case from the other two, קדשים and גירושין fail, but תרומה can be learned from them. That frees 'גם אתם' to teach רבי ינאי's rule that a שליח must be one of the בני ברית. The גמרא defends that need against רבי יוחנן's principle and against the view of רבי שמעון. Finally it asks how רבי יונתן, who reads the פסח פסוק differently, learns שליחות for קדשים, and his ברייתא continues on the next עמוד.
