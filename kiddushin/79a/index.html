Kiddushin 79a

קידושין ע״ט ע״א

Learn Kiddushin 79a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Kiddushin 79a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Competing קידושין by a שליח and the principal, and the dispute between רב and שמואל on whether a girl's present בגרות reaches back in time.

Overview

The amud finishes the משנה about a father, or a woman, who sent a שליח to arrange קידושין and then made קידושין personally. Whichever קידושין came first counts. If nobody knows which came first, both men give a גט. The גמרא explains why both cases are needed: a father knows lineage, and a woman is careful about whom she marries. Next, רב and שמואל argue about a father who betrothed his daughter on the road while she betrothed herself in the city, when she is now a בוגרת. The גמרא sets the case on the exact day her six months of נערות ended. רב says her present status reaches back to the morning, while שמואל says she may have become a בוגרת only now. The גמרא challenges שמואל from a lacking מקוה and from a barrel of wine found turned to vinegar. Each time it answers that those cases have two weaknesses against the old status, while hers has only one. Finally, the גמרא suggests that the dispute matches an argument between תנאים, and that source continues on the next amud.
