Topic

Learning from "את", and the ban on benefit from עגלה ערופה and ציפורי מצורע: its source and when it begins

Overview

The גמרא first explains that one opinion does not learn laws from the little word "את". It backs this up with the story of שמעון העמסוני, who stopped expounding "את" at "את ה׳ אלהיך תירא", until רבי עקיבא taught that this "את" includes תלמידי חכמים. Next, the גמרא returns to the משנה's list. It learns that an עגלה ערופה is forbidden for benefit from the word כפרה, just like קדשים. It learns that ציפורי מצורע are forbidden because the item that enables is treated like the item that atones. רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש then argue about when the birds become forbidden: at שחיטה, or already when they are taken. רבי יוחנן challenges ריש לקיש three times. The last challenge pushes ריש לקיש to say the issue is an argument among תנאים. Finally, the גמרא asks why the ברייתא gives the permitting פסוק to the sent-away bird and not to the slaughtered one. רב שמואל בר רב יצחק begins to challenge the answer, and his challenge continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

״אֶת״ לָא דָּרֵישׁ.

He does not expound the word "et."

כִּדְתַנְיָא: שִׁמְעוֹן הָעַמְסוֹנִי

As it is taught: Shimon HaAmasoni,

וְאָמְרִי לַהּ נְחֶמְיָה הָעַמְסוֹנִי

and some say it was Nechemya HaAmasoni,

הָיָה דּוֹרֵשׁ כׇּל אֶתִּין שֶׁבַּתּוֹרָה.

would expound every "et" in the Torah.

The steps (28)

  1. ResolutionThis opinion does not learn from the word "את"
  2. Proofשמעון העמסוני stopped learning from "את"
  3. QuestionThe students ask about all his earlier teachings
  4. ResponseReward for expounding and reward for stopping
  5. Statementרבי עקיבא: the "את" includes תלמידי חכמים
  6. QuestionWhere do we learn the ban on benefit from עגלה ערופה?
  7. Response"כפרה" is written by it, like קדשים
  8. QuestionWhere do we learn the ban on benefit from ציפורי מצורע?
  9. ResponseInside and outside: things that permit and things that atone
  10. ResponseFinishing the comparison: what is outside follows the pattern of what is inside
  11. StatementDispute: from when are the birds of a מצורע forbidden?
  12. StatementThe reasons behind each opinion
  13. QuestionFrom when is עגלה ערופה itself forbidden?
  14. Responseרבי ינאי forgot; the scholars say going down to the valley forbids it
  15. ChallengeChallenge to ריש לקיש: then the birds should not be forbidden from the taking
  16. ResolutionDefense: the birds have no other boundary
  17. Challengeרבי יוחנן challenges from the פסוק that forbids the slaughtered bird
  18. ResolutionAnswer: the פסוק teaches that שחיטה does not permit it, unlike קדשים
  19. ChallengeA slaughtered bird found to be a טריפה is permitted
  20. ResolutionThe defect was inside the bird from the start
  21. ChallengeA ברייתא about a bird slaughtered without the other items
  22. ChallengeBoth opinions agree the bird is not forbidden while alive
  23. ResolutionThis is an argument among תנאים
  24. ResolutionEnabling items are treated like atoning items, both inside and outside
  25. StatementBack to the ברייתא: the sent-away bird is permitted and the slaughtered bird is forbidden
  26. ChallengeWhy not flip the two פסוקים?
  27. ResolutionA living kosher creature is never found to be forbidden
  28. Challengeרב שמואל בר רב יצחק begins to challenge the principle
