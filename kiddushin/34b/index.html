Kiddushin 34b

קידושין ל״ד ע״ב

Learn Kiddushin 34b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Kiddushin 34b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Where the rules come from for women being exempt from time-bound מצוות and obligated in non-time-bound ones, and how the rule of שני כתובים הבאים כאחד protects those sources

Overview

The גמרא has a rule, learned from תפילין, that women are exempt from מצות עשה שהזמן גרמא (positive מצוות tied to a set time). It first explains why the תורה still writes special exclusions for סוכה and ראייה: they block a גזירה שוה from מצה or from הקהל that would have obligated women. Next it asks why the rule is not learned from שמחה, where women are included, to obligate them. אביי answers that a woman's שמחה is the duty of her husband, or for a widow, of the people she lives with. The גמרא then asks why the rule is not learned from הקהל. It answers that מצה and הקהל are שני כתובים הבאים כאחד, two verses teaching the same thing, so they cannot set a general rule. It shows that תפילין and ראייה are each needed, while women in הקהל already follows from the children, so the rule holds. It then asks what to say according to the opinion that such verses do teach, and adds a second difficulty. Women are obligated in non-time-bound מצוות, learned from מורא אב ואם, and תלמוד תורה and פריה ורביה cannot teach the opposite, because they are two verses that come as one. That question is taken up on the next amud.

Opening lines

אִיצְטְרִיךְ, סָלְקָא דַּעְתָּךְ אָמֵינָא:

It was needed, for you might have thought to say:

נֵילַף ״חֲמִשָּׁה עָשָׂר״ ״חֲמִשָּׁה עָשָׂר״ מֵחַג הַמַּצּוֹת,

Let us learn 'fifteenth' from 'fifteenth' written about the festival of מצות,

מָה לְהַלָּן נָשִׁים חַיָּיבוֹת –

just as there women are obligated,

אַף כָּאן נָשִׁים חַיָּיבוֹת,

so too here women are obligated.
