Kiddushin 72a

קידושין ע״ב ע״א

Open Kiddushin 72a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Which places in and around בבל have trusted or flawed lineage, with aggadah about Persia and רבי's deathbed warnings

Overview

The גמרא keeps mapping where בבל, with its trusted family lineage, begins and ends. It notes that the Persians moved the border bridge. It also brings רב יוסף's proof that בירם is fine, since the pure families of פומבדיתא marry from there. רב פפא and רב יוסף then argue whether the border dispute also applies to גיטין. Next the גמרא praises חביל ימא and its towns as the finest lineage in בבל, and it rejects a rumor that כותים mixed into them. It rules that the area 'between the rivers' and חלזון ניהוונד count like the גולה, and it settles an apparent contradiction in רבי יוחנן's borders. רבי אבא בר כהנא disagrees, because the ten tribes were exiled to those places, and the גמרא separates מושכי, which is flawed, from מושכני, which is kosher. After some aggadah on דניאל's bear and the Persians, רבי names towns in בבל with lineage problems on his deathbed, and his list continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְהָאִידָּנָא הוּא [דְּ]דַלְיוּהּ פָּרְסָאֵי.

And it is only now that the Persians moved it up.

אֲמַר לֵיהּ אַבָּיֵי לְרַב יוֹסֵף:

Abaye said to Rav Yosef:

לְהָא גִּיסָא דִפְרָת עַד הֵיכָא?

On this side of the Euphrates, until where?

אֲמַר לֵיהּ: מַאי דַּעְתָּיךְ?

He said to him: What are you thinking?
