Topic

Wordings for קידושין and גירושין, and giving them without explaining (רבי יוסי)

Overview

The גמרא finishes explaining שמואל: wordings where the man speaks about himself, like 'I am your husband', do nothing, because the פסוק says he takes her and sends her, not himself. A ברייתא lists six phrases that work for קידושין, and the גמרא explains that they are split into two groups only because that is how the תנא learned them. Next the חכמים ask about ten unclear phrases. 'לקוחתי' works because of 'כי יקח'. 'חרופה' works in יהודה, where people use that word for a betrothed woman. The גמרא shows the question must be about a couple already discussing קידושין, where he gave money and also said one of the phrases. The doubt is whether he meant קידושין or was hiring her for work, and it ends in תיקו. The גמרא then studies the משנה of רבי יוסי and רבי יהודה about giving without explaining. The rule needs the couple to still be on the topic, or at least on a related point (אביי), and the הלכה follows רבי יוסי. Someone who does not know this rule should not rule on גיטין and קידושין. Finally the גמרא lists phrases that work for divorce and begins a case of 'you are a free woman', which continues on the next עמוד.
