Topic

Hidden flawed lineage, ה׳'s secret names, and presumed lineage status in בבל, ארץ ישראל and other lands

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a teaching: when ה׳ clears up the lineage of the tribes, He starts with שבט לוי. רבי יהושע בן לוי adds that money helps ממזרים blend into regular families. רבי יצחק calls it a kindness that a family which has already blended in stays blended in. The גמרא then backs שמואל's ranking that lineage is most reliable in בבל, then ארץ ישראל, then other lands, with stories about רבי and רבי פנחס. רבי יוחנן, אביי and a ברייתא show that the חכמים chose not to expose families with hidden flaws, sharing such names only privately and rarely. This leads to a side discussion of ה׳'s secret names, the four-letter, twelve-letter and forty-two-letter names, and how carefully they were guarded. Finally, שמואל gives the presumed lineage status of families in בבל, other lands and ארץ ישראל. The גמרא challenges the ארץ ישראל rule as contradicting itself, and רב הונא בר תחליפא begins an answer that continues on the next amud.

מְטַהֵר שְׁבָטִים,

purifies the tribes,

שִׁבְטוֹ שֶׁל לֵוִי מְטַהֵר תְּחִילָּה,

He will purify the tribe of Levi first,

שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: ״וַיֵּשֶׁב מְצָרֵף וּמְטַהֵר כֶּסֶף

as it is stated: "And he shall sit as a refiner and purifier of silver,

וְטִהַר אֶת בְּנֵי לֵוִי

and he shall purify the sons of Levi,
