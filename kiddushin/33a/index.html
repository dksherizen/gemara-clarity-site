Kiddushin 33a

קידושין ל״ג ע״א

Learn Kiddushin 33a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Kiddushin 33a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The scope of קימה and הידור for elders and scholars: cost, place, distance, the elder's own duty, and who counts as a זקן

Overview

The גמרא first finishes proving from the wording of the פסוק that שיבה and זקן describe one person. It then works through the ברייתא line by line. קימה and הידור are compared in both directions, so honoring an elder never requires stopping work or spending money. From this comes the rule that בעלי אומניות may not stand for תלמידי חכמים while they are working, and the standing shown to those bringing ביכורים is explained as a special case. Next, the גמרא splits the bathhouse into rooms: there is no standing in the inner rooms, but standing is required in the outer rooms. The גמרא also teaches that a person may not close his eyes ahead of time to escape the מצוה. It sets the distance for standing at four אמות, and for one's רב מובהק it is as far as one can see. On the elder's side, he should not trouble people, and a scholar who walks around them is promised long life. Finally, רבי יוחנן rules like איסי בן יהודה that every elderly person deserves honor, and stories show how different אמוראים honored and helped elders.

Opening lines

״מִפְּנֵי שֵׂיבָה תָּקוּם וְהָדַרְתָּ,

"Before the old one you shall stand and honor,

תָּקוּם וְהָדַרְתָּ פְּנֵי זָקֵן״

you shall stand and honor the face of an elder."

וּמִדְּלָא כְּתַב הָכִי,

And since [the Torah] did not write it this way,

שְׁמַע מִינַּהּ חַד הוּא.

learn from it that it is one.
