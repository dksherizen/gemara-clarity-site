Topic

Which מצוות apply outside ארץ ישראל: the exceptions of השמטת כספים and שילוח עבדים, and the argument over the 'הלכה' that forbids ערלה outside the Land.

Overview

The amud opens with a rule: מצוות commanded before entering ארץ ישראל apply everywhere, and those commanded after entering apply only in the Land. The exceptions are השמטת כספים and שילוח עבדים. The גמרא asks why these two needed to be listed, since each is a חובת הגוף. For money-release, it answers that רבי links it to land-release, but only in time and not in place, because 'כי קרא שמטה לה׳' teaches that it applies everywhere. For freeing slaves, 'יובל היא' teaches that it applies everywhere. The word 'בארץ' teaches that it applies only when יובל is in force in the Land. Next comes a משנה: חדש is forbidden everywhere by the תורה, ערלה outside the Land by a 'הלכה', and כלאים by the סופרים. שמואל says that 'הלכה' means local practice, and רבי יוחנן says it means a הלכה למשה מסיני. עולא argues that his view explains why ספק ערלה is treated more strictly than ספק כלאים outside the Land, and he begins to challenge רב יהודה. That challenge continues on the next amud.
