Topic

Whether one witness can disqualify a כהן, the sources for a חלל's and a בעל מום's service, and the משנה on a child's lineage

Overview

The גמרא brings a proof that one witness is not believed in matters of forbidden relations. The proof is a ברייתא about a מקוה in יבנה that was assumed full but was measured and found too small. רבי טרפון says the earlier immersions are pure and רבי עקיבא says they are impure. Each compares the case to a כהן whose problem is found only after he served: a בן גרושה, whose service stays valid, or a בעל מום, whose service is invalid. רבי עקיבא shows that the מקוה is like the בעל מום, and רבי טרפון accepts this. The גמרא learns from the ברייתא that a silent בן גרושה still needs two witnesses, and אביי offers another way to read it: a blemish can simply be checked. Next the גמרא brings פסוקים showing why a חלל's past service counts and a בעל מום's does not. Then a new משנה gives four rules for whose status a child takes, and the גמרא begins questioning the first rule; the challenge continues on the next amud.
