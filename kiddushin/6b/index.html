Kiddushin 6b

קידושין ו׳ ע״ב

Learn Kiddushin 6b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Kiddushin 6b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Unclear release phrases for wives and slaves, קידושין with a loan or its benefit, and gifts given on condition they be returned

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a list of phrases that do nothing, such as telling a שפחה "you are permitted to any man." רבינא proves from a ברייתא about a גט שחרור, using a קל וחומר, that a husband who tells his wife "הרי את לעצמך" has fully divorced her. Next רבינא asks whether "אין לי עסק בך" frees a slave. A ברייתא about an אונו, explained by רב ששת, shows that it does. Then אביי rules that קידושין with a loan does not work. קידושין with the benefit of a loan does work, but it is forbidden as a trick around רבית. The גמרא defines that benefit as giving her more time to repay. רבא's rule about money given on condition it be returned is challenged, and רב אשי restates it: such a gift works everywhere except קידושין, and רבא was quoted that way. The amud ends as רבא begins a new case about a woman who says "give a מנה to so-and-so," which continues on the next amud.

The steps (18)

  1. StatementPhrases that accomplish nothing

  3. ResponseProof from freeing a slave: all the more so a wife
  4. QuestionA master says: I have no business with you
  5. ProofA ברייתא about selling a slave to a non-Jew
  6. QuestionWhat does the אונו document say?
  7. Responseרב ששת: the document says "I have no business with you"
  8. Statementאביי: קידושין with a loan versus with the benefit of a loan
  9. QuestionWhat is the benefit of a loan?
  10. RejectionFirst suggestion: lending four for five, which is rejected
  11. ResponseThe case is when he gives her more time on the loan
  12. StatementMoney given on condition it is returned does not work for sale, marriage, or redeeming a son
  13. StatementWith תרומה it does count, but it is forbidden
  14. ChallengeHow can רבא treat תרומה differently from the other cases?
  15. Challengeרבא's own אתרוג ruling contradicts him
  16. Resolutionרב אשי: it works for everything except a woman
  17. Proofרבא was quoted the way רב אשי says
