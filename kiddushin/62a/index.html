Kiddushin 62a

קידושין ס״ב ע״א

Learn Kiddushin 62a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Kiddushin 62a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

תנאי כפול in the פסוקים of סוטה and sprinkling; the משנה on mistaken or future קידושין; and רבי יוחנן's rule that what is in a person's power counts as done.

Overview

The גמרא first explains the strange phrase 'you shall eat the sword' (ישעיהו 1:20). רבא says it means harsh, unhealthy food. Next it tests the סוטה פסוק against the two opinions about תנאי כפול. רבי תנחום shows that the short spelling of הנקי also hints at חנקי, which gives רבי מאיר his second side. It also teaches רבי חנינא בן גמליאל that the punishment is severe. The גמרא then looks at the פסוקים about sprinkling for purity from a dead body and shows that each repeated phrase teaches its own law. A new משנה follows. A man's wrong assumption about a woman's family or wealth does not cancel קידושין. קידושין set to begin 'after' a change he cannot make happen now do not work, and an unborn girl can be betrothed only once the pregnancy is noticeable. In the גמרא, רבי יוחנן rules that anything in a person's power counts as already done. רב אסי challenges this from the convert case, and the גמרא answers that converting is not fully in the man's power. The reason, a teaching of רבי יוחנן, comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

״חֶרֶב תְּאֻכְּלוּ״?

"You shall eat the sword"?

אָמַר רָבָא:

Rava said:

מִילְחָא גְּלָלְנִיתָא,

Coarse grains of salt,

נַהֲמָא דִשְׂעָרֵי אַקּוּשָׁא, וּבֻצְלֵי.

hard barley bread, and onions.

The steps (22)

  1. QuestionHow can someone eat a sword?
  2. Responseרבא: harsh foods that hurt like a sword
  3. ProofSupport: these foods harm the body like swords
  4. ChallengeThe סוטה verse seems to leave out רבי מאיר's second half
  5. Resolutionרבי תנחום: the spelling hints at 'choked'
  6. ChallengeThen why does רבי חנינא בן גמליאל need the hint?
  7. ResolutionThe hint teaches that the guilty side is punished severely
  8. ChallengeNew challenge: the doubled verse about purification
  9. ResolutionBoth sprinkling days are required, not just one
  10. ChallengeWhy does the Torah repeat the sprinkling days?
  11. ResolutionThe days are fixed, even if you add days in between
  12. ChallengeWhy does the Torah add 'he shall purify him on the seventh day'?
  13. ResolutionTwo sprinklings are needed even for תרומה
  14. StatementMistaken about her family or wealth, she is still betrothed
  15. StatementBetrothal that depends on a future change fails
  16. StatementBetrothing an unborn daughter
  17. StatementNo תרומה from detached produce for attached produce
  18. QuestionWhat if the תרומה is set to take effect once the produce is picked
  19. ResponseAnything in a person's power counts as already done
  20. Challengeרב אסי quotes the משנה about 'after I convert' as a challenge
  21. ChallengeConverting is in his own hands, so why doesn't it work?
  22. ResolutionConverting is not really in his own hands
