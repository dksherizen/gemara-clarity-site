Kiddushin 14a

קידושין י״ד ע״א

Topic

How a יבמה is acquired and released: ביאה only, חליצה, the יבם's death, and why a גט and חליצה cannot replace each other

Overview

The גמרא brings the פסוק showing that ביאה makes the יבמה the יבם's wife. Using the word 'וְיִבְּמָהּ', it shows that only ביאה completes her acquisition (not כסף or שטר), and that the יבום can happen even against her will. Next comes the source that חליצה frees her to all of ישראל. This is learned from 'בישראל', which is written twice, while the public announcement 'חלוץ הנעל' comes from 'ונקרא שמו'. The גמרא then proves by a קל וחומר from an אשת איש that the יבם's death frees the יבמה, and רב אשי defends it against challenges. It asks why a married woman can't leave through חליצה ('ספר כריתת' blocks it), and why a יבמה can't leave through a גט. The answer 'ככה' is challenged from the goats of יום הכפורים. The גמרא then tries 'לה', and finally settles on 'נעל'. The amud ends with a ברייתא suggesting 'נעל' is needed to include anyone's shoe and to require a shoe that fits. The challenge continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

״יְבָמָהּ יָבֹא עָלֶיהָ

"Her brother-in-law shall come upon her

וּלְקָחָהּ לוֹ לְאִשָּׁה״.

and take her to him as a wife."

וְאֵימָא, לְכוּלַּהּ מִילְּתָא כְּאִשָּׁה!

But say: she is like a wife in every matter!

לָא סָלְקָא דַּעְתָּךְ, דְּתַנְיָא:

It cannot enter your mind, as it is taught:
