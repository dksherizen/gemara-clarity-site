Kiddushin 21a

Topic

Limits on redemption: borrowing or paying in parts (including for a מקדיש), and redemption by relatives for walled-city houses and an עבד עברי

Overview

The גמרא first settles two clashing ברייתות about borrowing money to redeem and redeeming in parts: one follows רבנן and the other follows רבי שמעון. It then defends the earlier lesson that a מקדיש also cannot borrow to redeem or redeem in parts. רב אחא בריה דרבא attacks the comparison to a walled-city house, and רב אחא סבא rebuilds it from what an ancestral field and a walled-city house share. מר זוטרא attacks that shared feature, and רבינא saves the proof with an עבד עברי sold to a non-Jew. Next, רב ששת rules that relatives cannot redeem a walled-city house. He is challenged from 'בכל גאולה תתנו' and from the triple 'יגאלנו', and the גמרא answers by reading those sources as being about unwalled houses and fields. Finally, the גמרא asks whether relatives can redeem an עבד עברי sold to a Jew. According to רבי they cannot. According to רבנן a proof is tried, rejected, and then the rejection is challenged.

Opening lines

הָא רַבָּנַן, הָא רַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן.

This one follows the Rabbis, that one follows Rabbi Shimon.

תָּנֵי חֲדָא: לֹוֶה וְגוֹאֵל

One ברייתא teaches: he may borrow and redeem,

וְגוֹאֵל לַחֲצָאִין,

and he may redeem in halves;

וְתַנְיָא אִידַּךְ: אֵין לֹוֶה וְגוֹאֵל

and another ברייתא teaches: he may not borrow and redeem,
