Topic

The rules of קנין אגב: piling, required wording, mixed deals, and paying for everything

Overview

This amud is about קנין אגב: acquiring movable items together with land. First the גמרא asks whether the movables must be piled on the land. Proofs from רבן גמליאל's tithes and from רב הונא's third type of שטר are each pushed away. A ברייתא then shows that a שטר is just like any movable, so the גמרא concludes that piling is not needed. Next it rules that the seller must say both 'אגב' and 'קני.' Two more questions follow. A field sold with movables given as a gift is proven from רבן גמליאל's words to יהושע. A field to one person with movables to another is not proven, because of how רבי עקיבא's part is explained. Finally, רבא rules that a buyer paying with money acquires only as much as he paid for, and a ברייתא comparing כסף, שטר and חזקה begins to support him; it continues on the next amud.
