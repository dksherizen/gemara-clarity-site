Kiddushin 44b

קידושין מ״ד ע״ב

Topic

A נערה appointing a שליח for her גט, and a קטנה who accepted קידושין without her father's consent

Overview

According to the רבנן, a נערה whose father is alive can receive her own גט. רבא asks רב נחמן whether she can also appoint a שליח to receive it. The גמרא shows that רבא already holds she is like her father's 'hand' and not his 'courtyard'. So the real question is whether her hand is strong enough to appoint a שליח. רב נחמן says no. A challenge from a משנה is answered by saying that the משנה speaks of a girl with no father, with words filled in. Next comes a new topic: a קטנה who accepted קידושין without her father's consent. שמואל and רב say she needs both a גט and מיאון, because we are unsure whether her father agreed. רב נחמן limits this to a case where a שידוך was arranged. עולא says she does not even need מיאון. רב כהנא challenges עולא and answers his own challenge, and רב המנונא begins another challenge that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

נַעֲרָה, מַהוּ שֶׁתַּעֲשֶׂה שָׁלִיחַ

A young woman: what is the law, may she appoint an agent

לְקַבֵּל גִּיטָּהּ מִיַּד בַּעְלָהּ?

to receive her bill of divorce from her husband's hand?

כְּיַד אָבִיהָ דָּמְיָא,

Is she like her father's hand,

אוֹ כַּחֲצַר אָבִיהָ דָּמְיָא?

or is she like her father's courtyard?
