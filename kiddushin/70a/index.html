Kiddushin 70a

קידושין ע׳ ע״א

Topic

Proofs for who went up from בָּבֶל, the gravity of unfit marriages, and the story of רַב יְהוּדָה and רַב נַחְמָן

Overview

The מִשְׁנָה lists the family-status groups that went up from בָּבֶל with עֶזְרָא, and the גְּמָרָא looks for a פָּסוּק behind each one. רַב חִסְדָּא shows from עֶזְרָא that גֵּרִים and freed slaves came along. A first proof that מַמְזֵרִים came, from טוֹבִיָּה הָעַמּוֹנִי, is knocked down by three challenges. It is replaced by a פָּסוּק in נְחֶמְיָה whose place-names (תֵּל מֶלַח, תֵּל חַרְשָׁא) hint at people of flawed lineage. The same פָּסוּק leads to teachings about how serious it is to marry a woman unfit in lineage, especially for money, and to the rule that whoever calls others unfit is unfit himself. A story follows: a man who habitually called people slaves insults רַב יְהוּדָה, who puts him in נִידּוּי and declares him a slave. The man summons רַב יְהוּדָה to רַב נַחְמָן's court. During the visit, רַב יְהוּדָה keeps correcting רַב נַחְמָן, about public labor, showy words, and a woman serving or being greeted. The amud ends in the middle of another teaching of שְׁמוּאֵל, which continues on the next amud.
