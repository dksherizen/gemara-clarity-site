Kiddushin 10a

קידושין י׳ ע״א

Open Kiddushin 10a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

רבי's view on the קנס and the word 'לבדו', how רבי יוחנן learns two laws from 'בעולת בעל', when קידושי ביאה takes effect, and whether ביאה creates נשואין or אירוסין

Overview

On the previous daf, רבי and the רבנן argued about several men who were with a נערה המאורסה in an unusual way. רבי זירא says that even רבי agrees all the men pay the קנס. The death penalty is different only because the פסוק says 'לבדו'. The רבנן use 'לבדו' for another lesson: the man can be put to death alone, as רבי יונתן teaches. Next, the גמרא shows how רבי יוחנן learns two laws from the words 'בעולת בעל'. The גמרא then asks whether קידושי ביאה takes effect at the start or at the end of the act. אמימר in the name of רבא answers that it takes effect at the end, because that is what the man has in mind. Last, the גמרא asks whether ביאה creates נשואין or only אירוסין. אביי's proof from a משנה is pushed aside, and רבא begins a new proof from a ברייתא about a girl three years and one day old, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אָמַר רַבִּי זֵירָא:

Rabbi Zeira said:

מוֹדֶה רַבִּי לְעִנְיַן קְנָס

Rabbi concedes regarding the fine

דְּכוּלְּהוּ מְשַׁלְּמִי.

that they all pay.

מַאי שְׁנָא מִקְּטָלָא?

What is different from the death penalty?

The steps (15)

  1. Statementרבי זירא: רבי agrees that everyone pays the fine
  2. ChallengeWhy is the fine different from the death penalty?
  3. ResolutionThe death penalty is different because of the word 'only'
  4. QuestionWhat do the רבנן do with the word 'לבדו'?
  5. ResponseThe רבנן use 'לבדו' for רבי יונתן's teaching
  6. QuestionWhere does רבי יוחנן get the other rule?
  7. Response'בעולת בעל' teaches two laws
  8. QuestionDoes betrothal by relations take effect at the start or the end?
  9. QuestionA second practical difference: a כהן גדול marrying a בתולה
  10. ResponseA man intends the act to count at its completion
  11. QuestionDoes ביאה create engagement or full marriage?
  12. Proofאביי brings a משנה about a father's rights over his daughter
  13. ProofThe משנה mentions intercourse first and marriage afterward
  14. Rejection'She married' refers to the other ways of betrothal
  15. Proofרבא begins a proof about a girl three years and one day old
