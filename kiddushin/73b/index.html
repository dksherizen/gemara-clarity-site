Topic

Abandoned babies (אסופי) and who is believed about a child's lineage or a sale

Overview

The גמרא lists cases of babies found abandoned and decides whether each one is an אסופי, a foundling whose lineage is in doubt. If the parents showed care, by tending the baby or leaving him somewhere safe where he would be found, he is not an אסופי. If he was left somewhere dangerous, he is. רבא adds a famine-year rule, and the גמרא shows that it belongs to רב's teaching: parents are believed about an abandoned baby only while he is still in the marketplace, but in famine years they are believed even after he is taken in. רב חסדא then lists three people believed only right away: the parents of an אסופי, a midwife who never left the room, and a woman who checked herself immediately and so frees her friends from טומאה. A ברייתא adds that a midwife is believed about which baby is a כהן, a לוי, a נתין or a ממזר unless there is an עַרְעָר. The גמרא gives two answers about whether that challenge needs two witnesses or only one. Finally, a seller is believed about which of two buyers he sold to, but only while the item is still in his hands.
