Kiddushin 4b

קידושין ד׳ ע״ב

Open Kiddushin 4b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The sources for קידושי כסף and קידושי ביאה, and why the תורה writes a פסוק when a קל וחומר seems to prove the same law

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an answer: the extra words 'ויצאה חנם' teach that even an איילונית can be sold as a maidservant and goes free at בגרות. The גמרא then explains that we only say 'the תורה just wrote out a קל וחומר' when no new lesson can be found in the words. Next, a ברייתא learns קידושי כסף from the word 'כי יקח' in the פסוק about divorce, linked to 'קח' when אברהם bought the field from עפרון. The ברייתא asks why a פסוק is needed when a קל וחומר from an אמה העבריה seems to prove the same thing. רב אשי answers that the קל וחומר is weak from its very start, because money can free a maidservant but can never end a marriage. The גמרא then shows why the תורה needed both 'ויצאה חנם' and 'כי יקח'. Finally, the ברייתא learns from 'ובעלה' that ביאה makes קידושין. רב אשי again explains why the פסוק is needed: the קל וחומר from יבמה fails, since a יבמה is already bound to the יבם and an ordinary woman is not.

Opening lines

קָא מַשְׁמַע לַן

It teaches us

״וְיָצְאָה חִנָּם כּוּ׳״.

'Then she shall go out for nothing,' etc.

וּלְמָר בַּר רַב אָשֵׁי, דְּאָמַר:

And according to Mar bar Rav Ashi, who said:

וְלָאו קַל וָחוֹמֶר הוּא,

'But isn't it a kal vachomer?'
