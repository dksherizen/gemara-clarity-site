Kiddushin 8b

קידושין ח׳ ע״ב

Learn Kiddushin 8b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Kiddushin 8b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Pledges in קידושין and sales, and when a woman's instructions about the money count as accepting קידושין

Overview

This amud opens with the end of רב נחמן's ruling: a man promised a מנה for קידושין but gave only a pledge, so nothing counts. רבא challenges this from a ברייתא that says קידושין with a pledge works. The גמרא answers that the ברייתא means a pledge the man holds from someone who owes him money. It backs this up with רבי יצחק's proof that a creditor really acquires a pledge. A story with רבי אמי applies the same rule to buying a maidservant. Next come a series of ברייתות about a woman who reacts to the קידושין money: she throws it away, tells him to give it to her father, his father or a stranger, or has it put on a rock or given to a dog or a poor person. The גמרא explains which actions show she accepted the money and why each ruling is needed. Two questions, a shared rock and a chasing dog, end in תיקו. The amud closes as a new story about a seller begins, and it continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מָנֶה אֵין כָּאן –

There is no one hundred dinars here –

מַשְׁכּוֹן אֵין כָּאן.

there is no pledge here.

אֵיתִיבֵיהּ רָבָא לְרַב נַחְמָן:

Rava raised an objection to Rav Naḥman:

קִידְּשָׁה בְּמַשְׁכּוֹן מְקוּדֶּשֶׁת!

If he betrothed her with a pledge, she is betrothed!

The steps (25)
