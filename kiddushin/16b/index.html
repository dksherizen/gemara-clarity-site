Kiddushin 16b

קידושין ט״ז ע״ב

Learn Kiddushin 16b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Kiddushin 16b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Whether an אמה העבריה goes free at her father's death (ריש לקיש is refuted), and who receives the הענקה, the parting gift for a freed Hebrew slave.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an answer: signs of maturity (סימנים) have a minimum age, and a ברייתא shows that hairs grown too early count only as a mole. Next the גמרא tests ריש לקיש's rule that an אמה העבריה goes free when her father dies. רב ששת challenges it from רבי שמעון's list of four ways of going free. The גמרא answers that the master's death is not on that list either, so the list holds only things with a set time. רב עמרם then brings a different ברייתא about the parting gift (הענקה). That list does include the master's death but leaves out the father's death, so ריש לקיש is refuted. His קל וחומר fails because signs change the girl's body and her father's death does not. Two ברייתות about who keeps the maidservant's gift are explained without helping ריש לקיש. Then the גמרא quotes the full ברייתא about the gift and its sources in the פסוקים. It closes by asking why a runaway gets no gift, since he must finish his six years anyway. The answer comes on the next amud.

לְמַטָּה.

who grew two hairs – it is a mole,

מִבֶּן תֵּשַׁע שָׁנִים וְיוֹם אֶחָד
