Topic

The father's right to his daughter's קידושין money, and why the אמה העבריה goes free at both נערות and בגרות

Overview

The גמרא is proving that the money from a נערה's קידושין goes to her father. The source is the פסוק about the אמה העבריה who goes free ׳without money׳: no money for this master, but money for another master, the father. The גמרא defends this comparison and answers that the words are already used for another teaching. רבינא's answer is that the extra י in אין is free to teach about the father, and the גמרא shows that this י really is extra. Next it explains why the תורה had to teach both that her קידושין and that her earnings go to her father. Then it returns to the ברייתא and asks why the תורה needs to say she goes free at בגרות if she already went free at נערות. רבה, אביי and מר בר רב אשי each give an answer. The last answer, from מר בר רב אשי, is that the teaching shows that the sale of an אילונית counts at all.

Opening lines

יְצִיאָה דִּכְווֹתַהּ קָא מְמַעֵט.

It excludes a leaving that is like it.

וְהָא לָא דָּמְיָא

But it is not similar:

הַאי יְצִיאָה לְהַאי יְצִיאָה,

this leaving to that leaving.

הָתָם נָפְקָא לַהּ מֵרְשׁוּת אָדוֹן לִגְמָרֵי,

There, she leaves the master's control completely,
