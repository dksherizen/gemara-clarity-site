Kiddushin 74b

קידושין ע״ד ע״ב

Open Kiddushin 74b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Several attempts to explain the משנה's 'כל האסורים לבוא בקהל' and רבי יהודה's dissent, ending with רבא's גר עמוני ומואבי reading, followed by a ברייתא on men who make a woman unfit for כהונה

Overview

The גמרא keeps trying to explain the משנה's line that those 'forbidden to enter the קהל' may marry 'זה בזה', and what רבי יהודה disagrees with. First it reads the line as being about people forbidden to כהונה, such as a girl who converted before age three years and a day. Counterexamples knock that reading down. Next, רב נתן בר הושעיא reads it as being about anyone whose daughter a כהן may not marry. Cases of a חלל and of a גר break that reading too. רב נחמן then says the dispute is about a child born from a brother and sister. That is rejected because another משנה already argues that point. Finally, רבא explains that the משנה's permission is about a גר עמוני ומואבי. רבי יהודה forbids only a regular גר from marrying a ממזרת. The amud ends with a ברייתא listing men whose relations make a woman unfit to marry a כהן. רבי יוסי gives a narrower rule, and רבן שמעון בן גמליאל's opinion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הָכִי קָאָמַר:

This is what it is saying:

כׇּל הָאֲסוּרִים לָבֹא בִּקְהַל כְּהוּנָּה,

All those forbidden to enter the congregation of the priesthood,

מַאי נִינְהוּ –

who are they?

גִּיּוֹרֶת פְּחוּתָה מִבַּת שָׁלֹשׁ שָׁנִים וְיוֹם אֶחָד,

a female convert younger than three years and one day,
