Topic

How a Canaanite slave goes free with money or a שטר: through others or by himself, according to רבי מאיר, the חכמים, and רבי שמעון בן אלעזר.

Overview

This amud opens by finishing the story of מר זוטרא, who acquired the slave of a dying convert just before the convert died. A second version says the slave was a minor, which fits the תנא קמא of a ברייתא but not אבא שאול, who says minor slaves of a dead convert do not go free. The גמרא then studies the משנה's dispute about how a slave goes free with money or with a שטר. For רבי מאיר, money works only when others pay and a שטר only when the slave receives it himself. The גמרא first explains this as a case where the slave agrees, showing that a slave owns nothing apart from his master, but the שטר clause makes trouble for that answer. אביי and רבא each give a different explanation of why money can work through others while a שטר cannot. Next the גמרא works through the חכמים' words and finally reads them as: money works both ways, but a שטר only through others. That שטר rule belongs to רבי שמעון בן אלעזר, so there are three opinions. רבה explains his reason by comparing a slave's שטר to a woman's גט. The amud ends as רבה begins a new question.

Opening lines

זֶה פֵּירַשׁ לְמִיתָה,

this one departed to death,

וְזֶה פֵּירַשׁ לְחַיִּים.

and that one departed to life.

וְאִיכָּא דְּאָמְרִי

And there are those who say

קָטָן הֲוָה,

he was a minor,
