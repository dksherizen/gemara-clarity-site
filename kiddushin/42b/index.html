Topic

Dividing an inheritance, and the limits of agency for a sin

Overview

The גמרא first settles two rulings of רב נחמן about orphans dividing property. A value mistake of a sixth cancels the division, and without such a mistake the orphans can only complain about which plots they got. רב נחמן then rules that brothers who divide an inheritance are like buyers, so the usual sixth-rules of אונאה apply. רבא adds limits to this. A brother who sent a שליח can undo even a small loss. Brothers who chose a court appraisal cannot undo even a big mistake, which matches רבן שמעון בן גמליאל in a משנה. Returning the overcharge applies only to movable things, but land divided by measurement is corrected for any error, as רבה teaches. The גמרא then turns to שליחות and explains, from the משנה about sending fire, that אין שליח לדבר עבירה. מעילה is an exception learned from תרומה. Together with שליחות יד, whose source is 'על כל דבר פשע' according to בית הלל, the two are 'two verses that come as one' and do not teach a general rule. The amud ends by asking how this works for בית שמאי, and that question continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הָא – דִּטְעוֹ,

This one is where they erred,

הָא – דְּלָא טְעוֹ.

what is meant by "they can protest"?
