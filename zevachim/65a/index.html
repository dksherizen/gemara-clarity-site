Topic

Mixed intentions in bird offerings, and how the bird עולה is offered: מליקה, draining the blood, and removing the crop

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה about bird offerings and then begins the גמרא on it. The משנה first explains that a bird חטאת with a שלא לשמה intention mixed in can never become פיגול. It then rules that mixing a wrong-place intention with a wrong-time intention makes the offering פסול without כרת. רבי יהודה says the first intention decides, and the חכמים say order does not matter. Half a כזית for eating and half for burning do not combine, so the offering stays כשר. The גמרא then reads the פסוקים of the bird עולה word by word: one bird is enough, a כהן must do the מליקה with his own body, the head is pinched from the back of the neck and fully separated, and the blood is drained on the upper wall from the סובב. רבי נחמיה and רבי אליעזר בן יעקב hold that the whole service must be at the top of the מזבח, and אביי and רבא explain the practical difference between the views. Finally, ברייתות explain how the crop is removed together with its feathers.

Opening lines

חַטַּאת הָעוֹף (שֶׁמְּלָקוֹ) [שֶׁמְּלָקָהּ] שֶׁלֹּא לִשְׁמָהּ,

A bird sin offering that he pinched not for its sake,

וּמִיצָּה הַדָּם חוּץ לִזְמַנּוֹ;

and squeezed out the blood with intent for beyond its time;
