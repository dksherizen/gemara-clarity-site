Topic

Whether the leftover blood (שירים) is essential to an offering, and the start of the משנה on communal and individual חטאות

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a challenge about squeezing out bird-offering blood (מיצוי), and the גמרא answers that two תנאים reported רבי ישמעאל's view differently. רמי בר חמא then says that a certain תנא holds the leftover blood (שירים) is essential. He quotes a ברייתא that uses the word ״אוֹתָהּ״ to teach that a חטאת whose blood belongs above the red line is invalid if the blood was placed below. The ברייתא tries several logical proofs for this rule, rejects each one, and falls back on the פסוק. רמי בר חמא argues that the ברייתא's phrase 'the inner מזבח does not complete them' refers to the leftovers. רבא answers that if that were so, a simple קל וחומר would have proven the rule without a פסוק, so the phrase must mean the sprinklings on the פרוכת. Next comes a ברייתא in which רבי עקיבא and רבי יהודה read ״וְכִלָּה מִכַּפֵּר״ in opposite ways. רבי יוחנן and רבי יהושע בן לוי disagree about whether their dispute is only about how to read the פסוק or about whether שירים are essential. The attempt to prove which אמורא holds which view is rejected. Finally, a new משנה begins the laws of the communal and individual חטאות.
