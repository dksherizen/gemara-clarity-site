Zevachim 77a

זבחים ע״ז ע״א

Topic

The doubtful מצורע's conditional offerings, and רבי אליעזר's rule that unfit limbs may go up on the מזבח 'as wood'

Overview

The amud opens by finishing רבי שמעון's plan for a person who may or may not be a מצורע. He brings his לוג of oil with a condition, and if he is not a מצורע the sprinkling counts only 'as wood.' The גמרא handles three problems with this plan. Leftover oil that never had a קמיצה is redeemed. The redemption happens inside the עזרה, because the oil becomes חולין there on its own. רבי שמעון allows donating oil here because it is needed to purify a person. רב רחומי then suggests making the animal an אשם תלוי instead, and tries to prove רבי שמעון's view from this. רבינא rejects him: the מצורע brings a lamb, but an אשם תלוי must be a ram. A new משנה follows about חטאת limbs mixed with עולה limbs. רבי אליעזר puts them all on the מזבח and treats the חטאת meat as wood, learning this from 'לא יעלו לריח ניחח'. The רבנן limit that rule to leaven and honey. A ברייתא of רבי יהודה says the real argument is about blemished limbs. The גמרא then asks why רבי אליעזר does not treat a רובע or נרבע the same way, and the discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לְשֵׁם עֵצִים.

For the sake of wood.

וְהָא אִיכָּא שִׁירַיִים

But there is the remainder

דְּבָעֵי (מילינהו) [מֵיכְלִינְהוּ],

which needs filling up (or, in other versions, eating),

וְאִיכָּא הָךְ פּוּרְתָּא

and there is that small bit
