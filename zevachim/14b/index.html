Topic

Who may do הולכה (a זר or only a כהן), and whether הולכה done without walking counts as הולכה.

Overview

The גמרא finishes a ברייתא that says a קרבן is פסול if certain people, including a זר, do קבלה, הולכה, or זריקה. This refutes רב חסדא, who held that a זר's הולכה is כשר. The פסוק he relied on is explained differently: the non-כהנים only held the blood like a shelf. רבה and רב יוסף then say the question depends on a מחלוקת. רבי שמעון holds that a skippable עבודה is not a real עבודה, so a זר may do it, while the רבנן disagree. Challenges from שחיטה, the פרה אדומה, and carrying the limbs to the ramp test that idea. A קל וחומר shows that carrying the blood should need a כהן, and עולא reports that even רבי שמעון agrees a זר's הולכה is פסול. Finally the גמרא asks whether moving the blood without walking counts as הולכה. Several proofs are pushed away, and רבי יוחנן rules that it does not count.

בְּקַבָּלָה בְּהוֹלָכָה בִּזְרִיקָה – פָּסוּל.

in collecting, in carrying, in sprinkling – it is disqualified.
