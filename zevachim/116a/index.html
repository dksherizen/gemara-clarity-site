Topic

Offerings before the משכן: which animals were allowed, whether בני נח brought שלמים, and when יתרו came

Overview

The גמרא explains a ברייתא about the offerings brought before the משכן was set up. Back then, males, females and even blemished animals could be offered, because animals were compared to birds. An animal missing a limb was still not allowed, and רבי אלעזר learns this from 'ומכל החי'. After a long back-and-forth, the גמרא concludes that a טריפה is excluded through 'אתך', because the animals had to be like נח, who was whole. 'לחיות זרע' then teaches that the animals had to be able to reproduce. Next the גמרא explains how נח knew which animals were 'pure': either the תיבה took them in, or they came on their own. It then discusses whether בני נח brought שלמים or only עולות. This becomes a מחלוקת tied to the question of when יתרו came, before or after מתן תורה. The amud ends with three תנאים explaining what news brought יתרו: the war with עמלק, מתן תורה (with the story of the kings and בלעם), or קריעת ים סוף.

Opening lines

זְכָרִים וּנְקֵבוֹת, תְּמִימִין וּבַעֲלֵי מוּמִין –

Males and females, unblemished and blemished —

דְּאָמַר מָר:

this is as the Master said:

תַּמּוּת וְזַכְרוּת בִּבְהֵמָה,

being unblemished and being male apply to animals,

וְאֵין תַּמּוּת וְזַכְרוּת בְּעוֹפוֹת.

but being unblemished and being male do not apply to birds.
