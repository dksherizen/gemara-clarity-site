Zevachim 102b

זבחים ק״ב ע״ב

Topic

Which כהנים get a share of the קדשים: the rule 'not fit, no share,' timing of purity, and the טבול יום debate

Overview

This amud is about which כהנים get a share of the קדשים meat. It first finishes explaining why a כהן with a temporary מום gets a share right away. It then tests the משנה's rule, 'whoever is not fit gets no share.' After trying 'fit to eat,' the גמרא goes back to 'fit for the עבודה.' The answer is that the משנה only says who is left out, and the בעל מום is a special inclusion from a פסוק. Next, the משנה makes purity at זריקת דמים the deciding moment, which disagrees with אבא שאול. אבא שאול requires purity until הקטר חלבים, and רב אשי's question about becoming טמא in between ends in תיקו. רבא then retells רבי אלעזר ברבי שמעון's debate between a טבול יום and a pure כהן over a מנחה, a חטאת and the חזה ושוק. Each time the logic fails, but a פסוק shows that only the כהן who can serve gets to eat. רב אחאי asks why a בכור round is missing, and the גמרא explains that the בכור פסוק says 'yours,' so the proof would not have worked. Finally, the גמרא explains why this was taught in the bathroom: he could not help thinking about it.

Opening lines

כִּי טָמֵא;

[he is] like an impure one;

מָה טָמֵא –

just as an impure one –

כַּמָּה דְּלָא טָהוֹר לָא אָכֵיל,

as long as he is not pure, he does not eat,

אַף הַאי נָמֵי –

so too this one also –
