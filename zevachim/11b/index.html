Zevachim 11b

זבחים י״א ע״ב

Open Zevachim 11b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

When an offering slaughtered for the sake of another offering stays kosher (רבי אליעזר, שמעון אחי עזריה), and בן בתירא's view that the whole 14th is the time of the פסח

Overview

The amud opens with רבי יהושע challenging רבי אליעזר's קל וחומר about offerings slaughtered for the sake of a פסח. רבי אליעזר answers that a פסח is special because it is kosher for its own sake only at its set time. The גמרא then turns to שמעון אחי עזריה, who says an offering slaughtered for the sake of a holier offering stays kosher, and it finds his source in the words ״אשר ירימו״. That same פסוק also teaches שמואל's law about eating טבל. רבי זירא asks whether, for שמעון אחי עזריה, such offerings also count for the owner. A proof from the בכור clause of the משנה is rejected, because that clause is needed to teach 'higher and lower' even among קדשים קלים. A new משנה follows. It brings the מחלוקת of רבי יהושע and בן בתירא about a פסח slaughtered שלא לשמו on the morning of the 14th, and בן עזאי's tradition from the seventy-two elders. The גמרא explains that for בן בתירא the whole 14th is the time of the פסח, because he reads 'בין הערבים' as 'between the two evenings.' It then tests this reading against the תמיד, the lamps of the מנורה and the קטרת, and the answer about the קטרת continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אָמַר לוֹ רַבִּי יְהוֹשֻׁעַ:

Rabbi Yehoshua said to him:

מָצִינוּ מוֹתַר חַטָּאת בָּא עוֹלָה,

We find that a leftover sin offering is brought as a burnt offering,

וְאֵין מוֹתַר עוֹלָה בָּא חַטָּאת;

but a leftover burnt offering is not brought as a sin offering.

וּמָה חַטָּאת, שֶׁמּוֹתָרָהּ בָּא עוֹלָה –

Now, just as a sin offering, whose leftover is brought as a burnt offering,
