Topic

Blood brought into the היכל: רבי עקיבא's source, the two-cups משנה, and the debate over קל וחומר versus the פסוק

Overview

The amud opens by explaining why the משנה did not suggest placing mixed blood inside first and then outside. That fix would not work for every קרבן, because a חטאת (and, for רבי אליעזר, an אשם) becomes פסול once its blood goes inside. The גמרא then looks for the source of רבי עקיבא's rule that the blood of all קדשים becomes פסול if it is brought into the היכל. שמואל explains it with a משל about a student mixing a drink. רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע rejects that reading and says the source is the extra words 'כל' and 'וכל' in front of 'חטאת'. A ברייתא shows רבי יוסי הגלילי using those same words only for other kinds of חטאת, and the גמרא explains that he was only arguing according to רבי עקיבא's approach. A new משנה then covers blood split into two cups, the question of when blood brought inside becomes פסול, and the limits of the ציץ. The גמרא brings the ברייתא where רבי יוסי הגלילי and the חכמים trade קל וחומר arguments and answers from the פסוק 'אשר יובא מדמה'. It ends by starting to explain, from 'ביום השלישי', why intent to bring blood inside does not disqualify, and that explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְנִיתֵּיב לְפָנִים

And let him place [blood] inside first

וַהֲדַר נִיתֵּיב לַחוּץ?

and then place [blood] outside?

כֵּיוָן דְּאִיכָּא חַטָּאת וְאָשָׁם

Since there are a sin offering and a guilt offering

דְּכִי נִכְנַס דָּמָן פְּסוּלִין

which, when their blood enters [inside], are disqualified
