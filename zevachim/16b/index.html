Topic

How we know an אונן's עבודה is invalid (via הצד השוה and רב משרשיא's קל וחומר), and רבא's limit of the rule to a קרבן יחיד, challenged by רבא בר אהילאי

Overview

The גמרא wants to prove that an אונן who does the עבודה makes it invalid. A קל וחומר from a בעל מום is pushed back, and so is a proof from a זר. Together they form a הצד השוה: anyone forbidden to serve ruins the עבודה if he serves. The גמרא then asks where an אונן is forbidden to serve, and finds the ban in 'הן הקריבו'. The הצד השוה is attacked, because an אונן's ban has an exception for a כהן גדול. A טמא is added to answer that attack, and the three-way proof stands. רב משרשיא offers another proof by קל וחומר from a כהן who serves sitting. It survives the objection that a sitting person is unfit to testify. Next, רבא limits the משנה: an אונן disqualifies only a קרבן יחיד, and he learns this by קל וחומר from טומאה. רבא בר אהילאי shows three times that this kind of קל וחומר can be flipped to reach false results, and a סימן sums up his three arguments.

Opening lines

בַּעַל מוּם, שֶׁאוֹכֵל –

A blemished kohen, who does eat [sacrificial meat]:

אִם עָבַד חִילֵּל;

if he served, he desecrated [the service];

אוֹנֵן, שֶׁאֵין אוֹכֵל –

a mourner, who does not eat [sacrificial meat]:

אֵינוֹ דִּין שֶׁאִם עָבַד חִילֵּל?!

is it not logical that if he served, he desecrated?!
