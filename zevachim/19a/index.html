Zevachim 19a

זבחים י״ט ע״א

Open Zevachim 19a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The כהן's belt, and what counts as extra garments or a barrier (חציצה) between a כהן's body and his priestly garments, including תפילין

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the previous discussion: the כהנים wore the אבנט at elbow height. רב אשי then tells how a Persian king straightened הונא בר נתן's belt because the Jews are 'a kingdom of כהנים', and אמימר calls this a fulfillment of ישעיהו's promise. Next the גמרא quotes a משנה from עירובין: a כהן with a hurt finger may wrap it with a reed in the מקדש on שבת. רבי יהודה בריה דרבי חייא and רבי יוחנן argue over whether a small cloth wrap is allowed, given in two versions: one about יתור בגדים (extra garments) and one about חציצה (a barrier), with רבא's rule in the name of רב חסדא about a three-by-three size. The גמרא then lists many questions about what blocks the garment from being 'on his flesh': wind, a louse, fine dust, the armpit, his own hand, a dangling thread and his hair. רבי זירא asks whether תפילין block, and רבי אמי answers from a settled tradition that they do. After challenges from ברייתות, the גמרא concludes that hand תפילין block and head תפילין do not. It begins explaining why the head is different, and that explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

כְּנֶגֶד אַצִּילֵי יְדֵיהֶן.

At the level of their elbows.

אָמַר רַב אָשֵׁי, אָמַר לִי הוּנָא בַּר נָתָן:

Rav Ashi said: Huna bar Natan said to me:

זִימְנָא חֲדָא הֲוָה קָאֵימְנָא

Once I was standing

קַמֵּיהּ דְּאִיזְגַּדַּר מַלְכָּא

before Izgadar the king,

The steps (42)

  1. StatementThe belt is worn at elbow level
  2. StatementA Persian king fixes a Jew's belt

  6. StatementExtra garments only where garments are worn

  11. ResponseA small finger wrap is different because it is important
  12. StatementSecond version: רבי יהודה says only a reed is allowed
  13. StatementSecond version: רבי יוחנן's size rule matches רב חסדא
  14. QuestionDoes this argue with רבי יהודה?
  15. ResponseA small sash is important
  16. ChallengeWhy did the משנה choose a reed?
  17. ResolutionThe משנה teaches that a reed heals
  18. QuestionWhat if wind lifts the garment off his body?
  19. QuestionDoes a louse under the garment count as a barrier?
  20. QuestionThe question is only about a live louse
  21. QuestionDoes dirt count as a barrier?
  22. ChallengeDirt is obviously a barrier
  23. ResolutionThe real question is about fine dust
  24. QuestionDoes the gap at the armpit count as a barrier?
  25. QuestionHis hand tucked inside his garment
  26. QuestionA thread under the garment

  29. QuestionHis hair inside his garment
  30. QuestionDo תפילין count as a barrier?

  32. QuestionThe real question: does a מצוה on the body block?
  33. Responseרבי אמי: we have a clear tradition that תפילין block

  39. ResolutionHand תפילין block; head תפילין do not
  40. QuestionWhy are the hand תפילין different?
  41. ChallengeThe head has a similar פסוק
  42. ResolutionThe hair showed between the ציץ and the מצנפת
