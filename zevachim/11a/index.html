Topic

Why an אשם brought inside stays כשר, what the comparisons of מנחה and אשם to חטאת teach, and offerings slaughtered as a פסח.

Overview

The גמרא first tries to prove by logic that an אשם whose blood was brought inside the היכל stays כשר. It uses עולה and מנחת חוטא, and finally a הצד השוה of both, but each proof is knocked down, the last one by רבא מברניש. So the גמרא concludes that the רבנן learn it from the word 'דמה': only a חטאת becomes פסול this way. It then shows how רבי אליעזר and the רבנן each use that word. Next, it asks what the comparison of a מנחה to both a חטאת and an אשם teaches each side. For the רבנן it teaches about שלא לשמה, and for רבי אליעזר it teaches רבי שמעון's rule about the right hand and a כלי שרת. For the רבנן, the comparison of אשם to חטאת teaches סמיכה. Finally, רבי יוחנן and רבה discuss how far יוסף בן חוני agrees with רבי אליעזר, and a ברייתא brings the debate between רבי אליעזר and רבי יהושע about other offerings slaughtered as a פסח on the 14th. That debate continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

עוֹלָה שֶׁהִיא כָּלִיל,

A burnt offering, which is entirely consumed,

נִכְנַס דָּמָהּ לִפְנִים כְּשֵׁרָה;

if its blood entered inside, it is fit;

אָשָׁם שֶׁאֵינוֹ כָּלִיל,

a guilt offering, which is not entirely consumed,

לֹא כׇּל שֶׁכֵּן?!

is it not all the more so?!
