Topic

Wooden versus earthenware כלי שרת, and the laws of מריקה ושטיפה for pots used to cook קדשים, including part of a vessel, which offerings it covers, and תרומה

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier point: even רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה, who accepts a כלי שרת made of wood, does not accept one made of earthenware. Then comes a story. רב יצחק בר יהודה left רמי בר חמא for רב ששת, because רב ששת answers from a משנה, while רמי answers from reasoning that one משנה can knock down. To test him, רב יצחק asks whether a pot used to cook a קרבן in only part of it needs מריקה ושטיפה in full. רמי says no and compares it to a garment splashed with blood. A ברייתא proves him wrong: the whole vessel needs it, and the source is the פסוק 'in a copper vessel'. The גמרא then brings a ברייתא that extends מריקה ושטיפה from חטאת to all קדשים, but not to תרומה, according to רבי יהודה. רבי שמעון limits it to קדשי קדשים. When a ברייתא shows that a תרומה pot does need cleaning, אביי, רבא and רבה בר עולא each explain that the פסוק frees תרומה from only one strict detail. Finally, a new משנה records רבי טרפון and חכמים arguing over how long a pot may be used during a רגל, and defines מריקה and שטיפה as done with cold water.
