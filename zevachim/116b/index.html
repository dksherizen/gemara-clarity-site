Zevachim 116b

זבחים קט״ז ע״ב

Learn Zevachim 116b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Zevachim 116b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The end of the רחב story, offerings brought by גוים outside the מקדש, דוד's purchase of the altar site, and the camps of the wilderness, ירושלים and שילה.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the אגדה about רחב הזונה: how she knew the fear of the Canaanite leaders, her age, and her conversion at fifty. The גמרא then learns from 'דבר אל בני ישראל' that the ban on שחוטי חוץ applies only to Jews, so a גוי may offer on his own במה even today. רב אסי says a Jew may not help or act as the גוי's messenger, and רבה says a Jew may still teach him how. The story of רבא and the offering of איפרא הורמיז shows this in practice. אביי asks why רבא required new wood. That question leads to the ברייתא about the 50 and 600 shekels דוד paid ארונה, and to the meaning of the word מוריגים. Next the גמרא explains the משנה that קדשים קלים were eaten throughout the מחנה ישראל in the wilderness: the camp kept its status even while traveling. Finally, רבי שמעון בן יוחאי teaches that שילה had only two camps, and רבה begins proving which camp was missing. That proof continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דַּאֲפִילּוּ אִקַּשּׁוֹיֵי נָמֵי לָא אִקַּשּׁוּ.

That they could not even become erect.

וּמְנָא יָדְעָה?

And how did she know?

דְּאָמַר מָר:

As the Master said:

אֵין לָךְ כׇּל שַׂר וְנָגִיד

You do not have any prince or ruler
