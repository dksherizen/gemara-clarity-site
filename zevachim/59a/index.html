Zevachim 59a

זבחים נ״ט ע״א

Topic

Where the כיור and מזבח stood, and the law of a damaged מזבח

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a ברייתא in which רבי יוסי הגלילי says only the מזבח stands at the entrance of the אהל מועד. He places the כיור between the אולם and the מזבח, pulled off to the south. The גמרא tests several views of where the מזבח stood and asks each time why the כיור was not placed directly between them, clear of the doorway. It concludes that he holds the whole מזבח was in the north, and that the word 'צפונה' keeps the north empty of vessels. רבי אליעזר בן יעקב disagrees: for him the north must be empty even of the מזבח. Next, רב rules that קרבנות slaughtered while the מזבח is damaged are פסול, and his forgotten source is found in 'וזבחת עליו'. רבי יוחנן disqualifies even living animals, and the two argue about whether בעלי חיים נדחים. The גמרא then defends רב against a ברייתא and against his own ruling about incense, explaining that blood needs a real מזבח. The amud ends with רבי יהודה's view that שלמה made the courtyard holy, and רבי יוסי's reply continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מִזְבַּח הָעוֹלָה וְגוֹ׳״ –

“The altar of the burnt offering, etc.” —

מִזְבֵּחַ בְּפֶתַח אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד,

the altar is at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting,

וְלֹא כִּיּוֹר בְּפֶתַח אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד.

but the Basin is not at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

הֵיכָן הָיָה נוֹתְנוֹ?

Where would he place it?
