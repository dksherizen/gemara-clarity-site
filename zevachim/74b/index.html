Topic

Mixtures: assuming the lost item was the forbidden one, fixing barrels of תרומה, how a טריפה gets mixed in, and סמיכה in mixed-up קרבנות

Overview

The גמרא first explains that a ברייתא's 'three' items means two permitted items plus the doubtful one that fell in, so a simple majority is enough. It then offers a second source for שמואל's strict view: he follows רבי אליעזר. ריש לקיש rules that if a barrel of תרומה is mixed with a hundred barrels and one barrel falls into ים המלח, the rest are permitted, because we assume the forbidden one fell. The גמרא explains why both this ruling and רב נחמן's ruling about rings of עבודה זרה are needed. רבה and רב יוסף then argue whether ריש לקיש's rule also works for a fig. רבי אלעזר, as reworded by רב נחמן, and רבי אושעיא teach how an opened barrel in a mixture of תרומה can be fixed, while closed barrels stay forbidden. Next, the גמרא asks how a טריפה can be mixed in with קרבנות without being recognized. רבי ינאי's school, ריש לקיש and רבי ירמיה each give a different case, and the גמרא explains why each rejects the others' answers. Finally, the גמרא asks how mixed-up קרבנות can be offered when each one needs סמיכה. רב יוסף limits the משנה to women's קרבנות, and the גמרא begins to question that answer.
