Zevachim 89b

זבחים פ״ט ע״ב

Learn Zevachim 89b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Zevachim 89b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The order of offerings (blood, limbs, and parts of חטאת, עולה, אשם and others), and whether אימורים of קדשים קלים that leave the עזרה before זריקה are פסול.

Overview

The גמרא asks where the משנה's order comes from: the blood of a חטאת goes before the blood of an עולה, but the limbs of an עולה are burned before the parts of a חטאת. A ברייתא learns this from two פסוקים about the bulls of the לויים, one calling the חטאת 'second' and one naming it first. The גמרא asks why all of the חטאת's blood placements go first, and רבינא and the scholars of ארץ ישראל each give an answer. Next come three open questions about mixed pairs: חטאת blood against עולה limbs, עולה blood against חטאת parts, and עולה blood against אשם blood. Each proof from the משנה is pushed aside. The גמרא then challenges each step of the משנה's order with 'אדרבה' and defends it with 'אפילו הכי'. Along the way, a ברייתא settles that a תודה comes before an איל נזיר. Finally, רבינא בר שילא rules that אימורים of קדשים קלים that leave the עזרה before the blood is sprinkled are פסול. The גמרא supports this from the משנה, tests a proof from רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש, and begins comparing it to a dispute between תנאים that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

גְּמָ׳

Gemara:

מְנָא הָנֵי מִילֵּי?

From where are these matters derived?

דְּתָנוּ רַבָּנַן:

As the Sages taught:

״וּפַר שֵׁנִי בֶן בָּקָר תִּקַּח לְחַטָּאת״ –

"And a second young bull you shall take for a sin offering"
