Topic

Which animals' fat is excluded from נבלה impurity, how far רבי מאיר's ruling about pinched birds reaches, the status of the עגלה ערופה, and the opening of the new פרק on mixed sacrifices.

Overview

The ברייתא finishes working out which animals' fat the פסוק about נבלה and טרפה is talking about. The word טרפה rules out non-kosher animals, and 'you shall not eat it' rules out a kosher חיה, so only a kosher בהמה is left. רבא explains to רב יעקב בר אבא that the end of the ברייתא is about a non-kosher bird, so a non-kosher animal's carcass is still טמא. The גמרא then returns to רבי מאיר's ruling in the משנה. רבי יוחנן limits it to unblemished birds, while רבי אלעזר extends it to blemished birds and even to geese and chickens. רבי ירמיה asks whether breaking a goat's neck would work the same way. רב דימי explains that the question assumes the עגלה ערופה itself is טהור, because the Torah speaks of כפרה with it. רב נתן challenges this from a ברייתא about its fat, and the גמרא answers that the ברייתא is about a calf that died, which leads to the rule that the calf becomes forbidden once it goes down to the valley. The פרק ends with הדרן, and a new משנה begins about sacrifices mixed with forbidden animals; its second case continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

כְּשֶׁבָּא בְּדֶרֶךְ זוֹ –

When one approaches it this way,

and when one approaches it that way,

הִיא בִּטְמֵאָה;

it is about a non-kosher animal;
