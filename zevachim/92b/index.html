Topic

Which חטאת blood requires כיבוס, and רבי אבין's and לוי's questions about the blood of a bird offering and of blood sprayed from garment to garment

Overview

The תורה says that if blood of a חטאת splashes on a garment, the garment must be washed (כיבוס). The גמרא first finishes showing that the inner sin offerings share many more features with the eaten animal חטאת than the bird offering does, so the bird חטאת is the one left out. רב יוסף gives another source: "יאכלנה" shows the excluded offering is an eaten one, and "זאת" shows there is an exclusion at all. רבה reads "אשר יזה" (sprinkling) to mean the passage is mainly about the inner offerings. "תורת" then adds the outer animal חטאת, and "זאת" leaves out the bird, because the animal offering is far more similar. רבי אבין then asks two questions about bird blood: is carrying a bird into the היכל with blood in its neck like bringing in a vessel, and is bird blood gathered from the floor valid? Proofs are suggested for each, and both are rejected. Finally לוי asks whether blood that sprays from one garment onto another still needs washing. רבי answers yes either way, relying on רבי עקיבא.

Opening lines

וְקֶרֶן, וְאֶצְבַּע,

and the corner, and the finger,

וְחוּדָּהּ, וְאִישִּׁים.

and its edge, and the altar fires.

אַדְּרַבָּה, חַטַּאת הָעוֹף הֲוָה לֵיהּ לְרַבּוֹיֵי –

On the contrary, he should have included the bird sin offering –

שֶׁכֵּן חוּץ כְּמוֹתָהּ,

since it is like it in being on the outer altar,

The steps (29)

  1. ResponseFinishing the list of shared features
  2. ChallengeChallenge: the bird offering seems more similar
  3. ResolutionAnswer: the inner offerings share more features
  4. Statementרב יוסף: "יאכלנה" points the exclusion at an eaten offering
  5. ChallengeThen why do we need "זאת"?
  6. ResolutionBoth words are needed
  7. Statementרבה: The verse speaks about sprinkled blood
  8. ChallengeBut the משנה says the verse is about eaten offerings
  9. ResolutionThe משנה means scouring pots, not washing clothes
  10. ChallengeThen the משנה's word order is backwards
  11. ResolutionSwitch the order in the משנה
  12. ChallengeThen the bird sin offering should need washing too
  13. ResolutionThe word זאת leaves out the bird offering
  14. ChallengeThen the outer sin offering should be left out too
  15. ResolutionThe word תורת adds the outer sin offering
  16. QuestionWhy include one and exclude the other?
  17. ResponseThe animal sin offering is much more similar
  18. ChallengeBut the bird offering is also sprinkled
  19. ResolutionMore shared features wins
  20. QuestionA bird sin offering carried inside with blood in its neck
  21. ProofProof from a bird that jerked inside
  22. RejectionThe same inference would fail for animals moved to the south
  23. ResolutionThe ברייתא was really contrasting with going outside the עזרה
  24. Questionרבי אבין asks about bird blood that spilled and was gathered
  25. QuestionThe two sides: no vessel needed, or a vessel ruled out
  26. Proofרבא's proof from the word 'This'
  27. RejectionThe case of a vessel pressed against the neck
  28. QuestionLevi asks about blood spraying from one garment to another
  29. ResponseEither way, the second garment must be laundered
