Zevachim 34b

זבחים ל״ד ע״ב

Topic

When a flawed blood service creates שירים, and when blood becomes דחוי

Overview

The amud opens by closing an earlier discussion: a ברייתא refutes ריש לקיש. The גמרא then asks when a flawed blood service turns the rest of the blood into שירים. רבי יוחנן says only a service done with intent for outside its time or place does this, because only such a service can make the offering פיגול. Three versions of the question follow. One asks about an unfit person, one asks about a cup of disqualified blood, and one asks about an unused valid cup, which ties into a dispute between the רבנן and רבי אלעזר ברבי שמעון. Next, the גמרא explains why the משנה needs three cases: an unfit person, the left hand, and a non-sacred vessel. It then asks why the blood is not דחוי in these cases. רבינא says the משנה follows חנן המצרי, who holds there is no דחוי. רב אשי says anything a person can fix himself is not דחוי, and רב שייא backs רב אשי from רבי יהודה's own rulings. The amud ends with the חכמים challenging רבי יהודה's ruling about sprinkling a cup of mixed blood, and his reply continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וּפָסוּל.

תְּיוּבְתָּא דְּרֵישׁ לָקִישׁ!

תְּיוּבְתָּא.

It is indeed a refutation.

And all of them that collected, etc.

The steps (30)

  1. Conclusionריש לקיש's view is refuted
  2. QuestionDoes an unfit person's service turn the rest of the blood into leftovers?
  3. ResponseOnly time or place intent creates leftovers
  4. Questionרב זביד's version: the question was about a disqualified cup
  5. ResponseThe cup follows the same rule as the unfit person
  6. Questionרב ירמיה's version: does one cup make the other cup rejected or leftover?
  7. Responseרבה: this is a known dispute
  8. ProofThe ברייתא sets up two פסוקים side by side
  9. StatementPlacements from all four cups: all the leftover blood goes to the base
  10. StatementAll four placements from one cup: only that cup goes to the base
  11. Statementרבי אלעזר ברבי שמעון: all four cups go to the base
  12. ChallengeBut another verse says only 'its blood'
  13. Resolutionרב אשי: 'its blood' excludes the blood left in the neck
  14. StatementQuoting the משנה: a fit priest handed the blood to an unfit person
  15. StatementWhy the unfit-person case alone would not be enough
  16. StatementWhy the left-hand case alone would not be enough
  17. ConclusionThe non-sacred vessel case alone would not teach the others, so all are needed
  18. ChallengeShouldn't the blood become permanently rejected?
  19. ResolutionThe משנה follows חנן המצרי, who does not hold of rejection
  20. ProofA ברייתא showing that חנן המצרי does not hold of rejection
  21. ResolutionAnything a person can fix himself is not rejected
  22. Statementרב שייא says רב אשי's answer is more reasonable
  23. ProofProof that רבי יהודה holds of rejection: the goats of יום הכיפורים
  24. ProofYet רבי יהודה says what is in one's power to fix is not rejected
  25. ConclusionConclusion: anything in one's power to fix is not rejected
  26. StatementThe full ברייתא: why fill one cup from the mixed blood
  27. ChallengeThe חכמים object: the mixed blood was not received in a vessel
  28. QuestionHow could the חכמים know that?
  29. ResponseRather, the חכמים meant: perhaps it was not received in a vessel
  30. Resolutionרבי יהודה begins his answer
