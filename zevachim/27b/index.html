Zevachim 27b

זבחים כ״ז ע״ב

Learn Zevachim 27b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Zevachim 27b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Disqualified offerings on the altar (wrongly placed blood, the inner versus outer altar), and a new משנה on wrong intent about place versus time

Overview

This amud starts by finishing a discussion about blood put on the wrong spot: above instead of below the red line on the altar, or inside instead of outside (or the reverse). The גמרא says our משנה fits the view that such blood may not be gathered up and redone. רב חסדא, quoting אבימי, says everyone agrees about above/below, and רבי יוסי and רבי שמעון argue only about inside/outside. רב נחמן בר יצחק then brings a ברייתא about which disqualified offerings must come down from the altar and which stay. Offerings that went wrong during the holy service stay, and animals that were unfit from the start come down. Since רבי יהודה does not argue about blood placed above or below, the altar must have 'taken hold' of it, so it may not be gathered up. Next, רבי אליעזר teaches that the inner altar also holds onto disqualified items, even a קומץ that never belongs there. The inner altar is like a כלי שרת, while the outer altar is like the floor. Finally, a new משנה teaches that a plan to do the service outside its place makes an offering פסול without כרת, while a plan to do it the next day makes it פיגול with כרת. The גמרא begins discussing the skin of the tail, and this continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וּמִשְׁנָתֵינוּ כְּדִבְרֵי

And our Mishnah is in line with the words of

הָאוֹמֵר לֹא יַאַסְפֶנּוּ.

the one who says: he may not gather it up.

וְרַב חִסְדָּא אָמַר אֲבִימִי:

And Rav Chisda said in the name of Avimi:

הַכֹּל מוֹדִין בַּנִּיתָּנִין לְמַטָּה

Everyone agrees about blood meant to be put below

The steps (19)

  1. ConclusionOur משנה fits the view that the blood may not be gathered up
  2. Statementרב חסדא's version: everyone agrees about above and below, the argument is only about inside and outside
  3. Proofרב נחמן בר יצחק brings a ברייתא: רבי יהודה's three exclusions
  4. Proofרבי שמעון's list of disqualified offerings that do not come down
  5. ResponseThe words 'תורת העולה' include all disqualified offerings that went up
  6. StatementThe word 'זאת' leaves out animals that were never fit
  7. QuestionWhy include the first group and exclude the second?
  8. ResponseDisqualified in holiness versus never fit at all
  9. Proofרבי יהודה agrees, so the altar has taken hold of the blood
  10. StatementThe inner altar also holds onto disqualified items
  11. ChallengeWhy do we need this? We already learned it
  12. ResolutionBlood fits the inner altar, but a קומץ does not
  13. ChallengeA ברייתא says only the outer altar holds disqualified items
  14. ResolutionReading the ברייתא correctly: only the outer altar is limited
  15. QuestionWhy the difference between the two altars?
  16. ResponseThe outer altar is like the floor, the inner altar is like a holy vessel
  17. StatementPlanning to do the service outside its place: פסול, but no כרת
  18. StatementPlanning to do the service the next day: פיגול, with כרת
