Zevachim 109a

זבחים ק״ט ע״א

Open Zevachim 109a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Offering outside the עזרה (העלאה בחוץ), and when an עולה's meat and אימורים combine to a כזית

Overview

The משנה teaches that a person is liable for offering קדשים outside the Temple courtyard. This is true both for fit offerings and for offerings that became unfit inside the holy area. It also teaches that a כזית made of עולה meat together with its אימורים adds up to make him liable. A ברייתא learns these rules from the פסוק in ויקרא 17 in three layers. עולה teaches the burnt offering, זבח adds the אימורים of all other offerings, and פתח אהל מועד adds the קומץ, קטורת, מנחות and נסכים. The words לא יביאנו לעשות then add פסולין that the altar would accept. Next the גמרא reads the משנה closely: only an עולה's meat and אימורים combine, not those of שלמים. A ברייתא says the same and adds that they combine for פיגול, נותר and טמא. The גמרא challenges this from a משנה that says all פיגול and all נותר combine. It answers that the ברייתא is about the thought that makes פיגול, and about a case where only a כזית was left before the blood was sprinkled. It says this ברייתא follows רבי יהושע and begins quoting his ruling, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳

MISHNA:

אֶחָד קֳדָשִׁים כְּשֵׁרִין

Both fit sacrificial animals

וְאֶחָד קָדָשִׁים פְּסוּלִין,

and unfit sacrificial animals

שֶׁהָיָה פְּסוּלָן בַּקּוֹדֶשׁ

whose disqualification happened in the holy area

The steps (16)

  1. StatementFit and unfit offerings sacrificed outside
  2. StatementAn olive-bulk made of meat and sacrificial portions
  3. QuestionThe verse mentions only a burnt offering: what about other sacrificial portions?
  4. ResponseThe word 'sacrifice' includes all sacrificial portions
  5. QuestionWhat about the handful, incense, priestly meal offerings, and libations?
  6. ResponseAnything brought to the Tent of Meeting is included
  7. QuestionWhat about offerings that became unfit?
  8. ResponseAnything the altar accepts makes you liable
  9. StatementClose reading of the משנה: עולה yes, שלמים no
  10. ProofA ברייתא says the same thing as our משנה
  11. ChallengeWhy would פיגול, נותר and טמא depend on the עולה?
  12. ChallengeA משנה says all פיגול and all נותר combine
  13. ResolutionEating פיגול versus the thought that makes פיגול
  14. ResolutionThe נותר contradiction is answered: eating versus what was left before sprinkling
  15. QuestionWhose opinion is the ברייתא?
  16. ResponseIt is רבי יהושע, and the גמרא begins quoting his ברייתא
