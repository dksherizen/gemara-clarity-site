Zevachim 110b

זבחים ק״י ע״ב

Topic

Offering outside the עזרה: blood sprinklings, the סוכות water libation and its source, and whether libation water has a fixed measure

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה about which acts done outside the עזרה make a person חייב. רבי אלעזר adds pouring the סוכות water libation, and רבי נחמיה adds offering the leftover blood. The גמרא first quotes רבא, who says רבי אלעזר agrees that even one sprinkling of blood done outside makes one חייב. The proof is רבי אלעזר's ruling about the יום כיפור sprinklings: if they are interrupted, the כהן גדול picks up from where he stopped. Next, רבי יוחנן says רבי אלעזר's water ruling follows רבי עקיבא, who learns ניסוך המים from the plural ״ונסכיה״. ריש לקיש objects that the water should then copy wine's rules, which are three לוגין and all year round. The גמרא answers with a teaching, given in רבי יוחנן's own name, that ניסוך המים is a הלכה למשה מסיני with its own rules. Finally, a ברייתא gives two views on pouring the water outside. רב נחמן בר יצחק explains that they argue over whether the water has a fixed measure, and רב פפא begins a second explanation that continues on the next amud.
