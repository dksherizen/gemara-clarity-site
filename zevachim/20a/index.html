Topic

Whether קידוש ידים ורגלים and the כיור water are affected by לינה (staying overnight)

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an explanation: a פסוק teaches that even services that do not hold back כפרה need קידוש ידים ורגלים. Then אילפא asks a question according to רבי אלעזר ברבי שמעון, who holds that washing is not undone overnight: does the water in the כיור become disqualified by לינה? One report says אילפא tied the water to the same dispute. רבי יצחק בר ביסנא objects. The tradition he knew from אילפא, 'the next day he does not wash from it,' was unclear, and the scholars never resolved it. The גמרא tries to prove the answer from the משנה about בן קטין's device for the כיור, but shows that this משנה can follow רבי. The bull's location in that משנה is explained by the weakness of the כהן גדול. Next, רבי יוחנן rules that washing for the pre-dawn תרומת הדשן still counts after dawn. אביי explains this according to רבי, and רבא explains it according to רבי אלעזר ברבי שמעון. The amud ends as the גמרא begins an objection from a משנה in תמיד, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַהוּ דְּתֵימָא:

You might have said:

הָנֵי מִילֵּי – עֲבוֹדָה דִּמְעַכְּבָא כַּפָּרָה,

this applies only to a service that holds back atonement,

אֲבָל עֲבוֹדָה דְּלָא מְעַכְּבָא כַּפָּרָה – לָא;

but a service that does not hold back atonement does not need it;

קָא מַשְׁמַע לַן.

so it teaches us otherwise.
