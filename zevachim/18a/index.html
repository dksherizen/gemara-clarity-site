Topic

The sources that a כהן lacking or misusing his garments (מחוסר בגדים) or a כהן who drank wine (שתויי יין) disqualifies the עבודה, and which garment problems disqualify.

Overview

The גמרא is looking for the source that a כהן serving without his proper garments (מחוסר בגדים) ruins the עבודה. A ברייתא links מחוסר בגדים, שתויי יין and unwashed hands and feet through the shared word "חוקה", but that link alone would cover only rites for which a זר is חייב מיתה. So a separate פסוק is needed to cover every rite. שתויי יין then borrows that full rule back from מחוסר בגדים through the same link. The פסוק "ולהבדיל" was the source only before the link was set up. Its lasting job is to teach that someone who drank wine should not give rulings, as רב practiced on יום טוב. The גמרא then shows why two more פסוקים are still needed. "ונתנו" adds rites that are not required for כפרה, and "וערכו" adds wearing extra garments, not just missing ones. Finally a ברייתא lists practical garment problems: garments that drag, are too short or are frayed are still כשר. A second list of cases begins, and its ruling comes on the next amud.
