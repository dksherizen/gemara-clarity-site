Topic

Whether parts of an offering like the fetus and placenta become פיגול along with the offering; רבי עקיבא on blemished animals and a female עולה; the start of a new משנה on wrong plans during שחיטה

Overview

The גמרא keeps testing רבי אלעזר's view that the flesh of an offering can be pulled into פיגול. First it rejects a proof from a משנה in מעילה about burned bulls and goats, saying its mention of לינה may mean only the אימורים. Then רבה challenges the view from a ברייתא that lists parts like the fetus and placenta as things that do not become פיגול. The גמרא answers that the ברייתא only means a wrong intention about those parts themselves does nothing. רבא then proves from our משנה that the fetus and placenta do become פיגול along with the offering. Next the גמרא turns to רבי עקיבא, who lets blemished animals stay on the מזבח. רבי יוחנן limits this to small eye blemishes that came after consecration, and רבי חייא בר אבא adds that a female עולה must come down. רבי זירא challenges this from the same ברייתא, and the גמרא answers by reading it as talking about אימורים. Finally a new משנה begins: a plan to leave the blood or אימורים overnight or take them out, and a list of plans to put the blood in the wrong place on the מזבח, which continues on the next amud.
