Topic

Where the laws of חוץ לזמנו, חוץ למקומו and נותר come from: which פסוק teaches each, which carries כרת, and רבא's reading of the whole long פסוק (ויקרא 7:18).

Overview

The גמרא finishes showing that the extra פסוק in ויקרא 19:7 teaches חוץ למקומו, and that the כרת in the next פסוק belongs to נותר, not to חוץ למקומו. It asks why the כרת is not given the other way around. It first answers that נותר is more like חוץ לזמנו (ז"ב), but אדרבה shows that חוץ למקומו is like it in even more ways (מקד"ש). So רבי יוחנן, quoting זבדי בר לוי, brings a firm source: a גזירה שוה of 'קדש'–'קדש' ties the כרת פסוק to נותר. The גמרא then asks why the long פסוק (ויקרא 7:18) is read as חוץ לזמנו and the קדושים פסוק as חוץ למקומו, and not the reverse. Here too the similarity argument is turned around with אדרבה. So רבא says all these laws come from the long פסוק alone, and he reads it phrase by phrase. 'האכל יאכל' covers eating by a person and by the מזבח. 'זבח שלמיו' sets which offerings are included, and 'שלישי' teaches חוץ לזמנו. 'לא ירצה' teaches that פגול needs all the מתירין to be offered, 'המקריב' teaches that the disqualification happens at the offering, and 'אותו' teaches that only the offering is disqualified, not the כהן. His reading of 'לא יחשב' continues on the next amud.
