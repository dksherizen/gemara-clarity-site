Zevachim 64a

זבחים ס״ד ע״א

Open Zevachim 64a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The jobs done at the southwest corner of the מזבח below and above the חוט הסיקרא, and רבי יוחנן's explanations about the עולת העוף and the נסכים.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a ברייתא from the previous daf. In that version, a חטאת העוף is valid even if its blood was only sprinkled and not squeezed out, as long as some blood went below the red line. The גמרא then gives a source for each of the three jobs done at the southwest corner below the line: the חטאת העוף, the הגשה of מנחות, and pouring the שירי הדם. Next it turns to the jobs done above the line. It asks why an עולת העוף normally belongs at the southeast corner, and רבי יוחנן answers that this corner is closest to the בית הדשן. רבי יוחנן then praises the strength of the כהנים, who could throw the light מוראה ונוצה more than thirty אמות. The גמרא checks this number against a משנה in תמיד and explains that the space where the כהן stands is not counted. Finally, רבי יוחנן explains that the כהנים doing the נסכים and the עולת העוף go straight to their corner so the smoke will not harm them. The גמרא challenges this from a משנה where the כהן גדול circles the מזבח and is then given wine to pour. רבי יוחנן's answer begins at the end of the amud and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שֶׁאִם הִיזָּה וְלֹא מִיצָּה – כְּשֵׁירָה;

because if he sprinkled but did not squeeze out, it is valid;

וּבִלְבַד שֶׁיִּתֵּן

provided that he places

מִחוּט הַסִּיקְרָא וּלְמַטָּה

from the red line and below

מִדַּם הַנֶּפֶשׁ.

of the blood of the soul.
