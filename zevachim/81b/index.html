Topic

Mixtures of blood from different קרבנות: whether the עולה's place serves for שירים, and a new משנה on mixing inner and outer blood

Overview

The גמרא quotes a ברייתא that explains why the תורה says 'דם' twice. The repetition teaches that עולה blood mixed with blood of any other קרבן is still placed on the מזבח, even the blood of a בכור, מעשר or פסח. The גמרא tries to prove from this that the place for עולה blood also works for leftover blood (שירים), but it pushes the proof aside. It then explains that תנאים disagree about which פסוק teaches that the blood of קרבנות does not cancel each other out. Next, the גמרא tries a second proof from a ברייתא about mixed blood placed above the red line. Scholars from ארץ ישראל read that ברייתא differently, and the גמרא debates whether it can be used here at all. A new משנה then rules that blood meant for inside the היכל that got mixed with blood meant for outside is poured out, and records a מחלוקת about the case where it was placed inside first. The גמרא explains that רבי אליעזר does not argue here, because inside blood must come before outside blood.

Opening lines

תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר ״דָּם״ ״דָּם״?

Why does the verse say "blood," "blood"?

שֶׁיָּכוֹל אֵין לִי אֶלָּא

For one might have thought: I only have

אֵלּוּ שֶׁנִּתְעָרְבוּ בִּתְמוּרָתָהּ,

these, where it got mixed with its substitute,

שֶׁאַף מֵחַיִּים תִּקְרַב;

since even when alive it would be offered;
