Topic

When a גט must be written for a specific woman, and רבא's contradictions about whether intent 'not of its kind' (like חולין) affects קדשים

Overview

The amud opens by looking for a source that a גט written סתם, for no particular woman, is invalid. The גמרא tries three 'יתר על כן' clauses of the משנה in גיטין and pushes each one off: the first two were written for someone else, and the third can be explained by אין ברירה. The proof finally comes from טופסי גיטין, where even 'הרי את מותרת לכל אדם' must be left blank. Next, רבא quotes רב: a חטאת slaughtered for an עולה is פסול, but slaughtered for חולין it is כשר, so only intent 'of its own kind' ruins it. He sets this against a גט written for a גויה, which is פסול. He answers that if you remove the foreign intent you are left with סתם, and a סתם גט is פסול while סתם קדשים are כשר. He then sets רב against the ברייתא that a כלי שטף protects inside a כלי חרס. He answers that חולין next to קדשים is like a partition in an oven, which has no effect at all. The גמרא brings the משנה about a divided oven and a broken barrel, where רבי אליעזר argues with a קל וחומר and the חכמים begin to reject it. Their reason continues on the next amud.
