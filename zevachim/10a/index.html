Zevachim 10a

זבחים י׳ ע״א

Learn Zevachim 10a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Zevachim 10a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Whether a thought during שחיטה about a later עבודה counts (רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש, רב נחמן and רבה), and רבי אליעזר's view that an אשם שלא לשמו is invalid.

Overview

This amud opens with a dispute between רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש about someone who slaughters an animal planning to sprinkle its blood or burn its fat for עבודה זרה. רבי יוחנן forbids the animal: a thought during one עבודה can affect another, and we learn חוץ from פנים. ריש לקיש permits it on both counts. The גמרא explains why we need their argument both here and in the earlier קרבן case of שלא לשמה. רב ירמיה supports רבי יוחנן with a קל וחומר, which is broken twice and finally rebuilt by רב אשי using a thought לשם פלוני. רבי אילא supports ריש לקיש from the extra verse about זריקה, and that proof goes through a back-and-forth of challenges and answers. רבה then retracts and agrees with רב נחמן that a חטאת slaughtered with a plan to do the זריקה שלא לשמה is invalid, because of רב אשי's קל וחומר. Finally, the גמרא turns to רבי אליעזר's view that an אשם brought שלא לשמו is also invalid. רבי יהושע and רבי אליעזר trade comparisons to חטאת and פסח, and רבי יהושע's next reply continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הַשּׁוֹחֵט אֶת הַבְּהֵמָה

One who slaughters the animal

עַל מְנָת לִזְרוֹק דָּמָהּ לַעֲבוֹדָה זָרָה,

in order to sprinkle its blood for idol worship,

וּלְהַקְטִיר חֶלְבָּהּ לַעֲבוֹדָה זָרָה –

or to burn its fat for idol worship –

רַבִּי יוֹחָנָן אָמַר: אֲסוּרָה;

Rabbi Yochanan says: it is forbidden;
