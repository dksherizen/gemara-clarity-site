Topic

The sources that every service of every קרבן must be for its own type and its owner (לשמה), and why a קרבן offered שלא לשמה is still כשר.

Overview

The גמרא first finishes rejecting the attempt to learn שינוי בעלים from שינוי קודש. Two of the differences turn out to be weak, but two strong ones remain, so the comparison fails. רב אשי then brings a direct source, 'ונרצה לו לכפר עליו', and the גמרא shows that this does not clash with רבי שמעון's use of the word 'עליו'. Next the גמרא looks for a source that קבלה must also be for the owner. It finds one in the פסוק about the איל נזיר and defends that reading against challenges based on the rules of כלל ופרט. The rule is then widened step by step: from the איל נזיר to all שלמים, and from שלמים to every קרבן, through the היקש of 'זאת התורה'. Finally the גמרא asks why a קרבן offered שלא לשמה is still כשר. It answers from 'מוצא שפתיך': such a קרבן becomes a נדבה. The גמרא then begins to explain why both פסוקים are needed, and that explanation continues on the next עמוד.
