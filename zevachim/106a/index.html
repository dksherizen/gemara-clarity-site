Zevachim 106a

זבחים ק״ו ע״א

Learn Zevachim 106a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Zevachim 106a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Where and how the burned offerings are burned, then the new פרק on slaughtering and offering up outside the עזרה and the source for its warning.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a derivation that the burned bulls and goats are burned east of ירושלים. It then asks where the רבנן burn them. A ברייתא answers: north of ירושלים, outside the three camps, while רבי יוסי הגלילי requires the בית הדשן. רבא says רבי אליעזר בן יעקב disagrees with him, because he reads 'שפך הדשן' as a sloped place. אביי replies that maybe he requires both ashes and a slope. A ברייתא then teaches that only someone who helps during the actual burning makes his clothes טמא, and that this stops once the animals become ash. רבי שמעון says it stops once the flesh is burned away, and רבא explains that the two views differ over a charred lump. Then פרק טבול יום ends, and a new משנה rules that one who slaughters and offers up a קרבן outside is liable for each act, with רבי יוסי הגלילי disagreeing. The parallel case of a טמא person eating קדשים follows. The גמרא then searches for the אזהרה against slaughtering outside. It finds ״ולא יזבחו עוד״, defends it with רבה's double reading, and begins a new challenge from a ברייתא that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אַף כָּאן – לְמִזְרָחָהּ שֶׁל יְרוּשָׁלַיִם.

so too here – to the east of Jerusalem.

אֶלָּא לְרַבָּנַן

But according to the Rabbis,

הֵיכָא שָׂרֵיף לְהוּ?

where does one burn them?

כִּדְתַנְיָא: הֵיכָן נִשְׂרָפִין?

As it is taught: Where are they burned?

The steps (25)

  1. ConclusionSo too here: east of ירושלים
  2. QuestionWhere do the רבנן burn them?
  3. ResponseA ברייתא: north of ירושלים, or on the ash pile
  4. Statementרבא identifies who disagrees with רבי יוסי הגלילי
  5. ProofThe ברייתא on 'where the ashes are poured'
  6. Rejectionאביי: maybe they only argue about the slope
  7. StatementOnly the one who actually burns becomes impure
  8. StatementImpurity stops once the animals turn to ash
  9. QuestionWhat is the difference between the two views?
  10. Responseרבא: a charred lump that is not yet ash
  11. Conclusionהדרן: closing פרק טבול יום
  12. StatementSlaughtering and offering up outside: liable for both
  13. Statementרבי יוסי הגלילי exempts offering up an animal slaughtered outside
  14. ChallengeThe חכמים answer: taking the animal out also makes it פסול
  15. StatementA טמא person eating קדשים is liable whether the food is טהור or טמא
  16. Statementרבי יוסי הגלילי exempts a טמא person who eats טמא קדשים
  17. ChallengeThe חכמים answer: touching the food already made it טמא
  18. StatementA טהור person eating טמא קדשים is exempt from this איסור
  19. StatementOffering up outside has both a punishment and a warning
  20. ProofWhy the word ״השמר″ counts as a warning
  21. QuestionWhere is the warning for slaughtering outside?
  22. ResponseThe warning comes from ״And they shall not slaughter anymore″
  23. ChallengeThat פסוק is already used by רבי אלעזר
  24. Resolutionרבה: read the פסוק two ways
  25. ChallengeThe verse is still needed for a teaching about the period of private altars
