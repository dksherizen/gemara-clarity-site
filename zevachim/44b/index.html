Topic

פיגול for the מצורע's oil and for נסכים, the gifts of the כהנים learned from במדבר 18:9, and רבי יוסי הגלילי's rule that פיגול in the inner offerings needs outside thoughts about outside acts.

Overview

The גמרא first shows that even רבי מאיר agrees a לוג of oil brought by itself, apart from the מצורע's אשם, cannot become פיגול. רבי ירמיה then fits the ברייתא about פיגול to רבי מאיר by removing נסכים from its list. אביי keeps the list as it is and explains that it means נסכים brought by themselves. Next, the גמרא asks for the source that the כהנים eat a חטאת העוף. It answers with לוי's ברייתא on במדבר 18:9, where each word 'כל' adds another gift for the כהנים: the מצורע's oil, the עומר and סוטה offerings, the חטאת העוף, and the אשם נזיר. The ברייתא also adds גזל הגר and teaches that these gifts fully belong to the כהן. Then a ברייתא of רבי אלעזר in the name of רבי יוסי הגלילי teaches that for the inner offerings, פיגול works only when both the thought and the act are outside. רבי יהושע בן לוי learns this from a הקש to the שלמים, and רב rules that the הלכה follows this view. The amud ends as רבא begins a new statement.
