Topic

A pinched or slaughtered bird that turns out to be a טריפה: the מחלוקת of רבי יהודה, רבי מאיר and רבי יוסי, the rule of דיו, and each side's source in the פסוקים.

Overview

This amud finishes a משנה about a bird offering killed by מליקה (pinching the neck) that then turns out to be a טריפה. Does it make the person who swallows it טמא, like any dead kosher bird? רבי יהודה says yes. רבי מאיר says no. He argues by קל וחומר from an animal that שחיטה protects a טריפה bird, and then says מליקה should work just like שחיטה. רבי יוסי limits him with the rule of דיו: what is learned from an animal cannot go beyond the animal, so only שחיטה helps. The גמרא asks how רבי מאיר could ignore דיו, which the Torah teaches through the story of מרים. It answers that רבי מאיר really learns his view from the פסוק "זאת תורת הבהמה והעוף": whatever makes an animal or bird fit to eat also protects a טריפה from טומאה. רבי יהודה learns his view from the extra word טרפה in ויקרא 17:15, which he reads as including a slaughtered טריפה. רב שיזבי challenges this reading by comparing the פסוק about fat, where the same word only leaves out non-kosher animals. The גמרא defends רבי יהודה with a ברייתא showing he leaves out a non-kosher bird using the word נבלה instead.

Opening lines

רַבִּי יְהוּדָה אוֹמֵר: מְטַמֵּא.

Rabbi Yehuda says: it renders impure.

אָמַר רַבִּי מֵאִיר: קַל וָחוֹמֶר;

Rabbi Meir said: it is an a fortiori argument;

אִם נִבְלַת בְּהֵמָה,

if the carcass of an animal,

שֶׁמְּטַמְּאָה בְּמַגָּע וּבְמַשָּׂא –

which makes impure through touching and through carrying –
