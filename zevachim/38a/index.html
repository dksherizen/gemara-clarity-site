Zevachim 38a

זבחים ל״ח ע״א

Learn Zevachim 38a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Zevachim 38a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Combining the reading and spelling of a פסוק, and the source for בית הלל that one placement of blood is enough for a חטאת, defended through the three times וְכִפֶּר

Overview

The גמרא finishes explaining how the mentions of סכת teach three walls for a סוכה, with a הלכה למשה מסיני reducing the third wall to a טפח. It then asks why we don't combine the reading and the spelling of שבעים, which would make a mother of a girl טמאה for forty-two days. The answer is that the word כנדתה shows the פסוק counts in weeks. Next, a ברייתא gives a source for בית הלל that one placement of blood works even for a חטאת. Logic could compare the animal חטאת to the bird חטאת or to the inner חטאות, and the two comparisons tie. So the three times וְכִפֶּר, which are extra because וְנִסְלַח already teaches atonement, teach that three, two, or even one placement is enough. The גמרא tests other readings of those words, such as blood put below or split above and below. רבא answers that the three words match the corners, and the גמרא adds that blood is never split half above and half below. It defends that rule against the יום כיפור sprinklings, which were done like whip strokes. The amud ends with a new challenge from the seven sprinklings on טׇהֳרוֹ of the מזבח, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הָתָם חַד קְרָא –

There, one mention in the verse

כּוּלֵּיהּ לְגוּפֵיהּ,

is entirely for itself,

וְחַד – לִסְכָכָה;

and one is for the covering;

אֲתַאי הִלְכְתָא,

the halakha came,
