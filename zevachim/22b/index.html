Topic

The water of the כיור, the source that a ערל כהן disqualifies the עבודה, and the זקני דרום's view that a טמא מת כהן can bring acceptance

Overview

This amud first finishes a discussion about the water of the כיור. The גמרא tries to prove it must be מים חיים, then answers that this is a dispute between תנאים: רבי ישמעאל requires spring water, and the חכמים allow any water. Next the גמרא asks for the source that a ערל כהן disqualifies the עבודה. רב חסדא says it comes from פסוקים in יחזקאל, not from the תורה of משה. A ברייתא explains that the פסוק includes both a ערל לב and a ערל בשר, and the גמרא shows why both had to be written. Then the גמרא turns to a טמא כהן. The זקני דרום say only a טמא שרץ disqualifies, while a טמא מת כהן's service is accepted. Through a series of challenges, including a קל וחומר from ריש לקיש, the גמרא clarifies their views about the פסח of a טמא owner. A new challenge from a פסוק begins at the end and continues on the next amud.

יָצְאוּ מֵי כִיּוֹר

Would you say excluded is the water of the Basin,

water that is fit to be water of the Basin?
