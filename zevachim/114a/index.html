Zevachim 114a

זבחים קי״ד ע״א

Learn Zevachim 114a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Zevachim 114a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

How each disqualified animal in the משנה could first be fit, why the משנה lists three 'not yet fit' cases, and the source for רבי שמעון's לאו on a מחוסר זמן.

Overview

The גמרא wants every case in the משנה to be an animal that was fit at first for the פתח אהל מועד and only later became disqualified. For רובע and נרבע this is easy: the animal was made holy first, and the act happened afterward. מוקצה and נעבד happen only with קדשים קלים, which רבי יוסי הגלילי holds are still the owner's property, and a ברייתא proves he holds this. אתנן, מחיר, כלאים and יוצא דופן are about the babies of holy animals, because the תנא holds such babies become holy only at birth. Next the גמרא explains why the משנה needs all three cases of the argument between רבי שמעון and the חכמים: temporarily blemished animals, doves whose time has not come, and אותו ואת בנו. Then it finds the source for רבי שמעון's לאו in 'לא תעשון'. In גלגל, obligatory offerings were like a מחוסר זמן compared to שילה, and משה forbade them. At the end, רבי ירמיה begins a challenge to this source, and the challenge continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בִּשְׁלָמָא רוֹבֵעַ וְנִרְבָּע –

Granted, an animal that had relations with a person, or was used for bestiality:

מַשְׁכַּחַתְּ לֵיהּ דְּאַקְדְּשִׁינְהוּ מֵעִיקָּרָא,

you can find it where he consecrated them at first,

וַהֲדַר (רבעו) [אִירְבַעוּ].

and afterward they were involved in bestiality.

אֶלָּא מוּקְצֶה וְנֶעֱבָד –

But an animal set aside for idol worship, or one that was worshipped:
