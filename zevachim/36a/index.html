Zevachim 36a

זבחים ל״ו ע״א

Topic

Which wrong intentions disqualify a קרבן, and the source and limits of רבי יהודה's view on intent to leave it over or take it out

Overview

The amud finishes the משנה's list of wrong intentions during שחיטה that leave a קרבן כשר. The משנה then gives the rule behind the list: only חוץ לזמנו, חוץ למקומו, and שלא לשמן (for a פסח or חטאת) disqualify. The גמרא asks for רבי יהודה's source for disqualifying intent to leave the קרבן over or take it out. רבי אלעזר points to the extra wording 'לא יניח' and defends it against a ברייתא that seems to use that פסוק up. The גמרא then suggests that רבי יהודה's reason is logic: intending is like doing. It explains why he still does not argue on the other cases, because in them the act itself would not ruin the קרבן. To explain why he ignores intent to put blood inside the היכל, the גמרא says he requires a 'threefold place'. Sources about רבי יהודה conflict on this point, and the גמרא settles it by saying two תנאים report his view differently. Finally it begins a new question about a חטאת slaughtered in the south, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בִּפְנִים – בַּחוּץ;

[or to place the blood meant for] inside — outside;

וְאֶת הַנִּיתָּנִין בַּחוּץ – בִּפְנִים;

and the blood that is placed outside — inside;

שֶׁיֹּאכְלוּהוּ טְמֵאִים; שֶׁיַּקְרִיבוּהוּ טְמֵאִים;

that impure people will eat it; that impure people will offer it;

שֶׁיֹּאכְלוּהוּ עֲרֵלִים; וְשֶׁיַּקְרִיבוּהוּ עֲרֵלִים;

that uncircumcised people will eat it; and that uncircumcised people will offer it;
