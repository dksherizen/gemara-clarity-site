Topic

שמואל's view on extinguishing coals, burning impure libations, and which חטאות require laundering of blood-splashed garments

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a challenge about שמואל. He allows putting out a metal coal on שבת but not a wood coal, so how can he follow רבי שמעון? The גמרא answers that he follows רבי שמעון on דבר שאין מתכוין, but follows רבי יהודה on מלאכה שאינה צריכה לגופה. Next, רב הונא rules that impure libations are burned on their own woodpile inside the Temple, based on 'בקדש באש תשרף'. A ברייתא supports him, and שמואל wanted to announce this ruling in front of ten people. Then פרק כל התדיר ends, and a new משנה teaches that blood of any valid חטאת, eaten or inner, that splashes on a garment requires כיבוס, while blood of a disqualified חטאת does not. The גמרא asks why a bird חטאת is left out. It works through the words of the פסוק: 'תשחט' (shall be slaughtered), ״תורת״ (the law of), which includes, and ״זאת״ (this), which excludes. The amud ends as the גמרא begins explaining why the inner animal חטאות are the ones included, since they are more like the eaten חטאות. That list continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֲבָל לֹא גַּחֶלֶת שֶׁל עֵץ.

but not a coal of wood.

וְאִי סָלְקָא דַעְתָּךְ

And if it enters your mind

כְּרַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן סְבִירָא לֵיהּ,

that he holds like Rabbi Shimon,

אֲפִילּוּ גַּחֶלֶת שֶׁל עֵץ נָמֵי!

then even a wood coal too [should be allowed]!
