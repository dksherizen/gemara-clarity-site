Zevachim 49a

Topic

Testing whether a secondary holy item can be stricter than its source, and the sources that an אשם must be slaughtered in the צפון

Overview

The amud opens by testing a rule from the ongoing discussion: something that gets its holiness from another holy thing is never stricter than its source. The גמרא raises three cases that seem to break this rule: food bought with מעשר שני money, a תמורה, and the leftover of a פסח. Each is answered by showing the difference has another reason, such as weak holiness, a holy source, or the animal having become a שלמים. The גמרא then gives a second source that a עולה slaughtered outside the צפון is invalid: the repeated word העולה. Next it asks how we know an אשם needs the צפון, and learns the שחיטה, the קבלה and the collector's own position from ויקרא 7:2. To show the rule is required and not just ideal, it brings ויקרא 14:13 about the מצורע's אשם. That פסוק seems to be needed to bring the מצורע's אשם back to its normal rules, but the גמרא answers that writing the צפון rule twice shows it is required. The amud ends by starting to ask whether this answer works under every view of יצא לידון בדבר החדש, and the question continues on the next amud.
