Zevachim 42a

זבחים מ״ב ע״א

Topic

A ברייתא about פיגול in the inner sprinklings challenges ריש לקיש's 'first intent' explanation of רבי מאיר. Several answers follow, ending with רבא and רב אשי.

Overview

Just before this amud, the גמרא brought two explanations of רבי מאיר's view that an offering becomes פיגול even when the bad intent was in only part of the מתיר. רבי יוחנן says half a מתיר is enough. ריש לקיש says a person keeps acting on his first intent. The גמרא now quotes a ברייתא about the blood sprinklings inside the היכל. There, רבי מאיר says it is פיגול even if the bad intent came in the second or third set, and that seems to go against ריש לקיש. רב יצחק בר אבין answers that the case is a new שחיטה, which is a complete מתיר. רבא explains that the חכמים who disagree follow רבי אליעזר, who requires the whole act. But רבא himself taught that רבי אליעזר agrees that with blood a partial service counts, so this answer falls. רבא then reads the ברייתא as bad intent in the first set, silence in the second, and bad intent again in the third. רב אשי objects that the ברייתא never says 'silent'. He reads it as bad intent in the first three sets and silence in the fourth, teaching that the first intent carries through every set.

Opening lines

אֲבָל דָּמִים הַנִּיתָּנִין עַל הַמִּזְבֵּחַ הַפְּנִימִי –

But blood that is placed on the inner altar,

כְּגוֹן אַרְבָּעִים וְשָׁלֹשׁ שֶׁל יוֹם הַכִּיפּוּרִים,

such as the forty-three of Yom Kippur,

וְאַחַד עָשָׂר שֶׁל פַּר כֹּהֵן מָשִׁיחַ,

and the eleven of the bull of the anointed priest,

וְאַחַד עָשָׂר שֶׁל פַּר הֶעְלֵם דָּבָר שֶׁל צִיבּוּר;

and the eleven of the bull for a hidden matter of the community;
