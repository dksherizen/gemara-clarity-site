Zevachim 94b

זבחים צ״ד ע״ב

Open Zevachim 94b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Washing leather and cloth on שבת, the sources for cleaning items touched by a חטאת, and items that left the courtyard

Overview

The amud opens with a question about which leather needs washing (כיבוס). A משנה in מסכת שבת allows pouring water on a soft leather cushion. רבא answers that washing without rubbing (כיסכוס) does not count as washing, though for cloth, soaking alone already counts. The גמרא ties this to רבא's ruling about throwing cloth and flax seed into water on שבת, and works out that flax seed is forbidden as לישה. Then it tells how רבא publicly retracted a ruling allowing a shoe to be washed on שבת. Next the גמרא brings the פסוקים for washing the garment, breaking the clay pot and scouring the copper pot in a holy place. It also shows that the משנה's 'stringency of a חטאת' is only one example out of several. A new משנה teaches that such items that left the courtyard are brought back in, and if they became טמא they are first torn or punctured. רבינא questions tearing the garment, and the challenge from רב הונא continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

כָּרִים וּכְסָתוֹת – דְּרַכִּין נִינְהוּ,

Cushions and blankets are of soft leather,

וּתְנַן:

and we learned in a משנה:

הָיְתָה שֶׁל עוֹר

If it was made of leather,

נוֹתֵן עָלֶיהָ מַיִם עַד שֶׁתִּכְלֶה!

he puts water on it until the dirt is gone!
