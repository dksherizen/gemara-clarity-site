Topic

Can a מחוסר כפורים of a זב burn the פרה אדומה, and where do we learn that a כהן missing his clothes invalidates the עבודה?

Overview

The גמרא explains that רבה's view rests on the idea that a זב who immersed but has not yet brought his קרבן (a מחוסר כפורים) is still considered like a זב. It then suggests this idea is a מחלוקת תנאים, citing a ברייתא where the תנא קמא lets a מחוסר כפורים burn the פרה אדומה and יוסף הבבלי does not. The גמרא rejects that suggestion: everyone agrees he is like a זב. The real argument is about how far the פסוק "וְהִזָּה הַטָּהֹר" goes. The תנא קמא says it covers impurity from anywhere in the תורה. יוסף הבבלי says it covers only the impurity of the פרה section, so lighter cases come in by קל וחומר but the זב, whose impurity comes from his own body, does not. The גמרא then turns to a new topic from the משנה: the source that a כהן missing his priestly clothes invalidates the עבודה. רבי יוחנן learns from a פסוק in שמות that without the clothes there is no כהונה. The גמרא challenges this with a ברייתא that seems to learn the rule from the wine passage in ויקרא, and the discussion continues on the next amud.
