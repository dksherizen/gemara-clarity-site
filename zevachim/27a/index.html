Topic

Blood placed in the wrong spot on the מזבח: when it can create פיגול, and how ריש לקיש, רבי יוחנן and שמואל explain the משנה's ruling.

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא about wrong intentions while offering a קרבן. Intent for 'outside its place' makes the קרבן פסול, while intent for 'outside its time' makes it פיגול with כרת. Intent to sprinkle the blood in the wrong spot on the next day makes it only פסול. The גמרא asks why it is not פיגול, if blood placed in the wrong spot counts as placed in the right spot. מר זוטרא answers from a פסוק that only a זריקה that permits the meat can create פיגול. רב נחמן בר יצחק adds that next-day intent can still make an offering פסול, like מחשבת הינוח according to רבי יהודה. ריש לקיש and רבי יוחנן then give two different ways to explain our משנה. ריש לקיש says the wrong-spot blood was placed with a stated intent. רבי יוחנן says it was placed in silence and simply does not count. The משנה's words 'no כרת' remain a difficulty for רבי יוחנן. He defends himself against a second challenge by following רבי יוסי, who holds that blood placed in the wrong area may not be gathered up again.

Opening lines

חוּץ לִמְקוֹמוֹ – פָּסוּל,

If he intended to do it outside its place, it is unfit,

וְאֵין בּוֹ כָּרֵת.

and there is no כרת for it.

חוּץ לִזְמַנּוֹ – פִּיגּוּל,

If he intended to do it outside its time, it is פיגול,

וְחַיָּיבִין עָלָיו כָּרֵת.

and one is liable to כרת for it.
