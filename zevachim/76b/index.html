Zevachim 76b

זבחים ע״ו ע״ב

Topic

How a doubtful מצורע brings his אשם and לוג of oil with a stipulation, and how the קומץ and seven sprinklings can both be done without breaking בל תקטירו

Overview

This amud finishes a teaching about a person who is not sure whether he is a מצורע. He brings an אשם and says: if I am not a מצורע, it is a voluntary שלמים. Because of this doubt, it must follow the stricter rules of both offerings, including being eaten for only a day and a night. The גמרא first uses this to challenge the claim that רבי שמעון does not allow bringing קדשים to a state of פסול. It answers that helping a person become purified is a special case. Next it asks about the מצורע's לוג of oil. He declares the oil a voluntary offering if he is not a מצורע, so the כהן takes a קומץ, refills the לוג, does the seven sprinklings and burns the קומץ. The גמרא then asks when the קומץ is burned. Burning it after the sprinklings fails because the leftovers shrank, and burning it before fails because of בל תקטירו. רב יהודה בריה דרבי שמעון בן פזי answers that the sprinklings are done לשם עצים, only as fuel and not as an offering. He supports this with a ברייתא of רבי אליעזר that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְאִם לָאו – אָשָׁם זֶה שֶׁל שַׁלְמֵי נְדָבָה.

And if not, this guilt offering is a voluntary peace offering.

וְאוֹתוֹ אָשָׁם טָעוּן שְׁחִיטָה בַּצָּפוֹן,

And that guilt offering requires slaughter in the north,

וּמַתַּן בְּהוֹנוֹת, וּסְמִיכָה,

and placing of blood on the thumbs, and leaning of hands,

וּנְסָכִים, וּתְנוּפַת חָזֶה וָשׁוֹק,

and libations, and waving of the breast and thigh,

The steps (16)

  1. ChallengeThe doubtful מצורע's אשם is eaten only a day and a night
  2. ResolutionHelping a person become purified is different
  3. ChallengeThe stipulation works for the אשם, but what about the לוג of oil?
  4. ResolutionHe declares the oil a voluntary offering
  5. ChallengeA voluntary oil offering needs קמיצה
  6. ResolutionThe קמיצה is done
  7. ChallengeA מצורע's oil needs seven sprinklings
  8. ResolutionThe seven sprinklings are done too
  9. ChallengeThe לוג is now missing oil
  10. ResolutionHe tops up the לוג, based on a משנה
  11. ChallengeThe handful needs to be burned
  12. ResolutionHe does burn it
  13. ChallengeWhen is it burned? Burning after the sprinklings fails
  14. ChallengeBurning before the sprinklings fails too
  15. ResolutionThe later sprinklings are done as if for firewood
  16. Proofרבי אליעזר's ברייתא: what can't go up as an offering can go up as fuel
