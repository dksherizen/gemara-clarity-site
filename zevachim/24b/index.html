Topic

Which services need the right hand: reading 'finger' in a פסוק, and the rule of אצבע and כהונה

Overview

The גמרא explains that רבי שמעון and the רבנן disagree about whether the word 'finger' in a פסוק describes only the words after it or the words on both sides. אביי shows that רבי אלעזר ברבי שמעון takes a third approach: 'finger' describes only the words right before it. Next, רבה בר בר חנה quotes רבי יוחנן: where the תורה says אצבע and כהונה, the right hand is required. After a first guess that both words are needed fails because of קמיצה, רבא explains that either word is enough. That rule is then limited to acts that hold back כפרה, like the מצורע. The גמרא shows that רבי שמעון holds that אצבע works alone but כהונה needs אצבע, and a ברייתא confirms that he allows קבלה and זריקה with the left hand. Finally, רבא's יד–יד link for קמיצה is shown to be needed, because one source covers taking the קומץ and the other covers placing it in a כלי שרת.
