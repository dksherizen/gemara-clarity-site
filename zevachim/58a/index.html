Zevachim 58a

זבחים נ״ח ע״א

Topic

Slaughtering קדשי קדשים on top of the מזבח: the views of רבי יוסי and רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה, and a משנה that supports רבי יוחנן

Overview

The משנה asks about קדשי קדשים that were slaughtered on top of the מזבח. רבי יוסי says this counts as the צפון. רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה says the northern half counts as צפון and the southern half counts as דרום. רבי יוחנן explains that for רבי יוסי the whole מזבח stands in the צפון. רבי זירא challenges this approach, and רב אסי answers that both תנאים learn their views from one פסוק, "וזבחת עליו את עולתיך ואת שלמיך". They argue over whether the whole מזבח, or only half of it, serves each kind of קרבן. The גמרא then works out what 'on the ground opposite them' means in רבי יוחנן's statement, and concludes that it is the spot where a shrunken מזבח used to stand. Finally, רבי זירא finds a משנה in תמיד about the second wood-pile that hints at רבי יוחנן's teaching. The גמרא begins to show that this משנה follows רבי יוסי, and the ברייתא it starts to quote continues on the next amud.
