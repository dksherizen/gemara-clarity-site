Topic

Which acts and which intentions can make a קרבן פיגול, including pouring the leftover blood and dipping the finger in an inner חטאת.

Overview

The גמרא first finishes a question about intentions made while collecting the blood. Planning to collect the blood tomorrow is not פיגול, but planning to pour the leftover blood tomorrow is. A ברייתא then shows that the altar's 'eating' counts for פיגול too. The answer is that it depends on which act the כהן is doing at the time: sprinkling the blood counts, but pouring the leftovers does not. Next, רבי יהודה בריה דרבי חייא and רבי יהושע בן לוי report that dipping the finger in the blood of an inner חטאת can make it פיגול. רב פדא and ריש לקיש object that פיגול is learned only from שלמים, which have no finger-dipping. After a long back-and-forth, the גמרא arrives at a general principle: whatever makes a קרבן פסול blocks פיגול, and whatever is required for it can create פיגול. רב מרי tries to prove this from a משנה about the מנחה, but the proof is pushed away. Finally, two ברייתות that disagree about dipping are explained as following the רבנן and רבי שמעון, and the גמרא begins to question that answer on the next amud.
