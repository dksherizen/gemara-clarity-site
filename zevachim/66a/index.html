Zevachim 66a

זבחים ס״ו ע״א

Open Zevachim 66a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Bird offerings: 'לא יבדיל' for a bird חטאת, squeezing blood of a bird עולה, and the new משנה on how place, method and intention affect bird חטאת and עולה

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the גמרא's reading of 'לא יבדיל', said about a bird חטאת. The words mean the כהן does not have to separate the head, not that he is forbidden to. רב אחא בריה דרבא challenges this from the open pit, where 'ולא יכסנו' surely cannot mean covering is optional. The גמרא answers that the pit verse is followed by 'the owner of the pit shall pay', and that 'והקריבו' had already set the bird חטאת apart from the bird עולה. Next, a ברייתא teaches that a bird עולה is valid if the body's blood was squeezed out, but not if only the head's blood was. רבינא explains that this makes sense because most of the blood is in the body. Then פרק קדשי קדשים ends with הדרן. A new משנה lists when a bird חטאת and a bird עולה are valid or invalid, based on place (below or above the red line), method, and intention.

Opening lines

אֵין צָרִיךְ לְהַבְדִּיל.

He does not have to separate [it].

אֶלָּא מֵעַתָּה,
