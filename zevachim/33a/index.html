Zevachim 33a

זבחים ל״ג ע״א

Topic

Whether partial entry counts as entry, and the מצורע's סמיכה at שער ניקנור: which parts of סמיכה and תכף לסמיכה שחיטה are from the Torah

Overview

This amud opens by closing an earlier discussion: both אביי and רבא hold that טומאה is only pushed aside (דחויה) for the ציבור, not fully permitted. The גמרא then returns to עולא's view that partial entry into the עזרה counts as entry. It offers a ברייתא as support: every סמיכה is followed right away by שחיטה, except a מצורע's, which happens at שער ניקנור because he may not enter yet. If reaching in with his hands were not 'entry', he could simply lean and have the animal slaughtered right there. רב יוסף rejects the proof: the ברייתא follows רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה, for whom the northern slaughtering area is far from the gate, and the מקדש's design cannot be changed to add an opening. In another version, רב יוסף says proper סמיכה needs head and most of the body inside, so hands alone will not do. The גמרא then asks why the מצורע does not just go in, if the Torah requires both סמיכה and the immediate slaughter. רב אדא בר מתנה answers with a גזירה against extra steps. Another version says the immediate slaughter is not from the Torah. A ברייתא disproves that version, so the גמרא reverses it: the מצורע's סמיכה is rabbinic, while תכף לסמיכה שחיטה is from the Torah.

Opening lines

מִכְּלָל דְּתַרְוַיְיהוּ סְבִירָא לְהוּ:

By inference, both of them hold:

טוּמְאָה דְּחוּיָה הִיא בְּצִבּוּר.

impurity is overridden in the case of the public.

לֵימָא מְסַיַּיע לֵיהּ:

Let us say that a source supports him:

כׇּל הַסְּמִיכוֹת שֶׁהָיוּ שָׁם

All the placings of hands that were there
