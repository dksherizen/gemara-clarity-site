Topic

Washing from the כיור: which vessels and which water are valid, and how its rules compare to a מקוה and a רביעית

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a ברייתא that lets a כהן wash from any כלי שרת, even a tiny one. That seems to clash with the rule that the כיור must hold water for four כהנים. רב אדא בר אחא explains that the small vessel is set into a hole drilled in the כיור. The גמרא then works out which vessels count. The word 'ירחצו' includes a כלי שרת, and the word 'ממנו' excludes a כלי חול, after אביי's קל וחומר from the כיור's base is knocked down. A כלי שרת is the one included because it was anointed like the כיור. Next, ריש לקיש rules that whatever may complete a מקוה may complete the כיור, but not a רביעית. רב פפא explains that this means adding a סאה of other liquid and then removing a סאה. רב פפא also allows immersing small items in a רביעית-sized hole drilled in a מקוה's wall. Finally, ריש לקיש says מקוה water is fit for the כיור, and the גמרא begins testing whether כיור water must be מים חיים; that discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בֵּין שֶׁאֵין בָּהֶן רְבִיעִית;

or whether they do not hold a quarter-log;

וּבִלְבַד שֶׁיְּהוּ כְּלֵי שָׁרֵת!

as long as they are service vessels!

אָמַר רַב אַדָּא בַּר אַחָא:

Rav Adda bar Aḥa said:

בְּקוֹדֵחַ בְּתוֹכוֹ.

It is where one drills a hole in it.
