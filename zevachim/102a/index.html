Zevachim 102a

זבחים ק״ב ע״א

Open Zevachim 102a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Was משה a כהן and a king? The disputes about משה's roles, and the sources that blemished כהנים get a share of the קרבנות

Overview

Earlier on the daf, רב said that משה served as a כהן. The גמרא tests this against a ברייתא that calls משה a זר regarding מרים's צרעת. רב נחמן בר יצחק answers that the job of examining נגעים is given only to אהרן and his sons. A second ברייתא about אלישבע's five joys calls משה a king. The גמרא answers that it means he was also a king. The גמרא then shows that משה's כהונה is a dispute among תנאים, with four views. This leads into side discussions: whether every חרון אף in the תורה leaves a mark, why one must respect royal authority, and עולא's claim that משה asked for kingship and was refused. The גמרא then returns to the משנה and learns from three verses of ״כל זכר״ that blemished כהנים get a share of the קרבנות. The amud ends as רב אשי begins to explain why a כהן with a temporary מום needed a special פסוק. His explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְאֵין זָר רוֹאֶה אֶת הַנְּגָעִים!

and a non-priest may not inspect the leprous marks!

וְאִם תֹּאמַר אַהֲרֹן הִסְגִּירָהּ –

And if you say Aaron quarantined her,

אַהֲרֹן קָרוֹב הוּא,

Aaron is a relative,

וְאֵין קָרוֹב רוֹאֶה אֶת הַנְּגָעִים!

and a relative may not inspect the leprous marks!

The steps (41)

  5. Challengeאלישבע's five joys: משה is called a king
  6. ChallengeThe ברייתא calls משה only a king
  7. ResolutionRead it as: also a king

  9. Statementרבי שמעון בן יוחי: the anger did leave a mark
  10. QuestionWhy call אהרן a לוי? He is a כהן!
  11. ResponseThe roles were switched: אהרן becomes כהן, משה becomes לוי

  14. QuestionWhy was a second verse needed?
  15. ResponseThe first verse might only be about future generations
  16. Challengeמשה left פרעה angry, but no result is recorded

  19. ResolutionSwitch the two opinions
  20. Statementרבי ינאי: always respect royal authority
  21. Proofרבי יוחנן's source for honoring a king: אליהו runs before אחאב
  22. Statementעולא: משה asked for kingship and was refused
  23. Challengeרבא challenges עולא: רבי ישמעאל says משה was a king
  24. Resolutionרבא: משה was refused kingship for his descendants

  26. ResolutionFirst answer: שאול's son איש בשת did become king
  27. ResolutionSecond answer: שאול is different because he lost the kingship himself
  28. ProofSupport: greatness lasts for generations unless a person becomes arrogant
  29. QuestionBack to the משנה: where do we learn the law of בעלי מומין?
  30. Response״כל זכר״ includes בעלי מומין
  31. QuestionIncluded for what? Eating is already taught elsewhere
  32. ResponseIt teaches that they get a share in the dividing
  33. StatementAnother ברייתא learns from ״כל זכר״ by the חטאת
  34. QuestionWhat does this second ״כל זכר״ add?
  35. ResponseIt adds a כהן born with a מום
  36. StatementA third ברייתא learns from ״כל זכר״ by the אשם
  37. QuestionWhat does the third ״כל זכר״ add?
  38. ResponseIt adds a כהן with a temporary מום
  39. ChallengeIsn't that backwards?
  40. Resolutionרב ששת: flip the wording
  41. Resolutionרב אשי: keep the wording and explain it
