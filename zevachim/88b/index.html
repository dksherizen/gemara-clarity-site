Topic

Washing and design of the בגדי כהונה, and how each garment atones for a specific sin

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a ברייתא about washing the בגדי כהונה. Very dirty garments may not be washed at all, and a second view says they are never washed, because אין עניות במקום עשירות. Next, a ברייתא describes the כהן גדול's מעיל: it was all תכלת, with woven pomegranates and golden bells on its hem. One view counts 72 bells and the other counts 36, and רבי עינייני בר ששון points out that the same 36-versus-72 dispute appears about the shades of נגעים. He also teaches that the בגדי כהונה bring atonement just as קרבנות do. He then matches each of the eight garments to a specific sin. The גמרא challenges this from רבי יהושע בן לוי, who says bloodshed is atoned for by the עגלה ערופה and לשון הרע by the קטרת. The גמרא answers by splitting the cases. The כתונת atones for a known killer who acted on purpose without a warning, and the עגלה ערופה atones when the killer is unknown. The קטרת atones for private לשון הרע, and the מעיל atones for public לשון הרע. The amud ends with the הדרן closing פרק המזבח מקדש.

הוּגְּעוּ לְנֶתֶר וְאָהָל –
