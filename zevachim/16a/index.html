Topic

How we know that a זר, and an אונן, ruin the עבודה: logical proofs and sources from פסוקים

Overview

The גמרא wants to prove that a זר who does the sacrificial service ruins it (חילל). It first tries a קל וחומר from a בעל מום. When that is knocked down, it builds a הצד השוה from a בעל מום and a טמא, and then from a בעל מום and an אונן. Each time it answers attacks along the way, including one about serving on a במה, which רב סמא pushes back on. רב משרשיא offers a different proof, a קל וחומר from a כהן who served sitting. The גמרא defends it, and also shows from 'לפני ה׳' that a sitting כהן is fit on a במה. Then the גמרא turns to the אונן and brings two פסוקים as sources: one from the כהן גדול who does not leave the מקדש, and רבי אלעזר's from אהרן's answer 'הן הקריבו'. It explains why each side does not use the other's פסוק. The amud ends as the school of רבי ישמעאל begins a קל וחומר from a בעל מום, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

זָר, שֶׁאֵינוֹ אוֹכֵל –

A non-priest, who does not eat,

אֵינוֹ דִּין שֶׁאִם עָבַד חִילֵּל?!

is it not logical that if he served, he desecrated the service?!

מָה לְבַעַל מוּם –

What about a blemished priest?

שֶׁכֵּן עָשָׂה בּוֹ קָרֵב כְּמַקְרִיב!

He is special, since with him the Torah made the offering like the one who offers it!
