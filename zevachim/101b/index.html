Zevachim 101b

זבחים ק״א ע״ב

Topic

Why only the ראש חודש goat was burned, when פינחס became a כהן, and רב's claim that משה was a כהן גדול

Overview

This amud finishes the story of the חטאת that was burned at the inauguration of the משכן. משה admits he heard the law and forgot it. The גמרא then checks the words "היום" and "הן היום" against both רבנן and רבי נחמיה. Next it explains the ברייתא's challenges to רבי נחמיה. Three goats were offered that day, but only the ראש חודש goat was burned, because אנינות does not disqualify one-time offerings. אנינות at night is also Torah law. And פינחס could not eat the offering because he was not yet a כהן, though רבי אלעזר and רב אשי disagree about when he became one. Finally, רב teaches that משה was a כהן גדול who shared in the offerings. The גמרא brings challenges against him, and the last one, about who quarantined מרים, continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וַיִּיטַב בְּעֵינָיו״ –

"And it was good in his eyes":

לֹא בּוֹשׁ מֹשֶׁה לוֹמַר

Moses was not embarrassed to say

״לֹא שָׁמַעְתִּי״,

"I did not hear,"

אֶלָּא ״שָׁמַעְתִּי וְשָׁכַחְתִּי״.

rather: "I heard, and I forgot."
