About Gemara Clarity

Gemara Clarity lays out every sugya in Shas step by step: the Aramaic beside English, phrase by phrase, with each statement, challenge, answer, proof and conclusion marked and colour-coded. You can follow a guided explanation (Sugya Flow) or work through the text yourself (Ameilus). It is free, has no ads, and works offline.

Gemara Clarity is a free, non-commercial project made by David Sherizen.

Where the content comes from

The Hebrew and Aramaic text, and the translation the English lines are based on, come from Sefaria: the William Davidson Edition (Koren Noé Talmud, with commentary by Rabbi Adin Even-Israel Steinsaltz), licensed CC BY-NC 4.0.

The explanations, the step-by-step breakdowns, the step titles and the summaries were written by AI. They have no haskama and have not been reviewed by a rav. They can contain mistakes, and they are not for practical halacha. Please check them against the Gemara and with your teacher.

How mistakes get fixed

Ahead of each week of Daf Yomi, the coming amudim are checked against the Gemara and Rashi, and mistakes are corrected. Corrections reach the app and these pages together. Review by a rav is planned.

If you spot a mistake, use Report a mistake on the amud or on any step in the app, or write to dksherizen@gmail.com. Please say which amud and step, what is wrong, and a source if you know one.
