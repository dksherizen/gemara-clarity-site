## 202611060149-rev92-review-fixes

Approved: Drafted by Claude from the Nov 5 2026 review and checked against the Gemara on Sefaria; David asked for them on Nov 5. Awaiting review by a rav.

- **Niddah 40a, step 21** (stepSummary): The summary said all twins need two קרבנות. The Gemara means twins born far apart; a ברייתא here says one קרבן covers births close together. Source: Niddah 40a (the ברייתא on 'זאת תורת היולדת' and the case of יהודה וחזקיה); Niddah 27a.
  - was: ״לבן או לבת״ teaches that even twins from one pregnancy need two.
  - now: ״לבן או לבת״ teaches that even twins from one pregnancy need two when they are born far apart, like רבי חייא's sons (see 27a). Twins born together still need only one.
- **Niddah 40a, step 21** (whatToRemember): Same over-generalisation as the summary. Source: Niddah 40a.
  - was: and ״לבן או לבת״ covers twins from one pregnancy. Each birth needs its own קרבן.
  - now: and ״לבן או לבת״ covers twins from one pregnancy born far apart. Twins born together still need only one קרבן.
- **Niddah 40a, step 15** (deeperAnalysis): Said one קרבן never covers several births, against the ברייתא on this amud. Source: Niddah 40a.
  - was: That means each birth creates its own obligation, so one קרבן does not cover several births.
  - now: So a birth that comes after the earlier birth's days are over needs its own קרבן, even for twins from one pregnancy. Births close together, like ordinary twins, are still covered by one קרבן.
- **Keritot 10b** (overview): The proof that ends at the top of 10b is אביי's, not רב אשי's; the site's own 10a page says so. Source: Keritot 10a, 'אמר אביי תא שמע: שלשים...'.
  - was: The amud opens by finishing רב אשי's proof
  - now: The amud opens by finishing אביי's proof (begun on 10a)
- **Keritot 10b, step 1** (stepSummary): Same wrong attribution. Source: Keritot 10a.
  - was: The גמרא finishes רב אשי's first proof.
  - now: The גמרא finishes אביי's proof, begun on 10a.
- **Keritot 10b, step 2** (title): 'A second' implied the first proof was also רב אשי's. Source: Keritot 10a–10b.
  - was: רב אשי brings a second ברייתא about the six days
  - now: רב אשי brings another ברייתא, about the six days
- **Chullin 110a, step 5** (stepSummary): The Oct 31 repair changed 'In plain words' to give the plain 'udder' to רב חייא, but the summary still gave it to רב יצחק בר אבודימי, so the step told two different stories without saying there are two readings. Source: Chullin 110a, 'ומפלפולו של רב חייא שנה ליה כחל סתם'; Steinsaltz there.
  - was: reports that רב יצחק בר אבודימי did teach about the udder of a nursing animal, but said it as a plain 'udder'.
  - now: reports that רב יצחק בר אבודימי taught about the udder of a nursing animal, and that רב חייא, relying on רב's sharpness, passed it on to רב as a plain 'udder'. (Some, like the Steinsaltz commentary, read it as רב יצחק בר אבודימי himself saying a plain 'udder'.)
- **Chullin 110a** (overview): Matches the repaired step 5. Source: Chullin 110a.
  - was: In a second version, he taught about a nursing animal's udder but said only 'udder'.
  - now: In a second version, he taught about a nursing animal's udder, and רב חייא passed it on to רב as a plain 'udder'.
- **Bekhorot 50a, step 10** (stepSummary): The Oct 31 weekly pass changed the translation to Rashi's reading (paying each year separately) but left the old wording in the summary. Source: Bekhorot 50a, 'קילבון לפרוטרוט', and Rashi there.
