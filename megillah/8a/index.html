Topic

Three 'אין בין' משניות: a full-benefit vow vs. a food-only vow, נדרים vs. נדבות, and a זב of two emissions vs. one of three.

Overview

This amud goes through three more משניות in the 'אין בין' series, each naming just one difference between two cases. First, a מודר הנאה differs from a מודר מאכל only in walking on the friend's land and borrowing non-food utensils. רבא explains that the ban on walking follows רבי אליעזר, who forbids even ויתור. Next, נדרים and נדבות differ only in the duty to replace a lost animal, though בל תאחר applies to both. A נדר ('הרי עלי') is like a load on your shoulders, so you must replace it, while a נדבה ('הרי זו') is tied only to that animal. Finally, a זב with two emissions and one with three differ only in the קרבן. The גמרא proves this from רבי סימאי's two פסוקים together with the word מזובו, and explains why both sources are needed. It then shows that the other מזובו teaches that a זב with two emissions must also count seven clean days. The amud ends as the ברייתא asks why a פסוק is needed when logic seems to teach this.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳ אֵין בֵּין

MISHNAH: There is no difference between

הַמּוּדָּר הֲנָאָה מֵחֲבֵירוֹ

one forbidden by vow from benefiting from his fellow

לַמּוּדָּר מִמֶּנּוּ מַאֲכָל,

and one forbidden by vow from his food,

אֶלָּא דְּרִיסַת הָרֶגֶל

except stepping foot (on his property)
