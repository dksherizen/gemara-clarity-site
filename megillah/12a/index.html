Topic

The seventy years of exile and כורש's role, followed by a phrase-by-phrase reading of אחשורוש's feast in the first chapter of the מגילה.

Overview

This עמוד starts by finishing the count of the seventy years of exile. A ברייתא shows that one year was still missing after בלשאצר, and רבא shows that even דניאל miscalculated. Two פסוקים seem to count the seventy years from different starting points. רבא explains that the count 'for בבל' ended only with פקידה, כורש's permission to return. A side teaching explains that God complained to משיח that כורש only let the Jews go up and did not rebuild. The גמרא then goes back to the opening פסוקים of the מגילה. It explains why פרס and מדי switch order, says that אחשורוש wore the בגדי כהונה, and records whether he was clever or foolish to feast the faraway guests first. רבי שמעון בן יוחאי explains that the Jews deserved destruction for bowing to the idol, but only for show, so ה׳ only threatened them. The גמרא then explains the feast's seating, fabrics, couches, floor stones, cups and wine, and the roles of מרדכי and המן. It ends with ושתי's feast in the royal house, and רבא's folk saying about it continues on the next עמוד.
