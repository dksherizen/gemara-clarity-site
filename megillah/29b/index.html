Topic

פרשת שקלים: its timing and content, and how to divide the Torah readings when special days coincide

Overview

The amud opens by explaining why the half-שקלים are announced on the first of אדר. According to רבי טבי, the קרבנות must be bought from the new collection starting in ניסן, and the money-changers' tables open on the fifteenth of אדר. Then רב and שמואל argue about which passage is פרשת שקלים: רב says the offerings passage, 'את קרבני לחמי', and שמואל says 'כי תשא'. The גמרא tests both views several times: the name of the reading, the purpose of the money, how ראש חודש אדר differs from other ראשי חדשים, the הפטרה of יהוידע הכהן, and when the passage comes up in the yearly cycle. Each test is answered, and a ברייתא supports שמואל. רבי יצחק נפחא rules that three ספרי תורה are used when ראש חודש אדר or ראש חודש טבת falls on שבת. On a weekday ראש חודש טבת, the הלכה is that three people read ראש חודש and one reads חנוכה. The amud ends with a new case, שקלים falling on the week of תצוה, and אביי's response begins on the next amud.
