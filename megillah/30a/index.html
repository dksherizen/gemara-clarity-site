Topic

The four special שבתות (שקלים, זכור, פרה, החדש): how they are read and which שבת each one falls on

Overview

This amud deals with the four special שבתות before פסח: שקלים, זכור, פרה, and החדש. It opens by finishing the debate about how to read שקלים when the weekly פרשה is next to it. רבי יצחק נפחא has the seventh reader simply continue into שקלים, while אביי has him go back and repeat it so it stands out. A ברייתא saying 'read it and repeat it' first seems to help אביי, but the גמרא explains that 'repeat' can mean reading it on two separate שבתות. When שקלים falls on כי תשא itself, אביי's plan of reading the whole פרשה and then repeating שקלים is supported by a ברייתא. Next, רב and שמואל argue about when ראש חודש אדר, or פורים, falls on Friday: רב moves שקלים or זכור to the earlier שבת, and שמואל pushes it to the next day. Each proof brought for רב is pushed away, and their dispute is matched to an older dispute between תנאים. The גמרא then defines the third שבת, פרה, and ends with a ברייתא listing all four readings with their הפטרות.
