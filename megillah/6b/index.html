Topic

Rome held in check, effort and success in תורה, provoking the wicked, and keeping פורים in a leap year

Overview

The amud opens by finishing רבי יצחק's explanation that גרממיא of אדום would destroy the world if it were let loose. רבי חמא בר חנינא adds that Rome and גרממיא fight each other every day, which keeps both sides weak. רבי יצחק then teaches that real success in תורה comes only through hard work. The גמרא limits this to sharpening one's understanding, since business success and remembering one's learning need help from Heaven. Next, רבי יצחק warns not to provoke a lucky רשע. The גמרא challenges this and gives three answers: personal affairs versus matters of Heaven, a צדיק גמור versus a lesser צדיק, and a רשע whose luck is shining. After עולא describes the huge city of רומי, a new משנה teaches that in a leap year the מגילה and the gifts to the poor belong in אדר שני. The גמרא matches the משנה to the opinions in a ברייתא, rules like רבן שמעון בן גמליאל, and shows the reasons for his view, a discussion that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

גֶּרְמַמְיָא שֶׁל אֱדוֹם,

This is Germamya of Edom,

שֶׁאִלְמָלֵי הֵן יוֹצְאִין,

for if they were to go out,

מַחֲרִיבִין כָּל הָעוֹלָם כּוּלּוֹ.

they would destroy the entire world.

וְאָמַר רַבִּי חָמָא בַּר חֲנִינָא:

And Rabbi Chama bar Chanina said:
