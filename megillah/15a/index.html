Megillah 15a

מגילה ט״ו ע״א

Learn Megillah 15a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Megillah 15a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Identifying prophets through their names, אגדות about famous women, and דרשות on מגילת אסתר chapters 4–5

Overview

The גמרא continues its list of prophets descended from רחב and asks how we know their fathers were prophets too. עולא answers that a father named in a prophetic verse was also a prophet, and a ברייתא adds that ancestors named with praise or blame share in it. רב נחמן's claim that מלאכי is מרדכי is refuted by a ברייתא that lists them separately. רב נחמן then supports the view of רבי יהושע בן קרחה that מלאכי is עזרא, since both fought intermarriage. Next come ברייתות about famous beautiful women, including the power of רחב's name. The גמרא then returns to explaining פסוקים in מגילת אסתר: מרדכי's cry, אסתר's fright, התך as דניאל, אסתר going to the king willingly, and her being clothed in רוח הקודש. A string of teachings from רבי אלעזר in the name of רבי חנינא follows: on the blessing and curse of a הדיוט, on quoting in the name of the speaker, and on the loss of a צדיק. The amud ends as רב חסדא begins to explain why seeing מרדכי made המן feel his success was worthless, and his explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בִּשְׁלָמָא אִינְהוּ — מִיפָּרְשִׁי,

Granted, regarding them, it is explicit,

אֶלָּא אֲבָהָתַיְיהוּ מְנָלַן?

but their fathers, from where do we derive it?

כִּדְעוּלָּא.

As Ulla taught.
