Megillah 11a

מגילה י״א ע״א

Learn Megillah 11a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Megillah 11a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Openings to the מגילה and a word-by-word reading of its first פסוק

Overview

This amud first lists many 'openings' (פתיחתות) to the מגילה. In each one, a חכם takes a verse from elsewhere in תנ״ך and shows that it hints to the פורים story: fire, water and abundance; a roaring lion and a hungry bear; laziness in Torah; 'a man and not a king'; and the joy of שושן. Then the גמרא starts explaining the first פסוק of the מגילה word by word. רב reads ויהי as a cry of woe, and שמואל and a ברייתא bring a verse showing that God never abandons ישראל in exile, sending מרדכי and אסתר in the days of המן. Four חכמים find hidden meanings in the name אחשורוש, and the word 'הוא' teaches that wicked people stayed wicked and righteous people stayed righteous, with דוד staying humble even as king. The גמרא then discusses 'המולך', 'מהודו ועד כוש' (with a parallel argument about שלמה), and the 127 provinces, which רב חסדא says came in three stages. The amud ends with a ברייתא naming three kings who ruled the whole world: אחאב, אחשורוש and נבוכדנצר.

Opening lines

״כִּי עֲבָדִים אֲנַחְנוּ

"For we are slaves,

וּבְעַבְדוּתֵנוּ לֹא עֲזָבָנוּ אֱלֹהֵינוּ

yet in our slavery our God did not abandon us,

וַיַּט עָלֵינוּ חֶסֶד

and He extended kindness to us

לִפְנֵי מַלְכֵי פָרַס״,

before the kings of Persia."

  7. StatementThe wicked ruler is Haman and the poor people is Israel

  10. Statementרב נחמן בר יצחק's opening: a man rose against us, not a king
  11. Statementרבא's opening: joy with the righteous, sighing under the wicked
  12. Statementרב מתנה opens with a verse about God being close to His nation
  13. Statementרב אשי opens with a verse about God taking a nation out of another nation
  14. Statementרב reads 'ויהי' as a cry of woe

  22. Statementרבי חנינא: everyone became poor in his days
  23. StatementThe word 'This is' shows the wicked stayed wicked
  24. StatementThe same word shows the righteous stayed righteous

  27. StatementWhy it could be praise and why it could be criticism

  30. StatementThe same argument about שלמה's kingdom: one end of the world to the other

  34. ResolutionAnswer: in the מגילה the number is extra
  35. Statementברייתא: three kings ruled the whole world
