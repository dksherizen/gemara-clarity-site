Megillah 3b

מגילה ג׳ ע״ב

Learn Megillah 3b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Megillah 3b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Priorities between תלמוד תורה, עבודה, מקרא מגילה and מת מצוה, and רבי יהושע בן לוי's rules defining a כרך

Overview

This עמוד first finishes a challenge to בית רבי, who said עבודה comes before תלמוד תורה. The angel who rebuked יהושע came because Torah study was neglected. The גמרא answers that Torah study of the רבים is greater than עבודה, but Torah study of a יחיד is less. It then challenges this from רבה בר הונא, who says festive-day mourning limits do not apply for a deceased תלמיד חכם. The גמרא answers that this rule proves the כבוד תורה of an individual, not the value of his study. Next, רבא ranks מצוות: מקרא מגילה comes before עבודה and תלמוד תורה, and a מת מצוה comes before both, learned from the word ״וּלְאַחוֹתוֹ״. He then asks whether מגילה or מת מצוה comes first, and answers that the מת מצוה wins because of כבוד הבריות. Finally, the גמרא studies rulings of רבי יהושע בן לוי about a כרך. These cover places near it or seen from it, a wall built after settling, the need for ten local בטלנין, and a city emptied of its בטלנין and then resettled. Another of his rulings begins at the end of the עמוד and continues on the next one.

Opening lines

מְלַמֵּד שֶׁלָּן בְּעוּמְקָהּ שֶׁל הֲלָכָה.

This teaches that he spent the night in the depth of halacha.

וְאָמַר רַב שְׁמוּאֵל בַּר אוּנְיָא:

And Rav Shmuel bar Unya said:

גָּדוֹל תַּלְמוּד תּוֹרָה יוֹתֵר מֵהַקְרָבַת תְּמִידִין,

Torah study is greater than offering the daily sacrifices,

שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: ״עַתָּה בָאתִי״!

as it says: 'Now I have come'!
