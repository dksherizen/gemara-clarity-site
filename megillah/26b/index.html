Topic

Selling and repurposing a בית כנסת, disposing of holy objects, and ranking levels of קדושה

Overview

This amud first finishes the topic of selling a בית כנסת. After a proper sale by the שבעה טובי העיר במעמד אנשי העיר, even the money from the sale may be spent on beer. רב אשי tells רבינא to buy the land of a ruined shul this way before planting it. רב פפא and רב הונא forbid רמי בר אבא to tear down an old shul before the new one is built, even though he planned to use its materials for the new one. רבא then rules that a shul may be sold or exchanged but not rented or mortgaged, and the same applies to its old bricks. רב אחא and רבינא disagree about giving a shul as a gift. Next, a ברייתא separates תשמישי מצוה, which may be thrown out, from תשמישי קדושה, which need גניזה. רבא applies this split to the reading stand, the ark curtain, broken arks, worn curtains and storage cases, and he fails to find a way for כהנים to pray in a shul next to a room with a dead body. The amud ends with rules for burying worn-out wrappings and ספרי תורה, and two opposite versions of רבא on turning a בית כנסת into a בית מדרש. A statement of רב אחא on this continues on the next amud.
