Topic

Identifying biblical places (רקת, קטרון, עקרון) and the opposing fortunes of קסרי (Rome) and ירושלים

Overview

This amud matches place names from תנ״ך with cities known in the time of the גמרא. It starts by identifying חמת, רקת and כנרת. רבא proves from eulogies said in טבריא that רקת is טבריא, and two explanations are given for why the city is also called טבריא. Next, זעירא says קטרון is ציפורי. The גמרא challenges him: קטרון was in זבולון's portion, and זבולון complained about his land, yet ציפורי was very fertile. Along the way it tells how הקדוש ברוך הוא comforted זבולון with the treasures of his sea and sand. The answer is that זבולון still wanted flat fields and vineyards like נפתלי had. The amud then reads verses about עקרון as being about קסרי, the Roman capital, and teaches that קסרי and ירושלים always have opposite fortunes. It ends with רבי יצחק's teachings about עשו, and the last of these continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְאִישְׁתְּכַח כְּווֹתִי.

and it was found in accordance with my opinion.

חַמַּת זוֹ טְבֶרְיָא,

Hammath is Tiberias,

וְלָמָּה נִקְרָא שְׁמָהּ חַמַּת?

and why was it called Hammath?

עַל שׁוּם חַמֵּי טְבֶרְיָא.

On account of the hot springs of Tiberias.
