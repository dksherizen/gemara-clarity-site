Megillah 19a

מגילה י״ט ע״א

Learn Megillah 19a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Megillah 19a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Writing a kosher מגילה, travelers between an עיר and a כרך on פורים, how much of the מגילה must be read, and the laws of the physical scroll

Overview

This amud first finishes the rules for writing a kosher מגילה. It defines three unfit materials (קנקנתום, דיפתרא, נייר), shows that the text must be in כתב אשורית, and learns from a גזירה שוה with ברוך's writing that it must be written with ink on parchment. Then a new משנה teaches that a traveler between an עיר (reads on the 14th) and a כרך (reads on the 15th) follows his home only if he plans to return. רבא explains that this means returning by the night of the 14th, and he proves it from the extra words 'היושבים בערי הפרזות'. רבא also rules that a בן כפר in a town always reads with the town, because the villages' early reading was only a leniency. The גמרא then defends that ruling against אביי's challenge from a ברייתא. Next comes the argument over where the required reading starts, with four opinions that רבי יוחנן and רב הונא each trace to a single פסוק. רב rules that the whole מגילה must be read, and that it must always be written in full. The amud ends with rules about sewing the scroll and about a מגילה written among the other books of כתובים, and it begins a story about לוי בר שמואל that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

קַנְקַנְתּוֹם — חַרְתָּא דְאוּשְׁכָּפֵי.

Kankantom — the black dye of shoemakers.

דִּיפְתְּרָא — דִּמְלִיחַ וּקְמִיחַ וְלָא עֲפִיץ.

Diftera — hide that was salted and floured, but not treated with gallnuts.

נְיָיר — מְחָקָא.

Neyar — reed paper.

עַד שֶׁתְּהֵא כְּתוּבָה אַשּׁוּרִית,

Unless it is written in Ashurit script,
