Megillah 27b

מגילה כ״ז ע״ב

Open Megillah 27b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Selling holy community property and a בית הכנסת, the רבית question on רבי מאיר, urinating near a place of prayer, and stories of Sages' long lives

Overview

The amud opens at the end of a ruling that charity goes to the local חבר עיר, and רב הונא adds that the poor of both towns rely on him. A new משנה follows. רבי מאיר forbids selling a community's holy item to an individual, while the רבנן say that by his logic even a sale from a big town to a small town would be forbidden. A second משנה gives three views on selling a בית הכנסת: רבי מאיר allows it only with a buy-back condition, the חכמים allow a permanent sale except for four disgraceful uses, and רבי יהודה allows a sale 'as a courtyard' with no limits. The גמרא asks how רבי מאיר's buy-back sale avoids רבית. It answers with צד אחד ברבית, and רבא offers a different reading of that dispute. Next comes שמואל's ruling that one may urinate within four אמות of where he prayed, and a ברייתא that is corrected so that one must only wait, not move away, after praying or urinating. רב אשי explains that the prayer is still on a person's lips. The amud ends with stories of Sages explaining their long lives, including רבי זכאי and רב הונא's sacrifices for קידוש wine. רבי פרידא's answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

חֲבֵר עִיר,

a town scholar,

אֲבָל יֵשׁ שָׁם חֲבֵר עִיר —

but if there is a town scholar there —

תִּינָּתֵן לַחֲבֵר עִיר.

it should be given to the town scholar.

וְכׇל שֶׁכֵּן

And all the more so
