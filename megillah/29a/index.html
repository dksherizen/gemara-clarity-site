Megillah 29a

מגילה כ״ט ע״א

Learn Megillah 29a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Megillah 29a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Honoring the dead, the שכינה in exile, the holiness of בתי כנסיות and בתי קברות, and the four special שבת readings

Overview

The amud opens with a ברייתא: we stop תלמוד תורה to escort the dead and to bring a bride to her wedding, but only when there are not already enough people. רב, עולא, and רב ששת each define 'enough' differently, and for a scholar who taught others there is no limit at all. Next, רבי שמעון בן יוחי teaches that the שכינה went with ישראל into every exile and will return with them. אביי names the synagogues in בבל where the שכינה rests, and two stories show the שכינה coming there. Several sages read פסוקים to show that בתי כנסיות and בתי מדרשות are a 'מקדש מעט', and רבי אלעזר הקפר says they will one day be moved to ארץ ישראל. בר קפרא teaches that the mountains that competed with סיני were 'blemished', and רב אשי learns that an arrogant person is a בעל מום. The גמרא then returns to the משנה's rules about a ruined בית כנסת: the shortcut ban and its exceptions, and the grass growing inside it. It also brings parallel rules about respecting a בית הקברות. Finally, a new משנה lists the four special שבתות readings and the other days that interrupt the regular order. The גמרא begins discussing it by quoting the משנה about the שקלים announcement, a discussion that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מְבַטְּלִין תַּלְמוּד תּוֹרָה

We interrupt Torah study

לְהוֹצָאַת הַמֵּת,

to carry out the dead for burial,

וּלְהַכְנָסַת הַכַּלָּה.

and to escort a bride to her wedding.

אָמְרוּ עָלָיו עַל רַבִּי יְהוּדָה בְּרַבִּי אִילְעַאי

They said about Rabbi Yehuda, son of Rabbi Elai,

The steps (43)

  1. StatementWe stop Torah learning for a funeral or a wedding
  2. Proofרבי יהודה ברבי אילעאי actually did this
  3. StatementOnly when there are not enough people already
  4. QuestionHow many people count as enough?
  5. Responseרב's answer: twelve thousand men and six thousand horns
  6. Responseעולא's answer: a line from the city gate to the burial place
  7. Responseרב ששת: as the Torah was given, so it is taken
  8. StatementFor one who taught others, there is no limit
  9. Statementרבי שמעון בן יוחי: the שכינה goes with ישראל into every exile
  10. ProofProof from the exile in Egypt
  11. ProofProof from the exile in Babylonia
  12. ProofIn the future redemption, God will return with them
  13. QuestionWhere in Babylonia does the Divine Presence rest?
  14. ResponseTwo synagogues, the Divine Presence alternating between them
  15. Statementאביי's practice of praying in these synagogues
  16. Proofשמואל's father and Levi leave when the Divine Presence comes
  17. Proofרב ששת stays, and is allowed to remain
  18. Statement"A small sanctuary" means the synagogues and study halls of Babylonia
  19. Statementרבי אלעזר: the house of our master in Babylonia
  20. Statement"You have been our dwelling place" means synagogues and study halls
  21. Conclusionאביי moves his Torah learning into the synagogue
  22. StatementThe synagogues of Babylonia will be moved to the Land of Israel
  23. ProofProving it with a קל וחומר from תבור and כרמל
  24. StatementA Heavenly Voice tells the mountains they cannot compete with Sinai
  25. ProofWhy 'peaked' means 'blemished'

  27. QuestionBack to the משנה: what is a קפנדריא?
  28. Responseרבא: the word means what it sounds like

  36. StatementHow to treat a בית הקברות
  37. QuestionWhich clause does 'respect for the dead' explain?
  38. RejectionIt cannot go on the last clause
