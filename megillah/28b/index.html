Megillah 28b

מגילה כ״ח ע״ב

Learn Megillah 28b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Megillah 28b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The holiness of a בית כנסת and בית מדרש, eulogizing Torah scholars, and the honor due to those who learn הלכות

Overview

This amud continues a ברייתא about what may and may not be done in a בית כנסת. Personal uses such as grooming, strolling, taking shelter from sun or rain, and a private הספד are forbidden. Torah reading, learning and a public הספד are allowed. רבי יהודה adds that a ruined בית כנסת is left with its grass as a sad reminder to rebuild, and the גמרא fills in a missing line about sweeping the floor. רב אסי teaches that the synagogues of בבל are built with a תנאי, but business calculations there are still forbidden and bring punishment. The גמרא then explains the ברייתא's clauses: scholars may treat the study hall like home, a person may enter for learning even when it rains, and someone who must call a friend out should first say some Torah. It defines a public הספד as one the whole community attends, and contrasts ריש לקיש's warm eulogy of a back-row scholar with רב נחמן's refusal to eulogize a man who was not proficient. It closes with teachings on not being served by Torah learners and on the reward for learning הלכות, and then opens a new ברייתא that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְאֵין נֵיאוֹתִין בָּהֶם,

And one may not adorn oneself (or take personal benefit) in them,

וְאֵין מְטַיְּילִין בָּהֶם,

and one may not stroll about in them,

וְאֵין נִכְנָסִין בָּהֶן בַּחַמָּה מִפְּנֵי הַחַמָּה,

and one may not enter them in the heat to escape the heat,

וּבַגְּשָׁמִים מִפְּנֵי הַגְּשָׁמִים.

or in the rain to escape the rain.
