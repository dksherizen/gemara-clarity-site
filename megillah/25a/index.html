Topic

Forbidden phrasings in public prayer, the reasons the prayer leader is silenced, and which sensitive passages are read, translated, or used as a הפטרה

Overview

This amud finishes a משנה about phrases a שליח ציבור may not say and improper ways of reading or translating the Torah in public. The גמרא explains why מודים מודים and על טוב יזכר שמך are a problem. Two אמוראים then dispute why על קן צפור יגיעו רחמיך is forbidden: it causes jealousy among the creations, or it turns ה׳'s decrees into mercy. Two stories follow. In the first, רבה praises a prayer leader only to sharpen אביי. In the second, רבי חנינא rebukes a man who added extra praises of ה׳. רבי חנינא also teaches that everything is in Heaven's hands except יראת שמים. רבי זירא compares repeating שמע to מודים מודים, and the גמרא distinguishes repeating word by word from repeating verse by verse. The גמרא explains the עריות and מולך clauses of the משנה. A new משנה then lists passages that are read but not translated, and passages not used as a הפטרה. Its ברייתא begins with מעשה בראשית, and that discussion continues on the next amud.
