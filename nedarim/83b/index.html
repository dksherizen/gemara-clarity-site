Nedarim 83b

נדרים פ״ג ע״ב

Open Nedarim 83b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Whether avoiding טומאת מת counts as a painful vow, and a new משנה about a woman's vow not to benefit from people: is her husband one of the 'people'?

Overview

This daf first finishes a discussion about a נזירה whose husband nullified her vow. The גמרא asks whether he could really have nullified only the painful wine part and not the טומאת מת part, since the ברייתא frees her from the offerings of an impure נזיר. It answers that avoiding the dead is also painful, because רבי מאיר teaches that whoever eulogizes, weeps for and buries others will receive the same. Then a new משנה begins. A woman who vows not to benefit from people cannot have the vow nullified by her husband, but she may still take לקט, שכחה and פאה. A vow forbidding כהנים and לוים in general does not stop them from taking their gifts. A vow against specific ones sends the gifts to others. The גמרא then asks whether a husband counts as one of the 'people' in her vow. עולא, רבא and רב נחמן each answer differently.

Opening lines

דִּלְמָא מִיַּיִן דְּאִית לַהּ צַעֲרָא —

Perhaps from wine, which causes her pain —

הֵפֵר לָהּ,

he nullified for her,

מִטּוּמְאַת מֵת, דְּלֵית לַהּ צַעֲרָא —

but from impurity of the dead, which causes her no pain —

לֹא הֵפֵר לָהּ?

he did not nullify for her?

The steps (10)

  1. ChallengeMaybe the husband only nullified the wine part
  2. ResolutionAvoiding the dead is also painful
  3. StatementA vow not to benefit from people
  4. StatementForbidding all כהנים and לוים from benefiting
  5. StatementForbidding specific כהנים and לוים
  6. StatementInference from the first clause: the husband is not part of 'people'
  7. ChallengeThe later clause implies the husband is part of 'people'
  8. Resolutionעולא: husband not included; the משנה gives two reasons
  9. Resolutionרבא: husband is included; the משנה is explaining its reason
  10. Resolutionרב נחמן: husband not included; the later clause is about after divorce
