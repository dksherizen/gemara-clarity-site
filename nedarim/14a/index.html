Topic

Vows that do not take effect: why 'חולין' is listed, the source that a נדר needs a דבר הנדור, and when an empty vow still needs a חכם

Overview

The גמרא finishes asking whose view the משנה's case of 'חולין' follows. Under רבי מאיר the needed inference does not work, and under רבי יהודה the case repeats the earlier part of the משנה. The first answer is that 'חולין' was simply listed together with pig meat and עבודה זרה. רבינא suggests instead that it teaches that these vows need no שאלה to a חכם. That idea is rejected, because the סיפא already shows this, so 'חולין' was mentioned only in passing. Next the גמרא brings the פסוק 'איש כי ידר נדר לה׳' as the source that a נדר works only when it is linked to a דבר הנדור. It explains that 'לאסור אסר' does not include things the תורה forbids. Then a ברייתא saying 'like my mother's flesh' means nothing seems to contradict the משנה. אביי answers that the vow has no force מדאורייתא but needs שאלה מדרבנן. רבא answers that the ברייתא is about תלמידי חכמים and the משנה is about an עם הארץ. Support for רבא comes from a ברייתא about vowing by the תורה, with the comments of רבי יוחנן and רב נחמן.
