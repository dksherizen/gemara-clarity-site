Nedarim 67a

נדרים ס״ז ע״א

Open Nedarim 67a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

For a נערה המאורסה, both the אב and the בעל must nullify her נדר, and the גמרא searches for the פסוק that teaches this.

Overview

The משנה rules that for a נערה המאורסה, if only the father or only the husband nullifies her נדר, it stays in force, and even more so if one of them ratified it. The גמרא first asks why this is needed, since the משנה's opening line already said 'her father and her husband nullify.' It answers that we might have read that line as 'father OR husband.' Next it asks why the משנה mentions ratifying, when one person nullifying alone already fails. The answer is a special case: one nullified, the other ratified and then had a חכם cancel his ratification. Even then both must actually nullify together. The גמרא then asks for the תורה source that both the אב and the בעל annul a betrothed girl's vows. רבה brings the פסוק 'And if she will be to a man, and her vows are upon her.' The גמרא suggests this פסוק is about a married woman, answers that a married woman has her own פסוק, and then challenges that both פסוקים might be about a married woman, with the second teaching that a husband cannot annul earlier vows. The reply comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

הֵפֵר הָאָב וְלֹא הֵפֵר הַבַּעַל,

If the father nullified and the husband did not nullify,

הֵפֵר הַבַּעַל וְלֹא הֵפֵר הָאָב —

or the husband nullified and the father did not nullify —

אֵינוֹ מוּפָר.

it is not nullified.

וְאֵין צָרִיךְ לוֹמַר

And needless to say,
