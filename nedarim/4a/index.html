Topic

How the prohibition of בל תאחר (do not delay a vow) can apply to a נזיר

Overview

The גמרא is looking for a way a person who vowed נזירות could break בל תאחר, the rule against delaying a vow. רב אחא בר יעקב suggests someone who vowed while in a cemetery. The answer holds even if the נזירות starts right away, because he is still putting off a pure נזירות. From this, רב אשי rules that a נזיר who makes himself טמא on purpose also breaks בל תאחר. רב אחא בריה דרב איקא says the delay can be in the final shaving, and מר זוטרא says it can be in the קרבנות. The גמרא then asks why we need the נזיר source for the קרבנות, since the פסוק 'כי דרש ידרשנו' already covers חטאות and אשמות. It answers that נזיר is a חידוש, and then tries to pin down what that חידוש is. Two suggestions are rejected, and a third begins at the end of the amud and continues on the next one.

Opening lines

רַב אַחָא בַּר יַעֲקֹב אָמַר:

Rav Acha bar Yaakov said:

כְּגוֹן דִּנְדַר וְהוּא בְּבֵית הַקְּבָרוֹת.

For example, where he vowed while he was in a cemetery.

הָנִיחָא לְמַאן דְּאָמַר

This works well according to the one who says

לָא חָיְילָא עֲלֵיהּ נְזִירוּת מֵאַלְתַּר.

the naziriteship does not take effect on him immediately.
