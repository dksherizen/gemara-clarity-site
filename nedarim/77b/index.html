Nedarim 77b

נדרים ע״ז ע״ב

Open Nedarim 77b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Rules for dissolving vows, the sin of vowing, and the wording a husband and a חכם must use to remove vows

Overview

This amud opens by finishing a report of a vow dissolved in a side room of the study hall, standing, by one person, at night. רבה quotes רב נחמן's ruling: vows may be dissolved standing, by a single expert, at night, on שבת, and by relatives, even if there was time before שבת. The גמרא challenges the 'standing' part from a ברייתא where רבן גמליאל got off his donkey and sat down to dissolve a vow. It answers that רבן גמליאל holds פותחין בחרטה, which needs careful examination, while רב נחמן does not. רבא then reports that the רבנן in ארץ ישראל told someone whose vow they dissolved to pray for mercy, because anyone who vows is called a sinner, as רב זביד learns from a פסוק. Next, a ברייתא lists which words of a husband do nothing and which confirm his wife's vow. It also teaches how he cancels her vow on שבת, with רבי יוחנן adding that he must cancel it in his heart, and בית שמאי and בית הלל argue about cancelling in the heart. Finally, רבי יוחנן rules that a חכם and a husband cannot use each other's wording. A ברייתא begins to prove from "זה הדבר" that a husband cannot release vows, and the proof continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בְּקִיטוֹנָא דְבֵי רַב,

In a side room of the study hall,

עוֹמֵד, יְחִידִי, וּבַלַּיְלָה.

standing, alone, and at night.

אָמַר רַבָּה אָמַר רַב נַחְמָן:

Rabba said that Rav Naḥman said:

הֲלָכָה, נִשְׁאָלִין נְדָרִים

The law is: vows may be asked about (to dissolve them)
