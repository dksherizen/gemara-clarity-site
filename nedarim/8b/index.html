Nedarim 8b

נדרים ח׳ ע״ב

Learn Nedarim 8b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Nedarim 8b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Dreams of נידוי; a husband as שליח for his wife's חרטה; releasing a נדר or a שמתא in one's teacher's town; and the healing sun for those who fear God's name, according to אביי and ריש לקיש.

Overview

This amud opens with the end of a comparison: just as grain always comes with straw, no dream is free of meaningless parts. So if someone dreams he was put in נידוי and then released, the release may be one of those meaningless parts. Next comes a story. רבינא's wife had a נדר, and he asked רב אשי whether a husband can be her שליח to tell the judges she regrets it. רב אשי answered: yes if the judges are already gathered, and no if they are not. The גמרא learns three rules from this story, and adds that a שמתא may be released even in one's teacher's town and by a single expert. Then a long chain of teachers explains the פסוק in מלאכי: 'those who fear My name' are people who never say שם שמים in vain, and a healing sun will rise for them. אביי learns from this that daytime sunshine heals. The גמרא notes that this disagrees with ריש לקיש, who says there is no גיהנם in עולם הבא. Instead, the uncovered sun will heal and delight the righteous and burn the wicked.

כָּךְ אִי אֶפְשָׁר לַחֲלוֹם

So too, it is impossible to dream

  2. Questionרבינא asks if a husband can bring his wife's regret to the judges
  3. Responseרב אשי: only if the judges are already gathered
  4. ConclusionThree lessons learned from the story
  5. StatementReleasing a ban is different: allowed in the teacher's town, and by one expert
  6. StatementThose who fear God's name receive a healing sun
  7. Statementאביי: the sunlit dust of the day heals
  8. Statementריש לקיש: no גיהנם, the sun heals the righteous
  9. ProofThe same sun punishes the wicked
