Nedarim 16a

נדרים ט״ז ע״א

Topic

How the wording of vows and oaths decides whether they take effect, and how נדרים differ from שבועות

Overview

The גמרא asks whose view our משנה about קרבן vows follows. It answers רבי מאיר, because רבי יהודה does not make the wording distinctions the משנה relies on. A challenge comes from an earlier משנה where רבי מאיר forbids 'לקרבן, I will not eat of yours.' The גמרא answers that our משנה is about 'לא לקרבן', meaning 'not a קרבן', which makes no vow. Next, a new משנה rules that several oath wordings forbid the food, including 'שבועה שאוכל לך.' A משנה in מסכת שבועות seems to read 'שאוכל' as 'I will eat.' אביי answers that the meaning depends on whether he was agreeing to eat or refusing. רב אשי instead reads the word as 'שאי אוכל', and the גמרא explains why each one rejected the other's answer. Finally, a new משנה teaches that oaths are stricter than vows in one way, and vows are stricter in another: a נדר can forbid using a סוכה, לולב or תפילין, but a שבועה to break a מצוה does not take effect.

Opening lines

<big><strong>גְּמָ׳</strong></big> מַנִּי מַתְנִיתִין —

GEMARA: Whose opinion is the משנה?

רַבִּי מֵאִיר הִיא,

It is רבי מאיר.

דְּאִי רַבִּי יְהוּדָה —

For if it were רבי יהודה,

לָא שָׁנֵי לֵיהּ ״קׇרְבָּן״

it makes no difference to him whether one says 'an offering'
