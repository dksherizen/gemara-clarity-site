Nedarim 50b

נדרים נ׳ ע״ב

Learn Nedarim 50b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Nedarim 50b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Stories of unexpected wealth and of תורה in plain 'vessels', the explanation of the ביצה טורמיטא and כלופסין, and stories about רבי and בר קפרא.

Overview

The גמרא finishes its list of the people through whom רבי עקיבא became rich. It then tells how רב גמדא's 'wasted' purchase of a monkey led him to a pile of pearls. Next, בת קיסר mocks רבי יהושע בן חנניה as an ugly vessel for beautiful תורה. He answers with the example of wine, which spoils in silver and gold. A woman who describes שמואל's looks in a mocking way is put in שמתא by רב יהודה and dies. The גמרא then returns to our משנה and explains the ביצה טורמיטא, a specially shrunk egg that doctors use. It also explains the word כלופסין, a kind of fig used for cooked dishes, and tells a story about a thousand kinds of fig dishes. The amud ends with stories about רבי's great wealth and his witty student-colleague בר קפרא, and that last story continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וּמִן אִשְׁתּוֹ שֶׁל טוֹרָנוּסְרוּפוּס,

And from the wife of Turnus Rufus,

וּמִן קְטִיעָא בַּר שָׁלוֹם.

and from Ketia bar Shalom.

רַב גַּמָּדָא יְהֵיב אַרְבָּעָה זוּזִי

Rav Gamda gave four zuz

לְסָפוֹנָאֵי לְאֵתוֹיֵי בְּהוֹן מִידַּעַם.

to sailors, to bring him something with them.
