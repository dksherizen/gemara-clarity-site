Nedarim 55b

נדרים נ״ה ע״ב

Learn Nedarim 55b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Nedarim 55b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Vows on foods and on clothing, mushrooms and their ברכה, and רבי יהודה's rule that a vow follows the vower's situation

Overview

This amud opens with the last line of the story about רבא and רב יוסף, a פסוק showing that a humble person is lifted up. Then a ברייתא explains what is included when someone vows off דגן (grain), 'produce of the year,' or 'produce of the land.' It says mushrooms count as 'growths of the ground' but not 'produce of the land.' The גמרא asks how this fits with the ברכה rule that mushrooms get שהכל because they do not grow from the ground. אביי answers that mushrooms grow from the earth but feed from the air, and the משנה in ברכות is read to match. Next comes a new משנה: a vow against clothing does not include sackcloth or sheets, and a vow against wool or flax does not include raw fleece or raw flax. רבי יהודה adds that everything follows the vower's situation. The גמרא brings ברייתות that list items that do count as clothing, discuss going out on שבת in rain coverings, and give examples of רבי יהודה's rule with wool and flax.

Opening lines

שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: ״כׇּל גֶּיא יִנָּשֵׂא״.

As it is said: 'Every valley shall be lifted up.'

תַּנְיָא: הַנּוֹדֵר מִן הַדָּגָן —

It was taught: one who vows off grain —

אָסוּר אַף בְּפוֹל הַמִּצְרִי יָבֵשׁ,

is forbidden even in dry Egyptian beans,

וּמוּתָּר בַּלַּח.

and permitted in fresh ones.
