Topic

Openings for dissolving vows (the launderer story and the נולד question, threat-vows against a wife) and cancelling future vows in advance

Overview

This amud finishes a story about a vow the חכמים struggled to dissolve. They sat so long that they moved from sun to shade and back. בטנית found the opening: would the man have vowed had he known the חכמים would suffer like this? He said no, and the vow was released. In a similar story, רבי ישמעאל בר רבי יוסי kept rejecting every opening. Then a launderer hit him, and he said he would not have vowed had he known that would happen. רב אחא מדיפתי objects that being hit is a נולד, a new situation, and we may not open a vow with a נולד. רבינא answers that attacks on the חכמים by heretics were common, so it was not a נולד. Next, רב יוסף releases אביי's threat-vow against his wife, and a ברייתא shows that רבי יוסי did the same in a similar case. Finally, a new משנה teaches that one may cancel future pressure-vows in advance, and the גמרא asks how that plan could still pressure the friend.
