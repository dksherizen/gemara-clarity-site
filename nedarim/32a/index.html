Nedarim 32a

נדרים ל״ב ע״א

Topic

The greatness of מילה, wholehearted trust in ה׳, and teachings about אברהם

Overview

This עמוד finishes the story of משה at the lodging place, where he was punished for dealing with the lodging before circumcising his son. רבן שמעון בן גמליאל says the danger was really aimed at the baby. רבי יהודה בר ביזנא describes how the angels אף and חימה swallowed משה until צפורה did the מילה. The גמרא then discusses whether משה later killed חימה. Next comes a ברייתא praising מילה: it made אברהם 'תמים', it equals all the מצוות, and it holds up heaven and earth. רבי אליעזר says that last praise belongs to תורה. Teachings follow about אברהם's fear and reassurance, 'אין מזל לישראל', the reward for being תמים, and the harm of chasing omens. The גמרא then gives three opinions on why אברהם's children were enslaved in מצרים. It ends with number-value teachings about אליעזר and about אברהם recognizing his Creator at age three, and begins a statement of רמי בר אבא that continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

מִפְּנֵי שֶׁנִּתְעַסֵּק בַּמָּלוֹן תְּחִלָּה,

Because he busied himself with the lodging first,

שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר ״וַיְהִי בַּדֶּרֶךְ בַּמָּלוֹן״.

as it says: "And it was on the way, at the lodging place."

רַבָּן שִׁמְעוֹן בֶּן גַּמְלִיאֵל אוֹמֵר:

Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says:

לֹא לְמֹשֶׁה רַבֵּינוּ בִּקֵּשׁ שָׂטָן לַהֲרוֹג,

It was not Moshe our teacher that the Satan wanted to kill,
