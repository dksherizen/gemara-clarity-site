Topic

Which of a wife's vows her husband can cancel (הפרה): vows of עינוי נפש versus vows about matters between him and her

Overview

This amud opens by finishing an earlier argument: a wife who stops bathing will not drive her husband away, because 'a fox does not die from the dust of its own den.' The גמרא then brings a ברייתא supporting רב אדא בר אהבה. Vows of עינוי נפש can always be cancelled, while other vows can be cancelled only if they touch the relationship between husband and wife. The ברייתא gives examples of vows he can cancel, vows he cannot cancel, and vows he does not even need to cancel, and רבן גמליאל says he should cancel even those. The גמרא shows that this ברייתא fits רבי יוסי, who treats eye paint and rouge as marital matters. It then explains the case of a vow against intimacy using רב כהנא's rule: she cannot forbid herself to him, but she can forbid her own pleasure, and then he must cancel it. Next, a ברייתא about keeping strict customs, based on 'לא יחל דברו', is shown to follow רבן גמליאל. Finally, רבא asks רב נחמן whether, for the רבנן, a vow against intimacy is עינוי נפש or a marital matter. רב נחמן begins answering from a משנה, and the answer continues on the next amud.
