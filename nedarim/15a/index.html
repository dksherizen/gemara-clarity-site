Topic

Conditional vows about sleep and benefit: whether one must hold back now when a later act could make a vow take effect backward (רב יהודה)

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a rule: a person may be careless about a condition (תנאי), but he is careful about a direct איסור. The גמרא then tests רב יהודה against the משנה of 'קונם שאני ישן'. Read literally, that cannot be a valid נדר, because sleep has no substance. So the גמרא tries 'קונם עיני בשינה'. A vow with no time limit cannot be kept, as רבי יוחנן taught. The גמרא then tries 'tomorrow if I sleep today', but that clashes with the rule that people are careful with a direct איסור. The final wording, 'today if I sleep tomorrow', seems to refute רב יהודה, and the גמרא answers that the משנה only speaks after the fact. רבינא instead keeps the plain wording and says the בל יחל is only rabbinic, proving from customs that a rabbinic בל יחל exists. Finally, the גמרא challenges רב יהודה from a משנה about a wife's conditional vow. רבי אבא answers, and the last clause of that משנה pushes back again, continuing on the next amud.

Opening lines

כִּי לָא מִזְדְּהַיר — בִּתְנָאָה,

For when a person is not careful, it is with a condition,

אֲבָל בְּאִיסּוּרָא — מִזְדְּהַר.

but with a prohibition he is careful.

תְּנַן:

We learned:

קֻוֽנָּם שֶׁאֲנִי יָשֵׁן,

"Konam that I sleep,"
