Nedarim 48a

נדרים מ״ח ע״א

Topic

Using town property under a נדר by writing one's share to the נשיא, and giving a gift through a third person to get around a נדר

Overview

The amud opens with the end of a משנה about two people forbidden by a נדר to benefit from each other. Things belonging to עולי בבל, like the הר הבית, are allowed to them. Things belonging to their town, like the בית הכנסת and the bathhouse, are forbidden. The fix is to write one's share of town property over to the נשיא. רבי יהודה and the חכמים argue about whether a formal transfer is needed. A ברייתא explains why the people of the גליל were already covered: they were quarrelsome, so their fathers signed their shares over ahead of time. A new משנה teaches that a person may give food to a third person as a gift, so that someone under a נדר can eat it. The story of a man in בית חורון shows the limit of this method: a gift the receiver could not make הקדש is not a real gift. The גמרא explains that the story illustrates a missing line about gifts that later turn out to be fake. רבא then discusses which wordings make such a gift forbidden, in two versions.

Opening lines

וַאֲסוּרִים בְּדָבָר שֶׁל אוֹתָהּ הָעִיר.

And they are forbidden to use something belonging to that town.

וְאֵיזֶהוּ דָּבָר שֶׁל עוֹלֵי בָּבֶל —

And what is something belonging to those who came up from Babylonia?

כְּגוֹן הַר הַבַּיִת, וְהָעֲזָרוֹת,

For example, the Temple Mount and the Temple courtyards,

וְהַבּוֹר שֶׁבְּאֶמְצַע הַדֶּרֶךְ.

and the water cistern in the middle of the road.
