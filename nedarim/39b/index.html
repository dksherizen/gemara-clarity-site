Topic

ביקור חולים: its source, its measure and its effect, with דרשות about קרח and משה

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a proof from the משנה's wording: the visitor may stand but not sit, which shows that the visitor's property is forbidden to the sick person. ריש לקיש then finds a רמז for ביקור חולים in משה's words about קרח, and רבא explains that a normal death includes lying sick in bed while people visit. רבא also explains 'בריאה יברא': if גיהנם exists, good, and if not, ה׳ should create it. A ברייתא says גיהנם was created before the world, so the גמרא re-reads the words as being about the mouth of גיהנם. In the end, משה was asking ה׳ to bring the existing mouth close. Another דרשה tells how the sun and moon stood up for משה, and how ה׳ rebuked them for caring more about a human's honor than His own. The גמרא then returns to ביקור חולים: it has no measure, meaning even a great person visits a lesser one (אביי) or one may visit even a hundred times a day (רבא). A visitor takes away one-sixtieth of the remaining pain, which is explained through רבי's rule of tenths for daughters. The amud ends as רב חלבו falls ill and רב כהנא begins an announcement, which continues on the next amud.
