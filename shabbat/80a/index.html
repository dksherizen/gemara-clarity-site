Shabbos 80a

שבת פ׳ ע״א

Shabbat 80a

Open Shabbos 80a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Combining half-amounts for הוצאה on שבת (ink, half-גרוגרות, separate domains), and the משנה's שיעורים for כחול, wax, glue, tar, חרסית and bran.

Overview

This amud continues the discussion of the minimum amounts (שיעורים) that make someone liable for הוצאה on שבת. A תוספתא sets the ink amount at two letters. רבא asks whether ink in different forms combines, and that question stays unresolved (תיקו). רבא then rules on writing while walking and on carrying ink one letter at a time. He also rules on two half-גרוגרות carried separately, which brings in the rules of 'within three טפחים' and the difference between throwing (זורק) and passing by hand (מעביר). A ברייתא teaches that two halves combine only within one העלם. רבי יוסי adds that both halves must also reach one רשות, and רבה, אביי and רבא disagree about what kind of divider makes two domains. The גמרא then explains several items from the משנה's list: eye paint (כחול), wax, glue, tar and sulfur, crushed earthenware, and bran. Each one is measured by a real, everyday use.
