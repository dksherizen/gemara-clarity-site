Topic

Carrying on שבת when the object shifts from its planned position on the body, and when two people carry one object together.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the previous topic: the unusual carrying habit of the people of הוצל does not count against what everyone else does. A new משנה teaches that if someone planned to carry an object in front of him and it slid behind him, he is פטור, but if he planned behind and it slid to the front, he is חייב. A woman's סינר, and according to רבי יהודה couriers' notes, are חייב either way, because their position commonly shifts. רבא explains the difference: aiming for better protection and getting worse is not like aiming for worse and getting better. רבי אלעזר says the משנה comes from two different תנאים, because the רישא and the סיפא seem to imply opposite rulings for 'behind and stayed behind.' רב אשי answers that the משנה is a 'לא מיבעיא' build-up, so there is no contradiction. A ברייתא about coins in a money belt then shows that everyone agrees 'behind and stayed behind' is חייב. A second משנה teaches that two people who carry one loaf together are פטור unless neither could carry it alone, and רבי שמעון says פטור even then. The גמרא lays out the opinions of רבי מאיר, רבי יהודה, and רבי שמעון and derives the rule from the word 'בעשותה': only one who does the whole act is חייב.

Opening lines

וְאִם תִּמְצָא לוֹמַר

And if you want to say

that the people of Hotzal do so

בָּטְלָה דַּעְתָּן אֵצֶל כׇּל אָדָם.

their view is cancelled out compared to all other people.
