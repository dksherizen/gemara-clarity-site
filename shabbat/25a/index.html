Topic

Why impure תרומה oil may be burned for light on a weekday, the פסוקים permitting benefit while it burns, and whether 'ממנו' permits impure תרומה or impure קדשים.

Overview

This amud opens with the end of an earlier argument: burning holy items cannot override יום טוב, because יום טוב is protected by both an עשה and a לא תעשה. The גמרא infers that lighting with impure תרומה oil is forbidden only on יום טוב, so on a weekday it is allowed, and it asks why. רב explains that impure תרומה must be burned, and the תורה lets you benefit from it while it burns. The גמרא brings two sources for this. The first is רב נחמן's reading of 'משמרת תרומותי … לך'. The second is רבי אבהו's reading of 'ולא בערתי ממנו בטמא'. The גמרא then argues over the word 'ממנו': does it permit impure תרומה or impure קדשים? A קל וחומר from מעשר seems to rule out קדשים, but the same logic would rule out תרומה too, and 'ממנו' must still permit something. The גמרא lists the חומרות of קדשים (פנ״ק עכ״ס) and then the חומרות of תרומה (מחפ״ז), and that list continues on the next amud.
