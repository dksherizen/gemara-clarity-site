Shabbos 75a

שבת ע״ה ע״א

Topic

Tearing to sew in the משכן, רב's teachings about stitching, the מגוש and calculating תקופות ומזלות, trapping and cracking a חלזון, and which מלאכה slaughtering falls under

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the discussion of tearing in order to sew. In the משכן, a curtain with a worm hole was torn further and then sewn neatly. רב זוטרא בר טוביה then quotes three teachings of רב. Tightening a loose stitch on שבת makes a person חייב חטאת. Learning even one thing from a מגוש makes a person liable for death. A person who can calculate תקופות ומזלות but does not should not be spoken with. The גמרא proves that רב holds a מגוש is a heretic (גדופי), and it brings sources showing that calculating the seasons is praised and is even a מצוה. Next, on trapping, a ברייתא brings a מחלוקת between the רבנן and רבי יהודה about whether cracking a חלזון adds a חיוב of דישה. The גמרא then asks why there is no חיוב for killing the חלזון, and it gives two answers: רבי יוחנן's and רבא's. Finally, רב and שמואל argue about whether a שוחט is liable for צובע or for נטילת נשמה.

Opening lines

שֶׁכֵּן יְרִיעָה שֶׁנָּפַל בָּהּ דַּרְנָא,

For a curtain that a worm got into (and made a hole),

קוֹרְעִין בָּהּ וְתוֹפְרִין אוֹתָהּ.

they would tear it and then sew it up.

אָמַר רַב זוּטְרָא בַּר טוֹבִיָּה אָמַר רַב:

Rav Zutra bar Toviya said that Rav said:

הַמּוֹתֵחַ חוּט שֶׁל תְּפִירָה בְּשַׁבָּת —

One who pulls tight a thread of stitching on Shabbat
