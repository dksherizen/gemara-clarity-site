Shabbos 115b

שבת קט״ו ע״ב

Shabbat 115b

Topic

Rescuing sacred writings from a fire on שבת: foreign languages, blessings and amulets, improper ink, and the 85-letter minimum for a worn ספר תורה

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a search for which תנאים argue the same way as רב הונא and רב חסדא about rescuing כתבי הקודש written in other languages. The answer is רבי יוסי against the תנא who rules about Coptic writing. A ברייתא then rules that written blessings and amulets are not rescued on שבת, even though they contain God's Name. A story follows in which רבי ישמעאל rebukes a scribe who sank such texts in water. The ריש גלותא asks about writings in לשון הקודש that were written in improper ink. Based on a ברייתא brought by רב אשי, the conclusion is that they are rescued. רב נחמן rules that a worn ספר תורה from which 85 letters cannot be gathered is not rescued, and he defends that ruling against a challenge from the Aramaic phrase יגר שהדותא. רב הונא and רב חסדא then argue about whether the 85 letters must be together or may be scattered. The amud ends by beginning a ברייתא about the special signs around ויהי בנסוע הארון, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אִילֵּימָא תַּנָּא קַמָּא דְּרַבִּי יוֹסֵי —

If you say it is the first Tanna who argues with Rabbi Yosei —

וְדִילְמָא בְּהָא קָמִיפַּלְגִי,

But perhaps they argue about this:

מָר סָבַר: נִיתְּנוּ לִקְרוֹת בָּהֶן,

One Master holds: they were given to be read from,

וּמָר סָבַר: לֹא נִיתְּנוּ לִקְרוֹת בָּהֶן.

and the other Master holds: they were not given to be read from.
