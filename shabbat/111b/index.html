Shabbos 111b

שבת קי״א ע״ב

Topic

Rose oil on שבת and רב's ruling like רבי שמעון "but not for his reason"; the close of פרק שמונה שרצים; and the opening משניות and גמרא on which knots are חייב, פטור, or permitted on שבת.

Overview

This עמוד opens by finishing the question of whether רב really rules like רבי שמעון about smearing rose oil on שבת. The barrel-tap case proves nothing, because it is a פסיק רישיה, where even רבי שמעון forbids. רבא then explains that רב follows רבי שמעון's ruling but not his reason. For רב, rose oil is permitted only where it is common, and in his area it was. The פרק שמונה שרצים ends with its הדרן, and a new פרק on knots begins. The משנה says permanent knots, like the camel drivers' and sailors' knots, make one חייב, while רבי מאיר exempts any knot that can be untied with one hand. The גמרא identifies these knots as the ones that hold the rings themselves in place. It leaves unresolved (תיקו) how רבי מאיר would rule on a bow. A second משנה lists everyday knots, and the גמרא resolves a contradiction in its wording by showing that it describes two kinds of non-liable knots. The details continue on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

אֲסִיר לְהַדּוֹקֵיהּ בְּיוֹמָא טָבָא!

It is forbidden to insert it tightly on a Festival!

בְּהָהִיא אֲפִילּוּ רַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן מוֹדֶה.

In that case even Rabbi Shimon agrees.

דְּאַבָּיֵי וְרָבָא דְּאָמְרִי תַּרְוַויְיהוּ:

For Abaye and Rava both said:

מוֹדֶה רַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן

Rabbi Shimon agrees
