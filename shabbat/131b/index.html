Shabbos 131b

שבת קל״א ע״ב

Topic

רבי אליעזר's sources for why the מכשירין of ציצית and מזוזה do not override שבת, while those of לולב, סוכה, מצה, שופר and מילה do

Overview

The amud opens by rejecting an earlier reason why, for רבי אליעזר, the preparations (מכשירין) of ציצית and מזוזה do not override שבת. The new reason is that a person can always make the garment or house הפקר and avoid the obligation. Next the גמרא goes through a ברייתא in which רבי אליעזר says that the מכשירין of לולב, סוכה, מצה, שופר and מילה override שבת, and it asks for his source for each one. לולב comes from ״ביום״, which means even on שבת. סוכה is learned from לולב through the גזירה שוה ״שבעת ימים״, and מצה is learned from סוכות through ״חמשה עשר״. שופר comes from ״יום תרועה״. Along the way the גמרא shows how the רבנן read the same words as 'by day, not by night,' and where רבי אליעזר learns that daytime rule instead. Each time it asks why the תורה did not write the rule in one place only. The answer is always that each מצוה has a special feature that blocks the comparison: צורך גבוה, four species, night as well as day, applying to women, or the unique power of the שופר. The amud ends as the גמרא begins searching for רבי אליעזר's source for מילה, and that search continues on the next amud.

  46. QuestionWhere does רבי אליעזר learn this?
