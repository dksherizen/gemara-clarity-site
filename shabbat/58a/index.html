Topic

Going out on שבת with a slave's seal or bell: who made it, metal versus clay, neck versus clothing, and the rules for an animal

Overview

This amud opens with the last line of an earlier discussion: an איסטמא is not included in the decree against עטרות כלות. רבי אבהו had said the כבול in the משנה is a woolen cap, and שמואל now says it is a slave's seal. The גמרא asks how this fits with שמואל's own ruling that a slave may go out with a seal on his neck. It answers that a seal the master made is allowed, and one the slave made for himself is forbidden. A seal on the clothing is forbidden even when the master made it, because if it breaks the frightened slave might fold the cloak and carry it on his shoulders. The גמרא supports this with רבי יוחנן's ruling and with שמואל's instruction to the רבנן of the ריש גלותא's house. Next, a ברייתא that forbids even the neck seal is resolved: a metal seal, which the master cares about, is forbidden, and a clay seal is allowed. The proof comes from the laws of טומאה. The גמרא then explains that the permitted bell on a slave's clothes is woven in, following רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע. Finally, it starts to question whether an animal's bell can become טמא, and that discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֵין בָּהּ מִשּׁוּם עַטְרוֹת כַּלּוֹת.

It does not fall under the decree of bridal crowns.

וּשְׁמוּאֵל אָמַר:

And Shmuel said:

כַּבְלָא דְעַבְדָּא תְּנַן.

It is a slave's seal that we learned about (in the mishna).

וּמִי אָמַר שְׁמוּאֵל הָכִי?

And did Shmuel say this?
