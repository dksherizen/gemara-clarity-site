Topic

How a קמיע becomes proven (writer versus amulet), and whether amulets have קדושה that would forbid bringing them into a בית הכסא.

Overview

This עמוד opens at the end of a ברייתא: even a proven amulet may not be fastened to a bracelet or ring on שבת, because of מראית העין. The גמרא then notes a contradiction about how an amulet becomes proven. It answers that one source proves the writer is an expert, and the other proves the amulet itself works. רב פפא lays out which cases prove the writer, which prove the amulet, and which prove both. He also asks about three amulets that healed one person, and that question ends in תיקו. Next the גמרא asks whether amulets have קדושה. After ruling out saving them from a fire and גניזה, it finds the real practical question: may one enter a בית הכסא with an amulet? It tries to prove from our משנה that amulets are not holy, and pushes the proof away several times. In the end, a healing amulet may even be held in the hand, so the proof fails, and the discussion continues on the next עמוד.
