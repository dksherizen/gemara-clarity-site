שבת מ״ח ע״ב

Topic

When attached things count as one unit (חיבור): for שבת, for טומאה, and for הזאה.

Overview

This amud is all about חיבור, the question of when two attached things count as one unit. It opens with a challenge to the ruling from the previous amud that opening a new shirt's neck on שבת is forbidden: why is that different from piercing a barrel's מגופה, which is allowed? רבא answers that the neck is part of the shirt, while the stopper is a separate object. Next, רבי ירמיה shows רבי זירא two משניות about חיבור לטומאה that seem to clash. רבי זירא explains that the deciding factor is whether the owner wants the items kept together when they are not in use. The גמרא then asks which תנא holds that anything attached to an object is like the object, and רב names רבי מאיר, based on the holders built into a stove. רבי מאיר's split ruling, טמא through contact but not through the air space, is explained as a deliberate rabbinic היכרא, so that people will not burn תרומה and קדשים by mistake. Finally, a ברייתא says separable tools are a חיבור for טומאה but not for הזאה. רבא begins to explain this: by Torah law, whether the tool is in use decides both laws the same way. The explanation of the ברייתא's split continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מָה בֵּין זוֹ לִמְגוּפַת חָבִית.

What is the difference between this and the stopper of a barrel?

אֲמַר לֵיהּ רָבָא:

Rava said to him:

זֶה חִיבּוּר, וְזֶה אֵינוֹ חִיבּוּר.

This is a connection, and that is not a connection.
