Topic

The מלאכה of בונה on שבת: the minimum amount, setting stones, and whether chiseling is בונה or מכה בפטיש

Overview

The משנה teaches that one who builds on שבת is liable for even a tiny amount (כל שהוא). The same is true of chiseling, the final hammer blow (מכה בפטיש), and drilling, because any מלאכה whose result lasts makes one liable. The גמרא asks what use a tiny building could have. After two examples are rejected, it settles on sealing a small hole, which parallels filling a worm hole in a משכן beam with lead. Next, שמואל rules that setting a stone in place is building. The גמרא answers a challenge to him by explaining that each row of a wall has its own requirement: dirt for the bottom row, mortar for the middle row, and mere placement for the top row. רב and שמואל then argue whether chiseling, making a hole in a chicken coop, and putting a pin in a hoe handle count as בונה or מכה בפטיש, and the גמרא explains why all three cases are needed. רבי יוחנן answers with a hand gesture that chiseling is מכה בפטיש. The גמרא reads the משנה to fit his answer, and then begins a proof that continues on the next amud.

MISHNAH: One who builds,

One who builds any amount,

and one who chisels, and one who strikes with a hammer or with an adze,
