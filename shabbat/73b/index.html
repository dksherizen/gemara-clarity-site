Shabbos 73b

שבת ע״ג ע״ב

Topic

The גמרא on the משנה of the 39 אבות מלאכות: why there is a count, which acts are grouped under each אב, cases of double liability, and why similar מלאכות are counted separately.

Overview

The גמרא explains why the משנה gives the number 39: someone who does all of them in one הֶעְלֵם brings a separate חטאת for each one. It explains that זורע comes before חורש because in ארץ ישראל farmers sowed first and then plowed. Then it goes through the list one מלאכה at a time, using ברייתות to group similar acts under one heading: planting-type acts under זורע, digging and furrowing under חורש, all kinds of harvesting under קוצר, and beating flax and stripping cotton under דש. Acts in the same group bring only one חטאת. Some single acts bring two, like pruning when he wants the wood, which is both קוצר and נוטע. Leveling ground counts as בונה in a house and חורש in a field. Digging only for the dirt is פטור, because it is מקלקל. רב פפא and רב אשי argue about knocking dates down with a clod, and רבא and אביי argue whether gathering salt counts as מעמר. Finally, the גמרא asks why זורה, בורר and מרקד are counted separately, and אביי and רבא begin an answer based on the משכן that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

גְּמָ׳

Gemara:

מִנְיָנָא לְמָה לִי?

Why do I need the count (of forty minus one)?

אָמַר רַבִּי יוֹחָנָן:

Rabbi Yochanan said:

שֶׁאִם עֲשָׂאָן כּוּלָּם בְּהֶעְלֵם אֶחָד —

That if he did all of them in one lapse of awareness —

The steps (26)

  1. QuestionWhy does the משנה give a number?
  2. ResponseThe count teaches a separate liability for each labor
  3. ChallengeWhy is sowing listed before plowing?
  4. ResolutionThe משנה follows farming in ארץ ישראל
  5. Statementברייתא: Sowing, pruning, planting, bending and grafting are one labor
  6. QuestionWhat is the ברייתא teaching?
  7. ResponseMany acts of one labor bring only one sin-offering
  8. Statementרבי אמי: Pruning is planting; planting, bending and grafting are sowing
  9. ChallengeOnly for sowing and not for planting?
  10. ResolutionCorrect the wording: also for sowing
  11. Statementרב כהנא: Pruning when you want the wood
  12. Statementרב יוסף: Cutting alfalfa
  13. Statementאביי: Cutting beet leaves
  14. StatementPlowing, digging and furrowing are one מלאכה
  15. StatementRemoving a mound: building in a house, plowing in a field
  16. StatementFilling a hole: building in a house, plowing in a field
  17. StatementDigging a hole only for its dirt is exempt
  18. ResolutionEven רבי יהודה agrees, because this digging is destructive
  19. StatementAll kinds of harvesting are one מלאכה
  20. StatementKnocking down dates with a clod: two liabilities
  21. Rejectionרב אשי: this is not the normal way of harvesting
  22. Statementרבא: gathering salt counts as מעמר
  23. Rejectionאביי: gathering applies only to things that grow from the ground
  24. StatementThreshing, beating flax, and stripping cotton are one labor
  25. ChallengeAren't winnowing, selecting, and sifting the same thing?
  26. Resolutionאביי and רבא begin their answer from the משכן
