Topic

The טומאה of עבודה זרה: is it like a שרץ or like a נדה? רבה and רבי אלעזר explain the מחלוקת between רבי עקיבא and the רבנן differently.

Overview

The גמרא finishes quoting a משנה from מסכת עבודה זרה about a house used for idol worship. Its stones, wood and dust are טמא. The תנא קמא compares this טומאה to a שרץ, but רבי עקיבא compares it to a נדה, which makes things טמא even through carrying (משא). רבה explains the פסוק ״תזרם״ as 'treat idols like strangers.' He then says everyone agrees about משא, and the argument is only about אבן מסמא, a very heavy stone. The גמרא asks what the other comparison teaches on each side. For רבי עקיבא, the שרץ comparison is for the idol's accessories. For the רבנן, the נדה comparison teaches משא. When the גמרא asks why the Torah did not simply compare it to a נבלה, it answers that the נדה comparison also teaches that there is no טומאה through limbs. The גמרא then shows that רב חמא בר גוריא's question about limbs was asked only according to רבי עקיבא. Finally, רבי אלעזר reverses רבה's approach: everyone agrees there is no אבן מסמא, and they argue about משא.
