Topic

Minimum amounts (שיעורים) for carrying materials on שבת, the size of a קשר מוכסין, and whether a used tax receipt or a repaid שטר still counts as having value.

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה's list of minimum amounts (שיעורים) for הוצאה, carrying from one domain to another on שבת. Each material is measured by the smallest useful thing people normally do with it, and רבי יהודה and רבי נחמיה disagree about some items. The גמרא then explains why rope does not get reed's smaller measure: people do not use rope that way. A ברייתא adds more items, and the גמרא shows that the סלע and the dried-fig measures for fat are really the same amount. Next the גמרא asks how big a קשר מוכסין (tax receipt) is. רב ששת and רבא each explain it differently, and a ברייתא about erased paper leaves רבא with an unanswered קשיא. Two ברייתות then ask whether a used tax receipt or a repaid שטר חוב still has value, with רבי יהודה saying it does 'because he needs it.' אמוראים offer different explanations of the practical difference in each dispute. The amud ends by asking how אביי reads the ברייתא's 'before repaid' and 'after repaid', and the answer comes on the next amud.
