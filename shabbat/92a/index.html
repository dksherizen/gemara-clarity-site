Topic

How objects are carried out on שבת: אגד כלי, normal versus unusual ways of carrying, carrying above ten טפחים, and carrying on the head

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a discussion about a thief who drags a purse out to the רשות הרבים on שבת. The גמרא shows how the theft and the שבת violation can happen at the same moment: the purse holds metal bars and has no laces, or its laces are wrapped around it. Next, רבא and אביי disagree about whether a vessel binds the things inside it (אגד כלי). In another dispute, about carrying fruit out by hand or in a vessel, each of them seems to hold the other's view, so the גמרא switches the names. It then defends the switched version by distinguishing a hand held below three טפחים from one held above. A new משנה follows: carrying in normal ways, such as in the hand, the lap, or on the shoulders, makes one חייב, because that is how the בני קהת carried. Unusual ways make one פטור. In the גמרא, רבי אלעזר rules that carrying above ten טפחים is still חייב. The גמרא proves the בני קהת carried that high, first from the height of משה and the לויים, and then from the ארון. Finally, רב quoting רבי חייא discusses carrying a load on the head. After two challenges, the גמרא concludes that it is פטור, and that statement continues on the next amud.
