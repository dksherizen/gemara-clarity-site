Shabbos 96b

שבת צ״ו ע״ב

Topic

Sources for הוצאה, הכנסה and four אמות in רשות הרבים; אב versus תולדה; and the מקושש

Overview

The גמרא asks where the מלאכה of הוצאה is written, since זריקה is only its תולדה. רבי יוחנן brings משה's command to stop carrying donations into the Levite camp, and a גזירה שוה of העברה with יום הכפורים shows this was on שבת. הכנסה is learned by סברא, but it is only a תולדה. The גמרא then explains what difference the labels אב and תולדה make: they decide how many חטאות a person brings, or, for רבי אליעזר, they are only names. Next the גמרא looks for a source in the משכן for throwing four אמות in רשות הרבים. Every proposal is rejected, and it concludes that all the rules of four אמות are a received tradition. Three opinions then argue over which מלאכה the מקושש did, and a hidden scroll of איסי בן יהודה explains why this matters. Finally, רבי עקיבא says the מקושש was צלפחד, and רבי יהודה בן בתירא rebukes him for revealing what the תורה kept hidden.

גְּמָ׳

מִכְּדֵי זְרִיקָה תּוֹלָדָה דְהוֹצָאָה הִיא,

  1. QuestionWhere is carrying out written in the Torah?

  3. ChallengeMaybe it was a weekday and the work was simply done

  5. ProofLaying out the two verses of the word link
  6. QuestionWhat is the source for carrying in?
  7. ResponseLogic: direction does not matter
  8. ConclusionCarrying out is primary, carrying in is a subcategory

  10. ResponseThe difference: how many חטאות you bring
  11. QuestionHow do the labels matter for רבי אליעזר?

  27. ChallengeMaybe the weavers sat right next to each other
  28. ResolutionThey would bump each other at the edge of the cloth
  29. ChallengeMaybe the weavers sat staggered

  31. ChallengeCarrying 4 אמות still has no source

  41. Statementרבי עקיבא teaches that the מקושש was צלפחד
