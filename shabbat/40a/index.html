Topic

Washing and bathing on שבת and יום טוב: רב vs. שמואל on pre-heated water, רבה's practice, and the history of the rabbinic bans on bathing and sweating

Overview

This amud opens by finishing a discussion from the previous daf: רבה בר בר חנה says he heard his ruling explicitly, so it does not depend on the rule of following the middle opinion. Then a new topic begins. רב says that on שבת one may wash the whole body, limb by limb, with water heated before שבת. שמואל allows only the face, hands and feet. רב survives two ברייתות by reading 'face, hands and feet' as a model for washing one part at a time, but a third ברייתא supports שמואל. רבה's version of רב, wash the whole body but leave out one limb, is refuted outright. The גמרא then asks whether רבה himself practiced that version, and answers that he followed רב's stringencies but not necessarily his leniencies. Next comes a ברייתא and a story about sweating in a sealed bathhouse on יום טוב, which was later forbidden because of 'transgressors'. בר קפרא explains the history: each permission was abused, step by step, until the final rule allowed the hot springs of טבריה but kept sweating forbidden. רבא learns that someone who breaks a rabbinic law may be called a 'transgressor', and the גמרא begins asking whose view this follows; the answer comes on the next amud.
