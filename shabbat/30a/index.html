Topic

Extinguishing a lamp for a sick person: the משנה follows רבי יהודה, and רבי תנחום's דרשה on the value of life versus death

Overview

The גמרא proves that our משנה follows רבי יהודה. It then asks why the first clause says פטור for a sick person. The answer is that the case is a dangerously ill person, where it is really מותר, and פטור was used only to match חייב in the next clause. A ברייתא of רבי אושעיא says פטור אבל אסור, and the גמרא explains that it is about a non-dangerous illness and follows רבי שמעון. Next, רבי תנחום is asked about putting out a lamp for a sick person on שבת. He opens his answer with a puzzle: verses of שלמה and דוד seem to disagree about whether the living or the dead are better. דוד's verse is explained to mean that only the living can do תורה ומצוות. שלמה's praise of the dead is explained three ways: the merit of the אבות, משה's lasting decrees, and the gates of the בית המקדש opening only in דוד's merit. Finally, the 'living dog' verse is explained through the story of דוד, who learned he would die on שבת and was refused even one extra day, because each day of תורה is precious.
