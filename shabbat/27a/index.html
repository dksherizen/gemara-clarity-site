Topic

Sources for טומאה of small and non-wool-or-linen fabrics, אביי and רבא on "או בגד", and how to reconcile the two teachings of דבי רבי ישמעאל.

Overview

The גמרא asks where רבי שמעון בן אלעזר learns that a 3x3 handbreadth piece of שאר בגדים (fabrics other than wool and linen) can become טמא. The answer is a ברייתא that reads the extra word "או" in "או בגד". אביי uses that word to include a tiny 3x3 fingerbreadth piece of צמר ופשתים for טומאת שרצים. רבא learns that tiny piece from נגעים instead. The two argue back and forth about whether נגעים and שרצים can be learned from each other. אביי then says that two teachers of דבי רבי ישמעאל disagree. רבא answers that they agree, because one speaks of a small piece and the other of a big piece. The גמרא objects that רבא said the opposite earlier, and answers that either רבא retracted or the statement was really רב פפא's. A reading in רב פפא's name, that 'אף כל' is about כלאים, is rejected as a mistake (בדותא). רב נחמן בר יצחק then starts a new reading, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מְנָא לֵיהּ?

From where does he derive it?

נָפְקָא מֵ״אוֹ בֶגֶד״.

It is derived from "or a garment."

דְּתַנְיָא:

As it was taught in a baraita:

״בֶּגֶד״ — אֵין לִי אֶלָּא בֶּגֶד,

"Garment" — I have only a garment;
