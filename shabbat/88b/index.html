Shabbos 88b

שבת פ״ח ע״ב

Shabbat 88b

Topic

Aggadic praise of מתן תורה: ישראל's bond with הקב"ה, the overwhelming power of each divine word at סיני, and משה's answer to the angels who objected to giving the Torah to humans.

Overview

This amud is all אגדה, with no legal discussion. It opens with the end of רבא's answer to a heretic who mocked ישראל for saying נעשה ונשמע: wholehearted trust guides the upright. A chain of teachings follows. Keeping the מצוות deepens God's love. עולא compares the עגל to a bride betraying her husband under the חופה, while רב notes that the gentle wording of the פסוק shows הקב"ה still loves us. The גמרא then praises the words of מתן תורה. Each word split into seventy languages, Torah is like a ruler that can give life or death, and each word was crowned with two crowns. רבי יהושע בן לוי reads פסוקים in שיר השירים as Israel's loyalty through suffering and as forgiveness for the עגל. He also describes the overwhelming power of each word at סיני: it filled the world with fragrance, made the people's souls leave them, and drove them back twelve מיל. The amud ends with the story of משה going up to heaven, where the angels object to giving the Torah to humans. משה answers that its commandments only fit people, and his answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דְּסָגֵינַן בִּשְׁלִימוּתָא

(We) who walk with wholeheartedness

כְּתִיב בַּן:

it is written about us:

״תֻּמַּת יְשָׁרִים תַּנְחֵם״,

'The integrity of the upright will guide them,'

הָנָךְ אִינָשֵׁי דְּסָגַן בַּעֲלִילוּתָא

those people who walk in deceit,
