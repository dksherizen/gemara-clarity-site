Topic

When a מוקצה object becomes usable on שבת through attachment, placement, or an action (stone on a barrel, branches, building stones), and the window-shutter dispute about adding to a temporary tent

Overview

This עמוד finishes the משנה: a branch tied to a pitcher may be used to draw water, and רבי אליעזר and the חכמים argue about a window shutter. The גמרא then discusses a stone sitting on a barrel. רבי יוחנן's ruling comes in two versions. In רבה's version, a stone placed there on purpose makes the barrel a בסיס לדבר האסור. In רב יוסף's version, it becomes a כיסוי for the barrel. Each version is tested against a משנה about a stone in a gourd. The dispute is explained as whether a physical מעשה is needed to make an object usable. This ties into a story about רבי and some building stones, where רבי אמי and רבי אסי each follow their own reasoning. Next, the גמרא asks whether the tied-branch rule contradicts רבן שמעון בן גמליאל, and רב ששת and רב אשי each answer. Finally, רבי יוחנן explains that the shutter dispute is only about adding to an existing אהל עראי, and the גמרא begins to explain 'בין כך ובין כך', with the answer continuing on the next עמוד.
