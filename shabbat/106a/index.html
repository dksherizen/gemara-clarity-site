Topic

Worry when a family or group member dies; whether destructive acts (מקלקל), especially חובל and מבעיר, are exempt on שבת; the measure of a double סיט; and the start of the משנה on trapping.

Overview

This amud opens by finishing a short אגדה: when one brother or one member of a group dies, everyone else should worry and examine their deeds. There are two versions of whether this applies more when the oldest dies or when the youngest dies. The גמרא then turns to the משנה's rule that anyone who does a מלאכה destructively (מקלקל) on שבת is exempt. רבי אבהו cites a ברייתא that makes wounding (חובל) and kindling (מבעיר) exceptions. רבי יוחנן rejects that ברייתא, or else limits it to cases where the act is useful, such as needing the blood for a dog or needing the ashes. The גמרא explains that the משנה follows רבי יהודה and the ברייתא follows רבי שמעון. רבי שמעון learns his view from the verse that permits מילה on שבת and from the ban on burning a בת כהן on שבת. רבי יהודה answers that both of those acts are constructive, as רב אשי explains. The גמרא then shows two ways to measure the double סיט for whitening. Finally, a new משנה about trapping a bird or a deer begins, and it continues on the next amud.
