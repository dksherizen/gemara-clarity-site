Topic

צידה on שבת and יום טוב: when an animal counts as already trapped (enclosures, weakened or swarming creatures), and who is liable when several people trap together.

Overview

This amud opens at the end of the משנה. חכמים rule that driving a deer into a garden, courtyard, or ביבר counts as trapping. רבן שמעון בן גמליאל says it depends on whether the animal still needs more catching (מחוסר צידה). The גמרא then contrasts a משנה in ביצה, which lets one take animals and birds from a ביבר on יום טוב, with a ברייתא that forbids it. For birds, the difference is a roofed versus an unroofed ביבר. A challenge from the house case is answered by רבה בר רב הונא: that case is about a צפור דרור, which is never controlled. Following the same method, the animal contradiction is solved by a large versus a small ביבר, and רב אשי gives tests for what counts as small. Next, שמואל rules like רבן שמעון בן גמליאל, and אביי teaches that learning means understanding, not just reciting. ברייתות then cover weakened deer and insects, all judged by whether the creature can still escape. The amud ends with משניות about locking a deer in a house and blocking a doorway, where only the person who completes the trapping is liable.

Opening lines

וּצְבִי לַגִּינָּה וְלֶחָצֵר

And a deer into a garden, or into a courtyard,

וְלַבֵּיבָרִין — [חַיָּיב].

or into enclosures — he is liable.

רַבָּן שִׁמְעוֹן בֶּן גַּמְלִיאֵל אוֹמֵר:

Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says:

לֹא כָּל הַבֵּיבָרִין שָׁוִין.

Not all enclosures are equal.
