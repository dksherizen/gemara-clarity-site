Shabbos 134b

שבת קל״ד ע״ב

Topic

Washing a baby after מילה on שבת, treating wounds on שבת, and doubtful cases of מילה

Overview

The משנה allows washing a baby on שבת before and after his מילה, with sprinkling by hand but not with a vessel. רבי אלעזר בן עזריה allows washing even on the third day, which the פסוק about שכם shows is a time of danger. The גמרא asks how washing can be allowed if a vessel is forbidden. רב יהודה and רבה בר אבוה read the משנה as 'washing means sprinkling by hand.' רבא reads it instead as two days: normal washing on the first day and only sprinkling on the third day. A ברייתא supports רבא, but he later regrets ruling against the elders, because the משנה's wording fits their reading. The הלכה follows רבי אלעזר בן עזריה: the baby may be washed in every case, because he is in danger. The גמרא then discusses hot water, oil, and bandages on a wound on שבת. Finally, it begins the source for why a doubtful מילה does not override שבת, a discussion that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳ מַרְחִיצִין אֶת הַקָּטָן

MISHNA: One may wash the baby

בֵּין לִפְנֵי הַמִּילָה

both before the circumcision

וּבֵין לְאַחַר הַמִּילָה,

and after the circumcision,

וּמְזַלְּפִין עָלָיו בַּיָּד,

and one may sprinkle (water) on him by hand,

  27. ResolutionHere too, the reason is squeezing
  28. ProofA ברייתא supports שמואל's way of applying oil and hot water

  30. ChallengeRags allowed and rags forbidden in the same ברייתא
  31. ResolutionNew rags versus old rags
  32. Conclusionאביי learns that rags heal
  33. StatementBack to the משנה: a doubtful baby and a hermaphrodite
