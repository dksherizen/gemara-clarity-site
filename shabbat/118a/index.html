Shabbos 118a

שבת קי״ח ע״א

Topic

The number of שבת meals (רבנן vs. רבי חידקא) tested against משניות, rinsing dishes on שבת, and the rewards for the שבת meals.

Overview

The amud opens by testing רבי חידקא, who holds that one eats four meals on שבת, against the משנה about saving food from a fire on שבת. Each clause seems to assume only three meals. Each time the גמרא answers that the person already ate a meal. Then רבי יוסי's words in the סיפא, 'always three,' show that the תנא קמא also holds three, so this משנה does not follow רבי חידקא. Next comes the משנה in פאה: someone with food for 14 meals may not take from the קופה. That number fits neither the רבנן (15) nor רבי חידקא (16). The answer for רבי חידקא is rejected because it would make a poor man fast all of Friday. The גמרא concludes that this משנה follows רבי עקיבא: 'Treat your שבת like a weekday rather than depend on other people.' The traveler clause of the same משנה, which gives three שבת meals, can fit רבי חידקא, because the traveler also eats the road meal he brought with him. A ברייתא then teaches when dishes may be rinsed on שבת. The amud ends with אגדה about the great rewards for eating three meals on שבת and for delighting in שבת, and that verse continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַצִּילִין מְזוֹן שָׁלֹשׁ סְעוּדוֹת,

one may rescue food for three meals,

מַאי לָאו — דְּלָא אֲכַל!

What, is it not that he has not eaten?!

לָא, דַּאֲכַל.

No, it is where he already ate.

שַׁחֲרִית מַצִּילִין מְזוֹן שְׁתֵּי סְעוּדוֹת,

In the morning, one may rescue food for two meals,
