Topic

Honesty at the time of the חורבן; rescuing food and clothing from a fire on שבת; stopping a fire indirectly

Overview

This amud opens in the middle of a discussion from the previous page. רבא had said ירושלים was destroyed because it had no אנשי אמנה (trustworthy people), and רב קטינא challenged him with a פסוק in ישעיה showing that people still admitted the truth about their Torah learning. The גמרא resolves this: people were honest about Torah, but not in business. A new משנה then allows saving a whole basket of loaves, a fig cake, or a barrel of wine from a fire on שבת, and discusses where the items may go and how many garments one may wear out. The גמרא reconciles this with the earlier three-meal limit, decides that filling one vessel counts as 'rescuing', and explains the helpers' 'calculation' after שבת. A ברייתא records רבי מאיר and רבי יוסי on wearing garments out of the fire, including רבי יוסי's list of eighteen garments. A final משנה allows blocking a fire with a goat hide or a wall of vessels, while רבי יוסי forbids new clay pots full of water. The גמרא quotes רב permitting water on the unburnt side of a garment and challenges him with a ברייתא; the answer comes on the next amud.
