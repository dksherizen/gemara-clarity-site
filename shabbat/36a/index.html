Topic

Three ברייתות on moving a שופר and חצוצרות on שבת matched to three תנאים, and lists of names that switched over time (רב חסדא, אביי, רב אשי)

Overview

The גמרא is in the middle of explaining contradictory ברייתות about whether one may move a שופר or חצוצרות on שבת. It first challenges the earlier split between a private and a communal שופר. A communal שופר can also feed a poor child, and a third ברייתא allows moving even trumpets. It then answers that each ברייתא follows a different תנא: רבי יהודה, רבי שמעון, or רבי נחמיה. To fix a wording problem in the ברייתא of רבי נחמיה, it quotes רב חסדא, who taught that three pairs of names switched after the בית המקדש was destroyed: שופר and חצוצרות, ערבה and צפצפה, and two words for a table. Each switch has a practical difference (נפקא מינה): the שופר of ראש השנה, the לולב, and buying and selling. אביי adds that the names of two animal stomachs switched, which matters for a needle found in the stomach wall in the laws of טריפות. Finally, רב אשי adds that the city names בבל and בורסיף switched.
