Shabbos 139b

שבת קל״ט ע״ב

Shabbat 139b

Topic

Burial on יום טוב, attachments on שבת, the limits of הערמה, and straining wine on שבת

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a story: רבי יוחנן ruled that non-Jews should bury someone who died on a יום טוב next to שבת. רבא then gives the rule. On the first day of יום טוב non-Jews bury, while on the second day Jews may bury, even on the second day of ראש השנה. רב מנשיא's earlier ban applied only to people who were not learned in Torah. Next, רב חמא בר גוריא allows wearing a canopy with its straps outside on שבת, because the straps are unimportant and become part of it, unlike ציצית. רבה בר רב הונא allows a trick (הערמה) of hanging a strainer for pomegranates on יום טוב, and רב אשי requires that pomegranates actually be hung in it, because otherwise the act looks forbidden. רב אשי also defends a young scholar's pretexts, since they involved only rabbinic rules and a scholar will not come to break them outright. A new משנה then permits straining wine that is already drinkable. The גמרא limits this with several conditions: only clear liquids, no hollow in the cloth, the basket kept low, and nothing that looks like a strainer. The amud ends by starting יעקב קרחה's explanation of the egg in a mustard strainer, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְלָא יָדַעְנָא אִי מִלְּפָנֶיהָ

And I do not know whether before it

אִי מִלְּאַחֲרֶיהָ,

or after it

וַאֲתוֹ לְקַמֵּיהּ דְּרַבִּי יוֹחָנָן,

And they came before Rabbi Yochanan

וַאֲמַר לְהוּ: יִתְעַסְּקוּן בֵּיהּ עַמְמִין.

and he said to them: Let non-Jews attend to him.
