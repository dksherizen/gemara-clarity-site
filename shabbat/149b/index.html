Shabbos 149b

שבת קמ״ט ע״ב

Shabbat 149b

Topic

Drawing lots with family versus outsiders, lots for weekday portions on יום טוב, the teaching about causing another's punishment, and a series of aggadot about נבוכדנצר.

Overview

The גמרא finishes explaining why drawing lots for food with outsiders is forbidden, adding that בית הלל would also see a problem of רבית. With one's own family it is allowed, based on רב's ruling that a father may even lend to his household with interest to teach them. The גמרא then rereads the משנה (חסורי מחסרא): with family even unequal portions are fine, but with others they are forbidden every day as קוביא. Next, רבי יעקב בריה דבת יעקב explains that כהנים may not draw lots on יום טוב for ordinary weekday portions, even though they tend to quarrel. The same אמורא teaches that whoever causes another to be punished is kept out of the מחיצה of הקב"ה. Two proofs are rejected, and a third, from משלי and תהלים, is accepted. The גמרא then proves that חלשים means lots from a פסוק calling נבוכדנצר 'חולש על גוים.' This leads to a series of teachings about נבוכדנצר: his cruelty, the world's joy at his fall, the danger of his ruins, his miraculous shame, and his lowly place in גיהנם. A new דרשה on 'איך שבת נוגש' begins at the end and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וּכְדִבְרֵי בֵּית הִלֵּל אַף מִשּׁוּם רִבִּית.

And according to the words of Beit Hillel, also because of interest.

אִי הָכִי בָּנָיו וּבְנֵי בֵּיתוֹ נָמֵי!

If so, his children and household members also (should be forbidden)!

בָּנָיו וּבְנֵי בֵּיתוֹ הַיְינוּ טַעְמָא —

His children and household members, this is the reason:

כִּדְרַב יְהוּדָה אָמַר רַב.

Like Rav Yehuda said in the name of Rav.
