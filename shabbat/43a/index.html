Topic

Using a vessel for an egg laid on שבת: רבה's common-case rule, רב יוסף's מבטל כלי מהיכנו, and רבי יצחק's rule that a vessel may only be moved for something movable.

Overview

On the previous amud, רב חסדא ruled that on שבת you may cover an egg to protect it, but you may not place a vessel under a hen to catch its egg. רבה explained that חכמים allowed moving a vessel to prevent a loss only in common situations. אביי challenged him three times (a lamp, a broken beam, a leak), and each time רבה showed that the case really was common. רב יוסף then gave a different reason: catching the egg traps the vessel holding something מוקצה, which is מבטל כלי מהיכנו. אביי challenged this with several cases: טבל, sparks, a beam, a leak, and a basket for chicks. Each is answered by showing the vessel never really becomes unusable. The basket discussion also teaches that anything מוקצה during בין השמשות stays מוקצה all day. רבי יצחק is stricter: you may not even cover the egg, because a vessel may only be moved for something that may itself be moved. The challenges to him are answered with צריך למקומו, or by showing the item is not מוקצה (stones, bricks, a hive with honey). רב עוקבא ממישן's question on the honey answer continues on the next amud.
