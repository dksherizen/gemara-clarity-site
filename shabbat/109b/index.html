Topic

Bathing in healing waters on שבת, the משנה on medicinal foods and drinks, and the גמרא's identification of those plants with related remedies

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a discussion about bathing in healing waters on שבת. The גמרא rejects the idea that the contradicting ברייתות follow a dispute among תנאים about טומאה וטהרה. It then settles on two answers. In the ים הגדול it depends on whether one bathes in the clean water or the polluted water. In מי משרה it depends on whether one lingered in the water. A new משנה then teaches that food healthy people do not eat, like איזוביון, is forbidden on שבת because eating it shows it is medicine. Normal foods and drinks are allowed even for healing. The גמרא identifies the plants in the משנה and explains what each one cures: איזוביון for intestinal worms, יועזר for liver worms, and אבוברואה for גילוי (drinking uncovered liquid). This leads into a long list of folk remedies for liver worms, swallowed venom, a swallowed snake, and a snakebite, with stories showing the cures at work. The last word of the amud begins a new story that continues on the next amud.
