Topic

Preparing foods, drinks and remedies on שבת: mustard, אֲלוּנְטִית, חלתית, and rubbing linen

Overview

This amud opens by finishing an earlier point: an egg put into a mustard strainer on שבת is not בורר, because it is only there for color. Then the גמרא asks how mustard kneaded before שבת may be dissolved on שבת. רב, שמואל, רבי אלעזר and רבי יוחנן disagree about whether the hand, a utensil, both, or neither is allowed. Stories show scholars acting differently, and מר זוטרא rules that both ways are fine if one mixes gently and does not beat. The same rule applies to cress and garlic. Next, a ברייתא allows the spiced wine drink אֵנוֹמֵלִין on שבת but forbids אֲלוּנְטִית, a cooling drink used as a remedy. A new משנה then forbids soaking חלתית as medicine and forbids actively cleaning vetches and straw. The גמרא decides that soaking חלתית is only a rabbinic ban, and that soaking it in cold water is forbidden except when stopping a treatment already begun would be dangerous. The amud ends with stories about rubbing a linen shirt and a scarf on שבת, and רב חסדא begins a new statement that continues on the next amud.
