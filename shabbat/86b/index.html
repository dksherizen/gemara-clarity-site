Topic

טבול יום at מתן תורה, how long שכבת זרע stays טמא, and the dispute over the date and weekday of מתן תורה

Overview

The גמרא asks how the תורה could be given when some women may have been טבולי יום at Sinai. Two אמוראים answer that the תורה can be given to a טבול יום, and מרימר clarifies that this only means it was fit to be given that way. רבי יצחק then explains why the immersion was not moved to twilight or to שבת morning: the תורה had to be given in daylight, and the whole nation had to arrive together. Next, רבי יוחנן reports that חכמים require six full עונות, and רב חסדא limits the dispute to semen discharged from a woman, defending this against רב ששת's challenge. רב פפא asks about semen inside a גויה and inside a בהמה, and both questions end in תיקו. Finally, a ברייתא records a מחלוקת over whether the עשרת הדברות were given on the 6th or 7th of סיון. רבא shows that both sides agree the people arrived at סיני on ראש חודש and that the תורה was given on שבת. The real dispute is which weekday ראש חודש fell on, and רבי יוסי's day-by-day timeline begins here and continues on the next amud.

But weren't they people who had immersed that day?
