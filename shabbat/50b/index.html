Topic

Shabbat 50b: Washing substances on שבת and beautifying oneself, then the pot in the basket and pulling objects out of earth or bricks on שבת.

Overview

The גמרא first finishes explaining two conflicting ברייתות about cleaning with sand and נתר. It concludes that both follow רבי יהודה and that they only disagree about whether these substances scrape. The permission to wash the face is explained as referring to someone with no beard. Next, several אמוראים list substances that anyone may use to wash on שבת, including ברדא, which is defined as a herbal mixture without a majority of אהלא. רב ששת forbids splitting olives for their oil even on a weekday, because it makes food disgusting, and the גמרא shows this does not contradict שמואל's ruling about bread. A story about אמימר, רב אשי and מר זוטרא shows two views on beautifying oneself, each based on a ברייתא. The גמרא then returns to the משנה of the pot in the basket and explains that everyone forbids returning the pot once the hole collapsed. The argument is only about worrying that it might collapse. Related rules follow about pulling a branch or knife out of earth, bricks or reeds on שבת. A strong challenge to these rules from a משנה about a buried turnip begins at the end and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

נָזִיר חוֹפֵף וּמְפַסְפֵּס,

A nazirite may wash and separate [his hair],

אֲבָל לֹא סוֹרֵק!

but he may not comb it!

אֶלָּא הָא וְהָא רַבִּי יְהוּדָה הִיא,

Rather, both this and that are Rabbi Yehuda,

וּתְרֵי תַנָּאֵי אַלִּיבָּא דְּרַבִּי יְהוּדָה.

and there are two tanna'im according to Rabbi Yehuda.
