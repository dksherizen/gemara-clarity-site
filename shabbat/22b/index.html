Topic

Lighting one חנוכה lamp from another (מנר לנר), and the איבעיא of whether הדלקה or הנחה fulfills the מצוה

Overview

This amud continues the מחלוקת between רב, who forbids lighting one חנוכה lamp from another (מנר לנר), and שמואל, who permits it. First, a ברייתא about מעשר שני seems to refute שמואל: holy money may not be used as a weight, even to redeem other מעשר שני. רבה answers that this ban is a special גזירה that applies only to weighing. Next, רב ששת challenges רב from the מנורה in the מקדש, where the other lamps were lit from the miraculous נר מערבי. That practice seems to contradict both reasons given for רב: בזוי מצוה and אכחושי מצוה. רב פפא answers that long wicks were used, which saves the בזוי מצוה reason, but the אכחושי מצוה reason remains a קשיא. רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע then rules that the law depends on whether the lighting (הדלקה) or the placing (הנחה) is the act that fulfills the מצוה. The גמרא brings this question as an איבעיא. It pushes off two proofs from רבא, since in both cases an onlooker would think the lamp was for personal use. The amud ends as the גמרא starts a third proof from רבי יהושע בן לוי, which continues on the next amud.
