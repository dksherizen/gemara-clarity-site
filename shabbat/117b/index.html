Shabbos 117b

שבת קי״ז ע״ב

Shabbat 117b

Topic

Saving food from a fire on שבת: the three-meal limit and its reason, the broken-barrel ברייתא and הערמה, bread customs learned from the מן, and the number of שבת meals

Overview

This amud opens by finishing a discussion from the previous משנה. רב אשי defines a closed alley and an open alley for bringing כתבי הקודש there during a fire, and he explains that even רבי אליעזר agrees one לחי is enough for a ספר תורה. A new משנה then teaches that during a fire on שבת one may save food only for the meals still left, while רבי יוסי says one may always save three meals' worth. רבא explains the limit: a panicked owner might come to put out the fire. אביי challenges this from a ברייתא about a broken barrel on a roof, and רבא answers that the barrel case has its own decree about carrying through רשות הרבים. The גמרא then studies that ברייתא in full, including the rule about guests and a dispute over הערמה. It asks whether that dispute matches the argument of רבי אליעזר and רבי יהושע about an animal and its offspring in a pit, and it rejects the comparison. More ברייתות follow about which bread may be saved and about bread left in the oven, which must be removed with a knife to make a change on שבת. Next come teachings learned from the מן: rising early on Friday, לחם משנה, and customs about breaking the שבת bread. Last, the גמרא returns to the משנה, brings the dispute over whether there are three or four שבת meals, and begins checking whose view the משנה follows, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא אָמַר רַב אָשֵׁי:

Rather, Rav Ashi said:

שָׁלֹשׁ מְחִיצּוֹת וְלֶחִי אֶחָד —

Three walls and one side-post —

זֶה מָבוֹי שֶׁאֵינוֹ מְפוּלָּשׁ.

this is the alley that is not open (closed).

שָׁלֹשׁ מְחִיצּוֹת בְּלֹא לֶחִי —

Three walls without a side-post —
