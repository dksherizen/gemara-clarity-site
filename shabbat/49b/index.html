Shabbos 49b

Topic

Craftsman's hides as מוקצה, the source of the 39 מלאכות, and moving insulating fleece on שבת

Overview

The amud opens with a tanner who had his hides brought out on שבת to sit on, which suggests a craftsman's hides are not מוקצה. A ברייתא about a craftsman's boards challenges this. The גמרא answers that boards are different, because a craftsman protects them from damage. A second ברייתא permitting all hides is limited to a homeowner, and the גמרא then shows that the craftsman question is a dispute between the תנא קמא and רבי יוסי. Next, the חכמים ask what the 39 אבות מלאכות correspond to. רבי חנינא בר חמא says they match the work of the משכן, and another view says they match the 39 times the word מלאכה appears in the תורה. רב יוסף asks whether the יוסף verse is part of that count, and the question ends in תיקו. A ברייתא then supports the משכן view, including how carrying is learned from moving the משכן's boards. Finally, the גמרא returns to the משנה about wool fleece. רבא says fleece used for insulating may be moved, and a new student challenges him from the משנה; the challenge continues on the next amud.
