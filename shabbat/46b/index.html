Shabbos 46b

שבת מ״ו ע״ב

Topic

רבי שמעון's view of מוקצה and of unintended acts (דבר שאין מתכוין), shown through the lamp, the בכור, and vows

Overview

This amud opens at the end of a story. רב אויא gives his last answer to רבא: a ברייתא says jewelry may be moved in a courtyard on שבת, and עולא explains that this is because it has the status of a כלי. The same reason works for רב אויא's case, and רב נחמן בר יצחק thanks God that רבא did not embarrass him. Then אביי raises a contradiction before רבה. רבי שמעון allows leftover lamp oil, which suggests he does not hold of מוקצה. Yet he forbids a בכור whose blemish was not noticeable before יום טוב. רבה answers that a person expects his lamp to go out, but nobody counts on a blemish, a permanent one, and a חכם willing to examine it. Two challenges from vows are answered: a wife's vow always depends on her husband, and three ordinary people can release a vow if no חכם will. Next, אביי asks רב יוסף why רבי שמעון forbids moving a burning lamp, since he permits unintended acts like dragging furniture. The first answer splits Torah-level and rabbinic results. רבא knocks that answer down with the case of clothing merchants, and the amud ends just as רבא begins a new explanation, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הַשֵּׁירִים וְהַנְּזָמִים וְהַטַּבָּעוֹת,

Bracelets, nose-rings, and rings,

הֲרֵי הֵן כְּכׇל הַכֵּלִים

they are like all the vessels

הַנִּטָּלִים בֶּחָצֵר.

that may be moved in the courtyard.

וְאָמַר עוּלָּא: מַה טַּעַם?

And Ulla said: What is the reason?
