Topic

The amount of קלף and דוכסוסטוס for carrying on שבת, and רב's ruling on which hide may be used for תפילין and מזוזה

Overview

The גמרא starts with the משנה's rule that carrying קלף on שבת makes you liable once there is enough to write the shortest פרשה of תפילין. A ברייתא seems to say 'enough to write a מזוזה.' The גמרא settles this by reading the ברייתא as giving a separate amount for each material: a מזוזה for דוכסוסטוס, and שמע ישראל for קלף. Then רב rules that דוכסוסטוס is like קלף, so תפילין may be written on it. The גמרא challenges רב several times. It answers that the sources describe the ideal way (למצוה), or speak about a מזוזה, or reflect a מחלוקת תנאים. Proposed proofs for רב, from תנא דבי מנשה and from worn-out תפילין, are each rejected. Since רבי מאיר wrote a מזוזה on קלף, the גמרא finally rereads רב to mean that קלף, like דוכסוסטוס, is valid for a מזוזה. The amud ends by quoting the next line of the משנה, about ink.

Opening lines

קְלָף כְּדֵי לִכְתּוֹב עָלָיו
