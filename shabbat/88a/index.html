Shabbos 88a

שבת פ״ח ע״א

Shabbat 88a

Topic

The date of מתן תורה (the dispute between the רבנן and רבי יוסי), followed by אגדות about Israel's acceptance of the תורה and the praise of נעשה ונשמע.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a calendar answer about the date of מתן תורה: did it happen on 6 סיון, as the רבנן say, or on 7 סיון, as רבי יוסי says? A ברייתא from סדר עולם puts ראש חודש סיון on Monday, but רבי יוסי answers that it follows the רבנן. A ברייתא in רבי יוסי's own name says משה 'had no time' on the sixth. The גמרא explains this as שבת preparation, not מתן תורה. A Galilean's דרשה about 'threes' is shown to follow the רבנן. Then the גמרא turns to אגדות about מתן תורה: the mountain held over Israel like a tub, the claim of force this raises, and its fix through the willing acceptance in the days of אחשורוש. Next comes ריש לקיש's teaching that all of creation depended on Israel accepting the תורה. A series of teachings then praises Israel for saying נעשה before נשמע: the two crowns, the angels' secret, and the apple tree. The amud ends with a מין mocking רבא as one of a rash nation, and רבא's answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לְרַבָּנַן שְׁמוֹנָה חֲסֵרִים עֲבוּד.

According to the Rabbis, they established eight lacking months.

תָּא שְׁמַע, דְּתַנְיָא בְּ״סֵדֶר עוֹלָם״:

Come and hear, as it was taught in Seder Olam:

נִיסָן שֶׁבּוֹ יָצְאוּ יִשְׂרָאֵל מִמִּצְרַיִם

The Nissan in which Israel left Egypt:

בְּאַרְבָּעָה עָשָׂר שָׁחֲטוּ פִּסְחֵיהֶן,

on the fourteenth they slaughtered their Pesach offerings,
