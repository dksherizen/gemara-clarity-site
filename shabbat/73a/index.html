Topic

The dispute between אביי and רבא about doing a forbidden act on שבת without intending its forbidden form, followed by the משנה listing the 39 אבות מלאכות.

Overview

This amud opens at the end of a proof for רבא from a ברייתא that contrasts שבת with שאר מצות. The proof reads the ברייתא as saying that one who meant to cut a detached plant but cut an attached one is פטור on שבת. אביי answers that the ברייתא is talking about one who meant only to lift the plant, and in that case even he agrees the person is פטור. The גמרא then brings two more cases where אביי and רבא argue the same way: meaning to throw two אמות and throwing four, and throwing in what he thought was a רשות היחיד that turned out to be a רשות הרבים. It explains why all three cases are needed. Next it challenges רבא from רבי יוחנן's rule that one who does all 39 מלאכות in one lapse of awareness is liable for each: how can someone know it is שבת yet be unaware of the מלאכות? The answer is that he knew the לאו but not the כרת (per רבי יוחנן), or he knew only the Torah law of תחומין (per ריש לקיש and רבי עקיבא). Finally a new משנה lists all 39 אבות מלאכות: bread-making, cloth-making, leather-making, and a closing group ending with carrying between domains.
