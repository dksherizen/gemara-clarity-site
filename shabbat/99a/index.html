Topic

The משכן's curtains and wagons as the model for רשות הרבים, and how a pit and its bank combine to make a separate domain

Overview

This amud finishes a description of the משכן's beauty: the clasps in the loops shone like stars. A ברייתא then teaches that the plain goat-hair curtains needed more חכמה than the fine lower curtains, because the women spun the hair while it was still on the goats. The גמרא returns to the משנה of the two balconies. רב, quoting רבי חייא, says the spaces under, between, and beside the wagons that carried the קרשים were רשות הרבים. אביי and רבא give the wagons' measurements and explain why the extra room was needed. The total comes to fifteen אמות, so the גמרא asks how a public road can be sixteen. It answers that one more אמה was where a לוי stood to catch slipping קרשים. A new משנה then teaches that a pit's bank or a rock that is ten טפחים high and four wide is its own domain. The גמרא learns from the משנה's wording that a pit and its bank combine to make ten, supporting רבי יוחנן, and starts a ברייתא that continues on the next amud.
