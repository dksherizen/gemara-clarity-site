Topic

Reassembling a collapsible bed, catching lamp sparks on שבת, and the opening of במה טומנין (materials for wrapping food)

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא about putting together a bed made of separate parts. The תנא קמא forbids it, and fastening the parts tightly counts as Torah-level בונה. רבן שמעון בן גמליאל permits it if the parts fit loosely, and in a story about a bed in רב חמא's house, רבא says he follows that view. A new משנה then permits placing a vessel under a lamp to catch sparks, but forbids putting water in it, because the water puts the sparks out. The גמרא explains that the vessel does not become unusable, because sparks have no real substance. It also asks whether the ban on water follows רבי יוסי's view on indirect extinguishing. It rejects that idea, and רב אשי explains that even the רבנן forbid it, because the water speeds up the extinguishing. After the הדרן for פרק כירה, the new chapter במה טומנין lists materials one may not wrap food in, some forbidden always and some only when moist. The גמרא asks whether the forbidden גפת means olive or sesame residue, tries a proof from רבי זירא, and begins rejecting it. That rejection continues on the next amud.
