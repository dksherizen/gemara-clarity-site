Shabbos 103a

Shabbat 103a

Topic

Ending the previous משנה (drilling, 'זה הכלל', the anvil), the משנה on field labors and their minimum amounts, and the משנה on writing two letters

Overview

The גמרא finishes the previous משנה. It explains how drilling a tiny hole counts as a finished מלאכה even for שמואל, what the words 'זה הכלל' add, and why striking an anvil makes one liable: the משכן metalworkers did it, not because it trains the hand. A new משנה then teaches that plowing, weeding, and pruning make one liable for any amount, while gathering wood or grass has a minimum that depends on the purpose. A ברייתא gives similar purpose-based measures for cutting plants. The גמרא asks why every cut does not count as improving the land, and answers with a swamp (רבה and רב יוסף) or someone else's field (אביי). Next, a משנה rules that writing two letters makes one liable, with reasons from רבי יוסי (marking, as on the boards of the משכן) and רבי יהודה (a short name inside a longer one). The גמרא explains why the left hand counts: either an ambidextrous person (אביי) or the view of רבי יוסי. Finally, it asks whether רבי יהודה requires two different letters, and begins quoting a ברייתא on 'מאחת' that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הַקּוֹדֵחַ כׇּל שֶׁהוּא — חַיָּיב.

One who drills any amount — is liable.

בִּשְׁלָמָא לְרַב —

It makes sense according to Rav —

מֶיחְזֵי כְּמַאן דְּחַר חוֹרְתָא לְבִנְיָינָא,

he looks like one who makes a hole for building,

אֶלָּא לִשְׁמוּאֵל —

but according to Shmuel —

The steps (33)

  1. StatementThe משנה's rule: drilling any size hole makes one liable
  2. ChallengeAccording to שמואל, drilling is not a finished labor
  3. ResolutionThe case is one where an iron nail drilled the hole and was left in
  4. QuestionWhat does 'this is the principle' come to include?
  5. ResponseIt includes carving a small measure in wood meant for a larger one
  6. QuestionWhy is striking the anvil with a hammer a labor?
  7. Responseרבה and רב יוסף: he is training his hand
  8. Challengeבני רחבה: then watching and learning a craft would be liable too
  9. Resolutionאביי and רבא: the משכן metalworkers did this
  10. ProofA ברייתא supports אביי and רבא
  11. StatementNew משנה: plowing, weeding, and pruning any amount
  12. StatementGathering wood or grass: the amount depends on the purpose
  13. QuestionWhat is plowing a tiny amount good for?
  14. ResponseEnough to plant one pumpkin seed
  15. StatementMinimum amounts for cutting plants depend on the purpose
  16. ChallengeDoesn't every cut improve the land?
  17. ResolutionThe case is a swamp
  18. Resolutionאביי: The person did not intend to improve the land
  19. ChallengeBut a result that is certain counts anyway
  20. ResolutionIt is someone else's field
  21. StatementWriting two letters makes one liable
  22. Statementרבי יוסי: Two letters count because they are a mark
  23. Statementרבי יהודה: A short name inside a longer name
  24. ChallengeWhy is writing with the left hand liable?
  25. ResolutionThe משנה is about a left-handed person
  26. ChallengeThen the right hand should not count for him
  27. ResolutionThe משנה is about someone who uses both hands equally
  28. ResolutionAnother answer: the משנה follows רבי יוסי
  29. ChallengeBut רבי יוסי is named only in the later part
  30. ResolutionThe whole משנה is רבי יוסי
  31. QuestionDoes רבי יהודה require two different letters?
  32. ChallengeA ברייתא: liable even without finishing the whole job
  33. ChallengeThe ברייתא worries that even one letter, one thread, or one sieve-eye might be enough
