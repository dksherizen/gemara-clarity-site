שבת צ״ז ע״א

Topic

Hidden sins of צדיקים and related aggadot, then throwing across domains on שבת: קלוטה כמה שהונחה, the height zones, and the source of לבוד

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the argument between רבי עקיבא and רבי יהודה בן בתירא about revealing sins the Torah keeps hidden. רבי יהודה בן בתירא never received the גזירה שוה that makes צלפחד the מקושש, so for him צלפחד was one of the מעפילים. They argue the same way about whether אהרן also got צרעת, and a ברייתא supports רבי עקיבא. Several aggadic teachings follow. רֵישׁ לָקִישׁ says one who suspects innocent people is struck in his body, as משה was. Good from ה׳ comes faster than punishment. אהרן's staff swallowing the other staffs was a miracle within a miracle. The גמרא then returns to the משנה about throwing from one רשות היחיד to another through רשות הרבים. It proves that רבי עקיבא and the חכמים argue below ten טפחים about קלוטה כמה שהונחה. It then brings other views on the height zones and shows that לבוד is a הלכה למשה מסיני. The amud ends with רבי's view on throwing across a roofed private domain, and רב חנא begins to raise a difficulty that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְאֶלָּא הָא גָּמַר גְּזֵירָה שָׁוָה!

But didn't he (Rabbi Akiva) learn it from a verbal analogy?!

גְּזֵירָה שָׁוָה לָא גָּמַר.

He (Rabbi Yehuda ben Beteira) did not learn the verbal analogy.

אֶלָּא מֵהֵיכָא הֲוָה?

But then, from where (which group) was he?

מִ״וַּיַּעְפִּילוּ״ הֲוָה.

He was from (those of) 'And they presumed [to go up].'
