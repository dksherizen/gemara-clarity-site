Shabbos 65a

שבת ס״ה ע״א

Shabbat 65a

Learn Shabbos 65a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Shabbos 65a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

What a woman may wear or carry on her body outside on שבת: cloths, mouth items, false teeth, a healing coin, girls' ear threads and fastened cloaks, plus the strict practices of שמואל's father.

Overview

This amud opens with the end of a ברייתא about spreading wet clothes in the sun. It shows that תנאים argue whether a מראית העין ban applies even in private. The גמרא then explains our משנה's list of things a woman may wear outside on שבת. A מוך in the ear or sandal must be tied, but a menstrual מוך need not be, because it is repulsive and she will not carry it. Two great sages went out with untied ear cloths, and the גמרא explains that a cloth pressed in tightly needs no tying. The גמרא explains the pepper, salt and spices in her mouth, and limits the dispute about a false tooth to gold only. אביי shows that רבי, רבי אליעזר and רבי שמעון בן אלעזר share one idea: a woman will not take off and show an item whose removal makes her look bad. A new משנה permits a coin on a foot wound, threads in girls' ears, veils and fastened cloaks. The גמרא explains why a סלע heals the wound. It then tells of שמואל's father's strict practices with his daughters, and that discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שׁוֹטְחָן בַּחַמָּה,

He may spread them out in the sun,

אֲבָל לֹא כְּנֶגֶד הָעָם.

but not in front of the people.

רַבִּי אֱלִיעֶזֶר וְרַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן אוֹסְרִין.

Rabbi Eliezer and Rabbi Shimon forbid it.

וּבְמוֹךְ שֶׁבְּאׇזְנָהּ.

And with a cloth that is in her ear.
