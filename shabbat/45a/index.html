Topic

Does רב follow רבי יהודה on מוקצה, and which kinds of מוקצה does even רבי שמעון accept?

Overview

The גמרא brings a support that רב follows רבי יהודה, who holds there is a prohibition of מוקצה. The support is רב's ruling that a lamp may be placed on a palm tree for שבת but not for יום טוב. It then challenges this from a case where רב allowed moving a נר חנוכה on שבת. It answers that he relied on רבי שמעון only in an emergency (שעת הדחק). Next, ריש לקיש asks רבי יוחנן whether רבי שמעון agrees to מוקצה when a person actively set something aside, like sown wheat or eggs under a hen. רבי יוחנן answers that for רבי שמעון the only מוקצה is something like lamp oil while it burns. A ברייתא about סוכה decorations, shown to follow רבי שמעון, leads the גמרא to clarify that setting something aside for a מצוה is itself enough to make it forbidden. Then שמואל says רבי שמעון's מוקצה applies only to figs and grapes set out to dry. A ברייתא about all drying fruits challenges him, and the גמרא answers that this ברייתא follows רבי יהודה. The amud ends as רבי שמעון בר רבי begins to ask his father רבי a question, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הָכִי נָמֵי מִיסְתַּבְּרָא

So too, it is reasonable

דְּרַב כְּרַבִּי יְהוּדָה סְבִירָא לֵיהּ,

that Rav holds like Rabbi Yehuda,

דְּאָמַר רַב:

for Rav said:

מַנִּיחִין נֵר עַל גַּבֵּי דֶּקֶל בְּשַׁבָּת,

One may place a lamp on top of a palm tree for Shabbat,
