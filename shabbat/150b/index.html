Topic

Calculations on שבת, the משנה of אין מחשיכין, רב יהודה's rule about speaking of plans that have a permitted version, working before הבדלה, and אבא שאול's general rule

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא about calculations on שבת and settles a contradiction with a second ברייתא. Counting past expenses is forbidden only when wages are still owed, because then the counting has a practical use. The גמרא then turns to the משנה of אין מחשיכין. It tells the story of a חסיד who would not fix his fence because he had thought about fixing it on שבת, and a caper bush grew in the gap and supported his family. רב יהודה in the name of שמואל rules that you may speak on שבת about a trip planned for after שבת, because with בורגנין the walk itself would be permitted. The גמרא tests this rule against the משנה, a ברייתא of רבי אושעיא, and a ברייתא about a bride and a dead person. Each time it answers that the case has no permitted version on שבת: attached fruit, rotten hay, and cutting myrtle or cloth. Next the גמרא asks how one may guard his field at nightfall before הבדלה. It answers either that he stands among winepresses where wine is available, or that saying 'המבדיל בין קודש לחול' is enough to begin work. Finally, the גמרא begins to ask which clause of the משנה אבא שאול's general rule refers to, and this question continues on the next amud.
