Shabbos 156b

שבת קנ״ו ע״ב

Topic

אין מזל לישראל: צדקה and יראת שמים can overcome fate. Then a new משנה on מוקצה, and the dispute over whether the הלכה follows רבי יהודה or רבי שמעון.

Overview

This amud finishes the idea of אין מזל לישראל: the stars do not lock in a Jew's fate. First 'ה tells אברהם that He will move the planet צדק (Jupiter) from west to east, and a פסוק in ישעיהו is read as a hint to this. Then come three stories. A man predicted to die from a snakebite is saved because he spared a poor friend embarrassment, and שמואל teaches that צדקה saves from death itself. רבי עקיבא's daughter is saved on her wedding day because she gave her portion to a poor man, and רבי עקיבא teaches the same lesson. רב נחמן בר יצחק was predicted to be a thief, and his mother protected him with a head covering and תפילה; the one time it slipped off, his nature showed itself. Next a new משנה allows cutting food for animals on שבת, but רבי יהודה forbids an animal that died on שבת because it is מוקצה. The גמרא lists who rules like רבי יהודה (עולא, רב, לוי) and who rules like רבי שמעון (שמואל, זעירי, רבי יוחנן). It then challenges רבי יוחנן's view from his rule to follow a סתם משנה, and that challenge continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דְּקָאֵי צֶדֶק בְּמַעֲרָב —

Is it because Jupiter stands in the west?

מְהַדַּרְנָא וּמוֹקֵימְנָא לֵיהּ בְּמִזְרָח.

I will turn it back and set it in the east.

וְהַיְינוּ דִּכְתִיב:

And this is what is written:

״מִי הֵעִיר מִמִּזְרָח
