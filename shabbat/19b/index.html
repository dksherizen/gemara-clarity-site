Shabbos 19b

שבת י״ט ע״ב

Shabbat 19b

Topic

Whose opinion our משנה follows on liquids that flow out on שבת, the מוקצה disputes of רב and שמואל, and a new משנה on roasting and baking before שבת

Overview

This amud finishes the discussion of whose opinion our משנה follows when it lets a person load an olive press or winepress right before שבת. רבי יוסי בר חנינא says it follows רבי ישמעאל. The אמורא רבי אלעזר says it follows the תנא רבי אלעזר, who permits honey and even olive and grape juice that flow out by themselves on שבת. The גמרא explains why each אמורא did not accept the other's source, and reports that רבי יוסי בר חנינא ruled like רבי ישמעאל in practice. Next comes a list of מוקצה cases: oil and mats of olive pressers, ship mats, and animals or dates set aside for purposes other than eating. In every case רב forbids and שמואל permits, following the מחלוקת of רבי יהודה and רבי שמעון. Two short stories follow. In the first, a student who ruled like רבי שמעון in רב's town was placed in נידוי for disrespect. In the second, two students rescued items from a fire in different ways. Finally a new משנה begins: roasting and baking may start just before שבת only if the food reaches a basic stage before dark, with the קרבן פסח and the fire of the בית המוקד as exceptions.

Opening lines

לֹא יִגְמוֹר.

He may not finish.

וְרַבִּי אֶלְעָזָר אָמַר:

And Rabbi Elazar said:

רַבִּי אֶלְעָזָר הִיא.

It is [the opinion of] Rabbi Elazar.

דִּתְנַן: חַלּוֹת דְּבַשׁ שֶׁרִיסְּקָן בְּעֶרֶב שַׁבָּת

As we learned: honeycombs that he crushed on Shabbat eve
