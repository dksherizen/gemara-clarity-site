Topic

The כלל that a חטאת requires שגגה from beginning to end in throwing and carrying, and how intention makes an unusual spot count as a place

Overview

The משנה teaches that someone who throws on שבת by mistake is פטור if he remembers mid-flight, or if the object is caught or burned before landing. It then states a כלל: a חטאת is brought only when both the beginning and the end of the act were בשגגה. The גמרא asks why the opening case implies that he is חייב if the object lands, when he already remembered. רב כהנא and רבא try several answers. רבא finally reads the opening line as two separate exempt cases. רב אשי instead fills in missing words: if the object lands, he is חייב only if he forgot again before it landed. Next, רבה and רבא discuss a case of two אמות שוגג, two מזיד, and two שוגג. The גמרא shows that רבה is speaking of carrying and רבא of throwing, so they do not really disagree. Finally, רבה rules that throwing on purpose into a dog's mouth or a furnace makes one חייב, because his intention makes it count as a place. רב ביבי בר אביי supports this from carrying food out in one's mouth, and the chapter ends.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳ הַזּוֹרֵק,

MISHNA: One who throws,

וְנִזְכַּר מֵאַחַר שֶׁיָּצְתָה מִיָּדוֹ,

and remembered after it left his hand,

קְלָטָהּ אַחֵר,

if another person caught it,

קְלָטָהּ כֶּלֶב אוֹ שֶׁנִּשְׂרְפָה —

a dog caught it, or it was burned —
