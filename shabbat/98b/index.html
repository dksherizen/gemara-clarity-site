Shabbos 98b

שבת צ״ח ע״ב

Shabbat 98b

Topic

The shape of the קרשים of the משכן (tapered or even, רבי יהודה vs. רבי נחמיה) and how this changes the way the curtains covered the משכן.

Overview

This amud opens with שמואל finishing an earlier question about the wagons of the משכן: what lay under them was the narrow stakes (יתדות). Then a ברייתא brings a מחלוקת about the beams (קרשים). רבי יהודה says they narrowed to a fingerbreadth at the top. רבי נחמיה says they stayed one אמה thick all the way up. Each side explains the other's word in the פסוק (תמים, יחדו), and רבי יהודה answers a challenge from the corner beams by saying the beams were sloped like mountains. A ברייתא adds that the middle bar (בריח התיכון) stood by a miracle. The גמרא then measures the inner curtains and the goat-hair curtains and shows how much of the beams and sockets each view leaves covered or showing. The extra back curtain leads to a question on רבי נחמיה from the word תסרח, which he reads as 'hangs longer than the others.' The amud ends by starting a new ברייתא about grooved beams and hollow sockets, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אָמַר שְׁמוּאֵל: בִּיתֵדוֹת.

Shmuel said: With the stakes.

תָּנוּ רַבָּנַן:

The Sages taught:

קְרָשִׁים מִלְּמַטָּן עוֹבְיָין אַמָּה,

The beams were one cubit thick at the bottom,

וּמִלְּמַעְלָן כָּלִין וְהוֹלְכִין עַד כְּאֶצְבַּע,

and at the top they kept narrowing until they were like a fingerbreadth,
