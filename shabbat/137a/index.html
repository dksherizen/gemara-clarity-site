Shabbos 137a

שבת קל״ז ע״א

Shabbat 137a

Topic

רבי יהודה on the אנדרוגינוס, mistakenly circumcising the wrong baby on שבת, the days a ברית may be delayed, and an incomplete מילה

Overview

The גמרא finishes showing that רבי יהודה does not treat an אנדרוגינוס as a definite male in general. It proves this from an unattributed ספרא and from a משנה about the פרה אדומה waters, and explains that מילה is different because the פסוק says "כׇּל זָכָר". A new משנה then discusses a father who mixed up two babies and circumcised the wrong one on שבת. רב הונא and רב יהודה read the משנה differently, and three ברייתות report the dispute between רבי אליעזר and רבי יהושע in different ways. רבי אליעזר learns from עבודה זרה that any mistaken forbidden act requires a חטאת. רבי יהושע exempts someone who made the mistake while busy with a מצוה. A puzzle in רבי חייא's version is solved by asking whether שבת was actually set aside for this particular person. Next, a משנה explains why a ברית can be pushed from day 8 up to day 12, and that a sick baby waits until he is healthy, which שמואל defines as seven full days after the fever leaves. The amud ends with a משנה about leftover skin that makes a מילה incomplete, and about fixing a fleshy baby only because of מראית העין.

Opening lines

וּסְתָם סִפְרָא רַבִּי יְהוּדָה.

And an unattributed teaching in the Sifra is Rabbi Yehuda.

אָמַר רַב נַחְמָן בַּר יִצְחָק:

Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak said:

אַף אֲנַן נָמֵי תְּנֵינָא:

We too have also learned this in a mishnah:

הַכֹּל כְּשֵׁרִים לְקַדֵּשׁ,

Everyone is fit to sanctify the purification waters,
