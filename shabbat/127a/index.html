Shabbos 127a

שבת קכ״ז ע״א

Topic

Clearing space on שבת for guests and Torah study: the storeroom rules, piled grain and מוקצה, the limits on clearing, and the greatness of הכנסת אורחין

Overview

This amud finishes explaining the משנה about clearing baskets of produce on שבת to make room for guests or for a בית המדרש. The גמרא says 'four or five' is loose speech. It also says 'not the storeroom' means you may not empty the whole storeroom, in case you fill in holes in the floor. The משנה follows רבי שמעון, who has no מוקצה prohibition. Two ברייתות follow. The first is about starting a storeroom and making a path with one's feet. The second is about piled grain, where the names are swapped so that רבי שמעון is the lenient one. Piled grain is defined as a לתך. Next come two איבעיות. The first asks whether the limit is about fewer trips or lighter loads, and the answer is fewer trips. The second asks whether the amount grows with the number of guests: stories about רבי and רבי חייא clearing a whole field show that it does. A proof that one man may clear for everyone is pushed off. The amud closes with praise of הכנסת אורחין, which is compared to the בית המדרש and even to greeting the שכינה. It also gives רבי יוחנן's list of six מצוות whose fruits are eaten in this world. A challenge to that list from a משנה is answered on the next amud.

Opening lines

כִּדְאָמְרִי אִינָשֵׁי,

as people say,

וְאִי בָּעֵי אֲפִילּוּ טוּבָא נָמֵי מְפַנִּין.

and if he wants, he may clear even many.

וּמַאי ״אֲבָל לֹא אֶת הָאוֹצָר״ —

And what is 'but not the storeroom'?

שֶׁלֹּא יִגְמוֹר כּוּלּוֹ,

that he should not finish all of it,
