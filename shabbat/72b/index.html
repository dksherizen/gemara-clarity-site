Topic

מתעסק on שבת: the מחלוקת between אביי and רבא, and the גמרא's analysis of the ברייתא comparing שבת to other מצוות, which turns out to mean עבודה זרה.

Overview

The גמרא first rules that someone who only meant to lift a detached plant, and accidentally cut an attached one, is פטור. Then אביי and רבא disagree about someone who meant to cut a detached plant and cut an attached one instead: רבא says פטור, and אביי says חייב. רבא brings a ברייתא as proof. It says that in other מצוות, 'שגג בלא מתכוין' is חייב, but on שבת it is not. Before using the proof, the גמרא works out the ברייתא's first line. It concludes that 'other מצוות' means עבודה זרה, following רבי אמי. The גמרא then looks for a case of 'שגג בלא מתכוין' in עבודה זרה. Bowing in a supposed בית הכנסת fails, and so does bowing to a statue. Bowing out of love or fear works only for אביי. The case of one who thinks it is 'מותר' clashes with רבא's own question to רב נחמן. The answer to that challenge comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

נִתְכַּוֵּין לְהַגְבִּיהַּ אֶת הַתָּלוּשׁ,

If he intended to lift a detached plant,

וְחָתַךְ אֶת הַמְחוּבָּר — פָּטוּר.

and he cut an attached plant, he is exempt.
