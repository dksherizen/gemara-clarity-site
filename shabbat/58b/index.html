Topic

When a bell (זוג) can become טמא: the role of the clapper (עינבל), a child's bell versus an adult's, and why a bell stays טמא after its clapper is removed.

Overview

This amud asks when a bell (זוג) can become טמא. It finishes a ברייתא teaching that an animal's bell is טמא while a door's bell, which counts as part of the house, is טהור. The reason given is that a כלי enters טומאה through מחשבה alone but leaves it only through a שינוי מעשה. This ברייתא contradicts an earlier source that called the animal's bell טהור, so the גמרא answers that one source speaks of a bell with an עינבל (clapper) and the other of a bell without one. When challenged on how a clapper could matter, the גמרא brings רבי יונתן's teaching that a metal object that makes sound counts as a כלי. The גמרא then distinguishes a child's bell, which is meant for sound, from an adult's bell, which is a תכשיט and is a כלי even without a clapper. Next, אביי explains why a bell stays טמא after its clapper is removed: an ordinary person can put the clapper back. רבא challenges this from a source calling bell and clapper a חיבור, and רבה's rule about tools in use and not in use shows that חיבור means parts that are actually attached. So אביי's answer falls, and the גמרא begins רבא's new explanation, which continues on the next amud.
