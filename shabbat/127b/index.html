Topic

Judging others favorably (דן לכף זכות) through three stories, then the משנה's list of foods that may be moved on שבת

Overview

This amud opens by finishing an earlier question. A ברייתא listed more rewarding good deeds than the משנה did, and the גמרא explains that the extra items already fit inside the משנה's shorter list. Next, a ברייתא teaches that one who judges others favorably (לכף זכות) is judged favorably by Heaven. Three stories show this in action: a worker who trusts his employer even though he is refused his wages, students who trust a חסיד who kept a ransomed girl near him, and students who trust רבי יהושע after his private meeting with a Roman matron. Each story ends with the same oath and the blessing 'just as you judged me favorably, may ה׳ judge you favorably.' The גמרא then returns to the משנה's list of foods that may be moved on שבת. It explains why each item had to be mentioned: תרומה held by a ישראל, דמאי, מעשר ראשון taken early on the stalks, and מעשר שני redeemed without the חומש. Finally it notes that only dry lupine may be moved, because moist lupine is too bitter even for animals.
