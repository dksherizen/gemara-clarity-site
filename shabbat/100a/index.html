Topic

Throwing on שבת: when an object counts as resting (on water, a wall, a hole, or after rolling) and what creates or cancels a רשות היחיד (pits, holes, mounds)

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a point: an object floating on water is not considered 'resting' (הנחה). רבא asks about a nut resting in a floating vessel, and the question stays unresolved (תיקו). The גמרא notes that oil floating on wine is already a מחלוקת between רבי יוחנן בן נורי and the רבנן. אביי then rules on pits in the רשות הרבים: a thrown mat that divides a pit cancels its walls, and water filling a pit does not cancel them but fruit does. A ברייתא of רבי שמעון about throwing into the sea supports him. A new משנה teaches that an object hitting a wall below ten טפחים counts as landing, and רבי יוחנן explains that this means a sticky fig-cake. Related teachings cover an object landing in a small hole (the מחלוקת about חוקקין להשלים) and a gradual mound that counts as a wall. A second משנה deals with thrown objects that roll after landing, and רבי יוחנן and a ברייתא require that the object actually rested for a moment. רבא adds that for the רבנן even an object within three טפחים of the ground must truly rest, and רבינא begins to question מרימר about this. His question continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לָאו הַיְינוּ הַנָּחָתָן.

that is not considered their placement.

בָּעֵי רָבָא:

Rava asked:

אֱגוֹז בִּכְלִי

a nut in a vessel

וּכְלִי צָף עַל גַּבֵּי מַיִם — מַהוּ?

and the vessel floats on water, what is the law?
