Topic

How the טומאה of עבודה זרה works: testing רבה's and רבי אלעזר's explanations against ברייתות, רב אשי's reading of היסט in both directions, and how an idol can move something.

Overview

On the previous עמוד, רבה and רבי אלעזר gave two explanations of רבי עקיבא's comparison of עבודה זרה to נדה. רבה said the dispute is only about אבן מסמא. רבי אלעזר said the dispute is about משא itself. The גמרא first asks what the comparison to נדה adds in רבי אלעזר's view, and answers that an idol is not מטמא through its separate limbs. It then explains that רב חמא בר גוריא's question about limbs followed רבה's reading. Next, two ברייתות seem to support רבי אלעזר against רבה. רבה defends himself, and both אמוראים reword the second ברייתא to fit their views. רב אשי then asks what the word 'הן' adds, and he reads the ברייתא as separating two directions of היסט: an impure thing moving others, and others moving it. Finally the גמרא asks how an idol could ever move something. רמי בריה דרב ייבא starts to answer with a משנה about a balance scale, and the answer continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

וְרַבִּי עֲקִיבָא לְמַאי הִלְכְתָא

And according to Rabbi Akiva, for what law

אִיתַּקַּשׁ לְנִדָּה?

was it compared to a menstruating woman?

— לְמַשָּׂא, לַוקְּשֵׁיהּ לִנְבֵלָה!

If for carrying, let him compare it to a carcass!

אִין, הָכִי נָמֵי.

Yes, it is indeed so.
