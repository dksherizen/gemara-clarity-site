Topic

Going out on שבת with perfume containers, followed by אגדות about the luxury, immorality, and punishment of שומרון and ירושלים.

Overview

The גמרא opens with a ברייתא in which רבי מאיר and רבי אליעזר disagree about a woman who goes out on שבת with a key, a כובלת, or a flask of פלייטון. It fixes a gap in the ברייתא and learns that an empty perfume flask makes her liable. רב אדא בר אהבה uses this to rule that carrying פחות מכשיעור of food in a vessel makes one liable, but רב אשי rejects the proof. Because perfume came up, the גמרא turns to the verses in עמוס about the luxuries of שומרון. It concludes that after the חורבן the רבנן banned only things that bring both pleasure and joy. Next, רבי אבהו explains the sin of the סְרֻחִים as wife-swapping, and three behaviors are listed that bring a person to poverty. The גמרא then explains the verses in ישעיה about the immodest בנות ציון and their punishments, each one matching the sin. It ends with רב's statement that the people of ירושלים were arrogant, and its example continues on the next amud.
