Topic

The traveler's pouch on שבת: חרש or קטן, the hidden method of carrying less than four אמות, the eighteen גזירות, putting the pouch on a donkey, and liability for מחמר

Overview

The amud continues the משנה about a traveler whose money pouch is with him when שבת begins. First the גמרא asks whether the pouch should go to a חרש or a קטן. According to רבי אליעזר it goes to the קטן, since a חרש may have some understanding. According to the רבנן the two are equal, and two traditions are recorded without a decision. If nobody at all is available, רבי יצחק reveals a method the חכמים kept hidden: carry it less than four אמות at a time. They hid it so people would not slip into carrying a full four אמות in a רשות הרבים. A ברייתא then records רבי אליעזר and רבי יהושע disagreeing over whether the eighteen גזירות strengthened the תורה or overloaded the people, each with a parable. Back to the משנה: placing the pouch on a donkey seems to be the forbidden act of מחמר. רב אדא בר אהבה answers that the pouch is put on and taken off only while the donkey walks. רב פפא then explains why a donkey is allowed but a fellow Jew is not. רב אדא בר אהבה also rules that a person may run home with a package already on his shoulder. Finally, רמי בר חמא says מחמר brings חטאת or סקילה, and רבא offers two refutations. The second refutation is pushed aside, while the first, a comparison to עבודה זרה, stands.

Opening lines

לֹא תֵּצֵא לְחוּלִּין,

it does not go out to non-sacred status,

מִפְּנֵי שֶׁהוּא סָפֵק.

because he is a doubtful case.

כִּי תִּיבְּעֵי לָךְ אַלִּיבָּא דְּרַבָּנַן,

When you raise this question, it is according to the Rabbis,

דִּתְנַן: חֲמִשָּׁה לֹא יִתְרוֹמוּ,

as we learned: five may not separate teruma,

  1. ProofEnd of the source: a חרש's תרומה stays in doubt
