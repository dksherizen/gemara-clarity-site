Topic

End of the נבוכדנצר אגדה, then the משנה on arranging weekday matters on שבת: hinting versus speaking, speech versus thought, and permitted talk about חפצי שמים and calculations.

Overview

This amud first finishes the אגדה about נבוכדנצר and בבל: two readings of a verse about בבל's greed for gold, and a teaching that נבוכדנצר even ruled over wild beasts. A new משנה then forbids hiring workers on שבת, asking someone else to hire them, or waiting at the edge of the תחום in order to hire workers or bring produce. Waiting there in order to guard produce is allowed. אבא שאול sums it up: whatever I may talk about on שבת, I may wait at the תחום for. The גמרא asks why the משנה mentions 'his friend' at all. After רב פפא's answer (the friend is a גוי) is rejected, רב אשי explains that the clause implies a hint like 'will you join me this evening?' is permitted. That is the view of רבי יהושע בן קרחה, and the הלכה follows him. רבי יוחנן derives it from the פסוק 'ודבר דבר': speaking is forbidden, but thinking is allowed. A side discussion squares this with the ban on thinking Torah in a bathroom, and with רב יהודה's rule against reciting קריאת שמע before a naked גוי. Finally the גמרא shows that talk about מצוה and community matters is permitted, because only 'your business' is forbidden, not חפצי שמים. It then begins to discuss which calculations are permitted on שבת, a discussion that continues on the next amud.
