Shabbos 47a

שבת מ״ז ע״א

Topic

Carrying a coal pan of ashes on שבת (from אביי's incense and גרף של רעי answers to רבא's answer about useful ashes), then reassembling a weaver's frame on שבת

Overview

This עמוד opens with the last line of an earlier discussion: a lamp that was burning when שבת began became a בסיס לדבר האסור, so it may not be moved. Then רבי רומנוס reports that רבי allowed him to carry a coal pan with its ashes. רבי זירא challenges this, because רבי יוחנן allows carrying a basket with a stone only when it also holds fruit. רבי אסי answers that the pan held bits of incense, but אביי rejects this: such bits mean nothing in רבי's wealthy home. אביי then compares the pan to a גרף של רעי, and רבא rejects that comparison for two reasons. רבא gives his own answer: the ashes are useful, so the pan may be carried for their sake, and broken wood on it does not matter. A challenge from wick fragments in a lamp is answered by אביי: that ruling reflects the גליל, where oil is cheap. Finally, a new topic begins about reassembling a weaver's frame on שבת. Two rabbis permit it, רב יהודה quotes רב and שמואל that it is liable to a חטאת, and the גמרא answers that the lenient rabbis follow a different תנא, whose ברייתא continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

הַנַּח לְנֵר שֶׁמֶן וּפְתִילָה,

Leave aside the lamp, the oil, and the wick,

הוֹאִיל דְּנַעֲשָׂה בָּסִיס לַדָּבָר הָאָסוּר.

since it became a base for a forbidden thing.

אָמַר רַבִּי זֵירָא אָמַר רַבִּי אַסִּי

Rabbi Zeira said that Rabbi Asi said

אָמַר רַבִּי יוֹחָנָן אָמַר רַבִּי חֲנִינָא

that Rabbi Yoḥanan said that Rabbi Ḥanina said

The steps (16)

  1. ConclusionThe burning lamp stays forbidden because it was a base for the flame
  2. Statementרבי יהודה הנשיא allowed carrying a coal pan with its ashes
  3. Challengeרבי זירא asks: רבי יוחנן allows the basket with a stone only because of the fruit
  4. ResolutionAfter a stunned silence, רבי אסי answers: the pan had bits of incense
  5. Challengeאביי objects: tiny bits are worthless in רבי's wealthy house
  6. RejectionThe idea that the bits are useful to the poor is rejected
  7. Resolutionאביי's own answer: the pan is like a chamber pot of waste
  8. Rejectionרבא rejects the chamber pot comparison
  9. Resolutionרבא: the pan may be moved because of the ashes
  10. ChallengeChallenge from wick fragments in a lamp
  11. Resolutionאביי: that ruling was taught in the גליל
  12. Questionלוי בר שמואל asks about reassembling a weaver's frame
  13. ResponseThey answer that it is permitted
  14. Rejectionרב יהודה: רב and שמואל say one is liable to a חטאת
  15. ChallengeChallenge from the ברייתא about reassembling objects
  16. ResolutionThe lenient rabbis follow a different תנא
