Topic

מוקצה of כלים: רבה's rereading of the early sources, the משנה of לצורך ושלא לצורך, and גזירה יום טוב אטו שבת

Overview

This amud first finishes the list of sources (the poles, the thick-headed bolt, the mortar) that seemed to show some מוקצה rules came from before התרת כלים. רבה then argues that each source has its own reason. The rods and poles were not needed, the bolt is about carrying without an עירוב, and the mortar follows רבי נחמיה. Next comes a new משנה: the תנא קמא lets כלים be moved 'לצורך ושלא לצורך,' but רבי נחמיה allows only 'לצורך.' רבה defines these terms by the kind of item and the reason for moving it, using צורך גופו and צורך מקומו. רבא rejects that and gives his own definition, in which 'שלא לצורך' means even moving an item from sun to shade. The bowls question against רבה is answered by comparing dirty bowls to a chamber pot. אביי's challenges to רבא from the mortar, the pestle, and the firewood are answered with sun-to-shade, a decree on יום טוב because of שבת, and the rule that only a real כלי can be moved. Finally, two משניות clash over whether we decree יום טוב because of שבת. רב יוסף's answer is rejected, and רב פפא starts a new answer from בית שמאי and בית הלל that continues on the next amud.
