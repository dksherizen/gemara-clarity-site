Topic

Splitting letters and נוטריקון in the מלאכה of writing, ידיעה for a חצי שיעור, and the start of the new chapter on the minimum amounts for weaving, sewing and tearing.

Overview

This amud opens with a quick קשיא and תירוץ. Someone who splits one letter into two is חייב only if the new letters do not still need their crowns (זיונין). Next comes the מחלוקת about writing one letter as a נוטריקון: רבי יהושע בן בתירה holds him חייב and the חכמים hold him פטור. The גמרא then gives a long list of נוטריקון readings to show that the Torah really uses abbreviations: אב המון, אנכי, ירט, כרמל, נמרצת and נצטדק. A new משנה follows about two letters written in two separate העלמות. The גמרא explains that רבן גמליאל and the חכמים argue over whether awareness (ידיעה) divides a חצי שיעור. After the הדרן for פרק הבונה, a new chapter opens with the minimum amounts for weaving, making loops, sewing and tearing. The גמרא resolves the dispute over 'two' or 'three' threads, quotes two ברייתות and identifies whose view each follows, and defines the loom terms. It then explains why the משנה repeats sewing and tearing: to lead into tearing in anger or for the dead. At the end it asks when one would tear in order to sew, and the answer comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְעָלוּ בְּיָדוֹ שְׁתַּיִם — חַיָּיב.

and he ended up with two letters — he is liable.

וְהָתְנַן פָּטוּר!

But didn't we learn: he is exempt!

לָא קַשְׁיָא:

It is not difficult:

הָא דְּבָעֵי זַיּוֹנֵי,

that one (exempt) is where they need crowns,
