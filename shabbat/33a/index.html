Topic

Sins and their punishments: the ברייתא from גזל through גלות and נבלות פה, הדרוקן as a sign of sin, and the four signs

Overview

This amud continues a long ברייתא that matches sins with the national punishments they bring, and it proves each punishment from פסוקים. It opens with the last proofs for גזל: locusts, famine, and, through an אל תקרי reading, people eating their own children. Corrupt judging and ביטול תורה bring sword, plague, and famine. False and empty oaths, חילול השם, and חילול שבת bring wild beasts and empty roads, and a גזירה שוה on the word חילול links them together. שפיכות דמים destroys the בית המקדש and drives away the שכינה. גילוי עריות, עבודה זרה, and neglecting שמיטה and יובל bring גלות. נבלות פה brings harsh troubles, and the אמוראים add that it reverses good decrees and deepens גיהנם, even for a silent listener. The גמרא then discusses הדרוקן as a sign of sin, shows that it has other causes too, and tells how שמואל הקטן, אביי, and רבא got it without sinning. The amud ends with four signs of four sins and the opening of a new ברייתא about אסכרה, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דְּאׇכְלָן וְלָא עָבְדָן.

who eat and do not work.

וּכְתִיב: ״הִכֵּיתִי אֶתְכֶם בַּשִּׁדָּפוֹן וּבַיֵּרָקוֹן

And it is written: 'I struck you with blight and mildew;'

הַרְבּוֹת גַּנּוֹתֵיכֶם וְכַרְמֵיכֶם

the multitude of your gardens and your vineyards,

וּתְאֵנֵיכֶם וְזֵיתֵיכֶם יֹאכַל הַגָּזָם״,

your fig trees and your olive trees, the gazam-locust devoured.'
