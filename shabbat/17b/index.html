Topic

The last of the eighteen decrees, and the new משנה on starting work before שבת that continues on שבת

Overview

The גמרא finishes listing the eighteen decrees (י״ח דבר) that the Sages made on one day. First, שמואל teaches that produce grown from planted תרומה is also תרומה. רבי חנינא says the worry was a ישראל cheating the כהן, but רבא rejects this and explains that the worry was a כהן holding on to impure תרומה. Next comes the traveler caught on the road when שבת begins, who must give his purse to a גוי. Then the גמרא adds the decrees on the bread, oil, wine, and daughters of non-Jews. It works out a chain in which each decree protects something more serious, ending with a decree that a non-Jewish boy carries the טומאה of a זב. This makes the count eighteen for both רבי יוסי and רבי מאיר. Finally, a new משנה begins: בית שמאי and בית הלל argue about starting work before שבת that will continue by itself on שבת. The amud ends in the middle of בית הלל's ruling, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

טָבִי רִישְׁבָּא אָמַר שְׁמוּאֵל:

Tavi the bird hunter said that Shmuel said:

אַף גִּידּוּלֵי תְרוּמָה תְּרוּמָה,

Also, the growths of terumah are terumah,

בּוֹ בַּיּוֹם גָּזְרוּ.

they decreed this on that day.

מַאי טַעְמָא?

What is the reason?
