Topic

אגדות about מתן תורה: משה and the angels, שטן's search for the תורה, the crowns on the letters, the sin of the עגל, and the meaning of the name סיני.

Overview

This amud continues an אגדה from the previous daf. The angels had objected to giving the תורה to humans, and משה shows them that every command in the עשרת הדברות fits human life, not angels. The angels agree, each one gives משה a gift, and even מלאך המות teaches him that קטורת stops a plague. Then רבי יהושע בן לוי tells three more stories. In the first, שטן searches all of creation for the תורה, is sent to משה, and משה humbly refuses credit, so the תורה is called by his name: תורת משה. In the second, משה finds God tying crowns on the letters and learns that he should wish his Master success. In the third, the word בשש is reread as באו שש, explaining how שטן tricked the people into believing משה had died, which led to the עגל. Finally, the גמרא asks why the mountain is called סיני. Two answers are rejected, and the accepted one is that hatred (שנאה) came down on the nations there. This is supported by רבי יוסי ברבי חנינא's list of the desert's five names, which continues on the next amud.
