Topic

גרם כיבוי and indirect action on שבת: candles near doors, the barrier of vessels, and ה׳'s name written on the skin

Overview

The גמרא first shows that an earlier lenient ruling follows רבי שמעון בן ננס, who permits גרם כיבוי. It proves this from רבי יוסי's dissent in the משנה. Next come two ברייתות about a candle that might go out. A candle on a board may be shaken off, but only if it was forgotten there. Otherwise the board is a בסיס לדבר האסור. רב cursed the ruling allowing a door to be opened with a candle behind it, because that is a פסיק רישיה, which even רבי שמעון forbids. רב יהודה and אביי argue about opening a door opposite a fire, and their argument turns on whether to make a גזירה. The גמרא then settles which side forbids גרם כיבוי by reading a ברייתא as missing words. It also resolves a contradiction from the case of ה׳'s name written on the skin: the reed there is about not standing undressed before the name, and the argument narrows to whether one must search for a reed. The explanation of the רבנן's view continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הוּא דְּאָמַר כְּרַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן בֶּן נַנָּס.

It was he who said it according to Rabbi Shimon ben Nannas.

אֵימַר דְּאָמַר רַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן בֶּן נַנָּס

Say that Rabbi Shimon ben Nannas said

״מִפְּנֵי שֶׁהוּא מְחָרֵךְ״,

'because it only singes,'

גְּרַם כִּיבּוּי מִי אָמַר?

did he say that indirectly causing extinguishing is permitted?
