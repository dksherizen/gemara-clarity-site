Topic

Trying to prove from other cases of טומאה that the impure loaf being discussed can disqualify תרומה. Each proposed source is pushed back with a מה ל- argument.

Overview

The גמרא is searching for a source that the impure loaf, which is food, can disqualify תרומה. It tries a טבול יום, but he comes from a type that can include an אב הטומאה. It then tries a כלי חרש, but it has the special power of airspace. Each source cancels the other's weak point, so the גמרא combines them through their common feature (הצד השוה) to prove the law by קל וחומר. The later generation rejects this because both sources have some strict side, while רבן יוחנן בן זכאי does not accept that objection. Next, a ברייתא brings רבי יוסי's קל וחומר from a מחוסר כיפורים that קודש has a fourth level of impurity, and the גמרא shows from ויקרא 7:19 that the third level comes from the תורה. רבי יוחנן challenges the קל וחומר with food touched by a טבול יום, based on a three-way ברייתא between אבא שאול, רבי מאיר and the חכמים. רב פפא asks whether רבי יוסי might hold like אבא שאול. The גמרא answers that if he did, he would have used an easier קל וחומר, and that argument continues on the next amud.
