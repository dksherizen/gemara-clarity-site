Topic

The עגלה ערופה: which blemishes and work disqualify it compared with the פרה אדומה, the measure of pulling a yoke, and the meaning behind the ceremony

Overview

The משנה finishes the עגלה ערופה ceremony. The כהנים pray for forgiveness, and the end of the פסוק is רוח הקודש promising that the blood is forgiven. The גמרא asks why a blemish does not disqualify the עגלה when it does disqualify the פרה אדומה. The answer is that the word בה limits the blemish rule to the פרה, while a גזירה שוה from the word עול carries the work rule from the עגלה to the פרה. This leads to a long side discussion about how we know that work does not disqualify קדשים. The גמרא then looks directly at רב's ruling that a load of sacks disqualifies the פרה, while the עגלה is disqualified only once it pulls. It explains that this depends on a dispute of תנאים about the source of the פרה's work rule. Then רבי יוחנן gives the measure of pulling: the width of a yoke, a טפח. רבי יוחנן בן שאול explains the symbolism of killing a barren heifer in a barren stream for a victim who lost his chance to do מצוות. Finally, the גמרא begins to prove that נחל איתן means a hard stream, and the proof continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְהַכֹּהֲנִים אוֹמְרִים:

And the priests say:

״כַּפֵּר לְעַמְּךָ יִשְׂרָאֵל

"Forgive Your people Israel,

אֲשֶׁר פָּדִיתָ ה׳

whom You have redeemed, ה׳,

וְאַל תִּתֵּן דָּם נָקִי

and do not place innocent blood
