Sotah 7a

סוטה ז׳ ע״א

Topic

Bringing the סוטה to be tested: the escort of תלמידי חכמים, רבי יהודה's view, and the court's efforts to make her confess

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier point: witnesses overseas are too rare to make people suspect innocent women. A new משנה then says the husband brings his wife to בית דין, which sends two תלמידי חכמים along so he is not with her on the way. רבי יהודה says the husband is trusted. The גמרא tries twice to prove rules of רב about יחוד from this משנה. Both proofs are turned aside: the two scholars are there to serve as witnesses, and they must be scholars because they know how to warn him. רבי יהודה's view rests on a קל וחומר from נדה. The רבנן turn it around, and the גמרא explains that after his קל וחומר was refuted he relied on a פסוק. A second משנה describes how the great בית דין in ירושלים presses the סוטה to confess. If she confesses, she loses her כתובה and is divorced. If she claims innocence, she is brought to שער ניקנור, where the כהן disgraces her by tearing her clothes and dressing her in black, and the משנה continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

יֵשׁ לָהּ עֵדִים בִּמְדִינַת הַיָּם!

There are witnesses for her in a country overseas!

לָא שְׁכִיחָא.

It is not common.

מַתְנִי׳

MISHNA:

כֵּיצַד עוֹשֶׂה לָהּ?

What does he do with her?
