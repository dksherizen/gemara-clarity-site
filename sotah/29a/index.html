Topic

The verses that forbid a סוטה, two sources for doubtful טומאה, and a קל וחומר for a third-level loaf.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the argument between רבי עקיבא and רבי ישמעאל about what the word ונטמאה forbids for a סוטה. The גמרא rejects one explanation, then says רבי עקיבא has four verses: husband, בועל, כהונה and תרומה. רבי ישמעאל has three verses and learns כהונה through a קל וחומר. The גמרא explains why רבי ישמעאל uses his verse for תרומה: like the husband and בועל, תרומה matters while the husband is alive. It then gives two ways to explain רבי עקיבא. Next, רב גידל in the name of רב brings a verse as a second source for doubtful טומאה. It teaches that something that can be asked is impure when in doubt, and something that cannot be asked is pure. The גמרא shows why both this verse and the lesson from סוטה are needed. Finally it turns to the משנה about a third-level loaf. רב explains רבן יוחנן בן זכאי's view with a קל וחומר from a טבול יום. That קל וחומר is refuted, and a new source for it begins at the end of the amud and continues on the next amud.
