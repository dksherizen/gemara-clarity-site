Sotah 42a

Topic

The seriousness of חנופה, and the opening of פרק משוח מלחמה: the war-priest's speech and who gives it

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a proof that ירמיהו was punished for flattering חנניה: חנניה's grandson is the one who arrested him. רבי אלעזר then teaches, from the word גלמוד, that a group with חנופה is repulsive like a נדה and will end up in exile. רבי ירמיה בר אבא lists four groups who will not greet the שכינה: scoffers, flatterers, liars, and those who speak לשון הרע. Each one is proven from a פסוק. Then פרק אלו נאמרין ends, and a new פרק opens with a משנה about the כהן משוח מלחמה, who speaks to the army in לשון הקודש. The משנה explains his speech line by line: real enemies show no mercy, do not fear the noises of battle, and ה׳ fights for you through the ארון. The גמרא explains the לשון הקודש rule with a גזירה שוה from מתן תורה. It then shows that the speaking כהן must be appointed and have a superior in his own job, which rules out the כהן גדול and the סגן. Finally, it explains 'שמע ישראל' as a hint to the merit of קריאת שמע, and begins a ברייתא saying the כהן speaks twice, at the border and at the battle.

Opening lines

״וַיְהִי הוּא בְּשַׁעַר בִּנְיָמִן

"And when he was at the Benjamin Gate,

וְשָׁם בַּעַל פְּקִדֻת

there was an officer of the guard there,

וּשְׁמוֹ יִרְאִיָּה בֶּן שֶׁלֶמְיָה בֶּן חֲנַנְיָה

whose name was Irijah son of Shelemiah son of Hananiah;

וַיִּתְפֹּשׂ אֶת יִרְמְיָהוּ הַנָּבִיא לֵאמֹר

and he seized Jeremiah the prophet, saying:
