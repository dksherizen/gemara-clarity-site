Sotah 7b

סוטה ז׳ ע״ב

Open Sotah 7b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The סוטה ceremony and the court's pressure on her, stories of יהודה and ראובן confessing, and whether the משנה proves that a receipt (שובר) is written

Overview

The משנה finishes describing how the כהן strips the סוטה of her jewelry and dignity, and who may and may not watch. The גמרא learns from the shared word 'תורה' that her case goes before the court of seventy-one. It then sets the משנה's 'they pressure her' against a ברייתא saying the court also urges an innocent woman to drink. The answer is that the משנה speaks of the time before the scroll is erased, and the ברייתא of the time after. A ברייתא then lists the stories told to the סוטה: יהודה and ראובן admitted their sins and were rewarded. This leads to רבי יוחנן's story of יהודה's bones and משה's prayers, and to the question of why ראובן admitted in public. The גמרא next tries to prove from the משנה that a receipt (שובר) is written; אביי and רבא each block the proof. The amud ends as the גמרא begins questioning the משנה's words 'they bring her up' to the Eastern Gate, a discussion that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְקַטְלֵיאוֹת, נְזָמִים וְטַבָּעוֹת —

or chokers, nose rings, and finger rings —

מַעֲבִירִין מִמֶּנָּה, כְּדֵי לְנַוְּולָהּ.

they remove them from her, in order to make her unattractive.

וְאַחַר כָּךְ מֵבִיא חֶבֶל מִצְרִי

And afterward he brings an Egyptian rope

וְקוֹשְׁרוֹ לְמַעְלָה מִדַּדֶּיהָ,

and ties it above her breasts.
