Topic

The סוטה scroll and ink, the order of her מנחה and drinking, why she is rushed out, and how long merit delays her punishment

Overview

The amud opens with two versions of רבי מאיר's rule about which ink must be erasable. One version covers the פרשת סוטה even in a ספר תורה; the other covers only the סוטה scroll of the מקדש. רבי ירמיה explains that they differ on whether the סוטה passage may be erased from a ספר תורה for her. The גמרא suggests this matches a ברייתא about reusing one סוטה's scroll for another, but רב פפא and רב נחמן בר יצחק each show the two disputes need not line up. The גמרא then defends רבי אחי בר יאשיה from the rule of a גט: for a סוטה, only the erasing must be done for her. Next, the גמרא shows the משנה follows רבי עקיבא, who has the מנחה offered before she drinks and agrees with the רבנן that merit delays punishment. It explains that she is rushed out not because she may die, since a corpse may enter מחנה לויה, but because sudden fright may make her a נדה. Finally, it shows that the משנה's years of delay fit none of the three תנאים in a ברייתא, who give three, nine and twelve months. The hint for twelve months continues on the next amud.
