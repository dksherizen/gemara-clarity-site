Sotah 14b

סוטה י״ד ע״ב

Open Sotah 14b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The סוטה's מנחה, and the step-by-step order of bringing a regular מנחה, with a source for each stage

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a proof about the סוטה. According to the view that she drinks before her מנחה is offered, the scroll is already erased, so tiring her out cannot be about saving the scroll. Next the גמרא quotes our משנה, which says every מנחה is in a כלי שרת from start to finish. It challenges this from a ברייתא in which the owner brings his מנחה from home in silver and gold baskets. רב פפא answers that the משנה means vessels fit to become a כלי שרת. The גמרא shows that even רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה, who allows wooden service vessels, would reject a cheap basket. The גמרא then goes through the ברייתא line by line and gives a source or reason for each stage: placing the מנחה in a כלי שרת, oil and לבונה, bringing it to the כהן and to the southwest corner of the מזבח, moving the לבונה aside, taking the קומץ from the oily spot, a second קידוש like blood, and gathering the לבונה. The amud ends in the middle of the ברייתא, as the כהן brings the קומץ up.

Opening lines

מַשְׁקָהּ, וְאַחַר כָּךְ

he gives her to drink, and afterward

מַקְרִיב אֶת מִנְחָתָהּ.

he offers her meal-offering.

דְּאִי מִשּׁוּם מְגִילָּה

So if it were because of the scroll,

הָא אִימְּחִיקָא לַהּ.

it has already been erased!

The steps (34)

  1. ProofThe scroll is already erased, so the reason must be different
  2. ChallengeA ברייתא seems to contradict the משנה about service vessels
  3. ChallengeThe ברייתא continues: taking the handful and burning it
  4. ChallengeThe ברייתא ends: what the כהנים may do with the leftovers
  5. ChallengeThe challenge: the ברייתא says silver and gold baskets
  6. Resolutionרב פפא: vessels fit to become service vessels
  7. ChallengeSo a cheap basket is not fit; whose view is that?
  8. ResolutionEven רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה rejects cheap vessels

  16. ProofThe ברייתא keeps both phrases by using the southwest corner
  17. Statementרבי אלעזר: the south side of the corner is enough
  18. QuestionHow does the south side keep 'לפני ה׳'?
  19. Responseרב אשי: this תנא holds the whole מזבח is in the north
  20. QuestionWhat does 'ודיו' add?
  21. Responseרב אשי: the מנחה does not need to touch the corner itself
  22. ChallengeWhy not say the מנחה itself must touch the corner?
  23. ResolutionA פסוק shows the מנחה is brought to the מזבח in a vessel
  24. StatementThe לבונה is moved to one side
  25. ProofA משנה shows a קומץ with לבונה in it is פסול
  26. StatementThe קומץ is taken from where the oil collected
  27. QuestionWhere do we learn this?
  28. ResponseTwo פסוקים mention flour together with oil
  29. StatementThe קומץ is put in a כלי שרת to make it holy
  30. ChallengeWhy make it holy again?
  31. ResolutionJust like blood, which is made holy twice
  32. StatementThe כהן gathers the frankincense and puts it on the handful
  33. ProofThe פסוק that teaches gathering all the frankincense
  34. StatementThe ברייתא's next step: he brings it up
