Sotah 41b

סוטה מ״א ע״ב

Open Sotah 41b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The king's Torah reading at הקהל, אגריפס and the people's flattery, and the גמרא's teachings on the sin of flattery

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier point: הקהל takes place at the start of the festival. Then the גמרא goes through the משנה's description of the king's Torah reading. The ספר תורה is passed up through officials to the king, and אביי explains that this whole chain exists to honor the king. The king may read sitting because the reading was in the עזרת נשים. The חכמים praised אגריפס for standing, and although a king may not give up his honor, standing for a מצוה is different. When אגריפס cried at the words 'you may not appoint a foreigner', the people told him 'you are our brother', and רבי נתן says Israel deserved destruction for that flattery. The גמרא then discusses flattery in general. Its terrible effects are set against a limited permission to flatter רשעים in this world, with a dispute about how to read יעקב's words to עשו. The amud ends with a series of רבי אלעזר's teachings on the punishments of a flatterer. The proof of the last teaching continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מֵאַתְחַלְתָּא דְּמוֹעֵד.

From the beginning of the festival.

וְנוֹתְנוֹ לְרֹאשׁ הַכְּנֶסֶת.
