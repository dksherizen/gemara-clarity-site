Sotah 40a

סוטה מ׳ ע״א

Open Sotah 40a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The congregation's responses during ברכת כהנים and מודים, stories of humility, and respect for the congregation

Overview

The גמרא asks what the people say while the כהנים bless them at נעילה on יום כיפור. The answer is three פסוקים from תהלים. The אמוראים then disagree about when to say them and how many to say each time. Some say these פסוקים should not be said at all, outside the מקדש or even inside it, because someone being blessed should listen. רבי אחא בר חנינא says they should be said everywhere. רבי אבהו stopped saying them after seeing רבי אבא דמן עכו, and this leads into stories about the humility of both scholars. Next the גמרא lists the words the people say during the שליח ציבור's מודים, and רב פפא rules that we say all of them together. Then the גמרא looks for sources that one must respect the congregation, and רב אשי rejects one of the proofs. The amud ends by quoting the next line of the משנה: in the מקדש, ברכת כהנים is one blessing.

The steps (29)

  1. QuestionWhat is said at נעילה of יום כיפור?

  9. Statementרבי אבהו stopped saying the פסוקים

  12. Responseרבי אבהו does not care who gets the credit
  13. Responseרבי אבהו gives up the head position for רבי אבא דמן עכו
  14. ResponseEveryone leaves רבי חייא בר אבא's class for רבי אבהו's
  15. StatementThe parable of the jewel seller and the cheap goods seller
  16. Statementרבי אבהו escorts רבי חייא בר אבא home
  17. QuestionWhat do the people say during מודים?
  18. Responseרב's wording
  19. Responseשמואל's wording

  23. StatementAlways respect the congregation, like the כהנים do

  25. QuestionWhy both 'my brothers' and 'my people'?
  26. ResponseBrothers if you listen, subjects if you don't
