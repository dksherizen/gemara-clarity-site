Topic

The names on the אבני אפוד, the refutation of רב כהנא, and how יוסף and יהודה sanctified God's name

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא showing that the tribes' names on the אבני אפוד were arranged as in ספר שמות and written in their birth form, such as ראובן and not ראובני. Since this does not match רב כהנא, he is refuted. The גמרא then reads 'והחציו' as teaching that the half facing הר גריזים was larger. That was even though לוי stood below, because the numerous בני יוסף were there, and a side story explains that עין הרע has no power over them. Next the גמרא asks how the names reach fifty letters. רבי יצחק's answer, the ה in יהוסף, is rejected, and the extra letter is found in the full spelling בנימין. רבי שמעון חסידא teaches that יוסף got a letter of God's name for sanctifying it in private, while יהודה, who did so in public, carries the whole Name. The גמרא tells how יוסף resisted אשת פוטיפר, what he gained and lost, and how בנימין named his sons after him. It then tells how the added letter helped יוסף learn seventy languages before פרעה. Finally it begins רבי מאיר's story of the tribes at the sea to show יהודה's public act, which continues on the next amud.
