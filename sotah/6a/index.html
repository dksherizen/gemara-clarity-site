Sotah 6a

סוטה ו׳ ע״א

Open Sotah 6a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Why a סוטה has no יבום, and רב ששת's rule that the bitter water does not test a woman with witnesses overseas

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the discussion of why a סוטה is not taken in יבום. A second version of רב יוסף reads the word "אחר" as a criticism of the man who marries her. רבא gives a קל וחומר instead: if the suspicion forbids her to her own husband, she is surely forbidden to his brother. אביי tests this קל וחומר with a כהן גדול's widow and then with a כהן's raped wife, and the גמרא shows why neither case fits. Next, a new משנה lists five suspected wives of a כהן who may not eat תרומה. רב ששת learns from it that the bitter water does not test a woman who has witnesses overseas. רב יוסף disagrees, saying her merit may have held off the punishment, and the גמרא ties their argument to רבי's idea of 'wasting away.' רב שימי בר אשי challenges רב ששת using רבי שמעון's worry about giving pure women a bad name, and the גמרא answers that רב ששת does not follow רבי שמעון. Finally, רב begins a new challenge from a משנה about women whose meal-offerings are burned, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מִי קָא רָמֵינַן לַהּ

Do we impose her

עֲלֵיהּ בְּעַל כֻּרְחֵיהּ?

on him against his will?

וְאִיכָּא דְּאָמְרִי, אָמַר רַב יוֹסֵף:

And there are those who say: Rav Yosef said:

הַכָּתוּב קְרָאוֹ ״אַחֵר״,

The verse calls him "another,"
