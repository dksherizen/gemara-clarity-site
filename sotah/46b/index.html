Sotah 46b

סוטה מ״ו ע״ב

Open Sotah 46b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The עגלה ערופה ceremony and the great importance of לויה, escorting travelers

Overview

This amud finishes explaining the משנה about the עגלה ערופה. First a ברייתא shows that איתן means 'hard', and אחרים say it means 'old'. The heifer is killed from behind, learned from the word עריפה by the bird חטאת העוף. רבי יאשיה and רבי יונתן argue whether the valley must never have been worked in the past. רבא says both agree it may not be worked from then on. A ברייתא teaches that only work on the ground itself is forbidden there, so combing flax and cutting stones are allowed. The זקנים wash over the spot where the neck was broken. Their declaration 'our hands did not spill this blood' means they did not send the traveler off without food or לויה. This opens a long section on לויה: its endless reward (the man of לוז, פרעה and אברהם), how far each person must escort, stories of אמוראים escorting their teachers, and רבי מאיר's warning that not escorting is like spilling blood. The proof is the story of אלישע and the youths, and the גמרא explains the youths' taunt, the words 'young lads', and what אלישע 'saw', ending with the two bears.

Opening lines

״אֵיתָן מוֹשָׁבֶךָ וְשִׂים בַּסֶּלַע קִנֶּךָ״,

"Firm is your dwelling-place, and your nest is set in the rock"

וְאוֹמֵר:

and it says:

״שִׁמְעוּ הָרִים אֶת רִיב ה׳

"Hear, O mountains, the Lord's controversy,

וְהָאֵיתָנִים מֹסְדֵי אָרֶץ״.

and the enduring rocks, the foundations of the earth"
