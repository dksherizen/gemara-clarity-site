Topic

Who the waters check besides the סוטה, how רבי עקיבא and רבי learn it from the פסוקים, and the lessons of the repeated "נטמאה", ending with a קל וחומר from סוטה to ספק טומאה.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the question of who "אוֹתוֹ" (him) is in the משנה. It cannot be the בעל, since the waters do not work at all if the husband has sinned. So it must be the בועל, and the different wording in the סיפא is just style. Next the גמרא asks how רבי עקיבא's proof "ובאו ובאו" reads, and proves from רבי's opposing view that רבי עקיבא learns from the extra letter ו. So for רבי עקיבא there are six lessons, three for the woman and three for the man, while רבי has only three. רבי gets the rule that the waters check the man from "לצבות בטן ולנפל ירך". רבי עקיבא uses that same phrase to teach that the כהן tells her the belly is struck before the thigh. Finally, a ברייתא counts three mentions of "נטמאה": for the husband, the paramour, and תרומה. רבי ישמעאל forbids her to a כהן by a קל וחומר. The ברייתא then learns that a ספק סוטה is forbidden and starts a קל וחומר from סוטה to ספק טומאת שרץ, which finishes on the next amud.

Opening lines

דְּאִי אִית בֵּיהּ עָוֹן

that if he has an iniquity

בָּדְקִי לֵיהּ מַיָּא,

the water checks him,

כִּי אִית בֵּיהּ עָוֹן בְּדִידֵיהּ

when he himself has an iniquity,

מִי בָּדְקִי לַהּ מַיָּא לְדִידַהּ?

does the water check her at all?
