Sotah 22a

סוטה כ״ב ע״א

Topic

Learning without understanding: the רשע ערום, the עם הארץ, and the משנה's list of מבלי עולם

Overview

The amud opens with the last definition of the משנה's רשע ערום: someone who read מקרא and learned משנה but never served תלמידי חכמים. אמוראים argue over what to call such a person: עם הארץ, בור, כותי, or מגוש. רב נחמן בר יצחק backs the מגוש view with a popular saying. A ברייתא then lists definitions of an עם הארץ, ending with the view that even one who learned without serving scholars is one. The גמרא explains 'שונים' in משלי and the ברייתא calling the תנאים מבלי עולם. Both refer to people who repeat הלכות or rule from their משנה without understanding. Returning to the משנה, a ברייתא lists a praying maiden, a visiting widow, and a premature baby as מבלי עולם. The גמרא limits the maiden to fake piety like יוחני בת רטיבי. It explains the premature baby either as a scholar who scorns his teachers or as one who rules before he is ready. רב's reading of a פסוק warns against both ruling too early and refusing to rule when qualified.

Opening lines

שֶׁקָּרָא וְשָׁנָה

who read and learned

וְלֹא שִׁימֵּשׁ תַּלְמִידֵי חֲכָמִים.

but did not serve Torah scholars.

אִתְּמַר:

It was stated:

קָרָא וְשָׁנָה

One who read and learned
