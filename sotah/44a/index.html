Sotah 44a

סוטה מ״ד ע״א

Open Sotah 44a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

How far a corpse's טומאה spreads, and who is sent home from war or kept from fleeing

Overview

The amud opens with the rule that a מת 'takes up' four אמות for טומאה. It supports this with a משנה about a חצר הקבר, where בית שמאי and בית הלל argue about its size. The גמרא flips that משנה's split between an entrance from above and an entrance from the side. It then limits the smaller sizes to a courtyard with clear walls. Next, the גמרא explains the war exemptions in דברים 20. A man who betrothed a woman goes home, and this includes a יבם whose יבמה is waiting for him, but not a man in a forbidden marriage. רבה's rule shows that even רבי יוסי הגלילי can agree with this. A ברייתא learns from the order of the פסוקים to build a house, plant a vineyard and only then marry, and it gives several readings of a פסוק in משלי about the order of learning and good deeds. The גמרא then explains which builders do not go home, and that a new husband stays home completely and may not even do support jobs. A new משנה brings רבי עקיבא, רבי יוסי הגלילי and רבי יוסי on who counts as 'הירא ורך הלבב'. It then describes guards with iron rods who stop soldiers from fleeing, and this משנה continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מֵת תּוֹפֵס אַרְבַּע אַמּוֹת לְטוּמְאָה.

A corpse takes up four cubits with regard to impurity.

וְתַנָּא תּוּנָא:

And our tanna also taught:

חֲצַר הַקֶּבֶר, הָעוֹמֵד בְּתוֹכָהּ — טָהוֹר,

A courtyard of a grave: one who stands inside it is pure,

וְהוּא שֶׁיְּהוּ בָּהּ אַרְבַּע אַמּוֹת,

provided that it has four cubits in it,
