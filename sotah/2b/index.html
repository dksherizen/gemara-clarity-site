Sotah 2b

סוטה ב׳ ע״ב

Topic

How many witnesses a סוטה case needs for the defilement, the warning and the seclusion, and the meaning of קינוי

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא that learns a rule from דברים: when the Torah says עד without a number, it means two witnesses. So the סוטה verse 'ועד אין בה' means there were not two witnesses, only one, and that one witness is enough to forbid her. The גמרא asks why this rule was needed and works through several possible misreadings of the verse. Next it explains the dispute in the משנה. רבי יהושע needs two witnesses for both the warning (קינוי) and the seclusion (סתירה). רבי אליעזר needs two only for the warning, because seclusion is the start of טומאה. A ברייתא then brings רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה's reverse report of רבי אליעזר: the warning works on one witness or the husband's own word, while the seclusion needs two. The חכמים object that this has 'no end,' since a husband could falsely claim he warned her, and רבי יוחנן's comment on this objection is reworded. רב חנינא מסורא rules that today a husband should not warn his wife, since there is no bitter water to clear her. The amud ends with ריש לקיש and אביי giving two meanings of the word קינוי, which reflect whether the warning needs witnesses.

מִמַּשְׁמַע שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר

From the implication of what is stated:

״לֹא יָקוּם עֵד בְּאִישׁ״,

"A witness shall not rise up against a man,"

אֵינִי יוֹדֵעַ שֶׁהוּא אֶחָד?

do I not know that it means one?

מָה תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר ״אֶחָד״?

What does the verse teach by saying "one"?

The steps (41)

  1. QuestionWhy does the verse add the word 'one'?

  4. ChallengeWhy was the rule needed? With no witness at all, how could she be forbidden?
  5. ResolutionWithout the rule, we might think one witness is not believed about her
  6. ChallengeIf two witnesses are needed, the verse would be unnecessary
  7. ResolutionA סוטה might have been believed with one witness
  8. ChallengeThe verse's own words show she is forbidden
  9. ResolutionThe rule was still needed, because the reading could survive the phrase 'she was not seized'
  10. QuestionWhat is the source for רבי יהושע's view?
  11. Responseרבי יהושע learns from 'בה': not for warning, not for seclusion

  16. ResolutionThe word 'בה' must exclude at least one of them

  24. ChallengeThe Sages object: there would be no end to it
