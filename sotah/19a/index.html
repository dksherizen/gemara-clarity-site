Sotah 19a

סוטה י״ט ע״א

Learn Sotah 19a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Sotah 19a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Whether a סוטה drinks the water a second time (from ״זֹאת״ and ״תּוֹרַת״), then how her מנחה is waved and whether it is offered before or after she drinks.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a dispute in a ברייתא: can a סוטה be made to drink the bitter water a second time? Everyone agrees on two cases. With one husband and one בועל she does not drink again (״זֹאת״). With two husbands and two בועלים she does (״תּוֹרַת״). The תנא קמא, the רבנן בתראי and רבי יהודה each read these two words differently for the two middle cases. רבי יהודה makes it depend only on the number of husbands. Then the chapter היה מביא ends, and a new משנה describes how the מנחת סוטה is waved by the כהן and the woman together, and argues about whether the drinking or the מנחה comes first. The גמרא learns that the owner takes part in the waving from a גזירה שוה of 'יד' 'יד' from שלמים. It then explains that the משנה's list of steps is just the general order of any מנחה. The real timing question is a dispute between the רבנן and רבי שמעון. Finally it quotes the ruling that if she drank first, the מנחה is still valid, and the discussion of that line continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דִּכְתִיב: ״זֹאת״.

as it is written: "This".

בִּשְׁנֵי אֲנָשִׁים וּשְׁנֵי בּוֹעֲלִין —

With two husbands and two paramours —

דְּכוּלֵּי עָלְמָא לָא פְּלִיגִי

everyone does not disagree (everyone agrees)

דְּהָאִשָּׁה שׁוֹתָה וְשׁוֹנָה,

that the woman drinks and drinks again,
