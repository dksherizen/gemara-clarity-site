Topic

רבי יהושע בן לוי's teachings on ברכת כהנים and stinginess, and who is included in ברכת כהנים

Overview

The amud opens by ending an earlier argument: the הלכה follows אביי and not רב חסדא about calling out 'כהנים'. Next comes a סימן, a list of keywords for a series of teachings by רבי יהושע בן לוי. He teaches that ה׳ desires ברכת כהנים, that a כהן who blesses is blessed, and that a כהן who skips the blessing breaks three מצוות עשה. רב adds that a כהן who never goes up may be unfit, and the גמרא shows the two views fit together. A כהן must start moving toward the דוכן during the ברכה of עבודה, and even a slight movement counts. Then come teachings about generous and stingy people, ending with the claim that the עגלה ערופה is brought because of stingy people who send travelers off without food or an escort. Finally, the גמרא discusses a בית הכנסת made up entirely of כהנים, who is included in the blessing, and whether barriers or standing at the sides matter. That last question is answered from a משנה in מסכת פרה, and the proof continues on the next amud.
