Topic

The miracles and events of the day ישראל crossed the ירדן, the hornet, and the split of the tribes on the mountains and the אפוד stones

Overview

The amud opens by naming the author of the earlier ברייתא: it follows רבי שמעון. Then it describes the miracles on the day ישראל crossed the ירדן. They traveled more than sixty מיל to הר גריזים and הר עיבל, and no enemy could stand against them. A פסוק in שירת הים hints at two entries into the land. The גמרא concludes that ישראל deserved miracles the second time too, but sin prevented them. Next comes the rest of that day: building the מזבח, writing the תורה on the stones in seventy languages, the offerings, the blessings and curses, and carrying the stones to גלגל. A ברייתא says the hornet did not cross the ירדן. ריש לקיש and רב פפא each answer the challenge from a פסוק. Finally the גמרא returns to the משנה. רב כהנא says the tribes were split on the mountains the same way as on the stones of the אפוד, and a ברייתא about those stones is brought to challenge him; it continues on the next amud.
