Topic

The crossing of the ירדן: how high the water piled up, what יהושע told the people in the river, the memorial stones, and the weight of the מרגלים' grape cluster

Overview

The גמרא describes the crossing of the ירדן. The moment the feet of the כהנים touched the water, the river stopped and piled up. רבי יהודה says the heap was 12 מיל, the size of the camp of ישראל. רבי אלעזר ברבי שמעון objects that water moves faster than people and would have flooded them, so he says it piled up more than 300 מיל, high enough for kings everywhere to see. He supports this from פסוקים about the kings' terror and from רחב's words. Next comes a series of things יהושע said while the people stood in the river: a warning that the water could return and sweep away 'me and you,' and instructions about memorial stones. A report that each stone weighed about forty סאה, combined with a tradition about carrying, lets the גמרא work out the weight of the מרגלים' grape cluster. רבי יצחק adds that eight spies carried it, and that יהושע and כלב carried nothing. The amud ends as רבי אמי and רבי יצחק נפחא begin to disagree about the water dispute, and their argument continues on the next amud.
