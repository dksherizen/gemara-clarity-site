Sotah 39b

סוטה ל״ט ע״ב

Open Sotah 39b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Rules of order and respect for ברכת כהנים, the Torah reading and the הפטרה, and the פסוקים the people say during ברכת כהנים.

Overview

This amud finishes the short prayer the כהנים say after ברכת כהנים, ending with the פסוק 'השקיפה'. רב חסדא, partly through רבי זירא, then gives timing rules. The כהנים keep their fingers spread until they turn away. The caller, the כהנים and the ציבור each wait for the one before to finish, and the כהנים turn and leave only around שים שלום. The same waiting order applies to the ברכה, the אמן, each פסוק and the תרגום at the Torah reading. Next, רבי תנחום in the name of רבי יהושע בן לוי gives rules about the הפטרה, uncovering the ark, and when the ציבור may leave after the reading. שמואל's view on leaving seems to differ, but the גמרא shows the two rulings fit shuls with and without a second exit. רבא gives the source from 'אחרי ה׳ אלהיכם תלכו'. Finally, the גמרא lists the פסוקים the people say during ברכת כהנים on a regular day, at מוסף of שבת, and at מנחה of a תענית.
