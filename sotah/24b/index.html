Topic

Why does the תורה need a special verse to exclude an ארוסה (betrothed woman) and a שומרת יבם from the סוטה waters, when a ברייתא already seems to exclude them?

Overview

The amud opens with the last item of a list from the earlier ברייתא: the court gives the warning on behalf of a husband who is a שעמום. The גמרא then asks what רבי יאשיה learns from ״תחת אישה״, which רבי יונתן uses to exclude a שומרת יבם. The answer is that רבי יאשיה uses it for a comparison (היקש) between man and woman. Next the גמרא asks why verses are needed at all, since a ברייתא brought by רבי אחא בר חנינא teaches from ״מבלעדי אישך״ that a woman is a סוטה only if her husband was with her before the other man. רמי בר חמא answers that the verse covers a case where the ארוס was with her in her father's house. Applying this to a שומרת יבם runs into רב's ruling that a יבם who was with her makes her his full wife. The גמרא answers that רבי יונתן holds like שמואל, who says the יבם acquires her only for the matters in the תורה's section. Finally, the גמרא shows that רב can explain his view even according to רבי יונתן: the need for a פסוק to exclude her proves she is otherwise a full wife.
