Sotah 16a

סוטה ט״ז ע״א

Learn Sotah 16a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Sotah 16a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The source of the סוטה dust, whether ashes may replace it, and רבי ישמעאל's list of places where the הלכה goes beyond the פסוק

Overview

The גמרא finishes איסי בן יהודה's list of places where the משכן stood, showing that the סוטה dust is taken from the Sanctuary floor wherever it is. איסי בן מנחם says no פסוק is needed for this, since a קל וחומר teaches it. He reads 'בקרקע המשכן' as teaching that the dust must come from the floor itself, not from a basket. The גמרא then asks whether ashes may be used if there is no dust. It explains that this question only arises according to בית הלל. To answer it, the גמרא brings רבי ישמעאל's list of three places where the הלכה goes beyond the פסוק: dust for covering blood, a נזיר's razor, and the scroll of a גט. Since סוטה is not on the list, it seems ashes are not allowed. The גמרא answers that the list is not complete and points to the מצורע, who shaves his whole body even though the פסוק, read as a כלל ופרט וכלל, seems narrower. רב נחמן בר יצחק, רב פפא and רב אשי then each explain why רבי ישמעאל did not count the מצורע.

Opening lines

שִׁילֹה נוֹב וְגִבְעוֹן

Shiloh, Nob, and Gibeon,
