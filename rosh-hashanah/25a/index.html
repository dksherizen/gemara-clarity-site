Rosh Hashanah 25a

ראש השנה כ״ה ע״א

Topic

קידוש החודש by בית דין: רבן גמליאל's acceptance of doubtful witnesses, the clash with רבי יהושע, and the binding authority of every generation's court

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה about witnesses for קידוש החודש. In two cases רבן גמליאל accepts witnesses whom other חכמים call עדי שקר. In the second case רבי יהושע sides with רבי דוסא בן הורכינס, so רבן גמליאל orders him to come with his staff and money on the day רבי יהושע counts as יום הכפורים. רבי עקיבא and רבי דוסא convince him that the בית דין's dates stand, and רבן גמליאל greets him as 'my teacher and my student'. The גמרא then explains רבן גמליאל's family tradition that the moon's path is sometimes long and sometimes short. It also tells stories about the moon's timing: רבי חייא and the old moon, and רבן גמליאל blocking a ראש חודש declared on the 29th. Finally it expands on רבי עקיבא's teaching that אֹתָם can be read as אַתֶּם, and on why the seventy elders are not named: every generation's בית דין carries full authority.

Opening lines

וְעַרְבִית בַּמַּעֲרָב.

and in the evening in the west.

אָמַר רַבִּי יוֹחָנָן בֶּן נוּרִי:

Rabbi Yochanan ben Nuri said:

עֵדֵי שֶׁקֶר הֵם.
