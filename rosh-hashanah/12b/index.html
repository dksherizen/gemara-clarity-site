Topic

The third-year tithes, the new year for vows, and the source for tying crops to a year once they reach one-third of their growth.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a ברייתא: produce from two different years may not be tithed one for the other. In year two of the שמיטה cycle you give מעשר ראשון and מעשר שני, and in year three you give מעשר ראשון and מעשר עני. רבי יהושע בן לוי learns from 'שנת המעשר' that only one of the two earlier tithes, מעשר ראשון, continues in year three, so מעשר שני drops out. A הקש to נחלה shows that מעשר ראשון never stops. A second ברייתא brings two different sources for מעשר ראשון: רבי יהודה learns it from 'ובא הלוי', and רבי אליעזר בן יעקב learns it from נחלה. Next, the גמרא turns to vows. A vow 'for a year' lasts twelve months, but a vow 'for this year' ends at תשרי, because vows follow how people normally speak. Finally, a משנה says grain and olives belong to the year in which they reached one-third of their growth. רב אסי learns this from the פסוק linking שנת השמטה with סוכות. רבי זירא begins to respond, and his words continue on the next amud.

one may not separate תרומה and tithes from this batch for that one,

because one may not separate תרומה and tithes

neither from the new produce for the old,
