Rosh Hashanah 14a

ראש השנה י״ד ע״א

Topic

Why שמואל needed all three statements; the year for tithing vegetables; and why the new year for trees falls in שבט

Overview

First the גמרא explains why שמואל needed all three of his statements about tithing cowpeas: הלכה כרבי שמעון שזורי, לכל אין בילה, and הכל הולך אחר גמר פרי. Each statement alone could be misunderstood, and another statement prevents that mistake. The last statement is limited to the beans in רבי שמעון שזורי's dispute, not grain and olives. The גמרא then asks why לכל אין בילה is needed at all. It answers that it teaches that wine and oil do blend. Next, a ברייתא brings רבי יוסי הגלילי and רבי עקיבא, who learn from the same פסוק which year vegetables are tithed for, each using a different test. רבי אבהו shows that their practical difference is seedless onions and cowpeas whose water was held back before ראש השנה. Finally the גמרא explains that the first of שבט is the new year for trees because most of the year's rain has fallen by then, even though most of the winter is still ahead. It then begins a story about רבי עקיבא's אתרוג that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וּצְרִיכָא: דְּאִי אַשְׁמְעִינַן

And it is necessary: for if he had taught us only

הֲלָכָה כְּרַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן שֵׁזוּרִי,

that the halacha follows Rabbi Shimon Shezuri,

הֲוָה אָמֵינָא: מִשּׁוּם דְּקָסָבַר יֵשׁ בִּילָּה,

I would have said: it is because he holds that there is mixing,

קָא מַשְׁמַע לַן: לַכֹּל אֵין בִּילָּה.

so he teaches us: for everything there is no mixing.
