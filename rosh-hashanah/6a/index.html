Topic

The laws of בל תאחר: whom the sin affects, the פסוק "מוצא שפתיך", צדקה, and delay past one רגל

Overview

The גמרא continues explaining the words "וְהָיָה בְךָ חֵטְא" about a person who delays paying a נדר. It rejects the idea that these words teach that a late קרבן is still valid, because בן עזאי already learns that from the word "אותו". Instead, the words teach that the sin falls on the man himself, and his wife does not die for it. Next, a ברייתא breaks the פסוק "מוצא שפתיך" into separate laws: an עשה, a לא תעשה, a warning to בית דין, and a list of what is included, from קרבנות to בדק הבית to צדקה. The גמרא asks why these laws are needed when other פסוקים already teach them. It answers that one set covers a person who only spoke his vow and the other covers a person who set aside an animal, and it explains why both are needed. רבא then teaches that pledged צדקה must be given right away when poor people are available, and that skipping even one רגל without bringing a pledged קרבן breaks an עשה. The גמרא challenges this rule from רבי פפייס, whose family held a שלמים calf past שבועות. רב זביד begins רבא's answer, which continues on the next amud.
