Topic

The מקדש שופר and trumpets, gold versus silver plating, the יובל and ראש השנה ברכות, and the start of the משנה on a cracked שופר

Overview

The גמרא explains several details from the משנה about the שופר used in the בית המקדש. First, it shows that the gold on the ראש השנה שופר was near the mouthpiece but not where the lips touch, since gold there makes a שופר פסול. Next it asks how the שופר could be heard over the two trumpets blown with it. It first suggests that the long שופר blast was heard alone at the end, but drops this after a challenge from a שופר blown into a pit. Its final answer is that two sounds from two different people can be heard, especially a sound people love, like שופר, הלל or מגילה. The long blast is only a sign that the שופר is the main מצוה of the day. The fast-day שופר is silver, either because gathering instruments are silver or to save money, while ראש השנה gets gold to honor the יום טוב. רבא, a ברייתא and a פסוק teach that blowing שופר and trumpets together was done only in the מקדש. The גמרא then discusses whether the יובל can share ראש השנה's prayer line about creation, and a new משנה opens: a cracked or glued-together שופר is פסול.
