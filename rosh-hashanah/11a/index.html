Rosh Hashanah 11a

ראש השנה י״א ע״א

Learn Rosh Hashanah 11a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Rosh Hashanah 11a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The מחלוקת of רבי אליעזר and רבי יהושע over תשרי or ניסן as the month of creation, the אבות and the גאולה, and the sources for events tied to פסח and ראש השנה

Overview

This amud finishes רבי אליעזר's list of big events tied to תשרי. It then gives רבי יהושע's list, which moves creation, the birth and death of the אבות, and the future גאולה to ניסן. A ברייתא brings each side's proof that the world was created in their month, from the פסוקים of creation and from the season. Each side then explains away the other's פסוק. Next they argue over when the אבות were born, from 'ירח האיתנים' or 'חודש זיו'. This brings in רב יהודה's ברכה on blossoming trees. Both agree the אבות died in their birth month, because ה׳ completes the years of צדיקים exactly. The גמרא then proves that יצחק was born on פסח, working through pregnancy lengths and a leap year. It shows from two גזרות שוות that שרה, רחל and חנה were remembered on ראש השנה, and it begins the source that יוסף left prison on ראש השנה, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בְּרֹאשׁ הַשָּׁנָה בָּטְלָה עֲבוֹדָה

On Rosh HaShana the slave labor stopped

מֵאֲבוֹתֵינוּ בְּמִצְרַיִם,

for our forefathers in Egypt;

בְּנִיסָן נִגְאֲלוּ,

in Nisan they were redeemed;

בְּתִשְׁרִי עֲתִידִין לִיגָּאֵל.

in Tishrei they will be redeemed in the future.
