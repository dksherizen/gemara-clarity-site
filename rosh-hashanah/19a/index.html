Topic

Why מגילת תענית listed the third of תשרי, which holidays need reinforcement days, and whether מגילת תענית was cancelled after the חורבן

Overview

The גמרא asks why מגילת תענית had to list the third of תשרי as a day without fasting. In the time of the בית המקדש, the day גדליה בן אחיקם was killed was already a happy day. רב answers that the listing was needed to forbid fasting on the day before, the second of תשרי. The גמרא asks whether that day is already covered because it comes after ראש חודש, or because it comes before the day of גדליה. The answer to both is no. A ברייתא teaches that Torah-level days do not need 'reinforcement' days around them, and רב אשי adds that דברי קבלה count like Torah law. Then רב טובי בר מתנה challenges the view that מגילת תענית was cancelled after the חורבן. He points out that a new festive day, the 28th of אדר, was added to it after a Roman decree was overturned. The גמרא shows this happened after the חורבן, because יהודה בן שמוע, who led the protest, was a student of רבי מאיר. It begins proving this from a משנה about glass vessels, and that proof continues on the next amud.
