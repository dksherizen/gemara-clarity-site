Rosh Hashanah 13b

ראש השנה י״ג ע״ב

Learn Rosh Hashanah 13b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Rosh Hashanah 13b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Which point in a crop's growth decides its year for מעשרות and שביעית. The גמרא finishes the source for the 'one-third' (שליש) rule, then asks how the rule applies to rice, millet, poppy and sesame.

Overview

The גמרא finishes learning the שליש rule for grain by re-reading לשלש ("for three") as לשליש ("for one-third"). It defends this reading by showing that another פסוק already teaches the plain three-year blessing. Next it quotes a משנה: rice, millet, poppy and sesame go by the year in which they took root. רבה lists the usual rules: trees by חנטה, grain and olives by שליש, vegetables by לקיטה. He asks which group these four crops belong to. He answers that they ripen little by little, so the חכמים used rooting to keep each field in one year. אביי objects with a ברייתא about the Egyptian bean: there, piling the crop together and separating from the mix solves the mixed-years problem. רבה replies that this works only for רבי שמעון שזורי, who holds יש בילה. The רבנן hold אין בילה. Finally, שמואל rules like that ברייתא. רבי זירא asks how, since שמואל says only liquids mix. The answer is that שמואל holds a crop's year follows גמר פרי, so all the beans belong to one year anyway.

Opening lines

אַל תִּקְרֵי ״לִשְׁלֹשׁ״,
