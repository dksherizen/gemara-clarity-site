Topic

The songs of the לויים in the בית המקדש (weekdays, שבת מוסף and מנחה), and the ten journeys of the שכינה and the exiles of the סנהדרין

Overview

The amud opens by rejecting a proof from the previous amud about which song the לויים sang when they were unsure whether it was ראש השנה. Then a ברייתא of רבי עקיבא lists the שיר של יום for each day of the week. Each song is tied to what ה׳ created on that day, and the שבת song is tied to a future 'day that is all שבת.' רבי נחמיה disagrees and explains the שבת song as referring to the first שבת, when ה׳ rested. The גמרא connects their argument to a disagreement between רב קטינא and אביי over how long the world will be destroyed. Next it gives the songs of שבת מוסף (האזינו in six sections, הזיו לך) and שבת מנחה (שירת הים and שירת הבאר), and proves that one section was sung each שבת. Finally, רבי יוחנן teaches that the שכינה left the מקדש in ten stages, each proven from a פסוק, and that it waited six months in the wilderness for תשובה. The matching ten exiles of the סנהדרין begin here and continue on the next amud.
