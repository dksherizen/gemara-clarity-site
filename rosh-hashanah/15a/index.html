Rosh Hashanah 15a

ראש השנה ט״ו ע״א

Learn Rosh Hashanah 15a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Rosh Hashanah 15a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The new year of the אתרוג (שבט, by the calendar, even in a leap year), and how an אתרוג crossing into or out of שביעית is treated for מעשר and ביעור.

Overview

The גמרא shows that רבה בר רב הונא's earlier statement cannot be right, because a ברייתא that follows רבן גמליאל's 'picking' rule still uses שבט. So his statement is corrected: even for רבן גמליאל, the אתרוג's new year is שבט. The גמרא then explains why the אתרוג ברייתא uses the example of the third year going into the fourth. An אתרוג tree handled by everyone in שביעית gives no fruit for three years. רבי ינאי teaches that the אתרוג's new year is the calendar month of שבט, and that it stays there even in a leap year. רבה and רב המנונא disagree about an אתרוג that crosses into or out of שביעית, and they treat מעשר and ביעור differently. A ברייתא of רבי שמעון challenges them both. The גמרא answers that the matter is a מחלוקת תנאים, and it brings the testimony of the five Elders and the vote at אושא. The amud ends by asking why the אושא ruling mentions שביעית at all, and the answer comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַאן שָׁמְעַתְּ לֵיהּ דְּאָזֵיל בָּתַר לְקִיטָה —

Whom have you heard who goes after the picking?

רַבָּן גַּמְלִיאֵל, וְקָתָנֵי שְׁבָט!

Rabban Gamliel. And yet it teaches Shevat!

אֶלָּא, אִי אִתְּמַר הָכִי אִתְּמַר:

Rather, if it was said, this is how it was said:

אָמַר רַבָּה בַּר רַב הוּנָא:

Rabba bar Rav Huna said:
