Rosh Hashanah 8a

ראש השנה ח׳ ע״א

Learn Rosh Hashanah 8a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Rosh Hashanah 8a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Why the משנה lists four ראשי השנים, the source of the dispute over the New Year for מעשר בהמה, and what 'the first of תשרי is ראש השנה for years' means: documents, the תקופה, or judgment

Overview

This amud first finishes the question of why the משנה counts only four ראשי השנים. The גמרא defends the rule about יובל by saying the משנה follows רבי ישמעאל בנו של רבי יוחנן בן ברוקה. רב אשי then gives another answer: the משנה lists the New Years that fall on a ראש חודש, with the first of שבט being part of the dispute between בית שמאי and בית הלל. Next, רבי יוחנן says רבי מאיר (אלול) and רבי אלעזר and רבי שמעון (תשרי) argue about מעשר בהמה based on one פסוק in תהלים about when sheep conceive. The order of that פסוק fits only רבי מאיר, so רבא moves the argument to ״עַשֵּׂר תְּעַשֵּׂר״, a היקש between מעשר בהמה and מעשר דגן that each side reads differently. The גמרא then asks what 'the first of תשרי is ראש השנה for years' is for. Three answers are given: dating documents (רב פפא, with ניסן for Jewish kings and תשרי for non-Jewish kings), the solar cycles (רבי זירא), and the day of judgment (רב נחמן בר יצחק). The amud ends as the גמרא begins proving from the שופר פסוק in תהלים that the judgment is in תשרי, a proof that continues on the next amud.
