Rosh Hashanah 30b

ראש השנה ל׳ ע״ב

Topic

רבן יוחנן בן זכאי's rulings after the חורבן on חדש and on accepting witnesses for the new month, and the mistake the לויים made in the שיר

Overview

The amud opens by explaining why רבן יוחנן בן זכאי forbade new grain (חדש) for the whole sixteenth of ניסן after the חורבן. He followed רבי יהודה, who reads 'עד עצם היום הזה' as 'until and including that day,' so the prohibition is from the Torah. The גמרא deals with a משנה where רבי יהודה seems to argue with him. It answers that רבי יהודה misunderstood him, and that 'התקין' there means 'he explained the פסוק and set the practice.' Then a new משנה tells a three-stage history: witnesses for the new month were first accepted all day. After a late arrival made the לויים go wrong in their שיר, they were accepted only until מנחה. After the חורבן, רבן יוחנן בן זכאי brought back the all-day practice. The גמרא asks what the לויים' mistake was. The scholars in בבל say they sang nothing, while רבי זירא says they sang the weekday song. רבי זירא's proof from the word 'שיבוש' is answered. Then רב אחא בר הונא brings a ברייתא about a Thursday ראש השנה. Its words 'says it and repeats it' show that the weekday song was sung while the day was in doubt.

Opening lines

בְּשִׁיטַת רַבִּי יְהוּדָה אַמְרַהּ,

He said it following the approach of Rabbi Yehuda,

דְּאָמַר: ״עַד עֶצֶם הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה״,

who said: "Until this very day"

עַד עַצְמוֹ שֶׁל יוֹם.

means until the day itself.

וְקָסָבַר: עַד, וְעַד בַּכְּלָל.

And he holds: "until" means until and including.
