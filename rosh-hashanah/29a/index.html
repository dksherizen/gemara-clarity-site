Rosh Hashanah 29a

ראש השנה כ״ט ע״א

Open Rosh Hashanah 29a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The intent needed for שופר, who can blow for others, and saying ברכות on behalf of others.

Overview

This עמוד finishes the story of רבי זירא asking his attendant to have him in mind while blowing. The גמרא learns from it that the one blowing must have the listener in mind. It defends this view against our משנה about a passerby: there the blower is a שליח ציבור, who has everyone in mind. It also answers a ברייתא that seems to disagree: תנאים argue about this, and רבי זירא follows רבי יוסי. A new משנה then teaches that משה's raised hands and the copper snake had no power of their own. What helped was Israel turning their hearts to their Father in Heaven. The משנה rules that a חרש, a שוטה, or a קטן cannot blow for others, because only someone obligated can fulfill a מצוה for others. The גמרא lists who is obligated and explains why כהנים had to be mentioned. It then rules, like רב נחמן, that a half slave, half free person cannot fulfill the מצוה even for himself. Finally, אהבה בריה דרבי זירא teaches when a person may say ברכות for others, and רבא begins a question that continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

אִיכַּוַּון וּתְקַע לִי.
