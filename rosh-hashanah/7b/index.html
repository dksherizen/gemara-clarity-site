Topic

The New Years of ניסן and אלול: שקלים, house rentals, the authorship of the משנה's clauses, and why the עומר and שתי הלחם are not counted

Overview

This amud opens by finishing an earlier answer: a ברייתא had to teach that an individual's donated קרבנות צבור are valid, even though we might worry he never fully handed them over. The גמרא explains that our משנה leaves out ניסן as the New Year for שקלים because that rule is not absolute: one who brings from the old collection is still יוצא. Next it discusses the יש אומרים who make ניסן the New Year for house rentals. It explains why a rental 'for this year' ends at ניסן and not תשרי, and why other תנאים disagree, since rain still falls in ניסן. The גמרא then turns to the משנה's clauses on רגלים and מעשר בהמה, which seem to follow different תנאים. רב יוסף says רבי picked different views for each topic, and רבא and רב נחמן בר יצחק explain how the משנה can still say 'four'. Finally, a ברייתא adds the עומר and שתי הלחם as New Years. רב פפא and רב שישא בריה דרב אידי each give a rule for why the משנה leaves them out, and each rule is defended against a challenge from רגלים, and רב פפא's rule also against one from יובל.

[therefore] it teaches us [that this is not a concern].

Since it is taught: If he brought [from the old collection], he has fulfilled [his obligation] —
