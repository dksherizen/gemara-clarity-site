Rosh Hashanah 33b

ראש השנה ל״ג ע״ב

Open Rosh Hashanah 33b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Intention in blowing שופר, the order and length of the blasts, and their sources in the פסוקים

Overview

The עמוד opens by finishing an answer about children: we teach a קטן to blow שופר once he has reached חינוך, but before that we just do not stop him. Next the גמרא reads the משנה's rules about a מתעסק. It tries to prove רבא's ruling that blowing לשיר counts, and to challenge רבי זירא, who wanted the blower to have him in mind. Both attempts are pushed away, because the משנה's wording does not force those readings. Then a new משנה lays out the order of the blasts (three sets of תקיעה, תרועה, תקיעה) and how long each sound must be. It also covers someone who got a שופר only after the ברכות, and a dispute about whether the שליח ציבור covers everyone. אביי settles one clash with a ברייתא about the length of a תקיעה, but admits a real מחלוקת about whether a תרועה is moaning (שברים) or whimpering (יבבות). Finally, a ברייתא uses the פסוק about the יובל to teach that we blow with a שופר, with a plain תקיעה before and after each תרועה. The words 'in the seventh month' apply all of this to ראש השנה.

Opening lines

בְּקָטָן שֶׁהִגִּיעַ לְחִינּוּךְ,

[That teaching is] about a minor who has reached the age of training;

כָּאן בְּקָטָן שֶׁלֹּא הִגִּיעַ לְחִינּוּךְ.

here, [it is] about a minor who has not reached the age of training.

וְהַמִּתְעַסֵּק לֹא יָצָא.

And one who acts unawares has not fulfilled [his obligation].

הָא תּוֹקֵעַ לָשִׁיר — יָצָא.

This implies: one who blows for music has fulfilled [it].
