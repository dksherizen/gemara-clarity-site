Topic

The torch signals, the witnesses' courtyard and their walking rights on שבת, and how witnesses to the new moon were questioned

Overview

This amud finishes the torch signals for ראש חודש. בית בלתין is identified as בירם. The גולה that looked like a bonfire is פומבדיתא, where everyone held a torch on his roof. A ברייתא adds more stations, and רבי יוחנן says the stations were eight פרסאות apart. אביי explains that the trip is longer today because the direct roads were blocked, and two פסוקים are brought for this. A new משנה describes בית יעזק, the courtyard where witnesses were hosted with big meals. It also tells how רבן גמליאל הזקן let them, and also midwives and rescuers, walk 2,000 אמות on שבת. The גמרא asks whether the name is בית יעזק or בית יזק, and the question is not settled. Another משנה describes how the witnesses were questioned about the moon. אביי explains that 'in front of the sun' asks which way the moon's hollow side faced. רבי יוחנן supports this with a פסוק, and his reason about the rainbow continues on the next amud.
