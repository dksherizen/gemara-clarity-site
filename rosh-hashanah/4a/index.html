Topic

Proofs that כורש became corrupt, and פסח as ראש השנה לרגלים for בל תאחר

Overview

The גמרא continues asking whether כורש really 'soured' (אחמיץ). רב כהנא points to the king's generous daily gifts for the קרבנות. רבי יצחק answers from the next verse: the king gave so the Jews would pray for him and his sons. A ברייתא says that giving 'so my children will live' still makes one a צדיק גמור, but the גמרא answers that this applies to a Jew and not to a non-Jew. The גמרא then brings more proofs that כורש became corrupt. First, he built a burnable row of wood into the בית המקדש, and the גמרא shows how this differed from what שלמה did. Second, he kept a שגל, explained as a she-dog, beside him. A third proof, from the limits he placed on his gifts, is rejected. The גמרא then turns to the משנה's phrase 'ראש השנה לרגלים.' רב חסדא explains that פסח begins the order of festivals, which matters for בל תאחר according to רבי שמעון. A new ברייתא listing the obligations covered by בל תאחר begins and continues on the next amud.
