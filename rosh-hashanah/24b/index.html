Topic

Which images are forbidden under לא תעשון אתי, and why רבן גמליאל's moon pictures were allowed

Overview

This amud asks why רבן גמליאל was allowed to have pictures of the moon. It first finishes a ברייתא about copying Temple objects: a seven-branched מנורה may not be made, even of other metals, though the חכמים reject רבי יוסי בר יהודה's claim that wood is also forbidden. The גמרא then tests the ban of לא תעשון אתי on picturing God's heavenly attendants. אביי limits it first to the four faces together, then to the attendants of the upper heaven. Along the way, the human face turns out to be forbidden from its own source, לא תעשון אותי. A ברייתא that clearly forbids making images of the sun and moon knocks out that approach. So the גמרא answers that others made רבן גמליאל's pictures for him, and it discusses the worry of suspicion with רב יהודה's ring and a בית כנסת in נהרדעא. It ends with three answers: a נשיא is never alone, the pictures were in pieces, or he made them in order to learn. A new משנה then begins about two witnesses who saw the moon in the morning in the east.
