Tamid 31b

תמיד ל״א ע״ב

Learn Tamid 31b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Tamid 31b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The nine כהנים who carry the תמיד, how the lamb was tied, the tables of the מקדש, and where the תמיד was slaughtered.

Overview

The משנה lists nine כהנים in order. Six carry the parts of the תמיד lamb, and the משנה explains exactly how each part is held. Three more carry the סולת, the חביתין, and the wine. Everything is placed on the lower western half of the כבש and salted, and the כהנים go to לשכת הגזית to say קריאת שמע. The גמרא explains that the lamb was tied foreleg to hind leg, like עקידת יצחק. It then gives two reasons why all four legs were not tied together (כפיתה): disgrace to holy things, or copying the ways of the nations. It quotes a משנה listing the thirteen tables in the מקדש and explains that marble was used because metal heats up. Next, it learns from 'שנים ליום' that the תמיד is slaughtered facing the daylight. The amud ends by opening the story of the ten questions אלכסנדרוס מוקדון asked the זקני הנגב, and the questions themselves come on the next amud.

Opening lines

הָרִאשׁוֹן – בָּרֹאשׁ וּבָרֶגֶל.

The first – with the head and with the leg.

הָרֹאשׁ בִּימִינוֹ,

The head in his right hand,

וְחוֹטְמוֹ כְּלַפֵּי זְרוֹעוֹ,

and its nose toward his arm,

קַרְנָיו בֵּין אֶצְבְּעוֹתָיו,

its horns between his fingers,
