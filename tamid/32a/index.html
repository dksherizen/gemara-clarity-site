Topic

אלכסנדרוס מוקדון questions the זקני הנגב about creation, wisdom and life, then sets out for אפריקי.

Overview

This amud continues the story of אלכסנדרוס מוקדון questioning the זקני הנגב. First he asks whether the sky is higher than the world is wide. The elders say east to west is farther. חכמים say the two distances are equal, based on a פסוק in תהלים. Next he asks about the order of creation. The elders answer that the heavens came first, but they refuse to say whether light or darkness came first, so that he will not go on to ask about forbidden topics. He then asks who is truly wise, mighty and rich, how to live, and how to win people's favor. On that last question he offers his own answer. After a tense exchange in which he threatens the elders, he honors them instead. Finally they advise him how to cross the Mountains of Darkness. He reaches a town of women, who talk him out of fighting and serve him bread of gold. The story continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מִן הַשָּׁמַיִם לָאָרֶץ רָחוֹק,

Is it farther from the heavens to the earth,

אוֹ מִמִּזְרָח לַמַּעֲרָב?

or from east to west?

אָמְרוּ לוֹ: מִמִּזְרָח לַמַּעֲרָב.

They said to him: from east to west.

תֵּדַע, שֶׁהֲרֵי חַמָּה בַּמִּזְרָח –

Know that this is so, for when the sun is in the east,
