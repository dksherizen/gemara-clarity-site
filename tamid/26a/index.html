Tamid 26a

תמיד כ״ו ע״א

Topic

The impure כהן's night routine, the morning lottery for תרומת הדשן, and the source for the כהנים guarding in three places

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the משנה about a כהן who became impure during the night in the לשכת המוקד. He walks through a lit tunnel under the בית המקדש to the immersion room, which has a fire and a private bathroom. There he immerses, dries off and warms up, then waits with the other כהנים until the gates open and he leaves. The משנה then begins the morning service. Any כהן who wants to do תרומת הדשן must immerse before the ממונה arrives around קרות הגבר. The ממונה then runs a lottery among those who immersed. The גמרא asks for the source of the rule that the כהנים guard the בית המקדש in three places. אביי brings the פסוק about משה, אהרן and his sons camping east of the משכן. The גמרא reads that פסוק in two parts: משה stands for the לוים, while אהרן and his sons are the כהנים guarding in three separate places. רב אשי gives another source: the three 'guard' words at the end of the same פסוק.

Opening lines

בִּמְסִיבָּה הַהוֹלֶכֶת לוֹ תַּחַת הַבִּירָה.

Through the winding passage that runs beneath the Temple building.

וְנֵרוֹת דּוֹלְקִין מִכָּאן וּמִכָּאן.

And lamps were burning on this side and on that side,

עַד שֶׁהוּא מַגִּיעַ לְבֵית הַטְּבִילָה.

until he reaches the Chamber of Immersion.

וּמְדוּרָה הָיְתָה שָׁם.
