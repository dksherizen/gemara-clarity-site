Topic

Things that help or harm Torah learning, honor for the leaders of the סנהדרין, and the story behind the labels 'אחרים' and 'יש אומרים'

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a point about how destructive mice are. A ברייתא then lists five things that make a person forget his learning and five things that bring it back. Two items are supported by sayings of רבי יוחנן (olive oil) and רבא (wine and spices). The salt item leads to a dispute about one or two fingers. A list of ten things harmful to learning follows. Next, a ברייתא describes the honor shown to the נשיא, the אב בית דין and the חכם, and how scholars and their sons are seated. רב פפא, רבא and רב add limits to some of these rules. Finally, רבי יוחנן tells the story behind the honor rules: רבי מאיר and רבי נתן tried to embarrass and replace רבן שמעון בן גמליאל, failed, and were punished by having their teachings quoted as 'אחרים' and 'יש אומרים'. The amud ends with רבי teaching his son a teaching of 'אחרים', and that exchange continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

רַב פָּפָּא אָמַר:

Rav Pappa said:

אֲפִילּוּ שׁוּפְתָּא [דְּ]מָרָא גָּיְיצִי.

They gnaw even on the handle of a hoe.

תָּנוּ רַבָּנַן.

The Rabbis taught:

חֲמִשָּׁה דְּבָרִים מְשַׁכְּחִים אֶת הַתַּלְמוּד:

Five things make one forget his learning:
