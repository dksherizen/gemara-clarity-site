Topic

Who is liable for acting on בית דין's mistaken ruling: the judge or תלמיד who knew better, the משנה's general rule, and the מחלוקת between רבי יהודה and חכמים

Overview

The amud first finishes a question about the order of cases in the משנה. Then it asks why the משנה lists both a judge who knew בית דין erred and a תלמיד ראוי להוראה. רבא explains that the extra case teaches that even someone who is only learned (גמיר), or only sharp (סביר), is liable for acting on his own knowledge. He names שמעון בן עזאי and שמעון בן זומא as examples. אביי objects that such great scholars would be sinning on purpose (מזיד). רבא answers that their mistake was thinking they must obey the חכמים even when the חכמים are wrong. Next the גמרא explains what each half of the משנה's general rule (זה הכלל) comes to include. Finally, שמואל says the משנה follows רבי יהודה, while חכמים hold that an individual who follows בית דין's ruling is חייב. The גמרא proves from the ברייתא's structure that, for חכמים, a minority who followed a ruling brings its own קרבן. It then starts to show why the first ברייתא belongs to רבי יהודה, and that proof continues on the next amud.
