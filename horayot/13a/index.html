Topic

Who and what comes first: the order of קרבנות, of people in rescue and honor, and of lineage, where Torah learning outranks every status.

Overview

The amud is all about who or what comes first. It starts with קרבנות. The כהן משיח's bull comes before the community's bull, and every חטאת comes before an עולה. The one exception is the bull and goat for עבודה זרה, explained by the missing א in לחטת or by the word כמשפט. A ברייתא then ranks more offerings: communal before individual, king before commoner, female goat against ewe (a disagreement of תנאים), and the עומר and שתי הלחם before their sheep. A new משנה turns to people: when a man comes first and when a woman comes first for rescue, clothing and captivity. The גמרא ranks a person, his teacher and his parents, then חכם, מלך, כהן גדול, נביא, and all the levels of כהנים and Temple officials. It also explains why the סגן, not the משוח מלחמה, stays טהור. A final משנה ranks people by lineage, from כהן down to freed slave, but a learned ממזר comes before an ignorant כהן גדול, because Torah is 'more precious than pearls.' The amud closes with questions students asked about converts, freed maidservants, cats and mice.

Opening lines

פַּר כֹּהֵן מָשׁוּחַ וּפַר עֵדָה כּוּ׳.

The bull of the anointed priest and the bull of the congregation, etc.

מְנָא הָנֵי מִילֵּי?

From where are these matters derived?

דְּתָנוּ רַבָּנַן:

As the Sages taught:

״וְשָׂרַף אוֹתוֹ כַּאֲשֶׁר שָׂרַף

"And he shall burn it as he burned
