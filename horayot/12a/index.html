Topic

Anointing kings and כהנים, good signs, and who counts as the כהן משיח of our משנה.

Overview

Just before this, the גמרא said that יהואחז was anointed as king. Now it asks how that could be, since a ברייתא teaches that יאשיהו hid the שמן המשחה together with the ארון. רב פפא answers that יהואחז was anointed with pure balsam oil. Next come the details of anointing: kings get a crown shape and כהנים get the shape of the Greek letter chi. Two ברייתות disagree about whether pouring on the head or smearing between the eyes comes first. A story about oil drops on אהרן's beard follows, and a בת קול tells משה and אהרן that neither committed מעילה. Kings are anointed by a spring as a good sign, and this opens a list of signs: tests for life, business, and travel, אביי's ראש השנה foods, רב משרשיא's advice to his sons, and anointing from a horn. Finally the גמרא returns to the משנה. It defines the כהן משיח and shows that our משנה follows the רבנן, not רבי מאיר, about the כהן גדול appointed only by wearing the extra garments.
