Topic

Examining a בכור's blemish on יום טוב, and whether there is מוקצה for half of שבת

Overview

The amud opens with the end of a ברייתא about a בכור (firstborn animal) whose blemish is examined on יום טוב. רבה בר רב הונא teaches that a בכור born with its blemish may be examined even לכתחילה. רב נחמן objects from his father's teaching that it works only בדיעבד. אביי defends רבה בר רב הונא by reading the ברייתא as three levels of permission. The גמרא then brings רב אושעיא's ברייתא, which reads differently, and dismisses אביי's ברייתא as the mixed-up version of אדא בר אוכמי. רב נחמן בר יצחק proves from the wording of the משנה that the examination is valid only בדיעבד. Next, הלל asks רבא whether something can be מוקצה for half of שבת: fit, then unfit, then fit again. Two versions of רבא's answer are given. Proofs from the blemished בכור and from fruit drying on the roof are both pushed aside. רבי זירא begins a new proof from beans and lentils cooked on יום טוב, and it continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בְּיוֹם טוֹב, רַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן אוֹמֵר:

on the Festival, Rabbi Shimon says:

אֵין זֶה מִן הַמּוּכָן.

This is not among the prepared.

וְשָׁוִין שֶׁאִם נוֹלַד הוּא וּמוּמוֹ עִמּוֹ

And they agree that if it was born with its blemish

— שֶׁזֶּה מִן הַמּוּכָן.

that this is among the prepared.
