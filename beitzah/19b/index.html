Beitzah 19b

ביצה י״ט ע״ב

Open Beitzah 19b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Bringing a תודה on the festivals, and the timing of בל תאחר

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the previous discussion: everyone agrees that נדרים and נדבות are not offered on יום טוב itself. The גמרא then quotes a ברייתא with three opinions about bringing a תודה on the festivals. The תנא קמא forbids it on פסח and on שבועות but allows it on סוכות, and the גמרא explains that this means חול המועד. It also explains that the line about פסח really refers to ערב פסח, because קדשים should not be put in a situation where they are more likely to become disqualified. רבי זירא challenges רבי שמעון's rule that all three festivals are alike. אביי answers that everyone allows bringing these offerings on חול המועד, and that the real argument is about when a person breaks בל תאחר: after any three רגלים, or only after three in order. רבי אלעזר ברבי שמעון allows a תודה even on יום טוב itself. He names סוכות because, unlike his father, he holds that סוכות itself is the deadline for vows. Finally, the תודה counts for שמחה but not for חגיגה, even if the person said so when he made the vow. The amud ends with a related question from ריש לקיש to רבי יוחנן that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֲבָל נְדָרִים וּנְדָבוֹת —

However, vow-offerings and gift-offerings:

דִּבְרֵי הַכֹּל אֵין קְרֵיבִין בְּיוֹם טוֹב.

everyone agrees they are not offered on the festival day.

וְהָנֵי תַּנָּאֵי כִּי הָנֵי תַּנָּאֵי,

And these tannaim are like those tannaim,

דְּתַנְיָא: אֵין מְבִיאִין תּוֹדָה

as it is taught: One may not bring a thanks-offering
