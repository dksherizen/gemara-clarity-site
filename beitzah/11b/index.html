Beitzah 11b

ביצה י״א ע״ב

Learn Beitzah 11b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Beitzah 11b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Salting on יום טוב, 'התירו סופן משום תחלתן' (permitting the end because of the beginning), and the dispute over store shutters (תריסין)

Overview

This amud opens by finishing an earlier discussion. Spreading out a hide on יום טוב is allowed, because a hide can be used to lie on. Spreading out fats is not allowed, because a person might reason his way into salting them. שמואל rules that one may salt several pieces of meat at once even if only one is needed, and רב אדא בר אהבה used a clever method to salt piece after piece. Then a new משנה begins: בית שמאי forbid removing shop shutters on יום טוב, while בית הלל permit removing them and even putting them back. עולא explains that these are shop shutters. He teaches that in three cases the Sages 'permitted the end because of the beginning': the hide, the shutters, and a bandage in the מקדש. רחבא adds a fourth case, following רבי יהודה. The גמרא shows that each case teaches a new limit, and explains that עולא lists only cases with no live dispute. Finally, a ברייתא of רבי שמעון בן אלעזר disagrees with our משנה, a second ברייתא contradicts it, and אביי resolves the contradiction by dividing shutters into three kinds of hinges.

Opening lines

הָתָם לָא מוֹכְחָא מִלְּתָא,

There (with the hide), the matter is not evident,

מִשּׁוּם דַּחֲזֵי לְמִזְגֵּא עֲלֵיהּ.

because it is fit to recline on.

הָכָא אָתֵי לְמֵימַר:

Here (with fats), he will come to say:

מַאי טַעְמָא שָׁרוּ לִי רַבָּנַן —

What is the reason the Sages permitted it to me?
