Topic

Immersing (טבילה) on שבת, יום טוב and יום הכיפורים, רבא's 'since' reasoning, and purifying water and vessels on יום טוב

Overview

The גמרא finishes explaining why one may immerse (טבילה) on שבת and יום טוב: it doesn't clearly look like purifying, because people sometimes bathe just to cool off. It then tests this answer. In the rainy season nobody bathes to cool off, but רב נחמן בר יצחק says people also wash off mud and filth. On יום הכיפורים all ordinary bathing is forbidden, so רבא gives a rule instead: whatever is allowed on שבת is also allowed on יום הכיפורים. The גמרא asks whether רבא really uses this kind of 'since' (הואיל) reasoning, because in the case of vinegar for a toothache he did not. It concludes that רבא took back his vinegar answer, and a ברייתא confirms that טבילה is allowed on יום הכיפורים and תשעה באב. Then the גמרא turns to a line of the משנה about purifying water in a stone vessel. שמואל explains that one may not immerse an impure vessel together with its water on יום טוב. This reading seems to match neither רבי nor the חכמים, and the גמרא begins to show that it can fit רבי by placing his two rulings on יום טוב and שבת. The answer that follows the חכמים comes on the next amud.
