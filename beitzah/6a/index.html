Beitzah 6a

ביצה ו׳ ע״א

Learn Beitzah 6a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Beitzah 6a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Burial on יום טוב שני, the two days of ראש השנה (egg and עירובי תבשילין), and a chick that hatched on יום טוב.

Overview

רבא rules that on יום טוב ראשון only non-Jews handle a burial, but on יום טוב שני Jews may do it themselves. This applies even to ראש השנה, though an egg laid on the first day of ראש השנה stays forbidden on the second day. The נהרדעי disagree about the egg, because since עזרא's time אלול has never been made 30 days long. מר זוטרא limits burial on יום טוב שני to a burial that was already delayed. רב אשי says we never postpone it, because for a מת the Rabbis treated יום טוב שני like a weekday, and רבינא adds that nowadays the danger from the חברי holds us back. A story follows: רב אשי forgot his עירובי תבשילין before a Thursday-Friday ראש השנה, and he explains that רבא's תנאי does not work there, since he does not accept the נהרדעי's view. Finally, רב forbids a chick that hatched on יום טוב as מוקצה, while שמואל (or רבי יוחנן) permits it. רב is challenged with a newborn calf and with a calf born to a טריפה, and רבה gives the answer רב could have given. אביי's reply begins and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אָמַר רָבָא: מֵת בְּיוֹם טוֹב רִאשׁוֹן —

Rava said: If one died on the first day of Yom Tov,

יִתְעַסְּקוּ בּוֹ עֲמָמִים.

non-Jews should attend to him (his burial).

מֵת בְּיוֹם טוֹב שֵׁנִי —

If he died on the second day of Yom Tov,

יִתְעַסְּקוּ בּוֹ יִשְׂרָאֵל,

Jews should attend to him,

The steps (20)

  1. Statementרבא: Who handles a burial on the first and second day of יום טוב
  2. StatementThe נהרדעי: Even the egg is permitted
  3. ProofThe נהרדעי's reason: אלול has not been made a full month since עזרא
  4. Statementמר זוטרא: Jews bury on the second day only if burial was already delayed
  5. Statementרב אשי: We never postpone the burial
  6. ResponseThe reason: For the dead, the second day is like a weekday
  7. StatementNowadays we worry about the hostile priests
  8. StatementThe story: רב אשי is sad on ראש השנה
  9. Challengeרבינא suggests making the עירוב now with a תנאי
  10. Resolutionרבא's rule does not apply to Rosh HaShana
  11. Challengeרבינא challenges from the נהרדעי
  12. Resolutionרב אשי explicitly rejected the נהרדעי view
  13. StatementDispute about a chick that hatched on יום טוב
  14. StatementThe reasons for each side
  15. ChallengeChallenge: how is a chick different from a newborn calf?
  16. ResolutionThe calf was ready through its mother
  17. ChallengeChallenge from a calf born to a טריפה
  18. Conclusionרב was silent
  19. Resolutionרבה gives the answer רב could have said
