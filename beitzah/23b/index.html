Beitzah 23b

ביצה כ״ג ע״ב

Open Beitzah 23b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The pepper mill and the child's wagon (טומאה and dragging on שבת), and the start of פרק אין צדין on trapping creatures on יום טוב

Overview

This amud has two short משניות that close the פרק, and then the opening of a new פרק. A pepper mill can become טמא for three reasons, and a ברייתא matches each reason to one part of the mill: the bottom holds, the middle sifts, and the top is metal. A child's wagon can get מדרס טומאה because the child leans on it. It may also be picked up on שבת because it is a כלי. The משנה says the wagon may be dragged only over a covered surface, since on bare ground it digs a groove. The גמרא says this follows רבי יהודה, who forbids a דבר שאין מתכוין, and not רבי שמעון. The סיפא, where רבי יהודה says a wagon only presses the earth, seems to contradict this. The גמרא answers that two תנאים report רבי יהודה's view differently. After the הדרן, the new פרק אין צדין begins: fish may not be trapped from ponds on יום טוב, but animals and birds may be taken from enclosures and fed. רבן שמעון בן גמליאל starts a general rule about enclosures, which continues on the next amud.

  1. StatementA pepper mill can become impure for three reasons
  2. StatementWhich part of the mill matches which reason
  3. StatementA child's wagon: impurity, carrying, and dragging on שבת
