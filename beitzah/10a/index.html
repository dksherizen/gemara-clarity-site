Beitzah 10a

ביצה י׳ ע״א

Learn Beitzah 10a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Beitzah 10a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

רבי יוחנן's מוחלפת השיטה tested against several משניות, then preparing young doves before יום טוב and the question of בְּרֵירָה

Overview

The amud first tests whether רבי יוחנן was right that the names of בית שמאי and בית הלל are switched (מוחלפת השיטה) in the משנה about covering the blood (כיסוי הדם). The גמרא brings three cases where בית שמאי are strict and בית הלל are lenient: the pestle (עלי), the raw hide (עור), and the shop shutters (תריסין). Each one seems to clash with the blood משנה. The pestle and hide cases are each pushed aside by showing that the cases are different. With the shutters, בית שמאי can be explained, but בית הלל remain a problem. רבי יוחנן solves that problem by swapping the names, or else the shutters are special because there is no building or taking apart with כלים. Then a new משנה begins about preparing young doves before יום טוב. רב חנן בר אמי limits the dispute to the first brood. The גמרא then asks why בית הלל require naming specific doves, which leads to a discussion of בְּרֵירָה using a case of a corpse in a house with many doorways. רבא explains that the real worry is handling מוקצה birds, and the answer to the final challenge continues on the next amud.

אֲבָל הֵיכָא דְּלֵיכָּא דָּקָר נָעוּץ — לָא.

But where there is no embedded shovel — no.
