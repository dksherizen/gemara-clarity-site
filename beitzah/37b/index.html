Beitzah 37b

ביצה ל״ז ע״ב

Learn Beitzah 37b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Beitzah 37b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Whose תחום an animal or shared object follows on יום טוב, and the debate over ברירה (retroactive clarification).

Overview

Our משנה says an animal given to a shepherd still follows the owner's תחום. A ברייתא of רבי דוסא says it follows the shepherd. The גמרא settles the clash: the ברייתא is a town with one shepherd, and the משנה is a town with two. The same split explains how רבי יוחנן can rule like רבי דוסא and also follow a סתם משנה. A ברייתא then teaches that a robe borrowed by two partners may only go where both of them may walk. רב and שמואל argue about a barrel and an animal bought together, which leads to the question of ברירה, whether a later division reveals what was true from the start. רב's students challenge his reason for the animal, and רב is silent. The גמרא reports a dispute between רבי הושעיא and רבי יוחנן about ברירה, reverses it, and then challenges רבי יוחנן's new position using a ruling about brothers who divide land and a ברייתא of איו about עירובין. That challenge continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דְּלָא כְּרַבִּי דּוֹסָא,

is not in accordance with Rabbi Dosa,

דְּתַנְיָא: רַבִּי דּוֹסָא אוֹמֵר,

As it is taught: Rabbi Dosa says,

וְאָמְרִי לַהּ: אַבָּא שָׁאוּל אוֹמֵר,

and some say: Abba Shaul says,

הַלּוֹקֵחַ בְּהֵמָה מֵחֲבֵרוֹ מֵעֶרֶב יוֹם טוֹב,

one who buys an animal from his friend on the eve of the Festival,
