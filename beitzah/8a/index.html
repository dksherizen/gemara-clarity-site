Beitzah 8a

ביצה ח׳ ע״א

Learn Beitzah 8a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Beitzah 8a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Prepared materials for כיסוי הדם on יום טוב: loose earth, stove ashes, earth brought in before יום טוב, and the כוי challenge

Overview

The גמרא finishes explaining how one may dig with a דקר to cover blood on יום טוב. The earth is loose (עפר תיחוח), so nothing is crushed. The hole left behind does not matter either, because following רבי אבא, a hole dug only for its earth is פטור. Next, רבה explains that the משנה's mention of stove ashes (אפר כירה) is an extra ruling: ashes also count as prepared. רב limits this, and a ברייתא backs him up. Ashes count as prepared only if the fire was lit before יום טוב, unless they are still hot enough to roast an egg. The גמרא then teaches that earth brought in before יום טוב is prepared, and רב יהודה allows a basketful to be used for all needs. מר זוטרא adds that this holds only if it was kept in a set-aside corner. Finally, the גמרא defends רב יהודה against a challenge from the כוי. It explains that the כוי case speaks of someone with no prepared earth, and that it teaches that even a doubtful obligation to cover blood is not pushed aside by שמחת יום טוב.

Opening lines

בְּעָפָר תִּיחוּחַ.

With loose earth.

וְהָא קָא עָבֵיד גּוּמָּא!

But he is making a hole!

כִּדְרַבִּי אַבָּא.

In accordance with Rabbi Abba.

דְּאָמַר רַבִּי אַבָּא:

As Rabbi Abba said:

The steps (16)

  1. ResolutionThe digging is in loose earth
  2. ChallengeBut he is still making a hole
  3. ResolutionFollowing רבי אבא, an unwanted hole brings no liability
  4. StatementQuoting the משנה about stove ashes
  5. ChallengeWho mentioned ashes?
  6. Resolutionרבה: read it as 'and also stove ashes are prepared'
  7. Statementרב: ashes count only if the stove was lit before יום טוב
  8. StatementException: ashes hot enough to roast an egg
  9. ProofA ברייתא supports the rule about stove ashes
  10. StatementEarth brought into a garden or ruin may be used for covering
  11. Statementרב יהודה: a basketful of earth may be used for all needs
  12. Statementמר זוטרא limits the rule to earth set aside in a corner
  13. ChallengeChallenge from the כוי: why not cover its blood with prepared earth?
  14. ResolutionThe ruling must speak about someone who has no prepared earth
