Beitzah 17b

ביצה י״ז ע״ב

Learn Beitzah 17b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Beitzah 17b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Food cooked without an עירוב תבשילין, how many dishes the עירוב needs, and immersing before a יום טוב that falls after שבת

Overview

The amud opens with a question: if someone cooked or baked on יום טוב for שבת without an עירוב תבשילין, may the food be eaten? The גמרא tries many proofs that the food is forbidden: the ברייתא's silence, a ruling about הערמה, and cases of tithing, immersing vessels, and cooking on שבת done on purpose. Each proof is pushed away. A ברייתא teaches only permitted solutions, הערמה is treated more strictly, the strict ברייתא may follow בית שמאי, and in the other cases the loss is small or שבת is more severe. So the question is not decided. Next the גמרא brings רבי שמעון בן אלעזר's different version of the dispute between בית שמאי and בית הלל about two dishes. רבא rules like our משנה and בית הלל: one cooked dish is enough. אביי adds that if you began your dough and the עירוב was eaten afterward, you may finish. A new משנה then discusses immersing people and vessels when יום טוב falls right after שבת. The גמרא begins asking why vessels may not be immersed on שבת. רבה starts to answer that it is a גזרה, and his answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְאִי אִיתָא,

And if it is so (that it is permitted),

לִיתְנֵי: עָבַר וְאָפָה מוּתָּר!

let it teach: if he transgressed and baked, it is permitted!

אָמַר רַב אַדָּא בַּר מַתְנָה:

Rav Adda bar Mattana said:

תַּנָּא, תַּקַּנְתָּא דְהֶיתֵּרָא — קָתָנֵי,

the tanna teaches a remedy that is permitted,
