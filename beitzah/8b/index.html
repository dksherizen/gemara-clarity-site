Beitzah 8b

ביצה ח׳ ע״ב

Topic

Covering blood on יום טוב: why a כוי's blood is not covered, mixed blood, and blood left from ערב יום טוב

Overview

This amud asks why the blood of a כוי, an animal whose status is doubtful, may not be covered on יום טוב. The גמרא first shows that the person does have covering material, so the problem must be something else. רבה says אפר כירה is מוכן for a definite (ודאי) case but not for a doubtful (ספק) one. The גמרא tests two reasons for this. Making a hole (גומא) fails because of רבי אבא. Crushing (כתישה) also fails, because יום טוב is both an עשה and a לא תעשה, and an עשה alone cannot override both. רבא then explains that when the owner prepared the ashes, he only had definite needs in mind, which matches his ruling about earth brought in for צואה and דם צפור. רמי בריה דרב ייבא gives a different reason: a גזירה, so that people will not think the כוי is a חיה and eat its חלב. The amud ends with mixed blood, which may be covered only with one shovel thrust, and רבה's ruling that blood of a bird slaughtered on ערב יום טוב is not covered on יום טוב.
