Topic

Mistaken egg sales, when an egg counts as laid, attached eggs as meat, and eggs found on יום טוב

Overview

The amud opens by finishing two stories in which רבי אמי rules that selling eggs that cannot hatch to a buyer who wanted eggs that can hatch is a מקח טעות, and the sale is cancelled. Then the גמרא offers two readings of רב's phrase 'עם יציאתה נגמרה': either the egg counts as finished once most of it is out, like רבי יוחנן, or only once all of it is out. Next, with 'גופא', it examines a ברייתא: fully formed eggs found in a slaughtered hen may be eaten with milk, but רבי יעקב forbids them if they are still attached by sinews. The גמרא asks whether another ברייתא, about which parts of a dead kosher bird make the eater טמא, follows רבי יעקב. רב יוסף links it to רבי יעקב, and in both versions אביי shows the link is not proven. A ברייתא then teaches that a chicken mates and lays only by day. That supports רב מרי's ruling that an egg found at dawn on יום טוב, in a nest that was empty the evening before, is permitted. רב's opposite ruling is explained as speaking of an egg 'from the earth', which a hen does not form when a rooster is present. The amud ends as רב גמדא begins to say how close the rooster must be, and his answer continues on the next amud.

(eggs) of a live hen, who has?

יְהַבוּ לֵיהּ בֵּיעֵי דִשְׁחוּטָה.

They gave him eggs of a slaughtered hen.

אֲתָא לְקַמֵּיהּ דְּרַבִּי אַמֵּי,

He came before Rabbi Ami,

אֲמַר לְהוּ: מִקָּח טָעוּת הוּא, וְהָדַר.

He said to them: it is a mistaken purchase, and it is reversed.
