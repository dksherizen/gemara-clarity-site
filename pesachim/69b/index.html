Pesachim 69b

פסחים ס״ט ע״ב

Topic

Why מילה but not הזאה overrides שבת for רבי אליעזר, the הלכה like רבי עקיבא on preparations, and the משנה about the חגיגה brought with the פסח.

Overview

According to רבי אליעזר, preparations for the קרבן פסח override שבת, so the גמרא asks why מילה overrides שבת but הזאה does not. רבא first answers with a mirror principle: a community of ערלים must circumcise, so an individual must too, but a community of טמאים brings the פסח בטומאה, so an individual is not required to purify. רבא defends this principle against a challenge from פסח שני. A ברייתא then shows that even a טמא שרץ gets כרת, which breaks the mirror principle. So רבא gives a new answer: one may slaughter for a טמא שרץ or a טמא מת on his seventh day. The הזאה is needed only for eating, and eating the פסח is not essential. He explains that such a person is not שלא לאוכליו, because he is fit and only not yet prepared. רב rules like רבי עקיבא that only what cannot be done before שבת overrides שבת, for both פסח and מילה, and the גמרא explains why both rulings are needed. A new משנה then teaches when a חגיגה is brought with the פסח and what animals it may come from. The גמרא explains that the חגיגה fits here because, like carrying the פסח, it does not override שבת. רב אשי begins drawing a lesson about the חגיגה of the fourteenth, and his statement continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְכׇל שֶׁאִילּוּ בְּיָחִיד נִדְחֶה,

And anything (any impurity) which, for an individual, would push him off,

בְּצִיבּוּר עָבְדִי בְּטוּמְאָה.

for a community, they perform it in impurity.

וְכׇל מִילְּתָא דְּאִיתָא בְּצִיבּוּר,

And any matter that applies to the community,

אִיתָא בְּיָחִיד.

applies to the individual.
