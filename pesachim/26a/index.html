Topic

אביי and רבא on unavoidable but intended benefit (לא אפשר ומתכוין), and a side discussion of when sound, sight, smell, and finished-מצוה items are subject to מעילה.

Overview

אביי and רבא argue about a benefit that cannot be avoided when the person intends to enjoy it (לא אפשר ומתכוין). אביי permits it and רבא forbids it. רבא explains that רבי יהודה's rule about intent only works to make things stricter. אביי brings proof from רבן יוחנן בן זכאי, who taught in the shade of the היכל, but רבא answers that the היכל was built for its inside, so its shade was never forbidden. רבא's own proofs are also pushed aside: the workers lowered in boxes into the בית קדשי הקדשים were only a special מעלה, and בר קפרא's teaching can refer to people standing outside. The גמרא then examines בר קפרא's rule that sound, sight, and smell are not מעילה, and רב פפא corrects it: smell is free of מעילה only after the smoke rises, once the מצוה is done. That principle is tested against תרומת הדשן, and the גמרא explains that it and בגדי כהונה (or עגלה ערופה) are exceptions that teach no general rule. Finally, the גמרא returns to אביי and רבא with a heifer that threshed grain, and challenges the answer that its verse disqualifies any work at all.

Opening lines

וְרָבָא אָמַר לָךְ:

And Rava would say to you:

עַד כָּאן לָא קָאָמַר רַבִּי יְהוּדָה

Rabbi Yehuda only said

שֶׁאֵין מִתְכַּוֵּין כְּמִתְכַּוֵּין —

that one who does not intend is like one who intends

אֶלָּא לְחוּמְרָא,

only to be strict;
