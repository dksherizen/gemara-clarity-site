Pesachim 44a

פסחים מ״ד ע״א

Topic

Whether היתר joins איסור to make a כזית, and רב דימי's defense that a כזית בכדי אכילת פרס is Torah law, against אביי's challenges from the טבול יום soup, כותח הבבלי, and the ברייתות of mixed תרומה.

Overview

This amud opens by agreeing that the rule היתר מצטרף לאיסור, learned from the פסוק about leaven on the מזבח, also applies to חמץ on פסח. It adds that this rule rejects אביי's view that offering less than a כזית counts as a הקטרה. Then רב דימי teaches that in all other Torah prohibitions היתר does not join איסור, except for a נזיר. אביי challenges him from a משנה about a חולין soup with תרומה spices that was touched by a טבול יום. A זר is lashed for a כזית of that soup, which seems to show that היתר joins איסור. רב דימי answers that the lashes come from eating a כזית of the תרומה itself within כדי אכילת פרס, which he holds is Torah law. He defends this against a question from כותח הבבלי. He also answers two ברייתות about mixed-up pots and baskets by explaining that each case involves only rabbinic תרומה. Finally the גמרא asks whether the word מִשְׁרַת in the נזיר פסוק is really free to teach this rule, and the ברייתא it starts quoting continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לְעִנְיַן חָמֵץ בַּפֶּסַח נָמֵי.

With regard to חמץ on Pesach also [should permitted join forbidden]?

אִין הָכִי נָמֵי.

Yes, indeed it is so.

וּלְאַפּוֹקֵי מִדְּאַבַּיֵּי דְּאָמַר:

And [it comes] to exclude [the view] of Abaye, who said:

יֵשׁ הַקְטָרָה לְפָחוֹת מִכְּזַיִת,

There is [a valid] offering for less than an olive-bulk,
