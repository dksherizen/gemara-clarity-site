פסחים ס״ה ע״ב

Topic

The blood-filled עזרה on ערב פסח, the end of פרק תמיד נשחט, and the start of פרק אלו דברים: which acts of the פסח override שבת

Overview

This amud opens by finishing a קשיא against רבי יהודה. He holds that only the life-blood atones, yet his cup of mixed floor blood must contain דם התמצית. The גמרא answers that he holds אין דם מבטל דם, so the valid blood in the cup is not cancelled. A ברייתא then records רבי יהודה asking the חכמים why the עזרה was plugged. They answer that it honored the כהנים to walk in blood. The גמרא solves two problems with this. Wet blood is not a חציצה. As for soiled garments, the walking happened while carrying wood, which is not an עבודה, and during real עבודות the כהנים walked on raised platforms. After two short explanations of the משנה, about the basin of אמורים and carrying the פסח home in its hide, the chapter תמיד נשחט ends. The new chapter's משנה lists which parts of the פסח override שבת. It then presents the dispute over שבות: רבי אליעזר argues by קל וחומר that they override, and רבי יהושע and רבי עקיבא bring counter-examples. רבי אליעזר's reply about הזאה continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דָּם שֶׁהַנֶּפֶשׁ יוֹצְאָה בּוֹ — מְכַפֵּר.

Blood with which the life goes out — atones.

דָּם שֶׁאֵין הַנֶּפֶשׁ יוֹצְאָה בּוֹ — אֵינוֹ מְכַפֵּר.

Blood with which the life does not go out — does not atone.

אֶלָּא רַבִּי יְהוּדָה לְטַעְמֵיהּ,

Rather, Rabbi Yehuda follows his own reasoning,

דְּאָמַר: אֵין דָּם מְבַטֵּל דָּם.

for he said: blood does not nullify blood.
