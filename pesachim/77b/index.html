Topic

The disagreement of רבי אליעזר and רבי יהושע on whether blood needs meat, and whether our משנה of five public offerings brought in טומאה can follow רבי יהושע

Overview

Background: רבי יהושע holds that a קרבן needs both its blood and its meat. רבי אליעזר holds that the blood is thrown even when there is no meat. First the גמרא explains how each of them reads the פסוק 'ועשית עלתיך הבשר והדם... ודם זבחיך ישפך... והבשר תאכל'. It also explains where each one learns that the meat may not be eaten until the blood is thrown: רבי אליעזר from the פסוק, and רבי יהושע from a קל וחומר. Then the גמרא asks whether our משנה can follow רבי יהושע. That משנה says five public offerings, such as the עומר and the שתי הלחם, may be brought in טומאה. One answer is that for רבי יהושע the ציץ fixes the parts burned on the מזבח and that his rule does not apply to מנחות. A ברייתא of רבי יוסי knocks this down. A second answer is that the ציץ also fixes the eaten parts. A משנה about מנחות and a ברייתא of רבי יהושע knock this down too. Finally the גמרא suggests that רבי יהושע is strict only לכתחילה, and it proves he makes that distinction. But the wording פסולה and חמשה דברים באין shows that this distinction cannot explain our משנה.

Opening lines

וְהָנֵי תְּרֵי קְרָאֵי

And these two verses

לְמָה לִי?

why do I need them?

חַד בְּעוֹלָה,

One is about a burnt-offering,

וְחַד בִּשְׁלָמִים.

and one is about a peace-offering.
