Pesachim 108a

פסחים ק״ח ע״א

Topic

Wine and appetite, רב ששת's fast on ערב פסח, and the laws of הסיבה at the סדר

Overview

This amud first finishes an earlier debate: does wine fill you up (מסעד סעיד) or make you hungrier (מגרר גריר)? The גמרא proves from a משנה about extra drinking between the cups that wine whets the appetite. Next it reports that רב ששת fasted all day on ערב פסח. The גמרא suggests a legal reason: the eating ban protects the קרבן פסח, and בן בתירא allows the פסח all day. It then rejects this, because רב ששת was simply delicate and needed an appetite at night. The amud then turns to הסיבה (reclining). מצה needs הסיבה, מרור does not, and all four cups need it. It explains that one must lean left, not on the back or to the right. It then rules on who must recline: a wife, a son, a תלמיד before his רב, and a שמש. Finally, רבי יהושע בן לוי says women are obligated in the four cups, and the reason continues on the next amud.

בֵּין הַכּוֹסוֹת הַלָּלוּ

Between these cups

אִם רָצָה לִשְׁתּוֹת — יִשְׁתֶּה,

if he wants to drink, he may drink,

בֵּין שְׁלִישִׁי לִרְבִיעִי — לֹא יִשְׁתֶּה.

between the third and the fourth, he may not drink.

וְאִי אָמְרַתְּ מִסְעָד סָעֵיד,

And if you say it (wine) satisfies,
