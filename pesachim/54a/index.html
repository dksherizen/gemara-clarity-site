Topic

The ברכה over fire after שבת and יום הכפורים, and when fire (ours and גיהנם's) was created

Overview

This amud opens by finishing a story: עולא silently disagreed with a report of רבי יוחנן's view, and רבה בר בר חנה understood him. Their view is that we bless over fire both after שבת and after יום הכפורים. A ברייתא seems to limit the ברכה to מוצאי שבת, because fire was first created then. The גמרא answers that after יום הכפורים we bless only on fire that 'rested', while brand-new fire from wood or stones is blessed only on מוצאי שבת. The גמרא then asks whether fire was really created on מוצאי שבת, since a ברייתא lists fire among things made on ערב שבת. After several rounds, the conclusion is this: the space of גיהנם came before the world, and its fire came on day two. Our fire was planned on ערב שבת and made on מוצאי שבת, when אדם הראשון rubbed two stones together. A side discussion about the first mule leads to a teaching that ענה was born of a forbidden relationship. The amud ends by quoting in full the ברייתא of ten things created at בין השמשות, whose list of extra opinions continues on the next amud.
