Pesachim 64a

פסחים ס״ד ע״א

Open Pesachim 64a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The איסור of offering a קרבן while owning חמץ (sources and contradictions), and the משנה's description of how the קרבן פסח was slaughtered in three groups.

Overview

The amud opens by settling two contradictions between ברייתות about offering a קרבן while owning חמץ. Both ברייתות follow רבי שמעון. The מליקה difference is about timing: the fourteenth of ניסן versus חול המועד. The הקטרה difference is a dispute among תנאים over whether burning is compared to slaughtering. The גמרא then explains the sources in the משנה: רבי יהודה reads זבחי as the תמיד, and רבי שמעון reads the doubled זבחי as "זבח" and "זבחיי" to split the fourteenth from the festival. Next it challenges רבי שמעון's ruling about a פסח slaughtered during the festival, since that ruling seems to imply that a פסח needs עקירה to become a שלמים. רבי חייא בר גמדא answers that the case is owners who were deferred to פסח שני. A new משנה then describes the slaughter of the פסח in three groups: the trumpet blasts, the rows of כהנים passing the blood, the saying of הלל, what changes on שבת, and how the animals were hung and skinned. It ends as רבי אליעזר begins a statement that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

קַשְׁיָא מְלִיקָה אַמְּלִיקָה,

There is a contradiction: pinching against pinching,

קַשְׁיָא הַקְטָרָה אַהַקְטָרָה!

there is a contradiction: burning against burning!

וּלְטַעְמָיךְ, תִּיקְשֵׁי לָךְ הִיא גּוּפָא.

And according to your reasoning, ask about the ברייתא itself.

דְּקָתָנֵי: לֹא אָמְרוּ אֶלָּא בְּפֶסַח בִּלְבַד,

For it teaches: They said this only about the Paschal lamb,
