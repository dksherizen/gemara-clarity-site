Pesachim 82b

פסחים פ״ב ע״ב

Learn Pesachim 82b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Pesachim 82b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Why disqualified קדשים are burned, the timing of burning (עיבור צורה) for פיגול, and when owners became טמא before or after זריקה

Overview

The גמרא asks for the source that a disqualified קרבן, such as one whose blood spilled or left the courtyard, must be burned. It first tries רבי שמעון's reading of 'בקדש באש תשרף', but that does not cover the meat of קדשים קלים like the פסח. So it concludes that burning anything disqualified in the קודש is a received tradition (גמרא גמירי לה), and the פסוק only teaches that the burning happens in the holy place. In a side discussion, the תנא דבי רבה בר אבוה learns from נותר that even פיגול must wait for עיבור צורה, and explains that אהרן's חטאת was a הוראת שעה. A second פסוק, about טמא meat, is needed to teach that even טומאה, which also applies to חולין, requires burning and not burial. The גמרא then returns to the משנה about owners who became טמא or died. רב יוסף's version of the dispute is challenged by a ברייתא and reversed: the מחלוקת is before זריקה, and after זריקה all agree עיבור צורה is needed. רבי יוחנן holds the מחלוקת applies even after זריקה, and supports this from רבי נחמיה, who says אהרן's חטאת was burned immediately because of אנינות.

Opening lines

נִשְׁפַּךְ דָּמָהּ,

its blood was spilled,

יָצָא דָּמָהּ חוּץ לַקְּלָעִים —

its blood went out beyond the curtains —

דְּקַיְימָא לַן בִּשְׂרֵיפָה,

which we hold must be burned,

מְנָלַן?

from where do we know this?

The steps (25)

  1. QuestionWhere do we learn that these disqualified offerings are burned?
  2. Responseרבי שמעון's verse: a sin-offering is burned in the holy place
  3. QuestionWhat about other disqualified holy offerings?
  4. ResponseThe word 'all' widens the rule
  5. ChallengeBut what about the meat of lesser-holy offerings?
  6. ResolutionThe rule is a received tradition
  7. Resolutionאהרן's burned חטאת is just history
  8. QuestionWhat is the source that even פיגול must wait for its form to decay?
  9. ResponseLearned from leftover meat through the shared word 'iniquity'
  10. ChallengeWhy not learn from אהרן's חטאת instead?
  11. Resolutionאהרן's חטאת was a one-time ruling
  12. ChallengeIf burning is a tradition, why do we need the verse?
  13. ResolutionThe verse teaches where the burning takes place
  14. ChallengeWhy do we need the verse about impure meat being burned?
  15. ResolutionThe verse is needed because impurity also applies to ordinary meat
  16. StatementReturning to the משנה: owners who became impure or died
  17. Statementרב יוסף limits the dispute to impurity after the blood was sprinkled
  18. ChallengeA ברייתא about which disqualified offerings are burned immediately
  19. ChallengeOwners are compared to blood, so the case is before זריקה
  20. Resolutionרב יוסף's statement is reversed
  21. Statementרבי יוחנן says the dispute applies even after זריקה
  22. Statementרבי יוחנן is consistent with his view on רבי יוחנן בן ברוקה and רבי נחמיה
  23. QuestionWhere does רבי נחמיה say this?
  24. Responseרבי נחמיה: אהרן's חטאת was burned because of mourning
  25. ProofMourning is like a problem after זריקה, and it was burned immediately
