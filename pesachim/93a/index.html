Pesachim 93a

פסחים צ״ג ע״א

Learn Pesachim 93a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Pesachim 93a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Who brings פסח שני and what it is: הורצה for the traveler on a דרך רחוקה, whether women bring it, and the three-way dispute over כרת for skipping the first or second פסח.

Overview

The amud opens with the end of רבי עקיבא's comparison: a person on a דרך רחוקה is like a טמא. This was a challenge to רב נחמן, who holds that a פסח offered for the traveler counts (הורצה). רב נחמן answers that רבי עקיבא follows his own view that we do not slaughter for a טמא שרץ, a view רב נחמן rejects. A ברייתא then lists who brings פסח שני. After correcting its question, the ברייתא says the תורה names the traveler to exempt him from כרת, and this fits רב נחמן's view of הורצה. The גמרא resolves a contradiction about whether women bring פסח שני by assigning the two ברייתות to different תנאים. Next comes a three-way dispute about כרת. רבי holds that פסח שני is its own holiday. רבי נתן holds that it is only a make-up. רבי חנניא בן עקביא holds that it repairs the first. All three learn their view from one פסוק. The amud ends by asking how we know that חטאו ישא means כרת, and the answer comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

מָה טָמֵא,

Just as an impure person,

שֶׁסִּפֵּק בְּיָדוֹ לַעֲשׂוֹת וְאֵינוֹ עוֹשֶׂה.

has the ability to do it, yet does not do it,

אַף דֶּרֶךְ רְחוֹקָה נָמֵי,

so too a distant journey,

שֶׁסִּפֵּק בְּיָדוֹ לַעֲשׂוֹת וְאֵינוֹ עוֹשֶׂה!

is one who has the ability to do it, yet does not do it!
