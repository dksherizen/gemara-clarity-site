Pesachim 42b

פסחים מ״ב ע״ב

Learn Pesachim 42b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Pesachim 42b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Explaining the משנה's list of חמץ mixtures, with side discussions on Edomite vinegar, tithing תמד, and קולן של סופרים

Overview

This amud opens by finishing a medical tangent. The גמרא defines פת נקיה, בשר שמן and יין ישן, and names five foods that help the whole body. It then returns to the משנה's list of חמץ mixtures. It explains that שכר המדי and חומץ האדומי both have barley in them. This leads to a then-and-now story: Judean wine was once so strong that it needed barley to sour, and now the same is true of Edomite wine. Two פסוקים show that Israel and its enemies never thrive at the same time. A ברייתא of רבי יהודה about tithing vinegar bought from an עם הארץ seems to clash with his own view on תמד, and the גמרא gives two answers. Next the גמרא explains זיתום המצרי, dyers' bran water, cooks' dough, and קולן של סופרים, and it debates whether that last item is a cosmetic paste or shoemakers' glue. Finally, it rereads רבי אליעזר's 'women's jewelry' as women's cosmetics and begins a supporting statement of רב יהודה in the name of רב, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דִּסְמִידָא,

[Refined bread is bread] of fine flour;

בָּשָׂר שָׁמֵן — דִּצְפִירְתָּא דְּלָא אִפְּתַח,

fatty meat — of a young female goat that has not yet given birth;

יַיִן יָשָׁן — עַתִּיק עַתִּיקֵי.

aged wine — very, very old [wine].

כׇּל מִילֵּי דִּמְעַלֵּי לְהַאי — קָשֵׁה לְהַאי,

Anything that is beneficial for this is harmful for that,
