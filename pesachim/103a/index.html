Pesachim 103a

פסחים ק״ג ע״א

Topic

The order of ברכות at קידוש and הבדלה, the order of spices and light, and a second ברכה on the כוס של ברכה

Overview

The גמרא finishes listing opinions on the order of the ברכות when יום טוב starts on מוצאי שבת. The ברכות involved are יין, קידוש, נר, הבדלה, and later זמן. שמואל's father asks רבי, who answers with רבי יהושע בן חנניה's order נהי"ק. רבי חנינא explains that order with a parable: first escort out the departing king, then greet the arriving governor. The גמרא then asks for the practical ruling. אביי and רבא both follow רב's order and argue only about where זמן goes, and the הלכה follows רבא: יקנה"ז. Next comes a story: רבא blesses spices before light at הבדלה and is challenged from a משנה. He answers that רבי יהודה's version of the dispute between בית שמאי and בית הלל supports him, and that רבי יוחנן reports the people's custom follows it. Finally, רב יעקב בר אבא asks why רבא says בורא פרי הגפן again on the כוס של ברכה. רבא's answer, a story about רב's students, continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְרַבָּה אָמַר: יַהְנֵ״ק.

And Rabba said: wine, havdala, candle, kiddush.

וְלֵוִי אָמַר: קְנִיָּ״ה.

And Levi said: kiddush, candle, wine, havdala.

וְרַבָּנַן אָמְרִי: קִינָ״ה.

And the Rabbis say: kiddush, wine, candle, havdala.

מָר בְּרֵיהּ דְּרַבְנָא אָמַר: נְקִיָּ״ה.

Mar the son of Ravna said: candle, kiddush, wine, havdala.
