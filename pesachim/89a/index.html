Topic

The failed attempts at a conditional פסח, the father's race to ירושלים, and the deadlines for joining or leaving a פסח

Overview

This amud opens in the middle of a discussion about five people who each brought a פסח, where one animal turned out to have a מום and nobody knows whose it was. The גמרא tries again and again to let them bring a new animal with a condition: if I still owe a פסח, it is a פסח; if not, it is a שלמים or a מותר הפסח. Each time, the גמרא finds a difference between the two offerings that blocks the condition: the חזה ושוק, the eating time, סמיכה, the number of blood placements, and pouring versus throwing the blood. The final answer is that pouring שלמים blood is valid only בדיעבד, never as a plan לכתחילה, so the condition cannot work. Next comes a משנה about a father who slaughters a פסח for whichever son reaches ירושלים first. The גמרא asks if this proves ברירה. רבי יוחנן answers that all the children were registered from the start, and the race was only to make them eager for מצוות. A precise reading of the משנה and a ברייתא support him. Last, a new משנה teaches that people may join a פסח as long as each gets a כזית, and may register or withdraw until the שחיטה. אביי explains that רבי שמעון extends only the time for withdrawing, until the blood is thrown, and a ברייתא confirms this.
