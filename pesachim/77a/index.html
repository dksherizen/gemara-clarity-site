Topic

Sources that קרבנות ציבור override שבת and טומאה, and whether the משנה fits רבי יהושע's view that blood and meat depend on each other

Overview

The גמרא explains why the משנה lists the goats of ראש חודש: we might have thought ראש חודש is not a מועד, and אביי's פסוק from איכה shows that it is. A ברייתא then gives the sources that קרבנות ציבור override שבת and טומאה. The source for the תמיד and the פסח is במועדו. The source for the other קרבנות ציבור is ״אלה תעשו לה׳ במועדיכם״. The source for the עומר and the שתי הלחם is ״וידבר משה את מעדי ה׳״. A צריכותא shows that each source is needed, because each offering has a feature the others lack. Next the גמרא sets out two assumptions. The first is that טומאה is only דחויה בציבור, so the ציץ is needed; only רבי יהודה holds הותרה. The second is that the ציץ does not make the impure eaten portions count as accepted within the offering; only רבי אליעזר disagrees, and even he does not permit eating the impure meat. On that basis it suggests that our משנה does not follow רבי יהושע, who holds that blood and meat depend on each other, against רבי אליעזר, who holds that blood alone is enough.

Opening lines

שְׂעִירֵי רָאשֵׁי חֳדָשִׁים אִיצְטְרִיכָא לֵיהּ,

The goats of the New Moons — it was necessary for him (to mention them),

סָלְקָא דַּעְתָּךְ אָמֵינָא

it could enter your mind to say

הָא לָא כְּתִיב בְּהוּ ״מוֹעֵד״,

'appointed time' is not written about them,

קָא מַשְׁמַע לַן

so it teaches us
