Topic

Using one cup of wine for two separate מצוות: the rule that we do not do מצות חבילות חבילות

Overview

This amud opens with רבי יוסי's ruling that after a meal that ran into שבת, one cup is used for ברכת המזון and a second cup for קידוש. The גמרא asks why one cup is not enough. רב ששת answers that two קדושות are not said over one cup, and רב נחמן בר יצחק explains that we do not do מצוות in bundles (חבילות חבילות). The גמרא first challenges this from a ברייתא where one cup serves both ברכת המזון and הבדלה, and answers that a person with only one cup is different. It then challenges from רב's order יקנ"ה for a יום טוב that falls on מוצאי שבת. It answers that since רב left out זמן, he must mean שביעי של פסח, when the wine has run out. That answer falls when אביי and רבא are shown to use one cup even on the first night, when there is plenty of wine. So the גמרא concludes that קידוש and הבדלה are one matter and may share a cup, while ברכת המזון and קידוש are two matters and may not. Finally, the גמרא turns to the dispute between רב (יקנ"ה) and שמואל (ינה"ק) about the order of the ברכות, a discussion that continues on the next amud.
