Pesachim 36b

פסחים ל״ו ע״ב

Open Pesachim 36b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Which foods and which kinds of מצה are valid for the מצוה of מצה: ביכורים versus מעשר שני, the אונן dispute, and what ״לחם עוני״ excludes.

Overview

This amud asks which foods can be used for the מצוה of מצה. The גמרא first shows that רבי עקיבא retracted an earlier derivation. A ברייתא then gives his final view: ביכורים are excluded by ״בכל מושבותיכם״, while מעשר שני is included by ״מצות״ ״מצות״. The difference is that מעשר שני can become permitted outside ירושלים through redemption, as רבי אליעזר teaches, while ביכורים never can. The גמרא then explains why רבי יוסי הגלילי does not exclude ביכורים with ״לחם עוני״. He holds like רבי שמעון that an אונן may eat ביכורים, and this leads into the dispute between רבי שמעון and the רבנן. Next, a ברייתא teaches that ״לחם עוני״ rules out boiled מצה and large cakes (אשישה), but not fine flour. The גמרא proves from a פסוק about דוד המלך that אשישה is something important, and records a disagreement over what the word means. The amud ends with בית שמאי forbidding thick bread on פסח, and that discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אוֹצִיא חִיטִּין וּשְׂעוֹרִין

I would exclude wheat and barley

שֶׁיֵּשׁ בְּמִינָן בִּיכּוּרִים.

since among their species there are first fruits.

תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר: ״מַצּוֹת״ ״מַצּוֹת״ רִיבָּה.

The verse states: "matzot", "matzot" — it included.

אִי ״מַצּוֹת״ ״מַצּוֹת״ רִיבָּה,

If "matzot", "matzot" included,

The steps (27)

  1. ChallengeWorry: maybe wheat and barley are excluded entirely
  2. ResolutionThe repeated word ״מצות״ widens the law
  3. ChallengeIf the word includes, then include first fruits too
  4. Resolutionרבי עקיבא changed his mind
  5. Proofברייתא: first fruits are excluded by 'in all your dwellings'
  6. Statementברייתא: second tithe is included by 'matzot, matzot'
  7. QuestionWhy include second tithe and not first fruits?
  8. ResponseSecond tithe can become permitted everywhere; first fruits never can
  9. Proofרבי אליעזר's source: impure second tithe is redeemed even in Jerusalem
  10. ProofThe word for carrying actually means eating
  11. Conclusionרבי עקיבא must have changed his mind
  12. ChallengeWhy doesn't רבי יוסי הגלילי use ״לחם עוני״?
  13. ResolutionHe holds like רבי שמעון that a mourner may eat first fruits
  14. QuestionWhat is the source for the רבנן's view?
  15. ResponseFirst fruits are compared to tithe
  16. Responseרבי שמעון compares first fruits to תרומה
  17. ChallengeBut joy is written about first fruits
  18. ResolutionJoy refers to the season, not the person
  19. StatementPoor man's bread excludes boiled מצה and large cakes
  20. QuestionMaybe only coarse bread is allowed
  21. ResponseEven the finest מצה is allowed
  22. ConclusionSo poor man's bread only rules out boiled מצה and large cakes
  23. QuestionHow do we know אשישה means something fancy?
  24. Responseדוד המלך handed out אשישה at a celebration
  25. Statementרב חנן בר אבא explains אשפר and אשישה
  26. Statementשמואל says אשישה is a jug of wine
  27. Statementבית שמאי: no baking thick bread on פסח
