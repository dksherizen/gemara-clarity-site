Topic

שמואל's rule linking חטאת and פסח, whether חצות or שחיטה decides when a lost פסח becomes a שלמים, and a new משנה on an animal unfit to be a פסח.

Overview

The גמרא is trying to fit שמואל's two-part rule into a known opinion. The rule says that where a חטאת dies, a פסח is brought as a שלמים, and where a חטאת grazes, a פסח grazes. Following רבי, it finds a grazing חטאת in רבי אושעיא's backup case, but in that same case a פסח becomes a שלמים, so the parallel breaks. It then says שמואל follows רבי שמעון, for whom these חטאות always die, and explains that שמואל really stated only one rule: whatever makes a חטאת die makes a פסח into a שלמים. שמואל's new point is to argue with רבי יוחנן: for שמואל, חצות, not the slaughter of the replacement, decides whether a lost פסח counts as pushed aside. A second version runs the discussion differently. It first tries to say שמואל holds like רבה (the slaughter decides), rejects that because of רבי יוחנן's dispute, and concludes that שמואל holds like רבי, with חצות as the deciding moment. The amud ends with a new משנה: an animal that could never be a valid פסח, such as a female or a two-year-old male, grazes until blemished, is sold, and its money buys a voluntary שלמים.

Like Rabbi Oshaya.

If one separated two sin-offerings as a guarantee —

מִתְכַּפֵּר בְּאַחַת מֵהֶן,

he gains atonement with one of them,
