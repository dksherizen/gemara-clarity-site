Topic

The source and limits of טומאת התהום for the תמיד, the נזיר and the פסח, and where an impure פסח is burned

Overview

The amud opens by rejecting a קל וחומר that would allow טומאת התהום for a כהן bringing communal offerings, because a קל וחומר cannot be built on a הלכה למשה מסיני. רבא instead learns the leniency for the תמיד from a גזירה שוה of "מועדו" from the פסח. The גמרא then looks for a verse that teaches טומאת התהום itself: רבי אלעזר points to "עליו" by the נזיר, and רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש point to "לכם" and "דרך" by the פסח. A ברייתא says that if even one person anywhere knew of the corpse, it is not טומאת התהום, and none of the three readings fits that test. So the גמרא concludes that טומאת התהום is a received הלכה and the verses are only an אסמכתא. מר בר רב אשי's ruling on whether the ציץ helps when the impurity was known before the זריקה is corrected to say that it does help, and the גמרא then studies the ברייתא about a corpse found across a road, with its many limits. A new משנה explains where an impure or leftover פסח is burned, and רבי יוסי בר חנינא says the public burning is meant to embarrass careless owners. The amud ends as the גמרא begins a contradiction from meat carried out of ירושלים, which continues on the next amud.

Is it not logical

שֶׁהוּתְּרָה לוֹ טוּמְאַת הַתְּהוֹם!

that impurity of the deep is permitted for him!

אָמְרִי: וּמִי דָּיְינִינַן

They say: And do we derive

קַל וָחוֹמֶר מֵהֲלָכָה?

an a fortiori inference from a halakha (from Sinai)?
