Topic

Harmful spirits, dangerous shadows, amulets, and everyday habits that cause harm (Pesachim 111b)

Overview

This amud continues the גמרא's list of everyday dangers from רוחות and שדים. It opens with dangers connected to a palm tree, then lists dangerous shadows. Two general rules follow: many branches or hard wood make a shadow dangerous, with the כרו משא tree as an exception. Next the גמרא names the demons found in different places and explains that the names, and even their number, matter for writing a קמיע. Stories of a blind demon and of sixty demons in a sorb tree show these points. Then it describes the two קטב demons, tells how אביי protected a student from one, and gives the season and places where they are found. The amud ends with practical warnings from רב יוסף and others about habits that harm eyesight, bring poverty, or cause fear.
