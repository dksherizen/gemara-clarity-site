Pesachim 98b

פסחים צ״ח ע״ב

Open Pesachim 98b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Mixed-up and lost קרבנות פסח: מביאין קדשים לבית הפסול, the agent sent to find a lost פסח, and swapping registrations for mixed-up פסחים

Overview

The amud opens with the גמרא on a פסח that got mixed up with בכורות. It asks how רבי שמעון can let the whole mixture be eaten under the stricter rules of a פסח, since that shrinks the בכור's eating time. The answer is that רבי שמעון holds מביאין קדשים לבית הפסול, as he rules when an אשם is mixed with שלמים. The רבנן forbid this, so רבא gives their solution: wait for a blemish, transfer the פסח's holiness onto a fat animal, and eat the rest as a בכור בעל מום. A new משנה then deals with a group that lost its פסח and sent one member to find it while they slaughtered a replacement. Who eats from which animal depends on who told whom to slaughter on their behalf and on which animal was slaughtered first. When the order is unknown, the animal in doubt is burned, but no one needs פסח שני. The משנה then explains how groups, and also two individuals, whose פסחים got mixed up can trade members and make an 'if it's ours, if it's yours' declaration. The גמרא begins with a ברייתא on the agent case that matches the משנה.

Opening lines

גְּמָ׳ וְהָא קָא מַיְיתֵי

Gemara: But he is bringing

קָדָשִׁים לְבֵית הַפְּסוּל?

holy offerings to a state of disqualification?

רַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן לְטַעְמֵיהּ,

Rabbi Shimon follows his own reasoning,

דְּאָמַר: מְבִיאִין קָדָשִׁים לְבֵית הַפְּסוּל.

for he said: we may bring holy offerings to a state of disqualification.
