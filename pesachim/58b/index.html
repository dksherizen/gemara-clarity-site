Pesachim 58b

פסחים נ״ח ע״ב

Topic

The time of the afternoon תמיד on שבת and ערב פסח (אביי, רבא, רבה בר עולא), and the rule that the two תמידים open and close the day's offerings

Overview

The גמרא continues the dispute between רבי ישמעאל and רבי עקיבא about when the afternoon תמיד is brought on שבת. Following רבא's reading, it asks why רבי עקיבא does not bring the תמיד at six and a half hours. It answers that the מוספין (six hours) and the בזיכין (seven hours) must come first, which pushes the תמיד to seven and a half. רבה בר עולא objects that the ברייתא never mentions ערב פסח. He rereads it as an argument about an ordinary שבת, so our משנה about ערב פסח fits everyone, and the argument is about a גזירה on שבת because of the weekday's נדרים and נדבות. Two more ברייתות then test אביי and רבא, and each אמורא defends himself by rereading one phrase. Next a ברייתא teaches that nothing comes before the morning תמיד (רבא: from 'העולה') and nothing comes after the afternoon תמיד. For that second rule, רבא rejects אביי's proof and reads 'השלמים' as 'השלם': all offerings are completed by the afternoon תמיד. The amud ends with a ברייתא giving the ערב פסח order: תמיד, פסח, קטורת, then נרות. The discussion of that ברייתא continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אִי לָא חָיְישִׁינַן,

If we are not concerned,

נִיעְבְּדֵיהּ בְּשֵׁשׁ וּמֶחְצָה?

let him offer it at six and a half hours!

קָא סָבַר:

He holds:

מוּסָפִין קוֹדְמִין לְבָזִיכִין.

the additional offerings precede the bowls of frankincense.
