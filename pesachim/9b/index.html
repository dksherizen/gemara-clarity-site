Topic

Doubts about חמץ moved by animals: the נפל in the pit, hiding leftover חמץ (אביי, רבא, רב מרי), and mouse cases compared to קבוע, רוב, and שאני אומר.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a ברייתא: a woman threw a נפל into a pit, a כהן looked in, and the חכמים ruled him טהור because weasels and martens (חולדה וברדלס) live there. The גמרא asks whether this means a ספק overrides a ודאי, and it gives two answers. Either it is ספק against ספק, because maybe it was not a real נפל, or it is ודאי against ודאי, because such animals certainly drag food away. Next, the גמרא asks why the next משנה says to hide leftover חמץ if we do not worry about a חולדה. אביי splits the 13th from the 14th of ניסן. רבא rejects this ('is the marten a prophet?') and says we hide it only so a חולדה won't take it in front of us, and a ברייתא supports him. רב מרי gives another reason: one might put down ten pieces and find nine. Finally, the גמרא matches cases of mice taking מצה or חמץ to known rules: קבוע (the nine stores), פירש (follow the majority), and שאני אומר (the two baskets). It begins to push back on the last comparison, and that pushback continues on the next amud.
