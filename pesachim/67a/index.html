Topic

How a communal פסח is brought בטומאה (only for טומאת מת), whether a מצורע gets מלקות for entering a forbidden camp, and the three camps for impure people

Overview

This amud opens with the end of ריש לקיש's proof that when most of the people are טמא מת, the קרבן פסח is still brought בטומאה. He reads the extra mention of זבין and מצורעים in the verse about sending impure people out of the camp as hinting to a פסח הבא בטומאה. אביי shows that the same method would let זבין take part, which a משנה forbids. So אביי goes back to רבי יוחנן's verse. There, the word איש teaches that an individual is pushed off to פסח שני. The word לנפש teaches that a ציבור brings the פסח בטומאה only when the impurity is טומאת מת. Next, רב חסדא rules that a מצורע who enters a forbidden camp gets no מלקות, because the Torah turned his לאו into an עשה. A ברייתא says he gets ארבעים, and the גמרא answers that this is a dispute between רבי יהודה and רבי שמעון, with רב חסדא following רבי שמעון. Finally, the גמרא tests רבי שמעון's claim that a זב is more severe than a טמא מת and a טמא שרץ. The answer to the last challenge continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא יֵשׁ לְךָ שָׁעָה

Rather, you have a time

שֶׁזָּבִין וּמְצוֹרָעִין מִשְׁתַּלְּחִין

when zavin and lepers are sent out

וְאֵין טְמֵאֵי מֵתִים מִשְׁתַּלְּחִין,

but those impure from a corpse are not sent out,

וְאֵיזֶה זֶה —

and which time is this?
