Topic

Testing רבה's principle of הואיל against plowing on יום טוב and against kindling in the five מלקות case, and whether מוקצה is a Torah prohibition.

Overview

The גמרא is testing רבה's principle of הואיל: an act on יום טוב is not punished if it could have served a permitted יום טוב need. First it quotes a ברייתא that counts plowing on יום טוב as punishable, even though the loosened dirt could be used for כיסוי הדם. The גמרא tries several kinds of ground: hard clods, then rock-hard צונמא, then rock above loose soil. Each answer is knocked down. מר בר רב אשי finally answers that the ground was moist earth (מתונתא), which can be planted but cannot cover blood. Next אביי brings a ברייתא that gives five sets of מלקות for cooking גיד הנשה in milk on יום טוב, one of them for kindling. רבה first replaces kindling with נבילה, but רבי חייא's count rules that out. So he replaces it with using עצי מוקצה, and he argues that מוקצה is a Torah prohibition. אביי then challenges רבה from רבה's own earlier question about a meadow lamb used for the תמיד, quoting a word-by-word דרשה on a verse in יחזקאל that continues on the next עמוד.

a priest and a nazirite, over a place of impurity.
