Pesachim 41a

פסחים מ״א ע״א

Open Pesachim 41a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

חרוסת and חמץ; the ban on boiling the פסח, and what counts as נא, raw, and צלי אש

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a discussion from the previous amud about flour mixed into חרוסת or mustard. רב כהנא holds that only the mustard case is disputed, and with חרוסת everyone agrees it must be burned. רב הונא disagrees with him. רב אשי tries to support רב כהנא from שמואל's ruling about vinegar, but the גמרא rejects that proof. Next, the גמרא turns to the משנה's rule against boiling the פסח. The רבנן learn that every liquid is forbidden through a קל וחומר, while רבי learns it from the words ״וּבָשֵׁל מְבֻשָּׁל״. The two sources lead to a practical difference about צלי קדר, meat cooked in a pot in its own juices. The גמרא then explains why boiling after roasting still makes one liable: רב כהנא says the ברייתא follows רבי יוסי, and עולא says it is a special rule from the פסוק. Two more ברייתות teach that charred meat and raw meat are not included in the לאו, although raw meat is still forbidden by the command of צלי אש. רב חסדא rules that boiling the פסח in the hot springs of טבריא breaks only the command of צלי אש. Finally, רבא begins a ruling about eating the פסח half-roasted, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אַחֲרוֹסֶת קָאָמַר מָר,

Was it about charoset that the Master said it,

אוֹ אַחַרְדָּל קָאָמַר מָר?

or was it about mustard that the Master said it?

אֲמַר לֵיהּ:

He said to him:

לְמַאי נָפְקָא מִינַּהּ?

What practical difference does it make?

The steps (30)

  1. QuestionWas the ruling about חרוסת or about mustard?
  2. QuestionWhat difference does it make?
  3. ResponseThe difference comes from רב כהנא's view
  4. Responseרב הונא does not hold like רב כהנא
  5. Statementרב אשי sides with רב כהנא

  10. Responseרבי learns it from the words ״וּבָשֵׁל מְבֻשָּׁל״

  13. QuestionWhat do the רבנן learn from the doubled phrase?
  14. ResponseThe phrase teaches liability for boiling before or after roasting
  15. ChallengeWhy is one liable for boiling meat that was already roasted?
  16. Resolutionרב כהנא: the ברייתא follows רבי יוסי
  17. ProofProof from רבי יוסי's view on a boiled מצה wafer
  18. Resolutionעולא: Even רבי מאיר agrees, because the verse says boiled in any way
  19. StatementA ברייתא: roasting the פסח fully is not forbidden
  20. QuestionWhat case is the baraita talking about?
  21. Responseרב אשי: He charred it
  22. StatementA ברייתא: eating the פסח raw is not included in this prohibition
  23. StatementRaw meat is still forbidden because of 'roasted with fire'
  24. QuestionWhat counts as partially roasted?
  25. Responseרב defines it as half-roasted, using a Persian word
  26. StatementCooking in hot springs: exempt on שבת, liable for the פסח
  27. ChallengeHot springs are not fire for the פסח either
  28. Resolutionרבא: liable for not roasting it with fire
  29. StatementAnother tradition reports רב חסדא's ruling with the explanation built in
  30. Statementרבא begins a new ruling about eating the פסח half-roasted
