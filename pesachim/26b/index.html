Pesachim 26b

פסחים כ״ו ע״ב

Open Pesachim 26b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Unintended benefit from forbidden things (פרה אדומה, אבידה, כלאים), and an oven heated with forbidden fuel (זה וזה גורם)

Overview

This amud finishes the debate between אביי and רבא about benefit a person does not intend. First the גמרא sets aside the case of the threshing פרה אדומה: a bird resting on the פרה leaves it valid, but a male animal mounting it makes it invalid. רב פפא explains from the כתיב (עָבַד) and the קרי (עֻבַּד) of the פסוק that work disqualifies the animal only when the owner is pleased with it. A proof from a lost object (אבידה) that may not be spread out in front of guests is rejected, because there the worry is damage from an evil eye or thieves. Then the משנה about merchants carrying כלאים garments shows that avoidable but unintended benefit is permitted, and the first version of רבא is refuted. The גמרא next turns to the משנה's words about heating an oven, and brings a ברייתא: an oven heated with ערלה or כלאי הכרם fuel must be smashed if new and cooled if old. רבי forbids bread baked in it and חכמים permit it. A contradicting ברייתא is first explained as רבי versus רבנן. That answer is rejected and replaced with רבי אליעזר versus רבנן, based on the rule of זה וזה גורם. The search for which ruling of רבי אליעזר is meant, starting with his ruling about leaven, continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הָא לָא דָּמְיָא אֶלָּא לְהָא:

This is comparable only to that:

שָׁכַן עָלֶיהָ עוֹף — כְּשֵׁירָה,

If a bird rested on it, it is valid,

עָלָה עָלֶיהָ זָכָר — פְּסוּלָה.

If a male animal mounted it, it is invalid.

מַאי טַעְמָא?

What is the reason?
