Pesachim 3b

פסחים ג׳ ע״ב

Learn Pesachim 3b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Pesachim 3b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Clean versus concise language in the תורה, and stories showing the value of careful, respectful speech.

Overview

The גמרא is in the middle of a discussion about how the תורה chooses clean, respectful wording. It finishes explaining why משה's wife is described as 'riding,' then asks the same about אביגיל and gives three answers: fear of the night, fear of דוד, or fear of the steep mountain. Next it asks why the תורה ever writes the blunt word טמא, and sets a rule: clean wording is used only when it is no longer than the blunt wording; otherwise shortness wins, as רב הונא taught, 'always teach your student in a short way.' It tests the rule on אביגיל's verse and answers that רכבת is spelled without a ו, so it is shorter. The גמרא then brings stories about speech: רב refuses to speak with a student who used a crude image, and a student who asked his question cleanly is promised he will become a halachic authority. A כהן who compared his portion to a lizard's tail is found to be flawed, and the גמרא explains how this fits the rule that a serving כהן is not investigated. A story follows of רבי יהודה בן בתירא exposing a gentile who ate the קרבן פסח. The amud ends with two stories about delivering bad news gently: the death of רב כהנא, and יוחנן חקוקאה's poorly chosen hint about the wheat crop.

Opening lines

מִשּׁוּם בָּנָיו —

Because of his children —

אוֹרְחָא הוּא.

it is the normal way.

וְהָכְתִיב:

But isn't it written:

״וְהִיא רֹכֶבֶת עַל הַחֲמוֹר״!

"And she was riding on the donkey"!
