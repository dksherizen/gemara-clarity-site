Topic

Women and the קרבן פסח on פסח ראשון and פסח שני, women's obligation in מצה, and the start of the משנה about an אונן eating the פסח.

Overview

This amud opens with the last word of the previous discussion, פריצותא. The גמרא then quotes a full ברייתא with three opinions about women and the קרבן פסח: may we slaughter for a woman alone, only let her join a group (טפילה), or not slaughter for her at all, on פסח ראשון and on פסח שני? The גמרא explains how רבי יהודה, רבי יוסי, and רבי שמעון each read the פסוקים, especially 'במכסת נפשות', 'האיש ההוא', and 'ככל חקת הפסח'. It then searches for the exact 'איש' verse רבי שמעון uses and settles on 'מאיש לפי אכלו', which teaches two laws. Next, after fixing its wording, the גמרא shows that רבי אלעזר's ruling about women and the two פסחים follows רבי יהודה. רבי יוחנן teaches that a פסח group should not be made up only of גרים. A ברייתא about פסח, מצה and מרור is reread so that it fits רבי אלעזר's rule that women are obligated to eat מצה by Torah law. Finally, a new משנה rules that an אונן may eat his קרבן פסח at night but not other קדשים, and begins a case that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

פְּרִיצוּתָא.

[Because of] immorality.

גּוּפָא:

The matter itself:

אִשָּׁה בָּרִאשׁוֹן — שׁוֹחֲטִין עָלֶיהָ בִּפְנֵי עַצְמָהּ,

A woman on the first [פסח]: we slaughter on her behalf by herself,

וּבַשֵּׁנִי עוֹשִׂין אוֹתָהּ טְפֵילָה לַאֲחֵרִים,

and on the second we make her secondary to others,
