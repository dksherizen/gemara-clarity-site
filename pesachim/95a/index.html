Topic

How פסח שני differs from פסח ראשון, and which laws of the first פסח carry over to the second.

Overview

The משנה lists how פסח שני differs from פסח ראשון: on פסח שני you may own חמץ, and no הלל is said at the meal. It also lists what the two share: הלל at the slaughter, eating the meat roasted with מצה and מרור, and overriding שבת. The גמרא brings a ברייתא showing that only the מצוות of the קרבן itself carry over to פסח שני, along with מצה and מרור eaten with it. The ban on breaking a bone sets this limit. איסי בן יהודה learns the same limit from the words 'יעשו אותו'. The גמרא then shows how each side uses the other's words: 'עצם' teaches that every bone is included, and 'יעשו' teaches that a פסח is not slaughtered for one person alone. A second ברייתא shows that positive מצוות, fixable prohibitions, and full prohibitions of the קרבן all apply on פסח שני. The גמרא then reads each phrase of the פסוק as both including and excluding: roasting and 'לא תוציא' are included, while removing חמץ and בל יראה are excluded, because a מצוה of the קרבן itself is preferred. The same analysis of 'עצם לא ישברו בו' begins at the end of the amud and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳

Mishnah:

מָה בֵּין פֶּסַח הָרִאשׁוֹן לַשֵּׁנִי?

What is the difference between the first Pesach and the second?

הָרִאשׁוֹן אָסוּר

The first is forbidden

בְּ״בַל יֵרָאֶה״ וּ״בַל יִמָּצֵא״,

under 'it shall not be seen' and 'it shall not be found,'

The steps (32)

  1. StatementFirst difference: owning חמץ
  2. StatementSecond difference: הלל while eating
  3. StatementWhat both פסח offerings share
  4. StatementOnly laws of the offering itself carry over

  6. ResponseMatzah and maror are stated explicitly

  8. ResponseBreaking a bone sets the limit
  9. Statementאיסי בן יהודה: the word 'it' is enough

  11. ResolutionOnce מצה and מרור were added, a broad reading became possible
  12. QuestionWhat does איסי בן יהודה do with the word 'bone'?
  13. Response'Bone' includes bones with and without marrow
  14. QuestionWhat do the רבנן do with 'they shall perform it'?
  15. ResponseNo פסח slaughtered for one person if others can be found
  16. StatementA ברייתא: פסח שני has no ban on owning חמץ
  17. QuestionWhere do we learn that prohibitions also carry over?
  18. QuestionWhere do we learn that a full prohibition carries over?
  19. ConclusionFrom the three examples, every similar מצוה is included
  20. QuestionWhat does 'מצות ומרורים' include?
  21. ResponseIt includes roasting over fire
  22. QuestionWhat does 'מצות ומרורים' exclude?
  23. ResponseIt excludes getting rid of חמץ
  24. ChallengeWhy not reverse the two?
  25. ResolutionA מצוה of the קרבן itself is stronger
  26. QuestionWhat does the general side of 'leave none of it until morning' include?
  27. ResponseIt includes the ban on taking the meat out
  28. QuestionWhat does the specific side exclude?
  29. ResponseIt excludes the bans on owning חמץ
  30. ChallengeWhy not switch which law is included and which is excluded?
  31. ResolutionA מצוה about the קרבן itself is preferred
