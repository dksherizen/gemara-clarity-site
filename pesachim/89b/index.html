Pesachim 89b

פסחים פ״ט ע״ב

Learn Pesachim 89b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Pesachim 89b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Limiting a big eater or splitting a חבורה, and money received for a share in a קרבן

Overview

The משנה rules that if a member of a פסח group adds an outsider to his own share, the group may give him just his portion, and he eats it with the outsider. The גמרא asks whether a group may tell a member with 'fine hands' (a big eater) to take his share and leave. Two proofs, from our משנה and from the משנה about the שמש by the oven, are each rejected. A ברייתא then rules clearly that the group may limit him, even at an ordinary friendly meal (סיבולת). A second version (איכא דאמרי) reads the same ברייתא to teach that a חבורה may split up only for a real reason. A story follows: רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע pressed רב פפא with these sources until רב פפא divided the bread, and later רב הונא joked about eating with רבינא. Finally, a ברייתא teaches that money taken for a share in a פסח or חגיגה is חולין, while selling an עולה or שלמים is void and the money goes to נדבה as a קנס. עולא then asks how the money's holiness can pass onto a lamb that is already הקדש.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳

Mishnah:

הַמְמַנֶּה עִמּוֹ אַחֵר בְּחֶלְקוֹ —

One who registers another person with him in his portion —

רַשָּׁאִין בְּנֵי חֲבוּרָה

the members of the group are permitted

לִיתֵּן לוֹ אֶת שֶׁלּוֹ,

to give him his own portion,

The steps (25)

  1. StatementThe משנה: adding another person into your share
  2. QuestionCan a group send away a member who grabs too much food?
  3. ProofTrying to prove the answer from the משנה
  4. RejectionRejecting the proof: adding a new person is different
  5. ProofProof from the waiter who ate by the oven
  6. RejectionA waiter is different: he was accepted to serve
  7. ProofA ברייתא openly allows limiting a big eater
  8. QuestionWhat does 'and not only that' mean?
  9. ResponseObviously by a פסח, and even by a friendly meal
  10. QuestionAn alternate version of the question: may a group split up?
  11. ProofThe 'fine hands' ברייתא shows they may split only for a reason
  12. StatementThe story: רב פפא eats four slices while רב הונא eats one
  13. Challengeרב הונא raises all the earlier objections
  14. Resolutionרב פפא deflects them as we did
  15. Challengeרב הונא cites the 'fine hands' ברייתא
  16. Resolutionרב פפא: that was about the קרבן פסח
  17. Challengeרב הונא cites the shared-meal clause
  18. Conclusionרב פפא agrees and divides the food
  19. StatementEating together with רבינא
  20. StatementA ברייתא on money for a share in a פסח versus selling an עולה or שלמים
  21. ChallengeIf the sale did nothing, why is the money taken?
  22. ResolutionIt is a penalty
  23. QuestionWhat does 'whatever amount' add?
  24. ResponseEven the overpayment is penalized
  25. ChallengeHow can holiness move onto something already holy?
