Pesachim 16b

פסחים ט״ז ע״ב

Open Pesachim 16b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Which blood counts as 'blood' for הכשר (making food susceptible to טומאה), and whether the טומאה of sacrificial blood is דאורייתא or דרבנן

Overview

The amud finishes a rule: only blood 'poured out like water' is מכשיר, so sacrificial blood caught for the מזבח is not. רב שמואל בר אמי challenges this from דם התמצית, which is poured like water yet is not מכשיר. רבי זירא answers that דם התמצית is not מכשיר even in חולין, because it does not count as 'blood' at all. רב שמואל בר אמי accepts this and supports it from 'כי הדם הוא הנפש': only blood that takes out the life is called דם. The גמרא then tries several proofs that sacrificial blood can become טמא מדאורייתא: a ברייתא about impure blood sprinkled on the מזבח, and a משנה about the ציץ. Both are pushed off as rabbinic טומאה that does not follow רבי יוסי בן יועזר איש צרידה. A ברייתא on 'ונשא אהרן את עון הקדשים' shows that the ציץ atones for טומאה, but רב פפא says it means impure קמצים, not blood. The amud ends by opening a new proof from the prophet חגי's question to the כהנים, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דָּם שֶׁאֵינוֹ נִשְׁפָּךְ כַּמַּיִם —

Blood that is not poured out like water

אֵינוֹ מַכְשִׁיר.

does not render produce susceptible.

מַתְקֵיף לַהּ רַב שְׁמוּאֵל בַּר אַמֵּי:

Rav Shmuel bar Ami strongly objected to it:

הֲרֵי דַּם הַתַּמְצִית —

There is the blood squeezed out after the main spurt
