Pesachim 107b

פסחים ק״ז ע״ב

Open Pesachim 107b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

When the ban on eating on ערב פסח begins, and which snacks and drinks are still allowed

Overview

The משנה says that on ערב פסח, close to מנחה, a person may not eat until dark. The גמרא asks which time is meant. Close to מנחה גדולה would be because of the קרבן פסח. Close to מנחה קטנה would be so that he eats the מצה with appetite. רבינא proves from a ברייתא about King אגריפס that the משנה means מנחה קטנה. The גמרא challenges the proof, then answers that the ברייתא teaches that even אגריפס, with his late mealtime, is bound by the ban. Next, a sage rules that light snacks are still allowed. This is supported by רבי יצחק's practice, by a ברייתא about a waiter serving intestines to the guests, and by a hint from a פסוק in ירמיה. Finally, רבא drank wine all day on ערב פסח to build his appetite for מצה. He begins to bring a משנה as proof that wine whets the appetite, and that משנה is quoted on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

וְאָתֵי לְאִימְּנוֹעֵי מִלְּמִיעְבַּד פִּסְחָא.

and he will come to refrain from performing the Pesach offering.

אוֹ דִילְמָא, סָמוּךְ לְמִנְחָה קְטַנָּה תְּנַן —

Or perhaps we learned 'close to the lesser מנחה'

וּמִשּׁוּם מַצָּה,

and the reason is because of matza:

דִּילְמָא אָתֵי לְמֵיכְלַהּ לְמַצָּה

perhaps he will come to eat the matza

  5. ProofUnder the מנחה גדולה reading, there is nothing special
