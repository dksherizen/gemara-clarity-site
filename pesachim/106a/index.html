Pesachim 106a

פסחים ק״ו ע״א

Open Pesachim 106a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The כוס של ברכה, the sources and text of night and daytime קידוש, and the time limit for making up a missed הבדלה.

Overview

This amud finishes a list of 'שמע מינה' lessons from a ברייתא about the כוס של ברכה. One cup can serve for two קדושות, and the ברייתא follows בית שמאי as רבי יהודה reports them. רב אשי says that טעמו פגמו is really the same lesson as the rule that the cup needs a שיעור, and three אמוראים were personally stricter about 'blemished' wine. Next, a ברייתא learns קידוש over wine from 'זכור את יום השבת'. The גמרא re-reads it so that the main קידוש is at night, and a second, daytime קידוש comes from the word 'day'. רב יהודה rules that the daytime קידוש is just בורא פרי הגפן, and a story about רב אשי in מחוזא shows this. Finally, the גמרא sets the time for a missed הבדלה: it can be made up until Wednesday, because the first three days count as 'after שבת', but without the ברכה on fire. The amud ends as רב ברונא begins a teaching in the name of רב, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וּשְׁמַע מִינַּהּ:

And learn from it:

אוֹמֵר שְׁתֵּי קְדוּשּׁוֹת עַל כּוֹס אֶחָד.

one recites two sanctifications over one cup.

וּשְׁמַע מִינַּהּ:

And learn from it:

בֵּית שַׁמַּאי הִיא,

it is the view of Beit Shammai,

The steps (21)

  1. ConclusionTwo sanctifications may be said over one cup
  2. ConclusionThe ברייתא follows בית שמאי as explained by רבי יהודה
  3. Rejectionרב אשי: two of the lessons are really one
  4. StatementTasting ruins the cup only because it lowers the amount
  5. StatementThree אמוראים who were strict about blemished wine
  6. StatementA ברייתא: remember שבת over wine, by day and by night
  7. ChallengeChallenge: night is the main time for קידוש
  8. ChallengeSecond challenge: a 'day' verse is used to prove night
  9. ResolutionCorrected reading: night is the base case and day is derived
  10. QuestionWhat blessing is said for daytime קידוש?
  11. Responseרב יהודה: just בורא פרי הגפן
  12. Statementרב אשי in מחוזא is asked to make the 'great קידוש'
  13. Questionרב אשי wonders what the 'great קידוש' is
  14. Responseרב אשי starts with the wine blessing and draws it out
  15. ConclusionAn elder drinks, showing the great קידוש was just the wine blessing
  16. StatementOne who missed הבדלה can make it during the week
  17. QuestionUntil when can late הבדלה be made
  18. Responseרבי זירא: until Wednesday
  19. ProofParallel from גיטין: the first three days count as after שבת
  20. StatementLate הבדלה leaves out the blessing over fire
  21. Statementרב ברונא introduces a teaching in the name of רב
