Topic

End of the Creation אגדה, the משנה on working on תשעה באב, and the גמרא on its twilight and washing rules

Overview

This amud first finishes an אגדה section. It ends the list of things created on the first ערב שבת with the garment of אדם הראשון. Then come two ברייתות: seven things hidden from people, and things הקב"ה built into the world because it needs them. Next a new משנה says that working on תשעה באב follows local מנהג. תלמידי חכמים refrain everywhere, and רבן שמעון בן גמליאל says anyone may act like a תלמיד חכם. The גמרא then discusses whether eating during the בין השמשות of תשעה באב is forbidden. שמואל permits it. רבא, also in the name of רבי יוחנן, forbids it. The גמרא works through several readings of רבי יוחנן's words 'תשעה באב is not like a תענית ציבור': work, נעילה, the twenty-four ברכות, and רב פפא's stringency. A ברייתא comparing תשעה באב to יום הכיפורים is explained as referring to doubt about the date of ראש חודש, and this supports רבי אלעזר's ban on dipping even a finger in water. A final challenge from washing rules on a תענית ציבור is answered by רב פפא on the next amud.

Opening lines

בִּגְדּוֹ שֶׁל אָדָם הָרִאשׁוֹן.

The garment of Adam, the first man.

תָּנוּ רַבָּנַן:

The Rabbis taught:

שִׁבְעָה דְּבָרִים מְכוּסִּים מִבְּנֵי אָדָם,

Seven things are hidden from people,

אֵלּוּ הֵן: יוֹם הַמִּיתָה,

and they are: the day of death,
