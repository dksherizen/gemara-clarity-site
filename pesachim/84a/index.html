Pesachim 84a

פסחים פ״ד ע״א

Open Pesachim 84a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Burning נותר on יום טוב, which soft parts of the קרבן פסח count as meat, and lashes for breaking a bone of the פסח

Overview

This amud begins by finishing the discussion of why leftover meat of the קרבן פסח (נותר) is not burned on יום טוב. רב אשי explains that יום טוב has both an עשה and a לא תעשה, and the single עשה of burning cannot override both. Then a new משנה teaches which soft parts of the קרבן פסח count as edible meat. רבה finds a contradiction inside the משנה and solves it by splitting it between two תנאים. רבא instead reads it as one rule: what boiling makes edible in a grown ox, roasting makes edible in a young kid. A ברייתא supports רבא. רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש then argue about sinews that will later harden, which turns on whether we judge by the present or by the end. רבי יוחנן is shown to have retracted and agreed with ריש לקיש. A second משנה rules that breaking a bone of a pure פסח earns forty lashes, while leaving over meat or breaking a bone of an impure פסח does not. The גמרא gives the sources for both exemptions and brings a dispute between the תנא קמא and רבי about how to derive the bone rule. רבי ירמיה starts to explain the practical difference, a פסח brought in טומאה, and his explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְלֹא מִילָה שֶׁלֹּא בִּזְמַנָּהּ,

and not a circumcision that is not at its proper time,

הַבָּאָה מִקַּל וָחוֹמֶר.

which would come through an a fortiori argument.

רַב אָשֵׁי אָמַר:

Rav Ashi said:

״שַׁבָּתוֹן״ דְּיוֹם טוֹב — עֲשֵׂה הוּא,

'Solemn rest' of a Festival is a positive command,
