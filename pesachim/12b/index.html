Pesachim 12b

פסחים י״ב ע״ב

Topic

How much do people mistake the time of day? Witnesses, the deadline for eating חמץ on ערב פסח, and mealtimes

Overview

The עמוד opens by finishing a discussion about witnesses who disagree on the hour of an event: people never confuse day with night, or morning (sun in the east) with afternoon (sun in the west). The גמרא then quotes the משנה where רבי מאיר and רבי יהודה disagree about how late on ערב פסח one may eat חמץ. It uses that משנה to challenge the error estimates of אביי, who answers that witnesses are careful while חמץ is for everyone. Next it tests רבא's larger estimates. It explains that the sixth hour is risky because the sun is overhead, and that the fifth hour is forbidden because of cloudy days, while the fourth hour is safe because it is everyone's mealtime. This leads to a ברייתא about who eats at each hour of the morning, which is reordered to match רב פפא. רב אשי then teaches that each תנא is consistent between עדות and חמץ, and that the earlier answers are real answers. Finally, רב שימי בר אשי rules that a disagreement about sunrise voids testimony, and the explanation of that ruling continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא בֵּין יְמָמָא לְלֵילְיָא

Rather, between day and night

לָא טָעוּ אִינָשֵׁי.

people do not err.

וּבְדִין הוּא דְּנִיתֵּיב לְקַמֵּיהּ טְפֵי,

And by right we should give even more (leeway) at the later end,

אֶלָּא שֶׁבְּחָמֵשׁ חַמָּה בַּמִּזְרָח,

except that at five (hours) the sun is in the east,
