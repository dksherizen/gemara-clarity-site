Topic

Intent for ערלים at the זריקה of the קרבן פסח: רב חסדא and רבה, their competing readings of a ברייתא on ״וכל ערל״ and ״זאת״, and רב אשי's objection

Overview

Someone slaughters the קרבן פסח for circumcised people, but intends that uncircumcised people (ערלים) get atonement through the sprinkling (זריקה) of its blood. רב חסדא says the offering is פסול, and רבה says it is כשר. The argument is whether intent for ערלים counts at the זריקה or only at the שחיטה. רבה brings a ברייתא that asks whether one ערל ruins the פסח for the whole group. The ברייתא tries comparing ערלה to טומאה and then to זמן, and each comparison can be countered, so it decides through the verse ״זאת״. In רבה's reading, ״וכל ערל״ teaches that only a fully ערל group disqualifies, and ״זאת״ limits even that to שחיטה, because intent about the eaters has no effect at זריקה. רב חסדא reads the ברייתא the opposite way: at זריקה even a partly ערל group disqualifies, because פיגול is fixed only at the זריקה. רב אשי rejects both readings, since ״וכל ערל״ might mean 'any ערל', and he begins his own explanation of the dispute, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שְׁחָטוֹ לְמוּלִין,

If he slaughtered it for circumcised people,

עַל מְנָת שֶׁיִּתְכַּפְּרוּ בּוֹ

on condition that there would be atonement through it

עֲרֵלִים בִּזְרִיקָה.

for uncircumcised people, through the sprinkling.

רַב חִסְדָּא אָמַר: פָּסוּל.

Rav Chisda said: it is disqualified.
