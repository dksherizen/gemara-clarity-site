Pesachim 55a

פסחים נ״ה ע״א

Open Pesachim 55a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Working on תשעה באב and on ערב פסח: יוהרא, custom versus prohibition, and finishing versus starting work

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier question: the תנא listed only the leniencies of תשעה באב. The גמרא then asks why רבן שמעון בן גמליאל and the רבנן seem to switch positions on יוהרא (showing off) between our משנה and the משנה about a חתן reading קריאת שמע. רבי יוחנן switches the names in one source. רב שישא keeps both texts and explains why each side is consistent. Next comes a new משנה about working on ערב פסח in יהודה and the גליל, and about the night before. The גמרא asks why one משנה calls it a custom and this one a prohibition. רבי יוחנן answers that the two משניות follow רבי מאיר and רבי יהודה. A ברייתא about replanting a stalk on the 13th raises a question on רבי יהודה, and רבא, רב ששת, רב אשי and רבינא each answer it. Finally, a new משנה gives רבי מאיר's rule that work begun before the 14th may be finished on it. The גמרא asks whether this applies to festival work, non-festival work, or both. A first proof from a small belt and hairnet is pushed off, and a second proof from רבי מאיר begins and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

תַּנָּא קוּלֵּי קוּלֵּי קָתָנֵי.

The Tanna is teaching a list of leniencies.

וּבְכׇל מָקוֹם תַּלְמִידֵי חֲכָמִים וְכוּ׳.

'And in all places Torah scholars [are idle],' etc.

לְמֵימְרָא דְּרַבָּן שִׁמְעוֹן בֶּן גַּמְלִיאֵל סָבַר:

Is that to say that Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel holds:

לָא חָיְישִׁינַן לְיוּהֲרָא,

we are not concerned about showing off,
