Topic

מעשר שני as מצה: רב אסי's ownership rule, ריש לקיש's question about חלה of מעשר שני, and the start of the תודה and נזיר loaves

Overview

The amud continues רב אסי's statement. רבי מאיר holds that מעשר שני belongs to Heaven, while חכמים hold that it belongs to the person. רב אסי applies this disagreement to מצה on פסח and to an אתרוג on סוכות. רב פפא objects: the Torah says 'yours' for dough and for אתרוג, but never 'your מצה.' רבא answers with a גזירה שוה on the word 'לחם,' which links מצה to חלה, so מצה must also be yours. A ברייתא about dough of מעשר שני is offered as possible support, and the גמרא explains why it is only a 'maybe.' Next, ריש לקיש asks whether חלה taken from מעשר שני dough can be used as מצה. The question is narrowed step by step and turns on the 'הואיל' argument, and רבא resolves it by ruling that all מעשר is one category. The amud ends as the גמרא begins to find the source for why חלות תודה and רקיקי נזיר do not work as מצה, and the verse comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַצּוֹת שֶׁל מַעֲשֵׂר שֵׁנִי,

Matzot made of second-tithe produce,

לְדִבְרֵי רַבִּי מֵאִיר —

according to the words of Rabbi Meir —

אֵין אָדָם יוֹצֵא בָּהּ יְדֵי חוֹבָתוֹ בַּפֶּסַח,

a person does not fulfill his obligation with it on Pesach;

לְדִבְרֵי חֲכָמִים —

according to the words of the Rabbis —
