Topic

Impure communities and פסח שני, and when the ציץ atones for impurity of the offering, the owner, or the כהן (including טומאת התהום)

Overview

This amud opens by finishing an earlier dispute: does only a פסח brought in טהרה push others off to פסח שני, or does one brought in טומאה do so too? Next, רבי מני בר פטיש rules on a community split into thirds: זבין, people who are pure, and טמאי מתים. The טמאי מתים lose both the first פסח and פסח שני, because the זבין are counted differently for each date. A new משנה then teaches that the ציץ makes up for impurity of the offering's blood or meat, but not for impurity of the person's body. The exception is טומאת התהום, a hidden impurity. The גמרא reads the משנה's wording closely and raises a contradiction from a ברייתא. רבינא and רבי שילא resolve it in two different ways. רמי בר חמא then asks whether the טומאת התהום leniency also covers the כהן. רבא tries to prove from רבי חייא that it does, and רב יוסף rejects the proof. The amud ends with a challenge to רב יוסף from רבי יוסי's ruling about a שומרת יום כנגד יום, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

טׇהֳרָה מְדַחֲיָא,

[A Pesach brought in] purity defers [others],

טוּמְאָה לָא מְדַחֲיָא.

[a Pesach brought in] impurity does not defer [others].

And the [other] Master holds —

even [a Pesach brought in] impurity also defers [others].
