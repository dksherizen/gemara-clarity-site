Pesachim 66a

פסחים ס״ו ע״א

Topic

Does the קרבן פסח and its preparation override שבת? The end of the מחלוקת between רבי אליעזר and רבי עקיבא, הלל's proofs to the בני בתירא, and the גמרא's analysis of those proofs.

Overview

This amud first finishes the משנה's debate between רבי אליעזר and רבי עקיבא. רבי עקיבא flips רבי אליעזר's קל וחומר and shows that the verse ״במועדו״ covers only the slaughter of the פסח. He concludes with a rule: anything that could have been done before שבת does not override שבת. The גמרא then brings a ברייתא about the time the בני בתירא forgot whether the פסח overrides שבת at all. הלל הבבלי proves that it does, with a גזירה שוה of ״מועדו״ from the תמיד and with a קל וחומר. He is made נשיא and rebukes them for not serving שמעיה ואבטליון. When he forgets what to do about a knife left behind before שבת, the people's practice of placing it in the animal's wool or between its horns reminds him of the הלכה. The גמרא then analyzes הלל's proofs. It finds that the תמיד's own source is ״עולת שבת בשבתו״. The קל וחומר is refutable, because the תמיד is תדיר and כליל, so הלל said it only as a rebuke. Finally the גמרא quotes the line about the knife in order to discuss it next.

Opening lines

אָמַר לוֹ רַבִּי עֲקִיבָא, אוֹ חִלּוּף:

Rabbi Akiva said to him: Or reverse it:

מָה אִם הַזָּאָה שֶׁהִיא מִשּׁוּם שְׁבוּת —

If sprinkling, which is forbidden only as a rabbinic rest-rule,

אֵינָהּ דּוֹחָה אֶת הַשַּׁבָּת,

does not override Shabbos,

שְׁחִיטָה שֶׁהִיא מִשּׁוּם מְלָאכָה —

then slaughter, which is forbidden as a Torah labor,
