Topic

The חזקה that houses are checked on the fourteenth, renting an unchecked house, and the Torah source for the חמץ deadline on ערב פסח.

Overview

Earlier, the גמרא asked whether a house on the fourteenth of ניסן is presumed already checked for חמץ. It first tries to prove this from the ברייתא that believes everyone, even women, slaves and minors, about removing חמץ. The proof says everyone counts as a חבר for בדיקת חמץ, just as a חבר's produce is presumed tithed. After several rounds of back-and-forth about the ברייתא's wording, the גמרא shows the ברייתא can still fit the view that houses are presumed checked. It explains that the ברייתא speaks of a house known to be unchecked. There these people are believed, because בדיקת חמץ is only rabbinic; by Torah law ביטול is enough. Next, an איבעיא asks whether renting out a house as 'checked' that turns out unchecked is a מקח טעות. אביי's statement shows it is not, since people are glad to do a מצוה themselves or with their own money. Finally, the גמרא quotes the dispute between רבי מאיר and רבי יהודה about when to stop eating חמץ and asks for the Torah source that חמץ is forbidden from the sixth hour. אביי compares two verses to include the fourteenth, the גמרא challenges that reading, and its answer continues on the next amud.
