Topic

The source and minimum amount for deliberate מעילה, three readings of the ברייתא's contrast, and why חמץ separated on פסח cannot become תרומה (״לו ולא לאורו״)

Overview

This עמוד continues a discussion from the previous עמוד, where רב חייא בר אבין explained a ברייתא to mean that for מעילה one is liable to death even for less than a כזית. The גמרא identifies the view that deliberate מעילה is punished by death as רבי's view. רבי אבהו explains that רבי learns it from תרומה through the shared word חטא. That link seems to require a כזית for מעילה, but רב פפא answers that רבי may measure תרומה itself by שוה פרוטה, like אבא שאול. The גמרא then concludes that רב פפא retracted a contrary statement of his own. Next, מר בריה דרבנא and רב נחמן בר יצחק each read the ברייתא's contrast differently: מעילה is stricter because one is liable even without intent for that object, or even as a מתעסק. The גמרא then turns to a ruling about תרומה and חמץ. חמץ separated as תרומה on פסח does not become holy, based on ״תתן לו ולא לאורו״. This is limited to grain that never had a שעת הכושר, and רב נחמן בר יצחק affirms that חמץ which leavened after being cut does become תרומה. The עמוד ends as a report from רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע begins; it continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

הֵזִיד בִּמְעִילָה בְּמִיתָה —

One who intentionally misuses sacred property is liable to death —

רַבִּי הִיא,

it is the view of Rabbi (Yehuda HaNasi),

דְּתַנְיָא: הֵזִיד בִּמְעִילָה,

as it was taught: one who intentionally misused sacred property,

רַבִּי אוֹמֵר: בְּמִיתָה,

Rabbi says: he is liable to death (at the hands of Heaven),
