Topic

When does a creditor acquire pledged property: retroactively (אביי) or from the due date on (רבא)? Challenges and proofs from רמי בר חמא's case, our משנה about חמץ, and a ברייתא of תנאים

Overview

This amud asks when a lender (מלוה) acquires property pledged for a loan. First, the גמרא finishes a case everyone agrees on: if the borrower consecrated the property, the lender redeems it with one extra דינר. The real argument is when the lender himself sold or consecrated it before the due date. אביי says the lender acquires למפרע (retroactively), so his act works. רבא says he acquires only מכאן ולהבא (from the due date on), so it does not. The גמרא challenges רבא from his own advice in a case of רמי בר חמא. There, רבא lets שמעון pay ראובן's orphans with land and then take it back, which seems to require למפרע. It answers that this works for a different reason: רבי נתן's rule that a debtor's debtor pays the original creditor directly. Next, the גמרא tries to prove למפרע from our משנה about a גוי who lent against a Jew's חמץ. It deflects the proof: the חמץ was deposited with the גוי. Finally, it suggests that תנאים argue about this question, but rejects the idea from the end clause of the same ברייתא.
