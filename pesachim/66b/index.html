Topic

Objections to carrying knives on the פסח animals on שבת, lessons on haughtiness and anger, and the source that the פסח and תמיד override טומאה.

Overview

On the previous amud, the גמרא told how the people stuck their knives into the wool and horns of their פסח animals, so the animals carried the knives to the עזרה on שבת. The גמרא now raises objections to this practice. Isn't it using קדשים for work? No, because like הלל they were only מקדיש the animals once they reached the עזרה. But how can anyone be מקדיש on שבת? A קרבן with a fixed time may be consecrated on its day, as רבי יוחנן says. Isn't leading a loaded animal מחמר? It is done כלאחר יד, and whether a מצוה permits breaking a שבות this way was exactly what הלל was asked; he forgot, and he told them to rely on the Jewish people. From הלל's forgetting, the גמרא teaches that haughtiness and anger make a חכם lose wisdom and a נביא lose prophecy, with proofs from הלל, דבורה, משה, אלישע and אליאב. Then the גמרא returns to the קרבנות and asks how we know the תמיד and פסח override טומאה. רבי יוחנן brings a proof from 'איש' in the פסח שני פסוק, ריש לקיש rejects it, and he begins a new proof from the פסוק about sending impure people out of the camp, which continues on the next amud.

מִיָּמָיו לֹא מָעַל אָדָם בְּעוֹלָתוֹ.

In all his days, no person misused his burnt offering.
