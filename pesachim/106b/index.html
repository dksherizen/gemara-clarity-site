Pesachim 106b

פסחים ק״ו ע״ב

Open Pesachim 106b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Whether קידוש is an interruption after washing, and whether someone who has tasted food (טעם) may still make קידוש or הבדלה

Overview

This amud opens in the middle of a story. Someone has cited a ruling in רב's name that one who washed his hands for bread should not make קידוש. רב יצחק בר שמואל בר מרתא rejects this. He testifies that he saw רב make קידוש over bread whenever he preferred bread, so קידוש is not an interruption after washing. The גמרא then turns to someone who ate before קידוש or הבדלה. רב הונא quotes רב that one who tasted food cannot make קידוש. On הבדלה, רב הונא in his own name says one who tasted may still make הבדלה, while רב אסי says he may not. In a story at רב אסי's house, רב ירמיה בר אבא makes הבדלה after eating, and רב אסי explains that he is following his teacher רב. Finally, two opposite reports of שמואל's view appear: one says tasting blocks both קידוש and הבדלה, and the other says it blocks neither.

Opening lines

הַנּוֹטֵל יָדָיו

One who washes his hands

לֹא יְקַדֵּשׁ.

should not recite kiddush.

אֲמַר לְהוּ רַב יִצְחָק בַּר שְׁמוּאֵל בַּר מָרְתָא:

Rav Yitzchak bar Shmuel bar Marta said to them:

אַכַּתִּי לָא נָח נַפְשֵׁיהּ דְּרַב

Rav is still alive

The steps (11)

  1. StatementOne who washed for bread should not make קידוש
  2. ChallengeWe have already forgotten רב's teachings
  3. Proofרב made קידוש over bread when he preferred bread
  4. StatementOne who tasted food cannot make קידוש
  5. QuestionWhat about הבדלה after tasting?
  6. Responseרב הונא permits הבדלה, רב אסי forbids
  7. Proofרב ירמיה בר אבא makes הבדלה after eating
  8. Challengeרב אסי's wife questions it
  9. ResolutionHe follows his teacher רב
  10. Statementשמואל: tasting blocks both קידוש and הבדלה
  11. StatementAnother report of שמואל: tasting blocks neither
