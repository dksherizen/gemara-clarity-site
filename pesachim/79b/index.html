Pesachim 79b

פסחים ע״ט ע״ב

Topic

Who brings which פסח when the community is half טהור and half טמא: רב vs. two versions of רב כהנא, tested against the משנה and three ברייתות

Overview

This amud deals with a community that is exactly half טהור and half טמא at פסח time. רב holds that each half brings its own first פסח, the טמאים בטומאה. רב כהנא has two versions: in one the טמאים go to פסח שני, and in the other they bring no פסח at all. The גמרא tests both views against the רישא and סיפא of the משנה, and each side reads the words 'רובו' and 'מיעוט' to fit its view. Next, the גמרא brings three ברייתות, one for each position. It then shows how each opinion explains away the ברייתות that disagree with it, mostly by asking whether women are counted for the first פסח and for פסח שני. For the remaining ברייתא, רב כהנא answers that תנאים argue whether half counts like a majority. Finally, the גמרא studies that ברייתא directly: if the טמאים outnumber the טהורים by even one, everyone brings the פסח בטומאה, because a קרבן ציבור is not divided. רבי אלעזר בן מתיא disagrees, and his verse comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

וּטְמֵאִין אֵין עוֹשִׂין

And the impure do not perform (the Pesach)

לֹא אֶת הָרִאשׁוֹן וְלֹא אֶת הַשֵּׁנִי.

neither the first nor the second.

בָּרִאשׁוֹן לָא עָבְדִי —

On the first they do not perform it —

דְּלָא הָווּ רוּבָּא.

because they are not a majority.
