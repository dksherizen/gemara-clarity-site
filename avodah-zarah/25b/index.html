Avodah Zarah 25b

עבודה זרה כ״ה ע״ב

Topic

Being alone (יחוד) with גוים: the concern of עריות for a woman, and safety rules against שפיכות דמים for a man traveling with a גוי

Overview

This amud first settles which case the משנה means when it forbids a woman from being alone with גוים. It is a case of three גוים where the man's wife is also there, because a Jew's wife guards him but a גוי's wife does not. The גמרא then asks why the משנה gives עריות as the reason and not the danger of שפיכות דמים. רבי ירמיה answers that she is an important woman whom they are afraid to kill. רב אידי answers that a woman's being a woman protects her from being killed. The גמרא shows where these two answers differ, and brings a ברייתא that supports רב אידי. Next it turns to a man alone with גוים and gives safety rules for traveling with one: which side to keep him on, staying higher on a slope, and naming a farther destination. That last rule is learned from יעקב and עשו and shown in a story about רבי עקיבא's students. A new story about רב מנשה begins at the end and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא בִּתְלָתָא.

Rather, [the mishnah is speaking] of three.

דִּכְוָותַהּ גַּבֵּי יִשְׂרָאֵל,

The matching case regarding Jews:

בִּפְרוּצִים מִי שְׁרֵי?

with immoral people, is it permitted?

וְהָתְנַן: ״אֲבָל אִשָּׁה אַחַת

But didn't we learn: 'But one woman'
