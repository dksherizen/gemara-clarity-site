Topic

Medical remedies (hemorrhoids, ear pain) and which dangerous conditions, especially eye problems, may be treated on שבת

Overview

This עמוד opens with practical medical advice. First comes a remedy for hemorrhoids, with backup cures for the deeper kind. Next is the cure taught to רבי אבהו for ear pain: lukewarm fluid from a goat's kidney, followed by a chain of fallback cures and a memory aid about moist and dry treatments. The גמרא then returns to healing on שבת. רבי חנינא allows putting the ears back in place, and there are two versions of which method is allowed. רב rules that a dangerously sick eye may be treated on שבת, and רבי יעקב reports from רב יהודה that this applies even if it needs grinding and carrying. A story about רב שמואל בר יהודה and שמואל's maidservant shows the source of the ruling. The reason given is that the eye is linked to the heart. רב יהודה then lists which eye problems count, and the עמוד ends with his warnings about bathhouses and hot and cold remedies, which finish on the next עמוד.
