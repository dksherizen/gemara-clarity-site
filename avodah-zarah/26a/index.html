Topic

The end of רב מנשה's story with the thieves, the משנה and ברייתא on a Jewish woman delivering or nursing a נכרית's baby (and the reverse), and how far the concern of איבה reaches

Overview

This amud finishes the story of רב מנשה. He tricks thieves by naming a far-off destination, and when they insult his teacher רב יהודה, he puts them under רב יהודה's ban. The ban works: the thieves fail for 22 years, and the one who will not have it lifted is eaten by a lion. A new משנה then teaches that a Jewish woman may not deliver or nurse a נכרית's baby. A נכרית may help a Jewish woman, but she may nurse only in the Jewish woman's domain. In a ברייתא, רבי מאיר forbids a נכרית midwife or nurse even when others are watching, because she could kill the baby secretly. The חכמים permit it when others are watching, and the גמרא explains why both cases had to be taught. רב יוסף then allows delivering a נכרית's baby for pay because of איבה (hostility). He tries to stretch this to delivering on שבת, to nursing, and to pulling someone out of a pit. Each time אביי answers that a believable excuse prevents איבה. The amud ends as רבי אבהו begins a related teaching before רבי יוחנן, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לְבֵי תוֹרְתָּא,

to Bei Torta.

פְּגַעוּ בֵּיהּ גַּנָּבֵי,

Thieves encountered him.

אֲמַרוּ לֵיהּ: לְאָן קָאָזְלַתְּ?

They said to him: Where are you going?

אָמַר לָהֶן: לְפוּמְבְּדִיתָא.

He said to them: To Pumbedita.

The steps (24)

  1. Statementרב מנשה outsmarts the thieves and curses them
  2. StatementThe ban takes effect on the thieves
  3. ConclusionThieves of בבל versus bandits of ארץ ישראל
  4. Statementמשנה: delivering a baby
  5. Statementמשנה: nursing a baby
  6. Statementרבי מאיר forbids midwifery in both directions
  7. StatementThe חכמים permit a נכרית midwife when others are watching
  8. Statementרבי מאיר says watching women are not enough
  9. ProofA story of a boasting midwife supports רבי מאיר
  10. RejectionThe חכמים say she was only bragging
  11. Statementרבי מאיר forbids nursing in both directions
  12. StatementThe חכמים allow nursing when others are present
  13. Statementרבי מאיר: poison on the breast cannot be seen
  14. StatementWhy the midwife case alone would not be enough
  15. ConclusionWhy the nursing case alone would not be enough
  16. ChallengeA ברייתא allows delivering a non-Jewish baby for pay
  17. Resolutionרב יוסף: pay makes it permitted because of hostility
  18. Statementרב יוסף wants to permit delivering on שבת for pay
  19. Rejectionאביי: she has an easy excuse about שבת
  20. Statementרב יוסף wants to permit paid nursing
  21. Rejectionאביי: she has an excuse whether single or married
  22. Statementרב יוסף wants to permit pulling a non-Jew out of a pit for pay
  23. Rejectionאביי: he can say he is busy
  24. Statementרבי אבהו recites a teaching before רבי יוחנן about not raising certain people
