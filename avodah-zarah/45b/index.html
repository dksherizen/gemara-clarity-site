Topic

A tree planted normally and later worshipped, and how each side reads the פסוקים about destroying idols

Overview

The גמרא explains that the תנא קמא and רבי יוסי הגלילי argue about a tree that was planted normally and only later worshipped: the תנא קמא permits it, and רבי יוסי הגלילי forbids it. It proves רבי יוסי הגלילי's view from the משנה's extra rule that 'anything with human handling is forbidden.' רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה also forbids such a tree, based on the פסוק 'burn their אשרים with fire.' The phrase 'under every leafy tree' only marks where idols are found, as רבי עקיבא taught. The גמרא then works out which פסוק each side uses for which law. For רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה, 'burn' covers a tree planted for worship from the start, which leaves 'hew down their אשרים' free to teach the later-worshipped tree. The רבנן use 'hew down' for רבי יהושע בן לוי's order: chop the idols, conquer the land, then destroy them fully. The amud ends with the doubled verb 'אבד תאבדון' and 'destroy their name,' which the two sides read in opposite ways, and with a ברייתא that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְהָכָא בָּאִילָן שֶׁנְּטָעוֹ

And here, regarding a tree that one planted

וּלְבַסּוֹף עֲבָדוֹ קָמִיפַּלְגִי,

and only afterward worshipped, they disagree

תַּנָּא קַמָּא סָבַר:

The first tanna holds:

אִילָן שֶׁנְּטָעוֹ וּלְבַסּוֹף עֲבָדוֹ — מוּתָּר,

a tree that one planted and afterward worshipped is permitted,
