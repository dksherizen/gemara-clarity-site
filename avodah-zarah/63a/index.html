Topic

When does a payment to a זונה count as אתנן, and a challenge to רבי יוחנן's ruling about שביעית

Overview

The גמרא asks why a payment given to a זונה before the act does not become forbidden as אתנן once the act happens. רבי אליעזר answers that she offered it as a קרבן before the act. The גמרא shows this works only in a middle case: he left the animal with her until the act, but let her acquire it right away if she needed it. רב הושעיא then asks whether merely consecrating the animal before the act is enough, and the question ends in תיקו. Next, two ברייתות contradict each other about a payment given after the act. רב חסדא explains that a specific named lamb is אתנן even if given later, while an unspecified lamb is not. The גמרא solves the problem of missing משיכה in one of two ways: either she is a non-Jew, or the lamb stands in her חצר and was set up as an אפותיקי. Finally, רב ששת returns to רבי יוחנן's ruling about borrowing produce during שביעית and begins an objection from a ברייתא about workers given a דינר; the objection continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לֵיחוּל עֲלַהּ אִיסּוּר אֶתְנַן לְמַפְרֵעַ!

Let the prohibition of a prostitute's payment take effect on it retroactively!

אָמַר רַבִּי אֱלִיעֶזֶר:

Rabbi Eliezer said:

כְּשֶׁקָּדְמָה וְהִקְרִיבַתּוּ.

When she went ahead and sacrificed it first.

הֵיכִי דָמֵי?

What are the circumstances?
