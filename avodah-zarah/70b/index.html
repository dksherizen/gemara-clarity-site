Avodah Zarah 70b

עבודה זרה ע׳ ע״ב

Topic

When a non-Jew or an עם הארץ may have touched wine or pure items, and when we assume they did not

Overview

This amud brings several real-life stories about wine that a non-Jew might have touched. רבא permits wine near a gentile girl holding froth, because she probably took it from the outside of the barrel. An army opened many barrels in נהרדעא. רב דימי reports that רבי אלעזר permitted such a case but is unsure why, and the גמרא defends one possible reason: the soldiers were hunting for money, not wine. A wine-shop owner gave her key to a gentile woman, and the בית מדרש ruled that she only handed over the job of guarding the key. אביי supports this from a משנה about keys and טהרות, which leads to a dispute between רב and רבי יוחנן about a courtyard split by a low partition. Each side is challenged from ברייתות about courtyards and roofs, and each is defended. The amud ends with a new משנה: soldiers who enter a city in peacetime make open barrels forbidden, but in wartime all barrels stay permitted.

Opening lines

הָהִיא רְבִיתָא דְּאִישְׁתְּכַח

There was a certain girl who was found

דַּהֲוָת בֵּי דַנֵּי,

that she was among the wine barrels,

וַהֲוָת נְקִיטָא אוּפְיָא בִּידַהּ,

and she was holding froth in her hand.

אָמַר רָבָא: חַמְרָא שְׁרֵי,

Rava said: The wine is permitted,
