Topic

Buying animals from gentiles for קרבנות, why רביעה disqualifies the פרה אדומה, and שילא's source for רבי אליעזר

Overview

Earlier, רבי פדת said that our משנה follows רבי אליעזר, and that the ברייתא allowing people to buy animals from gentiles for קרבנות follows the רבנן. The גמרא suggests that maybe the רבנן allow this only for an expensive פרה אדומה. It rejects that idea with two proofs: the ברייתא would then match no one, and the רבנן quoted a פסוק about all offerings. Next, the גמרא notes that everyone agrees a definite act of רביעה disqualifies the פרה. It tries to prove from this that the פרה is קדשי מזבח. That proof is rejected. First the גמרא suggests the פרה is special because the תורה calls it a חטאת, but that fails because of רבי שמעון's ruling on a יוצא דופן. The final answer is that a מום disqualifies the פרה, and 'corruption' (sexual sin and עבודה זרה) counts as a מום. Then the גמרא examines שילא's source for רבי אליעזר, 'the children of ישראל shall take.' It challenges that reading with רבי אליעזר's own story of buying stones for the אפוד from a gentile, and the story continues on the next amud.
