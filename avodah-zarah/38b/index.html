Topic

בישולי גוים and other foods of גויים: which are permitted and which are forbidden

Overview

This amud is about food that גויים cooked or prepared (בישולי גוים), and when it is still permitted. First the גמרא rules that food is forbidden only if a גוי did all of the cooking. If a Jew placed the food on the fire or turned it over, it is permitted, and רבינא applies this to bread, even when the Jew only stoked the coals. Next come disputes about salted fish and roasted eggs, and a story at the house of ריש גלותא where רב זביד rules like רבי יוחנן that a roasted egg is forbidden. A ברייתא lists simple foods of גויים that are permitted, and the גמרא explains what the bean called שיעתא is and how it was grown. Then come rules about date husks cooked in large or small pots, which depend on נותן טעם לפגם. רב ספרא challenges רב ששת's ban on cooked oil, and רבי אסי says boiled medium dates are forbidden. רב and לוי argue about the dish שתיתא, and in practice people were lenient. Finally, חזקיה and רבי יוחנן argue about benefiting from pickles of גויים that are known to contain wine. The answer to the last question comes on the next amud.
