Topic

Why גוים are suspected of bestiality, and when that suspicion applies

Overview

Our משנה says we may not leave an animal alone with a גוי, because גוים are suspected of bestiality. The גמרא asks why a ברייתא lets us buy animals from גוים for a קרבן without this worry. רב answers that a גוי protects his own animal so it will not become barren, and רב כהנא adds that with males it weakens their flesh. A גוי shepherd also holds back, because he fears losing his wages. That fear only works when the owner is another גוי who could catch him, so we may not hand our animal to a גוי shepherd. The גמרא then discusses gentile women: an animal following her around would expose her, but a dog would not, which is why a widow should not raise a dog. Next it explains why even female animals may not be left with gentile women, and traces the desire back to the זוהמא the נחש put into חוה, which ended for ישראל at הר סיני. Finally, two eyewitness stories show that the worry includes birds too.

Opening lines

גְּמָ׳ וּרְמִינְהִי:

GEMARA: And they raised a contradiction:

לוֹקְחִין מֵהֶן בְּהֵמָה לְקׇרְבָּן,

We may buy an animal from them for an offering,

וְאֵין חוֹשְׁשִׁין לֹא מִשּׁוּם רוֹבֵעַ,

and we are not concerned, not because of an animal that mounted a person,

וְלֹא מִשּׁוּם נִרְבָּע,

and not because of an animal that was mounted,
