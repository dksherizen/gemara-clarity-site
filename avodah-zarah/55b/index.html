Avodah Zarah 55b

עבודה זרה נ״ה ע״ב

Topic

The end of the משנה on helping with wine and bread made in טומאה, and רב הונא's rule that flowing wine can become יין נסך, defended through the earlier and later versions of the law.

Overview

The משנה finishes its rules about helping with wine-making. You may not harvest grapes with a נכרי, and you may not tread or harvest with a Jew who makes his wine in טומאה. You may carry barrels to and from the press with that Jew. A baker who works in טומאה follows the same pattern: no kneading or shaping, but carrying the finished bread is allowed. The גמרא then brings רב הונא, who says that once wine starts to flow, a נכרי's touch can make it יין נסך. Two challenges from our משנה are answered by saying it is talking about a plugged, full press where nothing flows. A third challenge, that the wine outside the pit is permitted, cannot be answered that way. So רב הונא says our משנה follows an earlier ruling, while his rule follows the later one. A ברייתא proves this: at first the חכמים allowed treading with a נכרי, but later they forbade it because of רב הונא's concern.

Opening lines

אֲבָל לֹא בּוֹצְרִין עִמּוֹ.

But one may not harvest grapes with him.

יִשְׂרָאֵל שֶׁהוּא עוֹשֶׂה בְּטוּמְאָה —

A Jew who makes his wine in ritual impurity —

לֹא דּוֹרְכִין וְלֹא בּוֹצְרִין עִמּוֹ,

one may not tread or harvest with him,

אֲבָל מוֹלִיכִין עִמּוֹ חָבִיּוֹת לַגַּת,

but one may carry barrels with him to the winepress,
