Avodah Zarah 47a

עבודה זרה מ״ז ע״א

Topic

Does worshipping a stalk of wheat forbid its flour for the altar? Comparing it to a pregnant animal used for bestiality, then rejecting that comparison. A new question follows: may a לולב be taken from a worshipped palm tree?

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a comparison: wheat stalks someone bowed to are like an animal that was already pregnant when it was used for bestiality, so the flour should be forbidden for מנחות. The גמרא rejects this, because a fetus stays an animal while wheat turns into flour, a new form. ריש לקיש then asks whether a לולב from a worshipped palm tree may be used for the מצוה. רב דימי reports the question differently, as asking whether something pushed away from a מצוה stays pushed away. A proof from covering blood (אין דיחוי אצל מצות) is rejected, and the question ends in תיקו. רב פפא asks whether parts of a worshipped animal may be used for תכלת or for the instruments of the מקדש. That question also ends in תיקו, according to the view that the main song is with the mouth. רבה asks whether water from a privately owned spring someone bowed to may be used for נסכים. Finally, a new משנה teaches that a fallen wall next to a house of עבודה זרה may not be rebuilt in place, and it begins the case of a jointly owned wall.

Opening lines

אֲבָל עִיבְּרוּ וּלְבַסּוֹף נִרְבְּעוּ —

But if they became pregnant and later were used for bestiality,

דִּבְרֵי הַכֹּל אֲסוּרִין,

all agree they (the offspring) are forbidden,

וְהָכָא נָמֵי

and here too,

כְּעִיבְּרוּ וּלְבַסּוֹף נִרְבְּעוּ דָּמֵי.

it is like 'they became pregnant and later were used for bestiality.'
