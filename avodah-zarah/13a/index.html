Avodah Zarah 13a

עבודה זרה י״ג ע״א

Topic

Whether causing benefit (מהנה) to עבודה זרה is forbidden: ריש לקיש vs. רבי יוחנן, רבי נתן's ברייתא, buying at a gentile fair, and the details of destroying a forbidden animal.

Overview

The amud asks whether it is forbidden only to get benefit from עבודה זרה (נהנה), or also to give it benefit (מהנה). ריש לקיש allows giving benefit. רבי יוחנן forbids it with a קל וחומר. The גמרא challenges ריש לקיש from a ברייתא of רבי נתן. In that ברייתא, a Jew facing an idol's tax break is trapped between benefiting and causing benefit, so goods from an idol's market must be destroyed. רב משרשיא answers that ריש לקיש follows the רבנן, who argue with רבי נתן, while רבי יוחנן holds they agree. A ברייתא that allows buying at a gentile fair seems to prove they argue. On the way, it teaches when a כהן may become impure to go to court, to learn תורה, or to marry, and the הלכה follows רבי יוסי. רבי יוחנן's side answers that רבי נתן speaks of a merchant who pays the idol's tax, and the other ברייתא of a homeowner who does not. The גמרא then defends the rule of maiming the animal against צער בעלי חיים, and begins comparing it with a ברייתא about הקדש nowadays, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֲבָל מְהַנֶּה שְׁרֵי.

but causing benefit is permitted.

וְרַבִּי יוֹחָנָן אָמַר:

And Rabbi Yochanan says:

אֲפִילּוּ מְעוּטָּרוֹת בְּפֵירוֹת נָמֵי אָסוּר,

Even if they are decorated with fruit, it is also forbidden,

קַל וָחוֹמֶר: נֶהֱנֶה — אָסוּר,

a kal vachomer: deriving benefit is forbidden,
