Topic

When may we sell something that a buyer might misuse? This covers sales during שביעית, sales to someone suspected of reselling to gentiles, and sales of weapons.

Overview

The גמרא brings a משנה in שביעית as proof that we may assume a buyer will use an animal in a permitted way. רבה rejects the proof by distinguishing cases where a person is commanded to let something rest. אביי (a field) and רב אשי (tools) knock down that distinction. רב אשי then gives a new rule: we assume a permitted use whenever one is realistic, and only then. Next comes a story: רבה sold a donkey to a Jew suspected of selling animals to gentiles. אביי proves from two ברייתות about כותים that one may not sell to someone likely to pass the item on to a gentile. A ruling of רב נחמן settles it, and רבה runs after the buyer to undo the sale, without success. The amud ends with רב דימי בר אבא's ban on selling weapons to a Jewish robber and a ברייתא about selling shields, where רב's explanation continues on the next amud.
