Topic

Identifying kosher fish products from a גוי: brine, salted fish, scales, and fish roe

Overview

This amud is about how to tell whether fish products bought from a גוי are kosher. First, the גמרא explains that brine in open barrels needs two כילבית fish, while sealed barrels need only one. Next, רב הונא and רב נחמן argue whether salted fish needs both head and spine to be recognizable, or just one of them. עולא limits their argument to dipping food in the brine, and רב פפא rules that the head and spine of every single fish must be recognizable. He explains that a ברייתא permitting a whole jug of pieces is talking about pieces that fit together. Then comes a story: רב הונא בר חיננא permitted a boatload of fish because he saw scales, while רבא forbade it, and two reports disagree about what exactly was permitted. רב חיננא בר אידי rules on a boat full of barrels where a כילבית was found in one of them. Finally, רב rules that fish innards and roe must be bought from a מומחה. The גמרא shows that non-kosher fish also have eggs, and רבא explains that an expert is needed when the eggs have melted. The amud ends by beginning a question on רבי דוסתאי's view, which continues on the next amud.
