Topic

An אשרה: sitting in its shade, walking under it, and planting beneath it (זה וזה גורם)

Overview

The משנה teaches rules about an אשרה, a tree that people worship. You may not sit in its shade, though if you did you stay טהור. You may not walk under it, and if you did you become טמא. רבי יוחנן explains that the ban on sitting reaches even the farther shade (צל צילה). A second version says the חידוש is that a person stays טהור even in the main shade. Walking under the tree makes a person טמא because some idolatrous offering is surely lying under it, and רבי יהודה בן בתירא learns from a פסוק that such an offering makes things טמא in a tent, like a dead body. For a tree hanging over a public road, you may pass under it if there is no other way; רב ששת still hurried past because an אדם חשוב must be extra careful. A new משנה discusses planting vegetables under an אשרה, and the גמרא asks whether רבי יוסי and the רבנן argue about זה וזה גורם. It resolves a clash with another משנה either by swapping the names or by saying the leaves' benefit is cancelled by the shade's harm. It then starts to question whether רבי יוסי really forbids זה וזה גורם, citing a teaching about ערלה, and that discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳

MISHNA:

לֹא יֵשֵׁב בְּצִילָּהּ,

One may not sit in its shade,

וְאִם יָשַׁב — טָהוֹר,

but if he sat — he is pure,

וְלֹא יַעֲבוֹר תַּחְתֶּיהָ,

and one may not pass beneath it,
