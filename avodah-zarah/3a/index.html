Topic

Whether non-Jews are rewarded for keeping the seven מצוות בני נח, and the nations' claim against ישראל before הקדוש ברוך הוא

Overview

This amud first finishes explaining what it means that הקדוש ברוך הוא 'released' the nations from the seven מצוות בני נח. The גמרא first says it means they get no reward for keeping them. רבי מאיר teaches that a גוי who learns תורה is like a כהן גדול, so the גמרא changes its answer: they get a smaller reward, like someone who is not commanded and does it anyway, as רבי חנינא teaches. Then the גמרא goes back to the story of the future judgment. The nations ask where ישראל actually kept the תורה. They reject God and heaven and earth as witnesses, until God brings witnesses from among the nations themselves. The nations then ask for the תורה again, and God gives them the easy מצוה of סוכה, even though this world is the only time for doing מצוות. They kick their סוכות away when the sun blazes, and the גמרא begins to explain on the next amud why that heat is not unfair.
