Topic

The kinds of אשרה trees, שמואל's ruling on a worshipped tree's new growth, and how to identify an אשרה.

Overview

The משנה lists three kinds of אשרה: a tree planted for עבודה זרה is fully forbidden, a tree cut back for עבודה זרה only needs its new growth removed, and a tree with an idol placed under it is permitted once the idol is removed. The גמרא explains that דבי רבי ינאי meant that even bending or grafting the tree does not forbid the whole tree; removing the new growth is enough. שמואל rules that bowing to a tree forbids its later growth. רבי אלעזר challenges him from the משנה, and the גמרא answers that שמואל follows רבי יוסי בר יהודה, not the רבנן. רב אשי suggests that maybe everyone forbids the new growth and the argument is only about the trunk. The גמרא shows how the משנה could still fit either side on that reading. A second משנה brings the argument between the חכמים and רבי שמעון over a tree with an idol under it, along with the story in צידן. Finally, רב and שמואל give signs for recognizing a tree of unknown status as an אשרה, and the הלכה follows שמואל.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳

MISHNAH:

שָׁלֹשׁ אֲשֵׁרוֹת הֵן —

There are three kinds of asheira trees:

אִילָן שֶׁנְּטָעוֹ מִתְּחִלָּה

A tree that one planted from the start

לְשֵׁם עֲבוֹדָה זָרָה, הֲרֵי זוֹ אֲסוּרָה.

for the sake of idol worship is forbidden.
