Topic

Defining the items in the משנה that may not be sold to עובדי עבודה זרה: איצטרובלין, בנות שוח, פטוטרות, לבונה, and the exception for selling a large חבילה.

Overview

The גמרא explains the hard words in the משנה's list of things you may not sell to an idol worshipper. איצטרובלין is first said to be תורניתא, but a ברייתא about שביעית rules that out, so רב ספרא and רבי אלעזר say it is cedar fruit. רבי יוחנן explains that בנות שוח are white figs and that פטוטרות means the fruits are forbidden only with their stems. ריש לקיש says לבונה means pure frankincense. A ברייתא allows selling any of these in a bundle of at least three מנים. אביי explains why possible resale is no concern: לפני עור only forbids helping the sinner directly. רבי יונה says a white rooster may be sold if the buyer just asks for 'a rooster.' The משנה seems to contradict him, and רב נחמן בר יצחק answers that the משנה is about a buyer who points to 'this one and that one.' A ברייתא supports this, and a clash about a son's feast is answered as a casual party. Finally the גמרא begins asking what 'without specifying' and 'with specifying' mean in the משנה, and this continues on the next amud.
