Topic

Whether משיכה (pulling an item) makes a real acquisition when a גוי is involved, shown through the ברייתא about a בן נח who steals less than a פרוטה. Then a new case where רב יוסף rules on a promise to sell land.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a proof that משיכה (pulling an item) is a real acquisition for a גוי. The proof comes from a ברייתא where a second בן נח who takes a stolen item from the first thief is also executed. Next come several sales cases. רב יוסף rules on a promise to sell land 'if I sell,' and אביי objects that no price was fixed. The גמרא then proves from a ברייתא about unloading produce that a sale is not final until the price is fixed. In the case of a promise to sell for one hundred followed by a sale to someone else for one hundred twenty, the הלכה follows רב יעקב מנהר פקוד: the extra money forced the sale, so the promise does not hold. The גמרא also rules on prices set 'as three assess' or 'as three say,' and holds that an agreed assessment stands. A new משנה follows about wine left in a funnel and about pouring wine from vessel to vessel. In its גמרא, רב הונא rules that a poured stream connects liquids for יין נסך. רב נחמן shows that the משנה in טהרות cannot be his source, and the גמרא begins a new proof from our funnel משנה, which continues on the next amud.
