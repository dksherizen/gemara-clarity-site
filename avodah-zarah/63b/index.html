Topic

Whether paying later for something forbidden counts as paying for the איסור, using the employer-and-workers ברייתא, followed by whether one may be paid to break barrels of יין נסך.

Overview

The amud starts in the middle of a ברייתא about an employer who feeds his workers. If he hands them a coin, he is not responsible for what they buy. If he says 'go eat and I'll pay,' he must worry about שביעית, מעשר and יין נסך. The גמרא infers from this that paying a debt later still counts as paying for the forbidden item. רב חסדא first limits the ברייתא to a storekeeper who regularly gives credit, but that answer is challenged twice. רבא then rules that a debt not tied to specific money is not forbidden. To explain why the ברייתא still worries, רב פפא says the employer paid a דינר in advance, and רב אשי says he handed over the food himself. Each of them reads the ברייתא's wording to fit his case. A new question follows: may someone be paid to break barrels of יין נסך? רב נחמן allows it and blesses him. The גמרא begins bringing a ברייתא about hoeing in a field of כלאים as possible support, and that discussion continues on the next amud.
