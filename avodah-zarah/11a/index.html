Topic

The story of אונקלוס the convert, אנטונינוס and רבי, and the burning done at the death of kings

Overview

This amud opens with a short line that closes the previous story: 'the bundle is separated.' Next comes the story of אונקלוס, a Roman who became a גר. The קיסר sends three troops of soldiers to bring him back, and אונקלוס wins each troop over. He uses פסוקים, then a parable about torches, then the meaning of the מזוזה, until the קיסר gives up. The גמרא then returns to אנטונינוס, using a דרשה to read 'two nations' in רבקה's womb as two great men, אנטונינוס and רבי, and settles a question about whether cucumbers are healthy. Back in the משנה, the גמרא first infers that רבי מאיר and the רבנן disagree about whether burning at a king's death is an idolatrous custom. A ברייתא shows that Jews burn items for kings, so the גמרא explains the argument differently. Everyone agrees burning is a sign of honor, and the only question is which deaths the idolaters celebrate. Finally, the גמרא studies that ברייתא: what may be burned or destroyed for kings and נשיאים, אונקלוס's burning for רבן גמליאל, and cutting animals' hooves. That last discussion continues on the next amud.

נִתְפָּרְדָה חֲבִילָה.

The bundle has come apart.

Onkelos son of Kelonimos converted;

שַׁדַּר קֵיסָר גּוּנְדָּא דְּרוֹמָאֵי אַבָּתְרֵיהּ,

the emperor sent a troop of Romans after him;

מַשְׁכִינְהוּ בִּקְרָאֵי, אִיגַּיּוּר.

he drew them in with verses, and they converted.
