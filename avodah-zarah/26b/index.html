Topic

Who counts as a משומד or מין and how they are treated, and whether a non-Jew may perform מילה on a Jew.

Overview

The amud opens with the end of a ברייתא: מינין, מסורות and משומדים may be lowered into a pit and are not pulled out. רבי יוחנן objects that the Torah includes a משומד in השבת אבידה, so he removes the word משומד. The גמרא explains that for רבי אבהו, someone who sins להכעיס is already a מין. רב אחא and רבינא then argue over whether a spiteful sinner is a מין or a משומד. A ברייתא about eating a flea is answered by saying that wanting to taste something forbidden counts as desire. The גמרא then explains that 'not raised' allows quietly removing a way out of the pit, such as a ledge, the opening or a ladder. A new ברייתא about מילה follows. רבי מאיר forbids a non-Jew to circumcise a Jew, while the חכמים allow it if others are watching. A contradiction from another ברייתא about a כותי and an ארמאי leads to swapping the names, then taking that swap back. The new answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְלֹא מוֹרִידִין,

and one may not lower them into a pit.

אֲבָל הַמִּינִין וְהַמָּסוֹרוֹת

But the heretics and the informers

וְהַמְשׁוּמָּדִים —

and the apostates

are lowered into a pit, and are not raised out of it.
