Topic

Business with those going to or from idol festivals and fairs, and making gentile wine containers usable

Overview

The amud opens by explaining that a Jew coming back from an idol festival (תרפות) is a problem because he has become attached to it and will go again. רב אשי limits a ברייתא that forbids him even on the way there to a מומר, who will surely go. A second ברייתא about a fair (יריד) permits business with a גוי both ways, but forbids it with a Jew on the way back, because his trip shows he went to sell idol items. ריש לקיש then limits the משנה's permission for those returning from a festival to people who are not traveling as a group. The גמרא turns to gentile wineskins: רב פפא and רב זביד each explain a ברייתא about a גוי tarring them, and רב אשי rejects רב פפי's extension to wine thrown into salt. A story about רב יצחק בר יוסף teaches that wineskins can be made usable by soaking them in water for three days, changing the water every twenty-four hours. This method works for ours and theirs, and for wineskins and jugs alike. A final ברייתא sums up the rules for gentile jugs, and a new question is opened that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דַּאֲבִיק בַּהּ,

Since he has cleaved to it,

מִהְדָּר הָדַר אָזֵיל.

he will go back to it again.

וְהָתַנְיָא:

But isn't it taught:

יִשְׂרָאֵל הַהוֹלֵךְ לַתַּרְפּוּת,

a Jew who goes to an idol festival,
