Topic

Pieces of an אשרה and a nest on it; found vessels with figures of the sun, moon and dragon, and רב ששת's list of forbidden images

Overview

The גמרא asks why fallen leaves of a worshipped tree stay forbidden, since shavings cut from an idol are permitted. רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע answers that an idol does not lose its status through its natural growth. ריש לקיש then challenges רבי יוחנן from a משנה that lets a person knock down a nest from an אשרה. The גמרא defends רבי יוחנן in two ways: either the bird brought the branches from elsewhere, or the permission covers only the chicks, not the nest. Chicks are permitted and eggs are forbidden, and רב אשי adds that chicks still needing their mother count as eggs. A new משנה rules that found vessels with figures of the sun, moon or a dragon must be thrown into ים המלח. אביי explains that people worship many things, but they only make images of these three in order to worship them. The גמרא then asks whether רב ששת's list of forbidden images is about making them or finding them, and tests each clause, with the discussion continuing on the next amud.
