Topic

מעילה in disqualified קדשי קדשים: שעת היתר לכהנים and why each case of the משנה is needed

Overview

The first משנה of מסכת מעילה asks whether מעילה (misusing holy property) still applies to קדשי קדשים that became פסול. It lists cases where the offering was ruined before it could be eaten: slaughter or collection of the blood in the south, slaughter or sprinkling at night, and slaughter with the intent חוץ לזמנו or חוץ למקומו. In all of these, מעילה still applies. רבי יהושע then gives the rule: if the offering ever had a שעת היתר לכהנים, there is no more מעילה, and if it never did, מעילה stays. The משנה gives examples of each side, such as meat left overnight, meat that became טמא, or blood handled by unfit people. The גמרא first asks why the south case even needs saying. It answers that we might have compared it to קדשים שמתו, but the south is a valid place for קדשים קלים, so the slaughter is still real. Next, the גמרא explains why every place and time case in the משנה is needed. Finally, it asks what wrong-intent offerings are still fit for, and answers that the blood service still takes effect on them by making them פיגול.
