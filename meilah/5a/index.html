Topic

Which stage of 'היתר' (שחיטה, זריקה, or אכילה) frees a disqualified קרבן from מעילה, and whether the משנה's wording can prove it.

Overview

The גמרא asks what 'היתר' means in the משנה that frees a later-disqualified קרבן from מעילה. There are three options: proper שחיטה, blood ready for זריקה, or meat permitted for אכילה. חזקיה says היתר שחיטה and רבי יוחנן says היתר אכילה. רבי זירא argues that the משנה fits neither. He first points to 'שלנה', reading it as blood left overnight unsprinkled, but the גמרא answers that it may mean meat left overnight after a proper זריקה. He then points to blood collected by פסולין and sprinkled by כשרים, where the משנה says מעילה still applies, so a valid שחיטה alone is not enough. רב יוסף objects with a משנה in זבחים about the blood of a disqualified חטאת and כיבוס. There, reading 'collected and sprinkled by פסולין' exactly leads to a result that contradicts the פסוק 'אשר יזה מדמה'. So that wording must be 'לאו דוקא', not exact, and the explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אוֹ הֶיתֵּר זְרִיקָה שָׁנִינוּ,

Or was it fitness for sprinkling that we learned,

אוֹ הֶיתֵּר אֲכִילָה שָׁנִינוּ?

or was it fitness for eating that we learned?

חִזְקִיָּה אָמַר: הֶיתֵּר שְׁחִיטָה שָׁנִינוּ,

Rabbi Yochanan says: we learned fitness of eating.
