Me’ilah 4a

מעילה ד׳ ע״א

Topic

Does wrong intent at שחיטה alone create full פיגול? Challenges from עולא, אילפא and the תודה loaves, and a suggested proof for רב גידל from a ברייתא about מעילה.

Overview

This amud continues a discussion of רב גידל's view: wrong intent during שחיטה alone does not create full פיגול. The גמרא first challenges this from עולא's קל וחומר about the קמיצה, and answers that עולא meant only an איסורא leading toward פיגול. Next, רבינא challenges רב אשי from אילפא, whose case of two סימנים cut with different wrong intentions seems to show that שחיטה alone makes פיגול. רב אשי answers that the final status is decided only once the blood is sprinkled. The גמרא then asks why the תודה loaves become holy at the שחיטה, and explains that 'קדש' there only means they can become פסול and need שריפה. Finally, the גמרא tries to support רב גידל from a ברייתא: 'פיגול is always subject to מעילה.' Two attempts to push off this proof are each challenged as making the ברייתא teach something obvious. First, it might be a case where the blood was not sprinkled. Second, it might be about an עולה. The discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְהָא קָתָנֵי:

But didn't he teach:

אִם אֲחֵרִים מֵבִיא לִידֵי פִּיגּוּל,

If it brings others to פיגול,

הוּא עַצְמוֹ לֹא כׇּל שֶׁכֵּן!

is it not all the more so for itself!

הָא נָמֵי, אִיסּוּרָא

This too is a prohibition
