Topic

The mission to Rome, and when different foods and kinds of טומאה join to reach a minimum amount

Overview

The amud finishes the story of the mission to Rome. רבי שמעון בן יוחאי is chosen because he is used to miracles, and רבי אלעזר בר רבי יוסי goes with him. On the way, רבי אלעזר answers a halachic question in front of his teacher, and רבי שמעון punishes him. A demon, בן תמליון, offers help and enters the emperor's daughter. רבי שמעון drives it out, and the grateful emperor lets the רבנן into his treasury, where they tear up the decree. The amud then turns to halacha with a new משנה: פיגול and נותר, and different sources of טומאה, do not join to reach a minimum amount. שמואל limits the rule about פיגול and נותר to טומאת ידים, because for eating one shared איסור covers both. A second משנה teaches that food at different levels of טומאה, and all foods or all liquids, do join for many standard amounts. רבא and רב אשי each explain why a ראשון and a שני join together.

Opening lines

יֵלֵךְ רַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן בֶּן יוֹחַאי,

Let Rabbi Shimon ben Yoḥai go,

שֶׁהוּא מְלוּמָּד בְּנִסִּים.

as he is used to miracles.

וְאַחֲרָיו מִי יֵלֵךְ?

And who will go after him?

רַבִּי אֶלְעָזָר בַּר רַבִּי יוֹסֵי.

Rabbi Elazar bar Rabbi Yosei.
