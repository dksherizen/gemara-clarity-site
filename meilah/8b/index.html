Topic

How strongly a טבול יום affects קדשים, why benefit stays forbidden after a bird חטאת's blood is sprinkled, and whether squeezing out its blood (מיצוי) is essential.

Overview

The עמוד opens with a ברייתא giving three views on how strongly a טבול יום affects קודש. אבא שאול treats him as a ראשון. רבי מאיר says he makes קודש טמא. חכמים say he only makes it פסול. רבא explains that all three agree on the Torah law, so our משנה fits everyone. The stricter views are a rabbinic מעלה for קדשים, and for חכמים immersion has weakened his טומאה. Next, the גמרא asks why benefit from a bird חטאת is still forbidden after its blood is sprinkled. רבי חנינא answers that the משנה speaks of meat that went out of its area, following רבי עקיבא. Then רב הונא and רב אדא בר אהבה report רב differently on whether squeezing out the blood (מיצוי) is essential. A פסוק is used to challenge רב הונא, and he reads it as 'only if blood is left.' Finally, רב אחא בריה דרבא challenges that reading from the leftover of a מנחה, a point that continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

תְּחִלָּה לַקֹּדֶשׁ.

Is first-degree impure with regard to sacred food.

רַבִּי מֵאִיר אוֹמֵר:

Rabbi Meir says:

מְטַמֵּא אֶת הַקֹּדֶשׁ,

He renders sacred food impure,

וּפוֹסֵל אֶת הַתְּרוּמָה.

And disqualifies תרומה.
