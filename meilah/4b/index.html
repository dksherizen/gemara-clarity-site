Topic

Proofs for רב גידל about unfit sprinkling and מעילה, and רבי יהושע's rule about שעת היתר לכהנים.

Overview

The גמרא tries to prove רב גידל's view that sprinkling the blood in an unfit way does not take a קרבן out of מעילה. First it brings the סיפא of a ברייתא: blood left overnight and then sprinkled is still under מעילה. That case must be about a חטאת, since for an עולה it would be obvious. The גמרא then pulls back, because הלנה is a problem made through action, while פיגול is only a wrong thought. Next it tries a ברייתא saying פיגול of קדשי קדשים has מעילה. The answer that this means before sprinkling seems to fail, because then the ברייתא should have split before and after sprinkling. That proof is also pushed aside, since קדשים קלים may simply be the clearer contrast. The גמרא then turns to רבי יהושע's rule in the משנה: an offering that was once permitted to the כהנים has no מעילה, and one that never was permitted keeps מעילה. The משנה gives examples of each. The amud ends as בר קפרא begins to ask whether this 'permission' means permission from a proper שחיטה, a question that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְעוֹד, דְּקָתָנֵי סֵיפָא:

And furthermore, as the latter clause teaches:

לָן דָּמָהּ,

If its blood was left overnight,

אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁחָזַר וּזְרָקוֹ –

even though he later sprinkled it,

מוֹעֲלִין בּוֹ.

one is liable for misuse of it.
