Topic

A personal-altar offering brought into the courtyard, and how פיגול affects מעילה

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a statement: once a personal-altar offering enters the courtyard, the courtyard walls 'take hold of it' for every law. רבי אלעזר then asks what happens if such an offering became disqualified and its limbs already went up on the altar: must they come down? The גמרא first infers that he was sure about a regular Temple offering, either like רבה or like רב יוסף. It then rejects that inference, since the personal-altar case could go either way under both views, and the question ends as תיקו. A new topic follows: רב גידל quotes רב that a פיגול sprinkling neither takes קדשי קדשים out of מעילה nor brings קדשים קלים into it. רב פפא challenges אביי with a משנה about the תודה bread, and רבי אבא answers that רב meant פיגול intent at the sprinkling itself. רב אשי then tests this with עולא's rule about a פיגול handful. רבא answers that עולא only meant something on the way to פיגול.

the partitions have taken hold of it for all matters.

בָּעֵי רַבִּי אֶלְעָזָר:

Rabbi Elazar asks:

A burnt offering of a personal altar that one brought in, and it became disqualified –

עָלוּ, מַהוּ שֶׁיֵּרְדוּ?

if they went up, what is the law: should they come down?
