Topic

Does רבי יהושע's 'time of fitness' mean fitness to eat the meat (היתר אכילה) or fitness to sprinkle the blood (היתר זריקה)? Also, what can an improper זריקה do for מעילה?

Overview

The גמרא is asking what kind of 'fitness' רבי יהושע meant in the משנה. He said that once an offering had a time of fitness for the כהנים, it is no longer subject to מעילה. Did he mean the meat became permitted to eat (היתר אכילה), or only that the blood was ready to be sprinkled (היתר זריקה)? First the גמרא brings a ברייתא of רבי שמעון about נותר, and then a matching one about פיגול. In both, 'before זריקה' there is מעילה and 'after זריקה' there is none. The גמרא tries to prove from these that fitness for sprinkling is not enough. Each time the proof is pushed away: רבי שמעון's words can mean 'before or after it was fit for זריקה'. A third proof, from פיגול of קדשי קדשים, is also turned aside. The גמרא then explains why that ברייתא contrasts קדשי קדשים with קדשים קלים. An improper זריקה, like one done with פיגול intent, can take an offering out of מעילה, but only a proper זריקה can bring something into מעילה.
