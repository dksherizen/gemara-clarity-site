Topic

Do kosher and non-kosher נבלות join together to make a כזית, for טומאה and for the איסור of eating?

Overview

The משנה taught that all נבלות join together to make a כזית. רב limits this to טומאה: for the איסור of eating, kosher and non-kosher carcasses stay separate. לוי says they join even for eating. רב אסי says kosher and non-kosher stay separate, and there are two versions of whether he argues with רב. The גמרא challenges רב אסי from a ברייתא about a dead cow and a live camel. It first answers that the ברייתא means two live animals join, following רבי יהודה on אבר מן החי. That answer is pushed back twice: the ברייתא's example would be pointless, and a second ברייתא says cow and camel join 'whether alive or dead.' Finally the גמרא answers for רב אסי that the ברייתא follows a תנא who holds איסור חל על איסור, which רב אסי does not hold.

Opening lines

לֹא שָׁנוּ אֶלָּא לְעִנְיַן טוּמְאָה,

They taught this only with regard to ritual impurity,

אֲבָל לְעִנְיַן אֲכִילָה –

but with regard to eating,

טְהוֹרִין בִּפְנֵי עַצְמָן,

kosher ones are by themselves,

וּטְמֵאִים בִּפְנֵי עַצְמָן.

and non-kosher ones are by themselves.
