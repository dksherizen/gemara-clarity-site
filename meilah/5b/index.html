Topic

What does 'a period of fitness' in the מעילה משנה mean, when does a פסול make the blood שיריים, and what does a פיגול sprinkling do to מעילה?

Overview

The גמרא wants to know what 'a period of fitness' in our משנה means: fitness to sprinkle the blood, or fitness to eat the meat. First it suggests our משנה's wording is not exact, like a similar משנה in זבחים. רב אסי objects that two משניות would not both use loose wording, so the גמרא decides our משנה about מעילה is exact. The זבחים wording instead teaches that when a פסול receives and sprinkles, the rest of the blood becomes שיריים (leftovers). The גמרא then challenges this from רבי יוחנן, who said only sprinkling with פיגול intent makes שיריים. It answers that רבי יוחנן's list covers only things invalid for the ציבור. A טמא, who can serve for the ציבור, does make שיריים, while other פסולין do not. Finally, the גמרא tries to prove the 'fitness to sprinkle' reading from a ברייתא that says פיגול is always subject to מעילה. It rejects that proof: the ברייתא may be speaking after sprinkling, and 'always' teaches רב גידל's rule that a פיגול sprinkling neither takes an offering out of מעילה nor brings it in.
