Topic

Sources for placing altar and מנורה ashes; מעילה on birds not yet fit or past their time, and on קדשים that died

Overview

The amud opens by finding sources for where removed ashes are placed. The ashes of the outer altar come from 'ושמו אצל המזבח.' רבי אלעזר learns the ashes of the inner altar from the extra words 'מקום הדשן' in the bird-offering פסוק. He also learns the ashes of the מנורה from the extra letter ה in הדשן. Then a new משנה brings רבי שמעון's view: doves too young to be offered carry מעילה, while pigeons too old to be offered do not. The גמרא explains רבי שמעון's reason. It then explains why the רבנן treat young doves differently from a too-young animal: an animal can be redeemed like a בעל מום, but birds have no פדיון. Next, עולא quotes רבי יוחנן that קדשים that died leave Torah-level מעילה. רב חסדא objects, and after accepting that there is rabbinic מעילה, he asks whether anything can gain מעילה only later. The גמרא suggests blood as an example and begins to answer that blood has מעילה from the start; the explanation continues on the next amud.

״וְשָׂמוֹ אֵצֶל הַמִּזְבֵּחַ״.
