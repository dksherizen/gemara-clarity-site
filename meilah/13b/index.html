Me’ilah 13b

מעילה י״ג ע״ב

Meilah 13b

Topic

Things forbidden to use but without מעילה, the measure of the water libation (ריש לקיש and רבי יוחנן), and a nest on a הקדש tree

Overview

The משנה lists things you may not use, but which carry no מעילה if you do. These are tree roots that cross between private and הקדש land, a spring flowing through a הקדש field, the water in the golden jug for the סוכות water libation, and the ערבה for the מזבח. Once the water is put into the flask, which is a כלי שרת, it does carry מעילה. רבי אלעזר ברבי צדוק adds that the elders used the ערבה in their לולבים. In the גמרא, ריש לקיש first seems to say that three לוגין in the jug carry מעילה. The גמרא challenges this from the משנה and moves his statement to the flask: there, only three לוגין carry מעילה, while רבי יוחנן says all of it does. The גמרא then asks whether ריש לקיש elsewhere implied that water has no measure, and answers that he was only arguing within מנחם יודאפה's approach. A new משנה then teaches about a nest on a הקדש tree, a nest on an אשירה, and a consecrated forest.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳ שׇׁרְשֵׁי אִילָן שֶׁל הֶדְיוֹט

MISHNA: The roots of a tree belonging to an ordinary person

הַבָּאִין בְּשֶׁל הֶקְדֵּשׁ,

that come into consecrated land,

וְשֶׁל הֶקְדֵּשׁ שֶׁבָּאִין בְּשֶׁל הֶדְיוֹט –

and roots of a consecrated tree that come into an ordinary person's land –

לֹא נֶהֱנִין וְלֹא מוֹעֲלִין.

one may not benefit from them, and there is no מעילה.
