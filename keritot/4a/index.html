Topic

Speech as an action, and whether eating the חלב of an ox, a sheep and a goat brings separate מלקות and separate חטאות

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a discussion of whether moving the lips to speak (עקימת שפתיו) counts as an action. רבא explains that a מגדף and עדים זוממין are exceptions: blasphemy is mainly in the heart, and testimony is mainly about what the witnesses saw. The גמרא then turns to eating חלב. A ברייתא records that רבי ישמעאל makes a person liable separately for the fat of an ox, a sheep and a goat, while the חכמים say he is liable only once. Again and again the גמרא tries to show that some of the animal names are extra, and each time the רבנן show why every name is needed, partly with the help of רבי חנניא's teaching about the בכור. Finally, the גמרא explains the dispute as being about learning a plain לאו from a לאו with כרת, or about permitting the אליה of חולין. רבי חנינא adds that even רבי ישמעאל requires only one חטאת. Last, a ברייתא about two חטאות for 'two categories' of חלב in one העלם is explained by רב חסדא and then by רב ששת, and the scholars in ארץ ישראל mock רב ששת's answer.
