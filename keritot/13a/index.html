Topic

Combining separate eatings within כדי אכילת פרס: רבי מאיר and חכמים, the refutation of רבנאי, and the rules of impure food, immersion, a pregnant woman and a nursing baby

Overview

The גמרא first proves from the exact wording of חכמים in the משנה ('עד שישהא') that רבי מאיר's ruling is a stringency. Next, רבנאי quotes שמואל: for חלבים and נבילה, the whole כזית must be eaten within כדי אכילת פרס. For impure foods and liquids, the eating counts even over a whole day. רב פפא explains this to mean that each כזית must be eaten within כדי אכילת פרס. A ברייתא then says that separate eatings of impure food or drink combine only if the time from the first bite to the last fits within כדי אכילת פרס, and רבנאי is refuted. The גמרא goes on to explain other lines of that ברייתא. Immersing in the middle of eating is forbidden, so a person won't rely on an immersion that doesn't count. A pregnant woman may eat in small portions, even a lot in total, because of danger. The ברייתא also says the baby of an impure nursing mother stays pure. רב נחמן's explanation for this is rejected by רבא, who says the baby is pure because of a double doubt about the amount he drank and the time it took. Finally, the גמרא challenges רבא's view that a woman's milk is like a spring and needs no הכשר, and begins quoting a ברייתא about a זב's liquids that continues on the next amud.
