Topic

Splitting a לאו שבכללות through extra words in the פסוק, the power of a גזירה שוה, and the laws and recipe of שמן המשחה.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a discussion of לאו שבכללות, one prohibition that covers several items and normally earns only one set of מלקות. Extra words in a פסוק can split such a לאו, so eating each kind of מעשר שני outside ירושלים is punished separately. The same idea applies to לחם, קלי and כרמל eaten before the עומר. The גמרא proves that קלי is extra by learning it from לחם and כרמל together, and shows from its place in the middle of the פסוק that each food earns its own מלקות. Next, a series of sages (רבי ינאי, רבי סימאי, אביי and רב אשי) teach that a גזירה שוה must never be taken lightly. Major laws rest on one alone: כרת for פיגול and נותר, the ban and the burning penalty for בתו מאנוסתו, and סקילה for certain forbidden relations. The גמרא then turns to the משנה's case of blending שמן המשחה. It learns from the קטרת that blending it for the community is פטור, and explains why half a batch of oil is פטור while half a batch of קטרת is חייב. Finally it discusses the recipe of the oil: the weight of each spice, whether the spices are weighed with a small surplus, and how the oil was made. That last question is a debate between רבי יהודה and רבי יוסי, and רבי יהודה's reply continues on the next amud.

כְּתַב רַחֲמָנָא: ״לֹא תוּכַל״,
