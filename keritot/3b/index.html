Topic

How the משנה counts its list of כריתות: mistakes in עבודה זרה, general categories, אוב and ידעוני, and what counts as an action

Overview

The amud opens by looking for a case of שגגת עבודה זרה וזדון עבודות. The גמרא gives three ways to picture it: someone who thinks idol worship is allowed, a תינוק שנשבה, or an adult who thinks only silver and gold idols are forbidden. רב ביבי then explains that the משנה's list of כריתות names general categories like שבת and עבודה זרה. The גמרא shows why his own question about 'thirty-seven' is still fair. רבי ירמיה asks about two punishments under one לאו, and רבי זירא points to אוב and ידעוני. רבי יוחנן says ידעוני is left off the כרת list because it shares one לאו with אוב. ריש לקיש says it is left off because it has no action. This leads to a discussion of what counts as an action for רבי עקיבא and for the רבנן, and why the משנה excludes only the מגדף. The amud ends with עולא, רבא and אביי defining the בעל אוב and the חובר חבר, and with a new challenge from רבי יוחנן's view that continues on the next amud.
