Topic

The law of a מתעסק on שבת and in other איסורים, רבי יוסי on work done at בין השמשות, and when an אשם תלוי is brought.

Overview

The גמרא first asks which case the rule 'a מתעסק is exempt' is about. It answers that the rule teaches a specific שבת case, which differs for אביי and רבא depending on how much intent to cut is needed. Next it explains רבי יוסי's claim that work done at בין השמשות is פטור because it is split across two days. He holds to this even for a quick lifting. רב יוסף then answers a question from רבי אליעזר's weaving rule. The גמרא then turns to the אשם תלוי. רבי יהודה and רבי שמעון read the same פסוק differently, and a ברייתא proves that רבי שמעון requires an אשם תלוי even when it is unclear whether one sinned at all. Finally, שמואל rules that a מתעסק is liable for חלבים and עריות because he had pleasure, but exempt on שבת because only מלאכת מחשבת is forbidden. He defends this rule against two challenges: the case of the two babies, which is about wounding, and the case of picking the wrong fruit.
