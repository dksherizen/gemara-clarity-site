Topic

Doubtful eating of חלב mixed with other pieces, sharing offerings, the פרוטה value of נותר, and איסור חל על איסור in קדשים

Overview

The משנה lays out cases of two pieces where one is definitely חלב and the other is ordinary meat (חולין), sacrificial meat (קודש), sacrificial חלב, or חלב that is also נותר. For each pair it rules on one person who ate one piece without knowing which, or ate both. It also rules on two people who each ate one piece. רבי עקיבא gives each person an אשם תלוי, רבי שמעון lets the two share one offering for the doubt, and רבי יוסי says two people cannot share an offering. The גמרא begins with רבא asking רב נחמן what רבי יוסי adds. The answer is that the anonymous תנא קמא is רבי יוסי himself. Next רבא asks why eating נותר does not also bring an אשם ודאי for מעילה. רב נחמן answers that the piece is worth less than a פרוטה, because it is small or because it spoiled in the summer heat. Finally רבא asks how רבי שמעון lets the איסור of נותר take effect on a piece that is already forbidden. After rejecting a few answers, the גמרא concludes that for קדשים the תורה revealed that one איסור can take effect on another.

Opening lines

אֵין שְׁנַיִם מְבִיאִין אָשָׁם אֶחָד.

Two people do not bring one guilt offering.

חֲתִיכַת חֵלֶב וַחֲתִיכַת חוּלִּין,

A piece of forbidden fat and a piece of ordinary meat,

אָכַל אַחַת מֵהֶן

he ate one of them

וְאֵינוֹ יוֹדֵעַ אֵיזֶה מֵהֶן אָכַל –

and does not know which of them he ate –
