Topic

Does רבי יהודה's rule that a second twin 'is like it does not exist' (וָלָד שֵׁנִי כְּמַאן דְּלֵיתֵיהּ דָּמֵי) apply only to the קרבן, or also to טומאה and טהרה?

Overview

The גמרא asks whether רבי יהודה's rule, that a second twin is treated as if it does not exist, applies only to the יולדת's קרבן or also to טומאה and טהרה. Another possibility is that he counts the second twin only when it makes things stricter. רב הונא מסורא tries to prove the answer from a ברייתא that lets the קרבן פסח be brought for a יולדת on day 40 or 80, which רב חסדא explained as following רבי יהודה. The גמרא rejects the proof. It reads the ברייתא as a פסח הבא בטומאה and works through what that means for immersion, a טבול יום and a מחוסר כפורים. It also gives רבא's reading, from רב אשי: an early, bloodless miscarriage before the fetus is formed. רבי שמעיה then brings a ברייתא saying the sixty days of purity after a girl must be continuous, and argues it must follow רבי יהודה. The גמרא shows it can fit רבנן too, in a case of twins, a girl and then a boy. Finally אביי brings a similar ברייתא about the thirty days after a boy, and the גמרא begins to ask whose view it is; that question continues on the next amud.
