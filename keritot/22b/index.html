Topic

אשם תלוי for a doubt of מעילה: the sources of רבי עקיבא and the חכמים, the two-שקלים minimum for every אשם, רבי טרפון's conditional אשם, and the new משנה on doubtful offerings.

Overview

The גמרא opens with a ברייתא: רבי עקיבא learns from the ו of ״ואם נפש״ that a person unsure whether he committed מעילה brings an אשם תלוי, and the חכמים exempt him. רב פפא shows that everyone learns an earlier passage from a later one. The חכמים instead rely on a גזירה שוה of ״מצות״ from חטאת, which limits אשם תלוי to sins carrying כרת, and מעילה has no כרת. The גמרא then works through who holds 'no גזירה שוה for half' and 'no היקש for half', and concludes that everyone agrees to both rules. רבי עקיבא learns from ״זאת תורת האשם״ that every אשם must be worth two silver שקלים. The חכמים still need the ו to apply that rule to the אשם תלוי. For a view that does not expound ״זאת תורת האשם״, the גמרא supplies the גזירות שוות ״בערכך״ and ״איל״. Next, רבא explains רבי טרפון's conditional אשם in the משנה, and teaches that an אשם ודאי does not need knowledge of the sin in advance. A new משנה follows: a doubtful bird חטאת that becomes definite, and the case of eating one of two pieces of meat, one חולין and one הקדש. רבי יוסי's opinion on that case continues on the next amud.
