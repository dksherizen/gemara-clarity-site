Topic

The blood prohibition: which creatures are included (including the כוי), and light versus severe טומאה for people and animals

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא on the פסוק "כל דם לא תאכלו... לעוף ולבהמה". The ברייתא excludes the blood of שרצים, eggs, fish and grasshoppers, and it explains why the פסוק names both bird and animal. The גמרא reads the פסוקים as a כלל ופרט וכלל, which includes anything similar to bird and animal. It explains that this follows the school of רבי ישמעאל, even though the two generalizations are not alike. The extra phrase "אף כל" includes the blood of a כוי, which רב אדא בר אבין holds is a creature of its own kind. The גמרא then finds sources for the כוי's fat, נבלה, גיד הנשה, טומאה and שחיטה. Next it resolves a contradiction about whether people get light (food) טומאה: a living person's flesh can, but a dead body cannot. Finally, a משנה about which נבילות need מחשבה and הכשר leads to a debate between רב and רבי חייא about a piece of נבילה smaller than a כזית, and that discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אוֹצִיא דַּם שְׁרָצִים –

I will exclude the blood of creeping creatures

שֶׁאֵין בָּהֶם טוּמְאָה חֲמוּרָה.

since they do not have a severe impurity.

אוֹצִיא דַּם בֵּיצִים –

I will exclude the blood of eggs

שֶׁאֵין מִין בָּשָׂר,

since they are not a type of meat,
