Topic

The sister's כרת, separate חטאות for separate לאוין, and how the משנה counts its 36 כרת sins

Overview

This amud finishes a discussion about why the Torah wrote a separate כרת for one's sister. The רבנן say it teaches that we do not punish based on a קל וחומר. רבי יצחק uses it to teach separate חטאות when one woman is his sister, his father's sister and his mother's sister. רבי הושעיא teaches that two separate לאוין sharing one כרת still require separate חטאות, as with blending and smearing the anointing oil. רב נחמן בר יצחק proves this from the order of the משנה. The גמרא then asks how the משנה reaches 36 כרת sins. רבי יוחנן says the list counts a man's sins, following רבי ישמעאל. רב says 33 are a man's חטאות and 3 more are added to complete the list of כרת sins. Next, the גמרא explains why שבת and עבודה זרה each count as one item. It then searches for a real case of mistaken idol worship, and that search continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לַאֲחוֹתוֹ שֶׁהִיא בַּת אָבִיו וּבַת אִמּוֹ,

It is for his sister who is his father's daughter and his mother's daughter,

לוֹמַר שֶׁאֵין עוֹנְשִׁין מִן הַדִּין.

to say that one does not punish based on an inference.

וְרַבִּי יִצְחָק, סָבַר:

And רבי יצחק holds:

עוֹנְשִׁין מִן הַדִּין.

one does punish based on an inference.
