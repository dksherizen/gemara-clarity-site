Topic

The שפחה חרופה comparisons, and a new משנה on when testimony that a person ate חלב obligates him to bring an offering

Overview

The amud opens with רב ששת's objection to a ברייתא that seems to compare things backwards. He explains that with a שפחה חרופה, the פסוק 'שכבת זרע' requires a complete, normal act, so the stronger-looking cases are exempt, just like the weaker ones. The פרק ארבעה מחוסרי כפרה then closes. A new משנה follows about testimony that a person ate חלב: when he brings a חטאת, when he brings an אשם תלוי, and when he is exempt. It includes the argument between רבי מאיר and the חכמים about two witnesses against his own denial. The משנה also teaches how many offerings are owed for eating forbidden foods in one העלם. The גמרא first reads 'אמרו לו' as two witnesses whom he denies, following רבי מאיר. A second view, using a משנה in יבמות, says 'אמרו' can mean even one witness, and that the case is where he stays silent. Finally the גמרא brings the Torah source, 'או הודע אליו חטאתו', and starts asking which case that ברייתא is about, a question that continues on the next amud.
