Topic

Lashes and sin offerings for forbidden fat and blood, the sources for כרת and לאוין on blood, and lashes for eating מעשר outside ירושלים

Overview

The amud opens by asking which תנא a ברייתא follows when it makes someone owe two חטאות for eating forbidden fat. Neither רבי ישמעאל nor רבי יהודה works, because both agree that only one חטאת is brought. So ריש לקיש explains that the person ate from two separate dishes, following רבי יהושע. The גמרא then turns to the lashes for eating חלב of מוקדשין: the רבנן say two sets of lashes and רבי יהודה says three. Their argument depends on whether the verse 'חלב שור' also covers קדשים, and on whether a לאו can be learned from a nearby verse of כרת. Next, the גמרא explains how each side uses the היקש between חלב and blood. For רבי יהודה, it gives blood three sets of lashes. For the רבנן, it teaches when blood joins with flesh to make a full amount, which also covers a snake. רבא then explains the Torah's three mentions of כרת and five לאוין about blood. Finally, רבי אילא rules that eating מעשר of grain, wine and oil outside ירושלים brings three sets of lashes, and the גמרא starts working out which verse is extra; that discussion continues on the next amud.
