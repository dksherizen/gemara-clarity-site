Topic

Knowledge and liability for a חטאת: ספק ידיעה in טומאת מקדש, and a sinner who does not know which sin he did

Overview

The amud finishes a ברייתא about a person who walked on one of two paths, one טמא and one טהור, and then entered the מקדש. רבא explains that רבי שמעון's exemption even in the first case is about מקצת ידיעה, partial knowledge. In the case where he became pure in between, the גמרא asks why he is liable without real ידיעה. ריש לקיש says the ברייתא follows רבי ישמעאל, who does not require ידיעה בתחלה. רבי יוחנן says that even the רבנן agree, because for טומאה, ספק ידיעה counts as ידיעה. The גמרא settles the apparent contradictions in both sages: רבי יוחנן's rule applies only to טומאה because of the wording 'ונעלם ממנו', and ריש לקיש chose רבי ישמעאל to teach that רבי ישמעאל rejects ידיעה בתחלה completely. A new משנה then brings cases where a person surely sinned but does not know which sin he did. רבי אליעזר obligates a חטאת, and רבי יהושע exempts, and several תנאים limit where this argument applies. The גמרא brings רבי אליעזר's 'מה נפשך' reasoning and רבי יהושע's proof from 'אשר חטא בה'. רבי אליעזר uses that word only to exclude a מתעסק.
