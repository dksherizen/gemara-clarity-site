Topic

רב חסדא and רבא on what רבי עקיבא asked about separate acts: the ברייתות about רבן גמליאל writing on two שבתות, the wording of the נדה proof, and the debate over one חטאת or many.

Overview

Writing two letters is the smallest amount of writing that makes someone liable on שבת, so one letter is only a חצי שיעור. Two ברייתות disagree about someone who wrote one letter on each of two שבתות: one says רבן גמליאל holds him חייב, and the other says פטור. רב חסדא and רבא argue about what רבי עקיבא asked רבי אליעזר. רב חסדא says it was about forgetting שבת, and whether the days in between split the sins. רבא says it was about forgetting the מלאכות, and whether separate שבתות count as one unit. Under רבא, the פטור ברייתא seems to have no case, so רבא says רבן גמליאל holds like רבי אליעזר, and the word 'ומודה' is explained through the argument about two half-figs carried into two רשויות. The גמרא then tests both views against the wording of the משנה: נדה or נדות, and 'minor girls'. Finally a ברייתא gives a different version of the debate, in which רבי עקיבא asks about repeated acts with a נדה, and רבי אליעזר's proofs from שבת, minor girls and an animal are each rejected. The פרק ends, and a new משנה begins about cases of doubt in eating חלב.

Opening lines

וְהָא דְּתַנְיָא חַיָּיב –

And that which is taught, "liable":

בְּשִׁגְגַת שַׁבָּת וּזְדוֹן מְלָאכוֹת

is where he forgot it was Shabbat but knew the labors were forbidden

(דְּשַׁבָּתוֹת כְּגוּפִין דָּמְיָין),

(even though Shabbatot are like separate bodies),

דְּקָסָבַר: אֵין יְדִיעָה לַחֲצִי שִׁיעוּר.

because he holds: there is no awareness for a half-measure.
