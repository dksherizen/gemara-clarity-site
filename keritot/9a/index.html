Topic

The גר's conversion קרבן, and the new משנה on קרבנות brought even for deliberate sins and one קרבן for many sins

Overview

This amud finishes the discussion of the קרבן a גר brings when he converts. It must be an offering with blood, not a מנחה. According to רבי, the גר enters the ברית just as our ancestors did: through מילה, טבילה, and sprinkling of blood. The גמרא shows that birds must come as a pair, and finds verses for each of the three steps. It also proves that גרים are accepted even today without a קרבן, and rules like רבי שמעון that a גר today does not set aside money for his birds. Another version places that ruling on a ברייתא about a גר תושב doing work on שבת. Then a new משנה lists cases where a קרבן is brought even for a deliberate sin: שפחה חרופה, a נזיר who became טמא, שבועת העדות, and שבועת הפקדון. The גמרא finds a source for each case. Finally, רבי יוחנן rules that relations with five different שפחות need five קרבנות, because they are separate bodies. His proof from the פסוק continues on the next amud.
