Topic

When an אשם תלוי is brought: the משנה's doubt cases, and whether a single doubtful piece is enough or two pieces are needed

Overview

The amud opens with the end of a משנה about the אשם תלוי, the offering brought when a person is unsure whether he sinned. The משנה gives three classic doubt cases: two pieces of fat, his wife and his sister, and שבת and a weekday. It then rules that אשם תלוי offerings are counted just like חטאת offerings. In the גמרא, רב אסי and חייא בר רב argue whether the משנה means a single doubtful piece or one piece out of two. Their argument depends on whether we follow the spelling of the word מצות (singular) or its reading מצוות (plural). רב rules that one of two pieces obligates an אשם תלוי, while a single doubtful piece exempts. רבא explains this from the plural reading. Sources that require an אשם תלוי for a single doubt, such as the כוי, the יבם's doubtful child and the blood found on the cloth, are answered as following רבי אליעזר. רבי זירא gives a different reason: the doubt must be one that can be checked. The two reasons differ in a case of an olive-size piece and a half-olive piece.
