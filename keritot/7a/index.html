Topic

Status of a בת כהן, the laws of applying שמן המשחה, whether יום הכיפורים and a חטאת atone for someone who refuses atonement, and whether a מגדף brings a קרבן.

Overview

The amud opens with a ברייתא about a בת כהן who married out of כהונה and ate תרומה. רבי מאיר judges her by her status now, while the חכמים say her original status stays with her. רב יוסף then limits the earlier מחלוקת about placing שמן המשחה to that oil alone. The גמרא learns that whoever may not apply שמן המשחה may not have it applied to him. It also teaches that even oil already on the כהן גדול's head stays holy, unlike used תרומה oil. Back on the משנה, ריש לקיש and רבי יוחנן explain why someone whose sin was covered by יום הכיפורים is not listed with those who bring no אשם תלוי. This leads to whether a חטאת or יום הכיפורים atones for someone who refuses the atonement, and why רבא changed his mind. Two ספרא sources about יום הכפורים seem to clash; אביי and רבא each resolve this, and רבא's proof from the כרת of יום הכפורים is pushed back. The amud ends with the מגדף: the חכמים answer רבי עקיבא, and the גמרא begins challenging רבי עקיבא's reason that כרת brings a קרבן, which continues on the next amud.

בַּת כֹּהֵן שֶׁנִּישֵּׂאת לְיִשְׂרָאֵל,

but does not pay the fifth,
