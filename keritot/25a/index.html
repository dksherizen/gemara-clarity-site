Topic

When the עגלה ערופה becomes forbidden for benefit, and whether an אשם תלוי can be brought as a voluntary gift

Overview

The amud opens with a question: from when is the עגלה ערופה forbidden for any benefit? Is it forbidden while still alive, or only after its neck is broken? רב המנונא proves it is forbidden while alive. In a משנה about אותו ואת בנו, רבי שמעון exempts someone who slaughters an עגלה ערופה. רבא tries to defend the opposite view, but רב המנונא's proof holds. רבא then brings his own proof from a משנה which says the עגלה is buried because it already 'atoned for its doubt.' That fits only if it becomes forbidden at the עריפה. The גמרא concludes that this is a dispute between תנאים, since a ברייתא holds it is forbidden while alive. A new משנה follows. רבי אליעזר allows a voluntary אשם תלוי, and the חכמים do not. It also teaches when יום כיפור cancels an owed offering. The גמרא explains both sides of the אשם תלוי dispute. The amud ends as רבי חייא begins reciting a teaching before רבא, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אָמַר רַבִּי יַנַּאי:

Rabbi Yannai said:

גְּבוּל שָׁמַעְתִּי בָּהּ וְשָׁכַחְתִּי,

I heard a boundary regarding it, but I forgot it,

וְנָסְבִין חַבְרַיָּיא לְמֵימַר:

and the colleagues were inclined to say:

יְרִידָתָהּ לְנַחַל אֵיתָן אוֹסַרְתָּהּ.

its going down to the rough stream is what forbids it.
