Gittin 2b

גיטין ב׳ ע״ב

Open Gittin 2b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The reasons of רבה (לשמה) and רבא (קיום) for the declaration 'בפני נכתב ובפני נחתם', their practical differences, and why a single שליח is enough according to רבה

Overview

The amud opens at the end of רבה's explanation: a שליח bringing a גט from מדינת הים must say 'בפני נכתב ובפני נחתם' because people overseas are not experts in writing a גט לשמה. רבא gives a different reason: no witnesses are available there to confirm the signatures (קיום). The גמרא then lists three cases where the two reasons lead to different results: two people bring the גט, a גט goes from region to region inside ארץ ישראל, and a גט stays within one region overseas. Next the גמרא challenges רבה: if the declaration is testimony about לשמה, it should need two witnesses. The first answer is that one witness is believed about איסורין. The גמרא rejects that answer, because a married woman is an established prohibition of ערוה, and such a matter needs two witnesses. The second answer is that most people are experts in לשמה. Even רבי מאיר, who worries about the minority, agrees, since court scribes know the rule, so the declaration is only a requirement of the רבנן. The explanation of why the רבנן accepted one person begins with 'והכא' and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לְפִי שֶׁאֵין בְּקִיאִין לִשְׁמָהּ.

Because they are not experts in writing it for her sake.

רָבָא אָמַר:

Rava says:

לְפִי שֶׁאֵין עֵדִים מְצוּיִין לְקַיְּימוֹ.

Because witnesses are not available to confirm it.

מַאי בֵּינַיְיהוּ?

What is the difference between them?
