Gittin 6b

גיטין ו׳ ע״ב

Open Gittin 6b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The value of 'בפני נכתב ובפני נחתם,' רבי אביתר's ruling and reliability, and the danger of excessive fear at home

Overview

The גמרא explains that even where a שליח does not have to say 'בפני נכתב ובפני נחתם,' saying it helps. If the husband later challenges the גט, nobody listens to him. A ברייתא proves this: רבי ישמעאל told a man from כפר סיסאי to make the declaration, even though רבי אלעאי pointed out that the village counts as ארץ ישראל. Next, רבי אביתר rules that גיטין coming from בבל to ארץ ישראל need no declaration. The גמרא explains that so many people travel between the two lands that witnesses to confirm the גט are easy to find. רב יוסף doubts רבי אביתר's reliability, because he wrote a פסוק without lined parchment. אביי defends him: that rule is a tradition he may not have heard, and הקדוש ברוך הוא Himself quotes him in the story of the פילגש בגבעה. From that story, רב חסדא and רב warn against ruling a home with too much fear. רבה בר בר חנה then begins a comment on the three reminders said at home before שבת, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְאִי עֲבַדְתְּ – אַהְנֵית.

And if you do it, you have helped.

מַאי ״אִי עֲבַדְתְּ אַהְנֵית״?

What does 'if you do it, you have helped' mean?

דְּאִי אָתֵי בַּעַל מְעַרְעַר,

That if the husband comes and challenges it,

לָא מַשְׁגְּחִינַן בֵּיהּ.

we pay no attention to him.
