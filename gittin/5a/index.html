Gittin 5a

Topic

Testing רבה's and רבא's reasons for בפני נכתב ובפני נחתם against three sources, and how רבה is defended each time

Overview

Earlier, two reasons were given for why a שליח bringing a גט from overseas must say "בפני נכתב ובפני נחתם." רבה says it is because people there did not know a גט must be written לשמה. רבא says it is because no one there could confirm the signatures. The גמרא tests both reasons against three sources. The first source is about a שליח who could not say the declaration, which רב יוסף explains as a healthy שליח who became a חרש. The second is רב הונא's ruling that two people bringing a גט do not need to say it. The third is a תוספתא that calls the declaration a leniency for the woman. Each source fits רבא easily but seems hard for רבה. Each time, the גמרא defends רבה: the case is from after people learned the law of לשמה, a single שליח still speaks because of a גזירה, and rare cases are left out of the גזירה. Along the way it explains why a woman bringing her own גט must still declare (one rule for all שליחות) while a husband need not, since he will not challenge a גט he holds himself. For the תוספתא, the גמרא explains that the woman had already remarried.

Opening lines

אִילֵימָא חֵרֵשׁ,

If you say it means a deaf-mute,

חֵרֵשׁ בַּר אֵיתוֹיֵי גִיטָּא הוּא?!

is a deaf-mute fit to bring a get?!

וְהָתְנַן: הַכֹּל כְּשֵׁרִים לְהָבִיא אֶת הַגֵּט,

But we learned: Everyone is fit to bring a get,

חוּץ מֵחֵרֵשׁ שׁוֹטֶה וְקָטָן!

except for a deaf-mute, an imbecile, and a minor!
