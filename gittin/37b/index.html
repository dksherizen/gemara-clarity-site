Gittin 37b

גיטין ל״ז ע״ב

Open Gittin 37b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Repaying debts in שביעית, a lender's claim of a lost פרוזבול, and the status of a captured slave who was redeemed

Overview

In the שמיטה year a lender must tell a borrower who repays 'I cancel it.' If the borrower answers 'Even so,' the lender may take the money. רבה adds that the lender may look on hopefully while he waits, and the גמרא tells how רבה's own borrower missed his cue until אביי taught him. Next, רב נחמן rules that a lender is believed if he says he had a פרוזבול and lost it, since nobody skips the easy permitted way. רב would even prompt lenders to make that claim. A משנה seems to disagree, and the גמרא answers that this is a debate among תנאים. A new משנה then asks about a captured slave who was redeemed: does he stay a slave? אביי explains the case as before the owner gave up hope, and רבא explains it as after, and each gives his own reason for רבן שמעון בן גמליאל. The גמרא then asks how the redeemer can own the slave at all, and ריש לקיש begins proving from a פסוק that a גוי can own another גוי's labor, a proof that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הַמַּחֲזִיר חוֹב לַחֲבֵירוֹ בַּשְּׁבִיעִית –

One who repays a debt to his fellow in the Sabbatical Year –

צָרִיךְ שֶׁיֹּאמַר לוֹ: ״מְשַׁמֵּט אֲנִי״;

he (the lender) must say to him: 'I cancel it';

וְאִם אָמַר לוֹ: ״אַף עַל פִּי כֵן״,

and if he (the borrower) said to him: 'Even so,'

יְקַבֵּל הֵימֶנּוּ.

he may accept it from him.
