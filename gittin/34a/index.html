Topic

The power of בית דין versus a husband's cancellation, and whether גילוי דעתא cancels a גט

Overview

The גמרא answers a challenge against רב נחמן. He let a husband cancel a גט even though this seems to weaken בית דין, yet in the case of orphans dividing an estate he argued 'otherwise, what power does בית דין have?' The answer is that money matters are different from matters of איסור. Next comes the story of גידול בר רעילאי. His wife put off receiving her גט, and he happily said 'ברוך הטוב והמטיב'. אביי and רבא disagree whether this cancelled the גט, and the root of their argument is גילוי דעתא: does clearly showing you don't want the גט count as cancelling it? Each side brings proofs from real cases: רב ששת, רב יהודה's witnesses with gourds in their ears, and the man stopped by the ferry. Each proof is pushed away. Two more stories about marriage deadlines are shown to depend on the same argument, since אין אונס בגיטין. The amud ends with two rulings like רב נחמן.

Opening lines

יְתוֹמִין שֶׁבָּאוּ לַחֲלוֹק בְּנִכְסֵי אֲבִיהֶן,

Orphans who came to divide their father's property,

בֵּית דִּין מַעֲמִידִין לָהֶן אַפּוֹטְרוֹפּוֹס,

the court appoints a guardian for them,

וּבוֹרְרִים לָהֶן חֵלֶק יָפֶה.

and they choose a fine portion for them.

הִגְדִּילוּ – יְכוֹלִין לְמַחוֹת.

When they have grown up, they can protest.
