Topic

רב הונא on a גט with a spoken condition according to רבי יוסי, and his rule that a sick man's גט, like his gift, is cancelled if he recovers.

Overview

The amud opens with רב הונא's doubt about a husband who adds a spoken condition to his גט. At first the גמרא says he was unsure whether רבי יוסי's rule, that the date on a document proves when it takes effect, applies in that case. A ברייתא about 'our Rabbis' shows that רבי יוסי does apply it. So the doubt is restated: does the הלכה follow רבי יוסי here? A ruling of רבא seems to settle this. The גמרא answers either that רבא was sure while רב הונא was not, or that רבא follows the רבנן and is only teaching how certain phrases are read. A second version puts רב הונא's statement on the 'after my death' case: according to רבי יוסי she must do חליצה, because רבי יוסי and רבי each hold their own view. Then the גמרא turns to a new line of the משנה, about a sick man's גט. רב הונא teaches that a sick man's גט works like his deathbed gift, so a real recovery cancels it. When the משנה seems to contradict this, the גמרא explains that the משנה's man never truly recovered and only walked leaning on his cane.
