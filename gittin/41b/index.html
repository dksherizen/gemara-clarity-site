Gittin 41b

גיטין מ״א ע״ב

Topic

Freeing a half-slave: the end of the משנה, and רבה and רב יוסף on whether a שטר or כסף can free half a slave

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה about a person who is half slave and half free. בית שמאי show he cannot marry anyone, and staying single goes against פריה ורביה. So, for תיקון העולם, the master is forced to free him, and the slave writes a note for half his value. בית הלל accept this ruling. The גמרא then brings a ברייתא where רבי and חכמים argue whether a master can free only half his slave. רבה says they argue only about freeing with a שטר, and everyone agrees about כסף. רב יוסף says they argue only about כסף, and everyone agrees about a שטר. A ברייתא refutes רב יוסף on the שטר, but he can still hold that they argue about כסף too. The גמרא then tests both views against another ברייתא and against our משנה, and רבינא's answer for רב יוסף continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בַּת חוֹרִין אִי אֶפְשָׁר,

A free woman, it is impossible,

שֶׁכְּבָר חֶצְיוֹ עֶבֶד.

since half of him is still a slave.

יִבָּטֵל?

Should he be idle?

וַהֲלֹא לֹא נִבְרָא הָעוֹלָם

But wasn't the world created
