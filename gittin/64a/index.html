Gittin 64a

גיטין ס״ד ע״א

Learn Gittin 64a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Gittin 64a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

When is the שליש holding a גט believed, and what proof is needed that a woman was divorced?

Overview

The amud opens with the last line of the משנה, which says three witnesses can make up both required pairs. The גמרא then brings a dispute about a גט held by a שליש: the husband says it was a פקדון, and the שליש says it was for divorce. רב הונא believes the husband, because he would have handed the גט to his wife directly. רב חסדא believes the שליש, because the husband trusted him. רבי אבא challenges רב הונא from a ברייתא that believes a שליש. The גמרא answers that money is different, since it can be forgiven, and defends that answer against two more ברייתות. The גמרא then works through the משנה's need for witnesses, explaining it through רבי אלעזר and a time of persecution. רבה shows when even רב הונא believes the wife, based on a מיגו. Finally, רבי יוחנן rules that a גט said to be lost needs two witnesses. The גמרא tests that ruling against the שליש, the husband, and the חזקה that an agent does his job, and the discussion of that חזקה continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אוֹ אֶחָד מִן הָרִאשׁוֹנִים,

or one from the first ones,

וְאֶחָד מִן הָאַחֲרוֹנִים,

and one from the latter ones,

וְאֶחָד מִצְטָרֵף עִמָּהֶן.

and one joins together with them.

גְּמָ׳
