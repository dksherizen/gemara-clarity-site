Gittin 17b

גיטין י״ז ע״ב

Learn Gittin 17b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Gittin 17b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Why a גט must be dated (the reasons of רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש and how they fit רבי שמעון and the רבנן), and אביי's questions to רב יוסף about how the dating rule works in practice.

Overview

Earlier, the גמרא gave two reasons why a גט must be dated. רבי יוחנן says the date stops a husband from covering for an unfaithful wife. ריש לקיש says it settles when the husband's rights to the פירות of her property end. The גמרא explains why each rejected the other's reason, and how each fits the dispute between רבי שמעון and the רבנן about a גט written by day and signed at night. A quoted dispute seems to give them the opposite views on the produce, and the גמרא answers by switching the names there. Then אביי asks רב יוסף a series of questions about the dating rule. An undated גט works after the fact, but she may not remarry with it from the start. We do not worry about a cheater who cuts off the date. Even a general date, like a seven-year cycle, is valid because it settles what happened before and after that span. At the end רבינא begins a new question to רבא, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

זְנוּת לָא שְׁכִיחָא.

Adultery is not common.

וְרַבִּי יוֹחָנָן, מַאי טַעְמָא

And Rabbi Yochanan, what is the reason

לָא אָמַר כְּרֵישׁ לָקִישׁ?

that he did not say like Reish Lakish?

קָסָבַר: יֵשׁ לְבַעַל פֵּירוֹת

He holds: the husband has the produce
