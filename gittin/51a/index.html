Gittin 51a

גיטין נ״א ע״א

Topic

What lets a debt be collected from sold property (fixed amount, writing, or timing), and the oath of one who finds a lost object.

Overview

The גמרא asks what an obligation needs in order to be collected from property already sold to a buyer (נכסים משועבדים): must it be both fixed in amount and written down, or is a fixed amount enough? It first tries to prove this from a daughter's dowry (פרנסה), which is fixed but unwritten. The proof is rejected, because a dowry is public knowledge and so counts as written. רב הונא בר מנוח then brings a משנה in which a stepdaughter collects food support even from buyers. The גמרא explains that a קנין was made for her, and after several attempts settles on why the man's own daughter does not collect this way: her father may already have given her money. Next a teaching of רבי נתן suggests that collecting from buyers depends only on timing. The גמרא answers that תנאים disagree, some saying the reason is that these debts are unwritten and רבי יוסי saying it is that they are unfixed. Finally, the גמרא turns to the משנה's rule that a finder of a lost object does not swear. It brings רבי יצחק's cases of tied pouches and tied oxen, then challenges how he can require an oath at all.

Opening lines

אוֹ דִּלְמָא קְצוּבִין,

Or perhaps it is enough that they are fixed,

וְאַף עַל פִּי שֶׁאֵינָן כְּתוּבִים?

even though they are not written?

תָּא שְׁמַע, דְּאִיתְּמַר:

Come and hear, for it was stated:

מִי שֶׁמֵּת וְהִנִּיחַ שְׁתֵּי בָּנוֹת וּבֵן,

One who died and left two daughters and a son,

The steps (25)

  1. QuestionThe second side of the question: is a fixed amount enough?
  2. ProofBringing a case: two daughters, a son, and the dowry

  14. ResolutionThe two girls get support for different reasons
  15. ChallengeCan an extra source of rights make her weaker?

  18. ResolutionIt is a dispute between תנאים
  19. Resolutionרבי יוסי gives a different reason: the amounts are not fixed

  23. ResponseOxen can come apart, pouches cannot
  24. StatementA finder who says he returned one ox does swear
