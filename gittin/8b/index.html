Topic

The special status of סוריא, and a slave's freedom document that also gives property

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא about the special in-between status of סוריא and explains each of its laws. The תנא who obligates סוריא in מעשר and שביעית holds that כיבוש יחיד counts as a real conquest. A person can enter סוריא in טהרה by being carried inside a closed box. That works because the חכמים decreed טומאה only on סוריא's soil, not on its air. רב ששת explains that comparing a field in סוריא to one near ירושלים means a bill of sale may be written for it even on שבת. The writing is done by a גוי, and the חכמים lifted their usual ban for the sake of ישוב ארץ ישראל. The גמרא then turns to a slave who brings his own freedom document that also gives him his master's property. אביי tries two opposite rulings, and רבא rejects both. רבא rules that the slave goes free but does not get the property. רב אדא בר מתנה says this follows only רבי שמעון, who holds that a statement can be divided, and the גמרא begins quoting the משנה in פאה. That quote continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

כְּקוֹנָהּ בְּפַרְוָארֵי יְרוּשָׁלַיִם.

is like one who buys it in the outskirts of Jerusalem.

חַיֶּיבֶת בְּמַעֲשֵׂר וּבִשְׁבִיעִית כְּאֶרֶץ יִשְׂרָאֵל –

It is obligated in tithes and the Sabbatical year like the Land of Israel –

קָסָבַר כִּיבּוּשׁ יָחִיד שְׁמֵיהּ כִּיבּוּשׁ.

he holds that a conquest by an individual is called a conquest.

וְהָרוֹצֶה לִיכָּנֵס לָהּ בְּטׇהֳרָה נִכְנָס –

And one who wants to enter it in purity may enter –
