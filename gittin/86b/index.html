Gittin 86b

גיטין פ״ו ע״ב

Learn Gittin 86b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Gittin 86b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Flawed גיטין: what happens after the fact, the הלכה like רבי אלעזר, and גיטין for several couples

Overview

Our משנה lists flawed גיטין, such as one signed by only one witness. רב says a single-witness גט leaves the child כשר only if the husband wrote it himself. שמואל says even a סופר's handwriting is enough, and each answers the other's proof from a later משנה. רב rules that a woman who remarried with one of these flawed גיטין stays with her second husband only if she has children. The גמרא defends this against a challenge from יבמות by explaining that the flaw is only a rabbinic concern. לוי and רבי יוחנן say she never has to leave, and along the way the גמרא discusses whether a קרצית disqualifies מי חטאת. Many reports then confirm that the הלכה follows רבי אלעזר, who validates a גט handed over before witnesses. A new משנה follows about mixed-up identical גיטין and about one גט written for five couples. The גמרא debates whether that משנה fits רבי אלעזר, and then defines a shared wording versus separate formulas. רבי יוחנן's answer is given, and ריש לקיש's answer begins on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְדַוְקָא כְּתַב יָדוֹ וְעֵד;

And specifically his handwriting and one witness;

אֲבָל כְּתַב סוֹפֵר וְעֵד – לֹא.

but a scribe's handwriting and one witness – no.

וּשְׁמוּאֵל אָמַר:

And Shmuel said:

אֲפִילּוּ כְּתַב סוֹפֵר וְעֵד,

even a scribe's handwriting and one witness,
