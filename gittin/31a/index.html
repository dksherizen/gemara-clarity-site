Gittin 31a

גיטין ל״א ע״א

Learn Gittin 31a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Gittin 31a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Finishing the derivation that תרומת מעשר works like תרומה גדולה, then a new משנה about produce or money set aside for separating תרומות ומעשרות, and when we must worry that it was lost

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a source: the פסוק speaks of two kinds of תרומה, so the rules of תרומה גדולה also apply to תרומת מעשר. Both can be taken by estimate and by thought, and the בעל הבית may separate both. Then a new משנה begins. Someone who sets aside produce for separating תרומות ומעשרות, or money for redeeming מעשר שני, may later separate with them on the presumption that they still exist. If they turn out to be lost, רבי אלעזר says he must worry 'מעת לעת'. רבי יהודה adds that wine set aside this way must be checked at three set seasons of the year. The גמרא asks what 'מעת לעת' means, and רבי יוחנן says it means counting back 24 hours from the check that found the items missing. A second explanation, in the name of רבי אלעזר ברבי ינאי, begins at the end and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בִּשְׁתֵּי תְּרוּמוֹת הַכָּתוּב מְדַבֵּר:

The verse speaks of two terumot:

אַחַת תְּרוּמָה גְּדוֹלָה

one is the great (standard) teruma

וְאַחַת תְּרוּמַת מַעֲשֵׂר;

and one is teruma of the tithe.

כְּשֵׁם שֶׁתְּרוּמָה גְּדוֹלָה

Just as the great teruma

The steps (8)

  1. ProofTwo kinds of תרומה are compared: both can be taken by estimate and by thought
  2. ConclusionThe homeowner may separate תרומת מעשר too
  3. StatementProduce or money set aside for later separations is presumed to still exist
  4. StatementIf they were lost, he must worry 'from time to time'
  5. Statementרבי יהודה: wine set aside is checked three times a year
  6. QuestionWhat does 'מעת לעת' mean?
  7. Responseרבי יוחנן: count back 24 hours from the check
  8. ResponseA second explanation is introduced in the name of רבי אלעזר ברבי ינאי
