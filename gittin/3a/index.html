Topic

Why one שליח is believed when he says בפני נכתב ובפני נחתם, and the debate between רבה and רבא over the reason

Overview

This amud continues the discussion of a שליח who brings a גט from overseas and says בפני נכתב ובפני נחתם. The גמרא explains that חכמים trusted a single שליח because of עיגונא, the worry that a woman could be left stuck in her marriage. It asks whether this is really a קולא, since the husband could contest a גט backed by only one witness. The answer is that the גט is handed over in front of two or three people, so the שליח checks carefully and will not risk shaming himself. Following רבא's reason, that witnesses for קיום are hard to find, the גמרא asks why two witnesses are not needed. It answers with רבי שמעון בן לקיש's teaching: by Torah law a signed document needs no קיום at all, so חכמים were free to relax their own rule because of עיגונא. The גמרא then sets רבה and רבא against each other. Each explains why he rejects the other's reason, and each explains the wording of the משנה according to his own view. The amud ends by asking which תנא matches רבה's view, and that question continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מִשּׁוּם עִיגּוּנָא אַקִּילוּ בַּהּ רַבָּנַן.

Because of the concern of being left a deserted wife, the Sages were lenient with her.

הַאי קוּלָּא הוּא?! חוּמְרָא הוּא!

Is this a leniency?! It is a stringency!

דְּאִי מַצְרְכַתְּ לֵיהּ תְּרֵי –

For if you require two witnesses for it,

לָא אָתֵי בַּעַל מְעַרְעֵר וּפָסֵיל לֵיהּ;

the husband does not come, contest, and invalidate it;
