Gittin 24b

גיטין כ״ד ע״ב

Learn Gittin 24b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Gittin 24b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

A גט must be written לשמה, for a specific woman. The גמרא finds the source in the פסוק, discusses same-name documents, and asks whether invalid גיטין still forbid a woman to a כהן.

Overview

The משנה finishes its list of invalid גיטין. A גט written for one wife cannot divorce his other wife with the same name, and a גט written 'for whichever wife I choose' is invalid for both. The גמרא explains that the משנה's first case is about scribes practicing, proven from the word מקרין. A ברייתא from the school of רבי ישמעאל shows how each 'יתר מיכן' goes one step further. The גמרא finds each step in the words of the פסוק: ספר כריתות, וכתב, and לה (לשמה). The last case teaches אין ברירה. רבא learns a rule about two people with the same name collecting a שטר חוב, but אביי pushes it away: the משנה works only because of עדי מסירה, following רבי אלעזר. Finally, רב and שמואל argue whether these invalid גיטין still forbid a woman to a כהן. שמואל's view fits his rule that an invalid גט or חליצה is 'פסול ופוסל'.

Opening lines

יָתֵר מִיכֵּן –

Moreover,

הָיוּ לוֹ שְׁתֵּי נָשִׁים

if he had two wives

וּשְׁמוֹתֵיהֶן שָׁווֹת,

and their names were the same,

כָּתַב לְגָרֵשׁ אֶת הַגְּדוֹלָה –

and he wrote [a גט] to divorce the older one,
