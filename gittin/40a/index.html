Gittin 40a

גיטין מ׳ ע״א

Open Gittin 40a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

How a slave gains freedom: masters' actions that prove freedom, a dying master's instructions, a slave made ownerless, and a partner's trick to avoid freeing a slave.

Overview

The amud asks when a master's actions prove he freed his slave. רבי יוחנן challenges רבי זירא's ruling about a slave who marries a free woman in front of his master. The גמרא answers that the master himself married the slave off, and it explains a ברייתא about a master betrothing his own שפחה as a debate over wording. Next, רבי יהושע בן לוי says a slave who puts on תפילין in front of his master goes free, and רבה בר רב שילא limits this to a case where the master put the תפילין on him. Two students then bring different reports of רבי יוחנן's ruling about a dying master's words regarding his maidservant: רב דימי and רב שמואל בר יהודה. אמימר rules that a slave whose master was מפקיר him has no תקנה, and he defends this against רב אשי by calling רב דימי's report a mistake. רבינא, relying on רב דימי, tells slaves who were sold to a non-Jew to get a גט שחרור from the sons of their first masters, and the הלכה follows רבינא. The amud ends with a partner who gave his share of a half-freed slave to his minor son to avoid freeing him, and רב פפא begins explaining how the trick will backfire. The explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

יָצָא לְחֵירוּת.

he goes out to freedom.
