Topic

Conditional גיטין: אביי's two-condition reading, 'your presence,' גט ישן, the משנה of 'מעכשיו' and the husband's death, and רבותינו's lenient ruling.

Overview

The עמוד opens by finishing the משנה's travel cases. Twice, a husband who turned back at אנטיפרס or at עכו seems to clash with the geography, and both times אביי answers that the husband set two conditions: reaching the destination, or thirty days on the road. Next comes the husband who said 'if I pass from your presence thirty days' and kept coming and going. רב הונא says 'your presence' means marital relations. רבי יוחנן says it is literal, and that the גט simply has not become a גט ישן. A ברייתא supports רבי יוחנן. The worry that he won her over is answered by a husband who said she is believed to say he did not come, and the גמרא brings two versions of where this answer was said. A new משנה then teaches that without 'מעכשיו' a husband's death within twelve months cancels the גט, while with it the גט is valid. The same משנה covers instructions to messengers to 'write and give,' where רבי יוסי disagrees, and a case where it is unknown whether the גט or the death came first. The גמרא reports that 'our Rabbis' (the court of רבי יהודה נשיאה) let her remarry, following רבי יוסי's rule that a document's date shows when it takes effect. It then discusses רבי אלעזר's question of when she was freed. At the end, אביי begins a statement about a גט tied to sunrise, which continues on the next עמוד.
