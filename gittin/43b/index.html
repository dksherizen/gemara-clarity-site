Topic

Freeing a half-שפחה, and when a slave sold or pledged to a גוי goes free

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier ruling with a מה נפשך argument. A half-שפחה half-בת חורין woman betrothed to two brothers is not 'the wife of two dead men', because only one of the two קידושין could have worked. Next comes a מחלוקת about what רב נחמן said when such a woman is freed: one report says freedom cancels her first קידושין, and the other says it completes it. רבי זירא brings a proof from the פסוק of שפחה חרופה, and אביי shows the פסוק can mean she was freed and then betrothed again. A story follows in which a master was forced to free a half-שפחה. The גמרא explains it was because men were treating her as ownerless, not necessarily because of פרו ורבו. Then a new משנה teaches that a slave sold to גוים or to חוץ לארץ goes free, and a ברייתא adds that he needs a גט שחרור unless the master wrote 'when you escape, I have no business with you'. Last, a slave pledged to a גוי for a loan goes free once the גוי does נִמוּסוֹ. After rejecting 'a seal', the גמרא explains נִמוּסוֹ as a deadline and settles a clash with a field ברייתא: the slave's body was pledged, but the field only for its produce.
