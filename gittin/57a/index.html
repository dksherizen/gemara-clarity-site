Gittin 57a

גיטין נ״ז ע״א

Topic

The end of the אונקלוס story and the destructions of ירושלים, טור מלכא, כפר סכניא and ביתר

Overview

This amud finishes the story of אונקלוס, who raises טיטוס, בלעם and ישו from the dead. Each one says Israel is most important in the next world, and each is punished in a way that fits his sin. רבי אלעזר then closes the story of בר קמצא with a lesson: shame is so powerful that it led to the חורבן. Next the גמרא tells how טור מלכא was destroyed. A fight over a rooster and a hen was reported to the קיסר as a revolt. The hero בר דרומא fell because of his own words, and the Jews' celebration brought the Romans back. The גמרא describes how huge הר המלך once was and explains, with the image of a deer's skin, how ארץ ישראל can have held so many people. Three stories about the righteous town of כפר סכניא follow, and רב יוסף explains that it was punished for not mourning ירושלים. Finally, a cedar cut down for a broken carriage pole leads to the destruction of ביתר, where the bloodshed was enormous.

Opening lines

בְּמַאי דִּפְסַיק אַנַּפְשֵׁיהּ –

With what he decreed upon himself –

כֹּל יוֹמָא מְכַנְּשִׁי לֵיהּ לְקִיטְמֵיהּ

every day they gather his ashes

וְדָיְינִי לֵיהּ, וְקָלוּ לֵיהּ

and they judge him, and they burn him

וּמְבַדְּרוּ [לֵיהּ] אַשַּׁב יַמֵּי.

and they scatter him over the seven seas.
