Gittin 42a

Topic

Freeing half a slave: רבה's limit on the מחלוקת, the two ברייתות about giving property to slaves, and the half-slave's daily status

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an answer: a person can be half slave and half free when two partners own a slave and one of them frees his share, and everyone agrees about that case. רבה then says that רבי and רבנן argue about freeing half a slave only when the master keeps the other half. If the master sells or gives away the other half, everyone agrees the freeing works. אביי challenges this with two ברייתות about a master who gives his property to two slaves: one says they free each other, the other says they get nothing. The גמרא offers several ways to settle the clash: 'all of it' versus 'half and half', one document versus two, and giving at the same time versus one after the other. The timing answer is rejected. רב אשי suggests that calling them 'my slaves' shows the master did not mean to free them, but רפרם proves from a משנה that 'my slave' can just mean 'who was my slave'. The גמרא then turns to a half-slave gored by an ox: the payment follows whose day it was, but marriage prohibitions do not switch by the day. Finally, a ברייתא about an ox that killed a half-slave is brought to test the day-by-day rule, and the discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בְּעֶבֶד שֶׁל שְׁנֵי שׁוּתָּפִין,

With a slave belonging to two partners,

וְדִבְרֵי הַכֹּל.

and that is everyone's opinion.

אָמַר רַבָּה:

Rabba said:

מַחֲלוֹקֶת – בְּשֶׁשִּׁיחְרֵר חֶצְיוֹ

The dispute is when he freed half of him
