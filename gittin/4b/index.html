Topic

Why must a שליח say בפני נכתב ובפני נחתם? Can רבה (not experts in לשמה) and רבא (no witnesses available) each explain the משניות, and what is the practical difference between them?

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a suggestion: maybe the argument in the משנה is the same as the argument between רבה and רבא. That argument is about why a שליח must say בפני נכתב ובפני נחתם. רבה says the reason is that people overseas are not experts in writing a גט לשמה. רבא says it is because witnesses are not around to confirm the signatures. The גמרא rejects the suggestion, because each of them can explain the משנה his own way. Next the גמרא reads an inference out of the משנה's words 'from region to region overseas.' It gives two versions of this challenge, one against רבה and one against רבא, and answers both. Along the way it explains that within ארץ ישראל witnesses are easy to find, first because of the festival pilgrims and later because of fixed courts. The case of the two districts of עססיות seems to fit only רבא. The גמרא answers that רבה holds רבא's reason too and simply adds his own. It then names two cases where they really differ: two people bringing the גט, and a גט brought within one region overseas. Finally, the גמרא quotes a משנה about a שליח who 'is unable to say' the declaration and starts to ask what those words mean.
