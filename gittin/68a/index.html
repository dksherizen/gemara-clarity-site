Gittin 68a

גיטין ס״ח ע״א

Topic

רב ששת's escape from the servants, and how שלמה captured אשמדאי to find the שמיר

Overview

This amud finishes the story of רב ששת at the house of ריש גלותא. The hostile servants find his uneaten meat hidden in his scarf. רב ששת proves, using a rule of רב חסדא, that the meat came from a sick animal. The servants then hide a pit to trap him, but a warning snort from רב חסדא, a child's פסוק and his own suspicion help him avoid it. Next the גמרא explains a פסוק in קהלת about שלמה's luxuries, which in בבל was read as saying שלמה had demons. To build the בית המקדש without iron, שלמה needed the שמיר, and the demons sent him to אשמדאי, their king. בניהו tricks אשמדאי with wine, chains him with God's Name, and starts bringing him back. The story continues on the next amud at a widow's hut.
