Gittin 79b

גיטין ע״ט ע״ב

Learn Gittin 79b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Gittin 79b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Where a thrown גט takes effect (roofs, courtyards, baskets), using a גט ישן, and the penalties for a wrongly dated גט

Overview

This amud opens by finishing a list of points about roofs. On שבת, שמואל says each roof is its own separate area. For a גט, though, a neighboring roof of the husband still works, because a husband does not mind her using it. אביי then rules on a case where her courtyard sits inside his, and his walls are taller than hers. In that case a thrown גט counts as hers once it drops below his walls. Baskets work differently: the גט must actually land in her basket, and this works only if his outer basket has no bottom. A new משנה follows. בית שמאי and בית הלל argue about a גט ישן, and the גמרא explains that the argument is about a גזרה that people might think her divorce came before her son. Another משנה lists improper ways to date or place a גט. If she remarried using such a גט, she must leave both husbands, loses her money rights, and faces other penalties as well.

Opening lines

וְהָא דְּאָמַר רַב יְהוּדָה אָמַר שְׁמוּאֵל:

And that which Rav Yehuda said in the name of Shmuel:

לֹא יַעֲמוֹד אָדָם בְּגַג זֶה

A person should not stand on this roof

וְיִקְלוֹט מֵי גְשָׁמִים

and catch rainwater

מִגַּגּוֹ שֶׁל חֲבֵירוֹ,

from the roof of his fellow,

  2. ConclusionThat rule is only for שבת, not for a גט

  5. ResponseThe outer walls protect the inner courtyard too
  6. StatementBaskets work differently from courtyards
