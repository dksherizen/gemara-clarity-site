Topic

Splitting one document into a part that works and a part that fails (רב נחמן and רב אשי); who contests a גט; a שליח who cannot say בפני נכתב; and how גיטי נשים and שחרורי עבדים are alike.

Overview

The עמוד opens at the end of רבי שמעון's view that a slave given 'all the property' goes free, which was brought to show that one document can work for one part and fail for another. The גמרא pushes back: רב נחמן ruled like רבי מאיר, not רבי שמעון. But רב נחמן also ruled that a שכיב מרע who wrote all his property to his slave and recovered takes back the property while the slave stays free, which looks like splitting a statement. רב אשי explains that רב נחמן followed רבי מאיר for a different reason: a release document must fully cut off the slave, and holding back property means it does not. Next, the גמרא asks who the 'contesters' of a גט are in the משנה. One person is not enough and two would create a tie, so it must be the husband himself. A new משנה teaches that if the שליח bringing a גט from overseas cannot say בפני נכתב ובפני נחתם, the גט is confirmed through its signing witnesses. רב יוסף explains that the שליח was capable when he delivered it and became a חרש afterward. Finally, a ברייתא begins listing three ways גיטי נשים and שחרורי עבדים are alike, and the third way continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

לְעוֹלָם הוּא בֶּן חוֹרִין,

He is always a free man,

עַד שֶׁיֹּאמַר ״כֹּל נְכָסַיי נְתוּנִין

unless he says: "All my property is given

לִפְלוֹנִי עַבְדִּי

to so-and-so my slave

חוּץ מֵאֶחָד מֵרִיבּוֹא שֶׁבָּהֶן״.

except for one ten-thousandth of it."
