Gittin 39b

Topic

Conflicting reports on אבא שאול and on רבי שמעון: whether a slave can be freed with money or only with a שטר

Overview

רבי זירא tests two opposite reports about whether the הלכה follows אבא שאול. רבי יעקב בר אידי reports in the name of רבי יהושע בן לוי that it does. רבי חייא בר אבא reports in the name of רבי יוחנן that it does not. For each report, רבי זירא asks whether it was heard directly or worked out from רבי's ruling that a slave goes free only with a שטר. רבי יוחנן explained that ruling with a גזירה שוה comparing a slave to a wife. רבי זירא shows that this comparison can be stretched in either direction, and both reporters answer that they heard their ruling directly. The גמרא then asks how רבי can say 'only a שטר' when another ברייתא has him letting a slave buy himself free. It answers that money or a שטר usually works, but here the master's money hold is already gone. This general rule rejects רבי שמעון in the name of רבי עקיבא, who learns from a פסוק that only a שטר fully frees a slave. Reports differ on whether the הלכה follows רבי שמעון. A story about רב נחמן and a master's hat turns out to rest on a different reason (כליו של מקנה), and the גמרא rules that the הלכה does not follow רבי שמעון. The amud ends by opening a new case: an עבד who marries a free woman in front of his master, with the ruling on the next amud.
