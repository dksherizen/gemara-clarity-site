Gittin 18b

גיטין י״ח ע״ב

Topic

How long a delay is allowed between writing and signing a גט, and what happens when a husband tells ten people to write a גט for his wife

Overview

This amud opens by finishing an argument about a delay between writing a גט and signing it. One view worries that during the delay the husband may have made up with his wife, so it allows only a short gap. רבי יוחנן says even ten days is fine, because a reconciliation would become known. The גמרא then brings a new argument between רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש about a husband who tells ten men to write a גט for his wife. Using a משנה, the גמרא shows that the case must be where he said 'all of you,' so all ten must sign. רבי יוחנן says only two are real עדים and the rest sign just to fulfill the husband's תנאי. ריש לקיש says all ten are real עדים. Their views differ in two cases: when only two signed on the day of writing and the rest signed later, and when one signer turns out to be a קרוב or פסול. Next comes a further dispute about an unfit person who signed first. The amud ends with a real case brought to רבי יהושע בן לוי, whose ruling begins on the next amud.

Opening lines

חָיְישִׁינַן שֶׁמָּא פִּיֵּיס.

We are worried that perhaps he made up with her.

וְרַבִּי יוֹחָנָן אָמַר:

And Rabbi Yoḥanan said:

אֲפִילּוּ מִכָּאן וְעַד עֲשָׂרָה יָמִים;

Even from now until ten days later the get is valid;

אִם אִיתָא דְּפַיֵּיס –

if it were so that he made up with her,
