Gittin 22b

גיטין כ״ב ע״ב

Topic

Limits on רבי אלעזר's ruling about an easily altered גט, and a new משנה on who may write a גט

Overview

The גמרא first explains that the ruling allowing a גט on a surface where writing is easy to change follows the תנא רבי אלעזר. He holds that עדי מסירה, the witnesses who watch the handing over, make the divorce work, so they can confirm what the גט said. The אמורא רבי אלעזר limits this in two ways. First, the witnesses must confirm the גט right away, in case a condition was erased. רבי יוחנן disagrees and says even days later is fine, because witnesses would remember a condition. Second, רבי אלעזר says the ruling covers only a גט and not other שטרות, based on a פסוק in ירמיהו about documents lasting many days. רבי יוחנן says it covers all שטרות, and the גמרא explains that the פסוק is only good advice. Then a new משנה teaches that anyone, even a חרש, שוטה or קטן, may write a גט, because its validity comes from its signers. The גמרא asks how people without understanding can write a גט, and רב הונא's answer begins on the next amud.

Opening lines

רַבִּי אֶלְעָזָר הִיא,

It is Rabbi Elazar's view,

דְּאָמַר: עֵדֵי מְסִירָה כָּרְתִי.

who says: the witnesses of the handing over make the divorce take effect.

וְאָמַר רַבִּי אֶלְעָזָר:

And Rabbi Elazar said:

לֹא הִכְשִׁיר רַבִּי אֶלְעָזָר אֶלָּא לְאַלְתַּר,

Rabbi Elazar only declared it valid right away,
