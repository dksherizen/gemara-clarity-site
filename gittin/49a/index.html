Gittin 49a

גיטין מ״ט ע״א

Learn Gittin 49a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Gittin 49a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Whose land sets the standard of עידית in damages, and what רבי עקיבא's 'קל וחומר for הקדש' refers to.

Overview

The גמרא explains the exact case where רבי ישמעאל and רבי עקיבא argue: the damager's worst land is as good as the injured party's best land. רבי ישמעאל measures 'best' by the injured party's land, using a גזירה שוה on the word 'field'. רבי עקיבא measures by the damager's own land, reading 'מיטב שדהו' plainly. רבי ישמעאל uses both the גזירה שוה and the plain פסוק. The גמרא then asks what רבי עקיבא means by 'a קל וחומר for הקדש'. It rejects a pledge to בדק הבית as the case, because a creditor collects only from בינונית and הקדש is weaker in damages. It settles on an ox goring a הקדש ox, following רבי שמעון בן מנסיא. The גמרא then asks whether the whole argument might only be about רבי שמעון בן מנסיא. It rejects this from רבי עקיבא's own words and begins a further proof from רב אשי that continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

הָכָא בְּמַאי עָסְקִינַן –

With what are we dealing here?

כְּגוֹן שֶׁהָיְתָה עִידִּית דְּנִיזָּק

Such as where the best land of the injured party

כְּזִיבּוּרִית דְּמַזִּיק;

was like the worst land of the damager;

רַבִּי יִשְׁמָעֵאל סָבַר:

Rabbi Yishmael holds:
