Gittin 67b

גיטין ס״ז ע״ב

Learn Gittin 67b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Gittin 67b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Telling a group to write or deliver a גט, the start of the chapter on a husband seized by קורדייקוס, and the remedies and stories that follow.

Overview

This amud starts by closing an earlier argument, noting that some switch the opinions of רבה and רב יוסף. Then a ברייתא explains what happens when a husband tells ten people to write or deliver a גט. A plain instruction lets one person act for all, but 'all of you' means everyone must be present. רב הונא and רבי יוחנן seem to argue about a husband who lists the people by name, and רב פפא explains that they are talking about different cases. רב יהודה offers a safe formula for speaking to a group, and רבא improves it by dropping 'all of you' completely. After the הדרן, a new chapter opens with a משנה about a husband seized by קורדייקוס and a mute husband who nods. The גמרא explains that קורדייקוס is confusion from new wine, named after a רוח. This leads into a list of remedies for fever and chills, and the story of how רב עמרם חסידא outsmarted the ריש גלותא's household. The amud ends with רב ששת proving that the ריש גלותא's servants are careless about אבר מן החי, and the story continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְאִיכָּא דְּאָפֵיךְ לְהוּ.

And there are those who reverse them.

אָמַר לַעֲשָׂרָה: ״כִּתְבוּ גֵּט״.

If he said to ten: 'Write a get.'

תָּנוּ רַבָּנַן,

The Rabbis taught:

אָמַר לַעֲשָׂרָה: ״כִּתְבוּ גֵּט, וּתְנוּ לְאִשְׁתִּי״ –

If one said to ten: 'Write a get and give it to my wife' –
