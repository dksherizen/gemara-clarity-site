Topic

Which תנא our משנה follows on writing and signing לשמה, and whether the opinions in the משנה reflect the two reasons for 'בפני נכתב ובפני נחתם'

Overview

Our משנה has the messenger say 'בפני נכתב ובפני נחתם', which suggests that both the writing and the signing of a גט must be לשמה. The גמרא finishes showing that רבי מאיר cannot be the author, because רב נחמן reports that רבי מאיר accepts a גט found in the garbage. It rejects the defense that this is only Torah law. It then explains the משנה as רבי אלעזר, who requires proper signing whenever witnesses do sign, and supports this with רבי אבא. רב אשי says instead that the משנה follows רבי יהודה, and the גמרא explains why it tried רבי מאיר and רבי אלעזר first. Next, the גמרא looks at רבן גמליאל and רבי אליעזר, who require the declaration even for towns right next to ארץ ישראל. It asks whether their argument with the תנא קמא matches the two reasons for the declaration: lack of experts in לשמה, or lack of witnesses to confirm the גט. It answers that רבה and רבא can each explain all three opinions with his own reason. Finally, it turns to the חכמים, who add 'the one who takes it overseas', and begins a similar suggestion that continues on the next amud.
