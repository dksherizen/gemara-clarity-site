Topic

A נזיר's hair: full shaving, רובו ככולו, washing and combing, and repeated sins

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier proof: ציצית written next to כלאים teaches that a positive מצוה can override a prohibition. Then the גמרא returns to the rule that a נזיר, מצורע or לוי who shaves without a razor, or leaves two hairs, has done nothing. From this רב אחא בריה דרב איקא learns that רובו ככולו is Torah law, since the Torah had to spell out a full shave for a נזיר. The scholars of ארץ ישראל defend this proof against רבי יוסי ברבי חנינא's objection. אביי and רבא then ask about leftover hairs that are shaved later or fall out, and רבינא rules that shaving a single last hair is no מצוה of shaving. A new משנה lets a נזיר wash and separate his hair but not comb it, and רבה explains that the whole משנה follows רבי שמעון because combing is done on purpose to remove loose hairs. רבי ישמעאל forbids washing with earth, and the גמרא's question about his exact wording ends in תיקו. The amud closes with a משנה teaching that a נזיר who keeps sinning is punished once, unless he was warned each time.

Opening lines

הָא ״גְּדִילִים תַּעֲשֶׂה לָּךְ״ מֵהֶם.

This teaches that 'You shall make for yourself fringes' can be from them.

אָמַר מָר:

The Master said:

וְכוּלָּם שֶׁגִּילְּחוּ שֶׁלֹּא בְּתַעַר,

And all of them who shaved not with a razor,

אוֹ שֶׁשִּׁיְּירוּ שְׁתֵּי שְׂעָרוֹת —

or who left two hairs —
