Nazir 17b

נזיר י״ז ע״ב

Learn Nazir 17b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Nazir 17b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

רבא's unresolved question about שהייה for a נזיר in a cemetery, and רב אשי's question whether one who vowed נזירות in a בית הקברות must shave.

Overview

This amud first finishes רבא's question. The case is a person carried into a בית הקברות inside a closed wooden container who vowed נזירות, and then someone opened the lid. Does the rule of שהייה, known from the מקדש, also apply to a נזיר in a cemetery? The question ends in תיקו. Then רב אשי asks a new question: if someone vowed נזירות while already in a cemetery, must he shave (תגלחת) when he becomes pure? The גמרא tries three sources: the משנה about one who vowed in a cemetery, a ברייתא listing the only difference between a טמא שנזר and a טהור שנטמא, and a ברייתא about a נזיר who became a מצורע. Each proof is turned aside by reading the source differently. In the last source, the words 'his hair is fit for shaving' are read as referring to the final תגלחת טהרה, not a תגלחת טומאה. The גמרא then starts to show why that reading makes more sense, and the argument continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא: כְּגוֹן שֶׁנִּכְנַס

Rather: for example, where he entered

בְּשִׁידָּה תֵּיבָה וּמִגְדָּל,

in a chest, box, or cabinet,

וּבָא חֲבֵירוֹ וּפָרַע

and his friend came and opened

מֵעָלָיו מַעֲזִיבָה.

the cover from above him.

The steps (16)

  1. ResolutionSetting up the exact case of רבא's question
  2. QuestionDoes the rule of delay apply outside the מקדש?
  3. ConclusionThe question stays unresolved
  4. QuestionDoes someone who vowed while impure need to shave?
  5. ProofTrying to prove from the משנה that he must shave
  6. RejectionThe משנה is giving a reason, not leaving out shaving
  7. ProofProof from the list of differences between the two kinds of impure נזיר
  8. RejectionNo, they are equal regarding lashes
  9. ChallengeIf shaving differs, the ברייתא should have said so
  10. Resolution'His seventh day' includes everything that follows from it
  11. ProofNew ברייתא: do days of confirmed צרעת count toward נזירות?
  12. RejectionThe ברייתא rejects its own comparison
  13. ResolutionThe ברייתא proves its point by comparing to a נזיר in a cemetery
  14. ProofTrying to prove that a נזיר in a cemetery must do a shaving of impurity
  15. RejectionNo, it means the shaving of purity
  16. ProofShowing that the shaving of purity is the better reading
