Nazir 36a

נזיר ל״ו ע״א

Open Nazir 36a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Does a permitted food join with a forbidden food to reach the minimum amount (היתר מצטרף לאיסור)? First, זעירי applies it to burning leaven on the מזבח. Then אביי challenges רב דימי's report of רבי יוחנן's rule.

Overview

זעירי teaches that the rule 'permitted food joins with forbidden food to make up the amount' also applies to the ban on offering leaven (שאור) on the מזבח. The גמרא says he follows רבי אלעזר, who learns from the word 'כל' that mixtures count. It asks why זעירי did not also mention eating חמץ on פסח. The answer is that the rule applies there too. זעירי's real point is to reject אביי and teach that offering less than a כזית is not punishable. Then רב דימי repeats רבי יוחנן's teaching that permitted joins with forbidden only for a נזיר. אביי objects from a משנה about a soup with תרומה garlic and oil that a טבול יום touched. רבי יוחנן himself explained that a זר is lashed for eating a כזית of that soup, even though most of it is חולין. אביי asks for the reason behind this, building a contradiction that the גמרא takes up on the next amud.

Opening lines

זְעֵירִי אָמַר:

Ze'eiri said:

אַף שְׂאוֹר בְּבַל תַּקְטִירוּ.

Also leaven, regarding 'you shall not burn it on the altar.'

כְּמַאן —

In accordance with whom?

כְּרַבִּי אֶלְעָזָר,

Like Rabbi Elazar,

  1. Statementזעירי: the joining rule also applies to offering leaven
  2. QuestionWhose opinion does זעירי follow?
  3. ResponseHe follows רבי אלעזר, who learns from 'כל'
  4. ChallengeThen the same should apply to eating חמץ
  5. ResolutionYes, and זעירי's point is no liability below a כזית
  6. Challengeאביי challenges רב דימי from the soup משנה
  7. Proofרבי יוחנן's own reason: a זר is lashed for a כזית
  8. QuestionAsking for the reason behind the lashes
