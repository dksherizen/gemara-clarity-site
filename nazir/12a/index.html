Nazir 12a

נזיר י״ב ע״א

Topic

Is a man forbidden to all women after sending an agent to betroth an unnamed woman? רבי יוחנן's ruling and ריש לקיש's challenge from bird offerings (קינים)

Overview

רבי יוחנן rules that if a man tells a שליח, 'Go betroth a woman for me' without naming her, he becomes forbidden to every woman in the world. We assume the שליח did his job, but the man does not know who his wife is, so any woman might be her close relative. ריש לקיש challenges this from a משנה about offering birds: when a consecrated bird flies away, we do not suspect every bird in the world of being the runaway. רבי יוחנן answers that a woman is fixed in her home, while a bird moves around, so with birds we follow the majority. Even if the woman was betrothed in the market, she goes back home, and a bird does not. רבא then limits the ruling: a woman with no relatives the שליח could have betrothed is permitted. This includes a woman whose relative was married when the שליח was sent and divorced later. The גמרא explains that a person sends a שליח only for women available at that moment. It then challenges this from a משנה about two people who each promise to pay for a נזיר's offerings, where the first seems to be able to pay for someone who was not yet a נזיר when he spoke. The answer comes on the next amud.
