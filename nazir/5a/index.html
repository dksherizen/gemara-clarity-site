Topic

How often אבשלום cut his hair, and the source for a thirty-day סתם נזירות

Overview

The amud continues a ברייתא about how often אבשלום, a נזיר, cut his hair. רבי says once every twelve months, learned by a גזירה שוה from the word ימים by בתי ערי חומה. רבי נהוראי says every thirty days, and רבי יוסי says every ערב שבת. The גמרא defends רבי's source against several challenges: other פסוקים where ימים means two years, thirty days, or appears as ימימה. It rules that a plain ימים must be learned from an identical plain ימים. It then explains רבי נהוראי's thirty days through the weight of the hair, and shows how רבי יוסי's weekly haircut still set אבשלום apart from his brothers. After a short note about the 'forty years' in the פסוק, a new משנה teaches that an unspecified נזירות lasts thirty days. רב מתנא learns this from the גימטריא of יהיה. בר פדא counts the word נזיר and gets twenty-nine, and the גמרא explains why רב מתנא does not use that count.
