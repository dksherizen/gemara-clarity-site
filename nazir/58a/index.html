Topic

Does the מצורע's shaving override the איסור of הקפת הראש, or the איסורים of נזיר? Two ברייתות are compared to see if they reflect a dispute about whether shaving the whole head counts as הקפה.

Overview

The גמרא finishes a ברייתא that uses the word 'ראשו' to teach that a מצורע may shave his head even though rounding the head is forbidden. A second ברייתא uses the same word to teach that a נזיר who is a מצורע must shave. The גמרא suggests the two ברייתות disagree about whether shaving the whole head counts as הקפה. רבא answers that everyone agrees it does not, but ריש לקיש's rule (keep both a עשה and a לא תעשה when you can) undercuts his answer. So the גמרא switches: everyone agrees that shaving the whole head is הקפה. The second ברייתא then learns that a עשה overrides a plain לאו from ציצית made of wool and linen. The first ברייתא can't use ציצית, because that פסוק is busy with רבא's rule about which strings fit which garments. Instead it learns the נזיר case from 'זקנו', which lets a כהן who is a מצורע shave his beard. The גמרא then asks why the second ברייתא doesn't learn from 'זקנו' too, and begins a 'וליטעמיך' reply that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

יָכוֹל אַף מְצוֹרָע כֵּן —

One might think that even a leper is likewise [forbidden to round his head]
