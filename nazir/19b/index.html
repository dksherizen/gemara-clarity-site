Topic

When טומאה cancels a נזיר's earlier days (refuting עולא's reading of רבי אליעזר, and the minimum 'days'). Also a new משנה on a נזיר who finished outside ארץ ישראל.

Overview

רבא gives a פסוק as the source for רבי אליעזר's view as עולא understood it. He reads 'כי טמא נזרו' as speaking about someone who vowed נזירות while already טמא. אביי refutes this with a ברייתא that applies 'והימים הראשונים יפלו' to a נזיר who was טהור when he vowed and only became impure later. The ברייתא gives three cases: impure on the first day, on day 100, and on day 99, and only the last case cancels his days. רב פפא then asks how many days count as 'days', and רבא answers from the word 'יפלו'. The גמרא explains that the words 'ימים' and 'יפלו' together set the minimum at one full day plus the start of a second. A new משנה follows about a נזיר who finished his term outside ארץ ישראל: בית שמאי require 30 more days, and בית הלל require the whole term again. The משנה tells the story of הילני המלכה, who ended up keeping 21 years, though רבי יהודה says only 14. The גמרא begins to suggest that the argument depends on whether the טומאה of ארץ העמים was decreed only on its earth, and this discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אָמַר רָבָא:

Rava said:

מַאי טַעְמָא דְּרַבִּי אֱלִיעֶזֶר?

What is the reason of Rabbi Eliezer?

אָמַר קְרָא: ״כִּי טָמֵא נִזְרוֹ״ —

The verse says: "because his nezirus was impure" —

מִשּׁוּם דִּבְטוּמְאָה נְזַר.

because he vowed nezirus while in impurity.
