Topic

How much of a dead person's bones and blood makes a נזיר shave: the רובע of bones from a spine and skull, the old dispute about רובע and רביעית, and what the משנה's words על אלו leave out.

Overview

The גמרא is asking whether a רובע (quarter-קב) of bones from a spine and skull makes a נזיר impure so that he must shave. It first deals with an apparent contradiction in רבא's views and answers that he changed his mind after hearing רבי עקיבא. It then tries to prove the answer from שמאי, who says even one bone from the spine or skull is impure. Both possible proofs from שמאי are pushed away, so neither side is proven. Next, רבי אליעזר reports that the early זקנים argued about whether a רובע of bones and a רביעית of blood make impure in every way. A later בית דין ruled that these smaller amounts count for תרומה and קדשים, but not for a נזיר or for someone bringing the קרבן פסח. The גמרא asks how a middle opinion like this can decide a dispute, and answers that it was a tradition from חגי, זכריה and מלאכי. Finally, the גמרא explains what the words על אלו in the משנה leave out, and quotes the next item in the list, a חצי קב of bones.
