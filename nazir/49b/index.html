Nazir 49b

נזיר מ״ט ע״ב

Open Nazir 49b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The words of the פסוק about a כהן גדול and the dead, the משנה's list of impurities that make a נזיר shave, and רבי יהודה's objection to סומכוס's version

Overview

This amud opens by finishing an earlier discussion about a כהן גדול. Each word of the פסוק "עַל כׇּל נַפְשׁוֹת מֵת לֹא יָבֹא" teaches a separate case: the unrelated dead, relatives, and a רביעית of blood that came from two dead people. Then a new משנה lists the kinds of טומאת מת that make a נזיר shave. It explains that he becomes טמא from them by touching, carrying, or being under one roof (אוהל). A barley-size bone is different: it counts only for touching and carrying. For all of these, he shaves, is sprinkled on the third and seventh days, loses his earlier days, and starts counting again only after he is pure and brings his קרבנות. The גמרא then tells a story. After רבי מאיר died, רבי יהודה barred his students, but סומכוס pushed his way in. סומכוס quoted רבי מאיר's version of the משנה, which lists both a whole מת and a כזית of a מת. רבי יהודה angrily asked why a whole מת needs to be listed at all, since if a כזית makes him shave, a whole מת surely does. The answer comes on the next amud.

"dead" — teaches about relatives,

"bodies" — teaches about a quarter-log of blood

that came out of two dead people,
