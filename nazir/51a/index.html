Topic

רקב of a מת: which corpse dust carries טומאה, and what counts as a mixture (גלגלין)

Overview

The משנה listed a ladle-full of רקב (decayed corpse dust) as a טומאה that makes a נזיר shave, and this amud asks which dust counts. A ברייתא teaches that the dust must come only from the body, not mixed with cloth, wood, or brick. עולא adds that it must come from flesh, גידים, and bones together, and he defends this against רבא's challenges. רבי יוחנן rules that two bodies buried together become a mixture (גלגלין). רבא explains that the teaching calling dust from two bodies טמא is about bodies that rotted separately. רבי יוחנן also rules that cut hair buried with the body makes a mixture, which leads to חזקיה's question about hair that is ready to be cut but still attached. רבי ירמיה then asks about dust that comes by way of the heel, and that question ends in תיקו. Finally, he asks whether a dead עובר inside its mother forms a mixture with her, and the follow-up question continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

תָּנוּ רַבָּנַן: אֵיזֶהוּ מֵת שֶׁיֵּשׁ לוֹ רָקָב?

The Rabbis taught: Which dead body has the law of dust?

מֵת שֶׁנִּקְבַּר עָרוֹם בְּאָרוֹן שֶׁל שַׁיִשׁ

A body that was buried naked in a marble coffin

אוֹ עַל גַּבֵּי רִצְפָּה שֶׁל אֲבָנִים —

or on top of a stone floor —

this is a body that has the law of dust.
