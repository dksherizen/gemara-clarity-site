Nazir 55b

נזיר נ״ה ע״ב

Learn Nazir 55b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Nazir 55b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Whether the days a נזיר spends as a מצורע count toward his נזירות: רב שרביה's proof from a משנה and his own answer, followed by the start of a new objection from רמי בר חמא.

Overview

The גמרא is checking whether the days a נזיר spends as a confirmed מצורע count toward his נזירות. First it finishes a proof from a משנה that says that after his צרעת shaving the נזיר 'starts counting immediately'. A short נזירות doesn't fit that wording, so the גמרא argues the משנה must be about a long נזירות. That would suggest the צרעת days are lost. רב שרביה, who brought this proof himself, then turns it back. He reads the משנה as a fifty-day נזירות in which the נזיר got צרעת after twenty days. The thirty days he still has left give him the hair growth he needs, so the משנה proves nothing. Finally, רמי בר חמא starts a new challenge from another משנה, about a נזיר whose טומאה and whose status as a confirmed מצורע are both uncertain. That quote continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא לָאו, בִּנְזִירוּת מְרוּבָּה,

Rather, is it not about a lengthy naziriteship,

וְקָתָנֵי: מַתְחִיל וּמוֹנֶה מִיָּד!

and it teaches: he starts counting immediately!

הוּא מוֹתֵיב לַהּ וְהוּא מְפָרֵק לַהּ:

He raised the objection and he resolved it:

בִּנְזִירוּת בַּת חֲמִשִּׁים יוֹם,

about a naziriteship of fifty days,

The steps (3)

  1. ProofThe משנה must be about a long נזירות, yet he starts counting right away
  2. RejectionA fifty-day נזירות explains the משנה
  3. Challengeרמי בר חמא begins a new objection from a משנה about doubtful status
