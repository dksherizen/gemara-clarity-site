Nazir 14b

נזיר י״ד ע״ב

Open Nazir 14b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

טומאה during צרעת and hair-growth days, רב חסדא's 'no fix' after the blood, and which איסורים still apply to a נזיר whose days are complete.

Overview

The amud opens with a second argument between רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש. A נזיר who had צרעת became טמא מת during his צרעת. Does that wipe out (סותר) his earlier days? רבי יוחנן says yes, because he is still inside his נזירות. ריש לקיש says no, because צרעת and נזירות are separate. The גמרא explains why this argument must be recorded both here and in the earlier case of נזירות for a son. Next, רב and שמואל argue about טומאה during the hair-growth days after the צרעת, and each shows his view fits even the opposing אמורא. רב חסדא then rules that once the hair was made holy by the blood, טומאה leaves 'no fix'. The גמרא explains he follows the רבנן and means only the מצוה of shaving. Finally, רבי יוסי ברבי חנינא rules that a נזיר whose days are over still gets מלקות for טומאה, but not for shaving or wine. The גמרא challenges why the word 'כל' does not extend shaving and wine too, and the answer comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

נִטְמָא בִּימֵי צָרַעְתּוֹ —

If he became impure during the days of his leprosy —

רַבִּי יוֹחָנָן אָמַר: סוֹתֵר.

Rabbi Yochanan says: it negates.

רֵישׁ לָקִישׁ אָמַר: אֵינוֹ סוֹתֵר.

Reish Lakish says: it does not negate.

רַבִּי יוֹחָנָן אָמַר סוֹתֵר,

Rabbi Yochanan says it negates,
