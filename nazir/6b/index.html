Nazir 6b

נזיר ו׳ ע״ב

Open Nazir 6b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Is a standard נזירות 30 days or 29? The גמרא tests this through רבי אליעזר's rulings and through the dispute of רבי יאשיה and רבי יונתן about the פסוק 'עד מלאת הימים'.

Overview

Earlier on the daf, רב מתנא holds that a standard נזירות is 30 days, and בר פדא holds it is 29 days. בר פדא supports his view from רבי אליעזר in a משנה: a נזיר who becomes טמא on day 30 loses only seven days, which sounds like his term was already over. The גמרא defends רב מתנא by saying רבי אליעזר holds מקצת היום ככולו, so part of day 30 counts as the whole day. It then challenges this from the next case in that משנה, a 100-day נזיר, where רבי אליעזר says he loses thirty days. ריש לקיש explains that ruling with a פסוק: a נזיר who becomes טמא on the day his term ends gets a standard נזירות. Next the גמרא suggests that רב מתנא and בר פדא follow רבי יאשיה and רבי יונתן, who learn the minimum term from different words in the פסוק. רב מתנא answers that both תנאים require thirty days and argue only about whether 'עד' includes the endpoint. Finally, the גמרא explains why רבי יונתן's 'days that need completing' means a month and not a week: a week is never short, but a month can be.

Opening lines

אָמַר לָךְ בַּר פְּדָא:

Bar Padda could say to you:

אֵימָא סֵיפָא,

Say the latter clause,

רַבִּי אֱלִיעֶזֶר אוֹמֵר:

Rabbi Eliezer says:

אֵינוֹ סוֹתֵר אֶלָּא שִׁבְעָה.

It cancels only seven days.
