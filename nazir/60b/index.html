Nazir 60b

נזיר ס׳ ע״ב

Learn Nazir 60b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Nazir 60b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Waiting periods for a נזיר with mixed doubtful and definite טומאה and צרעת, and רבי שמעון בן יוחאי's teaching that one shaving cannot count for both a נזיר and a מצורע.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the condition a doubtful נזיר says over his עולה. If he was simply a pure נזיר, the first עולה was the required one and this one is a gift. If he was טמא and a confirmed מצורע, this one is the required one. Next the גמרא lists three cases that mix doubtful or definite טומאת מת with doubtful or definite צרעת. For each case it says when he may eat קדשים and when he may drink wine and become טמא למת. Then רבי שמעון בן יוחאי's students ask whether a נזיר who is also a מצורע can shave once and have it count for both. He says no. The students suggest one pairing of shavings after another. Each time he points to a difference: removing hair versus growing it, shaving before versus after זריקת דמים, and shaving before versus after immersion. The amud ends with his summary of why no single shaving can serve both statuses.

Opening lines

וְאִי וַדַּאי נָזִיר הוּא —

And if he is definitely just a nazirite —

עוֹלָה הָרִאשׁוֹנָה חוֹבָה,

the first burnt-offering was the required one,

וְזוֹ נְדָבָה.

and this one is a voluntary gift.

וְאִם טָמֵא וּמוּחְלָט הוּא —

And if he is impure and a confirmed leper —

The steps (13)

  1. ConclusionFinishing the condition on the burnt-offering
  2. StatementPossibly impure, definitely a confirmed leper
  3. StatementPossibly a confirmed leper, definitely impure
  4. StatementDefinitely impure and definitely a confirmed leper
  5. QuestionCan one shaving serve both the נזיר and the מצורע?
  6. ResponseHe may not shave once for both
  7. ResponseReason: one shaving removes hair, the other grows it
  8. ChallengeThen let it count for his second, later shaving
  9. ResolutionDifference in timing: before or after the blood
  10. ChallengeThen pair the מצורע with an impure נזיר's shaving
  11. ResolutionDifference in timing: before or after immersion
  12. ChallengeThe students concede one pairing but propose another
  13. ResolutionTwo differences block every pairing
