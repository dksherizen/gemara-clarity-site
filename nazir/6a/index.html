Topic

Testing the views of רב מתנא (30 days) and בר פדא (29 days) on the length of a plain נזירות against several משניות

Overview

The משנה says that someone who takes two terms of נזירות shaves for the first on day 31 and for the second on day 61. But if he shaved the first on day 30, he shaves the second on day 60. The first half fits רב מתנא, who holds a plain נזירות lasts 30 days. בר פדא, who holds it lasts 29 days, explains that the first half is about someone who said 'thirty full days,' and that the second half supports him. רב מתנא explains the second half by saying day 30 counts for both terms. The גמרא then asks why the משנה repeats the idea of מקצת היום ככולו. It answers that we might have thought the idea works for one term of נזירות but not for two. Next, a משנה says shaving on day 59 after two terms is valid because day 30 counts. That line seems unneeded for בר פדא, but he answers that this line is his very source. Finally, a משנה says a נזיר who becomes טמא on day 30 loses his whole count. That fits רב מתנא but is hard for בר פדא, and the answer comes on the next עמוד.
