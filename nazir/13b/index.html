Nazir 13b

נזיר י״ג ע״ב

Learn Nazir 13b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Nazir 13b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Doubtful נזירות vows that depend on a son being born, and what happens when one נזירות term interrupts another

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a question about a friend who answers 'and I' when someone vows to be a נזיר once a certain person has a son. If that person is not there, does the friend mean his own future son, or is he saying he loves that person just as much? The גמרא finds no answer and leaves it as תיבעי. Then a new משנה teaches how to order two נזירות terms, one starting now and one starting when a son is born. If he mentioned his own term first, he finishes it and only then counts the son's term. If he mentioned the son's term first, he pauses his own, counts the son's, and then finishes his own. In the גמרא, רבא asks whether a 100-day נזירות can start now if a 30-day term will interrupt it after 20 days. The גמרא asks why רבא didn't ask about a short term instead, and answers that his question grew out of an earlier question. That earlier question is laid out on the next amud.

Opening lines

כְּסִיפָא לֵיהּ מִילְּתָא,

the matter is embarrassing for him,

אָמַר ״הֲרֵינִי נָזִיר

If one said: "I am hereby a nazir

לִכְשֶׁיְּהֵא לִפְלוֹנִי בֵּן״,

when so-and-so will have a son,"

וְשָׁמַע חֲבֵירוֹ וְאָמַר ״וַאֲנִי״,

and his friend heard and said: "And I,"

The steps (7)

  1. QuestionDoes 'And I' mean the friend's son or his own son?
  2. ConclusionThe question stays unresolved
  3. StatementOwn נזירות first, then the son's
  4. StatementSon's נזירות said first, so he pauses his own
  5. QuestionCan a 100-day נזירות start now if a 30-day term will interrupt it?
  6. ChallengeWhy not ask about a short נזירות?
  7. ResolutionOne question grew out of another
