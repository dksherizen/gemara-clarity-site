Nazir 31b

נזיר ל״א ע״ב

Learn Nazir 31b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Nazir 31b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

הקדש בטעות in בית שמאי's view, רב חסדא on ox colors, and the בית הלל–בית שמאי debate over a mistaken consecration.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a quote from a later משנה about travelers who make נזיר vows about a stranger. בית שמאי say they are all נזירים even though some must be wrong. The גמרא answers that בית שמאי do hold a mistaken consecration (הקדש בטעות) counts, but the black-ox משנה rests on a different reason. אביי instead explains the black-ox case as a man speaking at noon about the past who got the facts wrong. The future-tense wording of the משנה is then read as past tense to fit his explanation. Next, רב חסדא's statements about which ox color is better are tested against the משנה. The idea that people consecrate 'with a stingy eye' holds once we know that in the גליל wine is worth more than oil. רב חסדא's view is limited to the קרמנאי breed. A new משנה then teaches what happens when a חכם upholds or dissolves a נזיר vow. That leads to a debate between בית הלל and בית שמאי over whether a mistaken consecration takes effect, using the case of מעשר בהמה. The debate continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְאֶחָד בָּא כְּנֶגְדָּן,

And one person was coming toward them,

וְאָמַר אֶחָד: ״הֲרֵינִי נָזִיר שֶׁזֶּה פְּלוֹנִי״,

and one said: 'I am a nazir if this is so-and-so,'

וְאֶחָד אָמַר: ״הֲרֵינִי נָזִיר שֶׁאֵין זֶה פְּלוֹנִי״,

and one said: 'I am a nazir if this is not so-and-so,'

״הֲרֵינִי נָזִיר שֶׁאֶחָד מִכֶּם נָזִיר״,

'I am a nazir if one of you is a nazir,'
