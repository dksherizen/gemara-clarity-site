Nazir 34a

נזיר ל״ד ע״א

Learn Nazir 34a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Nazir 34a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Who wrote the משנה about the unidentified stranger, the vows about a כּוֹי, and the start of the new פרק on the three things forbidden to a נזיר.

Overview

The amud opens by asking who wrote the משנה about six people who vowed נזירות on the identity of an approaching stranger. That משנה says no one is a נזיר if the stranger turned back unidentified. The גמרא rejects רבי טרפון as the author, because he holds that such a vow never takes effect at all. It names רבי יהודה instead, who is lenient when a grain heap is lost and its size can never be checked. רבי שמעון is strict in both cases. A new משנה then describes people making opposite נזירות vows about whether a כּוֹי is a חיה or a בהמה, and it rules that all of them are נזירים. The גמרא compares two ברייתות, one counting nine נזירים and one counting nine terms of נזירות. רב ששת explains how a single person can carry nine terms. The פרק בית שמאי then ends, and a new פרק opens with the three things forbidden to a נזיר: טומאה, shaving, and grapevine products.

Opening lines

אִילֵימָא רַבִּי טַרְפוֹן —

If you say it is Rabbi Tarfon —

מִי הָוֵי נָזִיר?

is he a nazir at all?

כֵּיוָן דִּבְשַׁעְתָּא דְּקָא נָזַר

Since at the time that he vowed

לָא יָדַע אִי פְּלוֹנִי הוּא וְאִי לָא,

he did not know whether it was so-and-so or not,
