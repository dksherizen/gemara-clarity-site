Nazir 27b

נזיר כ״ז ע״ב

Topic

Is a blemished animal set aside for offerings like unallocated money (מעות סתומים)? רב המנונא and רבא challenge רב נחמן using ברייתות about a son using his father's offerings.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier answer: an animal fit for a חטאת cannot be offered as an עולה, because a חטאת is female and an עולה is male, so such animals already count as allocated. רב המנונא then challenges רב נחמן's rule that a blemished animal is like מעות סתומים. He quotes a ברייתא about a father and son who were both נזירים. In that ברייתא, the father's unallocated money goes to נדבה, while each set-aside animal is treated by its type. The גמרא first answers that the ברייתא means only an unblemished animal. It then asks why the ברייתא didn't simply say 'a blemished animal goes to נדבה.' The answer is that a blemished animal is holy only for its value, so it is already included in 'money.' רבא then opens a new objection from a ברייתא that learns from 'קרבנו' that a son cannot use his father's חטאת, even when their sins match. That ברייתא goes on to suggest that a father's money, unlike his animal, might be usable, just as in נזירות, and it continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מִי מָצֵית אָמְרַתְּ

Can you really say

הָדֵין דְּחַטָּאת תִּקְרַב עוֹלָה?

that this one, which is for a sin-offering, will be offered as a burnt-offering?

הָכָא נְקֵבָה, הָכָא זָכָר.

Here it is female, here it is male.
