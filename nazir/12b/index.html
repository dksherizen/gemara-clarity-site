Nazir 12b

נזיר י״ב ע״ב

Learn Nazir 12b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Nazir 12b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Whether a שליח can be appointed for what the sender cannot do now; vows to pay for half a נזיר; and נזירות that depends on the birth of a son or child.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a challenge to רבא, who ruled that a שליח for קידושין cannot marry the sender to a woman who was married when the שליח was appointed. The challenge compares this to a promise to pay for a future נזיר's shaving. The גמרא answers that a person only appoints a שליח for something he could do himself right now. It then tests that rule with a ברייתא about a husband who tells his אפוטרופוס to cancel his wife's future vows. Since the חכמים hold that a husband cannot cancel vows in advance, it seems a שליח can do more than the sender. The גמרא answers that the ברייתא may follow רבי אליעזר, who says a husband can cancel in advance. In that case, the husband appoints a steward only in case he forgets, gets angry or is busy. Next, a משנה discusses two people who each vow to pay for 'shaving half a נזיר', and רבא explains exactly which wording רבי מאיר and the חכמים argue about. Finally, a new משנה teaches that a vow to be a נזיר 'when I have a son' depends on a definite son, while 'when I have a child' is fulfilled by any baby.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא הָכִי קָאָמַר:

Rather, this is what he is saying:

אִי מַשְׁכַּחְנָא דְּהָוֵי נָזִיר, אֲגַלְּחֵיהּ.

If I find someone who becomes a nazirite, I will shave him.

הָכָא נָמֵי, הָכִי קָאָמַר לֵיהּ:

Here too, this is what he is saying to him:

אִי מַשְׁכַּחַתְּ דְּמִיגָּרְשָׁה, קַדֵּישׁ לִי.

If you find that she has been divorced, betroth her to me.
