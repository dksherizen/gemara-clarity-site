נזיר נ״ח ע״ב

Topic

How the מצורע's shaving overrides the prohibitions of a נזיר and a כהן, and the rules for removing body hair with a razor

Overview

The גמרא looks at the verse about a מצורע shaving "ראשו" (his head) and "זקנו" (his beard), and asks what each word teaches. First it challenges the view that an עשה does not override a לא תעשה ועשה: why not learn from a כהן מצורע, who must shave? The answer is that we cannot learn from a כהן, because his prohibition does not apply to everyone. For the other view, a ברייתא shows that "זקנו" teaches that a כהן מצורע must shave his beard with a razor. Another ברייתא proves that the beard prohibition covers only a razor. Then the גמרא explains that both words are needed, one for the נזיר and one for the כהן, because neither case can be learned from the other, and neither can serve as a source for other laws. Finally the topic turns to removing body hair. רב permits it with something like a razor, a ברייתא gives lashes for removing armpit or pubic hair with a real razor, and the גמרא concludes those lashes are only מדרבנן.
