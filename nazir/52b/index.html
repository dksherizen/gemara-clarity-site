Topic

Spine 'or' skull: the count of רבי עקיבא's six rulings, שמאי's view, and רמי בר חמא's question about a רובע of spine and skull bones

Overview

The גמרא continues asking whether רבי עקיבא's rule means a spine OR a skull alone makes a נזיר shave, or only both together. A proof is brought from the count of six rulings: if spine and skull were separate, there would be seven. The גמרא rejects this with four ways to explain why one item is left out of the six: the barley-grain bone, the limb from a living person, the barley-grain bone for a different reason, or רביעית דם, which רבי עקיבא never retracted. Along the way, a פסוק and רבי שמעון support that רבי עקיבא held on to his view on רביעית דם, and we hear that רבי שמעון fasted until his teeth turned black. Next, a ברייתא about רובע עצמות ends with שמאי saying even a bone from the spine or the skull makes things טמא. The גמרא pushes this proof away, and then pushes away the opposite proof too, so the spine-and-skull question is not settled. Finally, רמי בר חמא asks whether a רובע of bones from the spine and skull is enough, and רבא begins to answer from the wording of the משנה.
