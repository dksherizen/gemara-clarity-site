Topic

Can the חכמים uproot a Torah law? (יש כח ביד חכמים לעקור דבר מן התורה). The גמרא finishes deflecting one source and then brings a strong proof from the case of a זר who repays a כהן for eating תרומה.

Overview

The גמרא is checking whether the חכמים have the power to uproot a Torah law. First it explains that a woman eating תרומה on her husband's account refers only to תרומה דרבנן. Next it brings a ברייתא about a non-כהן who repaid a כהן with impure חולין. After רבא fills in missing words, it seems that סומכוס in the name of רבי מאיר cancels a payment the Torah considers valid, which would even affect a אשת איש. The גמרא rejects this: 'not payment' only means he must pay a second time. רב אחא בריה דרב איקא shows that the two sides still differ, on fining the accidental payer. Then the גמרא tries the case of impure blood sprinkled on purpose: the ציץ makes it acceptable by Torah law, yet the רבנן say it is not accepted. רבי יוסי בר חנינא explains that only eating the meat is forbidden. When the גמרא objects that this still cancels a Torah מצוה, the answer is that שב ואל תעשה is different.
