Topic

חליצה or ייבום with a יבמה who turns out to be pregnant, and whether such acts count

Overview

This amud deals with a surprise: a יבם does חליצה or ייבום, and then it turns out the widow was pregnant from her late husband. The משנה rules that everything depends on whether the baby is viable. A viable baby means there was never a יבום bond, so the חליצה is void and the ייבום was a forbidden marriage. A non-viable baby means the bond was real, so the חליצה counts and the ייבום stands. The גמרא then brings a dispute about a חליצה done with a pregnant woman who later miscarried. רבי יוחנן says it counts, and ריש לקיש says it does not. Each side tests its view against the משנה's wording, in two versions of the exchange. A ברייתא about the widow's צרה leads אביי to say that everyone agrees ביאה with a pregnant woman does not count, and that the argument is only about חליצה. רבא challenges that split at the end of the amud, and his proof continues on the next amud.
