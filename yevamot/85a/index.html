Topic

Women and the marriage bans of כהונה, and the money rights (כתובה, מזונות) of women in forbidden marriages.

Overview

The גמרא tries to prove from טומאת כהנים that רב יהודה's rule makes women liable even for a לאו שאין שוה בכל. The proof is rejected in two versions. Each version ties the verse "בני אהרן" to the words "לא יקחו" in a different way. Next, a story asks whether women who are כשרות are warned against marrying פסולין. רב פפא tries to prove it from the משנה of the ten lineages, and רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע rejects that proof. Then רב אידי בר אבין rules in the name of רב that they are not warned. רב ששת then teaches from a ברייתא that a woman who was a שניה to her first husband gets no כתובה, not even from the יבם. After that, רבי יוחנן rules that a woman in a marriage forbidden to a כהן gets no מזונות while her husband is alive. The ברייתא that gives her מזונות is about after his death. Finally, a ברייתא contrasts Torah-level forbidden marriages, where she keeps her money rights but becomes פסולה, with שניות, where she loses them but stays כשרה. רבי שמעון בן אלעזר begins explaining why, and his explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וַהֲרֵי טוּמְאָה,

But there is impurity,

דְּלָאו שֶׁאֵין שָׁוֶה בַּכֹּל,

which is a prohibition that is not equal for all,

וְטַעְמָא דִּכְתַב רַחֲמָנָא

and the reason is that the Merciful One wrote

״בְּנֵי אַהֲרֹן״ וְלֹא בְּנוֹת אַהֲרֹן,

"sons of Aaron", and not daughters of Aaron;
