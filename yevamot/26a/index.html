Topic

When the men who freed a woman may marry her, and the start of the chapter on two sisters who fall to the same brothers for יבום.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a point from the previous page: a rumor alone is not enough to take a woman away from her husband. Then a משנה teaches when the men who helped free a woman to remarry may marry her themselves. They may do so if their own wives died, or if she married someone else in between. She may also always marry their sons or brothers. The גמרא first discusses whether divorcing one's own wife also lifts the ban. It answers that this depends on whether there was a quarrel, or on who started it. Next it reads the 'married someone else' case as one death and one divorce, so the משנה fits רבי's view of חזקה after two times. It then explains why his sons and brothers may marry her, and why the משנה does not bother to mention his father. Then the chapter כיצד אשת אחיו ends, and a new משנה teaches that two sisters who fall to the same brothers for יבום must do חליצה, except when an ערוה cuts the bond. The גמרא asks whether this proves that זיקה is real, suggests another reason based on not losing the מצוה of יבום, and begins a new challenge that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בִּרְנָנָה — לָא מַפְּקִינַן,

Because of a rumor, we do not remove her,

הָכָא נָמֵי — בִּרְנָנָה לָא מַפְּקִינַן.

here too, because of a rumor we do not remove her.

מַתְנִי׳

MISHNA:

וְכוּלָּם שֶׁהָיוּ לָהֶם נָשִׁים וּמֵתוּ —

And all of them who had wives, and the wives died,
