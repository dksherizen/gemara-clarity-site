Topic

Three brothers, two married to sisters: when a נכרית becomes a צרה of an ערוה, the rule that 'forbidden once, forbidden forever', and רב נחמן against רב אשי on whether זיקה is real

Overview

This amud has a run of משניות about three brothers. Two of them are married to two sisters, and the third is married to an unrelated woman (נכרית). In the first two משניות, יבום makes the נכרית a צרה of a sister who is forbidden to the last brother, so both women go free. If only מאמר was done, the נכרית does חליצה and not יבום. From the first משנה, רב נחמן learns that אין זיקה, even with one brother. The גמרא then asks why the second משנה is needed at all, since it follows by קל וחומר. It answers that the תנא changed his ruling, and a משנה does not move from its place. The next משנה teaches that a sister who was forbidden to a brother at one moment stays forbidden to him forever. רב then gives a general rule about the moment of falling, and the גמרא shows what that rule adds beyond two משניות. The last משנה shows when a צרה is permitted because the sister was divorced first. From it, רב אשי learns that יש זיקה, even with two brothers. He then answers רב נחמן's proof by saying the mention of מאמר is there to reject בית שמאי, and this answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳ שְׁלֹשָׁה אַחִין,

MISHNA: Three brothers,

שְׁנַיִם מֵהֶם נְשׂוּאִים שְׁתֵּי אֲחָיוֹת,

two of them are married to two sisters,

וְאֶחָד נָשׂוּי נׇכְרִית.

and one is married to an unrelated woman.

מֵת אֶחָד מִבַּעֲלֵי אֲחָיוֹת,

One of the husbands of the sisters died (childless),
