Topic

Doubtful קידושין and גירושין, why a שטר קידושין has no date, and the new משנה about two widows tied to two brothers

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a challenge to רבה's reading of the משנה about doubtful קידושין and גירושין, using the case of בר שטיא. אביי says the משנה uses the style of "יגיד עליו ריעו", so each case of doubt applies to both topics. רבא objects from the word "זהו" and gives a one-way rule: every doubt in קידושין is also a doubt in גירושין, but גירושין has extra doubts, such as a problem with the date. The גמרא then asks why the חכמים did not require a date in a שטר קידושין. One answer is that most קידושין are done with money, unlike slaves, who are mostly bought with a document. A second answer is that a dated שטר קידושין could not be kept safe with her, with him, or with the witnesses, and that a גט is different because its date helps her. A new משנה follows about three brothers, a מאמר, and two widows who must do חליצה, with רבי שמעון disagreeing. The גמרא explains that this ruling is only a rabbinic decree and starts a second decree that continues on the next amud.
