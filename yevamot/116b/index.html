Yevamos 116b

יבמות קט״ז ע״ב

Topic

When a woman is believed that her husband died: a קטטה, רבי יהודה's signs of grief, whether the rule is limited to the original incident, and whether she collects her כתובה.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a discussion about a wife who had a קטטה with her husband. The גמרא then asks whether a single witness is believed in such a case, and the question ends as a תיקו. Next, the רבנן reject רבי יהודה's demand that a woman come crying with torn clothes, because a clever woman could fake it. A story at רבי יהודה's court shows people coaching a woman, and the גמרא explains they were not teaching her to lie. A new משנה brings בית הלל, who limit the woman's believability to cases like the original incident at the harvest. בית שמאי argue that the details only describe what happened, and בית הלל change their ruling. The גמרא tells the original snake-bite story. It then tests whether the מחלוקת of רבי חנניא בן עקיבא and the רבנן about carrying מי חטאת across the ירדן matches בית שמאי and בית הלל, and concludes that each case has its own reason. Finally, a new משנה asks whether the woman may collect her כתובה, and בית הלל's answer to בית שמאי's קל וחומר continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אִיכָּא בֵּינַיְיהוּ

There is a difference between them

דְּאַרְגֵּיל הוּא קְטָטָה.

where he started the quarrel.

אִיבַּעְיָא לְהוּ:

A question was asked before them:

עֵד אֶחָד בִּקְטָטָה, מַהוּ?

one witness in a case of a quarrel, what is the law?
