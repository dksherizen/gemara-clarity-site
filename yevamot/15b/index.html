Topic

Did בית שמאי act on their own rulings? Proofs from רבי יוחנן החורני's moist olives and from רבי יהושע's fear of ruling on צרת הבת.

Overview

This amud continues the גמרא's investigation into whether בית שמאי actually acted on their own rulings when they disagreed with בית הלל. It first finishes rejecting a proof about a widened water trough, since onlookers would think it was done just to get more water. Then it tells the story of רבי יוחנן החורני, a student of שמאי, who refused moist olives because he followed בית הלל's stricter rule about a barrel of olives needing a hole. The ברייתא praises him for always following בית הלל, and the גמרא reasons that this praise only makes sense if בית שמאי really did act on their own view. A second proof comes from רבי יהושע, who would not rule on צרת הבת because he was afraid. Instead he testified that great families descended from צרות produced כהנים גדולים. The גמרא explains that his fear makes sense: even though by his view the children of a צרה who did יבום are not ממזרים, they would still be פגום, as shown by a קל וחומר from an אלמנה married to a כהן גדול. Finally, the גמרא explains that רבי יהושע was really asked two questions, one about the צרות and one about their children. It begins matching his words to each question, and this continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הָרוֹאֶה אוֹמֵר:

Someone watching would say:

לְאַפּוֹשֵׁי מַיָּא הוּא דְּקָא עָבֵיד.

it is to increase the water that he is doing it.

תָּא שְׁמַע, דְּאָמַר רַבִּי אֶלְעָזָר בַּר צָדוֹק:

Come and hear, as Rabbi Elazar bar Tzadok said:

כְּשֶׁהָיִיתִי לוֹמֵד תּוֹרָה

When I was learning Torah
