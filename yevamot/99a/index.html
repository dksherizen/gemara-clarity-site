Topic

Cases of doubtful lineage: mixed-up babies and חליצה, רבי מאיר's 'five nations', a son selling his slave father for a כתובה, and the משנה on mixed-up babies for יבום and תרומה

Overview

This amud brings several cases about unusual family situations. First a ברייתא shows how a man might do חליצה with a woman who could be his mother, sister or daughter. This happens because babies born 'in hiding' got mixed up, so יבום is impossible and he does חליצה with every woman who might be his יבמה. Next, רבי מאיר shows how one couple, a slave and a maidservant, can have children of five different statuses: גר, גוי, עבד, ממזר and ישראל. The גמרא says the point is that the child of a גוי or עבד with a Jewish woman is a ממזר. Another ברייתא describes a son who sells his own slave father to pay his mother's כתובה. The גמרא explains that it follows רבי מאיר, who holds that מטלטלין are tied up for a כתובה, or else it teaches that a slave counts like קרקע. Then a new משנה rules on יבום and חליצה when a woman's baby got mixed up with her daughter-in-law's baby. Finally, the משנה starts a case of a כהנת's baby mixed up with her שפחה's baby: both eat תרומה but receive one share. This case continues on the next amud.
