Topic

Why a יבמה waits three months before חליצה, and who must support a יבמה while she waits

Overview

This amud opens with the last words of the משנה, where רבי יוסי says a widow may not become betrothed during the three months because she is mourning. The גמרא then asks why a יבמה may not do חליצה in the first three months, when only יבום risks the Torah prohibition of אשת אח. It first tries to use this to disprove רבי יוחנן, who holds that חליצה with a pregnant woman counts. It rejects that attempt and offers two reasons for the wait in turn: fear of confusion about her fitness to marry a כהן, and loss of her מזונות. Each reason fails for some type of woman. In the end the גמרא relies on רבי יוסי, who learns from ״ואם לא יחפץ״ that only a woman fit for יבום can do חליצה. רב חיננא challenges this from doubtful cases, such as two sisters, where a woman does חליצה but not יבום. The גמרא answers that the three-month wait is a fixed rule that even applies to a קטנה, so a woman in those months is truly unfit for יבום. The amud then covers who must support a יבמה, including the ruling that a woman waiting for a minor יבם gets nothing. It closes with a ברייתא that a woman who did חליצה within three months must still wait three months.

Opening lines

מִפְּנֵי הָאִיבּוּל.

because of the mourning.
