Topic

רבי אלעזר's reason for permitting the צרה, and the rules for identifying a dead man by his face and by סימנים

Overview

The amud opens by asking what difference it makes which reason stands behind רבי אלעזר's ruling. One possible reason is צרה מעידה לחברתה. The other is that the woman would not ruin herself by marrying if her husband were alive. The difference is whether her צרה may marry before she herself marries. Two proofs are brought from רבי אלעזר's words, and both are pushed away. The first is answered with 'לדבריהם קאמר'. The second is answered by reading 'permitted and married', and that reading is then defended by placing the case where she married a כהן. The רבנן reject the whole idea, because she might act like שמשון: 'תמות נפשי עם פלשתים'. A new משנה then teaches that one may testify that a man died only after seeing his face with the nose, seeing that he actually died, and seeing the body within three days. רבי יהודה בן בבא disagrees with the fixed three-day limit. The גמרא explains the face requirement with a ברייתא, a פסוק and the story of אבא בר מרתא. It then asks whether סימנים are valid by Torah law. אביי answers that this is a מחלוקת תנאים about a mole, and רבא gives several versions explaining that מחלוקת differently, which continue on the next amud.

Opening lines

לְאַנְסוֹבֵי לְצָרָה מִקַּמֵּי דִּידַהּ:

To let the rival wife marry before her:

אִי אָמְרַתְּ צָרָה מְעִידָה לַחֲבֶרְתָּהּ,

If you say a rival wife may testify for her fellow wife,

אַף עַל גַּב דְּלָא אִינְּסִיב —

even though she has not married,

מַנְסְבִינַן לַהּ לְצָרָה.

we let the rival wife marry.
