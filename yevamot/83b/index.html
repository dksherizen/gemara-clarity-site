Topic

רב and שמואל on the rulings of רבי יוסי, the טומטום and חליצה, and the status of an אנדרוגינוס for forbidden relations and for קדשים.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the list of cases where רב and שמואל rule like רבי יוסי. The last case is a vine spread over a friend's grain, where רבי יוסי says a person cannot forbid what is not his. The גמרא then asks about cases where only one of the two gave a ruling. שמואל is shown to reject רבי יוסי on אנדרוגינוס and on grafting. רב's view on קושי ends in תיקו. On קידוש, רב הונא's report wins: רב did not follow רבי יוסי. Next, the גמרא discusses רבי יהודה's view that an opened טומטום is a סריס, and the opinion of רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה that it is only a doubt; רבא spells out the practical differences for חליצה. It then turns to רב's ruling that relations with an אנדרוגינוס bring סקילה in both places, which follows רבי סימאי's reading of 'משכבי אשה' against the רבנן. Finally, רב חסדא limits רבי אליעזר's view that an אנדרוגינוס is a full male, proving from a ברייתא about bird offerings that it does not apply to קדשים.

Opening lines

אֵין אָדָם אוֹסֵר

A person cannot render forbidden

דָּבָר שֶׁאֵינוֹ שֶׁלּוֹ.

an item that is not his.

אִיבַּעְיָא לְהוּ:

A dilemma was raised before them:

בְּאַנְדְּרוֹגִינוֹס מָה לִי אָמַר שְׁמוּאֵל?

As for a hermaphrodite, what did Shmuel say?
