Topic

Marrying a nursing mother, the three-month wait before remarrying, and rules for deciding הלכה from a סתם משנה

Overview

The amud opens by explaining why a man may not marry a מינקת חברו: if she becomes pregnant, her milk may dry up and the baby could die. The new husband won't pay for other food, and אביי says she is too embarrassed to sue the יורשים in בית דין. Next, the גמרא explains that the משנה's phrase 'אחת בתולות ואחת בעולות' is one general rule: every woman must wait three months. In a short story, רבי אסי tells רבי אלעזר that רבי יוחנן ruled like רבי יוסי, who allows betrothal during the wait. A ברייתא shows that רבי מאיר and רבי יהודה argue about women who clearly cannot be pregnant. רבי יוחנן is then reported to have taken back his ruling because of the teaching of the sages in the vineyard of יבנה. Someone asks how רבי יוחנן could rule against a סתם משנה, and רבי אבהו answers with a rule: a סתם followed by a מחלוקת is not binding, while a מחלוקת followed by a סתם is. Finally, רבי נחום asks רבי אבהו how to rule when a משנה and a ברייתא conflict, and the last answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דִּלְמָא אִיעַבַּרָה וּמִעֲכַר חַלְבַהּ,

Perhaps she will become pregnant and her milk will dry up,

וְקָטְלָה לֵיהּ.

and she will kill him.

אִי הָכִי, דִּידֵיהּ נָמֵי!

If so, his own as well!

דִּידֵיהּ — מְמַסְמְסָא לֵיהּ בְּבֵיצִים. וְחָלָב

His own, she feeds him with eggs and milk.
