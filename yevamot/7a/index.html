Topic

Whether executions override שבת (the ברייתא of "לא תבערו"), and why the word עליה is needed, using the rules of דבר שהיה בכלל ויצא

Overview

The גמרא is explaining a ברייתא that uses the פסוק "לא תבערו" to teach that בית דין does not carry out executions on שבת. רב שמי בר אשי says the ברייתא's first idea, that executions might override שבת, came from a קל וחומר: execution overrides עבודה, and עבודה overrides שבת. The puzzling words "או אינו" are read as an attempt to break that קל וחומר from קבורת מת מצוה. The attempt fails, so the פסוק is needed. The גמרא then explains "או אינו" according to its earlier reading, in which the תנא went back and forth over whether an עשה can override a לאו that carries כרת. Next the גמרא gives a new reason why the word עליה is needed in the יבום פרשה. One might apply the rule of דבר שהיה בכלל ויצא מן הכלל ללמד and permit all עריות, the way the example of שלמים teaches about all קדשי מזבח. The גמרא rejects this comparison, because אשת אח is permitted while her group is forbidden. It says her case fits a different rule instead: something that left its group for a new law, יצא לידון בדבר החדש. A ברייתא illustrates that rule with the אשם of a מצורע, and its explanation continues on the next amud.
