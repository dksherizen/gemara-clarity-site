Topic

Where רב's rule comes from (losing תרומה and being unfit for כהונה after relations with an unfit man), whom it covers, and which men trigger it.

Overview

This amud continues an earlier discussion of רב's rule. A woman who has relations with a man who is unfit for her loses her right to eat תרומה and becomes unfit to marry a כהן. רב learns this from the פסוק "ובת כהן כי תהיה לאיש זר". First the גמרא checks that this פסוק is not already used up. It shows that the 'זר' verses and the extra words "בתרומת הקדשים" leave room for רב's rule. Next it finds a source to include a לויה and ישראלית: the extra word "ובת", which works even for the רבנן. A long back-and-forth follows about which source teaches תרומה and which teaches כהונה. It ends with "ובת" for תרומה and a קל וחומר from a גרושה for כהונה. That קל וחומר is allowed because it only reveals the reach of an existing ban. The גמרא then rejects limiting the rule to חייבי כריתות, since "כי תהיה" speaks of men she can actually marry. When it asks about a גוי or an עבד, it points to a separate teaching of רבי ישמעאל, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הָהוּא מִיבְּעֵי לֵיהּ לְגוּפֵיהּ!

That one is needed for itself!

תְּרֵי ״וְכׇל זָר״ כְּתִיבִי.

Two "and any common man" verses are written.

וְאַכַּתִּי מִיבְּעֵי לֵיהּ

But it is still needed

לְכִדְרַבִּי יוֹסֵי בְּרַבִּי חֲנִינָא,

for the teaching of Rabbi Yosei son of Rabbi Ḥanina,

The steps (23)

  1. ChallengeThat verse is needed for its own basic law
  2. ResolutionThere are two 'וכל זר' verses
  3. ChallengeThe second verse is needed for רבי יוסי ברבי חנינא
  4. Resolutionרבי יוסי ברבי חנינא learns from the extra word 'וכל'
  5. ChallengeThe verse is needed to forbid חזה ושוק after she returns
  6. ResolutionThe extra words teach two laws
  7. QuestionWhat about a לויה or ישראלית?
  8. ResponseThe extra 'and' in 'ובת' includes them
  9. ChallengeDoes this work only for רבי עקיבא?
  10. ResolutionEven the רבנן agree: the whole phrase is extra
  11. QuestionWhat about marrying a כהן?
  12. ResponseThe extra word must be about marrying a כהן
  13. RejectionShe can eat תרומה because of her son
  14. ResolutionNot eating through her son comes from a קל וחומר
  15. RejectionThe קל וחומר works the other way
  16. ResponseThe ban on marrying a כהן comes from a קל וחומר from a גרושה
  17. ChallengeCan we create a prohibition from logic?
  18. ResolutionIt only reveals what was already included
  19. ChallengeMaybe 'unfit man' means only חייבי כריתות
  20. Resolution״כי תהיה״ means men she could actually marry
  21. ChallengeThen a gentile or slave should not disqualify her
  22. ResolutionThey disqualify her through רבי ישמעאל's source
  23. Statementרבי ישמעאל's source about a gentile or slave
