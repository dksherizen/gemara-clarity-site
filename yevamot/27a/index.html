Topic

שמואל's rulings on which חליצה frees the צרות (sisters, a woman who got a גט, a woman who got a מאמר), and whether they rest on אין זיקה or on a weak זיקה that cannot make a צרה like an ערוה.

Overview

The גמרא finishes testing שמואל's ruling that one brother does חליצה for all of the sisters. It gives two answers: 'all' really means the middle sister, or a valid חליצה is needed only to free a צרה, not the woman herself. Next it quotes שמואל's full statement. חליצה with the sisters, with a woman who got a גט, or with a woman who got a מאמר does not free her צרה, but חליצה with the צרה frees her. The גמרא asks why the צרות' חליצה frees the sisters, since the צרות seem linked through זיקה too. One answer says שמואל ruled according to the view that there is no זיקה. That answer needs more fixing: the plural 'צרות' is general wording, and the order of the חליצות matters. רב אשי instead says there is זיקה, but it is too weak to make a צרה like an ערוה, and a ברייתא seems to support him. רבי אבא בר ממל pushes back: the ברייתא may follow בית שמאי, together with רבי יוחנן בן נורי's enactment that צרות only do חליצה, which later חכמים completed. The amud ends by opening a new question that continues on the next amud.
