Topic

Why the משנה says אלמנה, רב פפא's challenge from רבי יוחנן's מצרי rule (answered with תנא ושייר), and רב's ruling that fit women may marry disqualified men

Overview

This amud covers three topics. First, the גמרא explains why the משנה's first case speaks of an אלמנה: the word is borrowed from the סיפא, where a כהן גדול marrying an אלמנה makes her a חללה, so she is forbidden even to a כהן הדיוט brother. Next, רב פפא challenges the משנה. רבי יוחנן ruled that the son of a מצרי שני and a מצרית ראשונה is a מצרי שני, and that rule produces a new set of cases that the משנה should have listed. The גמרא answers תנא ושייר: the משנה did not list every case. After a long back-and-forth, it proves this by showing that פצוע דכא was also left out. Finally, the גמרא turns to רב's ruling that women fit for כהונה were not warned against marrying disqualified men. A suggested proof from the משנה's word 'fit' is rejected. רבא answers רבין בר נחמן's objection from 'they shall not take': a woman is forbidden only where the man is forbidden. The amud ends by explaining why the כהונה prohibitions need their own source to include women.

Opening lines

וְכִי תֵּימָא:

And if you would say

קָסָבַר הַאי תַּנָּא

that this tanna holds

נִישּׂוּאִין הָרִאשׁוֹנִים מַפִּילִין —

that the first marriage is what determines her status when she falls to yibum

הֲרֵי חָלָל שֶׁנָּשָׂא כְּשֵׁרָה,

but look, a ḥalal who married a woman fit for a priest
