Topic

How a ברייתא about a brother born after יבום or מאמר shows the limits of רבי שמעון's view, plus the dispute over מאמר done against the woman's will

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא about brothers, יבום, and a new brother born along the way. It proves from the ברייתא's wording that רבי שמעון disagrees with רבי מאיר only when the יבום happened before the new brother was born, just as רב פפא said. The גמרא then explains a line about a brother who 'did not manage' to do מאמר. It means he did מאמר against the woman's will, which counts for nothing according to the חכמים, who disagree with רבי. רבי learns מאמר from the act of יבום, which works even against her will. The חכמים learn it from ordinary קידושין, which need her consent. Next the גמרא pins down that רבי שמעון's view in the מאמר case applies only when the מאמר came before the birth. It also explains why חליצה with the woman who received מאמר cannot free her צרה: a doubt cannot remove a certainty. Finally, רב מנשה בר זביד's question turns out to be about the רבנן's reason. They learn from a פסוק that the first levirate bond stays on her. The amud ends as the גמרא starts a challenge to that from a משנה, which continues on the next amud.
