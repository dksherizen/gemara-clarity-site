Topic

Why a כהן may not marry an אילונית or a קטנה, and how many children fulfill פריה ורביה

Overview

The גמרא explains that the משנה says 'a כהן' only because רבי יהודה calls an אילונית a זונה, and רב הונא gives the פסוק behind that view. A ברייתא then brings רבי אליעזר's ruling that a כהן may not marry a קטנה. רבה explains that רבי אליעזר combines רבי מאיר's worry about the minority with רבי יהודה's view, but the גמרא challenges both halves from other ברייתות. רב אדא בר אהבה says the ruling is about a כהן גדול, and רבא rejects this. רבא instead explains that any כהן is affected, because a young wife might be seduced, which for a minor counts as אונס. רב פפא and רב נחמן בר יצחק each tie the ruling to a ברייתא teaching that a כהן גדול must marry a נערה. רב עמרם rules against רבי אלעזר's strict view of who is a זונה. A new משנה then teaches how many children fulfill פריה ורביה, and the גמרא discusses whether a man with children must still have a wife and why בית שמאי learn from משה; that answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְיֵשׁ לוֹ בָּנִים —

and he has children —

לֹא יִשָּׂא אַיְלוֹנִית,

he may not marry a woman who cannot bear children,

שֶׁהִיא זוֹנָה הָאֲמוּרָה בַּתּוֹרָה.

for she is the zonah stated in the Torah.

דְּאַזּוֹנָה כֹּהֲנִים הוּא דְּמִפַּקְדִי

And it is priests who were commanded about a zonah,
