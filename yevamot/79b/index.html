Topic

The נתינים, and when a סריס or איילונית is part of יבום and חליצה

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the topic of the נתינים. רבי refused to permit them, because even if we give up our share in them, no one can give up the מזבח's share. רבי יוחנן holds the reverse. Then a new משנה begins: רבי יהושע heard two traditions, that a סריס does חליצה and that he does not. רבי עקיבא explains that a סריס אדם, who was once fertile, does חליצה, while a סריס חמה does not. רבי אליעזר says the opposite. The משנה adds that a סריס and an איילונית have no יבום or חליצה, and that relations with them count as בעילת זנות. The גמרא asks how רבי עקיבא can allow a סריס אדם to do חליצה, since he treats a לאו like כרת. After the answers of רבי אמי and רבה are rejected, רב יוסף explains that for רבי עקיבא's school only לאוין of close relatives cancel a marriage. רבא then proves that fitness from an earlier time counts for ייבום, and the amud ends by beginning to define a סריס חמה.
