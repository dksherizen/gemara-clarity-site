Topic

The הלכה on the צרה of an אילונית, a ברייתא on women who use a מוך, and whether a minor can give birth

Overview

The amud opens with the final הלכה on the earlier discussion: the צרה of an אילונית may marry the יבם, even if the husband knew of her condition. To fit this ruling, the גמרא changes the משנה's word 'שנמצאו' to 'שהיו'. רבין then reports in the name of רבי יוחנן that the צרות of a ממאנת, an אילונית and a man who remarried his divorcée are all permitted. Next, רב ביבי teaches a ברייתא listing three women who use a מוך because pregnancy would be dangerous: a קטנה, a pregnant woman and a nursing woman. רבי מאיר defines the endangered קטנה as a girl aged eleven and a day to twelve and a day, while the חכמים say all these women live normally and rely on 'שומר פתאים ה׳'. The ברייתא's words 'perhaps she will die' seem to allow a minor to give birth, which would make possible a חמות who did מיאון, and the משנה says that cannot happen. The גמרא tries rereading the ברייתא and brings support from רבה בר ליואי, but רבה בר שמואל's ברייתא pushes it back to the original wording. רב ספרא then answers that having children counts as signs of adulthood, so a mother is no longer a minor who can do מיאון. A second version says children are even better than signs, and the practical difference is that even רבי יהודה agrees about children.

Opening lines

הִלְכְתָא: צָרַת אַיְלוֹנִית מוּתֶּרֶת,

The halakha is: the rival wife of an aylonit is permitted,

וַאֲפִילּוּ הִכִּיר בָּהּ.

and even if he knew about her.

וַאֲפִילּוּ צָרַת בִּתּוֹ אַיְלוֹנִית.

And even the rival wife of his daughter who is an aylonit.

וְאֶלָּא הָא דְּקָתָנֵי ״שֶׁנִּמְצְאוּ״

But then, that which it teaches, "that were found to be"?
