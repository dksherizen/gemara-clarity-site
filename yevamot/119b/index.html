Topic

חזקה and רוב in רבי מאיר's rulings, how long a woman waits before חליצה, and two sisters-in-law who testify about their husbands' deaths

Overview

The גמרא first finishes explaining why רבי מאיר is strict in the רישא of the משנה but lenient in the סיפא. In the רישא, the חזקה and the minority of women who miscarry together balance the רוב, so the woman can neither marry nor do יבום. In the סיפא, the חזקה and the רוב agree, and a brother being born is only a minority of a minority, which even רבי מאיר ignores. The גמרא then asks whether the woman in the רישא must wait forever. זעירי says she waits nine months and then does חליצה, which works either way. רבי חנינא says she must wait until the facts are known, because an unneeded חליצה would require an announcement that she may still marry a כהן. Some people would miss that announcement. The גמרא tests this reasoning against an earlier משנה and answers it in two ways. A new משנה follows, about two sisters-in-law who each say their husband died, and a ברייתא adds a case. The amud ends with רבא asking for the reason behind רבי אלעזר's leniency, and the גמרא asks what practical difference his two options make.

Opening lines

רֵישָׁא, חֲזָקָה לְיִיבּוּם,

The first clause: the presumption is toward levirate marriage,

וְרוּבָּא לַשּׁוּק.

and the majority is toward the market (marrying anyone).

וַחֲזָקָה לָא עֲדִיף כִּי רוּבָּא.

And a presumption is not as strong as a majority.

וְאַיְיתִי מִיעוּטָא דְּמַפִּילוֹת

But bring the minority of women who miscarry
