Topic

Did בית שמאי practice their own rulings, and how does that fit with לא תתגודדו?

Overview

This amud asks whether בית שמאי actually lived by their own rulings against בית הלל, or only argued in the study hall. ריש לקיש and רב say they did not act on them, while רבי יוחנן and שמואל say they certainly did. The גמרא explains both views twice. The first explanation places the dispute before the בת קול, where the question is whether the majority of בית הלל binds sharper opponents. The second places it after the בת קול, where the 'did act' side follows רבי יהושע, who ignores a heavenly voice. The 'did act' view seems to clash with לא תתגודדו, the ban on splitting into groups. אביי limits that ban to two courts in one city, and רבא limits it further to one court split in half. Stories about different towns practicing differently, even on שבת, and about רבי אבהו moving a lamp in one town but not another, are shown to fit these rules. Finally, the משנה says the two schools still married each other. The גמרא explains that בית שמאי did act on their view but warned בית הלל about problem families, and the משנה's clause about purity supports this.

Opening lines

מִי סָבְרַתְּ

Do you really think

עָשׂוּ בֵּית שַׁמַּאי כְּדִבְרֵיהֶם?

that Beit Shammai acted according to their words?

לֹא עָשׂוּ בֵּית שַׁמַּאי כְּדִבְרֵיהֶם.

Beit Shammai did not act according to their words.

וְרַבִּי יוֹחָנָן אָמַר: עָשׂוּ וְעָשׂוּ.

And Rabbi Yochanan said: they certainly did act.
