Topic

Penalties for a woman who remarried on a false report of her husband's death, the opinions of the lenient תנאים, and רב's ruling about a woman who remarried without the court's permission

Overview

The גמרא keeps explaining the משנה about a woman who was told her husband died, remarried, and then saw her first husband return. It shows that the ban on marrying a כהן was listed only next to the new point that a בת לוי loses מעשר, and רב ששת explains that this loss is a special קנס. The בת כהן loses even תרומה דרבנן. Her sons lose כתובת בנין דיכרין, because the קנס reaches her children too. Both husbands' brothers do חליצה and not יבום, but for different reasons, one by Torah law and one by Rabbinic law. רב הונא and רבי יוחנן then disagree about which of the lenient תנאים at the end of the משנה (רבי יוסי, רבי אלעזר, רבי שמעון) agree with which, each using a קל וחומר. רב rules like the משנה's line that a woman who remarried on witnesses alone may return to her husband. רב נחמן asks why he did not openly name רבי שמעון, and this question stays unanswered. רב ששת argues the ruling was not needed and brings a ברייתא as proof, and the גמרא shows two ways the ברייתא could follow רבי שמעון, so a real dispute may remain.
