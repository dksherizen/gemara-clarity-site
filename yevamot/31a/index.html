Topic

When a doubtful קידושין or גירושין requires חליצה for the צרה, and when the Rabbis worried this would lead people to allow יבום.

Overview

רבה and אביי continue arguing about why our משנה requires חליצה in a doubtful קידושין case but does not teach a doubtful גירושין case the same way. רבה says that in divorce, people might go from 'she does חליצה' to 'she may do יבום'. In the קידושין case that mistake would be harmless, because she stands on her חזקה. אביי challenges this from the collapsed-house case, where a doubt forbids a צרה despite her חזקה of being permitted. רבה answers in two ways. First, the Rabbis only made decrees for common cases like divorce. Second, in divorce people may think the Rabbis checked the גט, while nobody thinks a collapse can be solved. The גמרא then raises the משנה of a גט that lands halfway, where the צרה does חליצה with no worry. רבה and רב יוסף answer that that case has two contradicting sets of witnesses, which makes it a Torah-level doubt. The גמרא questions this and argues that two sets cancel out, bringing proof from בר שטיא's sale, and רב אשי's ruling there finishes on the next amud.

Opening lines

כֵּיוָן דְּקָא מַצְרְכַתְּ חֲלִיצָה —

Since you require ḥalitza,

מִידָּע יָדְעִי דְּחוּמְרָא בְּעָלְמָא הוּא.

people will surely know that it is merely a stringency.

אִי הָכִי, גֵּירוּשִׁין נָמֵי לִיתְנֵי,

If so, let it teach divorce as well,

וְלַיצְרְכַהּ חֲלִיצָה,

and require her to do ḥalitza,
