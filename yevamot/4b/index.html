Topic

How the ציצית/כלאים pairing is learned from סמוכים, and why the words 'צמר ופשתים' are extra yet still teach that wool and linen may be crossed in ציצית

Overview

The גמרא is explaining when רבי יהודה learns laws from verses placed side by side (סמוכים). He does this only when the link is obvious (מוכח) or a verse is extra (מופנה). The ציצית verse placed next to the כלאים verse qualifies for one of these two reasons. The גמרא first shows that both כלאים verses, 'לא יעלה עליך' and 'לא תלבש שעטנז', are needed, since each fixes a gap in the other. It then finds that the words 'צמר ופשתים' are extra, because the school of רבי ישמעאל teaches that every 'garment' in the תורה means wool or linen, and a word-link of 'שעטנז / שעטנז' covers the rest. Next the גמרא asks why these extra words are needed to allow כלאים in ציצית at all. The תורה already requires a wool תכלת thread, even on linen garments. The answer is that without 'צמר ופשתים' we might read 'הכנף' to mean the threads must match the garment's material. That reading would forbid wool threads on linen and linen threads on wool, and 'צמר ופשתים' teaches that this crossing is allowed.
