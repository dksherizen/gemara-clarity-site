Topic

A woman who remarried amid conflicting testimony: רב's ruling that she need not leave, שמואל's rule about contradicting a single witness, and why she needs a גט from the second man.

Overview

רב ruled that when two witnesses say a husband died and two say he is alive, a woman who remarried need not leave. The גמרא objects that the new couple would owe an אשם תלוי. It answers that she married one of her own witnesses and says 'ברי לי'. Next it asks what רב adds beyond רבי מנחם ברבי יוסי, and gives two versions of how רב relates to him. רבא then challenges רב from a ברייתא about forcing a כהן, and the גמרא gives three answers. רב אשי instead reads רב as saying she stays permitted to her first husband. שמואל adds that she leaves only if she does not contradict a single witness, and the גמרא reconciles this with עולא's rule using רבי נחמיה's majority rule. Finally the גמרא asks why she needs a גט from the second man. רב הונא explains it as a גזירה, and the גמרא defends this using רבי יוסי בן כיפר. A new challenge from the משנה's later clause begins and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְהַבָּא עָלֶיהָ

But the one who has relations with her

בְּאָשָׁם תָּלוּי קָאֵי!

stands obligated to bring an uncertain guilt-offering!

אָמַר רַב שֵׁשֶׁת:

Rav Sheshet said:

כְּגוֹן שֶׁנִּיסֵּת לְאֶחָד מֵעֵדֶיהָ.

For example, where she married one of her witnesses.
