Topic

Does רבי עקיבא hold that a person can transfer or forbid something that does not exist yet (דבר שלא בא לעולם)? A proof from the vow 'קונם whatever I make for your mouth', a rejection of that proof, and a chain of rabbis showing that רבי עקיבא does allow it.

Overview

The גמרא brings a משנה about a wife who uses a נדר to forbid her future earnings to her husband. The first opinion says the husband does not need to annul it. רבי עקיבא says he should annul it, which seems to show that a נדר can take hold on things that do not exist yet. The גמרא rejects this proof: רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע explains that she vowed on her hands, which already exist. The גמרא then notes that this conflicts with רב נחמן בר יצחק. He traces a chain of rabbis, from רב הונא back to רבי עקיבא, who all hold 'אדם מקנה דבר שלא בא לעולם'. The גמרא then shows the source for each name. For רב הונא it is his ruling on selling palm fruit before it grows, and רב נחמן disagrees with that ruling. For רב it is giving away a field before buying it. For רבי ינאי and רבי חייא it is a story about separating מעשר for fruit that had not arrived yet. רבי חייא supports his ruling with a ברייתא about 'all the days', and the גמרא begins working out what law that ברייתא teaches. That discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

קֻוֽנָּם שֶׁאֲנִי עוֹשָׂה לְפִיךָ —

Konam: whatever I make for your mouth

אֵין צָרִיךְ לְהָפֵר.

he does not need to annul it.

רַבִּי עֲקִיבָא אוֹמֵר: יָפֵר,

Rabbi Akiva says: He should annul it,

שֶׁמָּא תַּעֲדִיף עָלָיו

lest she produce extra for him,

The steps (13)

  1. ProofThe vow on future earnings: the first opinion
  2. Proofרבי עקיבא says the husband should annul
  3. RejectionRejecting the proof: she vowed on her hands
  4. StatementA chain of rabbis back to רבי עקיבא
  5. Statementרב הונא's source: selling palm-tree fruit before it grows
  6. Statementרב נחמן disagrees: the seller can back out even after the fruit grows
  7. Statementרב נחמן admits: if the buyer already ate, we don't take it back
  8. Statementרב's source: giving away a field before buying it
  9. Statementרבי ינאי's sharecropper is late, and he asks רבי חייא
  10. Responseרבי חייא approves what רבי ינאי did
  11. ProofA ברייתא reads "all the days" as including שבת and יום טוב
  12. QuestionWhat law does the ברייתא teach?
  13. RejectionIt cannot mean permission to separate מעשר and eat on שבת
