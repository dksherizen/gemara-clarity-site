Topic

The full brother and son status of a ממזר, and whether a sister who is also the father's wife's daughter brings one חיוב or two.

Overview

The amud finishes the earlier point that a ממזר brother also creates the bond of יבום. It then explains the משנה's words 'he is his brother in all respects': he inherits, and a כהן may become impure for him. That is true even though a כהן may not become impure for an unfit wife, because a wife is meant to be sent away but a brother stays a brother. A brother from a שפחה or a גויה does not count at all, because the child follows the mother. Any son, even a ממזר, frees his father's widow from יבום, learned from 'ובן אין לו'. A ממזר son is punished for hitting his father when the father has done תשובה, even though his sin can never be fully fixed. Then a ברייתא brings an argument between the רבנן and רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה: does a sister who is also his father's wife's daughter bring two חיובים or one? Along the way the amud teaches אין מזהירין מן הדין, and that a half-sister counts only if her mother could legally marry the father. רבא's teaching shows that a sister born to an אנוסה still counts.

Opening lines

מִיזְקָק נָמֵי זָקֵיק.

He also creates the levirate bond.

וְאָחִיו הוּא לְכׇל דָּבָר.

And he is his brother in every respect.

לְמַאי הִלְכְתָא?

For which law is this said?

לְיוֹרְשׁוֹ וְלִיטָּמֵא לוֹ.

To inherit from him, and to become impure for him.
