Yevamos 90b

יבמות צ׳ ע״ב

Topic

Whether חכמים can actively uproot Torah law, and the penalties on a woman who remarried in error

Overview

Right before this, רבה taught that חכמים can uproot a Torah law when they only tell a person to sit back and not act (שב ואל תעשה). רב חסדא lists cases he had planned to challenge him from, then drops the challenge, because every one of them only involves not acting. The גמרא then tries to prove that a Torah law can also be uprooted by active doing. It brings three sources: a נביא like אליהו at הר הכרמל, a canceled גט, and בית דין punishing beyond Torah law. Each proof is pushed off. A נביא's power comes from a פסוק, and emergency steps to guard the Torah (מיגדר מילתא) are a special case. For the גט, every marriage rests on the approval of the רבנן, so they can cancel the קידושין, even קידושין made through ביאה. Then the גמרא goes back to the משנה about a woman who remarried in error. It explains why neither husband may become טמא for her, take her found objects or earnings, or cancel her vows. Finally it quotes the next line of the משנה: a בת ישראל in this case can no longer marry a כהן.

Opening lines

אֲמַר לֵיהּ, בְּעַאי לְאוֹתוֹבָךְ:

He said to him: I wanted to challenge you

עָרֵל, הַזָּאָה.

from an uncircumcised man, and from sprinkling.

וְאִזְמֵל, סָדִין בְּצִיצִית.

And from the circumcision knife, and a linen cloak with ציצית.

וְכִבְשֵׂי עֲצֶרֶת, וְשׁוֹפָר, וְלוּלָב.

And from the lambs of עצרת, and the שופר, and the לולב.

The steps (25)

  1. Challengeרב חסדא's list of cases he planned to challenge from
  2. ResolutionAll the cases count as sitting back, so there is no challenge
  3. ChallengeA נביא can tell you to break a Torah מצוה
  4. ResolutionA נביא is different because the Torah commands us to obey him
  5. ChallengeLet us learn the power of the רבנן from the נביא
  6. ResolutionProtecting the people's observance is a special case
  7. ProofThe case of the canceled גט
  8. ChallengeThe רבנן let a married woman marry anyone
  9. ResolutionEvery קידושין depends on the רבנן
  10. ChallengeWhat about קידושין done through relations?
  11. ResolutionThe רבנן made his relations count as בעילת זנות
  12. Proofבית דין may punish beyond Torah law: the horse rider
  13. ProofA second story: the man under the fig tree
  14. RejectionGuarding the Torah is a special case
  15. StatementBack to the משנה: neither husband may become טמא for her
  16. QuestionWhere do we learn this?
  17. ResponseThe פסוק: "שארו" means his wife
  18. ResponseA husband becomes impure for a fit wife but not for a disqualified one
  19. StatementQuoting the משנה: neither husband gets her found objects
  20. ResponseWhy she keeps her found objects: let him resent her
  21. StatementQuoting the משנה: neither husband gets her earnings
  22. ResponseWhy she keeps her earnings: she gets no food from him
  23. StatementQuoting the משנה: neither husband cancels her vows
  24. ResponseWhy he cannot cancel her vows: let her become unattractive to him
  25. StatementQuoting the next line: she is disqualified from marrying a כהן
