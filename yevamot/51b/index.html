Topic

Whether רבנן and רבן גמליאל disagree about זיקה, and רבי יוחנן's list of תנאים who hold that מאמר is a full acquisition

Overview

The amud opens in the middle of an argument about a ברייתא where רבן גמליאל and רבנן disagree about a גט after a גט and a מאמר after a מאמר. The גמרא first tries to read their dispute as a dispute about זיקה. That reading would make רבנן clash with רבה בר רב הונא in the name of רב, who said an invalid חליצה must be repeated with all the brothers. He answers that both sides hold אין זיקה and argue only about a second גט or a second מאמר. The גמרא then quotes רבן גמליאל's ruling again and asks why the first woman cannot have ייבום. It answers that this is a rabbinic גזירה, so he won't come to do ייבום with the second woman. Next, רבי יוחנן says that רבן גמליאל, בית שמאי, רבי שמעון, בן עזאי and רבי נחמיה all hold that מאמר is a full acquisition. The גמרא brings a source for each of the five. Finally, it returns to the משנה's next line, 'כיצד? עשה מאמר', to begin a new discussion.

Opening lines

רַבָּנַן סָבְרִי יֵשׁ זִיקָּה,

The Rabbis hold there is a levirate bond,

וְקָתָנֵי סֵיפָא:

and the latter clause teaches:

וְכֵן אַתָּה אוֹמֵר

And you say the same

בִּשְׁנֵי יְבָמִין וִיבָמָה אַחַת.

regarding two yevamin and one yevama.

The steps (12)

  1. ChallengeIf the Rabbis hold there is a levirate bond, the ending of the ברייתא seems to clash with רב
  2. ResolutionBoth sides hold there is no levirate bond; they only argue about a second divorce or a second betrothal
  3. StatementQuoting רבן גמליאל's ruling about two levirate betrothals
  4. ChallengeIf the second betrothal does nothing, why can't he marry the first?
  5. ResolutionA rabbinic decree so he won't marry the second woman
  6. Statementרבי יוחנן: five תנאים hold that levirate betrothal is a full acquisition
  7. ProofThe source for רבן גמליאל is what was already said
  8. ProofThe source for including בית שמאי
  9. ProofThe source for including רבי שמעון
  10. ProofThe source for including בן עזאי
  11. ProofThe source for including רבי נחמיה
  12. StatementBack to the משנה: 'How so? He did מאמר'
