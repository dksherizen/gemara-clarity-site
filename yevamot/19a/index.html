Topic

How strong is the יבום bond (זיקה) for רבי שמעון, and when does a later-born brother's birth forbid the widow?

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a proof that רבי שמעון is in doubt about how strong the יבום bond (זיקה) is. Someone suggests that his requirement of חליצה is only a rabbinic גזירה. The גמרא rejects that, because a ברייתא shows רבי שמעון's real reason is a doubt about the second brother's מאמר. אביי then argues that רבי שמעון may hold זיקה ככנוסה when there is one יבם, and be unsure only when there are two. The גמרא tests this split against רבי שמעון's 'rule' about a brother born before or after the יבום, and against רב אושעיא's case of two brothers married to related women. רב עמרם and רבא each try to answer the second challenge, and both answers are rejected. רב אשי then saves the view: the women fell at the very same moment, which רבי שמעון holds is possible, like רבי יוסי הגלילי. Finally, רב פפא limits רבי שמעון's leniency to a case where the יבום came before the brother's birth, and a ברייתא is brought that supports him and refutes רבי אושעיא.

Opening lines

מִבַּיִת אֶחָד.

from one household.

יַבּוֹמֵי חֲדָא וְאִיפְּטוֹרֵ[י] אִידַּךְ

To do יבום with one and free the other,

לָא, דְּדִלְמָא

he may not, because perhaps

אֵין זִיקָה כִּכְנוּסָה,

the bond is not like full marriage,
