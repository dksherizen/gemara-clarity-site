Topic

The two readings of the משנה, the sources for excluding אשת אחיו שלא היה בעולמו and a maternal brother from יבום, and the argument of רב and רב יהודה about זיקה

Overview

The גמרא first settles a disagreement about the wording of the משנה: some call the woman who must leave 'the first' and others call her 'the second.' Both readings are shown to be correct. 'First' means first to fall to יבום, and 'second' means she has been married twice. Next, רב learns from 'כי ישבו אחים יחדו' that there is no יבום with אשת אחיו שלא היה בעולמו. From 'יחדו' he also learns that a brother from the mother only is excluded. רבה learns that second rule instead from a גזירה שוה of 'אחים' by the sons of יעקב. The גמרא explains why that source beats עריות and לוט, and why the תורה needed both 'אחים' and 'יחדו'. Then רב (quoted by רב הונא) says that if a שומרת יבם dies, the יבם may marry her mother, because אין זיקה, and the גמרא explains his exact wording and defends him from a משנה. Finally, רב יהודה forbids the mother because יש זיקה, and the גמרא begins explaining his wording, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

רִאשׁוֹנָה לָא מִשְׁתַּבַּשׁ:

[The one who teaches] 'the first' is not mistaken,

מַאי רִאשׁוֹנָה —

What does 'first' mean?

רִאשׁוֹנָה לִנְפִילָה,

The first to fall [to יבום].

וּמַאן דְּתָנֵי שְׁנִיָּה לָא מִשְׁתַּבַּשׁ:

And the one who teaches 'the second' is not mistaken,
