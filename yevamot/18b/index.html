Topic

שמואל's view that יש זיקה, and the משנה of a brother born after יבום, where רבי שמעון's leniency is traced to זיקה and challenged by רב יוסף

Overview

The גמרא first shows that שמואל holds יש זיקה, because he rules like רבי יהודה בן בתירה: a brother may not marry the sister of a שומרת יבם until the יבם does יבום or חליצה. After a short back-and-forth about why the ruling can't be רב's, רב זביד of נהרדעא reports a version where שמואל says it openly: even after a שומרת יבם dies, her mother is forbidden. The גמרא explains why both of שמואל's rulings are needed. One teaches that the bond is real even with two יבמים. The other teaches that it does not end by itself at death. A new משנה follows: if a brother is born after the second brother already did יבום, the חכמים exempt both wives, while רבי שמעון allows normal יבום. רב אושעיא proves from an otherwise extra משנה that רבי שמעון argues even when the brother was born before the יבום. The גמרא then explains that רבי שמעון's reason is that זיקה makes her like a married woman. רב יוסף objects that רבי שמעון is unsure even about זיקה plus מאמר, and the גמרא brings the משנה of three brothers to show that doubt. The discussion continues on the next amud.
