Topic

The strength of מאמר according to בית שמאי: whether it pushes away the צרה fully, and whether it makes נישואין or only אירוסין

Overview

The amud asks how strong מאמר, a יבם's verbal betrothal of his יבמה, is according to בית שמאי. An attempted proof from the two-sisters משנה fails, because a מאמר given where יבום is forbidden has no power at all. In רב אשי's version, רבי אלעזר says that for בית שמאי מאמר pushes away the צרה but leaves a remnant, so she still needs חליצה. רבי אבין supports this from the two-sisters משנה. The three-brothers משנה seems to show a full pushing away. The גמרא resolves this: a יבמה fit for full יבום gets a full-strength מאמר, and one who is not fit does not. Then רבה asks whether מאמר makes נישואין or only אירוסין. After אביי's challenges, the practical question becomes whether she still needs a חופה. The גמרא tries to answer from a משנה about a יבם cancelling a שומרת יבם's vows. Two answers block the proof: he may cancel only together with her father, or he cancels alone because the court made him support her.

Opening lines

וְאִי סָלְקָא דַּעְתָּךְ

And if it enters your mind

מַאֲמָר לְבֵית שַׁמַּאי

that levirate betrothal, according to Beit Shammai,

קוֹנֶה קִנְיָן גָּמוּר:

acquires as a complete acquisition,

זֶה יַעֲשֶׂה מַאֲמָר וְיִקְנֶה,

then this one should perform levirate betrothal and acquire,
