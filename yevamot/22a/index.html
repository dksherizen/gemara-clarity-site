Topic

שניות (their counting, scope, and application to converts), converted brothers as witnesses, and the new משנה on who counts as a brother or son for יבום

Overview

The גמרא finishes counting the list of שניות. It then rejects רב הלל's proof against אמימר, because no one can confirm that the list he saw was written by מר בריה דרבנא. Next comes a teaching from the school of רבי חייא that adds great-granddaughters and the wife's great-grandmothers as שניות. The גמרא explains why the wife is counted as a generation going up but not going down. It then asks whether these שניות have a stopping point; both proofs are pushed off, and the question is never settled. רב נחמן teaches that the רבנן did not decree שניות for גרים, and he rules on whether converted brothers may testify together, where אמימר is more lenient. The גמרא explains that testimony is in the hands of בית דין, and a גר is like a newborn. Finally, a new משנה teaches that any brother obligates יבום and any son exempts from it. The גמרא shows that 'of any kind' includes a ממזר, and it begins an argument that continues on the next amud.
