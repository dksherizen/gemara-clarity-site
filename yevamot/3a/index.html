Topic

The order of the relatives in the משנה's list, and the precise wording of פוטרות and מן החליצה ומן הייבום

Overview

The גמרא continues explaining why the משנה opens its list with בתו. All fifteen relatives need a דרשה for their יבום exemption, but only for בתו (a daughter from rape) is even the basic איסור learned through a דרשה, from the גזירה שוה links הנה–הנה and זמה–זמה. When this approach runs into trouble over where אחות אשה and the sister cases are placed, the גמרא drops it. In its place, it explains that the תנא lists the women by closeness of relation. He keeps related groups together, such as three generations, or sisters and siblings, even when that bends the order slightly. Next the גמרא examines the משנה's wording. It says פוטרות rather than אוסרות, because the צרה needs no חליצה and is forbidden only where the מצוה of יבום applies. It mentions both חליצה and יבום to teach that whoever is not fit for יבום does not need חליצה either. It lists חליצה first because the משנה follows אבא שאול, who gives חליצה priority. Finally, the גמרא asks what the exact numbers in the משנה come to exclude, and the answers come on the next amud.
