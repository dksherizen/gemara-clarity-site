Topic

The label שומרת יבם in a סוטה ברייתא, and whether a יבמה after מאמר (or after מאמר and a גט) may eat תרומה.

Overview

The amud opens by asking how a ברייתא about סוטה can call a woman a שומרת יבם after her יבם was intimate with her, since רב holds that such relations acquire her fully. The גמרא first answers 'like שמואל', but rejects that answer, since the whole discussion only matters according to רב. It then explains the ברייתא as a case where the יבם first did מאמר, following בית שמאי. It defends the ברייתא's wording by comparing the pair ארוסה and שומרת יבם to the pair נשואה and כנוסה. רב פפא and רב נחמן בר יצחק then give other ways to explain the ברייתא: one by pointing to a particular תנא, and one by extending the oath (גלגול). Next, a new topic begins: whether a יבמה after מאמר may eat תרומה. רב חנינא's report of רבי יוחנן fits no תנא, so רבין gives a corrected version. In that version, everyone agrees on plain מאמר and on a brother who is a חלל, and רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש argue only when a גט was given, each with his reasoning. Finally, the גמרא returns to the משנה and begins a question to שמואל about a כהן גדול who betrothed a minor who grew up while betrothed to him.

שׁוֹמֶרֶת יָבָם קָרֵית לַהּ?
