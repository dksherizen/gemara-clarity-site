Topic

A חרש's mind and his גט; divorcing a שוטה wife; רבי יוחנן בן נורי's challenge; a גט called a loan document

Overview

The amud opens by finishing רב אשי's question about a חרש. Is his mind weak but steady every day, or does it switch between clear and unclear? The difference is whether he can give his wife a גט, and the question ends in תיקו. Next the גמרא turns to a wife who became a שוטה. רבי יצחק says that by Torah law she could be divorced, but the רבנן forbade it so people would not treat her as הפקר. After challenges from the schools of רבי ינאי and רבי ישמעאל, the גמרא sets the case: she understands her גט but cannot protect herself. אביי supports this from the משנה's use of the word 'never' only for the husband. The גמרא then shows that רבי יוחנן בן נורי was challenging the רבנן on their own terms. רבא learns from רבי יוחנן בן גודגדא that a גט handed over as a 'loan document' still divorces. The amud ends with the start of a story about keys lost on שבת, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אִי לָאו דַּעְתָּא צִילּוּתָא,

or if his mind is not clear,

וּלְעוֹלָם חֲדָא דַּעְתָּא הוּא.

but he is always of one mind.

אוֹ דִלְמָא פְּשִׁיטָא לֵיהּ

Or perhaps it is obvious to him

דְּדַעְתֵּיהּ קְלִישְׁתָּא

that his mind is weak
