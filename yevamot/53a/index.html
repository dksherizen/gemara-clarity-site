Topic

The dispute about קידושין 'through the levirate bond' after חליצה, and a line-by-line study of the משנה on acts done after חליצה, מאמר, גט and ביאה

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a dispute between רבי and רבנן: a יבם did חליצה and then said 'be betrothed to me through the levirate bond.' Four explanations are given, each narrower than the last. The first is whether the זיקה is real. The second, from רב שרביא, is whether a חליצה פסולה frees her. The third, from רב אשי, is whether a תנאי works with חליצה. The fourth, from רבינא, is whether that תנאי must be a תנאי כפול. The גמרא then goes through the lines of the משנה one by one. אביי and רבא say the משנה should also teach 'nothing works after ביאה.' The תנא left it out because he preferred the case that frees the יבמה to marry anyone. The גמרא notes that the משנה does not follow בן עזאי. It tries to prove שמואל's view, and to refute רב יוסף, from the משנה's cases. Both proofs are rejected because the משנה says 'חלץ,' which only describes what was done after the fact. The proof for רבה בר רב הונא is also rejected. Finally, the גמרא asks why the משנה needs a line that seems obvious according to ריש לקיש. It answers that the תנא listed every combination of יבמין and יבמות just to complete the pattern.

Opening lines

יֵשׁ זִיקָה,

[רבי holds:] there is a bond,

וַאֲתַאי חֲלִיצָה אַפְקַעְתַּיהּ לְזִיקָּה.

and the חליצה came and cancelled the bond.

וְרַבָּנַן סָבְרִי: אֵין זִיקָּה.
