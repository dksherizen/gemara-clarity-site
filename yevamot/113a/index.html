Topic

Marriages of a חרש and חרשת: תרומה, כתובה, and אשם תלוי

Overview

The amud opens with the last words of an earlier answer: men would avoid marrying a חרשת if she could do מיאון forever. The גמרא then compares a קטנה and a חרשת, whose marriages are both only מדרבנן. A קטנה married to a כהן eats תרומה, but a חרשת does not. This is explained through a chain of גזירות: a חרש might feed a פקחת wife, and from תרומה דרבנן he could slide into תרומה דאורייתא. Next, a קטנה has a כתובה but a חרשת does not, so that men will not avoid marrying her. The גמרא brings a משנה and a ברייתא as sources. A פקחת married to a חרש also gets no כתובה, because women want to marry even more than men do. רבא praises רב מלכיו for giving a חרש's wife a כתובה from the חרש's own property, since a wife helps him even more than a maidservant. Then שמואל rules about an אשם תלוי for relations with a חרש's wife, in two versions. Both versions are built on רבי אלעזר's view that a חרש's acts are a ספק. The amud ends with רב אשי asking for רבי אלעזר's reason, and his question continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מִימַּנְעִי וְלָא נָסְבִי לַהּ.

They would hold back and not marry her.

וּמַאי שְׁנָא קְטַנָּה

And what is different about a minor girl

דְּאָכְלָה בִּתְרוּמָה,

that she eats תרומה,

וּמַאי שְׁנָא חֵרֶשֶׁת

and what is different about a deaf-mute woman

The steps (30)

  1. ResolutionPeople would avoid marrying her
  2. ChallengeWhy can a minor eat תרומה but a deaf-mute woman cannot?
  3. ResolutionA decree: a deaf-mute husband might feed a deaf-mute wife
  4. ChallengeSo what? She is like a minor eating forbidden food
  5. ResolutionA decree: a deaf-mute husband might feed a competent wife
  6. ChallengeLet her at least eat rabbinic תרומה
  7. ResolutionHe might end up feeding her Torah-level תרומה
  8. QuestionWhy does a minor get a marriage contract but a deaf-mute woman does not?
  9. ResponseMen would refuse to marry her
  10. QuestionWhat is the source that a minor gets a marriage contract?
  11. ResponseA משנה shows that a minor who is divorced has a marriage contract
  12. QuestionWhat is the source that a deaf-mute woman has no marriage contract?
  13. ResponseA ברייתא about deaf-mute and imbecile marriages
  14. ConclusionIf he did not choose to write one, she gets nothing
  15. ChallengeWhy not give a כתובה to a woman who marries a deaf man?
  16. ResolutionWomen want to marry even more than men do
  17. Statementרב מלכיו marries off a deaf neighbor with a כתובה
  18. Statementרבא praises רב מלכיו's wisdom
  19. StatementNo אשם תלוי for the wife of a deaf man
  20. ProofA possible support from the משנה about תרומה
  21. Rejectionשמואל follows רבי אלעזר, who treats a חרש's תרומה as doubtful
  22. ChallengeThen the man should bring a guilt-offering for uncertainty
  23. ResolutionA guilt-offering for uncertainty needs the case of one piece out of two
  24. Challengeרבי אלעזר requires a guilt-offering even for a כוי
  25. Resolutionשמואל agrees with רבי אלעזר on one point and disagrees on another
  26. StatementAnother version: שמואל requires an אשם תלוי for the wife of a deaf-mute
  27. ChallengeChallenge from the rule that five people may not separate תרומה
  28. Resolutionשמואל follows רבי אלעזר
  29. Questionרב אשי asks for רבי אלעזר's reason
  30. QuestionFirst possibility: a weak mind that may still be clear
