Topic

Did בית שמאי practice their view on צרת הבת? The story of רבי דוסא בן הרכינס and the three הלכות of חגי הנביא

Overview

The גמרא finishes its proofs that בית שמאי really acted on their view that the צרה of a daughter may marry the brothers, and concludes that they did. It then tells the full story behind one of those proofs. רבי יהושע, רבי אלעזר בן עזריה and רבי עקיבא visit the elderly, nearly blind רבי דוסא בן הרכינס, who welcomes each one warmly. When they ask about צרת הבת, he rules like בית הלל and explains that the ruling quoted as 'בן הרכינס' came from his brother יונתן, a student of שמאי. He swears that חגי הנביא taught three הלכות: צרת הבת is forbidden, עמון ומואב give מעשר עני in שביעית, and גרים are accepted from the קרדויים and תרמודים. רבי עקיבא then holds his ground against יונתן and answers his insult with humility. The גמרא explains חגי's second and third הלכות and settles a challenge about the קרדויים. Finally it brings רבי יוחנן's view that גרים are not accepted from the תרמודים and begins questioning it from a משנה about stains from רקם. That discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בְּנֵי צָרוֹת אֲנִי מֵעִיד לָכֶם.

Children of rival wives — I testify to you.

תָּא שְׁמַע:

Come and hear:

בִּימֵי רַבִּי דּוֹסָא בֶּן הַרְכִּינָס

In the days of Rabbi Dosa ben Harkinas

הוּתְּרָה צָרַת הַבַּת לָאַחִין.

the rival wife of a daughter was permitted to the brothers.
