Topic

How the מצוה of פריה ורביה is fulfilled: משה's example, רבי נתן's reports of בית שמאי and בית הלל, a convert's earlier children, and children who died.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a back-and-forth about the מצוה of פריה ורביה. Why don't בית הלל learn from משה, who separated from his wife after two sons? The answer is that משה acted on his own reasoning. A ברייתא lists three things משה decided on his own, and ה׳ agreed with each one: separating from his wife, breaking the לוחות, and adding a day before מתן תורה. The גמרא then brings two ברייתות in which רבי נתן reports the argument between בית שמאי and בית הלל differently, along with the פסוקים behind each version. Next, רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש argue whether a convert's children from before he converted count. They are consistent with how they rule about a בכור for inheritance. This leads to a discussion of whether gentiles and slaves have family lineage. Finally, רב הונא and רבי יוחנן argue whether a man whose children died has fulfilled the מצוה. The amud ends as the גמרא begins a challenge to רב הונא from a ברייתא, which continues on the next amud.
