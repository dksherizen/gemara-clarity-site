Topic

When sacred meat in a mixture can be nullified (בטל), and whether רבי יוחנן holds תרומה nowadays is דאורייתא

Overview

This amud continues the discussion of a ברייתא about a piece of חטאת meat mixed into one hundred other pieces. ריש לקיש explained that the ברייתא allows nullification only when the piece was crushed. The גמרא first explains that רבי יהודה disagrees because of his rule מין במינו לא בטיל. It then asks why the ברייתא switched to a case of חולין, and answers that the תנא preferred to teach a pure-in-pure case. Next it asks why the first clause allows nullification and the second does not. רב שישא בריה דרב אידי answers that the first clause is only a rabbinic impurity of liquids, while the second clause involves a Torah prohibition. רבה's answer (לאו versus כרת) is left as a קשיא, and רב אשי's answer (דבר שיש לו מתירין) is rejected as a mistake. Finally, the גמרא asks whether רבי יוחנן really holds that תרומה nowadays is from the Torah, given his lenient view in the two-baskets ברייתא. It answers that the ברייתא follows the רבנן.

Opening lines

רַבִּי יְהוּדָה לְטַעְמֵיהּ,

Rabbi Yehuda follows his own reasoning,

דְּאָמַר: מִין בְּמִינוֹ לֹא בָּטֵיל.

as he said: a type mixed with its own type is not nullified.

אֲבָל לֹא נִימּוֹחָה מַאי — לֹא תַּעֲלֶה,

But if it was not crushed, what? It is not nullified.

אַדְּתָנֵי: אֲבָל חֲתִיכָה שֶׁל חַטָּאת טְהוֹרָה

Instead of teaching: but a piece of pure sin-offering
