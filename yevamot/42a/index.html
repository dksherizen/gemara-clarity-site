Topic

The three-month wait before remarriage: when it starts, why it exists, why it must be three months, and the ban on marrying another man's pregnant or nursing wife

Overview

This amud discusses the three-month wait before a woman may remarry. First, a ברייתא about a יבמה shows that her three months count from her husband's death, not from the חליצה. רבא defends this with a קל וחומר: if the three months already lift a כרת prohibition, they surely lift a lighter לאו. Next the גמרא asks why all other women must wait too. שמואל says it is so we can tell apart the first husband's child from the second's. רבא says it is a גזירה so the child will not later fall into ערוה. The גמרא then shows why the wait must be a full three months: shorter waits, or checking her for pregnancy, still leave doubt about who the father is. Finally, it asks why a woman already known to be pregnant or nursing may not marry another man. It tests several reasons for this, and the last one, that a pregnant woman will soon be nursing, continues on the next amud.
