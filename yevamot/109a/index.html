Topic

רבי אלעזר's ban on ייבום after a divorce and remarriage, and two brothers married to two sisters who are minors

Overview

The משנה ends its cases of a man who divorced his wife, took her back, and died childless. The רבנן allow ייבום and רבי אלעזר forbids it, but everyone forbids it for a קטנה married off by her father who was divorced and then remarried. The גמרא looks for רבי אלעזר's reason. עיפה's answer is rejected. אביי says רבי אלעזר is unsure whether death or the first marriage links her to the יבם. רבא says it is a safeguard because people may not know about the remarriage. רב אשי says it is a decree because of the remarried minor 'orphan in her father's lifetime,' and the משנה and a ברייתא support him. רב נחמן rules that her צרה may still do ייבום, since we do not make a גזירה לגזירה. A new משנה discusses two brothers married to two sisters who are minors or deaf-mutes. When the adult sister is widowed, רבי אליעזר, רבן גמליאל and רבי יהושע give three different solutions. The גמרא defends teaching a minor to do מיאון when it is for a מצוה, and then begins explaining בר קפרא's list of things to cling to.
