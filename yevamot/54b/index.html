יבמות נ״ד ע״ב

Topic

Sources that every ערוה counts from העראה, what the extra words נדה and העראה teach, and which half-relatives are forbidden

Overview

The גמרא finishes rejecting an attempt to learn from נדה and אשת אח that every ערוה counts from the first stage of contact (העראה). Instead, רבי יונה brings a פסוק that compares all עריות to נדה. That leaves two words with no job. The word נדה written by אשת אח is used for רב הונא's teaching: a brother's wife is forbidden with כרת while her husband is alive, just as a נדה is forbidden with כרת until she becomes permitted. The extra העראה written by a father's sister and a mother's sister is moved over by רבא to teach that העראה counts even with a בהמה. A ברייתא then shows that the logic about these aunts can go either way, so a פסוק is needed to teach that they are forbidden through either parent. רבי אבהו explains why both aunts had to be written. רבא learns from the word דודו in the redemption laws that an uncle's wife is forbidden only through a paternal uncle. Finally, the גמרא quotes a משנה about a man who kept marrying his supposedly dead wife's sister, and the question about it comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

בְּיוֹמֵי תַּלְיָא מִילְּתָא.

The matter depends on days.

אֵשֶׁת אָח — בְּבָנִים תְּלָא רַחֲמָנָא.

A brother's wife — the Merciful One made it depend on children.

אֶלָּא פָּרֵיךְ הָכִי:

Rather, refute it like this:

מָה לְנִדָּה וְאֵשֶׁת אָח

What about a נדה and a brother's wife
