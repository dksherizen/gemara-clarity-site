Topic

The end of the רב–שמואל discussion about a שומרת יבם; a man who married a chain of half-sisters after false death reports; and the power of a nine-year-old יבם's ביאה, מאמר and גט

Overview

The גמרא finishes settling an apparent contradiction in שמואל's views. It offers several answers. Maybe שמואל agreed with רבי יוסי only on one point. Or maybe the dispute between רב and שמואל about a שומרת יבם being 'like an אשת איש' is about רב המנונא's rule on זנות, or about whether an outsider's קידושין take effect with her. Then a new משנה presents a man who heard false reports that his wife died and married her half-sisters one after another. It rules which of the women are his valid wives, and it explains how the answer flips if the first wife really did die. A second משנה teaches that a nine-year-old יבם and his adult brothers can each block the other from the יבמה. The גמרא works out that the boy's ביאה blocks his brothers even when it comes last, while his מאמר blocks only when it comes first. Finally, sources show that the boy also has a גט and a מאמר, and רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע explains that the boy's גט is real but weaker than an adult's.

Opening lines

וְדִלְמָא אַ״אֵינוֹ פּוֹסֵל״?!

But perhaps [שמואל's ruling refers] to 'he does not disqualify'?!

אִי נָמֵי: מִמַּאי דְּאִיתַהּ לִדְרַב הוּנָא?

Alternatively: from where [do we know] that רב הונא's [explanation] holds?

דִּלְמָא לֵיתַהּ דְּרַב הוּנָא כְּלָל,

Perhaps רב הונא's [explanation] does not hold at all,

וּבִדְרַב הַמְנוּנָא קָמִיפַּלְגִי.

and they are arguing about רב המנונא's [rule].
