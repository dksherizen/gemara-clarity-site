Topic

Sisters of a חלוצה, divorcée, or יבמה who fall to יבום; whether a יבמה forbidden for a time becomes permitted again; and the three-month wait before remarriage

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier question. The Rabbis forbade the צרה of a חלוצה's relative, because a relative comes to court with the חלוצה and people could mix them up. A צרה never comes along, so her relatives were not forbidden. A new משנה then teaches that a חלוצה's sister who falls to the same יבם needs חליצה. A divorcée's sister is fully exempt. ריש לקיש explains why: the first is forbidden only מדרבנן, the second מדאורייתא. The משנה also says that a brother who betrothed the sister of a שומרת יבם must wait until the יבמה is freed, and שמואל rules this way. The גמרא then asks: if the betrothed wife dies, may he do יבום with her sister? רב and רבי חנינא say yes. שמואל and רב אסי say no. רבא explains רב: a יבמה who was permitted, then forbidden, then permitted again returns to her first permission. רב המנונא challenges this from a ברייתא. רבא answers that the ברייתא follows רבי אלעזר, and the גמרא shows that even רבנן could agree with שמואל, since the ban began after נפילה. Finally, a new משנה requires a three-month wait before a יבמה or any woman remarries. רבי יהודה and רבי יוסי give exceptions, and רבי יוסי's reason continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הָךְ דְּאָזְלָא בַּהֲדַהּ לְבֵי דִינָא

That one, who goes together with her to court

גְּזַרוּ בְּהוּ רַבָּנַן

the Rabbis decreed regarding them.

הַאי דְּלָא אָזְלָא בַּהֲדַהּ לְבֵי דִּינָא

This one, who does not go together with her to court

לָא גְּזַרוּ בְּהוּ רַבָּנַן

the Rabbis did not decree regarding them.
