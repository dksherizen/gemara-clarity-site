Topic

How שעטנז teaches that an עשה pushes aside a לא תעשה, and why עליה is needed to block an עשה from pushing aside a לאו with כרת

Overview

This amud first finishes defending the idea that the side-by-side פסוקים of שעטנז and גדילים (דברים 22:11-12) can teach a lesson. A lesson like that needs a free, extra word. The גמרא tries 'גדילים', then 'צמר ופשתים יחדו', then 'שעטנז'. Each time it is pushed back, because the word has its own job. In the end it says the unusual word שעטנז carries everything, both the rule that the material must be combed, spun and twisted (שוע, טווי, נוז) and the link that teaches that an עשה pushes aside a plain לא תעשה. Then the גמרא asks why the word עליה was needed to stop יבום with a wife's sister, since we have no source that an עשה can push aside a לאו with כרת. It tries to learn such a source from מילה, פסח and תמיד, alone and in pairs and all together, and rejects every option. It then answers that we might have learned it from כיבוד אב ואם, and brings a ברייתא about honoring parents and שבת. The גמרא suggests reading that ברייתא as a father asking for real שבת labor, begins to reject that reading, and the discussion continues on the next amud.
