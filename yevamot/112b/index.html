Topic

The end of פרק בית שמאי (כופין like רב), then פרק חרש. It covers the marriage, divorce and יבום of a חרש or חרשת, and why the רבנן enacted marriage for them but not for a שוטה or קטן.

Overview

The amud opens at the end of a discussion about a woman who made a נדר against her husband's brother. The גמרא first suggests that the משנה is about a woman who has children. It then rejects that idea and concludes that בית דין forces the brother to do חליצה either way, like רב, and פרק בית שמאי closes with the Hadran. A new פרק begins with a משנה about a חרש or חרשת. They can marry and divorce through gestures, and the rule changes when one spouse loses capacity after the wedding. רבי יוחנן בן נורי asks why a wife who became a חרשת can be divorced but a husband who became a חרש cannot. The חכמים answer that a man divorces only by his own will, while a woman can be divorced even against hers. The משנה then works through many cases of two brothers, some deaf and some competent, married to sisters or to unrelated women, and rules on יבום, חליצה and divorce in each. In the גמרא, רמי בר חמא asks why the רבנן set up marriage for a חרש but not for a שוטה or a קטן, and the גמרא explains each difference, including the case of a קטנה. It then begins to ask why a קטנה can do מיאון but a חרשת cannot, and that answer continues on the next amud.
