Topic

Relatives forbidden after חליצה: whether שניות apply to a חלוצה, and the status of the חלוצה's צרה

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה about a man who did חליצה with his יבמה. The חכמים treat her somewhat like his wife: he may not marry her close relatives, and she may not marry his close relatives, but the other brothers are not affected. The גמרא then asks whether the חכמים also forbade the more distant relatives (שניות) of a חלוצה, as they did for a real wife. Several proofs from the משנה and from a ברייתא of רבי חייא are pushed off, because each ban could come from her real marriage to the dead brother. Finally, the case of the חולץ's daughter's son cannot be explained that way, so the גמרא concludes that the חכמים did decree שניות for a חלוצה. The גמרא then turns to the חלוצה's צרה (co-wife). שמואל rules that a child from her is a ממזר, and רב יוסף supports him from the משנה. רבי יוחנן escapes that proof, because a חלוצה's sister is forbidden only by the rabbis. The amud ends by asking why the משנה permits a relative of the צרה but forbids a צרה of the relative, and the answer comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

הוּא אָסוּר בְּאִמָּהּ,

He is forbidden to her mother,

וּבְאֵם אִמָּהּ, וּבְאֵם אָבִיהָ,

and to her mother's mother, and to her father's mother,

וּבְבִתָּהּ, וּבְבַת בִּתָּהּ, וּבְבַת בְּנָהּ,

and to her daughter, and to her daughter's daughter, and to her son's daughter,

וּבַאֲחוֹתָהּ בִּזְמַן שֶׁהִיא קַיֶּימֶת.

and to her sister while she is alive.
