Topic

Who is kept from the קרבן פסח and from holy food: the ערל, the בן נכר, the אונן, and a baby not yet due for מילה

Overview

This amud finishes explaining the extra words in the פסח פסוקים. The word בו teaches that a משומד is kept from the קרבן פסח but not from מעשר, and that a ערל is kept from the פסח but still eats מצה and מרור. The גמרא explains why the תורה had to mention both the ערל and the בן נכר, and says the two words ממנו are needed for a teaching of רבה in the name of רבי יצחק. Next it looks at רבי עקיבא, who learns from "איש איש" that a ערל may not eat holy food. The גמרא asks why that phrase includes the ערל and not the אונן, and decides for ערלות, either because it has more reasons or, as רבא says, because "איש" points to something found only in men. The גמרא then shows what each תנא does with "תושב ושכיר" and "איש איש". Finally, רב חמא בר עוקבא asks whether a baby too young for מילה counts as a ערל for oil of תרומה. רבי זירא tries to prove it from a ברייתא about the קרבן פסח, but רבא pushes the proof aside, and the back-and-forth continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מְשׁוּמָּדוּת פּוֹסֶלֶת,

Apostasy disqualifies,

וְאֵין מְשׁוּמָּדוּת פּוֹסֶלֶת בְּמַעֲשֵׂר.

but apostasy does not disqualify regarding tithe.

״כׇּל עָרֵל לֹא יֹאכַל בּוֹ״

"No uncircumcised person shall eat from it"

לְמָה לִי?

why do I need it?

The steps (32)

  1. ResponseBeing a משומד disqualifies from the פסח but not from מעשר
  2. QuestionWhy does the verse about the uncircumcised say 'from it'?
  3. ResponseThe ערל still eats מצה and מרור
  4. ResolutionWhy the תורה needed to mention both the ערל and the בן נכר
  5. QuestionWhy does the Torah say 'of it' twice?
  6. ResponseNeeded for the teaching of רבה in the name of רבי יצחק
  7. Statementרבי עקיבא learns the ערל from "איש איש"
  8. ChallengeMaybe the extra word includes an אונן instead
  9. Resolution"וכל זר": strangeness keeps a person out, mourning does not
  10. ChallengeMaybe "וכל זר" also excludes the ערל
  11. Resolution"איש איש" already includes the ערל
  12. QuestionWhy choose the ערל over the אונן?
  13. ResponseFive reasons ערלות is more serious
  14. ChallengeOn the contrary: אנינות seems more serious
  15. ResolutionThe reasons for including the ערל are more numerous
  16. Resolutionרבא: "איש איש" points to something found only in men
  17. QuestionWhat does רבי עקיבא learn from "תושב ושכיר"?
  18. Responseרב שמעיה: to include a circumcised Arab or Gibeonite
  19. ChallengeA משנה shows non-Jews never count as circumcised
  20. ResolutionNew answer: a convert who did not immerse, and a baby born circumcised
  21. ResolutionWhy רבי אליעזר does not need 'תושב ושכיר' to exclude anyone
  22. QuestionWhat does רבי אליעזר do with 'איש איש'?
  23. ResponseThe תורה speaks like people speak
  24. QuestionMay a baby not yet circumcised be anointed with oil of תרומה?
  25. Proofרבי זירא begins a proof from the ברייתא about sons and slaves at the קרבן פסח
  26. ProofA newborn between slaughter and eating seems to prove that early ערלות counts
  27. Rejectionרבא rejects the proof from the פסוק
  28. ResolutionThe ברייתא means a baby whose fever went away
  29. ChallengeA recovered baby should get seven days first
  30. ResolutionThe seven days were already given
  31. ChallengeThen circumcise him in the morning
  32. ResolutionThe answer begins: 'We require...'
