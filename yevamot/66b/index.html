Topic

Who keeps the נכסי צאן ברזל (guaranteed dowry property) after a divorce, the wife or the husband? A side discussion asks whether being financially responsible for something makes it count as yours, so that a כהן's animals and slaves may eat תרומה.

Overview

When a marriage ends, who gets the wife's guaranteed dowry items (נכסי צאן ברזל)? רב יהודה says she takes back the actual items, because they carry the honor of her father's family. רב אמי says the husband keeps them and pays their value, because the משנה says he carries their risk and that is why such slaves eat תרומה. רב ספרא answers that the משנה only says he is responsible for them, not that he owns them. The גמרא tests this with a rented cow and an appraised cow, and concludes that full responsibility, not partial, is what counts for תרומה. Next, a ברייתא is brought for each side, and רב נחמן rules like רב יהודה, because his reason is more sensible. Then comes a story: a widow's fine wool robe was used by the orphans as a shroud for her husband. רבא rules that the dead man has acquired it, since before she collects it the robe is still in the husband's possession. The amud ends as the גמרא starts showing that רבא is following his usual reasoning, with a list that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הַדִּין עִמָּהּ,

The law is with her;

וְרַב אַמֵּי אָמַר: הַדִּין עִמּוֹ.

and Rav Ami said: The law is with him.

רַב יְהוּדָה אָמַר הַדִּין עִמָּהּ —

Rav Yehuda said the law is with her

מִשּׁוּם שֶׁבַח בֵּית אָבִיהָ

because of the pride of her father's house;
