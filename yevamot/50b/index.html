Yevamos 50b

יבמות נ׳ ע״ב

Topic

The order of levirate acts (מאמר, גט, ביאה, חליצה) with two יבמות, and the גמרא's reasons for which acts still count afterward.

Overview

The משנה applies its rules for levirate acts done one after another to a יבם with two יבמות. It goes through cases that start with a מאמר and cases that start with a גט, and rules which ones need a גט and which still need חליצה. Then it gives the big rule: nothing counts after חליצה, wherever it comes in the series. A ביאה ends everything only when it is the first act, but רבי נחמיה says ביאה ends everything wherever it comes, just like חליצה. The גמרא first points out that everyone agrees a single גט or a single מאמר works on a יבמה. It explains that the רבנן gave them force so people would not think חליצה or ביאה is also ineffective. Next it explains that a flawed ביאה leaves room for later acts because of decrees against confusing it with forbidden cases. A flawed חליצה, though, needs no such decree, since any mix-up would cause no harm. The amud ends as רבא begins a new statement.

Opening lines

אֶחָד יְבָמָה אַחַת לְיָבָם אֶחָד,

Both one yevama to one yavam,

וְאֶחָד שְׁתֵּי יְבָמוֹת לְיָבָם אֶחָד.

and two yevamot to one yavam.

כֵּיצַד?

How so?

עָשָׂה מַאֲמָר בָּזוֹ וּמַאֲמָר בָּזוֹ —

If he did levirate betrothal with this one and levirate betrothal with that one —

The steps (18)

  1. StatementThe rules apply to one widow and to two widows
  2. StatementCases that start with a levirate betrothal

  5. StatementNo matter where the חליצה comes
  6. StatementWhere the ביאה comes in the series changes what it does

  8. StatementEveryone agrees one גט or one מאמר works
  9. QuestionWhy does a גט work for a יבמה?
  10. ResponseA גט works so people won't think חליצה doesn't work
  11. QuestionWhy does a מאמר work for a יבמה?

  13. QuestionWhy does a flawed ביאה leave room for more?
  14. ResponseTwo decrees keep a flawed ביאה from finishing things
  15. QuestionWhy does a flawed חליצה end everything?
  16. ResponseNo reason to worry about חליצה after חליצה
