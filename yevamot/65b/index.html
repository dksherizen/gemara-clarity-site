Topic

Miscarriage claims in the ten-year rule, whether a woman is commanded in פריה ורביה, and teachings on rebuke and peace

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the discussion of a couple married ten years without children. In two cases the wife is believed, because her claim gives her a worse status: she would not falsely call herself an עקרה, or falsely claim three miscarriages. Then a new משנה teaches that only the man is commanded in פריה ורביה, while רבי יוחנן בן ברוקה says both are commanded. The גמרא learns the first view from "וכבשוה", which is spelled as singular, and רב יוסף learns it from the singular "פרה ורבה" said to יעקב. Next the גמרא gathers more teachings of רבי אילעא: hold back rebuke that will not be heard, and you may change the facts for the sake of peace, as even הקדוש ברוך הוא did. Back on the משנה, רבי יוחנן and רבי יהושע בן לוי argue whether the הלכה follows רבי יוחנן בן ברוקה, and a story about רבי אמי and רבי אסי shows that רבי יוחנן ruled against him. A ruling of רבי יוחנן ordering a divorce with כתובה is pushed aside, since a woman can claim she needs children to care for her in old age. The amud ends with the story of יהודית, wife of רבי חייא, and that story continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הוּא אָמַר: אַפִּלַת בְּגוֹ עֲשַׂר.

He said: You miscarried within the ten [years].

וְהִיא אָמְרָה: לָא אַפֵּלִית.

And she said: I did not miscarry.

אָמַר רַבִּי אַמֵּי: אַף בְּזוֹ הִיא נֶאֱמֶנֶת.

Rabbi Ami said: Even in this case she is believed.

דְּאִם אִיתָא דְּאַפִּלָה —

For if it were true that she miscarried,

  1. StatementHe says she miscarried, she says she did not: she is believed
