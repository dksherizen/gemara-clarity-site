Topic

Family riddles, and whether brothers who converted or were freed together may marry each other's wives

Overview

The amud opens with riddles about strange family relationships. In the first, a woman's half brother is married to her mother, and רמי בר חמא notes this works only according to the רבנן of our משנה, not רבי יהודה. More riddles follow. Most are solved by a גוי who lived with a close relative, but the cousin riddle can happen through permitted marriages. Then a new משנה teaches that sons who converted with their mother, or were freed with her, do not do חליצה or יבום for each other's wives. In the גמרא, רב אחא בר יעקב lets freed brothers marry each other's wives, while רב ששת forbids it. The גמרא defines their dispute in two versions, then tests רב אחא's view against the משנה and its word 'אפילו', which turns out to fit either side. Finally it brings a ברייתא about twin converts, and its inference continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

״אַח מֵאַב וְלֹא מֵאֵם,

"A brother from the father and not from the mother,

וְהוּא בַּעֲלַהּ דְּאֵם,

and he is the husband of the mother,

וַאֲנָא בְּרַתַּהּ דְּאִנְתְּתֵיהּ״.

and I am the daughter of his wife."
