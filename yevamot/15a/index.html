Topic

Did בית שמאי act on their own rulings (עשו)? The גמרא tests several sources and stories, from the תקנה for צרות to רבי עקיבא's אתרוג, שמאי's סוכה, and the trough of יהוא.

Overview

The גמרא asks whether בית שמאי actually acted on their own rulings (עשו), especially their view that a צרה may do יבום. First it brings a ברייתא: the חכמים wanted to rule that all צרות do חליצה, and רבן שמעון בן גמליאל asked, 'What shall we do with the earlier צרות?' רב נחמן בר יצחק explains that this worry fits even if בית שמאי never acted. It is about צרות who married others without חליצה, following בית הלל. Next, רבי טרפון's wish to 'marry' his daughter's צרה is reread as 'marry her off,' and the story of רבן גמליאל doing יבום with his daughter's צרה is explained by the fact that she was an אילונית. Then רב משרשיא and מר זוטרא bring stories of action that looks like בית שמאי's view: רבי עקיבא's double tithe on an אתרוג, and שמאי's סוכה over a newborn. Each is answered: רבי עקיבא was unsure what בית הלל held, and onlookers would think שמאי just wanted fresh air. Finally מר זוטרא brings the trough of יהוא, which בית שמאי widened, and the גמרא's answer begins at the end of the amud and continues on the next one.
