Topic

Which sisters a כהן becomes טמא for, and whether a convert girl under three may marry a כהן

Overview

A כהן may become טמא to bury his unmarried sister, the בתולה of ויקרא 21:3. The amud opens by asking which sisters count. One view uses the word 'קרובה' to include a sister who was only betrothed and then divorced. The גמרא explains why that word does not also include a מוכת עץ. רבי יוסי holds the reverse and splits the words 'לא היתה לאיש' into two teachings. Next, רבי שמעון בן יוחאי rules that a convert girl under three years and a day may marry a כהן, proving it from the war with מדין. רב הונא and a ברייתא show that those פסוקים are about age, not actual experience. The גמרא then explains how the girls were checked: the ציץ for מדין, and a wine barrel for יבש גלעד, because the ציץ is never used to punish Jews. Finally, רבי יהושע בן לוי rules like רבי שמעון בן יוחאי, and the גמרא tests whether this ruling was heard directly or inferred. רב נחמן בר יצחק still orders a כהן who relied on it to divorce his wife. The amud ends as a new ברייתא of רבי שמעון בן יוחאי begins, continuing on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְהָא אָמַר רַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן:

But didn't Rabbi Shimon say:

רְאוּיָה לְכֹהֵן גָּדוֹל — מִטַּמֵּא לָהּ,

If she is fit for a High Priest, he becomes impure for her;

שֶׁאֵין רְאוּיָה לְכֹהֵן גָּדוֹל — אֵין מִטַּמֵּא לָהּ.

if she is not fit for a High Priest, he does not become impure for her.

שָׁאנֵי הָתָם,

It is different there,

The steps (41)

  1. ChallengeChallenge from רבי שמעון's rule about fitness for a High Priest
  2. ResolutionThe word 'near' adds the divorced betrothed sister
  3. ChallengeThen the word should also include an accidentally injured woman
  4. ResolutionThe word adds only one case, not two
  5. ChallengeWhy choose the divorced sister over the injured one?
  6. ResolutionA physical change happened to one, not the other
  7. QuestionWhere does רבי יוסי get his view on the injured woman?
  8. ResponseFrom the words 'who has not been with a man'
  9. ChallengeBut those words were already used for something else
  10. ResolutionThe phrase is split into two teachings
  11. ChallengeDoes 'to him' clash with 'complete virgin'?
  12. ResolutionThe two teachings actually come from one source
  13. StatementA convert under three may marry a כהן
  14. QuestionHow do the רבנן read the פסוק?

  16. ChallengeWhy stop at three years and a day?

  21. QuestionHow did they know which girls were old enough?
