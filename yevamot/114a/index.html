Topic

Must adults stop a קטן from doing an איסור? A series of proofs and rejections, and the meaning of 'warning adults about minors.'

Overview

The עמוד asks whether a בית דין must stop a קטן who does an איסור on his own. רבי פדת's advice to let children find lost keys on שבת suggests the answer is no. A ברייתא seems to support him, but אביי pushes it off: that case involves only rabbinic איסורים. A ברייתא about a child putting out a fire seems to say we must stop him. רבי יוחנן answers that the child there acts for his father, unlike a גוי, who acts for himself. Next comes a ברייתא about a חבר's son eating at his עם הארץ grandfather's house. רבי יוחנן pushes it off as דמאי, and the גמרא concludes that רבי יוחנן was unsure and simply pushed off each proof. Further proofs are also pushed off: from impure תרומה, answered as rabbinic תרומה, and from a baby nursing from a non-kosher source, answered as a case of danger. Along the way the גמרא explains אבא שאול's custom of nursing from an animal on יום טוב. Finally, three פסוקים that warn adults about minors are each read as forbidding only actively giving a child the איסור. The גמרא then begins to explain why all three פסוקים are needed, which continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

דְּבַר טְלֵי וְטַלְיָא

and lead boys and girls

וְלִיטַיְּילוּ הָתָם,

and let them walk around there,

דְּאִי מַשְׁכְּחִי לְהוּ,

for if they find them,

מַיְיתֵי לְהוּ.

they will bring them.
