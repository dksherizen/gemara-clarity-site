Topic

Comparing a גט with a מאמר, and the מחלוקת of רב and רבי יוחנן about two sister יבמות

Overview

The גמרא asks which is stronger: a יבמה who received a גט from the יבם, or one who received a מאמר. רב אשי proves from רבן גמליאל that each act still works after the other, so the two must be equal. Then a new topic begins: two sisters who fall to one יבם. רב rules that if either sister dies, the other becomes permitted for יבום. רבי יוחנן agrees about the first sister but says the second stays forbidden. His reason is that a woman who could not have יבום at the moment she fell is forbidden forever. The גמרא explains that רב limits this rule to a Torah איסור, while a זיקה is only דרבנן. רבי יוסי בר חנינא then challenges רבי יוחנן from a משנה about four brothers. רבי יוחנן answers that he doubts its wording, and the גמרא tests easier readings he could have given, with the last answer continuing on the next amud.

Opening lines

בַּעֲלַת הַגֵּט וּבַעֲלַת מַאֲמָר,

A woman who received a bill of divorce and a woman who received levirate betrothal,

אֵיזוֹ מֵהֶן קוֹדֶמֶת?

which of them has precedence?

בַּעֲלַת הַגֵּט עֲדִיפָא,

Is the woman who received a bill of divorce preferred,

מִשּׁוּם דְּאַתְחֵיל בַּהּ בַּחֲלִיצָה,

because he began the process of חליצה with her,
