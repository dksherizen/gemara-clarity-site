Eruvin 56b

עירובין נ״ו ע״ב

Open Eruvin 56b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Squaring a city for the תחום שבת, and the geometry of the מגרש around the ערי לויים

Overview

This amud deals with measuring areas around a city using squares and corners. First, a ברייתא teaches that when we measure the תחום שבת, we turn both the city and its 2000-אמה boundary into squares. At the corners, we add a full 2000×2000 square rather than measuring 2000 אמות on the diagonal. The ברייתא calculates that this gains 1200 אמות at each corner, and אביי says these numbers fit a round city 2000 אמות wide. The גמרא then brings a ברייתא about the ערי לויים: their area extends 2000 אמות, the first 1000 are a מגרש, and this מגרש is called 'a quarter' of the whole. רבא gives the פסוק as the source, and the גמרא asks how 1000 out of 2000 can be a quarter. Through a surveyor's calculation of areas, plus several objections, the גמרא shows that the quarter works out exactly for a round city: 9 out of 36. Finally, אביי begins a second calculation using a 1000×1000 city, which continues on the next amud.
