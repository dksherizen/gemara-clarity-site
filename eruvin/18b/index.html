Eruvin 18b

עירובין י״ח ע״ב

Learn Eruvin 18b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Eruvin 18b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The creation of חוה, rules of צניעות in walking behind a woman, and a series of aggadic sayings of רבי ירמיה בן אלעזר

Overview

This amud is a long chain of aggadic teachings. It begins by finishing a דרשה on ויבן: חוה was built like a storehouse, wide below so she can carry a child. From ויביאה אל האדם we learn that הקב"ה served as אדם's שושבין, so a great person should serve a lesser one. רב נחמן בר יצחק says that on the פרצוף view the man walked in front, and he brings a ברייתא forbidding a man to walk behind a woman. A related ברייתא warns that handing money to a woman as an excuse to gaze at her leads to the judgment of גיהנם. רב נחמן calls מנוח an עם הארץ for walking after his wife. רב נחמן בר יצחק answers that 'after her' means following her advice, as with אלקנה and אלישע, and רבי יוחנן then ranks what one should not walk behind. The rest of the amud is a series of sayings of רבי ירמיה בן אלעזר. They cover אדם fathering demons, with a challenge from רבי מאיר and its answer, partial praise to a person's face, the dove's olive leaf, תורה at night protecting a home, the two-letter Name יה after the חורבן, and the curses of בבל and שומרון. The proof about שומרון continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אוֹצָר, מָה אוֹצָר זֶה

[like] a storehouse; just as this storehouse

רָחָב מִלְּמַטָּה וְקָצָר מִלְמַעְלָה

is wide at the bottom and narrow at the top

כְּדֵי לְקַבֵּל אֶת הַפֵּירוֹת —

in order to hold the produce

אַף הָאִשָּׁה רְחָבָה מִלְּמַטָּה

so too the woman is wide at the bottom
