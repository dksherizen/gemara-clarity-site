Eruvin 19b

עירובין י״ט ע״ב

Learn Eruvin 19b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Eruvin 19b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

פסי ביראות: the allowed gaps between the posts, and אביי's questions to רבה about widening דיומדין, mounds, and reed barriers

Overview

This amud continues the laws of פסי ביראות, the boards placed around a well so people may draw water on שבת. It first finishes a ברייתא: for רבי מאיר the gap between the corner posts may be 10 אמות, and for רבי יהודה it may be 13⅓ אמות. The גמרא explains the ברייתא's wording 'like ten,' 'like thirteen,' and 'like fourteen.' רב פפא then shows which well sizes need extra single boards (פשוטין) according to each view. Next, after a סימן (memory aid), אביי asks רבה a series of questions. The first asks whether widening the corner posts (דיומדין) can replace adding single boards, and the wording 'ובלבד שירבה בפסין' shows that it can. The second asks how רבי יהודה closes a gap larger than 13⅓ אמות. The third asks whether a mound can serve as a דיומד, which leads to a dispute over whether we 'picture' (רואין) an object changed once or twice. The last asks whether a barrier of reeds can count as a דיומד. That discussion is given in two versions and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שֶׁהֵן כְּעֶשֶׂר,

so that they are approximately ten (cubits),

דִּבְרֵי רַבִּי מֵאִיר.

these are the words of Rabbi Meir.

רַבִּי יְהוּדָה אוֹמֵר:

Rabbi Yehuda says:

כִּשְׁלֹשׁ עֶשְׂרֵה אַמָּה

approximately thirteen cubits
