Topic

How many לחיים or פסין are needed to permit carrying in a מבוי or a breached חצר, and how big they must be.

Overview

This amud deals with the markers needed to permit carrying on שבת in a מבוי (alleyway) and in a חצר (courtyard) whose wall has been broken open. It opens with a story: רבי אליעזר tells his student to add a second לחי, and the student asks, 'Do I need to close it up?' The גמרא tries to prove from this that רבי אליעזר also requires a קורה, but rejects the proof. רב אשי explains רבן שמעון בן גמליאל's statement: a narrow alley needs only a לחי or a קורה. Then רב אחלי (some say רב יחיאל) says that an alley up to four טפחים wide needs nothing at all. The גמרא then discusses פסי חצר, the strips of wall left beside a breach: רבי requires two, while the רבנן allow one. Contradictions in the statements of רבי יוחנן and of שמואל are resolved the same way: a פס on one side must be four טפחים, but פסין on both sides can each be any size. Along the way we learn that the חכמים were lenient with partitions over water. The amud ends with a ברייתא about a sea inlet entering a courtyard and a question about how carrying inside that courtyard can be permitted. The answer to that question continues on the next amud.
