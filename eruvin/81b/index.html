Eruvin 81b

עירובין פ״א ע״ב

Open Eruvin 81b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Acquiring food for an עירוב with money, the four special times of the year, and the rule that the הלכה follows רבי יהודה in עירובין

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה about giving money to a grocer or baker to buy food for an עירוב. The חכמים agree that money given to any other person works, and רבי יהודה adds that an עירוב חצירות may be made even without a person's knowledge, because זכין לאדם שלא בפניו. The גמרא asks how רבי אליעזר can let money acquire without משיכה. It answers that he modeled this on the four special times of the year, when a butcher can be forced to slaughter for a customer who only paid. Two explanations of that butcher משנה follow. רבי שמואל בר יצחק says a third person acquired the meat for the buyer, which works only when it benefits him. רב אילא, in the name of רבי יוחנן, says that on those days the חכמים let the Torah rule stand, that money acquires. The גמרא then explains that 'all other people' means a בעל הבית. שמואל also limits the חכמים' ruling to a baker, to a coin, and to the words 'זכה לי'. Finally, שמואל rules that the הלכה follows רבי יהודה in עירובין, but not in laws of מחיצות. רב אחא asks why a ruling is needed if רבי יהודה only explains the חכמים, and the גמרא begins a new answer that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וּמוֹדִים בִּשְׁאָר כׇּל הָאָדָם,

And they (the Sages) agree regarding all other people,

שֶׁזָּכוּ לוֹ מְעוֹתָיו.

that his money acquires for him.

שֶׁאֵין מְעָרְבִין לָאָדָם

for one does not make an eruv for a person

אֶלָּא מִדַּעְתּוֹ.

except with his knowledge.

The steps (26)

  1. StatementThe חכמים agree that with anyone else the money works
  2. Statementרבי יהודה limits the consent rule to עירובי תחומין
  3. QuestionHow can money work when the buyer never pulled the food?
  4. Responseרבי אליעזר treated it like the four special times of the year
  5. ProofThe משנה about forcing the butcher to slaughter
  6. ProofThe משנה continues: if the ox dies, the buyer loses
  7. ChallengeWhy should the buyer lose if he never pulled the ox?
  8. Resolutionרב הונא: the buyer did pull the ox
  9. ChallengeThe משנה's second half disproves רב הונא
  10. Resolutionרבי שמואל בר יצחק: someone else acquired it for the buyer
  11. ResolutionA benefit can be given without the buyer present, a burden cannot
  12. Resolutionרב אילא: At the four times the חכמים followed Torah law
  13. StatementBy Torah law money acquires; pulling is a rabbinic decree
  14. QuestionWho are 'all other people' in the משנה?
  15. Responseרב: It means a homeowner
  16. Proofשמואל agrees: only a baker is excluded
  17. Statementשמואל: The rule is only about a coin, not a utensil
  18. Statementשמואל's third limit: 'acquire for me' versus 'make an עירוב for me'
  19. StatementThe הלכה follows רבי יהודה in all matters of עירובין
  20. QuestionDoes the rule include an alleyway whose beam or side post was removed?
  21. ResponseThe rule covers עירובין, not מחיצות
  22. ChallengeWhy say 'the הלכה follows' if there is no dispute?
  23. ChallengeDo they really not argue? A משנה requires informing
  24. ResolutionThat משנה is about a courtyard between two alleyways
  25. Challengeרב חסדא says his colleagues do disagree
  26. ResolutionDropping the failed answer and preparing a new one
