Topic

A קורה that is distanced from the walls (לבוד and חבוט), and whether carrying is permitted in the space between the לחיים and under the קורה

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a ברייתא about a קורה that is drawn away from the walls of a מבוי or suspended between them. If the gap is under three טפחים, the קורה is still valid because of לבוד. According to רבן שמעון בן גמליאל, the gap may be anything under four. The גמרא first tries to read the ברייתא against רבא, but answers that both cases are inside the מבוי: one has a gap on one side and the other has gaps on both sides. רב אשי offers another reading: one beam on bent pegs, joined by both לבוד and חבוט together. Then רבי זכאי teaches that the space between the לחיים and under the קורה is a כרמלית, and רבי יוחנן rejects this. אביי says carrying is still forbidden between the לחיים, while רבא permits it there too. Each side brings proofs, and רבא deflects the proofs from רב and from רבי יוחנן by saying they deal with an alley that opens into a כרמלית. The entrance space merges with a כרמלית because it is similar to it (מצא מין את מינו), but it does not merge with רשות הרבים.

מְשׁוּכָה אוֹ תְּלוּיָה,

Drawn away or suspended,

פָּחוֹת מִשְּׁלֹשָׁה —

less than three handbreadths away —

אֵין צָרִיךְ לְהָבִיא קוֹרָה אַחֶרֶת.

he does not need to bring another beam.

שְׁלֹשָׁה — צָרִיךְ לְהָבִיא קוֹרָה אַחֶרֶת.

Three handbreadths — he must bring another beam.
