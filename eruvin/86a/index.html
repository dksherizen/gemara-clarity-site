Eruvin 86a

עירובין פ״ו ע״א

Open Eruvin 86a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Honoring the wealthy, the landlord's hold through stored items, a resident away for שבת, and the partition in a shared cistern

Overview

This amud opens at the end of a story. רבי gives honor to visitors according to their wealth. רבי ישמעאל ברבי יוסי protests that בן בוניס, whose father owns a thousand ships and a thousand towns, is the richer one. The גמרא explains that רבי and רבי עקיבא honored the wealthy, based on רבא בר מרי's reading of a פסוק in תהלים. Back in הלכה, a landlord who stores a מוקצה item, like a plough pin, in his tenant's space keeps a real hold there, so the tenant does not forbid the courtyard. A new משנה asks whether someone who leaves home for שבת still forbids the courtyard: רבי מאיר, רבי יהודה, רבי יוסי and רבי שמעון give four views. רב rules like רבי שמעון, but only when the man went to his daughter's house, not his son's. A second משנה deals with a cistern shared by two courtyards, which needs a ten-טפחים partition. The גמרא then debates what 'below' and 'above' mean, and a new challenge from a beam in a ruin begins that finishes on the next amud.

Opening lines

פַּנּוּ מָקוֹם

Make room

לְבֶן מָאתַיִם מָנֶה.

for one worth two hundred maneh.

אָמַר לְפָנָיו רַבִּי יִשְׁמָעֵאל בְּרַבִּי יוֹסֵי:

Rabbi Yishmael son of Rabbi Yosei said before him:

רַבִּי, אָבִיו שֶׁל זֶה

Rebbe, the father of this one

The steps (24)

  1. Statementרבי calls for honor for a very wealthy visitor
  2. Statementרבי ישמעאל describes the father's enormous wealth
  3. Statementרבי says the boy should dress to match his wealth
  4. ConclusionWhy רבי and רבי עקיבא honored the wealthy
  5. StatementA plough pin is the example of the landlord's real hold
  6. ProofA ברייתא draws the line at whether the item may be moved
  7. ProofA ברייתא supports the rule about non-movable items
  8. StatementNew משנה: leaving home for שבת, and רבי מאיר says he still forbids
  9. Statementרבי יהודה says the absent resident does not forbid
  10. Statementרבי יוסי splits it: a נכרי forbids, a Jew does not
  11. Statementרבי שמעון: even in the same town, a Jew who left does not forbid
  12. Statementרב rules like רבי שמעון
  13. StatementOnly a daughter's house, not a son's
  14. ProofA folk saying about the dog
  15. StatementA cistern between two courtyards
  16. Statementבית שמאי and בית הלל on where the partition goes
  17. Statementרבי יהודה: the wall above is enough
  18. Statementרב הונא explains 'below' and 'above'
  19. Statementרב יהודה: measured from the water
  20. ChallengeWon't the water mix even with a partition below the water?
  21. ResolutionThe reeds must stick out a טפח above the water
  22. ChallengeWon't the water mix even with a partition above the water?
  23. ResolutionThe reeds must dip a טפח into the water
  24. ChallengeOpening a third challenge from the beam in a ruin
