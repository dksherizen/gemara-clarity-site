Eruvin 28b

עירובין כ״ח ע״ב

Learn Eruvin 28b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Eruvin 28b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Foods that may be used for an עירוב: arugula seeds, כשות and חזיז and their ברכות, unripe dates and קור (heart of palm), and the measures for several vegetable foods.

Overview

This amud finishes an explanation from the previous amud: the herbs fit for an עירוב are the garden kind. It then explains that arugula seeds count as food because earlier generations ground them as a spice for roast meat. A story follows: a schoolchild tells רבי זירא that כשות gets בורא פרי האדמה and חזיז gets שהכל. The גמרא rules like the child, because ripeness decides the ברכה, and even כשות really feeds from the ground. Next the גמרא challenges רב's ruling that unripe dates (כפניות) cannot be used for an עירוב, using a ברייתא that treats them as food. The final answer is that unripe dates count as food for טומאה only because cooking can sweeten them, a principle of רבי יוחנן about bitter almonds, while an עירוב needs food that is ready to eat. The גמרא then explains how רבי יהודה differs from the תנא קמא about קור (heart of palm): the difference is its ברכה, and the halacha follows שמואל that it is שהכל. Finally, the גמרא gives the measures for כשות, חזיז, the green קליא plant, and fresh beans. It ends with a challenge to raw beets, which continues on the next amud.

בִּדְגִנּוּנְיָיתָא.
