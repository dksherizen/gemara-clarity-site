Topic

The sources for the maximum height and width of a מבוי entrance: the משכן's courtyard gate, the doorway of the אולם, royal gateways, and the doorway of the היכל

Overview

The משנה limits a מבוי entrance to 20 אמות high and 10 אמות wide, and the גמרא is searching for the source of these numbers in the doorways of the מקדש and the משכן. It first rejects one proof that the משכן is called a מקדש and replaces it with the פסוק 'ועשו לי מקדש.' Next it asks why we don't learn from the courtyard gate of the משכן, which seems to be 20 wide and 5 high. It gives two answers: the gate is not called a plain 'doorway,' or the 15 אמות of hangings measure height, not width. The גמרא then challenges רב's claim that רבי יהודה learns from the doorway of the אולם. רבי יהודה's width dispute is found in a ברייתא, but his allowing 50 אמות goes past the אולם's 40. רב חסדא concludes that a ברייתא misled רב, and that רבי יהודה really learns from royal gateways. The גמרא then tests the רבנן's source, the doorway of the היכל. It asks about the היכל's doors (they were only for privacy) and about the צורת הפתח (רב corrected the משנה to read 'he must narrow it'). A new challenge opens at the end and continues on the next amud.

That verse is written about the Ark.

"And let them make Me a Sanctuary, that I may dwell among them."
