Topic

The laws of פסין around a well: courtyards that open into them, water that dries up or returns on שבת, and the strength of partitions even when crowds pass through.

Overview

This amud opens by finishing a rejection of a proof from a ברייתא: a fence listed twice means two types of fence, just as a tree listed twice means two types of tree. אביי then asks רבה about a חצר that opens into the area between the פסין around a well. רבה rules that one חצר may carry there but two courtyards may not. רב הונא forbids two courtyards even with an עירוב, as a גזירה, while רבא permits them with an עירוב. A ברייתא supports רבא, and the גמרא defends רב הונא by saying the ברייתא is about courtyards that physically became one. Next, רבה rules that if the well dries up on שבת the פסין stop working. אביי adds that if the water returns on שבת, they work again, because a partition made on שבת still counts; only if someone built it on purpose is carrying inside it forbidden. רבי אלעזר rules that throwing into פסי ביראות makes one חייב, which teaches that a partition still counts even when crowds walk through it. The amud ends by starting a new section on the משנה's words 'מותר להקריב לבאר', comparing the rule that a person drinking across רשויות must bring his head and most of his body inside.

תְּרֵי גַוְונֵי אִילָן?

[Don't you say it means] two types of tree?

הָכָא נָמֵי, תְּרֵי גַוְונֵי גָּדֵר.

Here too, [it means] two types of fence.

בְּעָא מִינֵּיהּ אַבָּיֵי מֵרַבָּה:

A courtyard whose [open] end enters between the boards —
