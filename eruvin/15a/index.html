Topic

לחי העומד מאליו: the dispute of אביי and רבא, whether it extends to a מחיצה, and whether a לחי is a מחיצה or a היכר

Overview

The גמרא brings a dispute between אביי and רבא about a לחי העומד מאליו, a post at the entrance of a מבוי that was not put there to be a לחי. At first it says they argue only when people relied on the post from before שבת. אביי accepts it, and רבא rejects it because it was not made for this purpose. The גמרא then assumes they also argue about a מחיצה that was not made to be a wall. It tests this against a סוכה among trees, trees and reed hedges serving as a דיומד, and a low-hanging tree, and it deflects each proof by saying the items were made on purpose. A ברייתא about a natural mound and cavity cannot be deflected this way. So the גמרא reframes the dispute: everyone agrees natural walls count. אביי holds that a לחי works as a מחיצה, while רבא holds that it is a היכר, which must be made by hand. Two ברייתות about wall stones and a recessed wall are deflected, but a story about רב and a palm tree seems to support אביי. The balcony of בר חבו shows they argue even when people relied on the item. A new משנה then begins: a לחי may be made from anything, even a living creature, though רבי מאיר forbids it.

Opening lines

אִיתְּמַר:

It was stated:

לֶחִי הָעוֹמֵד מֵאֵלָיו,

A side post that stands by itself,

אַבָּיֵי אָמַר: הָוֵי לֶחִי,

Abaye said: it is a valid side post,

רָבָא אָמַר: לָא הָוֵי לֶחִי.

Rava said: it is not a valid side post.
