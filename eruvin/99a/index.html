Eruvin 99a

עירובין צ״ט ע״א

Open Eruvin 99a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Carrying and drinking across domains on שבת: רבא's 'over the head' rule, liability for spitting and urinating, רבי יהודה on spittle, and whether the drinking rule applies to a כרמלית.

Overview

The amud opens by rejecting רב חיננא's view that the whole משנה follows רבי מאיר: the סיפא is רבי מאיר, but the רישא is רבנן. Next, the גמרא asks whether the משנה's words 'ובלבד שלא יוציא חוץ' support or refute רבא's rule about carrying four אמות over one's head. In both versions, the משנה turns out to be ambiguous, so it proves nothing either way. רב יוסף then rules that urinating or spitting from a רשות היחיד into a רשות הרבים is חייב חטאת, because a person's intent makes even a small spot count as a significant place (מחשבתו משויא ליה מקום). רבא asks whether we follow where the urine is uprooted or where it exits, and the question ends in תיקו. On spitting, a contradiction in רבי יהודה's view is resolved by רבי יוחנן's switch of names in the dried-fig משנה, after ריש לקיש's 'phlegm' answer is refuted. A side teaching warns against spitting phlegm in front of one's רבו. Finally, a new משנה forbids drinking across domains unless one's head and most of his body are inside. רב יוסף explains that even the רבנן agree when the person needs the object. אביי and רבא then dispute whether this applies to a כרמלית, and אביי begins a proof that continues on the next amud.

The steps (33)

  1. ResolutionWhy the earlier אמורא thought the whole משנה follows רבי מאיר
  2. RejectionRejecting that assumption: the two clauses have different authors
  3. StatementQuoting the משנה's limit and inferring liability
  4. ProofProposed support for רבא's rule about carrying over one's head
  5. RejectionRejecting the support: maybe it is only forbidden
  6. StatementSecond version: the inference is exempt but forbidden
  7. ChallengeChallenge: this would refute רבא
  8. RejectionRejecting the challenge: maybe it means liable
  9. Statementרב יוסף: urinating or spitting into the public domain makes one liable
  10. ChallengeBut there is no four-by-four place for lifting
  11. ResolutionHis intent makes it a significant place
  12. ProofProof from רבא: an object thrown into a dog's mouth or a furnace
  13. ConclusionIntent makes a place, both for רבא's case and for spitting
  14. Questionרבא asks: is liability decided by where the urine starts or where it leaves the body?
  15. ConclusionThe question remains unresolved
  16. QuestionDoes רבי יהודה forbid it even if the spittle was not turned over?
  17. ChallengeA משנה shows רבי יהודה holds unturned spittle is not detached
  18. Resolutionרבי יוחנן: The opinions are switched
  19. Resolutionריש לקיש: No switch, our משנה is about phlegm
  20. ChallengeA ברייתא seems to say detached spittle
  21. RejectionNo, it really means phlegm
  22. ChallengeA second ברייתא explicitly mentions spittle
  23. ConclusionBack to the original answer: the opinions are switched
  24. StatementCoughing up phlegm in front of one's teacher
  25. ChallengeBut coughing is involuntary
  26. ResolutionThe case is someone who had phlegm and chose to spit
  27. StatementNew משנה: drinking from one domain into another
  28. ChallengeThe previous משנה follows the רבנן, but this one follows רבי מאיר
  29. Resolutionרב יוסף: objects one needs, and everyone agrees
  30. QuestionWhat is the law if one area is a כרמלית?
  31. Responseאביי: A כרמלית follows the same rule
