Topic

Who removes impurity from the מקדש and who may enter the היכל for repairs; the cases behind רבי שמעון's principle; the end of מסכת עירובין.

Overview

רבי יוחנן shows that רבי שמעון בן ננס and רבי עקיבא learn their dispute about removing a שרץ from the מקדש from the same פסוק. In that פסוק, כהנים carried impurity out of the היכל and handed it to לויים in the courtyard. רבי שמעון בן ננס reads the handoff as proof that the עזרה needs no removal. רבי עקיבא reads it as a way to spare the כהנים from extra impurity. A ברייתא then teaches that anyone may enter the היכל to build, repair, or remove impurity: כהנים first, then לויים, then ישראלים, but only if they are טהור. רב כהנא's ברייתא disagrees and sends in even a טמא or blemished כהן before any ישראל. The גמרא asks whether a טמא or a בעל מום should go first. רב and רבי אלעזר give opposite answers, and the question is not decided. Next, the גמרא shows which cases רבי שמעון's principle in the משנה refers to: the person just outside the תחום, and the broken harp string, where only a bow (a שבות) is allowed and not a knot. The amud ends with the הדרן, completing מסכת עירובין.
