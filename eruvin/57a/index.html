Eruvin 57a

עירובין נ״ז ע״א

Open Eruvin 57a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The מגרש as a 'quarter' of the תחום, and the משנה's dispute about adding a קרפף to a city

Overview

The amud opens by checking the ברייתא's claim that the מגרש around a Levite city is one quarter (רביע) of the תחום. The plain arithmetic gives 8 out of 24, which is a third, so the גמרא offers three ways to reach a quarter: the city is round, רבינא says the מגרש is only along the sides, and רב אשי says it is only at the corners. רבינא objects that the פסוק says סביב, and רב אשי answers from the עולה, where 'around' means the corners. Next, רב חביבי and רב חנילאי from מחוזא challenge the round-city calculation, about jutting corners and about the 800 figure. רב אשי answers that the squaring is only imagined, and that the diagonal adds more than a quarter. A new משנה then brings a dispute about the קרפף: רבי מאיר adds one to every city, while the חכמים use it only to join nearby cities, including three villages in a triangle. In the גמרא, רבא finds the source in 'מקיר העיר וחוצה', and רב הונא and חייא בר רב disagree whether two cities get two strips or one. The סיפא of the משנה supports רב הונא, and חייא בר רב's defense continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דַּל אַרְבַּע דִּתְחוּמִין

Take out four of the side strips

וְאַרְבַּע דִּקְרָנוֹת.

and four of the corners.

כַּמָּה הָוֵי? תְּמָנְיָא.

How much is that? Eight.

תִּילְתָּא הָווּ!

It is a third!

The steps (25)

  2. ChallengeChallenge: that is a third, not a quarter
  3. ResolutionAnswer: the city is round, so the open space shrinks to a quarter

  5. Resolutionרב אשי: a quarter, with open space only at the corners
  6. Challengeרבינא objects: the verse says around

  8. Challengeרב חביבי asks about the jutting corners

  11. ResolutionWe only imagine it squared, we do not really square it

  20. Statementחייא בר רב: one shared strip
