Eruvin 90a

עירובין צ׳ ע״א

Open Eruvin 90a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Carrying on roofs on שבת: combining a roof with an אכסדרה or a ruin, and the dispute of רב and שמואל about a single roof and a ship

Overview

This amud begins with the end of an answer from the previous amud: a certain structure was built to guard a garden, not to reach the roofs. רמי בר חמא then asks about carrying two אמות on a roof and two אמות on a pillar. רבה objects that this is obvious, since a roof is a כרמלית and the pillar is a רשות היחיד. So the גמרא explains that the real question was about a roof and an אכסדרה, which is not fit to live in. רב ביבי בר אביי asks a similar question about a roof and a ruin. He explains that a ruin, unlike an אכסדרה, is fit to live in, even though no one lives there now. That question ends with תיקו, unresolved. Next, רב and שמואל disagree about carrying on a single roof: רב allows the whole roof, and שמואל allows only four אמות. The גמרא settles each one's apparent self-contradiction, using noticeable walls for רב and the size of the roof (more than two בית סאה, like a קרפף not enclosed for living) for שמואל. Finally, the same two argue about carrying on a ship, and the explanation of רב's view continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לְנַטּוֹרֵי תַּרְבִּיצָא

to guard the garden

הוּא דַּעֲבִידָא.

is what it was made for.

בְּעַי רָמֵי בַּר חָמָא:

Rami bar Chama asked:

שְׁתֵּי אַמּוֹת בְּגַג

two cubits on a roof
