Eruvin 67b

עירובין ס״ז ע״ב

Learn Eruvin 67b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Eruvin 67b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

A קרפף next to a courtyard and the מבוי, רבי יוחנן's view of a large קרפף's Torah-level status, and רבה's ruling about bringing water for a מילה on שבת

Overview

This amud opens by finishing a rule about a גוי whose courtyard has an opening outside the alley (מבוי). רבה and רב יוסף add a size rule for an opening into a קרפף. A גוי makes the alley אסור if his קרפף is בית סאתים or less, because that is too small for his needs. A ישראל is the reverse: he makes the alley אסור only if his קרפף is larger than בית סאתים, because he may not carry there and will use the alley. Next, רבי יוחנן rules that a large קרפף not fenced for living is still a רשות היחיד by Torah law. רב הונא בר חיננא challenges this from a ברייתא about a rock in the sea, and רבא and רב אשי each defend רבי יוחנן. רב אשי explains that the רבנן balanced two of their own rules and protected the more common case. The amud ends with a story: רבה allows asking a גוי to bring warm water for a baby's מילה. Afterward, אביי begins an objection from a ברייתא that calls both הזאה and אמירה לגוי a שבות, and his objection continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֲפִילּוּ פָּתוּחַ לְקַרְפֵּף.

Even if it opens into an enclosure.

רַבָּה וְרַב יוֹסֵף דְּאָמְרִי תַּרְוַיְיהוּ:

It is Rabba and Rav Yosef who both say:

גּוֹי בֵּית סָאתַיִם — אוֹסֵר,

A gentile with an enclosure of two beit se'a renders it prohibited,

יוֹתֵר מִבֵּית סָאתַיִם — אֵינוֹ אוֹסֵר.

More than two beit se'a: he does not render it prohibited.

The steps (26)

  1. StatementThe rule extends even to an opening into an enclosure
  2. StatementFor a gentile, the enclosure's size decides
  3. StatementFor a Jew, the result is reversed
  4. Questionרבא בר חקלאי asks about an opening into a קרפף
  5. Responseרב הונא answers that it depends on the size of the קרפף
  6. Statementרבי יוחנן rules that a large unfenced-for-living קרפף is a full private domain by Torah law
  7. Challengeרב הונא בר חיננא challenges from the ברייתא of a rock in the sea
  8. QuestionWhich clause does 'up to בית סאתים' refer to?
  9. RejectionThe limit cannot refer to the last clause
  10. ChallengeThe limit must refer to the tall rock, which seems to refute רבי יוחנן
  11. Resolutionרבא: the limit is about carrying on top of the rock
  12. Resolutionרב אשי: the limit does refer to rock and sea, but both rules are rabbinic
  13. ResolutionThe two rabbinic rules behind רב אשי's answer
  14. ResolutionA rock up to בית סאתים: no carrying to or from the sea
  15. ResolutionA rock bigger than בית סאתים: carrying to the sea allowed
  16. QuestionWhy favor one rabbinic rule over the other?
  17. ResponseCarrying on the rock is common; carrying to the sea is rare
  18. StatementA baby's warm water spills and רבה offers water from his house
  19. Challengeאביי objects that no עירוב was made
  20. Resolutionרבה suggests relying on the merging of alleyways
  21. Challengeאביי: No merging was made either
  22. Resolutionרבה: Tell a גוי to bring the water
  23. Statementאביי wanted to object but רב יוסף stopped him
  24. ProofRabbinic matters: act first, object later
  25. Questionרב יוסף asks what the objection was
  26. Responseאביי cites a ברייתא: הזאה and אמירה לגוי are both rabbinic
