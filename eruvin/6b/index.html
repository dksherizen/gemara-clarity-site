Eruvin 6b

עירובין ו׳ ע״ב

Topic

Fixing alleys open at both ends: whether a door is needed and must be locked, and whether one may combine the stringencies of רב and שמואל

Overview

The גמרא asks how a ברייתא could speak of making an עירוב for a רשות הרבים. The חכמים told רבי יהודה that a public street cannot be fixed with a לחי or קורה, and teachings about ירושלים and מחוזא show that only actually locked doors help. So רב יהודה re-reads the ברייתא as being about alleys open at both ends (מבואות המפולשין): a צורת הפתח on one end and a לחי or קורה on the other. רב rules like the תנא קמא, while שמואל rules like חנניה, who requires a door on one end. The גמרא asks whether that door must be locked. שמואל rules it need not be, and the story of רב נחמן clearing the gates of נהרדעא shows the door only has to be able to close. Next, an L-shaped alleyway in נהרדעא was required to have doors by combining רב's stringency (the L-shape counts as open) with שמואל's (an open alley needs doors). The גמרא challenges this with a ברייתא that condemns taking the stringencies of both בית שמאי and בית הלל. It first resolves a contradiction inside that ברייתא's wording, and the discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מִי שֶׁהָיוּ לוֹ שְׁנֵי בָתִּים

One who had two houses

מִשְּׁנֵי צִידֵּי רְשׁוּת הָרַבִּים,

on the two sides of the public domain,

עוֹשֶׂה לֶחִי מִכָּאן וְלֶחִי מִכָּאן,

places a side post here and a side post there,

אוֹ קוֹרָה מִכָּאן וְקוֹרָה מִכָּאן,

or a crossbeam here and a crossbeam there,

The steps (24)

  1. Statementרבי יהודה's case of two houses across a public street
  2. ChallengeThe חכמים reply: a public domain cannot be fixed this way
  3. ChallengeEven doors do not help unless they are locked: the case of ירושלים
  4. Proofעולא adds: the city gates of מחוזא
  5. Resolutionרב יהודה re-reads the ברייתא as being about open-ended alleys
  6. Statementרב and שמואל disagree on whose view is the הלכה
  7. QuestionMust the door actually be locked according to חנניה?
  8. Responseשמואל rules that the door need not be locked
  9. QuestionThe same question is put to רב ענן
  10. ProofProof from the half-buried gates of נהרדעא
  11. Rejectionרב כהנא: those gates were blocked, so they prove nothing
  12. Statementרב נחמן orders the earth cleared from the gates
  13. QuestionDoes רב נחמן hold the door must be locked?
  14. RejectionThe doors only need to be able to lock
  15. StatementAn L-shaped alleyway in נהרדעא needed doors
  16. Statementרב's stringency: the L-shape counts as open
  17. ChallengeBut רב rules leniently on open alleyways
  18. ResolutionThe doors come from שמואל, who follows חנניה
  19. ChallengeBut שמואל treats the L-shape as closed
  20. ResolutionCombining רב on the L-shape with שמואל on doors
  21. ChallengeMay one combine the stringencies of two opposing views?
  22. ChallengeThe ברייתא seems to contradict itself
  23. ResolutionOne line is from before the בת קול and one from after
  24. ResolutionA second answer: both lines are from after the בת קול
