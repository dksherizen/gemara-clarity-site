Eruvin 70a

עירובין ע׳ ע״א

Topic

ביטול רשות in a shared חצר: who may give away courtyard rights to whom, and whether one ביטול must be made to each resident

Overview

This amud continues the גמרא's discussion of ביטול רשות, where a resident who did not join the עירוב gives up his rights in a shared חצר so carrying becomes permitted on שבת. First the גמרא finishes explaining why the משנה needed to say that two may give rights to one, and why it needed to say that rights may not be received, even through 'קני על מנת להקנות.' Then אביי asks רבה whether a forgetful resident among five must give up his rights to each of the others separately, and רבה says yes. אביי challenges him from a ברייתא that lets a non-joiner give his rights 'to one who joined.' רבה answers that the second joiner died, and the two sides argue over how to read the ברייתא's clauses. The question against אביי's reading of 'to two' ends as a קשיא. The גמרא then goes through every clause of the ברייתא and explains why each one is needed according to both אביי and רבה. Finally, רבא asks רב נחמן whether an heir may do ביטול רשות, and the discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לִיגְזַר דִּילְמָא אָתֵי לְבַטּוֹלֵי לְהוּ.

Let us decree, lest he come to renounce to them (to two).

קָא מַשְׁמַע לַן.

It teaches us [that we do not decree].

וְאֵין נוֹטְלִין רְשׁוּת.

And they may not receive rights.

לְמָה לִי?

Why do I need this?
