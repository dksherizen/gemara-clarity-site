Topic

What רב חסדא asked about נחמיה, and whether a wall of people may be set up on שבת (the third versus fourth סוכה wall)

Overview

Earlier, נחמיה was so absorbed in learning that he walked past his תחום שבת, and רב נחמן ruled: 'Make a wall of people for him and let him come back in.' The גמרא first asks what רב חסדא was unsure about. It proves he was not asking about רבן גמליאל's rule, because רב already ruled on that, so he must have been asking about רבי אליעזר's rule that someone two אמות outside may come back in. Next, רב נחמן בר יצחק challenges רבא with a ברייתא that forbids standing a person in place of a fallen סוכה wall on שבת or יום טוב. רבא answers with another ברייתא that allows using a friend as a wall, so the two ברייתות contradict each other. The גמרא first says they follow רבי אליעזר and the חכמים, but רבי יוחנן says everyone forbids starting a temporary tent. It then tries רבי מאיר and רבי יהודה, but that fails as well, because רבי מאיר spoke only about an animal and could not permit a new wall whichever view of temporary tents he follows. The final answer is that both ברייתות follow the רבנן: making the third wall of a סוכה is forbidden, and making the extra fourth wall is permitted. The wording 'its wall fell' proves this.

אוֹ אֵין הֲלָכָה כְּרַבָּן גַּמְלִיאֵל.
