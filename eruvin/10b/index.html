Topic

Narrowing a wide מבוי, the rules of עומד and פרוץ (including the רב דימי–רבין dispute), and צורת הפתח

Overview

The גמרא explains how to narrow a מבוי whose entrance is wider than ten אמות. One way is to stand a board lengthwise down the alley. Another way follows רב יהודה: put a three-אמה board two אמות from the wall, which leaves an opening of exactly ten. The גמרא asks why other board arrangements were not suggested and tries to learn rules about עומד מרובה על הפרוץ and עומד כפרוץ. Each proof is rejected: a small board with open air on both sides is cancelled, and some setups are simply more work than the רבנן required. Next the גמרא worries that people will use the small gap instead of the main entrance. רב אדא בר מתנה answers that people do not leave a big entrance for a small one unless it saves them walking. The גמרא then brings a טומאה source about a leather seat and its hole, reported differently by רב דימי and רבין. אביי and רב דימי debate whether they truly disagree, and the dispute is narrowed to פרוץ כעומד. Finally the גמרא returns to the משנה: a צורת הפתח fixes a too-wide entrance, and the discussion of width and height continues on the next amud.
