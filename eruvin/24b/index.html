Eruvin 24b

Topic

Water in a קרפף, and how to permit carrying in the yard in פום נהרא using a לחי

Overview

Earlier, the גמרא ruled that water inside a קרפף is treated like planted trees, so it does not ruin the area's status as enclosed for living. אמימר limits this to water that is fit for use. רב אשי adds a limit for deep water covering more than בית סאתים, but the גמרא rejects his limit by comparing the water to a pile of fruit, which never ruins the קרפף however big it is. Then the גמרא tells of a large yard in פום נהרא that opened on one side to the town and on the other to a vineyard path leading to a riverbank. אביי looks for a way to permit carrying there. A wall on the riverbank fails because a מחיצה cannot be built on top of a מחיצה, and a doorframe fails because camels would knock it down. So אביי suggests a לחי at the vineyard path's opening. רבא objects that people would wrongly think a לחי works for any vineyard path, so he places the לחי at the yard's opening to the town instead. Carrying within the town and within the yard is then permitted. Carrying between them is disputed by רב אחא and רבינא.

Opening lines

אָמַר אַמֵּימָר:

Ameimar said:

וְהוּא דְּחַזְיִין לְתַשְׁמִישְׁתָּא,

This is only when [the water] is fit for use,

אֲבָל לָא חַזְיִין לְתַשְׁמִישְׁתָּא — לָא.

but if it is not fit for use — no.

אָמַר רַב אָשֵׁי:

Rav Ashi said:

The steps (12)

  1. Statementאמימר limits the water rule to usable water
  2. Statementרב אשי adds a size limit for deep water
  3. RejectionThe גמרא rejects רב אשי's limit using a pile of fruit
  4. StatementA new case: the yard in פום נהרא
  5. Questionאביי asks how to fix the yard and suggests a wall at the river
  6. RejectionA wall at the river does not help
  7. QuestionSecond idea: a doorframe at the path's entrance
  8. RejectionCamels would knock the doorframe down
  9. Responseאביי's solution: one side post at the path's opening
  10. Challengeרבא objects: people will misunderstand
  11. Responseרבא's solution: a side post at the opening of the town
  12. ConclusionPractical summary: carrying within each area, and the dispute about carrying between them
