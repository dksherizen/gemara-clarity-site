Eruvin 68a

עירובין ס״ח ע״א

Learn Eruvin 68a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Eruvin 68a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Using a non-Jew and ביטול רשות to bring water for a מילה on שבת, and the שיתוף in רבה and אביי's alleyway, including בְּרֵירָה with stored food

Overview

This amud opens by finishing an objection to רבה, who let a non-Jew carry hot water for a baby's מילה on שבת. Telling a non-Jew is only a שבות, and so is הזאה, which does not override שבת. The answer is that a שבות involving an action is stricter, and רבה only had the non-Jew carry the water, not heat it. רבה בר רב חנן then asks אביי why their alleyway has no עירוב or שיתוף. אביי explains why no one arranges it, and why he cannot make the שיתוף with his own bread or with an unmarked רביעית of vinegar in a barrel. Two ברייתות disagree about using stored food for a שיתוף. רב אושעיא explains that they follow בית שמאי and בית הלל, who argue about בְּרֵירָה, as a משנה about a corpse in a house with many doorways shows. The amud then tells two stories of a baby whose warm water for the מילה spilled. In the first, רבא allows a גוי to heat water for the mother. In the second, רבא gives up his rights in his courtyard, relying on רבי יוחנן against שמואל. רבינא's follow-up question begins at the end of the amud and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מָה הַזָּאָה שְׁבוּת,

Just as sprinkling is a rabbinic prohibition,

וְאֵינָהּ דּוֹחָה אֶת הַשַּׁבָּת.

and it does not override Shabbat,

אַף אֲמִירָה לְגוֹי שְׁבוּת,

so too telling a non-Jew is a rabbinic prohibition,

וְאֵינָהּ דּוֹחָה אֶת הַשַּׁבָּת.

and it does not override Shabbat.
