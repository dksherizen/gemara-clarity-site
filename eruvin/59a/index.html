Eruvin 59a

עירובין נ״ט ע״א

Open Eruvin 59a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Trusting surveyors of the תחום שבת, and making an ערוב for a city that changed between private and public

Overview

The גמרא first finishes the משנה about measuring the תחום שבת. It explains that a surveyor is trusted even when he extends the limit, and that when two surveyors disagree, we follow the one who extended. אביי limits this: the extra amount may not exceed the difference made by measuring along the diagonal. רבינא resolves a contradiction about whether חכמים were lenient or strict here. The system of תחומין was made as a stringency, but because it is only דרבנן, its measuring details may be lenient. A new משנה then turns to עירובי חצירות. A private city that became public may have one ערוב for all of it, but a public city that became private must leave a section outside the ערוב, and רבי יהודה and רבי שמעון argue about that section's size. The גמרא asks for an example of a private city that became public. רב יהודה names the ריש גלותא's village, while רב נחמן challenges that example and names the village of נתזואי instead. A ברייתא explains how to include a real רשות הרבים in such a city and forbids an ערוב for half the city. It then begins the case of a city that was always public, whose ruling comes on the next amud.
