Topic

Shrinking an oversized קרפף, partitions on top of partitions (for שבת and for acquiring a גר's property), and whether a roofed area counts toward a קרפף's size

Overview

This amud opens by finishing an earlier מחלוקת about carrying where there are no דיורין (residents). The גמרא then asks how to shrink a קרפף larger than בית סאתים so that carrying becomes permitted. Trees do not count. A column 4 טפחים wide, or a new partition set 4 טפחים back from the wall, does count. For the 3-to-4 טפחים range, רבה says it works because it is beyond לבוד, and רבא says it does not because it is not a מקום ארבעה. רב שימי reports a version where they argue only about plaster that cannot stand on its own. Next comes a partition built at a mound's edge, which is a 'partition on top of a partition.' The גמרא proves that רב חסדא holds it works for שבת, though it does not acquire a גר's property, while רב ששת says it does not work even for שבת. רבה בר בר חנה asks what happens if the lower walls sink. The גמרא argues the answer is already known both for acquiring property (the turnip case) and for שבת (a ברייתא about partitions made on שבת, with רב נחמן's limit applying only to מזיד). A story of רב נחמן ruling against a woman's partition on a partition follows. Finally, רבא and רבי זירא argue whether a roofed section adds to a קרפף's size, and the גמרא suggests linking this to רב and שמואל's dispute over פי תקרה יורד וסותם.

Opening lines

מַאן דְּשָׁרֵי — דְּהָא לֵיכָּא דָּיוֹרִין.

The one who permits — because there are no residents.

וּמַאן דְּאָסַר —

And the one who forbids —

זִימְנִין דְּהָוֵי בַּהּ דָּיוֹרִין

sometimes there are residents in it

וְאָתֵי לְטַלְטוֹלֵי.

and they will come to carry.
