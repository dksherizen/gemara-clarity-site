Topic

Fixing a broken harp string in the מקדש on שבת, and when a wart may be cut off for the מקדש service on שבת.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the discussion of a לוי's harp string that tore on שבת. The משנה allows tying it with a knot, but a ברייתא allows only a bow. The גמרא gives three answers: it is a מחלוקת between רבי שמעון and the רבנן; or both follow the רבנן and it depends on whether the string tore in the middle or at the side; or both speak of a tear in the middle and they argue about making a גזירה. Then a new משנה teaches that a wart may be cut off by hand in the מקדש on שבת, but not elsewhere, and never with a tool. The גמרא contrasts this with a משנה in פסחים that says cutting the wart of the קרבן פסח does not override שבת. Two אמוראים answer: either hand versus tool, or moist versus dry, and each defends his answer against the other. רב יוסף rejects the hand-versus-tool answer and says a שבות for the מקדש is permitted inside the מקדש but not out in the country. רב ספרא challenges this with a scroll on a threshold and with the פסח in the oven, and the גמרא answers both. Finally, רבא explains that our משנה follows רבי אליעזר, who still requires doing the act in a changed way, by hand.
