Topic

The twenty-אמה height limit for a מבוי's קורה and for סוכה: the אמלתרא (cornice) exception, the idea of noticeability, and a beam or סכך that is partly above twenty.

Overview

Earlier on the daf, רב learned the twenty-אמה limit for the קורה of a מבוי from the twenty-אמה doorway of the היכל. The גמרא challenges him: the היכל had אמלתראות (decorative cornices) and was still only twenty high, yet רב is quoted as saying a קורה with an אמלתרא works even above twenty. רב יוסף says the cornice rule is really a ברייתא, but אביי shows that a reliable teacher taught it. רב is then defended in two ways. Either the two ברייתות reflect a מחלוקת תנאים, or, as רב נחמן בר יצחק says, a קורה works because it is noticeable, so a cornice still works high up and the היכל is only a memory aid. The גמרא explains why רבי יהודה and the רבנן argue about height for both סוכה and מבוי, and then gives two definitions of אמלתרא: nest-shaped carvings or valuable cedar poles. Next it asks about a beam or סכך that is partly below and partly above twenty אמות. There are two opposite versions of רבה's ruling, each explained by רבא מפרזקיא and רבינא. Finally the גמרא asks for the practical הלכה: רבה בר רב עולא says both cases are פסול, and רבא says both are כשר, a statement that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לָא תַּיהֲנֵי לֵיהּ אֲמַלְתְּרָא,

a cornice should not be effective for it,

דְּהָא הֵיכָל אֲמַלְתְּרָא הַוְיָא לֵיהּ,

for the Sanctuary had a cornice,

וַאֲפִילּוּ הָכִי — עֶשְׂרִים אַמָּה הוּא דְּגָבוֹהַּ,

and even so, it was only twenty cubits high,

דִּתְנַן: חָמֵשׁ אֲמַלְתְּרָאוֹת שֶׁל מֵילָה

as we learned: five cornices of oak
