Eruvin 50b

עירובין נ׳ ע״ב

Learn Eruvin 50b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Eruvin 50b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Setting a שביתה under a tree without naming an exact spot: the size limits, the רב–שמואל dispute with a ברייתא on each side, and naming the trunk instead

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the dispute about a תודה brought with eighty loaves: one view holds he meant one large offering, so nothing becomes holy. The גמרא then returns to רב's ruling that saying 'my שביתה is under this tree' is not enough. אביי limits that ruling to a tree with twelve אמות beneath it. רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע objects that the person might have picked an end section, so he sets the line at eight אמות; at seven or less, every possible four-אמה section shares the center cubit. Next the גמרא brings a ברייתא supporting רב: a traveler who did not mark out his four אמות may not move from his place. שמואל answers by setting that ברייתא in a case where the tree is 2004 אמות away, so the far side is outside his תחום. A second ברייתא supports שמואל: a man with עירובין to the north and to the south is limited by both. The גמרא answers that רב is not refuted, because רב תנא הוא ופליג. Finally the גמרא quotes the משנה's next case: one who sets his שביתה at the tree's trunk may walk 4000 אמות after dark.

Opening lines

וּמָר סָבַר:

And the other Sage holds:

לְקׇרְבָּן גָּדוֹל קָא מְכַוֵּין.

he intends a large offering.

אָמַר אַבָּיֵי: לֹא שָׁנוּ אֶלָּא

Abaye said: They only taught this

בְּאִילָן שֶׁתַּחְתָּיו שְׁתֵּים עֶשְׂרֵה אַמָּה,

about a tree that has twelve cubits beneath it,
