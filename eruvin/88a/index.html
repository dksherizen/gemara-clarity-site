Topic

The leniencies for טבריא, pouring water from balconies and into small courtyards on שבת, and the rule that theft gives no rights in שבת domains

Overview

This amud finishes the leniencies רבי חנניה בן עקביא gave the people of טבריא. A ברייתא explains drying with a towel on שבת: the worry is wringing it out, and רבי שמעון even lets a person carry the towel home. Next, רבה בר רב הונא discusses pouring water through the balcony hole, and the גמרא brings two versions of his ruling. In both versions, רב שיזבי compares the case to an עוקה (a waste-water pit). רב then rules that two balconies far apart do not restrict each other, because a person does not restrict another 'by way of the air.' רבי חייא and רבי אושעיא teach that the law of theft applies to שבת domains, so a ruin stays with its owner. This leads to a discussion of partners who built a partition together. Finally, a new משנה teaches that a courtyard smaller than four by four אמות needs a two-סאה pit before one may pour water into it on שבת. רבה begins explaining the reason, and his explanation continues on the next amud.

  1. ResolutionThe people of טבריא are treated like workers saving time
