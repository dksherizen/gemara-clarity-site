Topic

The נזיר-on-the-day-משיח-comes ברייתא and תחומין above ten טפחים; רבן גמליאל's measuring tube; נחמיה outside the תחום and the human partition.

Overview

The גמרא is trying to answer whether תחומין (the שבת travel limits) apply in the air, more than ten טפחים above the ground. It uses a ברייתא about someone who vowed to be a נזיר on the day בן דוד comes: he may drink wine on שבת and יום טוב but not on weekdays. First it seems the ברייתא proves יש תחומין. Then the גמרא suggests a different reason: אליהו comes the day before משיח, and there is a promise that אליהו never comes on ערב שבת or ערב יום טוב. Next the גמרא asks why Sunday is not permitted, which seems to prove אין תחומין. It answers that the תנא was unsure about תחומין and ruled strictly, so the ברייתא proves nothing either way. The גמרא then explains that the permission applies only when the vow was made on שבת or יום טוב itself. Next it returns to the משנה about רבן גמליאל's boat. It explains that he had a tube for measuring 2,000 אמות, and adds some practical measuring tricks. Finally it tells of נחמיה, who walked past the תחום while learning, and רב נחמן's advice to make a human partition. It then begins asking what רב חסדא's exact doubt was, a discussion that continues on the next amud.

that is why on Shabbat and Festivals he is permitted.
