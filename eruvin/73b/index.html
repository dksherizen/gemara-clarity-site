Eruvin 73b

עירובין ע״ג ע״ב

Learn Eruvin 73b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Eruvin 73b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

How עירוב חצירות and שיתוף מבואות work together according to רבי מאיר, and why רב left 'opening into one another' out of the משנה.

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה about carrying in courtyards and an alleyway (מבוי). An עירוב חצירות joins the houses of one courtyard, and a שיתוף מבואות joins the courtyards that open into one alleyway. If both are made, carrying is allowed everywhere. If one courtyard resident forgot the עירוב but joined the שיתוף, carrying is still allowed everywhere. If one alleyway resident skipped the שיתוף, only the alleyway is forbidden. The גמרא says the משנה follows רבי מאיר, who requires both an עירוב and a שיתוף. After challenges that the משנה seems to switch to רבנן, it explains that רבי מאיר requires an עירוב only so children do not forget it. When most residents made one, that concern does not apply. The גמרא then reports that רב's version of the משנה left out 'courtyards opening into one another.' רב יוסף explains that a שיתוף's food must pass through the alleyway. That explanation is defended against two משניות but refuted by רבה בר חנן. The גמרא then gives a new reason: an alleyway needs houses and courtyards opening into it. It begins examining that rule of רב, which continues on the next amud.

The steps (26)

  1. StatementWhen the alleyway is joined, carrying is allowed everywhere
  2. StatementA courtyard resident forgot the courtyard עירוב
  3. StatementAn alleyway resident did not join the שיתוף
  4. QuestionWhose opinion is the משנה
  5. ResponseIt is רבי מאיר, who requires both
  6. ChallengeThe middle clause seems to follow רבנן
  7. ResolutionRead the middle clause as 'also merged'
  8. QuestionIn the next clause, the forgetful courtyard resident must have renounced his rights
  9. ChallengeIf renunciation works, why is the alleyway forbidden?
  10. ResolutionSuggestion: רבי מאיר holds renunciation does not work in an alleyway
  11. RejectionA ברייתא shows רבי מאיר accepts renunciation in an alleyway
  12. ProofThe alleyway resident did not renounce, so the courtyard resident did not renounce either
  13. ChallengeThe משנה seems split between two opinions
  14. ResolutionThe whole משנה follows רבי מאיר because his reason does not apply here
  15. Statementרב's version of the משנה leaves out 'opening into one another'
  16. Questionאביי asks why רב left out those words
  17. ResponseA שיתוף must pass through the alleyway's entrances
