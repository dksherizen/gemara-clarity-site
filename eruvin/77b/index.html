Topic

Objects that reduce (ממעט) the height of a wall between two courtyards: a buried ספל, Egyptian and Tyrian ladders, paired ladders, stacked platforms, and ladders with separate rungs

Overview

This עמוד deals with objects placed against a ten-טפחים wall between two courtyards that can 'reduce' (ממעט) its height, so the wall counts as an opening. It opens by finishing a ברייתא about buried turnips, which may be pulled out on שבת. The גמרא uses it to challenge רבי יחיאל's ruling that a buried basin reduces the wall. It answers that he meant a basin so stuck that only a hoe or spade could remove it. Next comes a teaching that a small Egyptian ladder (fewer than four rungs) does not reduce the wall, but a Tyrian ladder does. רב אשי explains with a rule from רב: anything that may be moved on שבת does not reduce a wall. The גמרא asks why the Tyrian ladder still counts and answers that its heaviness fixes it in place (כובדו קובעו). Then אביי, רב ביבי בר אביי, and רב נחמן in the name of רבה בר אבוה rule on two ladders on opposite sides of a wall, stacked platforms, and a ladder with separate rungs, using the rules of a four-טפחים surface and לבוד (a gap under three טפחים counts as closed). A further ruling from רב נחמן begins at the end and continues on the next עמוד.
