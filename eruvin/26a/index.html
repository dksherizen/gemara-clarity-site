Eruvin 26a

עירובין כ״ו ע״א

Learn Eruvin 26a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Eruvin 26a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The garden-reeds story and its challenges to רבא, the size limit of a garden (בית כור vs. forty סאה), ישעיהו at חזקיה's door, and the house of a neighbor who forgot the עירוב.

Overview

This amud opens in the middle of a story. Based on רבא's ruling that a pavilion made a whole garden count as enclosed for living, the reeds around the garden were removed, and רבא's students gathered them up. The next day the students challenged רבא three times: from a ברייתא about a town walled before it was settled, from רב אסי's rule about privacy screens, and from רב הונא's rule about a מחיצה made for convenience. The ריש גלותא ended the story with a פסוק: they were wise to ruin but not to fix. The גמרא then returns to the משנה, where רבי אלעאי allows carrying even in a בית כור. רבי יוחנן shows that רבי אלעאי and חנניה both learn from one פסוק about a king's courtyard, and they differ only on how big a medium-sized town is. A side story explains that ישעיהו set up a ישיבה at the sick חזקיה's door, though the גמרא warns against copying this. Finally, רבי אלעאי's second tradition about a neighbor who forgot the עירוב is challenged by a משנה, and רב ששת's answer begins here and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שַׁלְפִינְהוּ.

He removed them (the reeds).

אֲזַל רַב פָּפָּא

Rav Pappa went

וְרַב הוּנָא בְּרֵיהּ דְּרַב יְהוֹשֻׁעַ,

and Rav Huna, son of Rav Yehoshua,

נַקְטִינְהוּ מִבָּתְרֵיהּ.

and collected them after him.

The steps (20)

  1. StatementThe reeds are removed and then collected
  2. Challengeרבינא brings a ברייתא about measuring from a town
  3. StatementThe ברייתא defines new and old towns
  4. ChallengeThe garden is like a town walled before it was settled
  5. Challengeרב פפא: a fence made for privacy is not a real partition
  6. ChallengeA third challenge: a wall made for convenience is not a wall
  7. Proofרב הונא's rule in practice: the cattle troughs of מחוזא
  8. ConclusionThe ריש גלותא's verdict: wise to ruin, not to fix
  9. StatementBack to the משנה: רבי אלעאי allows even a בית כור
  10. StatementThe משנה does not follow חנניה
  11. StatementBoth opinions come from one verse about a royal courtyard
  12. QuestionWhat exactly do they disagree about?
  13. ResponseThey disagree about the size of a medium town
  14. QuestionWhy was ישעיהו in the middle courtyard?
  15. Responseישעיהו set up a ישיבה at the sick king's door
  16. StatementLearning a practice for a sick Torah scholar
  17. RejectionThe practice is rejected because it may stir up the שטן
  18. StatementSecond tradition: the house of the one who forgot the עירוב is forbidden
  19. ChallengeA משנה says the house is forbidden to everyone
  20. Resolutionרב ששת begins the answer: there is no contradiction
