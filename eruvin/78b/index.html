Eruvin 78b

עירובין ע״ח ע״ב

Topic

Ladders, trees, and ditches between two חצרות: when they count as an opening or a divider for עירוב

Overview

רב יוסף asks רבה a series of questions about a ladder that joins two חצרות so they can share one עירוב. If footholds are carved into the wall to widen a narrow ladder, they need to be ten טפחים high. If the whole 'ladder' is carved into the wall, it must reach the top, because bare grooves are hard to climb. רב יוסף then asks about a tree, which may not be climbed on שבת, and an אשירה, which may not be used for any benefit. The גמרא shows that each question could go either way, using the image of a doorway with a lion lying in it. רבה rules that a tree works but an אשירה does not. רב חסדא argues the reverse: only a שבת prohibition should matter for a שבת law. A teaching of רבי אלעזר (some say רבי יוחנן) supports him, while רב נחמן בר יצחק ties both cases to known disputes. A new משנה then teaches that a deep ditch between two חצרות divides them unless it is filled with dirt or pebbles, and that a board four טפחים wide makes a passage across it. The גמרא explains why hay does not fill the ditch. It then begins asking whether dirt fills it without ביטול, and that discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

חָקַק לְהַשְׁלִים בַּכּוֹתֶל,

If he carved into the wall to complete [the ladder's width],

בְּכַמָּה?

how much [high must it be]?

אֲמַר לֵיהּ: בַּעֲשָׂרָה.

He said to him: With ten [handbreadths].

אֲמַר לֵיהּ: חֲקָקוֹ כּוּלּוֹ בַּכּוֹתֶל,

He said to him: If he carved it entirely into the wall,
