Topic

The forgiveness of שאול, the spelling of מאברין/מעברין, and stories of precise, clever and careless speech

Overview

This amud finishes the side discussion about שאול. רבי יוחנן proves from a verse that he was forgiven for the sin of נוב. The גמרא then returns to the spelling question of מאברין or מעברין. בני יהודה, known for precise speech, answer that both versions are taught. The גמרא contrasts the exact speech of בני יהודה with the sloppy, comical speech of בני גליל. It then brings examples of clever coded speech (לשון חכמה) from רבי's maidservant, רבי יוסי בר אסיין, רבי אבהו and רבי אלעאי. Next, רבי יהושע בן חנניה tells how a woman, a young girl and a young boy each beat him in words. The amud ends with stories of ברוריה's sharp wisdom: she corrects רבי יוסי הגלילי for using too many words, then comes across a student learning in a whisper. Her reaction to the student comes on the next amud.
