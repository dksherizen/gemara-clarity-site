Eruvin 33b

עירובין ל״ג ע״ב

Learn Eruvin 33b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Eruvin 33b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

How רבי combines the views of רבי מאיר and רבי יהודה to rule on an עירוב placed in a basket hanging on a tree, and the source for רבי יהודה's requirement that an עירוב rest on a place four טפחים wide.

Overview

The גמרא explains רבי's ruling about an עירוב placed in a basket hanging from a tree. רבי combines two ideas. From רבי מאיר he takes חוקקין להשלים, so the basket counts toward widening the tree to four טפחים. From רבי יהודה he takes the rule that an עירוב must rest on a place four טפחים wide. The source for רבי יהודה is a ברייתא about a beam in רשות הרבים. The גמרא re-reads that ברייתא: only a beam ten טפחים high needs a top four wide, while a lower beam is part of רשות הרבים and needs no width. The גמרא then asks whether this basket explanation clashes with רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה, who treats a basket on a reed as a private domain. It answers that the basket's walls surround the reed but do not surround the tree. רבי ירמיה offers a different explanation: a basket can be tilted down to within ten טפחים. Finally, רב בר שבא challenges רב פפא's teaching from a משנה about one עירוב for יום טוב followed by שבת, and the discussion continues on the next amud.

and he holds like Rabbi Yehuda.

He holds like Rabbi Meir,

who said: we carve out to complete.
