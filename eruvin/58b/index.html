Topic

Measuring the תחום שבת across valleys and hills: spanning, piercing, and the lenient rules for surveyors

Overview

This amud continues the rules for measuring the תחום שבת when the path crosses a valley or a hill. If a valley's wall is sheer, so that a plumb line drops straight down, the measurer measures the width properly. רב יוסף allows spanning a valley up to two thousand אמות deep. אביי objects from a ברייתא that says one hundred, and the גמרא answers that רב יוסף follows אחרים. In a second version, רב יוסף allows spanning even beyond two thousand. The גמרא explains that he is speaking of a vertical wall, and אבימי defines 'vertical' as a slope of less than four אמות. רבא, in two versions, explains which hills are spanned and which are measured or estimated. רב כהנא explains why the measurer may not step outside the תחום. A ברייתא describes the 'piercing' method (קידור), which is not used for עגלה ערופה or ערי מקלט. A new משנה then rules that only an expert measures, that the larger measurement is followed, and that even an עבד or שפחה is believed, because the תחום rules are meant to be lenient.

But if a plumb line drops straight down alongside it —

he measures it with a proper measurement.

Rav Yosef said: two thousand.
