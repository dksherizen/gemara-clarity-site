Topic

Who holds of ברירה: רבי יוסי in דמאי and מעשר שני, רבי שמעון in עירוב, and why the wine of כותים may not be about ברירה at all.

Overview

This amud asks whether רבי יוסי holds of ברירה, the idea that a later choice can show what was meant from the start. Two sources seem to show that he does: a חבר buying food for an עם הארץ, and redeeming מעשר שני on whatever סלע comes out of the pouch. The גמרא switches the names in both (איפוך). It proves the second switch from the סיפא, where רבי יוסי 'agrees' about a single new coin. Then רבא asks רב נחמן which תנא rejects ברירה even in a rabbinic law like עירוב, and רב נחמן is silent. רב יוסף answers that תנאים argue about it, and after switching the ברייתא, רבי שמעון is that תנא, since for רב יוסף ברירה is all-or-nothing. רבא instead explains that רבי שמעון's strict ruling on the wine of כותים comes from the rule that תרומה must be a 'ראשית' whose leftovers are recognizable. He defends this rule against אביי's challenges from two pomegranates and from a pile of produce. The גמרא also offers another reason, the worry that the flask might burst, and it begins to explain how the רבנן spoke to רבי מאיר by their own view and by his. That explanation continues on the next amud.
