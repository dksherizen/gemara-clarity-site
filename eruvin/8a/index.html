Topic

A מבוי ending into a רחבה or courtyard, and partitions that might change or fail

Overview

The גמרא explains what רב and שמואל argue about regarding a מבוי that opens into a רחבה. Without a shared עירוב, they argue about an opening that looks closed from outside but open from inside (נראה מבחוץ ושוה מבפנים). With an עירוב, they argue about רב יוסף's rule that only a מבוי ending in the middle of the רחבה is permitted. רבה adds that the openings must not line up, and רב משרשיא adds that the רחבה must belong to many people, since a single owner may build on it. The source for this is a case brought before רבי: a מבוי closed by the sea and an אשפה, where he gave no ruling because those barriers might change. A private heap may be cleared away while a public one stays, and the same is true of a backyard. In practice, carrying in such a מבוי is forbidden. מרימר fenced סורא's alleys with nets, and רב חסדא rejected a loose mat as a לחי unless it is fastened with a peg. Finally, the גמרא examines רב's ruling about a מבוי breached into a courtyard and shows that it teaches something beyond the משנה and the ברייתא. That discussion continues on the next amud.
