Topic

Rescuing תפילין and a child on שבת, רבי יהודה's barrel passed beyond the תחום, and rolling back a dropped holy scroll

Overview

The amud continues the משנה about finding תפילין outside on שבת. אביי challenges the idea that the משנה's 'cover them' case means danger from gentiles. The גמרא answers that the משנה is missing words: with gentiles one covers them, and with bandits the תנא קמא and רבי שמעון argue about how to carry them in. The תנא קמא worries that a chain of people makes the שבת violation public. רבי שמעון worries that one person carrying short distances will forget and carry four אמות, and his ruling about a son born in the field applies the same reasoning. Next, the גמרא asks how רבי יהודה can let a barrel be passed beyond the owner's תחום. ריש לקיש's answer, that water has no substance, is rejected. רבא, רב יוסף, אביי, and רב אשי then each explain the case differently. Finally, a new משנה teaches when someone may roll back a holy scroll that slipped from his hand on שבת, and the גמרא begins asking what kind of threshold the משנה means. That discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֲמַר לֵיהּ אַבָּיֵי:
