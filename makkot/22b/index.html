Topic

The number of lashes, the rules of assessing a person's strength (אומד), and the procedure of מלקות

Overview

The amud opens with the end of a משנה about re-assessing how many lashes a person can survive. The גמרא explains that the חכמים give thirty-nine lashes because the פסוק says 'a number close to forty.' רבא learns from this that a great scholar deserves the same honor people give a ספר תורה. רבי יצחק brings a פסוק from זכריה as the source for רבי יהודה's full forty, and the רבנן explain that it is about schoolchildren. The גמרא then reads the משנה closely to learn that a new assessment exempts him only after real lashes. רב ששת settles a clash with a ברייתא by splitting a same-day assessment from a later-day one. A new משנה covers one sin with two לאוין, and רב ששת explains when one assessment can cover both, forty-one or forty-two. The last משנה describes how מלקות is given: the post, the strap, the פסוקים read aloud, and when the lashes stop early.

Opening lines

וְאָמְדוּ שֶׁאֵין יָכוֹל לְקַבֵּל אַרְבָּעִים – פָּטוּר.

And then they assessed that he cannot receive forty, he is exempt.
