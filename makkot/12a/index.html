Topic

The errors of יואב and of שרו של רומי, and the laws of the עיר מקלט: burial, outskirts, the גואל הדם, and a tree on the boundary

Overview

The amud opens by showing that both views on whether a disqualified כהן's כהונה is 'dead' or 'voided' can fit רבי יהושע. Then רב lists the mistakes יואב made when he grabbed the מזבח for safety, and אביי adds one more. ריש לקיש describes three mistakes the שרו של רומי will make when he seeks refuge in בצרה. Back in the משנה, רבי אבהו teaches that the ערי מקלט are not used for burial, except for the רוצח himself. The outskirts protect the רוצח, but he must live inside the city, and he may not even live in tunnels under the outskirts. The גמרא then discusses the רוצח who leaves the תחום. רבי יוסי הגלילי and רבי עקיבא argue whether killing him is a מצוה or only רשות for the גואל הדם, and רב follows רבי אליעזר that he may not be killed before a trial. Next the גמרא asks whether leaving the city by accident still exposes him, and whether a son can be the גואל הדם against his father. A new משנה rules that a tree on the boundary follows its branches. The גמרא compares this to מעשר שני, and רב כהנא's answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אַלִּיבָּא דְּרַבִּי אֱלִיעֶזֶר

According to Rabbi Eliezer

כּוּלֵּי עָלְמָא לָא פְלִיגִי,

everyone does not disagree,

כִּי פְּלִיגִי אַלִּיבָּא דְּרַבִּי יְהוֹשֻׁעַ:

when they disagree, it is according to Rabbi Yehoshua:

מַאן דְּאָמַר מֵתָה – כְּרַבִּי יְהוֹשֻׁעַ,

the one who says 'dead' is in line with Rabbi Yehoshua,
