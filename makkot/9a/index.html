Makkos 9a

מכות ט׳ ע״א

Topic

The גר תושב and גלות, and whether one who thought killing was permitted (אומר מותר) is close to מזיד or אנוס

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier answer: a blow that caused less than a פרוטה of damage brings מלקות, since there is no money to pay. On this view we do not compare hitting to cursing. Next the גמרא asks about the גר תושב in the משנה. רב כהנא explains that he goes to גלות for killing another גר תושב, but not for killing a Jew. The same split settles two פסוקים about the ערי מקלט. A ברייתא then seems to say a גר תושב is always executed, like a non-Jew. רב חסדא answers by contrasting a downward motion with an upward one, but רבא rejects this with a קל וחומר. רבא instead says the ברייתא is about אומר מותר, which he treats as close to מזיד, while רב חסדא calls it אנוס. Each side then brings proofs from the story of אבימלך and שרה, and the answer to אביי's challenge continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שֶׁאֵין בָּהּ שָׁוֶה פְּרוּטָה.

that does not have the value of a peruta.

דְּאָמַר רַבִּי אַמֵּי אָמַר רַבִּי יוֹחָנָן:

As Rabbi Ami said that Rabbi Yochanan said:

הִכָּהוּ הַכָּאָה

If he struck him a blow

שֶׁאֵין בָּהּ שָׁוֶה פְּרוּטָה – לוֹקֶה.

that does not have the value of a peruta, he is flogged.
