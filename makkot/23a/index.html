Topic

How מלקות is carried out, רב ששת's סמוכין teachings, and the new משנה that lashes free a person from כרת

Overview

The גמרא explains why the person being flogged has his clothes torn: the Torah calls him 'נקלה,' debased. רב ששת then gives four teachings in the name of רבי אלעזר בן עזריה, each learned from neighboring פסוקים (סמוכין). These cover the calf-hide strap, the יבמה and the מוכה שחין, contempt for the מועדים, and לשון הרע with false testimony. Next the גמרא goes through the details of the משנה about מלקות: donkey-hide side straps, an adjustable strap, one third of the lashes in front and two thirds behind, and a doubled strap learned from והפילו. It also discusses who may give the lashes, how the judges read and count, and the rule that no extra blow may be added. It then explains when soiling himself frees the person from more lashes, and שמואל's rule that a man who runs away from בית דין is exempt. Finally a new משנה teaches, in the name of רבי חנניה בן גמליאל, that lashes free a person from כרת. רבי שמעון begins a proof of his own, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

גְּמָ׳ מַאי טַעְמָא –

GEMARA: What is the reason?

מִשּׁוּם ״נִקְלָה״.

Because of 'debased.'

אָמַר רַב שֵׁשֶׁת מִשּׁוּם רַבִּי אֶלְעָזָר בֶּן עֲזַרְיָה:

Rav Sheshet said in the name of Rabbi Elazar ben Azarya:

מִנַּיִן לִרְצוּעָה שֶׁהִיא שֶׁל עֵגֶל –

From where do we know that the strap is from a calf?
