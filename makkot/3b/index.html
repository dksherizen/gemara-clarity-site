Topic

Loans and שביעית (10-year loans, conditions against שמיטה, the thirty-day rule), followed by rulings of רב about a neck-hole on שבת and wine-colored water in a מקוה.

Overview

This amud opens by finishing שמואל's ruling, which רב יהודה reports in his name: שביעית cancels even a 10-year loan, because the loan will eventually reach 'לא יגש'. רב כהנא challenges this from our משנה about עדים זוממין, who pay only the value of the time and not the whole loan. רבא answers that the משנה is about a loan with a משכון, or one whose documents were handed to בית דין, and שביעית never cancels those. In a second version (איכא דאמרי), שמואל rules the opposite, and the same משנה and the same answer of רבא appear again. Next, שמואל rules that a condition saying 'שביעית will not cancel my loan' does not work. The גמרא explains this by the wording of the condition: a person may give up his own money claim, as with אונאה, but he cannot cancel a law of the תורה. The גמרא then teaches that a loan with no set due date cannot be called in for thirty days, whether it was made with a שטר or by word of mouth. That rule is learned from the extra words 'שנת השמיטה'. Finally come two rulings of רב: one about opening a garment's neck-hole on שבת, and one about wine-colored water falling into a מקוה. Each is challenged and defended, and the discussion of the מקוה continues on the next amud.
