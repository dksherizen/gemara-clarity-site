Topic

Whether חייבי כריתות get מלקות, and how the רבנן and רבי יצחק each find sources for counting forbidden relationships separately, for one woman with three names, and for a full sister.

Overview

The גמרא finishes explaining why, according to the רבנן, a person who is חייב כרת can still get מלקות: כרת is never certain, because תשובה can erase it. Then it brings רבי יצחק, who learns from the extra mention of כרת for a sister that חייבי כריתות get כרת and not מלקות. The גמרא asks where each side learns that every forbidden relationship counts separately (לחלק), and concludes that both learn it from 'ואל אשה' in the פסוק about a נדה. According to the רבנן, the extra כרת for a sister then teaches a separate חיוב for one woman who is a man's sister, his father's sister and his mother's sister, a case that happens only with a רשע son of a רשע. For רבי יצחק, the גמרא first suggests a קל וחומר from רבי עקיבא's question about five נדות. It rejects that קל וחומר because it can be broken, and gives him the second 'אחותו' in the פסוק instead. The רבנן use that second 'אחותו' for a full sister, which teaches אין עונשין מן הדין. The amud ends while the גמרא is giving רבי יצחק's source for the full sister, and that answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

כִּדְאָמְרִינַן מֵעִיקָּרָא,

As we said at the start,

שֶׁאִם עָשׂוּ תְּשׁוּבָה –

that if they did repentance,

בֵּית דִּין שֶׁל מַעְלָה מוֹחֲלִין לָהֶן.

the heavenly court forgives them.

מַאי אָמְרַתְּ?

What would you say?
