Topic

When one unfit witness ruins a whole group's testimony, and who counts as a witness at all

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה about a group of witnesses where one turns out to be a relative or otherwise unfit. רבי יוסי says the whole group falls only in דיני נפשות. רבי says it falls in דיני ממונות too. The משנה adds that the group is ruined only by people who came as witnesses and gave התראה, not by bystanders like two brothers in a crowd. In the גמרא, רבא says many witnesses join into one testimony only within תוך כדי דיבור, and רבינא explains that each witness must begin right after the one before him. רב פפא then challenges רבי עקיבא's rule: the victim, and even the killer himself, saw the act, so shouldn't they ruin the testimony? רבא answers from the פסוק 'יקום דבר' that only people who come to establish the matter in court count as witnesses. רבא then explains that the court asks people whether they came to watch or to testify. The amud ends with an argument about the final הלכה: שמואל rules like רבי יוסי, and רב נחמן rules like רבי.

Opening lines

אָמַר רַבִּי יוֹסֵי:

Rabbi Yosei says:

בַּמֶּה דְּבָרִים אֲמוּרִים –

In what case are these words said?

בְּדִינֵי נְפָשׁוֹת,

In capital cases.

אֲבָל בְּדִינֵי מָמוֹנוֹת –

But in monetary cases,
