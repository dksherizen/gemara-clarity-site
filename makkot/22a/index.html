Topic

Counting multiple sets of lashes for one act, and the start of the משנה on how many lashes are given

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the count of five sets of lashes for someone who cooks a גיד הנשה in milk on יום טוב and eats it. The גמרא asks why kindling counts separately from cooking. It tries replacing kindling with נבילה, then with עצי אשירה, and finally settles on cooking with wood of הקדש. A סימן then introduces six אמוראים who each suggest another prohibition that one act of plowing could break. רבי זעירא and רבי מני then debate whether an oath not to plow on יום טוב should be counted. The final reason it is left out is that this תנא does not hold of איסור כולל. רבי הושעיא and רבי יצחק teach that a פסולי המוקדשין ox counts as two bodies. A new משנה begins: lashes are forty minus one, while רבי יהודה says a full forty. It also starts the rules for estimating how many lashes a person can take, which continue on the next amud.

Opening lines

מִשּׁוּם הַבְעָרָה.

for kindling.

וְאִם אִיתָא,

And if it is so,

מִשּׁוּם הַבְעָרָה לָא מִחַיַּיב,

he is not liable for kindling,

דְּהָא אִיחַיַּיב לֵיהּ מִשּׁוּם בִּשּׁוּלוֹ!

since he is already liable for his cooking!
