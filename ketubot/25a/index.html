Topic

Which signs prove someone is a כהן (תרומה, חלה, נשיאות כפים), whether they prove יוחסין, and whether תרומה and חלה are דאורייתא today

Overview

The גמרא is discussing how a person comes to be treated as a כהן, and whether that status is strong enough to prove his lineage (יוחסין). It first explains the ברייתא's words 'גדולה חזקה' about the כהנים who returned from בבל. One answer is that they moved up from eating תרומה דרבנן to eating תרומה דאורייתא. A second answer is that they still ate only תרומה דרבנן, and the חזקה simply stopped חכמים from making a protective decree. A פסוק in עזרא seems to say they ate תרומה דאורייתא, but the גמרא reads it as forbidding both תרומה and the כהנים' portions of קרבנות. Next, two ברייתות that list נשיאות כפים as proof of כהונה are brought to show it proves lineage. Each time the גמרא pushes the proof off: the listed signs only move a person up from a rabbinic-level gift to a Torah-level one, depending on whether תרומה or חלה is דאורייתא today. This leads to רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע's report of the רבנן in בי רב saying חלה is דאורייתא. He answers that it is the reverse, and he begins a proof from ״בבואכם״ that continues on the next amud.
