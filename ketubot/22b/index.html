Topic

Conflicting witnesses (תרי ותרי) about a husband's death, a divorce, or a betrothal, and whether a woman who remarried must leave.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier rule: a woman who gives a sensible excuse (אמתלא) for changing her claim is believed. Even so, שמואל, who reviewed this rule forty times, would not rely on it for himself. Next a ברייתא discusses two witnesses against two (תרי ותרי) about whether a husband died or whether a woman was divorced. She may not remarry, but if she did, the תנא קמא says she stays and רבי מנחם בר יוסי says she must leave. The גמרא asks how she can stay when the couple faces an אשם תלוי. It answers that she married one of her own witnesses and she says 'בָּרִי לִי'. רבי יוחנן then rules that after the fact she stays in the death case but must leave in the divorce case. The גמרא gives three explanations: אביי (one witness against one, and a death witness counts as two), רבא (in divorce she can contradict her husband), and רב אסי (the witnesses said 'just now,' so a divorce can be checked by asking for the גט). The amud ends with a ברייתא contrasting split testimony about betrothal, where she stays, with split testimony about divorce, where she must leave.

Opening lines

אַמַּתְלָא לִדְבָרֶיהָ — נֶאֱמֶנֶת.

[If she gives] a reason for her words, she is believed.

תְּנָא מִינֵּיהּ אַרְבָּעִים זִימְנִין,
