Topic

Why a childless marriage is not in the משנה's list of forced divorces; the disease ראתן; stories of רבי יהושע בן לוי and רבי חנינא בר פפא at their deaths; and the close of the perek.

Overview

The גמרא explains why the משנה's list of forced divorces leaves out a marriage with no children. In the listed cases, even מוכה שחין, a wife who wants to stay can find a way, such as living with him under the watch of witnesses. In a childless marriage she cannot stay at all. A ברייתא from רבי יוסי then teaches that marital relations harm people with skin diseases, and those with ראתן most of all. The גמרא gives the cause of ראתן, its signs, and אביי's dramatic cure. It then tells how the חכמים kept far from people with ראתן, while רבי יהושע בן לוי sat with them and learned תורה, trusting it to protect him. This leads into the stories of how רבי יהושע בן לוי entered גן עדן alive and how רבי חנינא בר פפא tried the same trick. The perek הַמַּדִּיר אֶת אִשְׁתּוֹ closes with why people in בבל did not get ראתן or צרעת, followed by the הדרן.
