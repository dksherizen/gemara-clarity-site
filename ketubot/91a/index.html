Topic

The extra דינר needed for כתובת בנין דכרין: what counts toward it, and when it is measured

Overview

A ברייתא has the תנא קמא and רבי שמעון both requiring one extra דינר beyond the two כתובות before sons can collect their mothers' כתובות, and the גמרא looks for the real difference between them. It tries movables versus land, then free versus liened land, then less than a דינר. Each attempt is challenged. It finally returns to the first two ideas with the opinions reversed, so רבי שמעון is the strict one. מר זוטרא then rules that when one wife died in the husband's lifetime and one after his death, the sons collect כתובת בנין דכרין, and one כתובה can count as the surplus for the other. The גמרא explains why both parts of his ruling are needed: we do not worry that the heirs will quarrel. A new משנה follows. If the estate covers only the two כתובות, all the sons split it equally. With one extra דינר, each family takes its mother's כתובה. בית דין sets the value, and future property does not count. רבי שמעון holds that only land counts. The גמרא then asks what happens if the estate grew after the father died. It brings the case of בר צרצור, which went from רב עמרם to רב נחמן, whose answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְהָכָא בְּדִינָר מְקַרְקְעֵי קָמִיפַּלְגִי.

And here they disagree about a dinar of land.

מָר סָבַר: מְקַרְקְעֵי — אִין,

One master holds: land — yes,

מִטַּלְטְלֵי — לָא.

movable items — no.

וּמָר סָבַר: אֲפִילּוּ מִטַּלְטְלִי.

And the other master holds: even movable items.
