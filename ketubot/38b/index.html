Topic

Who receives the קנס for a נערה: the readings of בתולה and אשר לא אורסה, the two versions of רבי עקיבא, and a girl who died

Overview

The גמרא explains how רבי עקיבא uses the words בתולה and אשר לא אורסה in the קנס פסוקים. After some back-and-forth, it concludes that בתולה excludes a בעולה, because her body changed. אשר לא אורסה is then used for the גזירה שוה between אונס and מפתה, so a divorced ארוסה still gets a קנס. רבי יוסי הגלילי learns the same comparison from the phrase כמוהר הבתולות instead. Next, the גמרא notes that רבי עקיבא's view is reported two ways: the fine goes to the girl or to her father. It answers that two תנאים reported his view differently. רב נחמן בר יצחק defends the ברייתא's version by reading the words as 'who is not betrothed now.' רבה follows the משנה's version instead. רב חסדא then explains a ברייתא's dispute over who gets the fine as this same split. Finally, אביי rules there is no קנס if the girl died, while רבא asks whether a dead girl can become a בוגרת in the grave, so that the fine would go to her son rather than her father.
