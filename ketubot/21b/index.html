Topic

Witnesses serving as judges (עד נעשה דיין) and how a court of three ratifies a שטר

Overview

The amud opens with a short story. רבנאי quotes שמואל saying that a witness and a judge can join together. אמימר praises the ruling, but רב אשי answers that רבא already refuted it. Next comes a ruling of רב about three judges ratifying a שטר when only two of them recognize the signatures: the two may testify before the third so he can sign with them. A challenge from רבא's rule, that a ratification written too early 'looks like a lie', leads the גמרא to correct the deadline from 'before they signed' to 'before they wrote'. The גמרא draws three lessons from this ruling, but רב אשי shows that only one holds up: עד נעשה דיין, a witness can become a judge. רב ספרא challenges that lesson from the new-moon משנה, and רב answers that עדות החודש is Torah law and stricter, while קיום שטרות is only rabbinic. Finally, רב rules on a judge whose fitness is challenged, and the גמרא begins asking what kind of challenge is meant. That question continues on the next amud.

Ravnai, the brother of Rabbi Chiyya bar Abba, happened to come
