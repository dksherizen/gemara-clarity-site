Topic

Stories of grooms who found no blood of בתולים, the new משנה on the amount of the כתובה, and word-readings starting from אלמנה

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier point: the cloth test used in ארץ ישראל can't work in בבל, because the stone used in גיהוץ would scrape the blood away. Next come three stories of grooms who found no blood. רבן גמליאל בר רבי uses a wine-barrel test, first tried on two שפחות, to show a bride is still a בתולה. רבן גמליאל הזקן confirms that the women of the דורקטי family have no blood at all. רבי sees that a starving couple's bodies were dried out, and once they are fed, blood appears. Along the way the גמרא discusses whether lacking blood is a blessing or a loss. Then a new משנה sets the כתובה at 200 for a בתולה and a מנה for a widow. The גמרא explains, through רב חנא בגדתאה, that the word אלמנה comes from מנה. It then brings more of his sayings, about rain and dates, along with word-readings of מזבח, דשא, דרגא and פוריא. The amud ends with the start of a statement by רב נחמן בר יצחק that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

גִּיהוּץ שֶׁלָּנוּ, כְּכִבּוּס שֶׁלָּהֶם.

Our calendering is like their laundering.

וְאִי אָמְרַתְּ נֶיעְבַּד גִּיהוּץ —

And if you say: let us do calendering —

מְעַבְּרָא לֵיהּ חוּמַרְתָּא.

the stone removes it.

הַהוּא דַּאֲתָא לְקַמֵּיהּ דְּרַבָּן גַּמְלִיאֵל בַּר רַבִּי,

A certain man who came before Rabban Gamliel son of Rabbi,
