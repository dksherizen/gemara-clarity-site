Topic

Vows a husband imposes on his wife, and wives who lose their כתובה for breaking דת משה or דת יהודית

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an explanation from before. A husband who stops his wife from visiting mourners is cutting her off, because if she never comforts others, no one will mourn her. רבי מאיר supports this from a פסוק in קהלת. The גמרא then explains other lines of the משנה. The husband's 'other reason' must be a real, well-known worry about immoral people. Saying 'shameful words' or doing a pointless chore that makes her look foolish are real harms, so she may leave with her כתובה. רב כהנא and a ברייתא add that stopping her from lending household tools ruins her name with the neighbors. A new משנה then lists wives who lose their כתובה for breaking דת משה or דת יהודית. The גמרא explains how a husband can find out about each דת משה case and why unkept vows matter. The חכמים reject the idea that he should simply keep fixing her wrongs himself. Finally, the גמרא asks why an uncovered head counts only as custom when it is a Torah law, and its answer continues on the next amud.
