Topic

What handing a bride over to the husband's messengers (מסירה) accomplishes: inheritance, כתובה, תרומה, and her punishment for adultery.

Overview

The עמוד opens with the end of the משנה. A bride moves into her husband's authority once her father, or his messengers, hand her over to the husband's messengers. If the father's side still goes along with her, she stays under her father. The גמרא explains that the word 'לעולם' rejects an earlier ruling (משנה ראשונה) that let a bride eat תרומה once the wedding date passed. Then אמוראים argue about what the handing over (מסירה) accomplishes. רב says it counts for everything except תרומה, and רב אסי, רבי יוחנן and רבי חנינא say even for תרומה. שמואל says it counts for inheritance, and ריש לקיש says for her כתובה, which רבינא explains as her later כתובה being a מנה. A ברייתא then says the handing over works for inheritance, but she may eat תרומה only after the חופה, and this refutes everyone who allowed תרומה. רב אשי settles a contradiction inside that ברייתא: with nothing said, her courtyard means lodging and his courtyard means marriage. Finally, a ברייתא rules that a bride handed to the husband's messengers who sins gets חנק, learned from the words 'בית אביה'.

Opening lines

לִרְשׁוּת הַבַּעַל לְנִשּׂוּאִין.

to the husband's authority for marriage.

מָסַר הָאָב לִשְׁלוּחֵי הַבַּעַל —

If the father handed her over to the husband's messengers,

הֲרֵי הִיא בִּרְשׁוּת הַבַּעַל.

she is under the husband's authority.

הָלַךְ הָאָב עִם שְׁלוּחֵי הַבַּעַל,

If the father went along with the husband's messengers,
