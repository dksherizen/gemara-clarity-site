Kesubos 9b

כתובות ט׳ ע״ב

Ketubot 9b

Topic

The soldiers' גט in דוד's wars, and whether a husband is believed with a claim of פתח פתוח, to forbid his wife or to take away her כתובה

Overview

This amud opens with the rule that every soldier in the wars of בית דוד wrote his wife a גט כריתות before leaving. The source is the פסוק 'ואת ערבתם תקח,' which רב יוסף reads as the marriage bond. The גמרא then turns to a husband's claim of פתח פתוח, that he found his bride was not a virgin. אביי tries to prove from the rule that a בתולה marries on Wednesday that this claim is believed to forbid her to him, as רבי אלעזר holds. The proof is rejected, because the משנה may be talking about a claim of 'no blood.' Next, רב יהודה quotes שמואל: a husband who claims פתח פתוח is believed to make his wife lose her כתובה. רב יוסף asks why שמואל needs to say this, since the משנה about a groom eating at his father-in-law's house in יהודה seems to teach it already. That proof is rejected the same way: the משנה may be about a claim of 'no blood,' so שמואל's ruling teaches something new.

Opening lines

כׇּל הַיּוֹצֵא לְמִלְחֶמֶת בֵּית דָּוִד,

Anyone who goes out to a war of the house of David

גֵּט כְּרִיתוּת כּוֹתֵב לְאִשְׁתּוֹ.

writes a bill of divorce for his wife.

דִּכְתִיב: ״וְאֶת אַחֶיךָ תִּפְקֹד לְשָׁלוֹם

As it is written: 'And greet your brothers in peace,'

וְאֶת עֲרֻבָּתָם תִּקָּח״,

'and take their pledge.'

The steps (13)

  1. StatementSoldiers in דוד's wars gave their wives a divorce
  2. ProofThe פסוק behind the soldiers' divorce
  3. Proofאביי brings a משנה to support רבי אלעזר
  4. QuestionWhat does his claim accomplish?
  5. ResponseThe claim is to forbid her to him
  6. Proofאביי's conclusion: the claim must be פתח פתוח
  7. RejectionNo, the claim could be about blood
  8. Statementשמואל: A husband claiming an open opening is believed about the כתובה
  9. Challengeרב יוסף: Why do we need this? A משנה already teaches it
  10. ProofReading the משנה: in the גליל he can make the claim
  11. QuestionWhat is the claim for? It cannot be about forbidding her
  12. ResponseSo it is about the כתובה, and the claim must be פתח פתוח
  13. RejectionNo: the משנה may be about a claim of no blood
