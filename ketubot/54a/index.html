Topic

A daughter's and a widow's rights to support from the estate, how a widow loses that support, local customs, and whether a widow's clothes count against her כתובה

Overview

The amud opens with a chain of questions about whether a daughter gets מזונות from her father's estate when her mother's כתובה is missing or not yet in force: a penalized mother, an ארוסה, and an אנוסה. All three end in תיקו. Next the גמרא turns to the widow's clause in the משנה. רב יוסף teaches that 'my house' does not include a hut, but she still keeps her support. Several אמוראים then list ways a widow loses support, such as agreeing to remarry, being unfaithful, or making herself up. The הלכה follows none of them. It follows שמואל: she loses support when she claims her כתובה in בית דין. רב and שמואל argue over whether the custom of יהודה or of גליל is the הלכה, and a story with רב נחמן shows that a woman follows her husband's place. They also argue whether a widow's clothes are appraised against her כתובה. רב נחמן and later רבינא rule like רב. The amud ends with cases about a father's instructions for his daughter's dowry, and the last case continues on the next amud.
