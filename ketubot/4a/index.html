Topic

A parent's death right before a wedding: the order of חופה, feast days and אבילות; רב חסדא's water-on-the-meat limit; and the couple sleeping apart in mourning and נדה cases

Overview

This עמוד finishes a ברייתא about a parent who died just before a wedding while the food was already prepared. The wedding goes first, then the seven feast days, and then the seven days of אבילות, and the couple sleeps apart the whole time. The גמרא limits this rule to the death of the חתן's father or the כלה's mother. רב חסדא limits it further to when water was already put on the meat. רבא and רב פפא add that a big city and a village each work differently, and רב אשי shows that רב חסדא's rule applies in a middle-sized town like מתא מחסיא. A ברייתא supports רב חסדא and adds rules about a כלה who became a נדה, about jewelry, and about not having the first relations on Friday or Saturday night. The 'sleep apart' line supports רבי יוחנן, who holds that private mourning practices still apply on a Festival. רבא limits the נדה rule to a couple who had not yet been together, and the גמרא debates whether that limit also covers mourning. The last challenge, from the word 'וכן', is answered on the next עמוד.

וּבוֹעֵל בְּעִילַת מִצְוָה וּפוֹרֵשׁ,

And he has the מצוה intercourse and then separates,

וְנוֹהֵג שִׁבְעַת יְמֵי הַמִּשְׁתֶּה,

and he keeps the seven days of the feast,

וְאַחַר כָּךְ נוֹהֵג שִׁבְעַת יְמֵי אֲבֵילוּת.

and afterward he keeps the seven days of mourning.

וְכׇל אוֹתָן הַיָּמִים,

And all those days,
