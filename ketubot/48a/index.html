Topic

A wife's clothing and intimacy rights, her funeral and burial costs, and the court supporting the family of an insane or absent husband

Overview

The amud opens with רבי אליעזר בן יעקב, who reads the words of the פסוק (שמות 21:10) as rules about a wife's clothing: the clothes must fit her age and the season. רב יוסף then teaches a ברייתא that reads שְׁאֵרָהּ as closeness of body during intimacy, which supports רב הונא's ruling about a husband who insists on staying clothed. Next the גמרא explains the משנה's argument over funeral flutes and a mourning woman. תנא קמא holds that a wife shares her husband's higher status only while alive. רבי יהודה holds that she shares it even after death, and the הלכה follows him. The גמרא then discusses how the בית דין uses an insane man's property to support his family, and why that differs from a man who went overseas. It also explains what 'something else' (jewelry or charity) means. It adds that an absent husband's wife is buried at least by his dignity. It ends by teaching that a man cannot order that his own burial not be paid from his property. Finally a new משנה begins about a daughter staying under her father's authority.

Opening lines

רַבִּי אֱלִיעֶזֶר בֶּן יַעֲקֹב אוֹמֵר:

Rabbi Eliezer ben Ya'akov says:

״שְׁאֵרָהּ כְּסוּתָהּ״ —

'Her flesh, her clothing' —

לְפוּם שְׁאֵרָהּ תֵּן כְּסוּתָהּ,

according to her flesh, give her clothing,

שֶׁלֹּא יִתֵּן לָהּ לֹא שֶׁל יַלְדָּה לִזְקֵינָהּ,

that he should not give her a young girl's clothes for an older woman,
