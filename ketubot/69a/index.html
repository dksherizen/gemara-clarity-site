Topic

The daughter's dowry-tenth: collecting it from mortgaged land, whether she is an heir or a creditor, and stories about collecting it

Overview

רב sends רבי a question about brothers who 'שיעבדו' their father's land: can the daughters still collect their dowry from it? רבי answers that whether the land was sold or pledged, the court takes it back for פרנסה (dowry) but not for מזונות (food). The גמרא explains that רב used the broad word on purpose, so one answer would cover both cases. רבי יוחנן disagrees, and the גמרא asks whether he never heard רבי's ruling or rejected it. A proof from a case of two daughters whose brother died is pushed aside. אמימר first holds that a daughter collecting her tenth is an heir, but רב אשי says she is a creditor, and אמימר comes to agree. A story about רבינא shows she is the brothers' creditor, not the father's. The amud ends with stories about collecting the tenth, including רב הונא's sharp reply to רב ענן, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

תְּלָה לֵיהּ רַב לְרַבִּי בֵּינֵי חִטֵּי:

Rav attached a question for Rabbi between the lines:

הָאַחִין שֶׁשִּׁיעְבְּדוּ, מַהוּ?

Brothers who mortgaged the land, what is the law?

הֲוָה יָתֵיב רַבִּי חִיָּיא קַמֵּיהּ.

Rabbi Chiya was sitting before him.

אֲמַר לֵיהּ: מָכְרוּ אוֹ מִשְׁכְּנוּ?

He said to him: Did they sell it, or did they pledge it?

The steps (34)

  1. Questionרב sends a question about brothers who mortgaged land
  2. Questionרבי חייא asks: sold or pledged?
  3. Responseרבי answers: it makes no difference
  4. ChallengeWhy didn't רב just say what he meant?
  5. Resolutionרב wanted both cases: why asking about 'sold' wouldn't work
  6. ResolutionWhy asking about 'pledged' wouldn't work either, so רב wrote 'mortgaged'
  7. Statementרבי יוחנן says the land is never taken back
  8. QuestionDid רבי יוחנן know רבי's ruling?
  9. ProofTwo daughters, one dowry already taken
  10. Statementרבי יוחנן: the second daughter gave up her tenth
  11. Challengeרבי חנינא pushes back using the dowry rule
  12. ProofProof: רבי יוחנן knew רבי's ruling and disagreed
  13. RejectionProof rejected: the daughters inherit plenty
  14. ChallengeWhat if she found a lost object?
  15. ResolutionOnly wealth from the estate counts
  16. Statementאמימר: a daughter is an heir
  17. QuestionCan the brothers pay her off with money?
  18. ResponseYes, cash is not enough
  19. QuestionCan they give her just one field?
  20. ResponseYes, even one field is not enough
  21. Statementרב אשי: a daughter is a creditor
  22. ProofA story shows אמימר changed his mind
  23. QuestionWhose creditor is she: father's or brothers'?
  24. QuestionWhat difference does it make?
  25. ResponseMiddle land without an oath, or poor land with an oath
  26. QuestionSo what is the ruling?
  27. Proofרבינא collects for רב אשי's daughter in two different ways
  28. StatementCollect the tenth even from the millstone land
  29. Statementרב אשי: we collected even from house rent
  30. Statementרב ענן's letter to רב הונא
  31. Challengeרב הונא's sharp reply: land or movables, and who outranks whom?
  32. Challengeרב ששת delivers the message respectfully
  33. Questionרב ענן complains to מר עוקבא about רב הונא's letter
  34. Responseמר עוקבא begins his reply with a warm greeting
