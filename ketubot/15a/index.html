Topic

Following a majority for lineage: the double-majority rule, the principle that anything קבוע counts as half and half, and רב's ruling about רבי יוסי

Overview

The גמרא says the earlier incident happened at the wagons of ציפורי. It explains, using רבי אמי and רבי ינאי, that a woman is fit for כהונה only when both the city and a passing group are mostly of proper lineage. If one man came out of the city alone, the child is a שתוקי. A teaching that we follow the city's majority and not the group's is challenged as backwards. It is reread to mean that two majorities are needed. Neither one alone is enough, because a man whom she went to inside the city is קבוע, and קבוע counts as half and half. The meat-store ברייתא seems to show that one majority is enough. The answer is that חכמים set a higher standard for יוחסין. The גמרא then finds רבי זירא's source that קבוע counts as half and half even when this leads to leniency. It brings the case of nine שרצים and one frog, and the Torah source is the פסוק 'וארב לו' about a stone thrown into a group. Finally, two reports of רב disagree about whether the הלכה follows רבי יוסי. רבי ירמיה begins a challenge from a משנה that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בִּקְרוֹנוֹת שֶׁל צִיפּוֹרִי הָיָה מַעֲשֶׂה,

The incident happened among the wagons of Tzippori,

וְכִדְרַבִּי אַמֵּי.

and it is in line with Rabbi Ami.

דְּאָמַר רַבִּי אַמֵּי:

As Rabbi Ami said:

וְהוּא שֶׁהָיְתָה סִיעָה

And that is only when there was a group
