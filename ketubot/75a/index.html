Topic

Public vows, remarrying a wife divorced over a vow, a woman's extra blemishes, praise of ארץ ישראל, and who must prove when a bride's blemishes began

Overview

This amud opens by asking how many people make a vow 'public', so that it cannot be released, as with the oath to the גבעונים. רב נחמן בר יצחק says three and רבי יצחק says ten. Next comes a משנה where רבי מאיר and רבי אלעזר argue about remarrying a wife who was divorced over a vow. The גמרא explains that the real question is whether a husband is willing to have his wife embarrassed in בית דין. רבא then explains a ברייתא about a woman betrothed on condition that she has no vows. The גמרא challenges him, and the answer is that a woman is happy with almost any husband, which several sages show with popular sayings. The גמרא then lists blemishes that count in a woman but not in a כהן: sweat, bad breath, a hidden mole, a dog-bite scar, a deep voice, and unusual breasts. A short aggadic passage praises ארץ ישראל and those who go up to it. Finally, a new משנה begins on who must prove when a bride's blemishes started, and the view of the חכמים continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

״וְלֹא הִכּוּם בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל

"And the children of Israel did not smite them,

כִּי נִשְׁבְּעוּ לָהֶם נְשִׂיאֵי הָעֵדָה״,

for the princes of the assembly had sworn to them."

וְכַמָּה רַבִּים?

And how many are 'many'?

רַב נַחְמָן בַּר יִצְחָק אָמַר: שְׁלֹשָׁה.

Rav Naḥman bar Yitzḥak said: Three.
