Topic

פתח פתוח: why a husband's claim is believed, ספק ספיקא, and reconciling רבי אלעזר's statements about what forbids a wife

Overview

רבי אלעזר rules that a husband who says 'I found an open opening' (פתח פתוח), meaning his bride was not a בתולה, is believed to forbid her to himself. The גמרא asks why, since there is a ספק ספיקא: maybe it happened before the אירוסין, and even if after, maybe it was אונס. It answers that the case is an אשת כהן, who is forbidden even through אונס, or a girl betrothed before age three, so only one doubt remains. The גמרא then asks what the ruling adds, since a משנה already shows a man can forbid things to himself by his own word. It answers that here he might be mistaken, yet he is still believed. Next, the גמרא raises a contradiction from another statement of רבי אלעזר: a wife is forbidden only through קינוי וסתירה, as learned from the story of דוד and בת שבע. After clearing up that statement's wording, the גמרא rereads it as being about evidence. Two witnesses forbid a wife, after קינוי וסתירה one witness is enough, and פתח פתוח counts like two witnesses. Finally, it asks why בת שבע was not forbidden. It answers that it was אונס, and begins a second answer from רבי שמואל בר נחמני in the name of רבי יונתן, which continues on the next amud.
