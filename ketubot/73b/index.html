Topic

The מחלוקת of רב and שמואל about a condition made at קידושין but not repeated at נישואין, and רבה's limits on that argument, tested by אביי's challenges.

Overview

The גמרא continues the מחלוקת between רב and שמואל about a man who made קידושין on condition that she has no נדרים and then married her without repeating the condition. It pushes away a proof against שמואל, and then reads the משנה so that it actually supports him. It then asks why, in a plain case with no condition at all, she loses her כתובה but still needs a גט. רבה and רב חסדא answer that the גט is only rabbinic. רבא answers that the תנא is in doubt, so he is lenient on money and strict on איסור. רבה then limits the argument of רב and שמואל. After אביי challenges him, רבה's statement is reworded: they argue only about one woman married twice, like two women, but in a plain one-woman mistake everyone agrees no גט is needed. אביי attacks this with a ברייתא about קידושין with less than a פרוטה and a ברייתא about 'on condition that my father agrees,' and the גמרא answers both. Finally אביי brings a ברייתא about a young girl who was divorced and taken back, and the challenge from it continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לָא, קִידְּשָׁה סְתָם,

No, he betrothed her without specification,

וּכְנָסָהּ סְתָם.

and married her without specification.

אֲבָל, קִידְּשָׁה עַל תְּנַאי וּכְנָסָהּ סְתָם —

But if he betrothed her with a condition and married her without specification,

הָכִי נָמֵי דְּלָא בָּעֲיָא גִּיטָּא?

is it indeed so that she does not need a bill of divorce?
