Topic

Is it allowed to have the first relations with a בתולה on שבת? The dispute between רב and שמואל, and how it connects to ר' שמעון's rule about an unintended forbidden result (דבר שאין מתכוון) and the exception of פסיק רישיה.

Overview

This page opens with the last words of a question from the previous page: in the case of first relations with a בתולה on שבת, does the הלכה follow ר' יהודה, who forbids an act with an unintended forbidden result, or ר' שמעון, who permits it? The גמרא quotes two opposite reports of a dispute between רב and שמואל. רב נחמן בר יצחק gives a memory aid: each city calls its own teacher the lenient one. The גמרא challenges the report that רב permits, since רב forbids pushing a cloth stopper tightly into a barrel on יום טוב. It answers that the barrel case is a פסיק רישיה, a certain result, which even ר' שמעון forbids. A second challenge points out that רב rules like ר' יהודה in general. The answer is that רב does follow ר' יהודה, but first relations are a destructive act (מקלקל) under either understanding of the blood, so they are permitted anyway. Finally רב חסדא quotes a משנה in נדה about the nights given to a bride, ending at מוצאי שבת, to build a new challenge that continues on the next page.
