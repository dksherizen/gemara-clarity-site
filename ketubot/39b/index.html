Topic

The payments and marriage rules of the אונס and the מפתה: צער, when the fine is paid, refusing the marriage, and the כתובה

Overview

The amud opens at the end of a ברייתא. In it, רבי שמעון says a rapist owes nothing for צער, since every wife feels that pain anyway, and the sages answer that forced relations are not like willing ones. רב נחמן in the name of רבה בר אבוה then defines the צער as the pain of having her legs spread by force. The גמרא asks why a מפתה does not pay this too. A parable about tearing someone's silk is rejected because the money belongs to her father. The final answer is that a willing girl feels hardly any pain, and three אמוראים quote women describing how mild it is. Next the גמרא rereads the משנה's rule on when the מפתה pays. It also discusses how both the girl and her father can refuse marriage to an אונס or a מפתה, with אביי and רבא giving different sources for the father's right. A second ברייתא explains that the wife of an אונס gets no more money if she leaves, and the רבנן and רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה argue over whether she gets a כתובה, depending on why a כתובה exists. The amud ends as רבא מפרזקיא begins a question to רב אשי about the rule that an אונס must keep her, and the question continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שֶׁסּוֹפָהּ לְהִצְטַעֵר תַּחַת בַּעֲלָהּ.

since in the end she will suffer [this pain] under her husband.

אָמְרוּ לוֹ:

They said to him:

אֵינוֹ דּוֹמֶה נִבְעֶלֶת בְּאוֹנֶס

A woman who has relations by force is not comparable

לְנִבְעֶלֶת בְּרָצוֹן.

to one who has relations willingly.
