Topic

The קנס for forced relations: when קידושין takes effect, the נדה case, and whether death from Heaven exempts from payment

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the search for the practical difference between two views on which women receive the קנס. One view asks whether קידושין can take effect, and the other asks whether he may keep her as a wife. Following רבי ישבב, the first answer is women forbidden only by a positive מצוה, such as an Egyptian or Edomite convert. The גמרא then says that if רבי ישבב's rule is broad, even those cases produce a ממזר. So it gives a new case instead: a בעולה with a כהן גדול, because that עשה does not apply to everyone. רב חסדא then teaches that everyone agrees a man who forced a נדה pays the קנס, since she passes both tests. Next, the גמרא notes that the משנה rejects רבי נחוניא בן הקנה, who held that anyone liable to death from Heaven is exempt from payment. אביי learns that view from the word אסון, which appears in two פסוקים. רב אדא בר אהבה challenges that proof, and the גמרא begins asking how to sort cold and heat, and lions and thieves, between harm from Heaven and harm from people. That discussion continues on the next amud.
