Topic

Is a woman believed that the man she was with was of fit lineage? This leads to the refutation of רב אסי, the status of her daughter, and the meaning of שתוקי.

Overview

The amud opens with the end of a source about a woman seen going off with a man to a hidden place or a ruin. She says he was a כשר כהן. רבן גמליאל and רבי אליעזר believe her, and רבי יהושע does not without proof. The גמרא asks why, according to רב אסי, the source lists two places. It concludes that the two cases are a ruin in the city and a ruin in the field, and that each one is needed. A ברייתא then records a back-and-forth between רבי יהושע and the חכמים, comparing this woman to a captive woman. Read properly, the ברייתא treats the 'speaking' woman and the pregnant woman as two separate cases, which refutes רב אסי. The גמרא learns from the ברייתא that the majority of people nearby does not decide this dispute, which supports רבי יהושע בן לוי. רבי יוחנן and רבי אלעזר then argue about whether the daughter's status follows the mother's. This leads to שמואל's case, which shows that a שתוקי can be fit and is only kept from כהונה. The amud ends by starting a story of an engaged couple before רב יוסף, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אוֹ לְחוּרְבָּה —

or into a ruin —

וְאָמְרוּ לָהּ: מָה טִיבוֹ שֶׁל אִישׁ זֶה?

and they said to her: What is the nature of this man?

כֹּהֵן הוּא, וּבֶן אֲחִי אַבָּא הוּא.

He is a priest, and he is the son of my father's brother.

רַבָּן גַּמְלִיאֵל וְרַבִּי אֱלִיעֶזֶר אוֹמְרִים: נֶאֱמֶנֶת,

Rabban Gamliel and Rabbi Eliezer say: She is believed,
