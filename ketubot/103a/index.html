Ketubot 103a

Topic

Supporting a stepdaughter, the millstone case and מדת סדום, the widow's housing and support, and רבי's last instructions

Overview

The גמרא first explains why the food goes 'to the place where her mother is': a daughter stays with her mother at any age. It then tells of a millstone that was rented out in exchange for grinding. רבינא compares this case to the משנה, רב עוירא rejects the comparison, and the גמרא rules that if the renter has other grinding work, refusing to pay money is מדת סדום. A new משנה teaches that a widow may stay in her husband's house and be supported there, but the יורשים do not have to support her in her father's house unless she has a good reason. The גמרא spells out her rights: she keeps the same household use, the יתומים cannot sell her home, and a fallen house need not be rebuilt. Using רב הונא's rule that a full house is blessed, the גמרא concludes that a widow living elsewhere still gets the cheaper home amount. Finally, a ברייתא tells of רבי's last instructions to his sons and his summons of the חכמי ישראל, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַאי לִמְקוֹם שֶׁאִמָּהּ?

What is meant by 'to the place where her mother is'?

שָׁמְעַתְּ מִינַּהּ בַּת אֵצֶל הָאֵם,

Learn from this: a daughter stays with the mother,

לָא שְׁנָא גְּדוֹלָה וְלָא שְׁנָא קְטַנָּה.

no difference whether she is an adult, and no difference whether she is a minor.

לֹא יֹאמְרוּ שְׁנֵיהֶם וְכוּ׳.

'The two of them may not say,' and so on.
