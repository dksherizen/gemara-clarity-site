Topic

Which day a בתולה marries on, and when danger or אונס allows a different day

Overview

The גמרא explains the phrase 'שקדו' with a ברייתא. A בתולה marries on Wednesday so the husband can bring a טענת בתולים to בית דין the next morning, and so he has three days to prepare the feast. The ברייתא adds three more rules: in a time of סכנה people married on Tuesday, Monday is allowed only because of אונס, and the first union is not on Friday night because of חבורה. רבה explains the 'danger': the government decreed that a Wednesday bride would first be violated by the הגמון, and modest women would rather die than submit. The גמרא explains why the חכמים did not simply teach that אונס is permitted, and why they did not cancel the Wednesday rule. A persecution is temporary, and the ruler will not bother over an uncertain Tuesday wedding. רבא explains the Monday 'אונס' as an army general coming to settle in the city, whose advance troops arrive on Tuesday. The גמרא then offers a second explanation: a death in the family after the wedding feast is already prepared.
