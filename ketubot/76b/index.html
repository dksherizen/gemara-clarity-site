Topic

Who carries the burden of proof in a doubtful case: two reports of שמואל's rule, tested against the כלה משנה and the needle ברייתא

Overview

The amud opens in the middle of a discussion about שמואל's ruling that, in a doubtful trade, the donkey owner must bring proof even though he holds the cow. The גמרא tests whether the משנה of a כלה found with blemishes really supports this. First the proof from כלה בבית אביה is challenged. Then רבי אבא's version from כלה בבית חמיה is challenged too. רב נחמן בר יצחק finally bases the proof on the father keeping the קידושין money, a proof that works according to all views. A ברייתא about a needle found in an animal's stomach then challenges שמואל, and the defense does not fit the ברייתא's general wording. So רמי בר יחזקאל rejects his brother יהודה's report and says שמואל's real rule is: whoever had possession when the doubt arose must bring proof. The needle ברייתא is challenged against this rule as well, and it is explained as a butcher who already paid, since normally no one hands over an animal before payment. The amud ends by turning to the חכמים's view that the משנה's rule applies only to hidden blemishes, introducing a statement of רב נחמן.

Opening lines

כַּלָּה בְּבֵית אָבִיהָ, מִי דָּמֵי?

A bride in her father's house — is it comparable?

הָתָם — מַיְיתֵי אָב רְאָיָה וּמַפֵּיק,

There, the father brings proof and takes out money,
