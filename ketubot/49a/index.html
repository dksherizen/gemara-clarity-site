Kesubos 49a

Ketubot 49a

Topic

Once a father hands his daughter over to the husband's messengers, she leaves her father's authority for good. Even if she later goes back to his house, she does not return to her old status.

Overview

The גמרא asks whether a girl whose father handed her to the husband's שלוחים goes back to her old status if she returns to her father's house. רבא answers from a ברייתא of the school of רבי ישמעאל about the vows of an אלמנה and גרושה: once she leaves her father's רשות even for a moment, he can never cancel her vows again. רב פפא brings support from a משנה that a נערה המאורסה gets סקילה only while she is 'in her father's house'. רב נחמן בר יצחק brings another משנה: once she enters the husband's רשות for marriage, she counts as an אשת איש and the punishment is חנק. Then a new משנה begins. It rules that a father is not obligated to support his daughter, based on רבי אלעזר בן עזריה's teaching in the vineyard at יבנה. The גמרא infers that sons are an obligation and daughters a מצוה, then finds that this fits none of the three opinions in a ברייתא: רבי מאיר, רבי יהודה and רבי יוחנן בן ברוקא. It answers that the משנה can fit any of them. It begins with רבי מאיר, and that explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְאֵימָא: הֵיכָא דְּהָדְרָא לְבֵי נָשָׁא

But say: where she returns to her family's house

הָדְרָא לְמִילְּתָא קַמַּיְיתָא?

she returns to the original situation?
