Topic

May a groom have first relations with his בתולה bride on שבת? The גמרא works through the חתן's exemption from קריאת שמע, a dispute of תנאים, and רבי שמעון's rule of דבר שאין מתכוין.

Overview

The גמרא asks whether a groom may have first relations with his בתולה bride on שבת, even though there may be bleeding. רבא reads the ברייתא about 'four nights until מוצאי שבת' as speaking of a groom who already had relations. On that reading it teaches, like שמואל's narrow opening, that later relations are allowed on שבת. רב יוסף objects from the משנה that a חתן is פטור from קריאת שמע through שבת. אביי answers that the exemption comes from the groom's worry, but רבא shows that worry alone does not exempt anyone, since even an אבל is חייב in all the מצוות. רבא then says the question is a dispute of תנאים, shown by two ברייתות about the third night. The same dispute appears openly in another ברייתא, where the חכמים permit first relations on שבת. רבה explains that the חכמים follow רבי שמעון's rule of דבר שאין מתכוין. He adds that this is not פסיק רישיה, because most men are skilled enough to avoid bleeding, and the groomsmen and sheet only guard against hiding blood. Finally, רבי אמי begins a new challenge from a משנה about draining an abscess on שבת, which continues on the next amud.
