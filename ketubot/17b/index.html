Kesubos 17b

כתובות י״ז ע״ב

Ketubot 17b

Topic

Proof that a bride married as a בתולה, and why the משנה about רבי יהושע uses the father's field (חזקה and protest)

Overview

This amud first finishes an earlier discussion: for someone who taught Torah to others, there is no limit to the honor at his funeral. The גמרא then returns to the משנה about a woman who says she married as a בתולה. It gives two explanations of the הינומא. It then explains that roasted grain was proof in יהודה, while in בבל the sign was smearing oil on the heads of the Sages. A widow's wedding had no roasted grain. Next, the גמרא asks why the משנה about רבי יהושע speaks of a field that belonged to the other person's father and not to the other person himself. It answers that only the father's field allows a real middle case: two years of use in the father's lifetime and one in the minor son's, which is no חזקה according to רב הונא. Along the way, the גמרא proves from the משנה of the three lands that a protest made away from the holder still counts, as רבי אבא בר ממל explains it. The amud ends with a question on his answer, which is answered on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֲבָל לְמַאן דְּמַתְנֵי,

But for one who teaches others,

לֵית לֵיהּ שִׁיעוּרָא.

there is no limit for him.

וְאִם יֵשׁ עֵדִים

And if there are witnesses

שֶׁיָּצְתָה בְּהִינוּמָא וְכוּ׳.

that she went out with a hinnuma, etc.

The steps (24)

  14. ResolutionWhy the witnesses clause can't work for 'your own field'

  17. ChallengeDid רב הונא just repeat the משנה?

  19. ChallengeWhy not build a similar split case with the owner himself, who ran away?
  20. ResolutionThe runaway case fails either way: fleeing for his life or fleeing over money
  21. ProofThe source: the 'three lands' for חזקה
  22. ChallengeEither view seems to clash with the משנה
  23. Resolutionרבי אבא בר ממל: the משנה speaks of a time of danger
