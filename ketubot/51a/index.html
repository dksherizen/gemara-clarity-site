Topic

Supporting daughters from land versus movable property, and the court-set conditions of the כתובה

Overview

This amud opens by finishing an argument about dates still on a palm tree. They count as land as long as they still need the tree. Next, רבא tells the people handling an estate to give an orphan boy extra so his sister can be supported. He defends this with a קל וחומר: we would buy the boy a maidservant, so we can certainly support his sister. A ברייתא then brings a מחלוקת. רבי allows support from both land and movable property. רבי שמעון בן אלעזר allows it only from land. The הלכה follows רבי שמעון בן אלעזר, as רבא ruled. A new משנה follows: the minimum כתובה, the guarantee on all of the husband's property, and the duty to redeem a captive wife all apply even if never written, as a תנאי בית דין. A husband may divorce his wife to avoid paying for her healing, but not to avoid redeeming her. The גמרא shows that the משנה's opening fits רבי מאיר, while its field clause seems to fit רבי יהודה, and it starts quoting the source for their argument about a missing guarantee clause.
