Topic

Proving a כתובה of two hundred through witnesses to virgin-wedding customs, guarding against double collection, and explaining those wedding customs

Overview

This amud first finishes רבינא's answer to why we do not simply assume the bride was a virgin, as most brides are. He changes 'every virgin's wedding is talked about' to 'most are', so the missing publicity only weakens the majority, and witnesses can still decide the case. The גמרא then asks about the משנה's witnesses to the הינומא: could she collect with witnesses and later collect again with her כתובה document? רבי אבהו says a שובר (receipt) is written. רב פפא says the משנה is about a place where no כתובה document is written. A second version puts this debate on a ברייתא about a lost, hidden or burned כתובה. That forces a new reading: a woman who says she lost it is treated like one who hid it, and she is paid only if witnesses say it burned. The גמרא adds that where there is no other protection, everyone agrees a שובר is written. It then explains two virgin-wedding customs: the cup of תרומה wine, which shows she is 'first', and the sealed or open barrel of wine. Finally, a new ברייתא begins asking what is said while dancing before the כלה, and that discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְזוֹ, הוֹאִיל וְאֵין לָהּ קוֹל —

And this one, since she has no publicity —

אִיתְּרַע לֵהּ רוּבָּא.

the majority is weakened for her.

אִי כׇּל הַנִּשֵּׂאת בְּתוּלָה יֵשׁ לָהּ קוֹל —

If every woman who marries as a virgin has publicity —

כִּי אֲתוֹ עֵדִים, מַאי הָוֵי?

when witnesses come, what of it?
