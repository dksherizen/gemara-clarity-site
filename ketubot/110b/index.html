Kesubos 110b

כתובות ק״י ע״ב

Ketubot 110b

Learn Kesubos 110b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Kesubos 110b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Forcing a spouse to move (town and city, nicer homes, ארץ ישראל and ירושלים), which currency pays a כתובה, and the value of living in ארץ ישראל.

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה about when a husband may make his wife move. He may not force a move between a small town (עיר) and a big city (כרך) in either direction. רבן שמעון בן גמליאל adds that she may refuse even a move to a nicer home, because any change of routine is hard on the body, as שמואל taught. A new משנה then rules that either spouse may force the family to move up to ארץ ישראל or to ירושלים, but no one may force them to leave. A ברייתא spells out the price of refusing: a wife loses her כתובה, and a husband must divorce her and pay it. Next the גמרא explains which country's money pays a כתובה. רבה says the first תנא holds a כתובה is דרבנן and so always allows the cheaper money, while רבן שמעון בן גמליאל holds it is דאורייתא. A ברייתא about loan documents is clarified along the way. The amud ends with strong praise for living in ארץ ישראל, and then begins the story of רבי זירא hiding from רב יהודה, who held that leaving בבל breaks a מצות עשה. That story continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֲבָל לֹא מֵעִיר לִכְרַךְ,

But not from a town to a big city,

וְלֹא מִכְּרַךְ לְעִיר.

and not from a big city to a town.

מוֹצִיאִין מִנָּוֶה הָרַע(ה) לְנָוֶה הַיָּפֶה,

One may move her from a bad home to a nice home,

אֲבָל לֹא מִנָּוֶה הַיָּפֶה לְנָוֶה הָרַע(ה).

but not from a nice home to a bad home.
