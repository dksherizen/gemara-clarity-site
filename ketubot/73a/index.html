Topic

Why רב requires a גט when a man made קידושין on a condition and then married her without mentioning it, why the רב–שמואל dispute appears in two cases, and a משנה that seems to refute שמואל.

Overview

This amud first pins down רב's real reason for requiring a גט when a man made קידושין on a condition and later married her without mentioning it. The reason is not that he dropped his condition. It is that a person does not want his relations to be בעילת זנות, so he intends them as real קידושין. The גמרא then asks why this dispute needs to be stated, since רב and שמואל already argue about the same idea. That other case is a קטנה who never did מיאון, grew up, and married someone else. The גמרא answers צריכא, in both directions. From the קטנה case alone, we might think רב agrees with שמואל when there is a condition. From the conditional case alone, we might think שמואל agrees with רב when there is no condition. Finally, the גמרא brings a משנה: a woman married without mention of a condition who turns out to have vows leaves without a כתובה. The גמרא infers from this that she still needs a גט, and suggests that this is a strong refutation of שמואל.

Opening lines

לָא תֵּימָא טַעְמֵיהּ דְּרַב

Do not say that Rav's reason

כֵּיוָן שֶׁכְּנָסָהּ סְתָם

is that since he married her without specifying

אַחוֹלֵי אַחֲלֵיהּ לִתְנָאֵיהּ,

he completely waived his condition;

אֶלָּא טַעְמָא דְרַב:
