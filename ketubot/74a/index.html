Topic

Does ביאה after a failed or conditional קידושין create a marriage? Conditions, mistaken חליצה, and the rulings of רבי יוחנן, רבי אלעזר, רבי אמי and עולא

Overview

This amud asks what happens when a man's קידושין did not work and he then lived with the woman: does the later ביאה make a real marriage? The גמרא first defends רבה's rule that, when only one woman is involved, the man relies on his first קידושין. It shows that the argument in the ברייתא about a minor's קידושין is only about whether people know such קידושין mean nothing. רבי יוחנן is quoted supporting רבה: after conditional קידושין and ביאה, she needs no גט. This is challenged from the rule that a 'mistaken חליצה' is valid. רבי יוחנן defines that as חליצה on condition that she pays 200 זוז, and the challenge argues that doing the act cancels the condition. The answer is that a condition only works on an act a שליח could do, as with בני גד ובני ראובן. קידושין through ביאה is an exception, because all ways of קידושין are linked together. Next, רבי אלעזר, רבי אמי and עולא give different rulings on when ביאה after a failed קידושין requires a גט. The amud ends by quoting רבי ישמעאל, whom עולא rejects: a woman with a mistaken קידושין is never married, even after having a child. His statement continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מִשּׁוּם רַבִּי אֱלִיעֶזֶר אָמְרוּ:

In the name of Rabbi Eliezer they said:

חוֹלֶצֶת וְלֹא מִתְיַיבֶּמֶת.

She performs chalitza but does not enter levirate marriage.

וְהָא הָכָא, דִּכְטָעוּת אִשָּׁה אַחַת דָּמֵי,

But here, it is like an error concerning one woman,

וּפְלִיגִי!

and yet they disagree!
