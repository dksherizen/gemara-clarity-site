Topic

The כתובה of a מוכת עץ and of a woman found not to be a בתולה: רב and שמואל, רבי מאיר and the רבנן, and רבא's retraction

Overview

The amud finishes the משנה about women who come into marriage already presumed to be non-virgins. These women get a כתובה of a מנה, and their husband has no טענת בתולים against them. The גמרא then brings a dispute between רב and שמואל about a minor boy who has relations with an adult woman. רב says this makes her a מוכת עץ, and שמואל says there is no מוכת עץ through flesh. Answering רב אושעיא's challenge, רבא shows that the case of מוכת עץ is itself a dispute between רבי מאיר and the רבנן. רמי בר חמא and then רבא try to explain when a מוכת עץ gets 200, a מנה, or nothing, depending on whether the husband knew about her. Along the way, רב ששת rules that a woman found to be a בעולה gets a מנה, and רב נחמן objects that a מקח טעות means she gets nothing. רבא agrees with רב נחמן and rereads a ברייתא so that a מוכת עץ gets a מנה. From that reading the גמרא concludes that רבא retracted his earlier ruling. The amud ends with a new ברייתא: once a first husband brought a woman in for marriage, a second husband cannot make a טענת בתולים.
