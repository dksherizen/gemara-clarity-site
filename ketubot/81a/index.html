Topic

Whether the יבם, as heir of the כתובה, must bury a שומרת יבם, and whether a יבמה's כתובה can count as collectible while she is bound to the יבם.

Overview

This amud asks who must pay to bury a שומרת יבם, a widow waiting for יבום. The rule is that the heirs of her כתובה bury her. אביי supports this from a משנה that speaks of a widow with two sets of heirs, which can only be a שומרת יבם. רבא asks why the יבם can't say, 'I inherit my brother, but burying his wife is not my job.' אביי answers that he is pressed from two sides: either bury her or pay her כתובה. רבא replies that a כתובה is not collected during the husband's lifetime, and the יבם stands in for the husband. The גמרא argues that this idea comes from בית שמאי, who also hold that a document ready to be collected counts as already collected, and proves it from the משנה about a סוטה whose husband died. The גמרא then cites the plain wording 'when you marry another', and רב אשי answers that the יבם also counts as 'another man.' Finally רבא sends אביי a new challenge from סומכוס's teaching: a יבם who wants to sell his brother's property must make a feast to win his wife over, or divorce her and remarry her.

Opening lines

יוֹרְשֶׁיהָ יוֹרְשֵׁי כְּתוּבָּתָהּ

Her heirs, the heirs of her marriage contract,

חַיָּיבִין בִּקְבוּרָתָהּ.

are obligated in her burial.

אָמַר אַבָּיֵי, אַף אֲנַן נָמֵי תְּנֵינָא:

Abaye said: We too have learned:

אַלְמָנָה נִיזּוֹנֶת מִנִּכְסֵי יְתוֹמִין,

A widow is supported from the property of the orphans,
