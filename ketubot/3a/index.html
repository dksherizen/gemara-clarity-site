Topic

Whether אונס counts in a conditional גט, how רבנן can cancel קידושין, and when the Wednesday wedding rule applies

Overview

The גמרא finishes explaining רבא's first version, that אונס does not cancel a conditional גט. One explanation is the worry about צנועות, who could wrongly stay stuck. The other is the worry about פרוצות, who could wrongly remarry. The גמרא asks how רבנן can free a woman whom the תורה would keep married. It answers that every man marries on the terms of רבנן, so they can cancel the קידושין, and if the קידושין were made through ביאה, they make it בעילת זנות. A second version of רבא says the opposite: אונס does count in גיטין. The גמרא tests this version against a משנה about 'if I don't return within twelve months' and against שמואל's case of the man stopped by the ferry. Each challenge is answered: death is a special case, and a common אונס is the husband's own fault for not planning. Finally, רב שמואל בר יצחק explains that the Wednesday wedding rule depends on courts sitting on Monday and Thursday. Where courts sit every day and the groom has already prepared the feast, a woman may marry on any day.

Opening lines

זִימְנִין דְּלָא אֲנִיס

Sometimes he was not held back by forces beyond his control

וְסָבְרָה דַּאֲנִיס,

but she thinks that he was held back,

וּמִיעַגְּנָא וְיָתְבָה.

so she stays stuck and sits as an עגונה.

וּמִשּׁוּם פְּרוּצוֹת —

And because of careless women —
