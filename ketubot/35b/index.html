Topic

When מלקות or מיתה cancels a payment, which תנא the קנס משנה follows, and a ברייתא about women who get no fine

Overview

The גמרא finishes defending the source that someone liable for מלקות does not also pay. It does this by describing a case where he hit a person and tore his silk in the same act. רב חייא then asks how the תנא דבי חזקיה knows the פסוק about striking speaks of a weekday and not שבת. The גמרא proves it from the פסוק itself: the striker was warned, yet he still pays for the animal. Next, רב פפא asks אביי which תנא our משנה follows according to רבה, who says a קנס is paid even by someone who gets מיתה. רבי מאיר, רבי נחוניא בן הקנה and רבי יצחק each run into a problem. The גמרא concludes that רבה must hold like רבי יוחנן, who says that without a warning the man pays. רב מתנא then asks who argues with רבי נחוניא according to ריש לקיש, and the answer is רבי מאיר or רבי יצחק. Finally, a ברייתא lists women who get no fine at all. The גמרא begins asking what 'עריות' means there, and that question continues on the next amud.
