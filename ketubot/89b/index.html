Topic

Proving a כתובה claim with a גט, death witnesses, or several documents, and preventing a woman from collecting twice

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the explanation of why a woman with a כתובה but no גט cannot collect: the husband can say he already paid her. The גמרא then rereads the whole משנה as the view of רבן שמעון בן גמליאל. With no witnesses to the divorce she collects nothing. With witnesses she collects the תוספת, but the main sum only with a גט, and from the time of danger onward even without a גט. רב כהנא and רב אסי challenge רב's view that a גט collects the main sum: a widow might collect once with עדי מיתה and again with a hidden גט. The גמרא concludes that wherever there is no other protection, the court writes a שובר. מר קשישא then asks for the source that a widow from אירוסין gets a כתובה, and every proof is pushed off with 'maybe he wrote her one.' רב נחמן raises the worry that one גט could be used to collect in many courts. רב הונא answers that the court tears it and writes the reason on its back. Finally, a new משנה explains when a woman holding several גיטין and כתובות collects one or two כתובות, and the גמרא reconciles it with רב נחמן's rule that a second document cancels the first.

Opening lines

יָכוֹל לְמֵימַר:

He can say:

גֵּירַשְׁתִּיהָ וְנָתַתִּי לָהּ כְּתוּבָּתָהּ.

I divorced her and gave her her marriage contract payment.
