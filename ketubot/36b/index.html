Topic

Virginity claims, a bad reputation and רב פפא's tainted document, and the משנה of girls who get no קנס, including רבי יהודה's view on a redeemed captive

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an answer about a בוגרת: a 'no blood' claim works against her, but an 'open entrance' claim does not. Next, סומכוס rules that a blind girl has no virginity claim against her. She may have been hurt in a fall without seeing it and telling her mother. רב ששת explains that a girl with a bad reputation from her youth gets no קנס. From this, רב פפא learns that a tainted document cannot collect a debt. The tainted document is one whose owner was caught by two witnesses trying to get it forged. A new משנה then lists girls who get no קנס: a convert, a captive or a maidservant freed after age three, and close relatives whose relations carry a court death penalty. The גמרא discusses רבי יהודה's view that a redeemed captive keeps her status, with רבה and אביי debating whether רבי דוסא truly agrees with him. Two answers, from רב פפא and רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע, explain an apparent contradiction in רבי יהודה's rulings about marrying a captive. A new contradiction from רב פפא בר שמואל begins at the very end and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אִי דְּקָא טָעֵין טַעֲנַת דָּמִים —

If he makes a claim about blood (that there was none),

הָכִי נָמֵי.

indeed, so it is (he has a valid claim).

הָכָא בְּמַאי עָסְקִינַן —

Here, with what are we dealing?

דְּקָטָעֵין טַעֲנַת פֶּתַח פָּתוּחַ.

With one who claims he found an 'open entrance.'
