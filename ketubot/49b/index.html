Topic

Feeding one's children: readings of the משנה, the תקנות of אושא, and how they were enforced

Overview

The גמרא first finishes matching our משנה ('a father is not obligated to feed his daughter') to רבי מאיר. It then offers two more readings, by רבי יהודה and by רבי יוחנן בן ברוקא. Next it brings a תקנה made in אושא: a man must feed his young sons and daughters. Stories about רב יהודה, רב חסדא and רבא show that judges only shamed such a father and did not force him. The exception is a wealthy father, whom the court does force, just as רבא forced רב נתן בר אמי to give charity. A second אושא rule follows: a man who gives all his property to his sons is still fed from it, together with his wife. The גמרא asks why that rule was needed when a widow is fed even after the property passed to the daughter's husband. It answers that a living husband might have been told to go work. Finally the גמרא asks whether the הלכה follows this rule and begins a story about רבי יונתן, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דַּאֲפִילּוּ בִּתּוֹ,

That even his daughter,

חוֹבָה הוּא דְּלֵיכָּא,

it is an obligation that there is not,

הָא מִצְוָה אִיכָּא.

but a מצוה there is.

וְאִיבָּעֵית אֵימָא רַבִּי יְהוּדָה,

And if you wish, say it is Rabbi Yehuda,
