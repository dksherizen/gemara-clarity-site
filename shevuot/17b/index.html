Topic

A טמא in the מקדש, speeding up עבודה, entering in an unusual way, and the עשה of נדה with its two חטאות.

Overview

The גמרא first asks how a טמא could be liable for staying in the מקדש if he left by the shortest way. אביי answers that on his way out he turned over a limb on the מזבח with a fork, which counts as עבודה, like רב הונא's rule about a זר. Examining רב הונא's rule, the גמרא concludes that anything that speeds up an עבודה is itself an עבודה. Next, רבי אושעיא teaches that someone who enters a בית המנוגע backward stays טהור unless his whole body is inside, because the Torah only counts the normal way of entering. רבא explains that a person fully inside is טמא like the vessels in the house, and a ברייתא about a טמא entering the היכל through the roofs supports the rule. The גמרא then shows that our משנה's words 'זו היא' point back to a משנה in הוריות contrasting the מקדש with נדה. The example for נדה is a man who withdraws immediately, and אמוראים rule he brings two חטאות. רבה begins asking what case this could be, and his search continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא אִי אָמְרַתְּ

But if you say

דַּוְקָא גְּמִירִי,

that it is learned specifically as a tradition,

הֵיכִי מַשְׁכַּחַתְּ לַהּ?

how can you find it?

אָמַר אַבָּיֵי: מַאי קוּשְׁיָא?

Abaye said: What is the difficulty?
