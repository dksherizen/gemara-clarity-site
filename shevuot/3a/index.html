Topic

How שבועות follows מכות, why its opening משנה lists every 'two that are four' set, what each set means, and whether the משנה fits רבי ישמעאל or רבי עקיבא

Overview

The גמרא explains that מסכת שבועות follows מכות, which ended with one prohibition that brings two liabilities, like the edges of the beard. So שבועות opens with a similar pattern: 'two that are four.' The גמרא asks why our משנה lists every 'two that are four' set, while the openings of שבת and נגעים list only their own. The answer is that שבועות and ידיעות הטומאה sit side by side in the תורה and share the קרבן עולה ויורד. Once two sets were listed, the משנה listed them all. The short topic of ידיעות הטומאה is explained first, and the long topic of שבועות comes after. The גמרא then defines each set of four: oaths, awareness of טומאה, carrying on שבת, and shades of נגעים. Next it asks whose view the משנה follows, since רבי ישמעאל and רבי עקיבא each reject liability in one of the lists. It answers that each list includes both liable and exempt cases. The גמרא begins to question that answer, and the challenge continues on the next amud.

And for the beard

two from here and two from there

וְאַחַת מִלְּמַטָּה;

and one from below;

חֲדָא דְּמִיחַיַּיב עֲלַהּ תַּרְתֵּי;

one for which he is liable two;
