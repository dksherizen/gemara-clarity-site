Topic

רבי's method of reading פסוקים and the שבועות ruling, the sources for ידיעה בתחילה ובסוף, and why our משנה counts the carrying cases of שבת as 'two that are four.'

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a question: why does רבי read the firstborn פסוקים as ריבוי ומיעוט when he usually uses כלל ופרט? The answer is that two general terms placed before the detail make the passage ריבוי ומיעוט, as the school of רבי ישמעאל taught about במים. The רבנן instead imagine the detail between the two general terms and read the passage as כלל ופרט. Since רבי normally uses כלל ופרט, the גמרא concludes that he wrote the שבועות ruling following רבי עקיבא. Next the גמרא studies the ברייתא in which רבי עקיבא and רבי disagree about how to learn ידיעה בתחילה and ידיעה בסוף from ״ונעלם״. רבא explains the wording, אביי rejects his explanation from סוטה and from a פסוק about the תורה, and אביי concludes that רבי counts ידיעת בית רבו as knowing. Finally, the גמרא asks why our משנה lists the carrying cases of שבת as only 'two that are four.' After one answer is rejected, רב פפא and רב אשי explain that the four are liable cases of carrying out and bringing in. The proof for רב אשי's claim continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

רִיבּוּיֵי וּמִיעוּטֵי.

amplifications and restrictions?

אִין, בְּעָלְמָא כְּלָלֵי וּפְרָטֵי דָּרֵישׁ;

Yes, in general he expounds generalizations and details;

וְהָכָא הַיְינוּ טַעְמָא –

but here, this is the reason:

כִּדְתָנָא דְּבֵי רַבִּי יִשְׁמָעֵאל.

as the school of רבי ישמעאל taught.
