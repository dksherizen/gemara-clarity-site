Topic

Finding the source that the קרבן עולה ויורד for טומאה covers קדשים and the מקדש but not תרומה

Overview

This amud asks where we learn that the קרבן עולה ויורד for טומאה covers eating holy food (קדשים) and entering the מקדש while טמא, and not eating תרומה while טמא. The גמרא first notes that a sin without כרת can still bring this קרבן, so it tries to exclude תרומה with the word ״בה״. When ״בה״ could just as well exclude the מקדש, רבא praises רבי's deep reading and quotes his ברייתא. That ברייתא is a גזירה שוה on ״בהמה טמאה״ showing the פסוק is about טומאת קודש. A היקש then links the מקדש to קודש, but that would bring in תרומה too, and the reasoning over which case ״בה״ excludes cuts both ways. רבא tries to use the three mentions of כרת by שלמים, and the חכמי נהרדעי try the three mentions of טומאה. Both are rejected because those mentions are already needed for רבי אבהו (or, for רבי שמעון, the חטאת הפנימית). Finally רבא begins a new גזירה שוה on the word ״טומאתו״, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

עוֹלֶה וְיוֹרֵד נַיְתֵי –

Let him bring a sliding-scale offering –

מִידֵּי דְּהָוֵה אַשְּׁמִיעַת קוֹל

just as it is with 'hearing the voice'

וְאַבִּיטּוּי שְׂפָתַיִם!

and with 'utterance of lips'!

אָמַר קְרָא: ״בָּהּ״;

The verse says: 'through which';
