Topic

שבועת ביטוי: future vs. past oaths, and which kinds of forgetting bring a קרבן

Overview

The amud opens by noting that one of the earlier statements was really said by רב פפא. It then asks whether a שבועת ביטוי covers only oaths about the future, as רבי ישמעאל says, or also oaths about the past, as רבי עקיבא says. רבי יוחנן explains that they read the פסוק (ויקרא 5:4) with different methods learned from their teachers. רבי עקיבא uses ריבה ומיעט, so almost everything is included except a דבר מצוה. רבי ישמעאל uses כלל ופרט וכלל, so only future oaths count. רבי יצחק and רב יצחק בר אבין each explain why the 'future' feature of the detail is the one that matters. A ברייתא then teaches that the קרבן is not brought by an אונס, shown by the story of רב כהנא and רב אסי. Nor is it brought by a מזיד, or by someone who forgot only the item rather than the oath. The rest of the amud asks whether forgetting the item without forgetting the oath can even happen. רב יוסף and אביי debate the bread-basket case. רב אשי's reminder test for someone who forgot both is rejected by רבינא, and the גמרא concludes that there is no difference. A new question from רבא to רב נחמן begins and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

חֲדָא מִינַּיְיהוּ

One of them (those statements)

רַב פָּפָּא אַמְרַהּ.

Rav Pappa said it.

רַבִּי יִשְׁמָעֵאל אוֹמֵר:

Rabbi Yishmael says:

אֵינוֹ חַיָּיב אֶלָּא עַל הֶעָתִיד לָבֹא.

He is liable only for (an oath about) the future.
