Topic

How רבי מאיר's view of 'מכלל לאו אתה שומע הן' is resolved, and the new משנה on שבועת הפקדון: who is liable, how many קרבנות, and the rule that a קנס brings no liability

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the question of whether רבי מאיר accepts the rule 'מכלל לאו אתה שומע הן', inferring a positive from a negative. The גמרא first reverses the opinions in the משנה and says רבי מאיר rejects the rule even in איסור. רבינא attacks this from the drunk and long-haired כהנים, whose death penalty is learned exactly this way. The final answer keeps the reversal: רבי מאיר rejects the rule only in money matters, and סוטה counts as an איסור that involves money. After the הדרן for פרק שבועת העדות, a new משנה begins about שבועת הפקדון, a false oath denying someone's money or property. It explains who is bound by this oath, the argument between רבי מאיר and חכמים about needing a בית דין, which states of mind bring an אשם, and how many קרבנות are owed for repeated oaths, several claimants, or several items. It ends with cases of a קנס and the general rule: a person is liable only if his own admission would have made him pay. The גמרא then opens with a story about three students in רבה's study hall, and רב כהנא's question continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא אֵיפוֹךְ –

Rather, reverse it:

אֲפִילּוּ בְּאִיסּוּרָא לֵית לֵיהּ.

even in ritual matters he does not accept it.

מַתְקֵיף לֵיהּ רָבִינָא:

Ravina objected to it:

וּבְאִיסּוּרָא לֵית לֵיהּ?!

And in ritual matters he does not accept it?!
