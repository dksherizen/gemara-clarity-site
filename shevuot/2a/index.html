Topic

The opening משנה of מסכת שבועות: the 'two that are four' list, and which קרבנות atone for entering the מקדש or eating קדשים while טמא, depending on what the person knew.

Overview

The מסכת opens with a list built on one repeating pattern: 'two that are four.' In four topics (שבועות, ידיעות הטומאה, יציאות השבת, and מראות נגעים), the תורה states two cases, and חז"ל show there are really four. The משנה then goes back to the case of a טמא person who entered the מקדש or ate קדשים. It explains what atones for him, depending on whether he knew about the sin before it happened, after it happened, both, or neither. If he knew both before and after, he brings a קרבן עולה ויורד. If he knew only before, the inner שעיר of יום הכיפורים holds off his punishment until he finds out. If he knew only after, the outer שעיר of יום הכיפורים atones, and a פסוק links the two goats to prove this. If he never knew at all, רבי יהודה says the goats of the רגלים and of ראש חודש atone, while רבי שמעון says only the רגלים goats do. The amud ends by asking what the ראש חודש goats atone for according to רבי שמעון, and the answer comes on the next amud.
