Topic

שבועת העדות: who it applies to; the source that witnesses are men; fair treatment of litigants in court

Overview

This amud opens the פרק of שבועת העדות. The משנה teaches that the oath only applies to people fit to testify: men and not women, non-relatives and not relatives, and כשרים and not פסולים. רבי מאיר and חכמים argue about whether a denial outside בית דין makes the witnesses liable. Witnesses who knew the testimony and swore falsely bring a קרבן עולה ויורד. The גמרא asks for the source that women do not testify. It brings three ברייתות that read ״ועמדו שני האנשים״ as speaking about עדים. Each ברייתא gives its own first proof and backs it up with a גזירה שוה from ״שני עדים״. Next, a ברייתא learns from the same פסוק that בעלי דינין should stand, and רבי יהודה says the real rule is equal treatment. ״בצדק תשפוט עמיתך״ teaches equal treatment in court and judging others לכף זכות. רב יוסף adds that a fellow in תורה and מצוות should be judged 'nicely.' The story of רב עולא's case before רב נחמן shows this means hearing his case first, not favoring him.

MISHNA: The oath of testimony

נוֹהֶגֶת בַּאֲנָשִׁים וְלֹא בְּנָשִׁים,

applies to men but not to women,

בִּרְחוֹקִין וְלֹא בִּקְרוֹבִין,

to non-relatives but not to relatives,

בִּכְשֵׁרִין וְלֹא בִּפְסוּלִין;

to those who are fit but not to those who are disqualified;
