Topic

Whether הוצאה includes bringing in, and whether the list of נגעים shades fits רבי עקיבא

Overview

The גמרא first shows that a תנא can use the word הוצאה, 'carrying out,' even for bringing an item into a private domain. It proves this from the משנה 'המוציא מרשות לרשות חייב,' whose general wording covers both directions. It then explains why: any removal of an item from its place counts as הוצאה. רבינא adds support from the opening משנה of שבת, which calls all the cases יציאות but then lists cases of bringing in. רבא rejects that proof, because he reads that משנה as counting the two domains of שבת. The גמרא then turns to the next line of our משנה, the shades of נגעים, and quotes the משנה in נגעים listing four shades of white. רבי חנינא says that list does not follow רבי עקיבא, because under רבי עקיבא's ranking the lime shade could not combine with any other shade. The amud ends with a challenge: the egg membrane shade has the same problem, so why didn't רבי חנינא mention it too?

Opening lines

דִּתְנַן: הַמּוֹצִיא מֵרְשׁוּת לִרְשׁוּת חַיָּיב.

As we learned: One who carries out from domain to domain is liable.

מִי לָא עָסְקִינַן

Are we not dealing

דְּקָא מְעַיֵּיל עַיּוֹלֵי –

with a case where he is actually bringing in –

וְקָא קָרֵי לֵיהּ ״הוֹצָאָה״?

and still it calls it 'carrying out'?

The steps (15)

  1. ProofProof from a משנה: carrying from one domain to another
  2. ChallengeMaybe the משנה only means carrying out
  3. ResolutionThe general wording must include bringing in
  4. QuestionWhy call bringing in 'carrying out'?
  5. ResponseAny removal of an item from its place is called carrying out
  6. Proofרבינא: the opening משנה of שבת shows this too
  7. ConclusionConclusion: carrying out includes bringing in
  8. Rejectionרבא: the משנה is counting domains, not acts of carrying
  9. StatementQuoting the next line of our משנה: shades of leprous marks
  10. StatementThe משנה in נגעים lists the four shades
  11. Questionרבי חנינא asks who taught this list
  12. ResponseThe list does not follow רבי עקיבא
  13. ProofWhy not רבי עקיבא: the lime shade could not combine
  14. ProofThe lime shade has no partner to combine with
  15. ChallengeWhy not bring the same proof from the egg membrane shade?
