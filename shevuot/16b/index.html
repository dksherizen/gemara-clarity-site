Topic

Liability for becoming טמא inside the עזרה: the source from משכן and מקדש, and the rules of bowing and staying

Overview

The משנה says a person who became טמא while already inside the עזרה can be liable. רבי אלעזר learns this from two פסוקים, one saying 'משכן ה׳' and one saying 'מקדש ה׳'. A ברייתא seems to use up both words for other lessons, so the גמרא explains that the lesson comes from the switch between the two names. Since each building can be called by either name, the תורה could have used one word twice. The גמרא then proves from שמות 25:8-9 that the משכן is also called 'מקדש'. Next, רבא explains the משנה's rule about bowing: bowing toward the קודש הקדשים makes a person liable right away, but bowing outward counts only if he stayed long enough to bow. The גמרא defines the long bow as lying fully spread out and measures it by the time it takes to say a פסוק from דברי הימים. A ברייתא then defines קידה, כריעה and השתחוואה. Finally, רבא asks whether this staying is also needed for מלקות, and that question continues on the next amud.
