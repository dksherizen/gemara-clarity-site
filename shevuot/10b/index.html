Topic

רבי שמעון's ladder of atonement for the goats of ראש חודש, the רגלים, and יום הכפורים, followed by רבי יוחנן's ruling that unneeded תמיד lambs may be redeemed while unblemished.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a proof that the outer goat of יום הכפורים is compared by a היקש to the inner goat. Then the גמרא turns to רבי שמעון בן יהודה's version of רבי שמעון's view in the משנה. In this version the goats of ראש חודש, the רגלים, and יום הכפורים form a ladder, and each covers more kinds of sin than the one below. Twice the גמרא asks why the lower goat cannot cover what the higher one covers, and each time it answers from a word in a פסוק. Each time it then challenges that the higher goat should not cover the lower one's sins either. In the second round the answer that רבי שמעון ignores 'אחת' is examined. In the end the גמרא concludes that he does learn from 'אחת', and only 'על קרנותיו' limits the inner goat to one atonement. A new topic follows: עולא quotes רבי יוחנן that unneeded תמיד lambs may be redeemed while unblemished. רב חסדא objects that their holiness cannot just disappear. רבה answers with a proof from leftover קטורת. רב חסדא replies that קטורת is different, and his reason continues on the next amud.
