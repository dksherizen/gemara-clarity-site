Topic

What each goat of ראש חודש, the רגלים and יום הכיפורים atones for, and the sources for the views of רבי מאיר and רבי שמעון

Overview

This amud explains which sins the חטאת goats of ראש חודש, the רגלים and יום הכיפורים atone for. First the גמרא finishes explaining the view in which each goat has its own job. The festival goats are linked to the ראש חודש goats, so they atone for a matter of קודש. They cannot take over another goat's case, and every case with awareness is already covered, so they must atone for טומאה with no awareness at all. Next the גמרא looks for רבי מאיר's source that all the goats atone alike. It tries the extra ו of 'ושעיר', and then the פסוק 'אלה תעשו לה׳ במועדיכם' from רבי יונה. It also shows that ראש חודש is called a מועד. רבי יוחנן adds that even רבי מאיר keeps the inner goat of יום הכיפורים separate, and a ברייתא supports this. Finally the גמרא asks how רבי שמעון knows that each goat's job is locked in both directions, using the words 'עון', 'אותה' and 'אחת'. The amud ends as the גמרא begins answering whether 'אחת', written about the inner goat, can teach about the outer one. That answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְאִיתַּקּוּשׁ שְׂעִירֵי הָרְגָלִים לִשְׂעִירֵי רָאשֵׁי חֳדָשִׁים;

And the goats of the festivals are compared to the goats of the New Moons;

מָה שָׂעִיר דְּרֹאשׁ חוֹדֶשׁ

just as the goat of the New Moon

בְּמִילְּתָא דְּקוֹדֶשׁ מְכַפְּרִי,

atones for a matter involving holy things,

אַף שְׂעִירֵי רְגָלִים

so too the goats of the festivals
