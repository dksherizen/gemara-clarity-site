Topic

Two חטאות for entering and withdrawing from a woman who becomes a נדה, and whether limp relations with עריות bring liability.

Overview

רבא looks for a case where a man brings two חטאות when a woman becomes a נדה during relations: one for entering and one for withdrawing. The near-the-וסת case does not work for an עם הארץ, and the not-near-the-וסת case does not work for anyone. So רבא settles on a man who knows one הלכה but not the other. רבא shows that both liabilities are taught in משניות: withdrawal in our משנה, and entry in the משנה in נדה about blood found on his cloth. רב אדא בר מתנא reads that נדה משנה as a withdrawal case instead. רבינא challenges him from the word 'found', and רבא backs רבינא. רב אדא brings a ברייתא calling it a מצות עשה, and רבא answers that words are missing from it. The גמרא then asks how the man should withdraw: he should stay still until he is limp. רבא learns from this that limp relations with עריות are exempt. אביי disagrees, and רבה בר חנן challenges אביי's view.

And if it is with an ignorant person,

this and that (entry and withdrawal)

is like 'he ate two olive-sizes of forbidden fat in one lapse of awareness'!
