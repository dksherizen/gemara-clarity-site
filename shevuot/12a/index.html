Topic

The source for לב בית דין מתנה, what רבי שמעון does with leftover תמיד lambs, and the גזירה against offering a former חטאת as an עולה

Overview

The גמרא is looking for a תנא who holds that the בית דין silently sets conditions on הקדש even for something that rarely happens. It tries three sources: the רבנן of קטורת, the רבנן of פרה אדומה, and the speakers of "אמרו לו" in the משנה. Each one is pushed aside. Incense cannot be left to graze, a פרה is very expensive, and the "אמרו לו" speaker might be רבי מאיר, who allows swapping the goats for a different reason. So the גמרא concludes that רבי יוחנן simply learned the dispute as a tradition. רבי יוחנן then explains that for רבי שמעון the leftover תמיד lambs are offered as filler offerings for the מזבח. רבי שמואל בר רב יצחק adds that leftover חטאת goats are not offered themselves; only their value is used, because of a גזירה made after atonement on account of before atonement. אביי, רבא and רבינא each bring a source supporting this גזירה, and the גמרא accepts it. Finally, a ברייתא is opened that asks what is brought from the leftover lambs, and it continues on the next amud.
