Topic

Contradicting new-moon witnesses, the storekeeper and money changer cases, and רב and שמואל's rule that a person cannot pass an oath down to his heirs, with the challenges to it.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a challenge to רב חסדא from a ברייתא about witnesses who disagree on the new moon's height. רבא explains that 'joining another testimony' means only another ראש חודש testimony, where it becomes two against one. Next the גמרא returns to the משנה's customer cases: רבי יהודה limits the oath to when the produce is still lying there, and the גמרא shows why the storekeeper case and the money changer case are both needed. Then the גמרא explains that orphans need an oath when they collect a debt from the borrower's orphans. רב and שמואל add that if the borrower died first, the lender's heirs cannot collect at all, because a person cannot pass an oath down to his children. רבי אלעזר disagrees, and רבי אמי tries to split the cases by whether the lender already stood in court. רב נחמן rejects that split and says the rule is all-or-nothing, a statement made within רבי מאיר's view. The rest of the amud brings משניות about a widow's heirs, a second wife's heirs, and a husband's heirs, all answered in line with רב and שמואל. A new challenge from רב נתן בר הושעיא begins at the end and continues on the next amud.
