Topic

שבועת שקר vs. שבועת שוא: which לאו each breaks, and when swearing gets lashes

Overview

This amud first finishes explaining a ברייתא that called שבועת שוא and שבועת שקר 'one,' and gives a second answer: both bring a קרבן, following רבי עקיבא. A ברייתא then seems to define שבועת שקר differently from רבי יוחנן, and the גמרא answers by reading it as 'he swears and later goes against it.' Next, רבין reports in רבי יוחנן's name that a false oath about the past breaks 'לא תשבעו בשמי לשקר,' a broken future oath breaks 'לא יחל דברו,' and a שבועת שוא is swearing against a known fact. רב פפא says רבי אבהו only worked this out by inference. The starting point is a rule that there are no lashes for a לאו without an action, except for swearing and two other cases. The גמרא finds the source for lashes for swearing in 'כי לא ינקה ה׳,' and for שבועת שקר in the second 'לשוא.' רבי אבהו shows that the false oath that gets lashes must be about the past, and רבא explains that this is because it is modeled on שבועת שוא. Finally, רבי ירמיה challenges this from a משנה, and the גמרא answers that the משנה follows רבי ישמעאל, who excludes past-tense oaths only from the קרבן, not from lashes.

just as one brings an offering for a false oath,
