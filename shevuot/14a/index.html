Topic

How the כהנים get atonement on יום הכפורים and the two confessions of the פר, then the new משנה on the four kinds of forgotten awareness of טומאה and the holiness of the added עזרה.

Overview

The ברייתא finishes explaining how the כהנים are forgiven for טומאת מקדש וקדשיו on יום הכפורים. It rules that the פר of אהרן covers them, not the inner שעיר, and backs this up with the פסוק 'בית אהרן'. The גמרא then defends the ברייתא's sources. 'אשר לעם' is free, because public funding is learned from another פסוק. The double 'אשר לו' is really about the כהן גדול paying from his own money, and the ברייתא was solving a logic problem about who pays. Next the גמרא returns to the מחלוקת of רבי יהודה and רבי שמעון about the שעיר המשתלח. It asks why, according to רבי יהודה, the bull needs two confessions. The answer is that the כהן גדול first atones for himself and then for his household, since an innocent person should atone for the guilty. After the הדרן closing פרק שבועות שתים, a new משנה lists the four kinds of forgotten awareness for eating קודש or entering the מקדש while טמא, which require a קרבן עולה ויורד. It adds that an addition to the עזרה is fully holy, because it is made through a king, a נביא, the אורים ותומים, a סנהדרין of 71, and a formal procession.

Opening lines

בְּטוּמְאַת מִקְדָּשׁ וְקָדָשָׁיו.

for defiling the Temple and its sacred foods.

בַּמָּה הֵם מִתְכַּפְּרִין?

Through what do they get atonement?

מוּטָב שֶׁיִּתְכַּפְּרוּ בְּפָרוֹ שֶׁל אַהֲרֹן,

It is better that they get atonement through Aaron's bull,

שֶׁהֲרֵי הוּתַּר מִכְּלָלוֹ אֵצֶל בֵּיתוֹ;

since its rule was already widened for his household;
