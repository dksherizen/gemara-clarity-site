Topic

What the יום הכיפורים and other goat offerings atone for, how the כהנים are atoned for, and why מסכת שבועות follows מכות.

Overview

This amud finishes the first משנה of שבועות, which explains what atones for each kind of sin. רבי מאיר says all the extra goat offerings atone for the same thing: טומאת מקדש וקדשיו. רבי שמעון gives each goat its own case, from ראש חודש through the רגלים to יום הכיפורים. The חכמים ask whether a goat set aside for one day may be brought on another. He says yes, because all of them come to atone for טומאת מקדש וקדשיו. רבי שמעון בן יהודה reports a second version of רבי שמעון's view, in which each set of goats covers more cases than the one before. The same question and answer then repeat for that version. The משנה then rules that deliberate טומאת מקדש וקדשיו is atoned for by the inside goat and the day of יום הכיפורים, and that the שעיר המשתלח atones for all other sins. The תנא קמא and רבי שמעון disagree about how far the כהן גדול's פר does for the כהנים what the goats do for Israel. Finally the גמרא opens by asking why שבועות follows מכות, and begins an answer that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

עַל הַטָּהוֹר שֶׁאָכַל אֶת הַטָּמֵא.

For a pure person who ate impure food.

רַבִּי מֵאִיר אוֹמֵר:

Rabbi Meir says:

כָּל הַשְׂעִירִין כַּפָּרָתָן שָׁוָה,

All the goats, their atonement is the same,

עַל טוּמְאַת מִקְדָּשׁ וְקָדָשָׁיו.

for impurity of the Temple and its sacred foods.
