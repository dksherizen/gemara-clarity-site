Topic

Consecrating an addition to the עזרה and ירושלים: why the שתי הלחם can't be used, the songs sung, using פסוקים for protection, and the procession of the two תודות.

Overview

The גמרא looks for a leavened offering to use when consecrating a new addition to the עזרה. Leavening the שירים of a regular מנחה is forbidden. The שתי הלחם of שבועות are leavened, but every possible timing fails, so the גמרא concludes it is impossible. It then turns to the songs sung at the consecration: תהלים 100 with instruments, ארוממך, and the 'song of פגעים' (תהלים 91 and תהלים 3). This leads to רבי יהושע בן לוי, who said these verses at bedtime. The גמרא explains that using פסוקים for protection is allowed, healing with them is forbidden, and spitting and then saying them costs a person עולם הבא. Next, the גמרא explains that in the procession the two תודות walk ahead of the בית דין. It brings a מחלוקת over whether the תודות walked side by side or one behind the other, and over what 'the inner one' means. It challenges the side-by-side view from the משנה's rule that the inner one is eaten, but shows the same question applies to both views. רבי יוחנן then begins a new explanation, which continues on the next amud.
