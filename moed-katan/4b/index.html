Moed Katan 4b

מועד קטן ד׳ ע״ב

Topic

Farm and water work on חול המועד and in שביעית: thinning and watering vegetables, ditches around vines, digging and repairing water channels, and clearing public rivers

Overview

This amud works through several lines of the משנה about farm work on חול המועד. First, the word מדלין is explained as thinning out vegetables. רבינא then shows a ברייתא where it clearly means drawing water, and that explanation is dropped. Next, עוגיות are explained as round ditches around vines: new ones may not be dug, but old ones may be cleared. The גמרא then asks why רבי אלעזר בן עזריה forbids digging a new water channel in שביעית. Two reasons are given, 'it looks like hoeing' and 'it readies the banks for planting', and the גמרא works out where they differ. אמימר's version of the משנה raises a contradiction from a manure pit, which is answered in two ways. The גמרא defines a 'damaged' channel that may be repaired, measuring it in handbreadths, and ends that discussion with a תיקו. Finally, three rulings allow towns to clear their rivers, because a river the public drinks from counts as a public need.

Opening lines

״מַדְלִין״ — שַׁלּוֹפֵי.

"Madlin" means pulling out.

כְּדִתְנַן: הַמֵּידֵל בַּגְּפָנִים,

As we learned: one who thins out the vines,

כְּשֵׁם שֶׁהוּא מֵידֵל בְּשֶׁלּוֹ —

just as he thins out his own,

כָּךְ הוּא מֵידֵל בְּשֶׁל עֲנִיִּים,

so too he may thin out those of the poor,
