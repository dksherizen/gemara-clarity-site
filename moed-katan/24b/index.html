Moed Katan 24b

מועד קטן כ״ד ע״ב

Topic

Mourning laws for young children, counting mourning days around the festivals, and a new משנה on tearing garments and the mourner's meal

Overview

This amud first finishes a ברייתא about babies who die. It explains how they are carried out for burial and which mourning customs apply at each age. Under thirty days there is no שורה, ברכת אבלים, or תנחומי אבלים. From thirty days a small coffin is used and these customs are done. A twelve-month-old goes out on a מטה, but רבי עקיבא limits this to a child with the age or body size of a two-year-old. Two תנאים then give tests for public involvement, and the ברייתא sets the age at which a child gets a הספד. רב rules like רבי יהודה's version: from five for the poor and the elderly, and from six for the wealthy. Next the גמרא teaches that one day of mourning before עצרת plus עצרת count as fourteen days toward the שלושים. Sages objected when this was taught without naming its source, רבי אלעזר in the name of רבי אושעיא, who learns from a פסוק that עצרת is like a seven-day festival. The count is extended to ראש השנה (only according to רבן גמליאל, as רבינא explains) and to סוכות with שמיני עצרת, which make twenty-one days. A new משנה then begins about who tears garments and is served the mourner's first meal during the festival.

Opening lines

אַבָּא שָׁאוּל אוֹמֵר:

Abba Shaul says:

אַף בְּאִישׁ אֶחָד וּשְׁתֵּי נָשִׁים.

Even with one man and two women.

וְאֵין עוֹמְדִין עָלָיו בְּשׁוּרָה.

And they do not stand for him in a line.

וְאֵין אוֹמְרִים עָלָיו בִּרְכַּת אֲבֵלִים

And they do not say over him the mourners' blessing
