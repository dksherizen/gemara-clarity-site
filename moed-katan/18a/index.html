Moed Katan 18a

מועד קטן י״ח ע״א

Learn Moed Katan 18a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Moed Katan 18a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Cutting nails and trimming the mustache during אבל and the מועד, the power of spoken words, and who may launder clothes on חול המועד.

Overview

The גמרא rules like רבי יוסי, who allows cutting nails on the מועד and during אבל, because in אבל we follow the lenient view. A story follows about שמואל and his brother פנחס. It teaches that spoken words can come true (ברית כרותה לשפתים), with a proof from what אברהם said at the עקידה. The גמרא then clarifies that an אבל may cut both fingernails and toenails, but not with special nail scissors. From רבי יוחנן biting his nails on חול המועד, it learns three rules, and it explains why throwing nails in the בית מדרש is not dangerous. רבי allowed a mourner to cut nails and trim the mustache, and the אמוראים discuss how much of the mustache may be cut. After two side teachings about פרעה, the גמרא returns to the משנה's laundering rules. It defends רבי יוחנן's leniencies for someone with only one shirt and for linen garments. רבא begins a challenge to the linen ruling, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הֲלָכָה כְּרַבִּי יוֹסֵי

The halacha follows Rabbi Yosei

בַּמּוֹעֵד וּבְאֵבֶל,

regarding the Festival and regarding mourning,

דְּאָמַר שְׁמוּאֵל:

as Shmuel said:

הֲלָכָה כְּדִבְרֵי הַמֵּיקֵל בְּאֵבֶל.

The halacha follows the words of the lenient one in mourning.

The steps (28)

  1. ConclusionThe ruling follows רבי יוסי on the מועד and on mourning
  2. Statementשמואל visits his mourning brother פנחס
  3. Questionשמואל asks why the nails are not cut

  6. Statementשמואל throws his nails and teaches about the power of the lips
  7. Proofרבי יוחנן proves from אברהם that words have power

  10. Statementרב limits it: no nail scissors
  11. Statementרבי יוחנן bit his nails on חול המועד

  13. ChallengeChallenge: throwing away nails is called wicked
  14. ResolutionWomen are not usually in the בית מדרש
  15. ChallengeThe nails might be swept outside
  16. ResolutionOnce the nails are moved, they lose their harm
  17. StatementScholars from חמתן ask רבי about cutting nails
  18. Statementשמואל: they really did ask about the mustache
  19. StatementThe mustache: from corner to corner
  20. Statementרבי אמי: only the part that gets in the way
  21. Statementרב נחמן בר יצחק: my whole mustache gets in the way
  22. StatementHow small פרעה was
  23. Statementפרעה was a sorcerer
  24. StatementSomeone with only one shirt may launder it
  25. ChallengeThe משנה's list seems to leave out the one-shirt case
  26. ResolutionThe משנה is about people who own two garments
  27. StatementLinen garments may be laundered on חול המועד
  28. Challengeרבא begins a challenge from the משנה's list of towels
