Topic

Which work is allowed on חול המועד: מעשה הדיוט versus מעשה אומן, field work judged by intention, and business permitted only to prevent a loss

Overview

This amud gives a long list of rulings about which work is allowed on חול המועד. It starts with jobs connected to mills and animals. רב יהודה and רבא permit several of these, and אביי brings a ברייתא that supports רבא's ruling that an animal may be given medical care. Next comes a key rule: simple work anyone can do (מעשה הדיוט) is allowed, but skilled craftsman's work (מעשה אומן) is forbidden. רבא then gives four field rulings: leveling ground, clearing twigs, letting water into a field, and cutting palm branches. Each one is allowed for a present, non-farming purpose and forbidden if it is done to improve the land, and the גמרא gives a visible sign for telling the two apart. Finally, the גמרא rules that business is forbidden on חול המועד unless there would be a real loss (דבר האבד). It illustrates this with stories about רבינא, and it begins a ברייתא about עבודה זרה that continues on the next amud.
