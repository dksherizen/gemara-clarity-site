Topic

Writing תפילין and spinning תכלת on חול המועד; how festivals affect mourning

Overview

The amud opens with the last line of a משנה: on חול המועד a person may spin תכלת for his ציצית on his thigh. A ברייתא gives three views on writing תפילין and מזוזות on חול המועד. רבי מאיר allows it for yourself, or for others as a free favor. רבי יהודה allows a trick: sell your own and write new ones. רבי יוסי allows normal writing and selling, enough to make a living, and the הלכה follows him. A second ברייתא argues over whether תכלת may be spun with a stone or a spindle, and the הלכה allows both. A new משנה then teaches that a רגל cancels mourning that began before it: שבעה if the burial was three days before, שלשים if it was eight days before. It explains that שבת counts as a mourning day but does not interrupt mourning, while רגלים interrupt mourning but do not count. It then records a מחלוקת about whether עצרת, ראש השנה and יום הכיפורים act like שבת or like the רגלים. The גמרא ends with רב and רב ששת arguing whether the רגל cancels only the mourning restrictions or the days too, and shows one practical result: a mourner who did not cut his hair before the רגל may not cut it after.

וְטוֹוֶה עַל יְרֵיכוֹ

And one may spin on his thigh
