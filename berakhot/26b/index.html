Topic

Missing הבדלה in a make-up prayer, and whether the daily prayers come from the אבות or match the קרבנות, with their time limits

Overview

The גמרא brings a ברייתא about someone who missed מנחה on ערב שבת or on שבת and makes it up by praying two עמידות at night. The ברייתא implies that if he left הבדלה out of the first עמידה on מוצאי שבת, it does not count. A תוספתא seems to say the opposite: forgetting הבדלה does not require going back, because it can be said over wine. The גמרא leaves this as a קשיא. Then a new topic begins: רבי יוסי ברבי חנינא says the אבות instituted the prayers, while רבי יהושע בן לוי says they were set up to match the תמידין. Each side has a ברייתא. One ties שחרית, מנחה and ערבית to אברהם, יצחק and יעקב. The other ties each prayer's deadline to its קרבן, and it defines מנחה גדולה and מנחה קטנה. The גמרא clarifies that רבי יהודה's פלג המנחה is the midpoint of the later מנחה. It also explains that even רבי יוסי ברבי חנינא agrees the רבנן based the prayers on the קרבנות, as מוסף proves. Finally, the גמרא asks whether רבי יהודה's 'until four hours' includes the fourth hour, and the proof continues on the next amud.

תָּנוּ רַבָּנַן:

The Rabbis taught:
