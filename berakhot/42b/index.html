Topic

Blessings on wine and food at a meal, and what makes a group a joint meal for ברכות

Overview

The משנה finishes its rules about wine at a meal. Each person blesses for himself on wine served during the meal, but one person blesses for everyone on wine served after the meal and also on the incense (מוגמר). רבי יוחנן and רבי יהושע בן לוי teach that wine before the meal covers wine after it only when the meal is set around wine, such as on שבת and יום טוב, and two stories about רבא and אביי show how this depends on what the person planned. The גמרא then asks whether wine drunk during the meal covers wine drunk after it, and the אמוראים disagree. Next comes the משנה's rule that bread's ברכה covers פרפרת but not the reverse; the גמרא cannot decide which line בית שמאי argue on, so it ends in תיקו. Finally, the משנה implies that only reclining makes a joint meal, and a ברייתא seems to say that sitting is enough. רב נחמן בר יצחק answers that sitting counts when the group planned ahead to eat at a set place. A story follows about רב's students, who could not answer this very question after his funeral, and רב אדא בר אהבה's response continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בָּא לָהֶם יַיִן בְּתוֹךְ הַמָּזוֹן —

If wine came to them during the meal —

כׇּל אֶחָד וְאֶחָד מְבָרֵךְ לְעַצְמוֹ.

each and every one makes a blessing for himself.

אַחַר הַמָּזוֹן —

If it came after the meal —

אֶחָד מְבָרֵךְ לְכוּלָּם.

one person makes the blessing for all of them.
