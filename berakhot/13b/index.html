Topic

How much of קריאת שמע needs כוונה, the special status of the first פסוק, body position while saying שמע, and greeting people during it

Overview

This amud asks how much of קריאת שמע needs כוונה. רבי יוחנן rules that the first paragraph needs it, in two versions that agree in practice. רב זוטרא and רבי יאשיה argue about which paragraph needs כוונה and which needs reading, and the גמרא defends each view against challenges from the פסוקים. רבא rules like רבי מאיר, who says only the first פסוק needs כוונה. The גמרא then teaches how to draw out the word אחד, and argues about how much of שמע must be said standing in place. Stories about רבי, רב נחמן and רבה show that the first פסוק alone fulfills the basic obligation. Next comes the rule against saying שמע while lying on one's back. The amud ends by explaining the משנה's rules for greeting people during שמע, where רבי מאיר and רבי יהודה disagree.

Opening lines

״אֲשֶׁר אָנֹכִי מְצַוְּךָ הַיּוֹם עַל לְבָבֶךָ״.

"Which I command you this day, upon your heart."

From here you learn

שֶׁכָּל הַפָּרָשָׁה כּוּלָּהּ צְרִיכָה כַּוָּונָה.

that the entire section, all of it, requires intent.

אָמַר רַבָּה בַּר בַּר חָנָה
