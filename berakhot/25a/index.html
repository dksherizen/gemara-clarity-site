Topic

Saying קריאת שמע and holy words near filth, bad smells, and urine: distances, doubts, and how dry is dry enough

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a rule: for תפלה a person must cover his heart. רב הונא then rules that someone who walked into a בית הכסא wearing תפילין covers them with his hand until the first mass is out, since holding back is dangerous. Next, רב הונא and רב חסדא argue about saying קריאת שמע when part of the body is not clean, and then about how far to move from a bad smell. A ברייתא supports רב חסדא, but רבא rules like a different, shorter list of forbidden filth. רב ששת teaches that a smell with no visible source does not stop תורה learning, but it does stop קריאת שמע. אביי and רבא argue about filth that is passing by, and רב פפא says a pig's mouth counts as passing filth. רב יהודה rules on cases of doubt: a doubt about feces is strict, while a doubt about urine is lenient, because urine on the ground is only forbidden by the רבנן. The גמרא then asks how dry feces and urine must be before they stop forbidding holy words. רבא rules that feces as dry as earthenware is still forbidden, and urine forbids only while it is wet. The amud ends by testing that ruling against two ברייתות.

Opening lines

אֲבָל לִתְפִלָּה

But for prayer

עַד שֶׁיְּכַסֶּה אֶת לִבּוֹ.

[he may not pray] until he covers his heart.

וְאָמַר רַב הוּנָא:

And Rav Huna said:

שָׁכַח וְנִכְנַס בִּתְפִילִּין לְבֵית הַכִּסֵּא —

If one forgot and entered the bathroom wearing tefillin —
