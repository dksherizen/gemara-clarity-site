Topic

Dreams: their fulfillment, interpretation, and how to respond to them

Overview

This amud is mostly about dreams. It opens by finishing the proof from יוסף's dream that even a true dream has a part that does not come true. It then counts 22 years before his dream was fulfilled. רב הונא teaches that a good person is not shown good dreams, and the גמרא works out what this means for someone like דוד. Next comes a procedure for making a troubling dream favorable before three people, using three sets of three verses. אמימר, מר זוטרא and רב אשי then share new teachings: a prayer during ברכת כהנים for a forgotten dream, protection from עין הרע, and when to reveal an illness. The amud ends with ideas about which dreams are true and the power of interpretation (כל החלומות הולכים אחר הפה), and with the teaching that dreams come from a person's own thoughts.

Opening lines

וְהַהִיא שַׁעְתָּא אִמֵּיהּ לָא הֲוָת.

And at that time, his mother was no longer alive.

אָמַר רַבִּי לֵוִי:

Rabbi Levi said:

לְעוֹלָם יְצַפֶּה אָדָם לַחֲלוֹם טוֹב

A person should always anticipate a good dream

עַד עֶשְׂרִים וּשְׁתַּיִם שָׁנָה.

for up to twenty-two years.
