Topic

Hearing your own words and saying them clearly in קריאת שמע, with related teachings on תחיית המתים and writing מזוזה

Overview

This עמוד first asks whether the משנה that disqualifies a חרש from reading the מגילה follows רבי יוסי or רבי יהודה. After several challenges, the גמרא shows that the whole משנה can follow רבי יהודה if there are two kinds of קטן. Two ברייתות then seem to fit neither רבי יהודה nor רבי יוסי. The גמרא solves this in two ways: one ברייתא is רבי יהודה's own view and the other is his teacher רבי אלעזר בן עזריה's, or the two are רבי יהודה and רבי מאיר. רב חסדא rules that you should hear your own קריאת שמע from the start, but it counts after the fact if you did not. רב יוסף limits the argument to קריאת שמע, and רבי טבי rules leniently in both disputes of the משנה. The עמוד then turns to related teachings: the grave gives back the dead like a womb (a source for תחיית המתים), even the commands are written in a מזוזה, and words that run together in שמע must be separated. Saying שמע carefully cools גיהנם. A new teaching of רבי חמא ברבי חנינא begins and continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

דִּילְמָא רַבִּי יְהוּדָה הִיא,

Perhaps it is Rabbi Yehuda,

וּלְכַתְּחִלָּה הוּא דְּלָא,

and it is only in the first place that he may not,

הָא דִיעֲבַד — שַׁפִּיר דָּמֵי.

but after the fact, it is fine.

לָא סָלְקָא דַּעְתָּךְ,

This cannot enter your mind,
