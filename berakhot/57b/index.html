Topic

Dream signs, lists of three, and blessings on seeing places in בבל

Overview

The amud opens with רבי יוחנן: a verse that comes to mind on waking is a small נבואה. The חכמים then list what it means to see certain kings, books of תנ"ך, and sages in a dream. Next come rules of the form “all are good in a dream except…” for animals, metals, fruit, vegetables, colors, and birds. Short challenges about the elephant and turnip heads are answered with small details. Memory aids then introduce groups of lists: things that do or do not benefit the body, tastes of עולם הבא (where תשמיש is explained as relieving oneself), things that one-sixtieth resemble something greater, and signs and foods that help or harm the sick. A ברייתא interprets dreams of a dead person in the house. The גמרא then turns to blessings: for מרקוליס, for a place cleared of עבודה זרה, and the five blessings of רב המנונא on seeing the ruins of בבל. It closes with stories of רבא and מר בריה דרבינא, and רב אשי's report that he said all five blessings on his own reasoning.

Opening lines

אָמַר רַבִּי יוֹחָנָן:

Rabbi Yoḥanan said:

הִשְׁכִּים וְנָפַל פָּסוּק לְתוֹךְ פִּיו —

If he woke early and a verse fell into his mouth—

הֲרֵי זֶה נְבוּאָה קְטַנָּה.

this is a small prophecy.

תָּנוּ רַבָּנַן, שְׁלֹשָׁה מְלָכִים הֵם:

The Sages taught: There are three kings:
