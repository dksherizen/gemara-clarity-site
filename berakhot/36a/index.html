Topic

Which ברכה to make on processed or unusual foods (oil, flour, heart of palm), and the caper-bush under ערלה outside ארץ ישראל

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the previous amud's discussion of oil drunk in אניגרון, a vegetable broth. The גמרא asks why the oil is not just a side ingredient (טפל) to the broth. It answers that the case is a person with a sore throat, for whom the oil is the main thing, and that he makes a ברכה because he enjoys it even though it is medicine. Next, רב יהודה and רב נחמן argue whether plain wheat flour gets בורא פרי האדמה or שהכל. The גמרא concludes that both wheat and barley flour get שהכל, and that even harmful barley flour needs a ברכה because a person enjoys it. Then רב יהודה and שמואל argue about קורא (heart of palm). The הלכה follows שמואל that it gets שהכל, because nobody plants a palm tree for its קורא. Finally, the גמרא discusses רב's ruling that for an ערלה caper-bush outside ארץ ישראל one throws away the berries but eats the buds, following the lenient view of רבי עקיבא. רבינא challenges מר בר רב אשי for not following בית שמאי, who are even more lenient, and that argument continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דְּכוּלְּהוּ שִׁלְקֵי.

of all boiled vegetables.

אִם כֵּן, הָוֵה לֵיהּ

If so, it would be that

אֲנִיגְרוֹן עִיקָּר וְשֶׁמֶן טָפֵל,

the anigeron is primary and the oil is secondary,

וּתְנַן, זֶה הַכְּלָל:

and we learned in a mishna: This is the principle:

The steps (35)

  1. StatementFinishing the definition of the vegetable broths
  2. ChallengeChallenge: the broth should be the main food, not the oil
  3. ResolutionAnswer: the case is a person with a sore throat

  5. ChallengeChallenge: isn't it obvious he makes a ברכה?
  6. ResolutionAnswer: medicine still needs a ברכה if you enjoy it
  7. StatementDispute over the blessing on wheat flour
  8. Proofרבא supports רב יהודה from the ברכה on olive oil
  9. RejectionOlive oil and flour are not comparable
  10. ChallengeChallenge from שמואל's ruling on barley flour
  11. ResolutionWheat flour also gets שהכל
  12. ChallengeWhy not mention wheat, which would teach barley automatically?
  13. ResolutionBarley had to be mentioned, or we would think it gets no ברכה
  14. ChallengeCould barley flour be worse than salt?
  15. ResolutionBarley flour is harmful, but any enjoyment requires a ברכה
  16. StatementDispute over the blessing on heart of palm
  17. StatementThe reasons behind each opinion
  18. Proofשמואל praises רב יהודה with a radish argument
  19. RejectionThe גמרא rejects the radish comparison
  20. ChallengeChallenge from the caper-bush
  21. ResolutionCaper-bushes are planted for their shoots, palms are not planted for קורא
  22. ConclusionThe הלכה follows שמואל
  23. StatementWith an ערלה caper-bush outside ארץ ישראל, throw away the berries but eat the buds
  24. ChallengeA משנה treats caper buds as fruit of the tree
  25. Resolutionרב follows רבי עקיבא, who says only the berries are fruit
  26. ChallengeWhy didn't רב just say the הלכה follows רבי עקיבא?
  27. ResolutionA lenient view in ערלה applies only outside ארץ ישראל
  28. ChallengeWhy not use the general rule about lenient opinions outside the land?
  29. ResolutionThe general rule might have been limited to tree tithes
  30. Statementמר בר רב אשי acts on רב's ruling
  31. ChallengeWhy not follow בית שמאי, who are even more lenient?
  32. ProofThe משנה showing בית שמאי's view on the caper-bush
  33. ChallengeThe משנה seems to contradict itself
  34. Resolutionבית שמאי are unsure, so they are strict both ways
  35. ChallengeFor בית שמאי the caper is only doubtful ערלה, which is permitted outside the land
