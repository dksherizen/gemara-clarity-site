Topic

Women's obligations in time-bound מצוות, קידוש and ברכת המזון, and the rules for a בעל קרי

Overview

The amud opens with the end of a משנה: נשים are exempt from קריאת שמע and תפילין, but obligated in תפילה, מזוזה, and ברכת המזון. For each item the גמרא asks 'isn't that obvious?' and answers with a מהו דתימא, a reason we might have ruled the other way. רב אדא בר אהבה then rules that women are obligated in קידוש by Torah law. אביי calls it only rabbinic, but רבא rejects that and proves it from זכור and שמור. רבינא asks whether women's ברכת המזון is Torah or rabbinic, which decides whether a woman can say it for her husband. A ברייתא is brought as proof, but it is pushed off as dealing with someone who ate only a rabbinic amount. That leads to an אגדה: הקב״ה favors ישראל because they bless even after a כזית or כביצה. Then a new משנה rules on a בעל קרי, and רבינא and רב חסדא disagree whether thinking the words counts like saying them.

Opening lines

וּמִן הַתְּפִילִּין,

And from tefillin,

וְחַיָּיבִין בִּתְפִילָּה וּבִמְזוּזָה

but they are obligated in prayer and in mezuzah

וּבְבִרְכַּת הַמָּזוֹן.

and in Grace after Meals.
