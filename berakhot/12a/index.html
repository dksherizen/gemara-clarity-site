Berachos 12a

ברכות י״ב ע״א

Berakhot 12a

Topic

The ברכות the כהנים said in the מקדש, why the עשרת הדברות were dropped, whether a ברכה follows its ending, and the wording and bowing in the ברכות of שמע and תפילה

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a question about the משנה in תמיד: was the single ברכה the כהנים said יוצר אור or אהבה רבה? The גמרא shows that because ריש לקיש's view was only inferred, his rule could be about the order of the ברכות, so nothing is proven. The משנה also says the כהנים read the עשרת הדברות. שמואל and a ברייתא of רבי נתן explain that this was stopped everywhere else because of the complaint of the מינים, and later חכמים who tried to bring it back were turned down. On שבת the outgoing משמר blessed the incoming one with love, brotherhood, peace and friendship. Back in our משנה, the גמרא asks what happens if a ברכה starts wrong but ends right, as when someone holds beer, thinks it is wine, and finishes with שהכל. Two proofs from a ברייתא ('everything follows the ending') are pushed off, so the question is never settled. The amud ends with רב's rulings: say אמת ויציב in the morning and אמת ואמונה at night, and in תפילה bow at ברוך and straighten up at ה׳'s name, which שמואל praised.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא, אִי אָמְרַתְּ

But if you say

״אַהֲבָה רַבָּה״ הֲווֹ אָמְרִי,

that they would recite 'An abounding love',

מַאי בְּרָכוֹת אֵין מְעַכְּבוֹת זוֹ אֶת זוֹ?

what proof is there that the blessings do not hold each other back?

דִּלְמָא הַאי דְּלָא אָמְרִי ״יוֹצֵר אוֹר״

Perhaps the fact that they did not say 'Who forms light'
