Topic

The ברכות over natural wonders in the משנה: earthquakes, thunder, winds, lightning, the rainbow, mountains and a clear sky

Overview

This amud goes through the משנה's list of natural wonders that get a ברכה. It opens by finishing an אגדה about the stars כימה and עיש and the מבול, and רב נחמן says עיש will get its stars back in the future. For זוועות, רב קטינא says they are earthquakes. He publicly calls a necromancer who explained them as הקדוש ברוך הוא's tears a liar, though the גמרא says he did so only so people would not follow the necromancer, and several חכמים give their own explanations. For thunder, many explanations are offered, and the גמרא favors רב אחא בר יעקב's link to lightning. אביי explains the משנה's 'winds' as gale-force winds, and רבא lists lightning and cloud patterns that are warning signs at night. The amud teaches the ברכה over a rainbow: we do not bow, but we say 'זוכר הברית ונאמן בבריתו וקיים במאמרו'. אביי and רבא explain how 'עושה מעשה בראשית' relates to lightning and to mountains. Finally, רבי יהושע בן לוי gives a ברכה for a perfectly clear sky, and רב חסדא says such a sky has not been seen since the חורבן.
