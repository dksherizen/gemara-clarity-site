Topic

Reading the weekly פרשה with the ציבור, advice from רבי יהושע בן לוי and רבא, and the deadline for the night שמע

Overview

The גמרא finishes the rule that a person should read each weekly פרשה twice in the original and once in תרגום, together with the ציבור, even verses that are only lists of place names. Then comes a story: רב ביבי בר אביי learns that ערב יום כיפור is a day for eating, not for catching up, and that he may not read ahead early either. רבי יהושע בן לוי gives his sons three pieces of advice: finish your portions with the ציבור, cut the ורידין when slaughtering, and keep honoring a זקן who forgot his learning. רבא also gives his sons advice: don't cut meat on your hand, don't sit on the bed of an ארמית, and don't pass behind a בית הכנסת during davening. אביי then limits that last rule. רבי עקיבא and רבן גמליאל praise good habits of the מדיים and the פרסיים. The גמרא then returns to the משנה and rules like רבן גמליאל: the night שמע may be read until dawn. It brings two ברייתות of רבי שמעון בן יוחי about reading שמע twice, once around dawn and once around sunrise, and finds a contradiction in each. The first contradiction is answered on this amud, and the answer to the second comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

וַאֲפִילּוּ ״עֲטָרוֹת וְדִיבֹן״.

And even 'Atarot and Divon.'

שֶׁכָּל הַמַּשְׁלִים פָּרָשִׁיּוֹתָיו עִם הַצִּבּוּר

For anyone who completes his portions with the congregation

מֵאֲרִיכִין לוֹ יָמָיו וּשְׁנוֹתָיו.

has his days and his years lengthened.

רַב בִּיבִי בַּר אַבָּיֵי סָבַר

Rav Beivai bar Abaye thought
