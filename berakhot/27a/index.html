Topic

The time limits for שחרית and מנחה according to רבי יהודה and the רבנן, the meaning of 'עד', the melting of the מן, and rules about davening near others.

Overview

The amud opens by asking whether רבי יהודה's word 'עד' (until) includes the end point. Each reading seems to cause a problem. The גמרא solves this by explaining that פלג המנחה means 10¾ hours, so 'עד' can include the end point. רב נחמן then proves the point from רבי יהודה בן בבא's testimony that the morning תמיד was offered at four hours, and רב כהנא rules like רבי יהודה for שחרית. Next the גמרא asks whose view a ברייתא follows when it says the מן melted at four hours. It shows that both רבי יהודה and the רבנן can agree with it, and רבי אחא בר יעקב explains that four hours is when the sun is hot but the shade is still cool. For מנחה, רב חסדא finds no clear ruling, so a person may follow either opinion. A story about רב davening the שבת prayer early teaches three הלכות, including that one may not pass within four אמות in front of someone davening. The amud ends as the גמרא begins to challenge how רבי ירמיה could daven behind his teacher, and that challenge continues on the next amud.
