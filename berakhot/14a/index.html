Topic

Interrupting קריאת שמע and הלל, tasting on a fast, and putting תפילה before greetings and business

Overview

The גמרא finishes its fuller reading of the משנה about greeting people during קריאת שמע. It brings a ברייתא with the views of רבי מאיר and רבי יהודה that supports this reading. רבי חייא rules that one may interrupt הלל and מגילה to greet someone. רבה adds that how much you may interrupt depends on whether it is a day when the full הלל is said. רבי אמי rules that a person who is fasting may taste food, and רבי אמי and רבי אסי tasted up to a רביעית. רב and שמואל teach from a פסוק in ישעיהו that you should not go out of your way to greet someone before praying. Other אמוראים add that taking care of your own affairs before תפילה is forbidden, and that one who prays first will have his needs cared for by ה׳. After a short side teaching about dreams, the גמרא returns to the משנה and rules like רבי יהודה that one may not stop between אלהיכם and אמת ויציב. It then begins to give his source from a פסוק, which continues on the next amud.
