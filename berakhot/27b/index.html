Berachos 27b

ברכות כ״ז ע״ב

Topic

Davening behind one's רב; davening early on ערב שבת and מוצאי שבת; whether עַרְבִית is רשות or חובה, and the removal of רבן גמליאל as נשיא.

Overview

The amud opens by asking how רבי ירמיה בר אבא could daven behind רב, since a student may not pray next to or behind his teacher. A ברייתא of רבי אליעזר makes this worse: it says such disrespect drives the שכינה away from ישראל. The גמרא answers that he was a תלמיד חבר, a student who was also a colleague. Next the גמרא discusses davening the שבת prayer early. Someone who does so on purpose must stop working, but a prayer said by mistake does not bind an individual, though a צבור's mistaken prayer stands. שמואל rules that one may daven שבת early and make קידוש, and daven מוצאי שבת early and make הבדלה. Then the גמרא explains the משנה's words 'תפלת הערב אין לה קבע' as meaning that עַרְבִית is רשות, the view of רבי יהושע against רבן גמליאל. A famous story follows: רבן גמליאל publicly embarrasses רבי יהושע over this question, the people remove רבן גמליאל as נשיא, and they choose רבי אלעזר בן עזריה to replace him. The amud ends as he consults his wife, and her answer comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

לֹא כְּנֶגֶד רַבּוֹ,

not next to his teacher,

וְלֹא אֲחוֹרֵי רַבּוֹ.

and not behind his teacher.

וְתַנְיָא, רַבִּי אֱלִיעֶזֶר אוֹמֵר:

And it was taught: Rabbi Eliezer says:

הַמִּתְפַּלֵּל אֲחוֹרֵי רַבּוֹ,

One who prays behind his teacher,
