Topic

Habits of תפילה and respect for the בית הכנסת, and a chain of sayings from רבי חלבו in the name of רב הונא

Overview

The amud opens with the last line about the תפילין of הקדוש ברוך הוא: all the פסוקים are written together in the arm-תפילין. Then come many teachings about תפילה and the בית הכנסת. When a regular misses בית הכנסת for ordinary business, ה׳ asks about him, because he should have trusted in ה׳. ה׳ is angry when there are not ten men in the בית הכנסת. A person should keep a fixed place for תפילה like אברהם, should not rush out of the בית הכנסת, and should run in and run to learn, even on שבת. A list of sayings follows about the 'main reward' of different מצוות: running, crowding, reasoning, silence, צדקה, crying at a הספד, and words at a wedding. רב הונא calls one who prays behind the בית הכנסת facing away a רשע, and a story about אליהו shows how serious this is, followed by explanations of 'כרם זלות לבני אדם'. The amud ends with more sayings of רבי חלבו in the name of רב הונא: be careful with מנחה (others add ערבית and שחרית), make a חתן happy, a God-fearing person is listened to, and greet others first and always return a greeting.

Opening lines

וְכוּלְּהוּ כְּתִיבִי בְּאֶדְרָעֵיהּ.

And all of them are written on His arm.

אָמַר רָבִין בַּר רַב אַדָּא, אָמַר רַבִּי יִצְחָק:

Ravin bar Rav Adda said that Rabbi Yitzchak said:

כׇּל הָרָגִיל לָבֹא לְבֵית הַכְּנֶסֶת

Anyone who is used to coming to the synagogue

וְלֹא בָּא יוֹם אֶחָד,

and one day did not come,
