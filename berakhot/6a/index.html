Berachos 6a

ברכות ו׳ ע״א

Berakhot 6a

Topic

Harmful spirits, where the שכינה rests, and God's תפילין

Overview

This amud starts by finishing an earlier topic: רבי יוסי ברבי חנינא says that someone who waits in the בית כנסת for his friend to finish praying earns the blessings of a פסוק in ישעיהו. Then אבא בנימין, אביי, רב הונא and רבא describe the countless מזיקין around us and the everyday troubles they cause. The גמרא gives a way to detect them and a way to see them, and tells how רב ביבי בר אביי was harmed after trying it. Next, אבא בנימין teaches that prayer is truly heard in the בית כנסת. רבי יצחק adds that the שכינה is with ten who pray, three who judge, two who learn, and even one who learns, and the גמרא explains what each larger group gains. Along the way רב אשי teaches that a מצוה someone planned but was forced to miss counts as if he did it. Finally, רבי יצחק teaches that God wears תפילין, which contain praise of ישראל such as 'Who is like Your people ישראל, one nation in the land'. The amud ends by showing how these פסוקים fit into four compartments.

Opening lines

אָמַר רַבִּי יוֹסֵי בְּרַבִּי חֲנִינָא:

Rabbi Yosei son of Rabbi Ḥanina said:

זוֹכֶה לַבְּרָכוֹת הַלָּלוּ,

He merits these blessings,

שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: ״לוּא הִקְשַׁבְתָּ לְמִצְוֹתָי

as it is said: 'If only you had listened to My commandments,'

וַיְהִי כַנָּהָר שְׁלוֹמֶךָ

then your peace would be like a river,
