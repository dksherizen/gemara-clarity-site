Temurah 13a

תמורה י״ג ע״א

Learn Temurah 13a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Temurah 13a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Finishing בית הפרס and the sources for several rules of תמורה: no תמורה from a תמורה or from offspring, and no תמורה for birds, meal offerings, communal offerings, partners, or בדק הבית.

Overview

This amud finishes the rule of בית הפרס: a field where a grave was plowed counts for a full furrow, which is 100 אמות. Next, the גמרא shows that the משנה's rule "no תרומה after תרומה" follows רבי עקיבא, based on a dispute about partners who each separated תרומה. It then gives sources in the פסוק for two more rules: a תמורה cannot make another תמורה ("ותמורתו"), and the offspring of a holy animal cannot make a תמורה ("הוא"). רבי יהודה disagrees about the offspring, and the רבנן use his word "יהיה" for a swap made by mistake. A new משנה rules that birds, meal offerings, the community, partners, and בדק הבית items cannot make a תמורה. רבי שמעון learns this by using the animal tithe (מעשר) as the model. The גמרא asks whether בדק הבית items are called "קרבן", and רבי חנינא answers that רבי שמעון and the רבנן disagree. The רבנן tell apart "קרבן ה׳" and "קרבן לה׳". Last, a ברייתא explains why the תורה repeats תמורה by מעשר, and רבי שמעון's list of מעשר's features continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שָׁלֹשׁ שָׂדוֹת וּשְׁתֵּי מַעֲנוֹת.

Three fields and two furrows.

וְכַמָּה מְלֹא מַעֲנֶה?

And how much is a full furrow?

מֵאָה אַמָּה,

One hundred cubits,

כִּדְתַנְיָא: הַחוֹרֵשׁ אֶת הַקֶּבֶר —

as it is taught: One who plows a grave

  9. ResponseThe offspring cannot make a substitute
  10. Responseרבי יהודה: the offspring can make a substitute
  11. ResolutionHow the חכמים use the word "shall be"
  12. StatementNew משנה: birds and meal offerings cannot make a substitute
  13. StatementThe community and partners cannot make a substitute
  14. StatementItems for the Temple's upkeep cannot make a substitute

  16. Responseרבי שמעון's answer: compare all offerings to the tithe
  17. StatementA ברייתא: בדק הבית items are not called an offering
  18. ChallengeChallenge: a ברייתא calls בדק הבית items an offering

  21. ChallengeA פסוק calls בדק הבית items an offering
  22. Resolution"The Lord's offering" is not "an offering to the Lord"
  23. QuestionWhy does the תורה repeat תמורה for מעשר?
  24. ResponseThe general פסוק would include every kind of offering
  25. QuestionWhy was מעשר singled out if it was already included?
