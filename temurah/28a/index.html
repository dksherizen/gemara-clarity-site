Topic

Animals forbidden for the מזבח: why they are never cancelled in a mixture, why our משנה is needed, and the source that רובע and נרבע are unfit.

Overview

The משנה lists animals that may never be brought on the מזבח, such as רובע, נרבע, מוקצה, נעבד, אתנן, מחיר, כלאים, טרפה and יוצא דופן. If one of them gets mixed in with good animals, the whole group is forbidden for the מזבח, no matter how small its share. The משנה also defines מוקצה and נעבד and says both may still be eaten. The גמרא explains that these animals are never cancelled by a majority, then asks why our משנה is needed when משניות in זבחים and עבודה זרה seem to teach the same thing. Through a chain of answers (צריכותא), it shows that each משנה teaches something new: our משנה covers animals that are only unfit for the מזבח and that got mixed while still חולין. Next the גמרא asks for the source that רובע and נרבע are unfit, and brings a ברייתא that learns it from the words מן הבהמה. The ברייתא tests whether a קל וחומר could teach this instead, and finds that it works only when two witnesses saw the sin. רבי שמעון offers a קל וחומר for the one-witness case too, and the גמרא explains that the רבנן who disagree with him still need the פסוק.

MISHNA: All animals that are forbidden for the altar

make [a mixture] forbidden in any amount

and the set-aside animal, and the worshipped animal
