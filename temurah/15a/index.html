Topic

The משנה on whether a replaced חטאת is left to die for a יחיד and for the ציבור, and what happens to the leftover animal when someone has two valid חטאות

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a count of years from the previous page. שמואל, שאול and דוד together reach forty years. Then a new משנה rules that a private (יחיד) חטאת whose owner already got כפרה with a replacement animal is left to die, but a communal (ציבור) one is not. רבי יהודה says even the communal one dies. רבי שמעון compares this case to the other 'dying חטאות', which apply only to a יחיד. The גמרא quotes a ברייתא that learns from a פסוק: when there are two valid חטאות, the owner may bring either one, but only one. It then asks what happens to the leftover animal. רב המנונא quotes a ברייתא in which רבי יהודה says it grazes and רבי שמעון says it dies. Since רבי יהודה says 'die' in the משנה, the גמרא tries switching the names, but that clashes with רבי שמעון's rule of five dying חטאות. It then gives two answers: either the two rulings deal with different cases, or רבי יהודה is explaining the views of רבי and of the רבנן. Finally, the גמרא asks in surprise whether anyone really holds that a communal חטאת is left to die. That discussion continues on the next amud.

one year that Saul and Samuel reigned,

וּשְׁתַּיִם שֶׁמָּלַךְ שָׁאוּל בְּעַצְמוֹ,

and two that Saul reigned by himself,

וּשְׁלֹשִׁים וָשֶׁבַע שֶׁמָּלַךְ דָּוִד.

and thirty-seven that David reigned.
