Topic

How different תנאים learn rules about בכור and מעשר שני from one פסוק, the end of פרק אלו קדשים, and the opening משנה about חטאות that are left to die.

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא in which several תנאים each learn something different from one פסוק. That פסוק (דברים 14:23) lists מעשר שני right next to בכור. First, רבי ישמעאל learns מעשר שני from בכור. The גמרא asks whether this breaks רבי יוחנן's rule against לָמֵד מִן הַלָּמֵד in קדשים, and answers that מעשר is חולין and that the meat and blood of a בכור count as one thing. רבי עקיבא learns that a בכור may be brought up only from where מעשר דגן is brought, meaning ארץ ישראל. בן עזאי learns that מעשר שני is eaten only inside the walls of ירושלים, and the גמרא explains that the idea of eating it anywhere within sight came from the rule in שילה. אחרים use the היקש to teach that a בכור does not become פסול when its year passes. The גמרא then shows where each side gets the rules the other side learns: 'שנה בשנה' and 'כחזה התנופה' cover not becoming פסול after a year and eating for two days and one night. The פרק ends with a הדרן, and a new משנה opens listing חטאות that are left to die.
