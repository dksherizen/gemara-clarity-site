Temurah 24a

תמורה כ״ד ע״א

Topic

Leftover חטאות that are left to die or to graze, and the ban on skinning holy animals from the feet

Overview

The amud opens with the last line of an earlier discussion about רבי אמי's teaching. Then רבי הושעיא rules that if someone sets aside two חטאות, one as a backup, he brings one and the other grazes. The גמרא asks whose view this follows, rules out the רבנן and רבי שמעון, and concludes it follows רבי, who says a leftover dies only when it replaced a lost animal. It defends this answer against a משנה of רבי אלעזר ברבי שמעון and against the משנה about the יום כיפור goats. Next, a ברייתא about when a leftover חטאת dies is corrected with a קל וחומר: if the animal was lost at the time of כפרה, everyone agrees it dies. The גמרא then brings a ברייתא forbidding skinning from the feet for a בכור and for פסולי המוקדשין. רב חסדא attributes the first rule to בית שמאי and the second to רבי אלעזר ברבי שמעון, and three reasons are given for the ban. The amud ends by closing the chapter ולד חטאת.

Opening lines

אֲבָל בִּתְרֵי סִדְרֵי לָא,

but with two sets, not;

קָא מַשְׁמַע לַן.

he teaches us.

אָמַר רַבִּי הוֹשַׁעְיָא:

Rabbi Hoshaya says:

הַמַּפְרִישׁ שְׁתֵּי חַטָּאוֹת לְאַחְרָיוּת,

one who sets aside two sin offerings as a guarantee,

  1. ConclusionClosing line: what רבי אמי's teaching adds

  8. ChallengeIf רבי אלעזר follows רבי, a backup would also die

  14. ChallengeA קל וחומר shows the version of the ברייתא cannot be right

  16. StatementBack to the משנה: רבי אלעזר ברבי שמעון's opinion

  20. QuestionWho holds the rule for disqualified sacrifices?

  23. ChallengeWhy not attribute both rules to בית שמאי
  24. Resolutionבית שמאי may only speak about a בכור, which is holy from birth
  25. ChallengeWhy not attribute both rules to רבי אלעזר ברבי שמעון
  26. Resolutionרבי אלעזר's view may apply only to disqualified consecrated animals
  27. ChallengeChallenge from the rule allowing sale in the butchers' market
  28. ResolutionSaving the hide ruins the meat
  29. ResolutionIn the West: it looks like working with holy animals
  30. ResolutionA rabbinic decree so people won't raise whole flocks
