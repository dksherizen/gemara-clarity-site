Temurah 32a

תמורה ל״ב ע״א

Open Temurah 32a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Whether קדשי מזבח can be made into חרמי כהנים: רב הונא's rule, its refutation, and עולא's reading of 'כל חרם'.

Overview

The משנה teaches rules shared by קדשי מזבח (items set aside for the altar) and קדשי בדק הבית (items set aside for Temple upkeep). You cannot switch their holiness to another kind, but you may consecrate their value (הקדש עילוי) or declare them חרם. If they die, they are buried, though רבי שמעון says dead בדק הבית animals are redeemed. In the גמרא, רב הונא rules from a פסוק that attaching קדשי מזבח to חרמי כהנים does nothing. The גמרא first challenges him from a ברייתא that leaves out the altar case, and it defends him: the ברייתא lists only items that fail in both directions. A second challenge comes from the משנה's word 'מחרימין', and the גמרא answers that both words there mean בדק הבית. Two ברייתות then show plainly that קדשי מזבח can become חרמי כהנים, so רב הונא is refuted. עולא explains the פסוק differently: 'כל חרם' includes everything. The גמרא then questions whether עולא really holds this, since he elsewhere said that attaching an עולה to בדק הבית creates only an עיכוב. That discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳ אֶחָד קׇדְשֵׁי מִזְבֵּחַ

MISHNA: Both items consecrated for the altar

and items consecrated for Temple maintenance:

אֵין מְשַׁנִּין אוֹתָן

one may not change them

from one sanctity to another sanctity,
