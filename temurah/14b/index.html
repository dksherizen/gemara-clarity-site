Topic

Sending הלכות in writing, offering נסכים at night, and the offerings brought during the festival

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a ruling about מנחת נסכים. Nothing needs to be erased from the ברייתא, because one ברייתא speaks of נסכים brought with a זבח and the other of נסכים brought by themselves. The גמרא then asks how such a ruling could be sent in a letter at all, since רבי יוחנן and a פסוק teach that writing down הלכות is forbidden. The answer is that a new teaching may be different, based on 'עת לעשות לה׳': it is better to set aside one rule than to let the תורה be forgotten. Back on נסכים, רב פפא rules that נסכים brought by themselves may be made holy and offered at night, a ברייתא supports him, and רב אדא בר אהבה adds that dawn disqualifies them. Next comes רבי יוחנן's word-by-word reading of the פסוק about festival offerings, which adds required offerings of individuals, compares נסכים to שלמים, and includes שלמי נזיר. אביי objects that a נזיר's offering is voluntary, and the reply is to remove ״נזירות״ from his source. The גמרא challenges this from אבשלום, who went to pay his vow in חברון. It concludes that he really went there to sacrifice, since private altars were permitted, and then asks from when the 'forty years' in that פסוק are counted.

And he should not delete 'the meal offering of libations' from the baraita.

there, it is about libations that come by themselves.
