Topic

אתנן זונה: when a payment to a זונה is forbidden for the מזבח, and which women and relationships it covers

Overview

The amud opens by finishing רבי אלעזר's case: a lamb given to a זונה is permitted for the מזבח if she offered it before the ביאה. The גמרא shows this only works when he said the lamb is hers at the ביאה, but hers right away if she needs it. רב אושעיא then asks about a lamb she only made הקדש before the ביאה, and the question ends in תיקו. Next, two ברייתות disagree about payment given after the ביאה. רב חנן בר רב חסדא explains that a specific promised lamb is אתנן, while an unspecified lamb is not. רב and לוי then argue about whether payment to one's own wife who is a נדה counts as אתנן, each relying on a different word in the פסוק. אביי holds that a gentile זונה's payment is forbidden but a Jewish זונה's is permitted, with the opposite result for a כהן's lashes. רבא makes both cases equal. A ברייתא is used to challenge both opinions, and the answer to the last challenge continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שֶׁקָּדְמָה וְהִקְרִיבַתּוּ.

Where she first went ahead and sacrificed it.

הֵיכִי דָּמֵי?

What are the circumstances?

אִי נֵימָא דְּאַקְנְיֵהּ נִיהֲלַיהּ לְאַלְתַּר —

If we say that he transferred it to her immediately,

פְּשִׁיטָא דְּמוּתָּר,

it is obvious that it is permitted,
