Topic

The sources for disqualifying רובע ונרבע, נעבד, מוקצה and נוגח from the מזבח, and the definition of מוקצה

Overview

The גמרא works through a ברייתא that reads ויקרא 1:2 word by word, with each word disqualifying a type of animal from being a קרבן. רב אשי explains why רובע ונרבע cannot be learned by קל וחומר from a בעל מום: a בעל מום's blemish is visible, but רובע ונרבע's is not. That is why 'מן הבהמה' is needed. Next, 'מן הבקר' excludes a נעבד. The גמרא shows that a קל וחומר from אתנן ומחיר could be flipped, so a פסוק is needed here too. It then explains why the two words cannot be swapped, and רבי שמעון explains why the Torah needs to exclude both רובע and נוגח. A different תנא, the school of רבי ישמעאל, learns these disqualifications from the word השחתה in the blemish verse. The גמרא then shows how each תנא finds a source for excluding sick, old and filthy animals. Finally, ריש לקיש rules that only an animal set aside for seven years is a forbidden מוקצה, based on גדעון's bull. That leads to רבא's answer, which cites רבי אבא בר כהנא's teaching that eight normally forbidden things were allowed to גדעון that night, a list that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אָמַר רַב אָשֵׁי:

Rav Ashi said:

מִשּׁוּם דְּאִיכָּא לְמֵימַר

Because one can say

מֵעִיקָּרָא דְּדִינָא פִּירְכָא —

there is a refutation from the very base of the argument —

מָה לְבַעַל מוּם

What is special about a blemished animal?
