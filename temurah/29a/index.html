Topic

מוקצה and נעבד animals: their source, when the status ends, and why they are permitted to eat; טרפה on the מזבח; and the definition of אתנן

Overview

This amud begins at the end of a list of normally forbidden things that were used in גדעון's sacrifice, among them מוקצה (an animal set aside for עבודה זרה) and נעבד (an animal that was itself worshipped). רבי יאשיה learns the rule of מוקצה from "תשמרו להקריב לי": an animal kept "for Me," and not for another master. Several opinions then explain how long an animal stays מוקצה. It is forbidden only until it is used for work, or handed to the idol's priests and fed their vetch, or sheared and worked. רב פפא then proves from "ממשקה ישראל" that מוקצה and נעבד animals are permitted to eat, since otherwise no פסוק would be needed to keep them off the מזבח. The גמרא challenges this from טרפה. It answers that each טרפה פסוק covers a different case: an animal that became a טרפה after it was made holy, and one that became a טרפה after it was born. A new משנה then defines אתנן, payment to a זונה, including a מחלוקת between רבי מאיר and חכמים. The גמרא explains why even a hundred lambs are forbidden, and then discusses a ברייתא about payment given at a different time from the act. רבי אלעזר's answer about why an early payment does not become אתנן backward in time continues on the next amud.
