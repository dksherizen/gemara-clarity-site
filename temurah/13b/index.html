Temurah 13b

תמורה י״ג ע״ב

Open Temurah 13b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

תמורה in special cases: the two kinds of substitution for מעשר בהמה, and רבי שמעון's view on an עולה bought with leftover money, ending the first פרק of תמורה.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a list of conditions for תמורה: the קרבן must not be one brought in partnership. רבי then explains why the תורה mentions תמורה separately for מעשר בהמה. מעשר has two kinds of substitution, תמורת שמו (a miscount that puts the 'tenth' name on the wrong animal) and תמורת גופו (a regular swap). He lists three ways they differ: being offered on the altar, being redeemed, and taking effect on a blemished animal. The גמרא explains that a separate mention was needed, because something taught with a new law has only that new law. Next, רבא explains that for רבי שמעון even a voluntary עולה makes a תמורה, and only an עולה bought with leftover money does not. The גמרא defends this, saying רבי שמעון agrees with רבי אליעזר that leftover money goes to a private offering but disagrees about תמורה. This raises a difficulty with רבי אבין's question about making a תמורה a second time. The גמרא answers that he asked about a תנא who combines both views, and the question ends unresolved (תיבעי). The amud closes the first פרק, הכל ממירין.

Opening lines

And an item that is not brought in partnership.

רַבִּי אוֹמֵר:

Why was the tithe singled out now?

To be discussed regarding 'substitution of its name'

  1. StatementThe last condition: not an item brought in partnership
  2. QuestionWhy does the תורה mention substitution for the animal tithe separately?

  4. StatementTwo differences: offering on the altar and redemption
  5. StatementThird difference: fit and unfit animals
  6. ChallengeWould a special extra law take away the regular law?

  10. QuestionFor רבי שמעון, does a voluntary עולה not make a תמורה?
  11. ResponseA voluntary עולה also counts once it is taken on
  12. ResponseThe rule only leaves out an עולה from leftover money
