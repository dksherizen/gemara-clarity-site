Temurah 27b

תמורה כ״ז ע״ב

Learn Temurah 27b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Temurah 27b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Paying the difference when הקדש is sold for the wrong price (אונאה, ביטול מקח, appraisals), and the closing משנה of כיצד מערימין on 'in place of' and unfit animals

Overview

רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש argue about someone who redeemed הקדש for less than it was worth: is the difference owed by Torah law or only by rabbinic law? A teaching in רבי יוחנן's name about אונאה and ביטול מקח seems to clash with his view. רבי ירמיה applies that teaching to regular land and רבי יונה applies it to הקדש. The גמרא first answers by switching the two names, but then shows that the switch works only for רבי יונה's version. רבי ירמיה says that in his version no switch is needed. The גמרא then explains the gap between רבי יונה and רבי ירמיה through שמואל's rule about redeeming הקדש for a פרוטה: both accept the rule, but they disagree about whether it applies only after the fact (דיעבד) or even from the start (לכתחילה). It also offers another answer: never switch the names, and read the משנה of אלו דברים the way רב חסדא does. עולא then rules that an appraisal of הקדש by three people is final, because the appraisal is only rabbinic. The פרק ends with a new משנה about saying 'in place of' an offering and about setting aside unfit animals 'for' an offering, and רב shows that this משנה does not follow רבי מאיר or רבי שמעון.

Opening lines

וְהָאָמַר רַבִּי יִרְמְיָה אַקַּרְקָעוֹת דְּחוּלִּין,

But didn't Rabbi Yirmeya say this regarding non-sacred lands,

וְרַבִּי יוֹנָה אָמַר אַהֶקְדֵּשׁוֹת,

and Rabbi Yona said it regarding consecrated property,

תַּרְוַויְיהוּ מִשְּׁמֵיהּ דְּרַבִּי יוֹחָנָן אָמְרִי:

and both of them said it in the name of Rabbi Yoḥanan:

אוֹנָאָה אֵין לָהֶן,

They do not have the rules of exploitation (overcharging),
