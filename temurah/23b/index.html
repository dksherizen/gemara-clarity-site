Topic

Temurah 23b: Leftover sin-offering animals and money. The views of רב הונא and רבי אבא are defended, and רבי אמי's ruling on two backup piles of money is examined.

Overview

This amud tests the views of רב הונא and רבי אבא about a lost חטאת that turns up after a replacement was set aside. First the גמרא says an earlier source follows only רבי, so it does not contradict רבי אבא. Then it brings two משניות, one about animals and one about money. Read closely, each seems to rule the same way in every case, which would refute both sages. Both times the גמרא answers that the משנה only teaches the clear-cut case and stays silent on cases that depend on other details. Next, רבי אמי rules that when someone sets aside two piles of money as a backup, the leftover pile goes to נדבה. The גמרא asks whose view needs this ruling and rejects רבי and the רבנן, since for them it is obvious. It says the ruling is needed for רבי שמעון, but then proves from the thirteen collection boxes and the teaching of יהוידע הכהן that רבי שמעון already holds of נדבה. The גמרא begins a new answer: maybe רבי שמעון allows נדבה only with one set of money. That answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַאי לַָאו אֲפִילּוּ רַבָּנַן?

What, is it not that it follows even the Rabbis?

לָא, רַבִּי הִיא.

No, it is Rabbi.

תְּנַן: הַמַּפְרִישׁ חַטָּאת וְאָבְדָה,

We learned: One who sets aside a sin offering and it was lost,

וְהִקְרִיב אַחֶרֶת תַּחְתֶּיהָ —

and he sacrificed another in its place,
