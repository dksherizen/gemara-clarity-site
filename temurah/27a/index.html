Topic

What תחת means when exchanging holy and non-holy animals, the chain of questions about mixed groups of animals, and whether paying the difference when redeeming a blemished animal is a תורה law.

Overview

The גמרא shows that the word תחת ('in place of') can mean two things. It can mean the holiness stays and spreads to the new animal, as in תמורה, or it can mean the holiness moves off one item onto another (אחולי). Context decides: with קדשי מזבח it means תמורה, and with קדשי בדק הבית it means moving the holiness. רבא adds that with a blemished holy animal, תחת can mean moving the holiness, and רב אשי adds that it can go either way, depending on which animal the owner's hand rests on. אביי, and later רב אשי, then ask a chain of questions about groups of holy and non-holy animals, where a person says 'these are in place of those.' Each question tests the rule that a person does not leave a permitted act to do a forbidden one. The chain ends with תיקו, unresolved. Next the גמרא quotes the משנה about redeeming a blemished holy animal. רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש argue whether paying הקדש the difference in value is a תורה law or a rabbinic one. The גמרא then tries to pin down what price gap they are arguing about.
