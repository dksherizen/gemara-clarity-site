Temurah 24b

תמורה כ״ד ע״ב

Open Temurah 24b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Using הערמה to keep an unborn animal from becoming a בכור, רב יהודה's ruling on blemishing a בכור before birth, and which cases the end of the משנה is about

Overview

The משנה teaches a strategy (הערמה): before an animal gives birth for the first time, the owner declares that the unborn male will be an עולה, so it never becomes a בכור for the כהן. It then goes through different birth outcomes: a male, a female, a male and a female, two males, two females, and a טומטום or אנדרוגינוס. In the גמרא, רב יהודה rules that one may give a בכור a blemish (מום) before it is born. The גמרא challenges him from the משנה, which seems to allow only an עולה, a stronger holiness. The גמרא answers that the משנה speaks of when the בית המקדש stands, while רב יהודה means today. It also explains why his ruling is still needed: there is no גזרה against it, because blemishing early stops the owner from shearing or working the בכור. Then the גמרא clears up the end of the משנה. The female שלמים case is about the young of a הקדש animal, and the two-males case is about an ordinary mother. The amud ends by quoting the טומטום and אנדרוגינוס line, and the discussion of it continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳ כֵּיצַד מַעֲרִימִין עַל הַבְּכוֹר?

MISHNA: How may one use a strategy regarding the firstborn?

מְבַכֶּרֶת שֶׁהָיְתָה מְעוּבֶּרֶת, אוֹמֵר:

An animal giving birth for the first time, while it was pregnant, he says:

״מַה שֶּׁבְּמֵעֶיהָ שֶׁל זוֹ

"What is in the womb of this one,

אִם זָכָר — עוֹלָה״,

if male, is a burnt offering,"
