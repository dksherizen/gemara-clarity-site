Topic

Which offspring and substitutes of קדשים are offered: שלמים, תודה and עולה

Overview

רבי חייא brings a ברייתא that reads two limits out of 'אם כשב הוא מקריב'. The first limit is that a שלמים's offspring is offered but the offspring's offspring is not. The second is that only a שלמים's offspring is offered, not that of other קדשים. The גמרא searches for what the second limit excludes and settles on the offspring of מעשר בהמה, because we cannot learn the possible from the impossible (בכור). Next, רבי פפייס's testimony about a cow eaten on פסח whose offspring was eaten 'on the חג' raises a בל תאחר problem. רב זביד answers that the animal was sick on שבועות, while רב אשי reads 'חג' as שבועות. The testimony itself shows, against רבי אליעזר, that a שלמים's offspring is offered. A new משנה rules that the offspring and substitutes of a תודה are like a תודה but come without loaves, and a ברייתא gives the source. A further משנה treats the substitute of an עולה and its offspring as an עולה. It also brings a dispute about the male calf of a female set aside as an עולה. רבה בר בר חנה and רבא explain why only the second case is disputed. Then רב חסדא and רבא resolve a contradiction in רבי אלעזר's view, and רבא's answer continues on the next amud.
