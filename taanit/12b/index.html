תענית י״ב ע״ב

Topic

Rules of private and public fasts: shoes, postponing a fast, dream fasts, and the stages of fasting for rain

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the definition of dozing (נמנום) before a fast. רב rules that someone who accepts a private fast may not wear shoes, in case he accepted a תענית צבור. The fix is to say clearly that it is a תענית יחיד. The גמרא then describes how different אמוראים handled shoes, and explains that in בבל only תשעה באב is a full communal fast. Next, רב teaches that a person may 'borrow' a private fast and repay it later, and there are two versions of שמואל's reaction. A story about a guest who would not eat shows that a תענית חלום cannot be postponed: it must be kept on the day of the dream, even on שבת. A new משנה then lays out the growing stages of public fasts when no rain falls, ending with mourning-like behavior and fasting by the יחידים until ניסן ends. The גמרא explains from a פסוק in יואל why work is forbidden on these fasts and why that ban begins in the morning. It ends with אביי's schedule for the fast day.

Opening lines

נִים וְלָא נִים,

Asleep and not asleep,

תִּיר וְלָא תִּיר.

awake and not awake.

דְּקָרוּ לֵיהּ וְעָנֵי,

That is, when they call him he answers,

וְלָא יָדַע אַהְדּוֹרֵי סְבָרָא,

but he does not know how to give a reasoned reply,
