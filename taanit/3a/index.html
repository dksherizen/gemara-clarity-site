Topic

The water libation on סוכות, who mentions rain on שמיני עצרת, and why mentioning dew and wind is optional

Overview

The גמרא continues discussing the water libation on סוכות, which is tied to when we start mentioning rain. It explains that רבי עקיבא takes the timing of the extra libation from the sixth day. It then brings רבי נתן, who learns from the wording 'הסך נסך' that there are two libations, one of water and one of wine. Next it asks who wrote the משנה that says the water is poured all seven days. A first answer, רבי יהודה בן בתירה together with רבי יהודה, is rejected. The final answer is that the משנה follows רבי יהושע, and the seven days are a הלכה למשה מסיני. The גמרא then asks which רבי יהושע and which בן בתירה said that on שמיני עצרת only the later prayer leader mentions rain. רב נחמן בר יצחק answers that both names refer to רבי יהושע בן בתירה. Finally, a ברייתא teaches that mentioning dew and wind is optional, because they are never held back, and the proof from אליהו continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אִי סָבַר לַהּ כְּרַבִּי יְהוּדָה בֶּן בְּתִירָה —

If he holds like Rabbi Yehuda ben Beteira,

נֵימָא כְּווֹתֵיהּ!

let him say like him!

Rabbi Akiva holds:

כִּי כְּתִיב נִיסּוּךְ יַתִּירָא —

when the extra libation is written,
