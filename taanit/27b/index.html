Topic

The משמרות and מעמדות: rebuilding the watches, why they read מעשה בראשית, their fasts, and how the Sunday reading is divided between readers

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא about the כהנים who returned from Babylon. Only four of the 24 משמרות came back, so the נביאים divided them into 24 by lottery and ruled that ידעיה keeps first place even if יהויריב returns. The גמרא then asks why the מעמדות read מעשה בראשית. רב אסי answers that the world stands because of the מעמדות. He shows this through אברהם's conversation with הקב״ה about the קרבנות, and he adds that reading the סדר קרבנות counts as bringing them. A ברייתא describes the מעמד fasting Monday through Thursday, each day for a different group in need, but not on Friday or שבת out of כבוד שבת. Three אמוראים give reasons for not fasting on Sunday. Finally, the גמרא asks how two people can read the five פסוקים of בראשית. רב says the second reader repeats a פסוק, and שמואל says they split one. A ברייתא then challenges both of them, and the answer continues on the next amud.
