Topic

רבי יוחנן's keys in ה׳'s hand, and the opinions on when to start mentioning rain in davening, with their sources

Overview

This amud finishes רבי יוחנן's list of keys that ה׳ keeps for Himself, with a verse for the key of תחיית המתים. The scholars of ארץ ישראל add a key of פרנסה, and the גמרא explains that for רבי יוחנן rain already includes פרנסה. Then the גמרא asks where רבי אליעזר learns that we start mentioning rain on the first יום טוב of סוכות: from לולב, which would mean starting by day, or from ניסוך המים, which would mean starting the night before. רבי אבהו answers that it is from לולב, and a ברייתא shows this. In that ברייתא, רבי אליעזר and רבי יהושע argue whether mentioning rain on סוכות is fitting, since rain on סוכות is a curse. רבי אליעזר answers that mentioning rain is only praise, like mentioning תחיית המתים. The גמרא answers for רבי יהושע: rain is not a blessing at every time, as a משנה about rain after ניסן shows. Finally, the גמרא explains why רבי יהודה בן בתירה starts on the second day and רבי עקיבא on the sixth day. Both base it on a hint in the תורה to ניסוך המים.

Opening lines

אֵלֶיהָ אֱלֹהִים וַיִּפְתַּח אֶת רַחְמָהּ״.

to her, and He opened her womb.

מַפְתֵּחַ שֶׁל תְּחִיַּית הַמֵּתִים מִנַּיִן —

From where do we know the key of the resurrection of the dead?

דִּכְתִיב:

As it is written:

״וִידַעְתֶּם כִּי אֲנִי ה׳

And you shall know that I am ה׳
