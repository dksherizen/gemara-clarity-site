Taanis 14a

Topic

Completeness of the משנה's fast-day list, where to say עננו, pregnant and nursing women on fasts, and what הַתְרָעָה means

Overview

The גמרא first asks whether the משנה's list for the last seven fasts leaves anything out. It shows that the תיבה and the twenty-four blessings are not real omissions, because the תנא teaches them in the next פרק. Then it asks where an individual adds עננו in the עמידה, and rules that it goes in שומע תפילה. Next, three ברייתות disagree about whether pregnant and nursing women fast. רב אשי settles this by saying they fast only on the middle three fasts. The גמרא then asks what 'sounding the alarm' (מתריעין) on the last seven fasts means. One opinion says it means שופרות, and another says it means the prayer עננו. In the end, everyone agrees that שופרות count, and the argument is only whether עננו is also called הַתְרָעָה. A משנה about sounding the alarm on שבת shows that some תנא does call prayer הַתְרָעָה. The amud ends by starting a story about a time of trouble in the days of רבי יהודה נשיאה, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְהָא שַׁיַּיר תֵּיבָה!

But he left out the ark!

אִי מִשּׁוּם תֵּיבָה — לָאו שִׁיּוּרָא הוּא.

If it is because of the ark, that is not an omission.

מִילֵּי דְצִינְעָא — קָתָנֵי,

He teaches matters done in private,

מִילֵּי דִבְפַרְהֶסְיָא — לָא קָתָנֵי.

he does not teach matters done in public.

  6. ResolutionThe ark is taught in another chapter

  25. ProofSo it must be עננו, and the משנה still calls it הַתְרָעָה
