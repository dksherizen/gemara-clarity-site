Topic

Hair and wool of a blemished בכור: the need for a מומחה, the three versions of עקביא's dispute with the חכמים, the ruling like רבי יהודה, and רבי ינאי's question about wool from an עולה.

Overview

The גמרא defends the rule that a מומחה must permit a blemished בכור before its wool may be used. It explains that a ברייתא calls any animal not yet permitted by a מומחה 'unblemished.' It then tries to show that this rule is a dispute between תנאים. To do so, it quotes a ברייתא in which the תנא קמא, רבי יהודה and רבי יוסי each describe the argument between עקביא בן מהללאל and the חכמים differently. רבא rejects that attempt. In his view, everyone requires a מומחה, and the three views differ only on whether the argument covers an animal that died, one that was slaughtered, or both. רב נחמן rules like רבי יהודה, citing a משנה in עדיות. רב נחמן בר יצחק supports this from the משנה about dangling wool, which only fits the חכמים in a slaughtered animal. Finally, רבי ינאי asks about wool that fell out by itself from an unblemished עולה. The גמרא sets aside the clear cases of חטאת, אשם, בכור and מעשר, and its reasoning about the עולה continues on the next amud.
