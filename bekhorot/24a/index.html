Topic

רב's ruling to follow the unnamed משנה over a ברייתא, and whether a mother animal nurses a baby that is not her own (רבן שמעון בן גמליאל, בכורה, אותו ואת בנו, the pig following a ewe)

Overview

The גמרא asks why רב said both 'the הלכה follows רבי יוסי בן המשולם' and 'the הלכה follows the whole chapter.' It answers that the separate statement shows 'the whole chapter' backs the unnamed משנה that matches רבן שמעון בן גמליאל, and that a disagreement found only in a ברייתא is not a real dispute. It then quotes that ברייתא: a nursing animal bought from a גוי, where the first opinion worries she may be nursing a baby that is not hers, while רבן שמעון בן גמליאל says we assume she is the mother. Next the גמרא asks whether רבן שמעון בן גמליאל means only that nursing proves she gave birth, or that the baby is surely hers. That difference matters for lashes under אותו ואת בנו. Three proofs are brought and all are pushed off. Then רבי יוחנן rules that a ewe followed by a pig-like animal is exempt from בכורה, but the animal may not be eaten until אליהו comes. After several challenges, the גמרא explains that the הלכה surely follows רבן שמעון בן גמליאל, but it is unclear whether he holds that a mother animal may nurse another's baby, even one of another species. The amud ends as the גמרא begins quoting a question of רבי יוחנן that supports this, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הֲלָכָה כְּרַבִּי יוֹסֵי בֶּן הַמְשׁוּלָּם

'The halakha follows Rabbi Yosei ben HaMeshullam'

לְמָה לִי?

why do I need it?

אִי אָמַר הִלְכְתָא דְּכוּלֵּיהּ פִּירְקִין,

Had he said 'the halakha follows the whole chapter,'

וְלָא אָמַר הִלְכְתָא כְּרַבִּי יוֹסֵי בֶּן הַמְשׁוּלָּם,

and not said 'the halakha follows Rabbi Yosei ben HaMeshullam,'
