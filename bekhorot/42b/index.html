Topic

Is a טומטום a doubtful male or female? Telling the gender of a טומטום animal, the principle 'since it has changed, it has changed,' and יבום with the wife of a טומטום.

Overview

The גמרא first shows that everyone agrees a טומטום is really male or female, not a separate creature. The real argument is about a טומטום animal that urinates from the female place. The רבנן worry its male organ may be turned inward, while רבי שמעון בן יהודה does not, matching רבי אלעזר's ruling that such an animal is חולין. רבי יוחנן objects that רבי אלעזר follows one opinion, the רבנן בתראי, against two. The גמרא explains that רבי אלעזר relies on ריש לקיש: in an animal, unlike a person, the place of urination shows whether it is male or female. רב אושעיא asks whether we should worry the organ was flipped. One answer ties that worry to רבי מאיר. Another says even the רבנן might worry, because 'since it has changed, it has changed.' The גמרא then tests whether two ברייתות about יבום with a טומטום's wife argue about this principle. It concludes that they really follow רבי אליעזר and רבי עקיבא, with the רבי עקיבא ברייתא matching רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה, who treats a טומטום as only a possible סריס חמה. The amud ends by asking how רבי יהודה and his son differ in practice, and רבא's answer comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

בֶּן יְהוּדָה לְמֵימַר:

[and רבי שמעון] בן יהודה comes to say:

טוּמְטוּם בְּרִיָּה הוּא וְלֹא קָדוֹשׁ!

a טומטום is a separate creature, and it is not holy!

לָא, דְּכוּלֵּי עָלְמָא

No, for everyone agrees:

טוּמְטוּם בִּבְרִיָּה לָא מְסַפְּקָא,

about a טומטום being a separate creature, there is no doubt,
