Topic

The crow slaughtered for practice, whether רבי שמעון forbids a פטר חמור after its neck is broken, and the minimum value for redeeming it

Overview

The גמרא first finishes its discussion of a פטר חמור slaughtered only for practice. It compares the case to an argument between נימוס and רבי אליעזר about a crow, and then suggests the crow may be special because it has kosher signs. Next it challenges רבה's view that even רבי שמעון forbids benefit from a פטר חמור after its neck is broken (עריפה). The challenge comes from a ברייתא, which the גמרא answers by saying both clauses deal with a live donkey: one with benefit from its value and one with benefit from its body. רב נחמן brings two proofs that רבי שמעון forbids the donkey after עריפה. Both proofs are pushed away. ריש לקיש agrees with רב נחמן, while רבי יוחנן (or רבי אלעזר) says the dispute continues even after death. Another version applies the question to the משנה in קידושין about betrothing with a פטר חמור. רב explains that משנה using רבי יוסי בר יהודה's rule that redemption needs at least a שקל. Finally, the גמרא examines the source for that minimum, and it begins to explain how the רבנן read רבא's פסוק, a discussion that continues on the next amud.
