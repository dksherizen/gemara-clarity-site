Topic

מעשר בהמה: the mistaken ninth animal, רבא's rule מנין הראוי פוטר and its source, and how counting works with two openings or extra lambs.

Overview

This amud deals with מעשר בהמה, where animals pass one by one out of a pen and every tenth is holy. It opens with a view that a ninth animal mistakenly called 'tenth' is holy only if the real tenth lost its name, just like the eleventh. That view is supported by a קל וחומר. The גמרא rejects the proof twice: with היא הנותנת, and with the point that the eleventh comes after the tenth was already identified while the ninth comes before. So the ninth is holy even without the uprooting. Next רבא teaches מנין הראוי פוטר: animals counted in a count that could reach ten are exempt even if no tenth was taken. A proof from a משנה is tried and falls, so רבא brings the word יעבר in the פסוק, and a ברייתא supports him. רבא then works through fourteen lambs leaving by two openings, and the גמרא explains that even a count that only might reach ten exempts. Finally רבא rules that with fifteen lambs one may not hand-pick ten. All must enter the pen, and the leftover five wait for the next גורן. A supporting ברייתא begins at the end and continues on the next amud.
