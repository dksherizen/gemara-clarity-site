Topic

Shed wool from an עולה and dangling wool, the end of פרק הלוקח בהמה, and the new משנה on how long an owner cares for a בכור before giving it to a כהן.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a question: does the רבנן's ban on wool shed from a בכור also apply to an עולה? The גמרא tries to answer from a ברייתא about plucked wool. That proof is pushed away, because the word 'plucks' may have been chosen only to show how far עקביא goes. Next the גמרא defines dangling wool that 'does not appear with the fleece'. ריש לקיש and רב נתן בר אושעיא give two different tests, and רבי אילעא explains ריש לקיש's view. Then פרק הלוקח בהמה ends, and a new משנה teaches how long an owner must care for a בכור, and how long it may be kept before it is eaten. The גמרא looks for the source of the 30 and 50 days. רב כהנא's pairing of פסוקים is shown to be reversible, so רבא learns the extra time for an ox from the word 'תעשה', and the exact number is left to the חכמים. Finally רב ששת explains why the owner may not hand the בכור over early. A ברייתא forbids giving priestly gifts as wages, and that ברייתא continues on the next amud.
