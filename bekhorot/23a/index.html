Topic

Is cancelled impurity still present? The back-and-forth between רב דימי and אביי about touch versus carrying, ending with רב דימי's silence and the 'rotted impurity' suggestion.

Overview

This amud asks whether impurity that was cancelled out (בטל) in a majority is still there in some way and can come back. רב דימי first brings a proof from רבי אליעזר's ruling and עולא's reason for it: a tiny cancelled bit of impurity can meet its own kind and 'wake up.' אביי answers that this case is different, because there impurity woke up impurity, while here the question is whether purity can wake up impurity. אביי then challenges the idea from a משנה about פרה אדומה ashes mixed with ordinary ashes, and רב דימי answers with רבי יוסי ברבי חנינא: the mixture does not make impure by touch, but it does by carrying. אביי pushes back with רב חסדא's rule about נבילה mixed into שחוטה. רב דימי replies that his school follows רבי חייא's version instead. Finally אביי brings רבי חייא's own teaching that a clump of blood makes impure neither by touch nor by carrying, and רב דימי is silent. The גמרא suggests he could have said the clump is rotted impurity. That answer works for בר פדא, and the גמרא begins to ask about רבי יוחנן's view, which continues on the next amud.
