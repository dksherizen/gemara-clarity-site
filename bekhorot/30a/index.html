Topic

How far suspicion reaches: רבא's penalty on a cheating seller, and the משנה about שביעית, מעשרות and טהרות with its explanations and authorship

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a story about a man who sold forbidden fat as permitted fat. רבא banned him from selling even nuts. רב פפא asks whether that follows רבי יהודה, and רבא answers that it follows רבי שמעון, because the man used nuts to bribe butchers' children into bringing him the forbidden fat. Next comes a new משנה. Suspicion about שביעית and suspicion about מעשרות do not carry over to each other. Suspicion about either carries over to טהרות, but suspicion about טהרות does not carry back. A suspected person may not judge or testify about the matter he is suspected of. The גמרא explains each part: מעשר feels serious because it must be eaten in ירושלים, and שביעית feels serious because it cannot be redeemed. Torah law outweighs rabbinic law. A contradiction from a ברייתא is answered in two ways. Finally, רבי יוחנן says the משנה follows רבי עקיבא, and two versions identify the disagreeing חכמים as רבי יהודה or רבי מאיר. Students then begin to argue over which version is right, and that argument continues on the next amud.
