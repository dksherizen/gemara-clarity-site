Topic

Why מעשר בהמה is not separated nowadays, why מוקדשין are exempt, which animals may be tithed together, and the source that תרומה cannot be separated across kinds

Overview

The amud opens by explaining why we still deal with a בכור nowadays but no longer separate מעשר בהמה. With a בכור, following רב יהודה, the owner may put a מום on it before it is born, so no unblemished holy animal exists to be misused. That fix does not work for מעשר. You cannot know which animal will come out tenth. You may not arrange the order, because of לא יבקר. Blemishing the whole herd would also leave no fit animals for קרבנות when the בית המקדש is rebuilt, and even buying animals would not help, since everyone would blemish their herds. Next, the גמרא explains the משנה's rule that מוקדשין are not tithed. It refers to קדשים קלים, which רבי יוסי הגלילי holds belong to the owner, and the פסוק יהיה קדש excludes any animal that is already holy. The גמרא then shows why this needed to be taught: without it, the strict rules of מעשר (no redeeming, no selling) might have applied. Then it shows why sheep and goats are tithed together (the word צאן) while animals from different years are not: a היקש to מעשר דגן, which רבא limits to the year using שנה שנה. Finally, it brings the source that תרומה may not be separated from one kind of produce for another: 'give the best (חלב) of this, and the best of that.'

Opening lines

אֶפְשָׁר כִּדְרַב יְהוּדָה,

It is possible, following Rav Yehuda,

דְּאָמַר רַב יְהוּדָה:

for Rav Yehuda said:

מוּתָּר לוֹ לָאָדָם

It is permitted for a person

לְהַטִּיל מוּם בִּבְכוֹר

to put a blemish on a firstborn

  1. ResolutionWith a firstborn, the problem can be avoided by רב יהודה's method
  2. ChallengeThen do the same for the tithe animal

  7. ResolutionOrdinary animals will still be available
  8. ChallengeWhy not just buy animals for the rebuilt Temple?
  9. ResolutionUnblemished animals would become too rare
  10. ChallengeWhy does the משנה need to exclude מוקדשין?

  12. ProofThe ברייתא where רבי יוסי הגלילי says this
  13. ResolutionSo the משנה is teaching something new
  14. ChallengeMaybe קדשים קלים really should be tithed

  16. ChallengeWhy would we need a פסוק for this at all?
  17. QuestionWhat is the source for that rule?
  18. ResponseThe משנה in תמורה: holiness cannot be switched
  19. Resolutionמעשר is different because every animal is ready for it
  20. QuestionWhat practical difference would it make?
  21. ResponseThe difference: no redeeming and no selling
  22. StatementThe משנה: herd and flock are tithed separately, but sheep and goats together
  23. ChallengeA logical argument that new and old animals should be tithed together
  24. RejectionThe verse compares animal tithe to grain tithe, so new and old cannot join
  25. ChallengeThen the comparison should also stop sheep and goats from joining
  26. ResolutionThe word צאן includes both sheep and goats
  27. ChallengeThen צאן should also join new and old animals
  28. ResolutionThe verse עשר תעשר keeps new and old apart
  29. ChallengeWhy apply the comparison to years but not to species?
  30. Resolutionרבא: the verse limits the comparison to the matter of the year
  31. StatementA משנה: no separating תרומה from one kind for another kind
  32. QuestionWhere does this rule come from?
  33. ResponseThe source: 'the best of this and the best of that'
