Topic

Feeding tests for eye blemishes, blemishes of the nose, lip and gums, and whether a limb missing inside the body disqualifies a קרבן

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a list of questions about how to feed a בכור whose eye blemish is being tested. Each question has one side that clearly works and one side that is unclear. The questions ask whether the feeding happens at every meal, before or after eating and drinking, with the animal tied or untied, alone or with another animal, and in the city or the field. רב אשי adds a question about a garden near the city, and all of these end in תיקו. רב נחמן בר יצחק says that the three checks of רבי חנניא בן אנטיגנוס must be evenly spaced. פנחס asks שמואל whether a blemish confirmed by the three-month test counts from the beginning or only from then on, which matters for מעילה with redemption money. שמואל has no answer and praises the question. New משניות list blemishes of the nose, lip and gums, and the גמרא explains that only blemishes that can be seen count. It also fills in missing words in a ברייתא about the double teeth, which brings out three different opinions. Finally, רב אחדבוי בר אמי asks whether a limb missing inside the body makes an animal unfit for the altar. The גמרא tests this with two ברייתות about an animal with one kidney and works through several ways to reconcile them, ending with a challenge to רבי יוחנן's answer that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אוֹ דִלְמָא בְּכׇל סְעוּדָה וּסְעוּדָה?

Or perhaps at each and every meal?

אִם תִּימְצֵי לוֹמַר בִּסְעוּדָה רִאשׁוֹנָה —

If you say at the first meal,

קוֹדֶם אֲכִילָה אוֹ לְאַחַר אֲכִילָה?

is it before eating or after eating?

קוֹדֶם אֲכִילָה וַדַּאי מְעַלֵּי לֵיהּ כְּסַמָּא,

Before eating, it is certainly effective for it, like a medicine;
