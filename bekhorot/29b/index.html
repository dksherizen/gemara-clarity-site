Topic

Paying a בכור examiner, and what one may buy from people suspected about בכורות, שביעית, and תרומה

Overview

The amud first finishes the earlier משנה about paying a כהן examiner. It gives two ways he could become טמא: a trodden בית הפרס, whose טומאה is only מדרבנן, or other kinds of טומאה a כהן may contract that still stop him from eating תרומה. An elderly examiner who must ride a donkey is paid like an idle worker, and אביי explains that this means idle from his own particular job. Then a new משנה lists what one may not buy from someone suspected about בכור animals: not even deer meat, untanned hides, or raw wool. Spun thread and garments are allowed, and רבי אליעזר also allows female hides. The גמרא explains each rule, including the argument over whether a seller could fake a female hide by blaming mice. It also shows that the 'extra' words in the משנה mean wool cleaned of its dirt, and felt. Two more משניות follow. One covers a person suspected about שביעית, where 'woven' means braided chains. The other covers a person suspected of selling תרומה as חולין, where רבי שמעון's wording includes fish innards mixed with olive oil. A story about a suspected butcher begins and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְרַב יְהוּדָה בַּר אַמֵּי מִשְּׁמֵיהּ דְּרַב יְהוּדָה אָמַר:

And Rav Yehuda bar Ami said in the name of Rav Yehuda:

בֵּית הַפְּרָס שֶׁנִּידַּשׁ טָהוֹר.

A beit haperas that has been trodden down is pure.

אִי נָמֵי:

Alternatively:

בִּשְׁאָר טוּמְאוֹת,

it is with other impurities,
