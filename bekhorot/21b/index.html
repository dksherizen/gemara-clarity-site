Topic

מחוסר זמן in מעשר, defining טינוף, buying an animal of unknown history from a Jew, and the lump of blood that must be buried.

Overview

The amud opens with a ברייתא showing that רבי שמעון lets a מחוסר זמן, an animal too young to be offered, enter the pen for מעשר, just like a בכור, which is holy at birth but offered later. The גמרא asks why מעשר is compared to בכור and not to ordinary קדשים. Each side has four similarities, so the גמרא concludes that רבי שמעון learns it from the גזירה שוה of 'העברה'. Next, the גמרא defines the טינוף discharge from the משנה: רבא calls it a murky discharge and שמואל calls it bubbles of blood. The 'חכם' who must examine it turns out to be an expert shepherd. רב חסדא asks how long an animal fetus takes to form, and the attempted answer from זעירי is pushed aside. The גמרא then asks about buying an animal from a Jew whose history is unknown. רב says its next calf is a sure בכור, שמואל says it is a doubtful בכור, and רבי יוחנן says it is surely חולין. A ברייתא supporting רבי יוחנן does not refute the others, because with a בכור it is the buyer's job to ask. Finally, a new משנה rules that a lump of blood from a large animal must be buried, and the גמרא asks why it must be buried if it gives off no טומאה.

Opening lines

וְהָתַנְיָא: רַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן בֶּן יְהוּדָה אוֹמֵר

And it is taught: Rabbi Shimon ben Yehuda says

מִשּׁוּם רַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן:

in the name of Rabbi Shimon:

An animal lacking time enters the pen to be tithed,

וַהֲרֵי הֵן כִּבְכוֹר —

and they are like a firstborn:
