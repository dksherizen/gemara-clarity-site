Bechoros 41a

Topic

Blemishes that do and do not permit slaughtering a בכור: גרב and חזזית, old, sick and sin-related animals, and whether the אנדרוגינוס's female organ counts as a 'crack'.

Overview

A ברייתא first explains that the 'joint' a calf's tail must reach is the upper joint, which is easy to see on a camel. A short משנה then says that all the listed blemishes let us slaughter a בכור and redeem פסולי המוקדשין. The גמרא explains that this line is a סתם ruling, so the הלכה follows even the blemishes taught by single תנאים. The next משנה lists conditions that are not real blemishes, and also discusses a טומטום and an אנדרוגינוס. The גמרא asks why גרב and חזזית are not blemishes when the Torah lists them, and it ends up splitting them into kinds that heal and kinds that never heal. It then finds פסוקים showing that old, sick, foul-smelling and sin-related animals cannot be brought as קרבנות. Last, it asks why an אנדרוגינוס is not slaughtered for the 'crack' of its female organ. אביי and רבא each prove that a crack in flesh is not a מום, and the גמרא explains how רבי ישמעאל disagrees with both.

Opening lines

גְּמָ׳ תָּנָא:

Gemara: It was taught:

קֶפֶץ הָעֶלְיוֹן וְלֹא קֶפֶץ הַתַּחְתּוֹן,

The upper joint, and not the lower joint;

וּכְנֶגְדּוֹ בְּגָמָל נִיכָּר.

and the matching one in a camel is noticeable.

מַתְנִי׳
