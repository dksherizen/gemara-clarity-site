Topic

Fines for selling animals and slaves to a גוי, and how much gentile partnership exempts an animal from בכורה

Overview

This amud first finishes the attempt to prove that a Jew may not sell an animal's future offspring to a גוי. The proof is rejected, because the ברייתא is about a fetus that already exists, as the masculine word בדמיו shows. The גמרא then discusses the קנס for selling a בהמה גסה to a גוי. In one version ריש לקיש says the fine is up to ten times the value. In another version he says up to one hundred, and the גמרא compares this with רבי יהושע בן לוי's fine for selling an עבד. Next the גמרא returns to an animal owned jointly with a גוי. רבי יהודה and חכמים both learn their views from the words ״כל בכור״, and the גמרא gives two ways to explain how each side reads them. רב הונא says even a share in the ear exempts the animal. רב חסדא and רבא say the share must be in a vital part, and their dispute depends on whether a טריפה can live. רב פפא rules that a share in the animal and a share in its mother are treated the same. The amud ends as רבי אלעזר asks רבי אסי what was taught in the בית מדרש, and the answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַאי לָאו אַבְּהֵמָה?

What, is it not referring to the animal?

לָא, אַעוּבָּר.

No, it is referring to a fetus.

דַּיְקָא נָמֵי דְּקָתָנֵי:

The wording is also precise, for it teaches:
