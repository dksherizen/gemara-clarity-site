Topic

The drill-hole versus סלע measure for a skull, the testimony of חזקיה אבי עקש about vessels with no inside, and the משנה on eye blemishes

Overview

The amud opens at the end of a challenge: the drill-hole measure of בית שמאי seems to equal the סלע of בית הלל, so the two would not really disagree. רב חסדא answers that the measure is a hole with room for the drill itself, and רב תחליפא says this is as certain as the testimony of חזקיה אבי עקש. The גמרא then quotes that testimony about vessels with no inside, and רבי יצחק בר אבין re-reads it as being about a כלי שטף rather than a כלי חרס. This leads to a long back-and-forth. Impure liquids make a vessel טמא only by rabbinic law, and the רבנן modeled this rule on a כלי חרס as a sign, so people would not burn תרומה and קדשים. The case is limited to vessels used for sitting, which can get מדרס, something a כלי חרס never gets. The גמרא then returns to the skull: רב פפא's answer would make בית שמאי the lenient side, which is not on the list of their leniencies, so רב נחמן explains the sizes with the larger סלע of Nero, which keeps בית שמאי the strict side. Finally, a new משנה lists blemishes of the eye and eyelid and defines a תבלול.
