Bechoros 31a

בכורות ל״א ע״א

Topic

Returning to חבר status, judging one's own animals, and how blemished holy animals are sold

Overview

The גמרא brings a ברייתא about people who took on the strict practices of a חבר and then dropped them. רבי מאיר says they are never accepted back. רבי יהודה makes it depend on private versus public, and an איכא דאמרי gives another version of that split. רבי שמעון and רבי יהושע בן קרחה say they are always accepted, and רבי יוחנן rules like them. A second ברייתא says that at first a חבר who became a גבאי could never return, but later the rule changed so that once he quits he is like anyone else. A story follows about רב הונא בר חייא, where רב יוסף and רבה each act on a different line of that ברייתא. Next comes a ברייתא that a person may not inspect his own בכור, though he may rule on his own קדשים, מעשר and טהרות. The גמרא explains that the ban applies to a יחיד מומחה, and that in the other cases he has no real reason to lie. The chapter then ends, and a new משנה teaches that פסולי המוקדשין are sold in the market and weighed by the litra, while בכור and מעשר are not.

Opening lines

תָּנוּ רַבָּנַן:

The Rabbis taught:

וְכוּלָּן שֶׁחָזְרוּ בָּהֶן

And all of them who went back on their commitment,

אֵין מְקַבְּלִין אוֹתָן עוֹלָמִית,

they are never accepted again,

דִּבְרֵי רַבִּי מֵאִיר.

these are the words of Rabbi Meir.
