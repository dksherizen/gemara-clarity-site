Topic

ביטול ברוב and the טומאה of a ולד, the skein measures for a fetus's head, and purifying brine from an עם הארץ

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an answer: the lump of blood is buried so people know the mother animal is now free from בכורה. The גמרא then asks why a real ולד like this gives no טומאה. רבי יוחנן answers that it is cancelled by the bigger mass around it (ביטול ברוב). He links this to רבי שמעון, who says a ולד inside a שליא dissolved before it came out. Next, a משנה teaches that a dead fetus gives טומאה only once its head is round like a פיקה (skein). The גמרא works out which skein is meant: a warp skein for a woman's fetus, a woof skein for an animal's fetus, and a large sack-makers' skein for an impure clod of earth. Then ריש לקיש rules that brine bought from an עם הארץ becomes טהור by touching it to מקוה water. רבי ירמיה limits this to dipping bread, because in a pot the cancelled water 'wakes up'. אביי questions whether cancelled טומאה can return, and רב דימי begins a proof from a משנה about impure תרומה, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

כְּדֵי לְפַרְסְמָהּ

In order to publicize about her

שֶׁנִּפְטְרָה מִן הַבְּכוֹרָה.

that she is exempt from the firstborn law.

לְמֵימְרָא דְּוָלָד מְעַלְּיָא הוּא,

That is to say it is a full-fledged offspring,

וְאַמַּאי אֵין מְטַמֵּא

so why does it not make things impure
