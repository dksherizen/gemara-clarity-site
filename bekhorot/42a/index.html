Topic

Is a טומטום just a doubt or its own category? The גמרא answers many ברייתות by removing 'טומטום', then suggests the question is argued by תנאים.

Overview

The גמרא is working with the view that a טומטום is really either male or female; we just cannot tell which. Only an אנדרוגינוס is treated as its own category that needs a פסוק to exclude it. The גמרא brings ברייתות on ערכין, שלמים and עולה that use a פסוק to exclude both a טומטום and an אנדרוגינוס. Each time it answers סמי מיכן טומטום: take the word טומטום out of the ברייתא. It does the same with a ברייתא about bird offerings, where רבי אליעזר says male and female do not matter for birds. It does the same again with רבי אלעזר's list of animals that cannot take part in תמורה, as explained by שמואל. When one version of that list counts 'five', the גמרא swaps טומטום for a יתום to keep the number right. Finally, the גמרא asks whether the question is argued by תנאים. In the ברייתא of רבי אילעאי, the חכמים and רבי שמעון בן יהודה, removing טומטום would make two opinions the same. So they must argue about a טומטום: the חכמים treat it as a ספק that is holy out of doubt, and רבי שמעון disagrees. The explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אַלְּמָה תַּנְיָא:

Why, then, is it taught in a baraita:

״הַזָּכָר״ — וְלֹא טוּמְטוּם וְאַנְדְּרוֹגִינוֹס!

"The male": but not a טומטום or an אנדרוגינוס!

סְמִי מִיכָּן טוּמְטוּם.

Remove טומטום from here.

תָּא שְׁמַע:

Come and hear:
