Topic

Redeeming a פטר חמור, the משנה's list of which מצוה comes first, and how ownership of an animal passes between a Jew and a נכרי for בכורה

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a question about when you redeem a פטר חמור: right away, or after thirty days. רב ששת says the thirty days is only when delay becomes a sin. רמי בר חמא's ברייתא does not fit that answer, so רבא says the two ברייתות follow רבי אליעזר, who compares the donkey to a firstborn son, and the רבנן, who do not. The משנה then teaches that an owner who will not redeem the donkey breaks its neck. It lists cases where one מצוה comes first: פדיה before עריפה, יעידה before פדיה, יבום before חליצה (later reversed), and the owner's own redemption of הקדש before anyone else's. The פרק ends, and a new משנה teaches that any share a נכרי has in an animal exempts its firstborn from בכורה, while כהנים and לוים are still obligated. The גמרא explains why the מסכת began with the donkey. Then it brings רבי אושעיא's rule that money alone transfers an animal between a Jew and a נכרי. אביי shows from 'מיד עמיתך' that buying from a גוי is done with money. The גמרא then begins the same analysis for a גוי buying from a Jew, and that discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

רַב שֵׁשֶׁת אָמַר:

Rav Sheshet said:

לוֹמַר שֶׁאֵינוֹ עוֹבֵר עָלָיו.

It comes to say that he does not transgress on it.

מֵתִיב רָמֵי בַּר חָמָא:

Rami bar Chama raised an objection:

מִצְוָתוֹ כׇּל שְׁלֹשִׁים יוֹם,

Its מצוה is all thirty days;
