Topic

Exact precision (אפשר לצמצם) in עגלה ערופה, and two male firstborn lambs born together: who gets which, and whether the second owes מתנות.

Overview

The amud opens by explaining that the earlier opinion follows רבי יוסי הגלילי, who holds that exact precision (אפשר לצמצם) is possible. The גמרא then looks at a ברייתא about a body found exactly between two towns (עגלה ערופה). It concludes that the תנאים there argue about the word קרובה, not about precision. Another ברייתא, where the חכמים say the towns share one calf with a condition, proves that the רבנן hold exact precision is impossible, even for things in human hands. The גמרא then returns to the משנה of two male lambs whose heads came out together. רבי טרפון gives the כהן the better lamb, because the healthier one probably came out first. רבי יוחנן explains that according to רבי עקיבא the כהן takes the leaner one, and proves this from the end of the משנה. Next comes the מחלוקת between רבי מאיר and רבי יוסי about whether the second lamb owes מתנות, each with its reason. רבי אלעזר limits that מחלוקת to cases where the כהן already holds a replacement. The amud ends as the גמרא begins a challenge from רבי יוסי's words, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

סָבַר לַהּ כְּרַבִּי יוֹסֵי הַגְּלִילִי,

He holds like Rabbi Yosei HaGelili,

דְּאָמַר: אֶפְשָׁר לְצַמְצֵם בִּידֵי שָׁמַיִם,

who said: it is possible to be exactly precise in Heaven's hands,

וְכׇל שֶׁכֵּן בִּידֵי אָדָם.

and all the more so in human hands.

נֵימָא כְּתַנָּאֵי:

Shall we say it is like an argument of the Tannaim:
