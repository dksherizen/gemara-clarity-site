Topic

The womb and בכורה for twins, and whether a פטר חמור is אסור בהנאה before redemption and after עריפה

Overview

This amud starts by asking whether the חכמים must hold that touching only part of the רחם makes an animal a בכור. רב answers that one newborn does not block another, because מין במינו אינו חוצץ. The גמרא then asks why the owner of a doubtful פטר חמור sets aside a lamb he keeps for himself. The answer is that the lamb removes the איסור, which shows our משנה follows רבי יהודה: an unredeemed פטר חמור is אסור בהנאה, while רבי שמעון permits it. עולא explains each side with a logical rule, and the גמרא defends each rule against challenges from מעשר שני, קידושין, and שביעית. A second explanation ties the dispute to how each תנא reads 'שורך' and 'צאנך'. That reading ends with a קשיא against רבי שמעון. Finally, רבה says even רבי שמעון forbids the donkey after its neck is broken, learning it from עגלה ערופה. רבא proves this from a ברייתא about which forbidden items count as food for impurity.

And the Rabbis: shall we say the Rabbis hold

מִקְצָת רֶחֶם מַקְדֵּישׁ?

that part of the womb sanctifies?

granted that it is impossible to be exactly precise,
