Topic

Which animals may be used to redeem a פטר חמור: the שה/פסח comparison, a בן פקועה, a נדמה, and פסולי המוקדשין.

Overview

The משנה lists animals that may not be used to redeem a פטר חמור, such as a calf, a wild animal, a טריפה, a כלאים and a כוי. רבי אליעזר permits a כלאים. The גמרא explains that the list follows בן בג בג, who links the word שה here to the word שה by the קרבן פסח. The doubled word תפדה then allows female, blemished and older lambs. Next the גמרא asks about a בן פקועה: מר זוטרא forbids it because of the פסח comparison, and רב אשי permits it. It then asks about a נדמה, a sheep that looks like a goat, according to the רבנן. Two proofs are brought from ברייתות, and both are pushed aside. Finally it asks about redeeming with פסולי המוקדשין, and רב מרי rules from 'כצבי וכאיל' that you may not. The amud ends as the גמרא begins to draw a new point from his answer, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳ אֵין פּוֹדִין

MISHNA: One may not redeem

לֹא בְּעֵגֶל וְלֹא בְּחַיָּה,

neither with a calf nor with a wild animal,

לֹא בִּשְׁחוּטָה וְלֹא בִּטְרֵיפָה,

nor with a slaughtered animal nor with a tereifa,

וְלֹא בְּכִלְאַיִם וְלֹא בְּכוֹי.

nor with a hybrid nor with a koy.
