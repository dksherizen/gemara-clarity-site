Topic

Whether רבי שמעון requires ראשו ורובו to permit eating an animal born to a kosher mother, plus a question about donkey urine

Overview

The גמרא asks what רבי שמעון holds: if a cow gives birth to a camel-like animal that has some of its mother's features, may it be eaten, or must its head and most of its body (ראשו ורובו) look like the mother? A first ברייתא is brought, but it may speak only about בכורה. A second ברייתא allows an animal that 'comes with one sign.' It sounds like רבי שמעון, but the גמרא answers that this תנא agrees with him on only one point. This happens twice, the second time after רבי ירמיה explains how a kosher animal can become pregnant from a קלוט born to a cow. Along the way, the גמרא shows that the argument between רבי אליעזר and רבי יהושע is not about זה וזה גורם. Each one keeps his usual rule and reads the פסוק 'שה כשבים ושה עזים' as a special case. Finally, a ברייתא of רבי שמעון about 'גמל גמל' proves that he requires ראשו ורובו even for eating. The amud ends with a new question put to רב ששת about donkey urine, and his answer from the wording of the משנה continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אִישְׁתַּנִּי מִגָּמָל

It is different from a camel

שַׁפִּיר דָּמֵי,

so it is fine (permitted to eat),

אוֹ דִלְמָא לָא שְׁנָא?

or perhaps there is no difference?

תָּא שְׁמַע:

Come and hear:
