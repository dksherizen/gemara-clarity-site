Topic

How מעשר בהמה can be separated without exact counting, and the search for the source of רבא's rule that the tenth animal becomes holy on its own

Overview

This amud opens in the middle of a proof supporting רבי יוסי ברבי יהודה, who holds that an animal separated as מעשר בהמה without proper counting is still valid. The chain runs like this. תרומה can be separated by estimate and by thought. The Torah calls מעשר 'תרומה', so the same is true of מעשר. A היקש then carries the rule over to מעשר בהמה. Next, רבא rules that the tenth animal becomes holy by itself (עשירי מאליו הוא קדוש), even if it is never named or taken out of the pen. The גמרא searches for רבא's source and tries four ברייתות. The first three are rejected, because in each case something else could explain the ruling: the animal was called 'holy', it left the pen or was pointed at, or the flock was exempted by מנין הראוי. The fourth ברייתא says outright that a tenth left in the pen is מעשר. But it clashes with another ברייתא that calls the mistakenly named ninth animal חולין. A תנא before רב ששת resolves the clash by assigning one ברייתא to רבי שמעון בן יהודה, and his words continue on the next amud.

So too, terumah of the tithe is taken

by estimate and by thought.

And the Merciful One calls tithe 'terumah',

as it is written: 'For the tithe of the children of Israel'
