Bechoros 47a

Topic

A convert's earlier children (inheritance and פריה ורביה), and when the son of a לוייה or כהנת is exempt from פדיון הבן.

Overview

This amud opens with the last line of an earlier discussion: the father's power of 'יכיר' is separate from ordinary face recognition. Then רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש argue about a man who had sons as a גוי and then converted. רבי יוחנן says those earlier sons still count, so he has no new בכור for inheritance and he has already fulfilled פריה ורביה. ריש לקיש says 'גר שנתגייר כקטן שנולד', so the earlier sons do not count. The גמרא defends רבי יוחנן against a משנה and a ברייתא, assigning the ברייתא to רבי יוסי הגלילי. Next, רב אדא בר אהבה rules that a לוייה's firstborn son is exempt from פדיון הבן. רב פפא explains that the father was a גוי, while רבא explains that the father was a ישראל but פדיון depends on the mother's womb. A משנה about a כהנת seems to challenge רב פפא, and the גמרא answers that the 'כהנת' there is a בת ישראל whose son is a כהן.

Opening lines

״יַכִּיר״ לְחוּד,

"He shall recognize" is separate,

וְהַכָּרַת פָּנִים לְחוּד.

and recognition of the face is separate.

אִיתְּמַר:

It was stated:

הָיוּ לוֹ בָּנִים בְּגַיּוּתוֹ

if he had sons while he was a gentile

  1. ConclusionClosing line of the previous discussion: two different kinds of recognition
  2. StatementNew dispute: does a convert who already had children have a firstborn for inheritance?
  3. StatementThe reasons: 'first of his strength' versus 'a convert is like a newborn'

  10. ChallengeThe father must be a convert who had sons, a challenge to רבי יוחנן
  11. ResolutionThe father is a Jew with no sons; the mother's status matters only for the כהן

  16. QuestionWho is the father? If he is a כהן or a לוי, why mention a לוייה at all?
  17. ChallengeIf the father is a regular Jew, lineage should follow him
  18. ResolutionThe father was a non-Jew, so the child counts as a לוי
  19. ResolutionThe father was a ישראל, but פדיון הבן depends on the womb
  20. ChallengeA משנה is quoted to challenge רב פפא
  21. ChallengeThe rest of the משנה being used to challenge רב פפא
  22. QuestionInference from the משנה and the question of who the father was
  23. ChallengeThe father cannot be a כהן or לוי

  26. Rejectionרבה rejects the comparison: a לוייה keeps her holiness
  27. Proofברייתא: a captured or violated לוייה still eats מעשר
  28. RejectionA כהנת loses her status after relations with a גוי
  29. ChallengeFine for מר בריה דרב יוסף, but how does רב פפא explain the משנה?
  30. ResolutionThe mother is a בת ישראל who became pregnant from a כהן
  31. QuestionWhy does the משנה call her a כהנת?
  32. ResponseShe is called כהנת because her son is a כהן
