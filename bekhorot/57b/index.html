Topic

The sources of a תנא on מעשר בהמה, רבי ישמעאל בן סתריאל's testimonies, and the משנה of the three גרנות and the tithing new year

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the identification of a תנא who brings יוצא דופן, מחוסר זמן, and יתום into the pen for מעשר בהמה. He follows רבי שמעון on a caesarean birth, רבי שמעון בן יהודה on מחוסר זמן, and רבי יהושע on a 'יתום' whose mother's hide (שלח) still exists. רבי ישמעאל בן סתריאל then testifies before רבי that in his town they dress a newborn in its dead mother's skin, and רבי says this reveals the reason of the משנה. He adds wonder-reports about huge lettuce, a giant cedar, and a בר יוכני egg that flooded sixty cities. The גמרא questions the egg story from a פסוק, and רב אשי answers that it was an unfertilized egg. A new משנה then lists the three yearly deadlines (גרנות) for מעשר בהמה and records the opinions of רבי עקיבא, בן עזאי, and רבי אלעזר with רבי שמעון. It also gives the dispute over whether the tithing year begins on 1 אלול or 1 תשרי. The משנה explains that ראש השנה divides the groups of animals, while a גורן only marks when slaughtering untithed animals becomes forbidden. The גמרא asks why there are three גרנות, and רבה בר שילא answers that there is one for each birthing season. It then asks why these particular times were chosen, and רבי תנחום's answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

סָבַר לַהּ כְּרַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן,

He holds in accordance with Rabbi Shimon,

דְּאָמַר: יוֹצֵא דּוֹפֶן —

who said: one born by caesarean section —

וָלָד מְעַלְּיָא הוּא,

is a full-fledged offspring,

וּדְלָא כְּרַבִּי יוֹחָנָן.

and not in accordance with Rabbi Yoḥanan.
