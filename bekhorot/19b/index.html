Topic

The end of the בכור לדבר אחד debate, and the new משנה about an animal bought from a גוי: when do we rely on רוב?

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an argument about the בכור. רבינא holds that an animal that is first in only one way still counts as a בכור. He reads the extra word 'בכור' as excluding a baby born after a caesarean-born sibling. רב אחא מדפתי brings a case this cannot explain: a female born by caesarean, then a male through the womb. So the גמרא concludes like אביי: a בכור לדבר אחד is not a בכור, and the פרק הלוקח עובר פרתו ends. A new משנה deals with an animal bought from a גוי whose birth history is unknown. רבי ישמעאל decides by the animal's age, while רבי עקיבא says only real knowledge counts, and in a ספק the owner eats the animal once it has a מום. The גמרא asks why רבי ישמעאל doesn't simply follow the רוב of animals, and suggests a difference between a majority in front of us and one that is not. A ברייתא about minors doing יבום challenges that difference, and רבא begins a new answer that continues on the next amud.
