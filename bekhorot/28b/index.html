Topic

Mistaken rulings: רבי מאיר's penalty, eye blemishes after death, and when an erring judge or examiner must pay

Overview

This amud moves through several connected topics about mistaken rulings. First, the גמרא shows from the wording of the משנה that רבי מאיר forbids a בכור slaughtered without an expert as a קנס, a penalty. It then asks whether all eye blemishes change after death, and proves from רבי יאשיה's words that only some do. Next it shows that the unnamed משנה about a non-expert's ruling can fit everyone's view. A ברייתא sets his payment at a quarter for a small animal and half for a large one, and the גמרא explains why. A new משנה teaches that a judge who rules wrongly pays from his own money unless he is a מומחה לבית דין. The גמרא explains that this is a case where the judge caused the loss directly with his own hands, so it does not depend on רבי מאיר's view of דינא דגרמי. Then comes the story of רבי טרפון's mistaken ruling about a cow whose womb was removed, and רבי עקיבא's two reasons why he does not pay. The amud ends as a new משנה begins about a paid examiner of firstborn animals.
