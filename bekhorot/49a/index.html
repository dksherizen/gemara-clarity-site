Topic

פדיון הבן: mixed-up babies, the thirty-day threshold, presumptions after the father's death, and redeeming before day thirty

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a case about two fathers whose babies got mixed up. One baby died before thirty days, so the כהן must return five סלעים. The גמרא says one father can collect for both with a הרשאה from the other. It defends this against נהרדעי's rule about מטלטלין, since the כהן does not deny receiving the money. רב הונא then adds that with two boys and a girl the כהן gets nothing, and the גמרא explains why our משנה left that case out. A new משנה rules that a בכור who dies within thirty days needs no פדיון, and day thirty counts like the days before it. רבי עקיבא treats day thirty as a doubt. The גמרא traces the רבנן's view to a גזירה שוה from the מדבר, and רבי עקיבא's doubt to the rule of שני כתובים הבאים כאחד. רב אשי adds that for mourning everyone treats day thirty as before, following שמואל's rule of leniency in mourning. The last משנה covers what to presume when the father dies, and whether a man redeems himself or his son first. The amud ends with רב and שמואל arguing over a פדיון done before day thirty, and רב brings a comparison to קידושין.

Opening lines

בְּבָא בְּהַרְשָׁאָה.

[It is a case] where he comes with authorization.

וְהָאָמְרִי נְהַרְדָּעֵי:

But didn't the Sages of Neharde'a say:

לָא כָּתְבִינַן אַדְרַכְתָּא אַמִּטַּלְטְלִי!

We do not write an authorization document for movable property!

הָנֵי מִילֵּי הֵיכָא דְּכַפְרֵיהּ,

This applies only where he denied it,
