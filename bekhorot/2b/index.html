Topic

Selling an unborn animal, or only the rights to an animal's fetuses, to a non-Jew

Overview

The גמרא asks whether רבי יהודה forbids selling an unborn animal (עובר) to a non-Jew, just as he forbids selling a large working animal. A ברייתא about a man fined for handing an animal to a non-Jew is pushed off: the fine is really about the mother animal. רב אשי then proves it from רבי יהודה's own reason. He allows selling a broken animal (שבורה) only because it can never heal and work. A fetus will work one day, so it is forbidden. A second version attaches this whole discussion to our משנה and reaches the same result. Next the גמרא asks a new question: may a Jew sell a non-Jew only the rights to an animal's fetuses? It works this out according to both רבי יהודה and the רבנן. A proof from the wording of our משנה is rejected. The amud ends by quoting a ברייתא of רבי יהודה and the חכמים about giving an animal to a non-Jew to raise, and the proof from it continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַאי לָאו אַעוּבָּר?

What, is it not referring to the fetus?

לָא, אַבְּהֵמָה.

No, it is referring to the animal.

וְהָא ״דָּמָיו״ קָתָנֵי!

But doesn't it teach 'its value' (masculine)!

תְּנִי ״דָּמֶיהָ״.

Teach: 'its value' (feminine).
