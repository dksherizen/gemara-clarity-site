Bechoros 32a

בכורות ל״ב ע״א

Topic

Selling blemished מעשר בהמה: the rabbinic ban and its exceptions, folding the price into the bones, and the גזירה שוה from חרמים that forbids selling מעשר but not בכור

Overview

This amud is about selling the meat of a blemished מעשר בהמה. רבא finishes explaining that by Torah law the meat may be sold. The חכמים banned normal sale, but the ban does not reach parts that are not priced while the animal is alive, and orphans may sell normally. רב שמואל בר רב יצחק agrees, learning it from the פסוק "תזבח ואכלת בשר". רבי חייא and רבי שמעון ברבי disagree about folding the meat's price into the bones, and the גמרא shows they do not really argue. The גמרא then asks why a בכור may be sold but מעשר may never be sold. The answer is a גזירה שוה of ״לא יגאל״ from חרמים, and the גמרא works hard to show that the phrase is "free", why it does not also cover בכור, and why ״הוא״ excludes בכור. Finally, רבא offers a shorter way to show the phrase by חרמים is free, and his explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דָּבָר שֶׁאֵינוֹ נִישּׁוֹם מֵחַיִּים —

An item that is not appraised while the animal is alive —

לָא גְּזוּר רַבָּנַן,

the Sages did not decree about it,

וּבְיַתְמֵי אוֹקְמוּהָ רַבָּנַן אַדְּאוֹרָיְיתָא.

and in the case of orphans, the Sages left it standing on Torah law.

וְאַף רַב שְׁמוּאֵל בַּר רַב יִצְחָק

And even Rav Shmuel bar Rav Yitzchak
