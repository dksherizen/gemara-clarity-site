Topic

When late proof can overturn a ruling, and which court a case must go to

Overview

The עמוד opens by finishing the discussion of a שליש (trustee). רב נחמן said a confirmed שטר cannot be weakened by the argument 'she could have burned it.' רבא challenges him from a ברייתא about a receipt, and רב נחמן is refuted. Next, the גמרא asks when a losing litigant may still bring new proof. רב דימי's report of רבי יוחנן is rejected. רב שמואל בר יהודה's version stands: once he told בית דין 'I have no witnesses, no proof,' he is blocked, unless the proof was something he could not have known about. Then the גמרא discusses whether a case is heard locally or at the central court (מקום הוועד). רבי אלעזר rules it stays local, the יבמה travels to the יבם for חליצה, and אמימר concludes that a lender can demand the central court while a borrower cannot. The עמוד ends with a letter to מר עוקבא, two explanations of its wording, and the הדרן closing פרק זה בורר.
