Sanhedrin 40b

סנהדרין מ׳ ע״ב

Open Sanhedrin 40b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The seven חקירות: their source, how far they extend, a possible eighth question, and the source for התראה

Overview

The משנה requires seven חקירות (questions to the witnesses), and the גמרא found them spread across three פסוקים. It explains that the shared word "היטב" creates a free גזירה שוה linking the three, so all seven questions apply to stoning and sword cases even though the cases differ. Strangling and burning cases are then added by a קל וחומר. Because רבי שמעון holds that burning is harsher, רב יהודה finds one spare word in the פסוקים to teach about burning. רבי אבהו suggests the spare word might add an eighth question instead, and the גמרא tests possible candidates. Next comes the debate between רבי יוסי and the חכמים over whether the court asks every time question even when it seems pointless. A ברייתא then lists the essential questions in murder and idol worship cases. Finally, עולא begins to show from the word "וראה" that the Torah requires התראה, an argument that finishes on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְאֵימָא: חֲדָא חֲדָא כְּדִכְתִיבָא?

But say: each one is as it is written?

דְּאִם כֵּן,

For if so,

לִיכְתְּבִינְהוּ רַחֲמָנָא בַּחֲדָא!

let the Merciful One write them in one place!

כֵּיוָן דְּכוּלְּהוּ בַּהֲדֵי הֲדָדֵי כְּתִיבָא,

Since they are all written alike,
