Topic

Praise of פלטי בן ליש; the mourning king in the משנה; why אבנר was punished; what a דרגש is

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a ranking of three men who held back from sin: יוסף, בועז, and פלטי בן ליש, with פלטי at the top. רבי יוחנן and רבי יונתן read פסוקים from משלי 31 as hints to this ranking. Two more readings of 'Grace is deceitful, beauty is vain' apply it to generations instead. One of them ends with the generation of רבי יהודה בר אילעאי, whose poor students shared one cloak and still learned תורה. Then a new משנה teaches that a mourning king does not leave his palace. רבי יהודה says he may follow the bier, as דוד did for אבנר, and the חכמים answer that דוד only did it to calm the people. The גמרא brings a ברייתא with the same argument about where women walk at a funeral. It then discusses why אבנר was punished: either he failed to protest to שאול, or he delayed דוד's kingdom. Finally, the גמרא asks what a דרגש is. עולא's 'bed of fortune' is rejected, רבין says it is a leather bed, and רבי יוחנן's explanation continues on the next amud.
