Sanhedrin 101b

סנהדרין ק״א ע״ב

Topic

מנשה and the value of suffering, אבא שאול on saying ה׳'s Name, and how ירבעם's arrogance led him to the golden calves and cost him עולם הבא.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing רבי עקיבא's proof that suffering is precious. חזקיה taught Torah to the whole world and surely taught his son מנשה, yet only suffering brought מנשה back to ה׳. A ברייתא then lists three who came to ה׳ with a demand: קין, עשו and מנשה. The גמרא returns to the משנה and explains אבא שאול's ruling: one who says ה׳'s Name as written, outside the מקדש and in casual speech, loses עולם הבא. Next it turns to the kings who lose עולם הבא, starting with ירבעם. It explains his name and his father נבט's names, and lists three who 'looked but did not see': נבט, אחיתופל and פרעה's astrologers. A פסוק proves ירבעם loses עולם הבא. רבי יוחנן and רב נחמן explain how he rose by rebuking שלמה and fell through public disrespect and arrogance. Fearing he would look like רחבעם's servant in the עזרה, ירבעם made two golden calves. He tricked the elders into agreeing by seating a wicked man next to each righteous one.
