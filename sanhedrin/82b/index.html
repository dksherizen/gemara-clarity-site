Sanhedrin 82b

סנהדרין פ״ב ע״ב

Topic

The end of the story of פנחס and זמרי, and whether a כהן who served while טמא is liable to מיתה בידי שמים

Overview

The amud finishes the story of פנחס and זמרי. פנחס tricks שבט שמעון into letting him in, and רבי יוחנן lists six miracles that helped him kill זמרי and כזבי. פנחס then argues with הקב"ה to stop the plague, which רבי אלעזר learns from the word ויפלל. The tribes mock his family background, but the תורה traces his line to אהרן, and הקב"ה has משה greet him with peace. The גמרא then gives more details about the sin and explains the real names of כזבי and זמרי. Next it returns to the משנה's case of a כהן who served while טמא. רב ששת proves from the משנה that he is not liable to מיתה בידי שמים. רב אחא בר הונא pushes back with the rule that people do not kill someone the Torah exempts, and he answers each of רב ששת's four counter-cases. The amud ends as רב אחא בר הונא starts a challenge from a ברייתא about Temple tasks done outside the מקדש, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

נִשְׁעָן וְהוֹלֵךְ עַל מַקְלוֹ,

walking along, leaning on his staff,

וְכֵיוָן שֶׁהִגִּיעַ אֵצֶל שִׁבְטוֹ שֶׁל שִׁמְעוֹן

and once he reached the tribe of Shimon

אָמַר: הֵיכָן מָצִינוּ

he said: Where do we find

שֶׁשִּׁבְטוֹ שֶׁל לֵוִי גָּדוֹל מִשֶּׁל שִׁמְעוֹן?

that the tribe of Levi is greater than that of Shimon?
