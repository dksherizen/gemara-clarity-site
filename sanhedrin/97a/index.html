Sanhedrin 97a

סנהדרין צ״ז ע״א

Topic

Signs of the generation of משיח, the dangers of calculating his arrival, and the six-thousand-year timeline of the world

Overview

This amud continues the גמרא's discussion of when משיח comes. רבי יצחק quotes רבי יוחנן: the generation of בן דוד will have few scholars, deep sorrow, and troubles that come one after another. A ברייתא lays out the seven-year cycle before משיח, and אביי answers רב יוסף's challenge by pointing out that the full signs, in order, never happened. Several תנאים (רבי יהודה, רבי נהוראי, רבי נחמיה) describe a generation with no shame, no respect, and no truth, and רבא tells the story of a town called קושטא where even one white lie brought death. A ברייתא learns from a פסוק in דברים that משיח comes only at a low point: informers, few students, poverty, or despair. רבי זירא warns against calculating the date, since משיח comes when no one expects him. The amud ends with teachings about the world's timeline: six thousand years, a period of ruin, and three eras of תוהו, תורה, and ימות המשיח.

Opening lines

אֶת סֻכַּת דָּוִיד הַנֹּפֶלֶת״.

the Tabernacle of David that is fallen.

אֲמַר לֵיהּ: הָכִי אָמַר רַבִּי יוֹחָנָן:

He said to him: This is what Rabbi Yochanan says:

דּוֹר שֶׁבֶּן דָּוִד בָּא בּוֹ,

The generation in which the son of David comes,

תַּלְמִידֵי חֲכָמִים מִתְמַעֲטִים,

Torah scholars become fewer,
