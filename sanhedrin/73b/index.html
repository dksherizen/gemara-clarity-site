Sanhedrin 73b

סנהדרין ע״ג ע״ב

Open Sanhedrin 73b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Why the פסוק of the betrothed נערה uses the words חטא, מות, נער and נערה: which forbidden relations allow saving the victim at the cost of the attacker's life (רודף), and which sins are excluded.

Overview

The amud opens in the middle of a צריכותא on the פסוק 'אין לנערה חטא מות'. The word 'חטא' is needed to include all עריות, 'מות' keeps out plain לאוין, and 'חטא' again brings in כריתות. If 'חטא מות' covers everything, the words 'נער' and 'נערה' come to exclude cases: עבודה זרה, בהמה and שבת, where we do not kill a person to stop the sin. The גמרא then shows how רבי שמעון בן יוחי and רבי אלעזר ברבי שמעון, who do not exclude עבודה זרה or שבת, use these two words. Next it returns to the ברייתא: רבי יהודה says that when the girl tells rescuers 'leave him alone' out of fear, the attacker is not killed. רבא explains that the רבנן base the law on her degradation, while רבי יהודה bases it on her readiness to die. אביי adds that only a big degradation counts, not a widow with a כהן גדול. Finally, the גמרא asks why a brother who rapes his sister pays a קנס if he could be killed. רב חסדא answers with two separate acts, and רבא answers that she gave in, following רבי יהודה.

Opening lines

מִשּׁוּם דְּהַאי לָאו אוֹרְחֵיהּ הוּא,

because for this one it is not his natural way,

וְהָא קָא פָגֵים לַהּ.

and that one, he degrades her.

אֲבָל שְׁאָר עֲרָיוֹת דְּאוֹרְחַיְיהוּ,

But the other forbidden women, for whom it is their natural way,

וְלָא נְפִישׁ פִּיגְמַיְיהוּ, אֵימָא לָא.

and their degradation is not great, I would say: no.
