Sanhedrin 95b

סנהדרין צ״ה ע״ב

Learn Sanhedrin 95b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Sanhedrin 95b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

יעקב's miraculous journey, the near-destruction of דוד's line, and the downfall of סנחריב's enormous army.

Overview

This amud opens by finishing the proof that the land 'jumped' for יעקב, so that he suddenly arrived at the place where his fathers prayed. It then reads ויפגע as תפילה and explains that the sun set early so יעקב would sleep there, which was later balanced by the sun rising early for him. Next the גמרא shows that דוד's line was nearly wiped out by עתליה, and that יואש survived only because אביתר had survived the killing of the כהנים in נוב. The גמרא then describes the enormous army of סנחריב: its divisions, the size of its camp, and how it drained a river while crossing. It explains that the 185,000 struck by the מלאך were only the officers, and lists many opinions on how they were struck and how many survived. Finally, רבי אבהו brings the startling פסוק about ה׳ 'shaving' the king of אשור. The story behind it begins with ה׳ appearing to סנחריב as an old man, and it continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דִּכְתִיב: ״וַיֵּצֵא יַעֲקֹב מִבְּאֵר שָׁבַע

As it is written: 'And Jacob left Beersheba

וַיֵּלֶךְ חָרָנָה״,

and went to Haran,'

וּכְתִיב: ״וַיִּפְגַּע בַּמָּקוֹם

and it is written: 'And he reached the place,

וַיָּלֶן שָׁם כִּי בָא הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ״.

and he slept there, for the sun had set.'
