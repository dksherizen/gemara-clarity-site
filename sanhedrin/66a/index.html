Topic

End of ניחוש; the משניות on מחלל שבת and on cursing a parent, and the sources for the punishment and the warning against cursing a parent

Overview

This amud finishes the examples of ניחוש, treating ordinary events or animals as signs of luck. A new משנה then teaches that one who desecrates Shabbos is stoned, but only for an act that brings כרת if done on purpose and a חטאת if done by accident. The גמרא infers that some desecration of Shabbos has neither punishment, such as תחומין according to רבי עקיבא or הבערה according to רבי יוסי. The next משנה teaches that one who curses a parent is stoned only if he uses the שם, and the גמרא identifies the חכמים who exempt a כינוי as רבי מנחם ברבי יוסי. A ברייתא then works through the פסוק about cursing a parent: who is included, whether one parent alone is enough (רבי יאשיה and רבי יונתן), and that the death is סקילה. Next it searches for the אזהרה. It tries a הצד השוה from the דיין, the נשיא and the חרש, but rejects it and learns the warning from the extra word אֱלֹהִים instead. Finally the גמרא shows how this works even for the view that אֱלֹהִים means ה׳, pointing to the Torah's choice of the word תְקַלֵּל.
