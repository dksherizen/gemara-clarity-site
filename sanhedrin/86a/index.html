Sanhedrin 86a

סנהדרין פ״ו ע״א

Open Sanhedrin 86a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Laws of kidnapping: who is exempt, the half-slave, the warning against kidnapping, and witnesses to half the crime

Overview

The amud opens with a ברייתא saying that a kidnapper who sells the victim to his own family is liable. רב ששת changes it to 'exempt,' because an unnamed ספרי follows רבי שמעון, and he requires the victim to be taken out of his family's domain. Next the גמרא explains why the רבנן exempt a father who kidnaps his son. The words ונמצא בידו leave out someone already in the kidnapper's hand, and רבא extends this to teachers and their students. The גמרא then turns to the half-slave. It compares רבי יהודה's view that slaves get no בושת, and explains his ruling through the rule of מיעוט אחר מיעוט, while the רבנן read the same פסוק differently. It then finds the warning against kidnapping, and two ברייתות use דבר הלמד מעניינו to show that 'לא תגנב' is about kidnapping and 'לא תגנבו' is about money. The amud ends with חזקיה and רבי יוחנן arguing whether witnesses to only the stealing or only the selling are killed as זוממין. Their dispute depends on whether 'half a matter' counts as testimony, and the case of the בן סורר ומורה continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

תָּנֵי תַּנָּא קַמֵּיהּ דְּרַב שֵׁשֶׁת.

A tanna recited before Rav Sheshet.

אֲמַר לֵיהּ:

He said to him:

אֲנִי שׁוֹנֶה רַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן אוֹמֵר

I teach that Rabbi Shimon says:

״מֵאֶחָיו״ –

"Of his brethren"
