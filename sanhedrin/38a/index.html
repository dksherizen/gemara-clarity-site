Topic

Exile as kindness, and why אדם הראשון was created alone, last, and from dust of the whole world

Overview

This amud finishes the drashos on the names of יכניה's descendants: שאלתיאל's miraculous birth, God undoing His oath, and זרובבל, who was really נחמיה בן חכליה. Next comes a story. After some wine at רבי's table, רבי חייא's sons say that משיח will not come until the houses of the ראש גולה and the נשיא come to an end. רבי חייא defends them with the line 'wine goes in, a secret comes out.' The גמרא then explains that God hurried the exile as a kindness. The exile of צדקיהו came while the Torah scholars of יכניה's exile were still alive, and according to עולא it came two years before ונושנתם. The גמרא then returns to the משנה and brings ברייתות about why אדם was created alone, why every face is different, and why people differ in voice, looks and mind. It also explains why אדם was created on ערב שבת, using the feast in משלי 9. The amud ends with רבי מאיר teaching that אדם's dust was gathered from the whole world. A new statement of רב אושעיא begins at the very end and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְהִיא נִתְעַבְּרָה מְעוּמָּד.

and she conceived while standing.

דָּבָר אַחֵר: שַׁלְתִּיאֵל –

Another explanation: Shealtiel —

שֶׁנִּשְׁאַל עַל אָלָתוֹ אֵל.

that God asked about His oath.

זְרוּבָּבֶל – שֶׁנִּזְרַע בְּבָבֶל.

Zerubbabel — that he was sown in Babylonia.
