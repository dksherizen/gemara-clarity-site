Sanhedrin 42a

סנהדרין מ״ב ע״א

Learn Sanhedrin 42a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Sanhedrin 42a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

End of ברכת הלבנה, then the close of פרק היו בודקין: witnesses' timing, the student who argues for acquittal, judges and wine, a deadlocked court, and נזדקן הדין

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the discussion of ברכת הלבנה. Both time limits are readings of רבי יוחנן. The moon gets no הטוב והמטיב because its changes are its normal nature. Saying the ברכה on time is like greeting the שכינה, so אביי rules that it is said standing. רב יהודה's full text of the ברכה is given. After a short teaching that winning the 'war' of Torah needs 'bundles' of משנה, the גמרא returns to the משנה. Witnesses who disagree about before sunrise versus during sunrise have void testimony. A student who argues for acquittal stays seated all day when his point is weak. Judges avoid wine because they deal with 'the secret of the world.' A court that stays deadlocked sets the defendant free. The amud ends by explaining the phrase נזדקן הדין, read either as 'the case is stuck' or 'the case has grown wise.' Then the הדרן closes פרק היו בודקין.

Opening lines

וְתַרְוַיְיהוּ כְּרַבִּי יוֹחָנָן סְבִירָא לְהוּ:

And both of them hold like Rabbi Yochanan:

הָא לְמִיהְוֵי כִּי יִתְרָא,

this one: until it becomes like a bowstring,

הָא לְמִיהְוֵי כִּי נָפְיָא.

that one: until it becomes like a sieve.

אֲמַר לֵיהּ רַב אַחָא מִדִּיפְתִּי לְרָבִינָא:

Rav Acha of Difti said to Ravina:
