Sanhedrin 79b

סנהדרין ע״ט ע״ב

Topic

Killing the wrong person according to דבי חזקיה, and a new משנה on convicts who got mixed up: who goes free, who gets which death, and who the 'others' are.

Overview

The amud opens at the end of רבי's proof that someone who aimed at one person and killed another pays money. רבא then shows from a ברייתא of דבי חזקיה, comparing killing an animal to killing a person, that this killer is neither executed nor made to pay. That view disagrees with both רבי and רבנן. A new משנה follows. If a murderer gets mixed in with others, all go free, but רבי יהודה says they are locked in the כיפה. If people sentenced to different deaths get mixed up, all receive the lightest one. רבי שמעון and חכמים argue whether שריפה or סקילה is harsher, and whether חנק or סייף is harsher, each bringing proofs. The גמרא then asks who the 'others' in the משנה are, since they cannot be innocent people. שמואל says they are sentenced murderers mixed with one whose verdict is not finished. ריש לקיש says the argument is really about oxen. רבא begins a third answer, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines
