Topic

The two names of רבן יוחנן בן זכאי, how witnesses' 'I do not know' affects their testimony in חקירות and בדיקות, how long a date mix-up is excused, and how long one may bless the new moon.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an explanation: the משנה calls him 'בן זכאי' and a ברייתא calls him 'רבן יוחנן בן זכאי' because one name is from his student years and the other from his teaching years. The גמרא then asks why the משנה needs to say that in בדיקות the testimony stands 'even if two' witnesses do not know. רב ששת connects the phrase to חקירות, following רבי עקיבא. רבא rejects that reading and explains that even in חקירות, two witnesses who know are enough, which is not like רבי עקיבא. In a story, רמי בר חמא asks רב כהנא and רב ספרא why חקירות and בדיקות are treated differently when both come from the Torah. They answer that without time and place the testimony cannot be checked through הזמה, but missing side details still leave הזמה possible. Next, רבי יוחנן rules that witnesses who are one day apart can be excused only until most of the month has passed. רבא tries to prove this from the משנה, but the proof is pushed off with the reasoning about שופר blasts. The amud ends with another teaching from רבי יוחנן: the blessing on the new moon may be said until it is filled in, which is either seven days or sixteen days.

like a student sitting before his teacher.
