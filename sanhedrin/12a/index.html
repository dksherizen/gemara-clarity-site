Sanhedrin 12a

סנהדרין י״ב ע״א

Open Sanhedrin 12a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

When בית דין may and may not add a leap month (עיבור השנה): famine, timing around ראש השנה, שביעית, and טומאה

Overview

This amud deals with when בית דין may add an extra month to the year (עיבור השנה), and when it may not. It opens with the rule that we do not add a month in years of famine, and proves it from אלישע. Even in בעל שלישה, the fastest-ripening place, only barley was ripe after the עומר, yet אלישע did not add a month because people were hungry. Next, a ברייתא rules that a leap year may not be set before ראש השנה. A coded letter sent to רבא seems to show the Sages doing it in אב, and the answer is that they may calculate early but may not announce it. The גמרא then shows from שלמה's officers that the code word נציב means a month. More limits follow: no deciding a year ahead, no three leap years in a row (the story of רבי עקיבא in prison is pushed aside), and no leap year in שביעית or the year after, where בית רבן גמליאל disagreed. The amud ends with a dispute about adding a month because of טומאה, where רבי יהודה brings the story of חזקיה. That story continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בִּשְׁנֵי רְעָבוֹן.

during years of famine.

תַּנְיָא, רַבִּי אוֹמֵר:

It is taught: Rabbi says:

״וְאִישׁ בָּא מִבַּעַל שָׁלִישָׁה

"And a man came from Baal Shalishah

וַיָּבֵא לְאִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים

and he brought to the man of God

The steps (24)

  1. StatementNo leap year during years of famine
  2. Proofרבי quotes the story of the man from בעל שלישה
  3. ProofEven in the fastest-ripening place, only one crop was ready
  4. ProofIt was barley, and it was after the עומר
  5. ConclusionThe year deserved a leap month, but famine stopped אלישע
  6. StatementNo leap year before ראש השנה; in an emergency, right after
  7. ChallengeA coded letter to רבא: two messengers were caught
  8. ChallengeThe Sages set up a leap year in the month of Av
  9. ResolutionCalculate early, but announce only later
  10. QuestionHow do we know נציב means a month?
  11. ResponseSolomon's twelve officers, one for each month
  12. ChallengeBut the פסוק mentions one more officer
  13. ResolutionThe extra officer: a head officer or the leap month
  14. StatementNo leap year decided a year ahead, and not three in a row
  15. Proofרבי שמעון's story: רבי עקיבא made three leap years in a row
  16. RejectionThe חכמים reject the proof: each year was set at its own time
  17. StatementNo leap year in שביעית or the year after; the usual time is the year before
  18. Statementבית רבן גמליאל made leap years in the year after שביעית
  19. StatementThis matches a dispute about bringing vegetables from outside ארץ ישראל
  20. QuestionWhat is the real point of the vegetable dispute?
  21. Responseרבי ירמיה: the dispute is about clods of earth
  22. StatementNo leap year because of טומאה
  23. Statementרבי יהודה says a leap year may be made because of טומאה
  24. ProofThe story of חזקיה making a leap year because of טומאה
