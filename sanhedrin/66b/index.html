Sanhedrin 66b

סנהדרין ס״ו ע״ב

Learn Sanhedrin 66b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Sanhedrin 66b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The conditions for סקילה with a נערה המאורסה, whether both parties must be equally punishable, and רבי's reading of 'תחל' for a בת כהן.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier discussion: the extra letter in 'לא תקלל' teaches two prohibitions. Then a new משנה teaches that a man who sleeps with a נערה המאורסה gets סקילה only if she is a נערה, a בתולה, a מאורסה, and still in her father's house. If two men sleep with her, the first gets סקילה and the second gets חנק. רב says this משנה follows רבי מאיר, who leaves out a קטנה, and רבינא proves it from the words 'עד שתהא'. רב rules that according to רבי מאיר, a man who sleeps with a betrothed קטנה loses only סקילה and still gets חנק. He is challenged from 'ומתו גם שניהם' and stays silent. The גמרא explains that this is a dispute between רבי יאשיה and רבי יונתן, and shows how each one uses the other's פסוק. Finally, רבי reads 'כי תחל' about a בת כהן as 'first', and two אמוראים explain him differently: as following רבי ישמעאל or as following רבי מאיר. The last question, what רבי's 'וכן' means according to רב יוסף, is answered on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַאי ״לֹא תְקַלֵּל״?

What is [the meaning of] 'You shall not curse'?

שְׁמַע מִינַּהּ תַּרְתֵּי.

Learn from it two [prohibitions].

מַתְנִי׳ הַבָּא עַל נַעֲרָה הַמְאוֹרָסָה

MISHNA: One who has relations with a betrothed young woman

אֵינוֹ חַיָּיב, עַד שֶׁתְּהֵא נַעֲרָה,

is not liable unless she is a young woman,

The steps (25)

  1. ConclusionThe extra letter in the word for cursing teaches two prohibitions
  2. StatementThe conditions for the death penalty of a betrothed young woman
  3. StatementWhen two men sleep with her, they get different punishments
  4. StatementA ברייתא explains what each condition leaves out
  5. StatementThe משנה follows רבי מאיר, but חכמים include a minor
  6. ChallengeHow do we know the משנה follows רבי מאיר?
  7. ResolutionThe words 'until she is' prove it is רבי מאיר
  8. QuestionAccording to רבי מאיר, is a man who sleeps with a betrothed קטנה fully exempt?
  9. Responseרב: he is only spared סקילה
  10. Challenge'They shall both die' requires both to be equal, and רב is silent
  11. QuestionWhy did רב stay silent?
  12. ResponseIt is an argument between תנאים
  13. QuestionWhat does רבי יונתן learn from 'both shall die'?
  14. Responseרבא: it excludes מעשה חידודים
  15. ResponseWhy רבי יאשיה doesn't need that lesson
  16. QuestionWhat does רבי יאשיה learn from 'alone'?
  17. Responseרבי יאשיה uses 'לבדו' for the case of ten men
  18. Statementרבי reads 'תחל' as 'first'
  19. QuestionWhat does רבי mean?
  20. Responseרבי holds like רבי ישמעאל: only an ארוסה is burned
  21. QuestionWhy does רבי say 'likewise'?
  22. ResponseBoth פסוקים speak of first intercourse
  23. RejectionMy teacher did not explain it that way
  24. Responseרבי follows רבי מאיר: burning only if her sin is what first profanes her
  25. QuestionSo how does 'and likewise' fit now?
