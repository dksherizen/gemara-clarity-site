Topic

How many judges sit on the סנהדרי גדולה; the story of אלדד ומידד; how a majority of two can arise; and rules and qualifications for the סנהדרין

Overview

The amud finishes the מחלוקת over whether the סנהדרי גדולה has 71 judges (the רבנן, who count משה with the seventy) or 70 (רבי יהודה, who reads each 'with you' differently). A ברייתא then tells the story of אלדד ומידד, who 'remained in the camp'. One view says their names were left in the lottery box. The ברייתא explains משה's lottery and compares it to a similar lottery for the extra בכורות. רבי שמעון says instead that they stayed behind out of humility, and their reward was prophecy that never stopped. The גמרא lists three views on what they prophesied and tests each one against what יהושע and משה said in the פסוקים. It then returns to the משנה's count of 23 judges and asks how a majority of exactly two can ever happen. רבי אבהו answers that it happens once judges are added, or in a court of seventy. The amud ends with rules for the סנהדרין: a unanimous guilty vote frees the defendant, and judges need special qualities and very sharp reasoning. רב begins to show how a שרץ could be argued pure, and the argument itself comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

עִמָּךְ״,

"...with you" (במדבר 11:16).

״עִמָּךְ״ – וְאַתְּ בַּהֲדַיְיהוּ.

"With you" means: and you are together with them.

וְרַבִּי יְהוּדָה:

And רבי יהודה?

״עִמָּךְ״ מִשּׁוּם שְׁכִינָה.

"With you" is said because of the Divine Presence.
