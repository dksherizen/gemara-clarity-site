Topic

Reversing a mistaken money ruling: several answers to the contradiction between our משנה and the משנה in בכורות

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier teaching: in דיני נפשות, the court begins its discussion with reasons to acquit. Then the גמרא quotes our משנה, which says a money ruling can be reopened. It contrasts this with a משנה in בכורות, which says a judge's mistaken ruling stays in place (מה שעשה עשוי) and the judge pays from his own money. רב יוסף splits the cases by expert versus non-expert, but this answer is rejected. רב נחמן then splits them by whether a greater court exists to review the ruling. רב ששת splits them by the type of mistake: an error in דבר משנה is reversed, while an error in שיקול הדעת stands. The גמרא then defines both terms. רב המנונא challenges רב ששת from the story of רבי טרפון and the cow, and רבא explains the force of that challenge. Finally, רב חסדא splits the cases by whether the judge physically handed over the item. The גמרא rereads our משנה to fit his answer, and the comparison to capital cases continues on the next amud.
