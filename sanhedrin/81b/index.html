Sanhedrin 81b

סנהדרין פ״א ע״ב

Open Sanhedrin 81b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The כיפה for repeat sinners and unconvictable killers, and the משנה of sinners whom קנאין may strike

Overview

The amud opens with the last line of the previous discussion: רבא tells רב אדא that a person cannot be killed with two death penalties. A new משנה then teaches that someone who was given מלקות and sinned again is locked in the כיפה and fed barley until he dies. ריש לקיש explains that this applies only to כרת sins, where the sinner already deserves death and has given up on his life. רבי יעקב narrows it further to repeating the same כרת sin. The גמרא then asks how many times are needed before the כיפה, using two ברייתות where the חכמים say three times and אבא שאול says four. It also describes the כיפה as a cell the person's exact height. A second משנה puts a killer who cannot be convicted for lack of valid witnesses into the כיפה, and the גמרא gives three ways the court could know he killed, then explains how the diets in the two משניות fit together. Finally, a third משנה lists sinners whom קנאין may strike, a כהן who served while טמא, and a זר who served. The גמרא explains קסוה and the curse 'with a קוסם', and begins a question from רב כהנא about the ארמית that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֲמַר לֵיהּ: אַדָּא בְּרִי,

He (Rava) said to him: Adda, my son,

בִּתְרֵי קְטָלֵי קָטְלַתְּ לֵיהּ?

will you kill him with two death penalties?

מַתְנִי׳ מִי שֶׁלָּקָה וְשָׁנָה,

MISHNA: One who was flogged and repeated (the sin),

בֵּית דִּין מַכְנִיסִין אוֹתוֹ לַכִּיפָּה,

the court places him into the vaulted chamber,
