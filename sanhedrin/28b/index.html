Topic

Which relatives by marriage may not testify, and whether a relationship that ends still disqualifies

Overview

This amud works out which relatives by marriage may not testify. רב was asked whether a man may testify about his stepson's wife, and the answer is that he may not. סורא and פומבדיתא report this answer with two different reasons, and the second reason is backed by a פסוק. The גמרא then asks why the משנה lists the stepfather's son. רבי ירמיה and רב חסדא explain that line in two different ways, and they disagree about the half-brother's half-brother. Next come rulings about the fathers of a bride and groom, who may testify for each other, and about a man's betrothed wife, whom he may not testify about because he already feels close to her. The גמרא sorts out a מחלוקת between רבי יהודה and רבי יוסי about the stepson and the brother-in-law. It then brings שמואל's ruling like רבי יוסי, which leads to a real case: רב יוסף could not uphold a gift document signed by two brothers-in-law. The amud ends with rulings on whether a former relative becomes fit again, and it opens a story about מר עוקבא's in-laws that continues on the next amud.
