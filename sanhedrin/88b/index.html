Sanhedrin 88b

סנהדרין פ״ח ע״ב

Open Sanhedrin 88b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Forgiving a זקן ממרא, the ladder of courts, when a זקן ממרא becomes liable, and why only תפילין fits his liability

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a ברייתא: parents may forgive a בן סורר ומורה, but the חכמים of the South refused to let a court forgive a זקן ממרא, so that disputes would not spread in ישראל. The גמרא calls this a תיובתא. רבי יוסי then describes how courts once kept the law united. A question climbed from the local court, to the courts at the הר הבית and the עזרה, and up to the great court in the לשכת הגזית, which answered from tradition or by majority vote. Disputes multiplied only when students of שמאי and הלל did not serve their teachers enough. The ברייתא also explains how wise, humble judges were promoted up the same ladder, and a message from ארץ ישראל describes who is fit for עולם הבא. The גמרא then asks what is new about a זקן ממרא who acts on his own ruling. It answers that he no longer needs a warning, and for a מסית, his excuse is no longer accepted. A new משנה teaches that he is liable only for adding to rulings of the Sages, like a fifth box in תפילין. רבי אושעיא says only תפילין fits this rule, and the גמרא shows why לולב does not fit. Its answer about ציצית continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בֵּן סוֹרֵר וּמוֹרֶה,

A stubborn and rebellious son,

שֶׁרָצוּ אָבִיו וְאִמּוֹ לִמְחוֹל לוֹ –

whose father and mother wanted to forgive him –

מוֹחֲלִין לוֹ.

they may forgive him.

זָקֵן מַמְרֵא שֶׁרָצוּ בֵּית דִּינוֹ לִמְחוֹל לוֹ –

A rebellious elder whose court wanted to forgive him –
