Topic

The limit of מלקות, three readings of 'עיבור החדש בשלשה', and the stages of judges for עיבור השנה

Overview

The amud opens at the end of a discussion about מלקות. Someone asks why the court doesn't give one more lash, since the man is still alive when it lands. רב אשי answers from 'ונקלה אחיך לעיניך': after the whipping he must still be 'your brother', meaning alive. Next the גמרא asks why the משנה says 'עיבור החדש בשלשה', since a month gets longer by itself if nobody declares the new month. אביי changes the wording to 'קידוש החדש', but רבא objects. Three readings that keep the word 'עיבור' follow. רבא says קידוש on the 30th day needs three, like רבי אלעזר בן צדוק. רב נחמן says קידוש on the 31st day needs three, like פלימו. רב אשי says the משנה means only the calculation, like רבי אליעזר, who holds that months are never formally sanctified. Then a ברייתא explains רבן שמעון בן גמליאל's stages for עיבור השנה: three judges begin, five debate, and seven decide, with a majority 'no' ending the process at any stage. The numbers three, five and seven are tied either to ברכת כהנים or to the king's officials, and רב יוסף's ברייתא backs the second view. A short exchange between אביי and רב יוסף follows. The amud ends with a memory aid and the start of a ברייתא on when a year may be extended, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לִמְחֲיֵיהּ עֶשְׂרִים וַחֲדָא,

Let them strike him twenty-one lashes,

וְכִי מָיֵית בְּהָךְ חֲדָא – לֵימוּת,

and if he dies from that one, let him die,

דְּהָא כִּי מָחֵי

for when they strike

אַגַּבָּא דְּחַיָּיא קָא מָחֵי.

it is on the back of a living person that they strike.
