Topic

How each kind of holy property found in an עיר הנדחת is treated: altar animals, בכור and מעשר, תרומה, and מעשר שני

Overview

The משנה said that holy property in an עיר הנדחת is redeemed. A ברייתא gives the details: altar animals die, בדק הבית items are redeemed, תרומה rots, and מעשר שני and holy writings are hidden away. רבי שמעון learns from 'its animals' and 'its spoils' that בכור, מעשר, and holy money are not the city's property at all. The גמרא asks why altar animals die instead of being sold after they get a מום. רבי יוחנן answers that a wicked person's offering is an abomination. ריש לקיש answers that these animals count as the owner's property, and the גמרא ends up applying this to קדשים קלים following רבי יוסי הגלילי. Next the גמרא explains which בכור and מעשר רבי שמעון means, and shows that שמואל disagrees. רב חסדא then explains which תרומה rots, and rewords his rule after רב יוסף objects. Finally, רב יוסף challenges רב חסדא's view that מעשר שני outside ירושלים belongs to Heaven. The גמרא answers that the משנה is about impure food bought with מעשר שני money inside ירושלים.

Opening lines

וְהַהֶקְדֵּשׁוֹת שֶׁבָּהּ יִפָּדוּ כּוּ׳.

And the consecrated property in it must be redeemed, etc.

תָּנוּ רַבָּנַן: הָיוּ בָּהּ קׇדְשֵׁי קֳדָשִׁים,

The Rabbis taught: If there were sacred offerings in it,

קׇדְשֵׁי מִזְבֵּחַ – יָמוּתוּ,

those consecrated for the altar must die;

קׇדְשֵׁי בֶּדֶק הַבַּיִת – יִפָּדוּ,

those consecrated for Temple upkeep must be redeemed;
