Topic

Carrying out stoning, the witnesses' hands and קרא כדכתיב, and who is hung after execution

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a derivation from the doubled verb סקול יסקל. It then explains how the stoning is carried out: both witnesses lift the heavy stone, but the second witness throws it alone so it lands with force. The משנה gives the law for when all of ישראל must join in, even though in practice the first stone always killed. A new stone was prepared after each execution, because the tools are buried near the executed person. שמואל rules that if the witnesses' hands were cut off after the trial, the person is not executed, because the פסוק requires the witnesses' hand to be first. The גמרא defends this rule. The murderer and the גואל הדם may be handled 'in any way', but they are two פסוקים that teach the same thing, so they are exceptions and do not set a general rule. Proofs for קרא כדכתיב are then tried from בן סורר ומורה and from עיר הנדחת, and the גמרא concludes that the question is a dispute between תנאים, as seen in the מצורע missing a right thumb. Finally, a new משנה asks which executed people are hung after death. The גמרא begins explaining that רבי אליעזר and the חכמים read the פסוקים with different methods, כלל ופרט against ריבוי ומיעוט, and this explanation continues on the next amud.
