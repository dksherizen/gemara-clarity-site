Topic

Unwitting עבודה זרה: רבא's view on worship out of love or fear, and whether several acts of עבודה זרה done in one העלם need one חטאת or many.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a challenge to רבא, who holds that worshipping an idol out of love or fear of a person is פטור. He answers that the unwitting case in the ברייתא is a כהן who thinks idol worship is entirely permitted, which is different from העלם דבר. Next, רבי זכאי teaches that someone who did four kinds of עבודה זרה in one העלם brings only one חטאת. רבי יוחנן rejects this: 'Go teach it outside.' רבי אבא ties רבי זכאי's view to רבי יוסי, who says הבערה on שבת was singled out only to make it a plain לאו, not to divide the מלאכות. רב יוסף objects that רבי יוסי already learns the dividing of שבת labors from 'ועשה מאחת מהנה', so for עבודה זרה he might agree that השתחוואה comes to divide. The גמרא tries to apply that same פסוק to עבודה זרה. It works out a case of unknowing עבודה זרה, but then shows the פסוק belongs to ויקרא chapter 4, which cannot be about עבודה זרה, and the attempt is dropped. The amud ends as רב שמואל בר יהודה arrives with a new teaching.
