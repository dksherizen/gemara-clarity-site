Topic

The coming of משיח: the suffering before him, his name and identity, and the day of ה׳

Overview

This amud is all about the coming of משיח. It opens with the end of a description of foreign armies overrunning the land. Then רב says משיח will come only after the evil kingdom rules over ישראל for nine months. עולא, רבה and later רבי יוחנן say they would rather not live to see משיח, because of the terrible suffering before he comes. רב יוסף disagrees and would accept any disgrace just to be there. רבה explains that even תורה and חסד may not protect him, since sin can cancel protection, as with יעקב's fear and the second entry into the land. רבי יוחנן explains his fear from a פסוק in ירמיה about the pain of that time. The גמרא then discusses whether ישראל will enjoy the years of משיח, for whom the world was created, what משיח's name is, who he might be, and how a 'new דוד' will rule beside the original דוד. It ends with רבי שמלאי's parable of the rooster and the bat about the day of ה׳.

Opening lines

שֶׁאֵין כׇּל דֶּקֶל וָדֶקֶל שֶׁבְּבָבֶל,

for there is no palm tree in Babylonia

שֶׁאֵין סוּס שֶׁל פָּרְסִיִּים נִקְשָׁר בּוֹ,

to which a horse of the Persians will not be tied,

וְאֵין לָךְ כׇּל אָרוֹן וְאָרוֹן שֶׁבְּאֶרֶץ יִשְׂרָאֵל,

and there is no coffin in Eretz Yisrael

שֶׁאֵין סוּס מָדִי אוֹכֵל בּוֹ תֶּבֶן.

from which a Median horse will not eat straw.
