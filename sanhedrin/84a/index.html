Sanhedrin 84a

סנהדרין פ״ד ע״א

Open Sanhedrin 84a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Sources and punishments for people who may not do the עבודה: אונן, sitting, בעל מום, מעילה, and a זר who served

Overview

The גמרא goes through a ברייתא that lists people who may not do the עבודה in the מקדש. For each one it finds a source and asks how serious the punishment is. It finishes the פסוק about an ערל. It learns the אונן by inference from the כהן גדול's פסוק, and רבא explains why a גזרה שוה cannot give the אונן מיתה. A sitting כהן is disqualified because he was chosen 'to stand and serve.' For a בעל מום and for deliberate מעילה, רבי gives מיתה through word-links: for the בעל מום the source ends up being a טמא ששימש. The רבנן use the word ״בּוֹ״ to limit death and give only an אזהרה. For a זר who served, רבי ישמעאל says death בידי שמים, רבי עקיבא says סקילה, and רבי יוחנן בן נורי says חנק. The גמרא explains the roots of these disputes and settles a contradiction about רבי עקיבא's view. Then the פרק אלו הן הנשרפין ends.

Opening lines

וְעֶרֶל בָּשָׂר לֹא יָבוֹא

"And one uncircumcised in flesh shall not enter

אֶל מִקְדָּשִׁי (לְשָׁרְתֵנִי)״.

My Sanctuary (to serve Me)."

אוֹנֵן מְנָלַן?

An acute mourner, from where do we derive it?

As it is written: "And from the Sanctuary he shall not go out,
