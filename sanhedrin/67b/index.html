Topic

The source for stoning a מכשף, the levels of forbidden and permitted magic, and stories about sorcery and the plagues of מצרים.

Overview

The amud opens at the end of a ברייתא about how a מכשף (sorcerer) is executed. רבי עקיבא finishes rejecting the sword opinion. בן עזאי learns סקילה from the verse placed right next to the מכשפה verse. רבי יהודה learns it from אוב and ידעוני, which the Torah singled out from the group of sorcerers. When the גמרא challenges רבי יהודה with the rule of שני כתובים הבאים כאחד, רבי זכריה answers that רבי יהודה holds such verses do teach. The גמרא then turns to teachings about sorcery: רבי יוחנן explains the name כשפים, and רבי חנינא says sorcery cannot touch a person of great merit. אביי tells demon-work apart from sorcery. He also divides magic into three levels like שבת: real magic gets סקילה, illusion is פטור אבל אסור, and creating through הלכות יצירה is fully permitted. Stories follow about tricks, spells and the limits of demons. Then comes a dispute about the plague of frogs. Finally the גמרא returns to רבי עקיבא's statement in the משנה.

Opening lines

מִיתָה אַחַת.

...one kind of death.

בֶּן עַזַּאי אוֹמֵר:

Ben Azzai says:

נֶאֱמַר ״מְכַשֵּׁפָה לֹא תְחַיֶּה״,

It is stated: 'You shall not let a witch live,'

וְנֶאֱמַר ״כׇּל שֹׁכֵב עִם בְּהֵמָה מוֹת יוּמָת״.

and it is stated: 'Whoever lies with an animal shall surely be put to death.'
