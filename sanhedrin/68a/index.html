Topic

From whom did רבי עקיבא learn the cucumber כישוף? The story of רבי אליעזר's death, and the rule that כישוף may be learned in order to understand and teach.

Overview

Just before this, the גמרא said that רבי עקיבא learned the laws of כישוף, such as making cucumbers appear, from רבי יהושע. Now it challenges this with a long ברייתא about the death of רבי אליעזר. The sick רבי אליעזר, who was under a ban, shows his mind is clear. He rebukes the חכמים for never coming to learn from him. He mourns that so much of his תורה was never asked about, except the laws of planting cucumbers, which only רבי עקיבא asked about and saw him perform. He answers the חכמים' questions about טומאה and dies saying the word 'טהור'. רבי יהושע then lifts the ban, and רבי עקיבא mourns him bitterly. The גמרא answers that רבי עקיבא first heard it from רבי אליעזר without understanding it, and only later understood it from רבי יהושע. It then asks how רבי אליעזר could perform כישוף at all, and answers that doing it in order to learn and teach is permitted. With this the פרק ארבע מיתות ends.
