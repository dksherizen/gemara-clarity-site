Sanhedrin 79a

סנהדרין ע״ט ע״א

Topic

Killing someone other than the intended victim: the dispute between the רבנן and רבי שמעון, and their sources in the פסוקים

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה about a blow that missed its target. The attacker is פטור unless the blow was strong enough to kill both where he aimed and where it actually landed. רבי שמעון says that even then, if he meant to kill one person and killed another, he is פטור. The גמרא shows that רבי שמעון is arguing on an inference from the first clause of the משנה, and it asks about unclear cases like 'one of them' or mistaken identity. רבי שמעון learns his view from the פסוק 'וארב לו וקם עליו'. The רבנן use that same פסוק to exclude someone who throws a stone into a group, which is needed when a non-Jew is קבוע among nine Jews. Finally, the רבנן bring proof from the fight that hurt a pregnant woman, where 'נפש תחת נפש' means death. רבי שמעון answers, like רבי, that it means money, and רבי's proof continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְהָיָה בָּהּ כְּדֵי לְהָמִית עַל לִבּוֹ,

and it [the blow] had enough force to kill on his heart,

וְהָלְכָה לָהּ עַל מׇתְנָיו,

but it went and landed on his loins,

וְלֹא הָיָה בָּהּ כְּדֵי לְהָמִית עַל מׇתְנָיו,

and it did not have enough force to kill on his loins,

וָמֵת – פָּטוּר.

and he died: [the attacker is] exempt.

  13. ResponseThe רבנן use the פסוק for throwing a stone into a group
  14. ChallengeWhy is a פסוק needed for the stone case?

  16. ChallengeThe רבנן have a source, so what does רבי שמעון do with 'a life for a life'?
  17. Resolutionרבי שמעון: 'a life for a life' means money
