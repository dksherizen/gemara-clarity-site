Sanhedrin 85b

סנהדרין פ״ה ע״ב

Open Sanhedrin 85b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

A son as the court's agent against his father, striking versus cursing a parent, and the laws of kidnapping

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier discussion. Cursing a father on his way to execution is punishable, just like cursing a parent after death. Then comes the final ruling: a son is never the court's agent to strike or curse his father, except when the father is a מסית. A new משנה teaches that hitting a parent is a capital crime only if it causes a חבורה, while cursing is punishable even after the parent's death. The גמרא finds the source for this in the פסוק and explains how רבי יאשיה and רבי יונתן each read the פסוקים about cursing a parent. It then shows that the תנא of the משנה compares striking to cursing, and it proves from two ברייתות about a כותי that תנאים argue about this comparison. A second משנה turns to גניבת נפש (kidnapping): when the kidnapper becomes liable, and the arguments about kidnapping one's own son or a half-slave. The גמרא discusses what counts as 'using' the victim and leaves רבי ירמיה's question unanswered (תיקו). It ends with ברייתות listing which kidnappers and victims bring liability.

Opening lines

מִידֵּי דְּהָוֵה לְאַחַר מִיתָה.

It is just like the case of after death.

מַאי הֲוָה עֲלַהּ?

What was concluded about it?

אָמַר רַבָּה בַּר רַב הוּנָא,

Rabba bar Rav Huna said,

וְכֵן תָּנָא דְּבֵי רַבִּי יִשְׁמָעֵאל:

and so too the school of Rabbi Yishmael taught:
