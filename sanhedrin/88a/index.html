Topic

Sanhedrin 88a: The disputes behind the משנה's categories for a זקן ממרא, and when he is executed (tradition against reasoning: רב כהנא and רבי אלעזר)

Overview

The גמרא goes through the words of the משנה about a זקן ממרא, an elder who rules against the בית דין הגדול. For each word it shows a real מחלוקת he could rule on. It finishes the נגעים example: רבי יונתן בן אבטולמוס learns from a גזירה שוה that a garment fully covered by a נגע is טהור. 'דברי' means ערכין, חרמים and הקדשות, each tied to a known dispute. 'ריבות' means סוטה, עגלה ערופה and טהרת מצורע, and 'בשעריך' means לקט, שכחה and פיאה, and each of these is also tied to a known dispute. Then the גמרא turns to the three courts. רב כהנא says the זקן is killed only when he relies on his own reasoning against the court's tradition, and he proves it from עקביא בן מהללאל. רבי אלעזר says that even a זקן with a tradition is killed, to keep disputes from spreading. He explains that עקביא was spared because he never gave a practical ruling. A proof from the משנה's wording is pushed aside, and a new proof from רבי יאשיה's three teachings begins and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לִפְרִיחָה בִּבְגָדִים, שֶׁהִיא טְהוֹרָה?

for a spreading in garments, that it is pure?

נֶאֶמְרָה קָרַחַת וְגַבַּחַת בָּאָדָם,

'Karachat' and 'gabachat' are stated regarding a person,

וְנֶאֶמְרָה קָרַחַת וְגַבַּחַת בִּבְגָדִים.

and 'karachat' and 'gabachat' are stated regarding garments.

מָה לְהַלָּן – פָּרַח בְּכוּלּוֹ טָהוֹר,

Just as there, if it spread over all of him, he is pure,
