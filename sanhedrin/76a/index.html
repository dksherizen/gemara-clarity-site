Topic

Learning a man's forbidden relatives from his wife's, and the source for his daughter from a woman he raped.

Overview

The amud opens by testing whether a man's own relatives (הוא) can be learned from his wife's relatives (היא). The גמרא knocks the comparison down in several ways, using the two views on whether סקילה or שריפה is the harsher death. רבא then shows that the ban on his wife's daughter-in-law cannot be learned from his own daughter-in-law under either method of comparison: דון מינה ומינה or דון מינה ואוקי באתרה. Next, the גמרא asks for the source that a man's daughter from a woman he raped is forbidden. It brings three answers: אביי's קל וחומר, רבא's double גזירה שוה, and the teaching of אבוה דרבי אבין from ובת איש כהן. It then looks for the matching אזהרה, and רבי אילעא brings 'אל תחלל את בתך להזנותה'. That פסוק leads to teachings about a father who marries his daughter to an old man or delays the marriage of his grown daughter. The amud ends as אביי begins to explain רבי עקיבא's saying about who is truly 'poor'. His answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מָה לְהִיא –

What about her relatives?

שֶׁכֵּן אִמָּהּ בִּשְׂרֵפָה,

they are special, since her mother is punished by burning;

תֹּאמַר בְּהוּא –

will you say the same about his relatives,

שֶׁאִמּוֹ בִּסְקִילָה?

where his mother is punished by stoning?
