Topic

The ברייתא of רבי זכאי comparing שבת to other מצוות, and why רבי יוחנן rejected it

Overview

רבי זכאי taught a ברייתא before רבי יוחנן saying שבת is stricter than other מצוות in one way, and other מצוות are stricter than שבת in another. The גמרא first tests the שבת side. Two different acts bring two חטאות everywhere, and the same act repeated brings one חטאת everywhere, so no difference appears. That explains why רבי יוחנן sent the ברייתא outside. The גמרא then suggests that 'other מצוות' means עבודה זרה, where רבי אמי says several acts in one lapse bring only one חטאת. But the second half of the ברייתא needs a case of שוגג בלא מתכוין in עבודה זרה. Every case tried fails: love or fear does not work for רבא, and 'thinks it is permitted' does not work for שבת. Next the גמרא suggests splitting the ברייתא, with the first half about עבודה זרה and the second half about ordinary מצוות, backed by שמואל's rule about a מתעסק. רבי יוחנן rejects this kind of split, as his statement about the barrel משנה shows. The גמרא then turns back to רבי אמי's statement itself, and that discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הָכִי תְּנָא קַמֵּיהּ:

This is what he taught before him:

חוֹמֶר בַּשַּׁבָּת מִשְּׁאָר מִצְוֹת,

There is a stringency in Shabbat over other מצוות,

חוֹמֶר בִּשְׁאָר מִצְוֹת מִבַּשַּׁבָּת.

and a stringency in other מצוות over Shabbat.

חוֹמֶר בַּשַּׁבָּת –

The stringency of Shabbat:
