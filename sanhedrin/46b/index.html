Topic

Hanging the executed, why the body is taken down, the source and purpose of burial, and the purpose of a הספד.

Overview

This amud opens with the last line of the משנה: the relatives of an executed person do not keep formal אבילות, but they may grieve in their hearts. The גמרא then teaches from the פסוק that the person is executed first and hung only afterward, right before sunset, and untied at once. The tree used must already be cut from the ground; רבי יוסי adds that it may not even be set back into the ground. Next the גמרא explains why the body must come down: רבי מאיר's parable of the twin king shows that a hanging body disgraces God, and אביי and רבא explain how the word קללת shows God's distress. The גמרא concludes that קללת teaches two lessons at once. It then learns from כי קבור תקברנו that leaving any dead person unburied overnight breaks a לא תעשה, and tells how King שבור asked רב חמא for a Torah source for burial. Finally the גמרא asks whether burial is for preventing disgrace or for atonement, and whether a הספד honors the living or the dead. For each question it tests several פסוקים, and every proof is turned away.

Opening lines

וְלֹא הָיוּ מִתְאַבְּלִין,

And they would not mourn,

אֲבָל אוֹנְנִין,

but they would grieve,

שֶׁאֵין אֲנִינוּת אֶלָּא בַּלֵּב.

since grief is only in the heart.

גְּמָ׳
