Sanhedrin 97b

סנהדרין צ״ז ע״ב

Topic

When will משיח come? Warnings against calculating the קץ, the 36 צדיקים, and whether the גאולה depends on תשובה

Overview

This amud continues the גמרא's discussion of when משיח will come. It first admits that our sins have delayed משיח, so years meant for him have already passed. Next come several attempts to date the end: אליהו's words to רב יהודה about 85 יובלות, which רב אשי explains, and a mysterious scroll found in Rome's archives. A ברייתא of רבי נתן then rejects every calculation of the קץ from פסוקים, and רבי יונתן curses those who calculate the end, because people lose hope when the date passes. The גמרא teaches that we must keep waiting, since ה׳ is waiting too, and that only מדת הדין holds the גאולה back. We wait in order to receive reward. From the same פסוק, אביי learns that there are at least 36 צדיקים in every generation who greet the שכינה, and the גמרא settles two challenges to this number. Finally, רב and שמואל argue whether the גאולה depends on תשובה, matching a dispute between רבי אליעזר and רבי יהושע. Each of them brings פסוקים, and their exchange continues on the next amud.
