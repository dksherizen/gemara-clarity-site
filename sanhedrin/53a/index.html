Sanhedrin 53a

סנהדרין נ״ג ע״א

Topic

The source for the four death penalties and for stoning the other נסקלין; the משנה listing the נסקלין and רבי יהודה's view on one's mother

Overview

The גמרא first asks how רבי יאשיה knows that חנק exists at all, since the תורה never mentions it. רבא answers that all four death penalties are a received tradition. The גמרא then explains that רבי יונתן said 'not because it is lenient' because he holds like רבי שמעון, for whom סייף is lighter than חנק. Next, רבי זירא asks אביי which phrase links the other stoned sinners to אוב וידעוני. אביי answers that the link is ״דמיהם בם״, and the phrase ״מות יומת״ teaches something else: a murderer may be killed in any way when the written death is not possible. Later, רבינא explains that linking through ״מות יומת״ would have pointed to אשת איש and her lighter death, חנק. A new משנה then lists everyone who is stoned and discusses the double prohibitions for one's mother, father's wife and daughter-in-law. The גמרא explains that רבי יהודה's view follows רבי עקיבא, that קידושין do not take effect in a relationship forbidden by a לאו. רב אושעיא begins a challenge from a משנה about יבום, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בִּשְׁלָמָא לְרַבִּי יוֹנָתָן,

Granted, according to Rabbi Yonatan,

כִּדְקָא מְפָרֵשׁ רַבִּי טַעְמָא.

it works as Rabbi explains the reason.

אֶלָּא לְרַבִּי יֹאשִׁיָּה,

But according to Rabbi Yoshiya,

מִמַּאי דְּאִיכָּא חֶנֶק בָּעוֹלָם?

from where do we know there is strangulation in the world at all?
