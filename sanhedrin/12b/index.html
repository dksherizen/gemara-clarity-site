Topic

Adding a leap month because of טומאה and on the thirtieth of אדר: חזקיהו's mistake, the opinions of רבי יהודה and רבי שמעון, and the seasons as a reason to add a month

Overview

This amud finishes the ברייתא about חזקיהו, who added a leap month because the people were טמא and then prayed for forgiveness. רבי שמעון says such a leap year counts. In his view חזקיהו's mistake was adding a second ניסן during ניסן, or, in another version, leading the people to keep פסח שני. The גמרא first infers that רבי יהודה holds טומאה is only 'pushed aside' for the public. A ברייתא about the ציץ shows him saying it is fully permitted. The גמרא solves this by filling in missing words in the ברייתא: רבי יהודה actually holds that a leap year made for טומאה does not count, and רבי שמעון allows it even לכתחילה. It explains that חזקיהו added the month on the thirtieth of אדר, a day that שמואל says may already count as ניסן. רב אשי explains the פסח שני version through a doubt about whether women count toward the majority. The amud then discusses what happens if a court adds the month on that thirtieth day, where רבא and רב נחמן disagree. It begins a new rule from שמואל about adding a month because of the seasons.
