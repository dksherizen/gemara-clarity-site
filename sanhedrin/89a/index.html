Topic

The execution of the זקן ממרא, public announcements of executions, and the punishments and examples of false prophets

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a discussion about a זקן ממרא who rules that one should add to ציצית or תפילין. Using רבי זירא's rule about an outer compartment that does not see the air, the גמרא shows that an extra compartment always ruins the תפילין. A new משנה then teaches where and when the זקן ממרא is executed. רבי עקיבא says he is executed at the בית דין הגדול in ירושלים during the רגל. רבי יהודה says the פסוק requires only that people 'hear,' so he is executed right away and letters announce it. A ברייתא lists four sinners whose execution must be announced, and explains why the פסוק about עדים זוממין says 'those who remain.' The next משנה teaches that a false prophet and one who prophesies in the name of an idol are executed by the court, while three other prophecy sins are punished בידי שמים. It also lists more cases of חנק, including the exception for the plotting witnesses against a בת כהן. The גמרא brings the פסוקים behind each group and gives examples: צדקיה בן כנענה, who should have noticed that all the prophets used identical wording; חנניה בן עזור, who announced his own קל וחומר as prophecy; the prophets of the בעל; and יונה. The example for one who ignores a prophet's words continues on the next amud.

אִי עֲבַד אַרְבְּעָה בָּתֵּי,

If he made four compartments,
