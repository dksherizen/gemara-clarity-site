Topic

Ranking the four death penalties: the רבנן's order (סקילה first) and רבי שמעון's order (שריפה first)

Overview

The גמרא ranks the four deaths of בית דין: סקילה, שריפה, סייף and חנק. For the רבנן, סקילה is the most severe. A betrothed בת כהן gets סקילה, not שריפה, and they prove the rest of their order the same way. Each time, the גמרא first gives a reason based on who receives a death, such as the מגדף, the בת כהן, or the people of an עיר הנדחת. It then pushes back with אדרבה, pointing to another sinner, like one who strikes a parent. Each push-back is settled by a stronger proof: the Torah moving a case to a harsher death, a גזרה שוה on the word "אביה", or the rule that the מדיח is worse than the people he led astray. Then the גמרא explains רבי שמעון, who puts שריפה first, because he holds that even a betrothed בת כהן is moved from סקילה to שריפה. The proof that שריפה is more severe than סייף for רבי שמעון begins at the end of the amud and finishes on the next amud.

Opening lines

קָסָבְרִי רַבָּנַן:

The Rabbis hold:

נְשׂוּאָה יוֹצְאָה לִשְׂרֵיפָה,

A married woman is taken out for burning,

וְלֹא אֲרוּסָה.

but not a betrothed one.

וּמִדְּאַפְּקַהּ רַחֲמָנָא לַאֲרוּסָה בִּסְקִילָה,

And since the Merciful One took out the betrothed woman for stoning,
