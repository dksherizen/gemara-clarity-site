Topic

Which מצוות of בני נח come from which פסוק, which ones carry the death penalty for a בן נח, and what the ברייתא means by acts 'similar to' robbery, יפת תואר and bloodshed

Overview

The גמרא finishes listing the sources of דבי מנשה for the מצוות of בני נח, such as עבודה זרה, גילוי עריות, שפיכות דמים, גזל, אבר מן החי, סירוס and כלאים. After each one it shows how the other view reads the same פסוק for a different lesson. Next it asks for which מצוות a בן נח is put to death. רב יוסף says three. רב ששת attacks that list and says four, adding עבודה זרה, and רב נחמן בר יצחק explains that for a בן נח 'their warning is their death.' רב's students say a בן נח is killed for all seven. The גמרא tests that view against a ברייתא about robbery that uses the words 'forbidden' and 'permitted.' It then asks what the ברייתא means by acts 'similar to robbery.' After two answers are rejected, it settles on withholding a worker's wages. It also explains 'similar to יפת תואר' as a maidservant set aside for a slave. Finally it notes that no 'similar to bloodshed' case is taught, and אביי links such a case to רבי יונתן בן שאול, whose teaching continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דִּכְתִיב: ״וַתִּשָּׁחֵת הָאָרֶץ לִפְנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים״.

As it is written: "And the earth was corrupt before God."

וְתָנָא דְּבֵי רַבִּי יִשְׁמָעֵאל:

And the school of Rabbi Yishmael taught:

בְּכׇל מָקוֹם שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר הַשְׁחָתָה,

Anywhere that "corruption" is stated,

אֵינוֹ אֶלָּא דְּבַר עֶרְוָה וַעֲבוֹדָה זָרָה.

it means nothing other than sexual immorality and idol worship.
