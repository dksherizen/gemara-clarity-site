Sanhedrin 57b

סנהדרין נ״ז ע״ב

Open Sanhedrin 57b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Court rules and punishments for a בן נח, and when a non-Jew who sins with Jewish עריות is judged by Jewish law

Overview

This amud opens with the last words of a ruling from the previous amud. A ברייתא then lists the special court rules for a בן נח: one judge, one witness, no התראה, a man and not a woman, and even a relative. רבי ישמעאל adds that a בן נח is executed even for killing a fetus. רב יהודה draws all of these rules from בראשית 9:5. The fetus rule comes from reading 'באדם' as 'a person inside a person', while תנא דבי מנשה reads the same word as teaching חנק. The גמרא then answers questions about women: they are excluded from judging, but a בת נח is still executed for murder and for adultery. Next, a ברייתא learns from 'איש איש' that a non-Jew who sins with Jewish עריות is judged by Jewish law. רבי יוחנן explains that this means a נערה המאורסה, and a later step adds a woman who entered חופה without relations. Finally the גמרא begins to question רבי מאיר's rule linking a בן נח's עריות to Jewish death penalties, and that discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

נֶהֱרָג עָלָיו.

He is executed for him.

אַשְׁכַּח רַבִּי יַעֲקֹב בַּר אַחָא

Rabbi Ya'akov bar Acha found

דַּהֲוָה כְּתִיב בְּסֵפֶר אַגַּדְתָּא דְּבֵי רַב:

that it was written in a book of Aggada of the house of Rav:

בֶּן נֹחַ נֶהֱרָג בְּדַיָּין אֶחָד,

A descendant of Noah is executed by one judge,
