Sanhedrin 93b

סנהדרין צ״ג ע״ב

Open Sanhedrin 93b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

רות's six blessed descendants, the meaning of סריסים, and why no book was named after נחמיה.

Overview

The גמרא explains that the six measures of barley בועז gave רות were a hint: six descendants would come from her, each blessed with six blessings. These are דוד, משיח, דניאל, חנניה, מישאל and עזריה. A פסוק lists דוד's six praises, and רב reads them as לשון הרע that דואג spoke about דוד's Torah greatness, which made שאול jealous. משיח's blessings come from ישעיהו 11, and רבא learns that משיח 'smells and judges', a test that בר כוזיבא failed. The virtues of דניאל and his friends are explained, along with a מחלוקת about their tribe. רב and רבי חנינא then argue whether 'סריסים' means real eunuchs or that עבודה זרה lost its power in their days, and each view is tested against פסוקים. Finally, the גמרא asks why no book was named after נחמיה. One answer is that he took credit for himself, and the other is that he spoke badly of earlier leaders, even דניאל, who was greater than the נביאים חגי, זכריה and מלאכי.

אֶלָּא שֵׁשׁ סְאִין.
