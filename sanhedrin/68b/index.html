Topic

בן סורר ומורה: the age window, the source that a קטן is exempt, and whether a son born to a קטן can qualify

Overview

The משנה teaches that a boy can become a בן סורר ומורה only during a short window. It starts when he shows two hairs, the sign of adulthood, and ends once the lower 'beard' has grown in. The פסוק 'כי יהיה לאיש בן' teaches 'a son, not a daughter' and 'a son, not a man,' and a קטן is exempt because he is not yet obligated in מצוות. The גמרא asks for the source that a קטן is exempt. The question is needed because a בן סורר ומורה is killed על שם סופו, and because 'בן ולא איש' could seem to include a קטן. רב יהודה in the name of רב answers that the פסוק means a son close to the strength of a man. רבי חייא and רב דימי then define exactly which hair growth ends the window. רב חסדא learns from the unusual wording 'לאיש בן' that a son born to a קטן can never be a בן סורר ומורה, and the גמרא concludes that the פסוק teaches both lessons. Finally, the גמרא notes that רבה disagrees, since he holds a קטן cannot father children at all. רבה learns this from a פסוק about stealing from a גר, and that discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳

MISHNA:

בֵּן סוֹרֵר וּמוֹרֶה,

A stubborn and rebellious son:

מֵאֵימָתַי נַעֲשֶׂה בֵּן סוֹרֵר וּמוֹרֶה?

from when does he become a stubborn and rebellious son?

מִשֶּׁיָּבִיא שְׁתֵּי שְׂעָרוֹת,

From when he grows two hairs,
