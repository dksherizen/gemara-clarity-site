Sanhedrin 95a

סנהדרין צ״ה ע״א

Learn Sanhedrin 95a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Sanhedrin 95a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

סנחריב's march on ירושלים and its downfall, the story of דוד and ישבי בנוב, and the three people for whom the land shrank.

Overview

The גמרא finishes reading the verses in ישעיהו 10 about סנחריב's march on ירושלים. 'עניה ענתות' hints that ירמיהו of ענתות will prophesy about נבוכדנצר. רבי יוחנן explains that ארי and ליש are both names for a lion, and the count of ten stops still works because עברו and מעברה are two places. רב הונא teaches that 'עוד היום בנב' means only one day was left of the punishment for the sin of נוב. סנחריב rushes to ירושלים, mocks the small city, and puts off the attack until morning, and that night an angel destroys his army. רב פפא sums it up: a judgment left overnight is cancelled. The theme of נוב then leads to the story of ישבי בנוב. He was sent to punish דוד for נוב, and אבישי rescued דוד through a holy שם and prayer. Finally, a ברייתא lists three people for whom the land shrank, and the list of sources for יעקב אבינו continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

״עֲנִיָּה עֲנָתוֹת״ –

"Poor Anathoth":

עָתִיד יִרְמְיָה בֶּן חִלְקִיָּה

וּמִתְנַבֵּא עֲלַהּ מֵעֲנָתוֹת,

to prophesy about it from Anathoth,

דִּכְתִיב:

as it is written:
