Sanhedrin 3a

סנהדרין ג׳ ע״א

Open Sanhedrin 3a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Lay judges in money cases, רבא and רב אחא on why three judges are needed, and why the משנה lists נזק and חצי נזק

Overview

The amud opens by finishing רבי חנינא's teaching. By Torah law, money cases need the same careful questioning of witnesses (דרישה וחקירה) as capital cases. The חכמים relaxed this so lenders won't stop lending, and that is also why non-expert judges (הדיוטות) may judge loan cases. Even so, הדיוטות must pay if they make a mistake, because otherwise lenders would be scared off even more. The גמרא then shows two problems with רבי אבהו's reading of the משנה. In response, רבא explains that the משנה teaches two separate rules. רב אחא בריה דרב איקא says instead that by Torah law one judge is enough, and the חכמים required three because of יושבי קרנות. The practical difference between them is שמואל's rule that a court of two is valid but called a בית דין חצוף. Finally, the גמרא asks why the משנה lists 'damage' and 'half damage', tries the answer that half damage is a קנס, rejects it, and begins a new answer that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: ״מִשְׁפַּט אֶחָד יִהְיֶה לָכֶם״.

As it says: 'You shall have one law.'

וּמָה טַעַם אָמְרוּ

And what is the reason they said

דִּינֵי מָמוֹנוֹת לָא בָּעֵינַן

that money cases do not need

דְּרִישָׁה וַחֲקִירָה?

questioning and cross-examination of witnesses?

The steps (21)

  1. StatementThe verse: one law for everyone
  2. QuestionThen why don't money cases need questioning of witnesses?
  3. ResponseSo lenders will keep lending
  4. ChallengeShould non-expert judges then not pay for mistakes?
  5. ResolutionNo, that would scare lenders even more
  6. ChallengeThe משנה should have openly said 'two rules'
  7. ChallengeWhy say 'three' twice?

  9. Statementרב אחא: One Judge Is Valid by Torah Law
  10. ChallengeCan't Three Judges Also Be Idlers?
  11. ResolutionAmong Three, One Will Be Learned
  12. ChallengeThen Shouldn't Mistaken Judges Be Free from Paying?
  13. ResolutionExempting Them Would Bring More Idlers
  14. QuestionWhat Is the Difference Between רבא and רב אחא?
  15. ResponseThe Difference: שמואל's Court of Two
  16. ChallengeIsn't 'Damage' the Same as 'Injuries'?
  17. ResolutionFull Damage Is Listed Alongside Half Damage
  18. ChallengeIsn't half damage the same as injuries?
  19. ResolutionHalf damage is a fine, not ordinary money
  20. ChallengeThat answer only works if half damage is a fine
