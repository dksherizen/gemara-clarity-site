Sanhedrin 94a

סנהדרין צ״ד ע״א

Learn Sanhedrin 94a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Sanhedrin 94a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

דניאל compared with חגי, זכריה and מלאכי; the hidden fear sensed by one's מזל and its remedies; and the closed ם in לםרבה, which hints that חזקיהו was meant to be the משיח

Overview

The amud opens by comparing דניאל with חגי, זכריה and מלאכי. They were נביאים, but only דניאל saw the vision. רבינא learns from their fear that when a person is frightened for no visible reason, his מזל has sensed something, and the גמרא gives remedies. Next, בר קפרא explains the closed ם in לםרבה: הקב״ה wanted to make חזקיהו the משיח, but מדת הדין objected because חזקיהו did not sing praise after his miracles. The earth sang in his place, but a בת קול answered 'My secret is Mine,' and the redemption was put off. The גמרא adds teachings about waiting through the night of exile, about the disgrace of not singing praise (חזקיהו, and משה with יתרו), and about the word ויחד. It then expounds ישעיהו 10:16: חזקיהו and סנחריב each had eight names. It also explains why סנחריב was honored for not speaking badly of ארץ ישראל, while the exiles did speak badly of it. Finally, it discusses how סנחריב's army was burned and begins comparing סנחריב with פרעה, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אִינְהוּ עֲדִיפִי מִינֵּיהּ,

They were greater than he,

וְאִיהוּ עֲדִיף מִנַּיְיהוּ.

and he was greater than they.

אִינְהוּ עֲדִיפִי מִינֵּיהּ –

They were greater than he —

דְּאִינְהוּ נְבִיאֵי, וְאִיהוּ לָאו נְבִיָּא.

for they were prophets, and he was not a prophet.
