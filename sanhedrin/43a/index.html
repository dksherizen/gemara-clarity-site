Sanhedrin 43a

סנהדרין מ״ג ע״א

Open Sanhedrin 43a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The place and method of stoning, who pays for execution and rescue, giving the condemned man every chance at acquittal, and the crier's announcement

Overview

The amud opens by finishing רב פפא's proof that stoning happens outside all three camps, taken from the story of the מקלל. The גמרא explains the words 'וַיִּרְגְּמוּ אֹתוֹ אָבֶן': he is stoned without his clothes, and one stone is enough if it kills him. It also explains why the תורה needs both 'אבן' and 'אבנים'. It then reads the ברייתא's גזירה שוה as only a backup. רב אשי offers another reading of the same פסוקים, but the רבנן challenge him from the פרים הנשרפים, and the question stays a קשיא. Next, רב הונא rules that the execution tools come from the community, and asks who pays for the cloth and horse used to stop an execution. The community pays for the calming wine, because the פסוק says 'תנו'. The גמרא asks about a student who said he could acquit and then went mute, and that question remains a תיבעי. The condemned man is returned twice no matter what; after that, two rabbis first check his claim. A new משנה describes the crier's announcement, and אביי adds that the day, hour and place must be announced. The amud ends with ישו הנוצרי's trial and the five disciples, each answered by a פסוק that plays on his name.

"And the children of Israel did

as the Lord commanded Moses."

אֶלָּא מֵעַתָּה:

But now, if so:

"And they stoned him with a stone"
