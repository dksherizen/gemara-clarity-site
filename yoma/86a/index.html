Yoma 86a

יומא פ״ו ע״א

Open Yoma 86a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

תשובה and כפרה: which sins need יום הכפורים, the ladder of atonement, חילול השם, and the greatness of תשובה

Overview

The amud opens by asking whether תשובה alone atones for an ordinary לא תעשה, or whether it also needs יום הכפורים. Two sources seem to say that only לא תשא is severe, and the גמרא answers that this means 'לא תשא and everything like it.' A third source, from the verse at חורב, says outright that all other לאוין are cleared, so the גמרא concludes this is a dispute between תנאים. רבי אלעזר then shows from 'ונקה לא ינקה' that God clears those who do תשובה. Next comes רבי ישמעאל's ladder of כפרה. For an עשה, תשובה alone works. For a לאו, יום הכפורים is also needed. For כרת and מיתות בית דין, יסורין are also needed. For חילול השם, only death completes the כפרה. The גמרא then gives examples of חילול השם, such as a great person who delays paying or is seen without תורה and תפילין. It ends with אביי's ברייתא contrasting a learned person who makes ה׳'s name beloved with one whose dishonesty desecrates it. The amud closes with sayings on the greatness of תשובה, including the levels of תשובה out of love, out of fear, and through suffering.

Opening lines

חוּץ מִ״לֹּא תִשָּׂא״!

except for 'You shall not take [the name of God in vain]'!

״לֹא תִשָּׂא״ וְכֹל דְּדָמֵי לֵיהּ.

'You shall not take' and everything that is similar to it.

תָּא שְׁמַע, רַבִּי יְהוּדָה אוֹמֵר:

Come and hear: Rabbi Yehuda says:

כֹּל שֶׁהוּא מִ״לֹּא תִשָּׂא״ וּלְמַטָּה —

anything that is from 'You shall not take' and below —
