Yoma 55a

יומא נ״ה ע״א

Topic

The sprinklings toward the כפורת on יום הכיפורים: where the blood goes, how many sprinklings there are, how they are counted, and רבי יהודה's concern about mix-ups

Overview

This amud explains how the כהן גדול sprinkles blood toward the כפורת on יום הכיפורים. A ברייתא teaches that the blood is aimed at the front edge of the כפורת, not onto its top. רבי זירא finds the source in an extra phrase about the שעיר, which shows that 'על' means 'not actually on.' The גמרא tries to reverse this with אדרבה, but rejects the reversal, because it would leave a phrase in the verse with no purpose. Next, a ברייתא works out that both the פר and the שעיר get one upward and seven downward sprinklings. After several competing comparisons, the extra words 'כאשר עשה' settle it. רבי מאיר and רבי יהודה give two styles of counting the sprinklings, and the גמרא says each simply followed his local custom. רבי אלעזר and רבי יוחנן give different reasons why the first sprinkling is counted with every later one. Finally, the גמרא turns to the golden stand and to רבי יהודה's worry about mix-ups in the bird-offering boxes, and the answer to אביי's challenge continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

כִּמְנַגְּדָנָא.

Like one who lashes with a whip.

תָּנָא, כְּשֶׁהוּא מַזֶּה —

It was taught: When he sprinkles —

אֵינוֹ מַזֶּה עַל הַכַּפּוֹרֶת,

he does not sprinkle on the Ark cover,

אֶלָּא כְּנֶגֶד עוֹבְיָהּ שֶׁל כַּפּוֹרֶת.

but rather facing the thickness of the Ark cover.

The steps (33)

  1. StatementFinishing the whip image

  5. ResponseThe היקש: 'upon' is like 'before', so it does not actually touch

  12. ChallengeOn the contrary: learn the goat from itself

  14. QuestionDownward is settled, but how many upward for the bull?
  15. ResponseUpward from upward: the bull gets one
  16. ChallengeOr learn the bull from itself: seven upward
  17. ResolutionUpward is better learned from upward
  18. ChallengeOn the contrary: learn the bull from itself

  25. Responseרבי יוחנן: learned from an extra word in the verse

  27. ResponseThe difference is when he did not count and did not make a mistake

  31. Responseרב יוסף: obligatory money might mix with voluntary money
