Yoma 61a

יומא ס״א ע״א

Learn Yoma 61a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Yoma 61a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The order and units of the יום הכפורים blood sprinklings, who is atoned for, and what happens when the blood (or a מצורע's oil) spills

Overview

This amud first closes an earlier point: the משנה simply is not talking about the קטורת. עולא then rules that slaughtering the שעיר before the פר's blood is sprinkled inside does not count. He explains that the משנה's case of 'just sprinkle again' refers only to the היכל sprinklings, and רבי אפס agrees. A ברייתא reads the verse 'וכפר את מקדש הקודש' as listing places and people atoned for. רבי יהודה says כהנים, ישראל, and לוים all get atonement from the שעיר המשתלח, while רבי שמעון says the כהנים get theirs from the פר. Another ברייתא teaches that the sprinklings in the לפני ולפנים, the היכל, and on the מזבח are each a separate כפרה. So if blood spills, a finished unit is never repeated. The unnamed חכמים (רבי מאיר) say an interrupted unit restarts, and רבי אלעזר and רבי שמעון say he continues where he stopped. רבי יוחנן explains that both sides read the word 'אחת' in one verse differently. The גמרא shows the same מחלוקת applies to the מצורע's לוג of oil, and explains from the double 'leftover' wording why the oil on the head is not essential. A new statement of רבי יוחנן begins at the end and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בִּקְטוֹרֶת לָא קָא מַיְירֵי.

It is not speaking about the incense.

אָמַר עוּלָּא:

Ulla said:

שָׂעִיר שֶׁשְּׁחָטוֹ קוֹדֶם מַתַּן דָּמוֹ שֶׁל פַּר —

A goat that he slaughtered before the sprinkling of the bull's blood —

לֹא עָשָׂה וְלֹא כְלוּם.

he has done nothing at all.
