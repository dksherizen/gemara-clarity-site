Topic

Matching pairs of animals (the יום הכפורים goats, the מצורע's lambs and birds, the תמיד and מוסף lambs), and liability for slaughtering the יום הכפורים goats outside the עזרה before the lottery

Overview

The גמרא begins with a ברייתא about the two goats of יום הכפורים. The extra word ״שני״ teaches that the goats should ideally be identical, and the repeated word ״שעיר״ teaches that they are still valid if they are not. Because ״שני״ appears three times, we might have thought matching was essential (עיכובא). Now that it is not, the three ״שני״ teach that the goats should match in appearance, height, and value. The same pattern is then applied to the מצורע's lambs and birds. There the word ״תהיה״ could have made matching essential, and it still makes the rest of the מצורע's procedures מעכב. The גמרא asks whether the two lambs of the תמיד should also match, and answers that ״שנים ליום״ is needed to teach that each lamb is slaughtered facing the sun. The two lambs of מוסף שבת, however, should certainly match. A new ברייתא then rules on slaughtering the יום הכפורים goats outside the עזרה, before and after the הגרלה. רב חסדא explains that before the lottery the goats are fit to be the outer מוסף goat, because waiting until later on the same day is not 'lacking time.' רבינא begins to apply רב חסדא's idea to שמואל's rule about שלמים slaughtered before the היכל doors were opened, and that discussion continues on the next amud.
