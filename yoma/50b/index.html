Yoma 50b

יומא נ׳ ע״ב

Learn Yoma 50b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Yoma 50b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Whether the פר יום הכפורים is a private or communal קרבן, and רבי אלעזר's question about whether a תמורה can take effect on it

Overview

The גמרא first tries to prove from רבי אלעזר's question that some תנא holds the פר יום הכפורים is a קרבן ציבור. It rejects the proof: the other view may be that the bull belongs to partners (שותפין). The גמרא then examines רבי אלעזר's question itself: according to the view that the bull is a קרבן יחיד, can a תמורה take effect on it? A first explanation, that the doubt is between the מקדיש and the מתכפר, is rejected as obvious, because תמורה always follows the מתכפר. So the real doubt is whether the fellow כהנים get atonement as an essential part of the bull or only incidentally. The גמרא tries to answer from a ברייתא about a זבח that overrides שבת and טומאה and can make a תמורה, which seems to fit only the bull. רב ששת says the ברייתא could mean the ram of אהרן, and the גמרא supports him, since a bull's substitute (a חטאת's substitute) is left to die. The גמרא answers that 'תמורה' in the ברייתא means substitutes in general, while 'זבח' means a specific offering. It begins to prove this from the strictures of תמורה, and the proof continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לְדִבְרֵי הָאוֹמֵר

According to the one who says

פַּר יוֹם הַכִּפּוּרִים קׇרְבַּן יָחִיד,

the Yom Kippur bull is an individual's offering,

עוֹשֶׂה תְּמוּרָה

does it make a substitution (take effect)

אוֹ אֵינוֹ עוֹשֶׂה תְּמוּרָה.

or does it not make a substitution?
