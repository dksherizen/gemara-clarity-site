Topic

Who authored מסכת מדות? רב הונא says רבי אליעזר בן יעקב and the גמרא brings proofs, but רב אדא בר אהבה suggests the low eastern wall could follow רבי יהודה.

Overview

On the previous amud, the גמרא found that two sources disagree about the chambers of the בית המוקד. This amud finishes quoting that list from מסכת מדות. רב הונא then resolves the conflict: מדות was taught by רבי אליעזר בן יעקב, who holds his own view. The first proof is a משנה about the four chambers of the עזרת נשים. In it, רבי אליעזר בן יעקב says 'I forgot' what the fourth chamber was used for, which shows he is the one reporting the list. The גמרא then brings a second proof from the low eastern wall, which let the כהן burning the פרה אדומה on הר המשחה see the entrance of the היכל. Adding up all the steps shows that only רבי אליעזר בן יעקב's extra step and דוכן make the entrance rise above the 20-אמה gate, so only his view explains why the wall had to be low. רב אדא בר אהבה rejects this proof. He says the low wall could instead follow רבי יהודה, who places the מזבח in the center of the עזרה. His explanation continues on the next amud.
