Yoma 62a

יומא ס״ב ע״א

Topic

Sending only one שעיר המשתלח and which one (ראשון or אחרון), then the new פרק's משנה on the two שעירי יום הכפורים

Overview

This amud finishes the discussion of the שעיר המשתלח. Someone challenges the rule that only one is sent, since sometimes several goats are burned. The answer is that the פסוק says "אותו" about the scapegoat, which limits it to one. Then two reports in the name of רבא disagree about which goat is sent when several are available. רב שימי says the last one, because the כפרה is completed with it. רב פפי says the first one, following רבי יוסי's rule of מצוה בראשון. The גמרא first tries to find this rule in רבי יוסי's ruling about the three labeled קופות, but rejects that source. It settles on his ruling about a lost-and-found קרבן פסח instead. After the הדרן, a new פרק opens with a משנה about the two שעירי יום הכפורים. The משנה covers how the goats should ideally match, and what to do if one dies before or after the lottery. It ends with the מחלוקת between the תנא קמא and רבי יהודה about the leftover goat.

Opening lines

וְכִי עֶדְרוֹ שׂוֹרֵף?

And does he burn his flock?

מִי דָּמֵי —

Is it comparable?

הָתָם לָא כְּתִיב ״אוֹתוֹ״,

There (about burning), "it" is not written,

הָכָא כְּתִיב ״אוֹתוֹ״.

here (about the scapegoat), "it" is written.
