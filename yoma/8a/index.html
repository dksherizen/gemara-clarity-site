Topic

The צִיץ verses for רבי יהודה and רבי שמעון, and the dispute over sprinkling the separated כהנים: whether הזאה is compared to טבילה

Overview

This עמוד opens by finishing a קל וחומר: if the כהן גדול may never take his mind off the צִיץ, which has God's name once, then all the more so a person may not take his mind off his תפילין, which have it many times. The גמרא then works through how רבי יהודה and רבי שמעון read the צִיץ verses. רבי יהודה says the צִיץ is מרצה only while it is worn. רבי שמעון says it is always מרצה, but only if it is whole and fit for the forehead, so a broken צִיץ does not work. Next the גמרא tries to link this dispute to a ברייתא about sprinkling the כהן גדול and the כהן who burns the פרה אדומה during their seven days of separation. רבי מאיר sprinkles every day, רבי יוסי only on the third and seventh days, and רבי חנינא סגן הכהנים splits the two cases. Two proposed reasons are rejected: טומאה דחויה versus הותרה, and whether טבילה בזמנה מצוה. The final answer is that רבי מאיר compares הזאה to טבילה and רבי יוסי does not. רבי חנינא does not compare them; the daily sprinkling for the כהן of the פרה is only an extra stringency (מעלה). The עמוד ends by starting to quote a ברייתא that lists the only difference between the two כהנים, and that ברייתא continues on the next עמוד.

שֶׁלֹּא יַסִּיחַ דַּעְתּוֹ מִמֶּנּוּ.

meaning that he should not take his mind off it.
