Topic

Is the lottery (הגרלה) over the two goats of יום הכיפורים indispensable (מעכבא)? Testing the second version of the dispute between רבי ינאי and רבי יוחנן against ברייתות

Overview

This amud asks whether the lottery (הגרלה) over the two goats of יום הכיפורים is מעכבא, meaning the service is invalid without it. It tests the second version (לישנא) of the dispute, where רבי ינאי holds that both רבי יהודה and רבי נחמיה require the lottery. First, a ברייתא saying 'if he did not draw lots, it is valid' is fixed by reading it as 'placing' the lots (להניח). A second ברייתא quotes רבי שמעון, who elsewhere allows a replacement goat with no lottery at all. That seems to block the fix. The גמרא answers that רבי שמעון was replying to both possible meanings of the רבנן. Then a third ברייתא about the order of the פר and the שעיר seems to show that the lottery can come late, and so is not required. The גמרא defends רבי ינאי in two ways. The ברייתא may follow רבי שמעון, or it may follow רבי יהודה and only free the lottery's order, not the lottery itself. The amud ends by introducing a ברייתא, on the next amud, showing that רבי יהודה and רבי שמעון are consistent with these views.
