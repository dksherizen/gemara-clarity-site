Topic

How the seven-day separation (פרישה) is learned from the מילואים: why the פרה אדומה is linked by the word צוה, and why 'לכפר' points to יום הכיפורים and not to קרבנות, the רגלים, or שמיני עצרת.

Overview

Earlier on the daf, the גמרא learned that the כהן who burns the פרה אדומה must be separated for seven days. It learned this through a גזירה שוה between the word צוה in the פרה section and the word צוה in the מילואים. Here the גמרא first defends that link. A צוה said before the action is not paired with the צוה of יום הכיפורים, which is said after the action. The exact form צוה beats the form צותו from the verse about קרבנות, because a merely similar word is used only when no identical word exists. Next the גמרא examines the word 'לכפר' in the מילואים, which teaches that the כהן גדול is separated before יום הכיפורים. It asks why the word should not mean ordinary קרבנות, the רגלים, one single רגל, or שמיני עצרת. Each time it finds a feature that only יום הכיפורים shares with the מילואים: a fixed time, once a year, seven days of separation before a one-day service, and no holiness right before it. When a קל וחומר challenges that last feature, רב משרשיא answers from the word 'הזה'. רב אשי answers instead that a secondary day like שמיני עצרת cannot be stricter than סוכות. His point that שמיני עצרת counts as a separate holiday only for certain laws continues on the next amud.
