Yoma 55b

יומא נ״ה ע״ב

Open Yoma 55b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

רבי יהודה and labels (כתיבה) in the מקדש: the single כן, the thirteen שופרות, the missing חטאת box, and רבי יהודה's rejection of ברירה.

Overview

The גמרא claims that רבי יהודה does not trust written labels (כתיבה) to prevent mix-ups. Its proof is his view that the blood of יום הכיפורים had only one stand (כן): if labels worked, two labeled stands would have been fine. A ברייתא challenges this. In רבי יהודה's own view, the מקדש had thirteen labeled collection boxes (שופרות), but no box for obligatory חטאת birds. רב דימי explains that the missing box is a rabbinic decree (גזירה) because of a חטאת whose owner has died. The גמרא objects that we normally presume an owner is alive. It answers that in a shared box, some donor's death is certain. Why not simply destroy four זוזים and permit the rest? Because רבי יהודה does not accept ברירה. The גמרא then asks for the source of this. It begins quoting a משנה about buying wine from כותים just before שבת, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לֵית לֵיהּ כְּתִיבָה.

[Rabbi Yehuda] does not accept writing.

דִּתְנַן, רַבִּי יְהוּדָה אוֹמֵר:

As we learned: Rabbi Yehuda says:

לֹא הָיָה שָׁם אֶלָּא כַּן אֶחָד בִּלְבַד.

There was only one stand there.

תְּרֵי מַאי טַעְמָא לָא —

Why not two [stands]?
