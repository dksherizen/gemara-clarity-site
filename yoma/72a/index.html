Topic

Strand counts of the priestly garments, prohibitions on damaging the בגדי כהונה and the ארון, and teachings about the משכן's boards

Overview

This amud first finishes working out how many strands go into each thread of the בגדי כהונה. A גזירה שוה from the פרוכת teaches that the מעיל uses six strands. The גמרא debates whether the robe's own רמונים, which have eight strands, would be a better source. The tie is broken by the ברייתא's extra mention of שש. The פרוכת has 24 strands: four materials of six strands each. The חושן and אפוד have 28 strands, because one strand of זהב is added to each of the four materials, as רב אחא בר יעקב and רב אשי prove. Next, רב יהודה and רבי אלעזר rule that tearing בגדי כהונה, detaching the חושן from the אפוד, or removing the staves of the ארון brings מלקות. That holds because the פסוק says לא and not שלא. The staves could shift inside their rings but never came out. The amud then gives three readings of עצי שטים עומדים, and it ends with רבי חמא בר חנינא asking about בגדי השרד, a question answered on the next amud.

Opening lines

כְּלִיל תְּכֵלֶת״,

"entirely of sky-blue"

וְיָלֵיף ״תְּכֵלֶת״ ״תְּכֵלֶת״ מִפָּרוֹכֶת:

and derive "sky-blue"–"sky-blue" from the curtain:

מָה לְהַלָּן שִׁשָּׁה,

just as there it is six,

אַף כָּאן שִׁשָּׁה.

so too here it is six.
