Yoma 87b

יומא פ״ז ע״ב

Open Yoma 87b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Forgiveness before יום הכפורים, the times and texts of וידוי, and the nature of the נעילה prayer

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a story. רב was reading before רבי. He started over for each great scholar who entered, but not for רבי חנינא בר חמא. רבי חנינא was hurt and refused רב's apologies for thirteen years. The גמרא explains both sides: רב went beyond the law, and רבי חנינא held back on purpose because of a dream that רב would lead in בבל. A ברייתא then teaches that וידוי is said before and after the last meal on ערב יום הכפורים and in every תפילה of יום הכפורים. Several אמוראים give different texts of וידוי, and מר זוטרא teaches that 'אבל אנחנו חטאנו' is the essential part. The גמרא then asks what נעילה is, and it rejects שמואל's view in favor of רב: נעילה is a full extra עמידה. Finally, רב rules that נעילה exempts one from ערבית. A ברייתא listing a ערבית after יום הכפורים challenges that ruling, and the discussion continues on the next amud.
