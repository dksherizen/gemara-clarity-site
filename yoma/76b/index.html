Yoma 76b

יומא ע״ו ע״ב

Open Yoma 76b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The meaning of שכר and תירוש, the two faces of wine, and the sources that refraining from anointing and bathing counts as עינוי on יום הכפורים.

Overview

This amud finishes a proof about the word שכר. It rejects the idea that sweet dried figs are included and learns from נזיר through a גזירה שוה that שכר means wine. The גמרא then asks whether תירוש means wine. A ברייתא about vows suggests it does not. The גמרא deflects two פסוקים, but a third, ״זנות ויין ותירוש יקח לב״, settles it: everyone agrees תירוש is wine in the פסוקים, and for vows we follow everyday speech. Wordplays on יין and תירוש, together with teachings of רב כהנא and רבא, show that wine can harm a person or raise him up, depending on whether he is worthy. Next the גמרא asks how we know that holding back from סיכה and רחיצה counts as עינוי on יום הכפורים. It proves anointing from דניאל's fast and the angel's word 'ולהתענות'. רב זוטרא's proof for bathing from תהלים is challenged by a משנה, so רב אשי learns bathing from ״וסוך לא סכתי״ itself. Finally, the גמרא asks what the angel meant by 'I have come because of your words' and begins an answer from יחזקאל's vision, which continues on the next amud.
