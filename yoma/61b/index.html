Topic

Replacing an אשם מצורע slaughtered שלא לשמה, how its blood is collected, and what happens to replaced יום הכפורים animals

Overview

An אשם מצורע that was slaughtered שלא לשמה still goes on the מזבח, but it does not make the מצורע pure. רבי יוחנן says that whether he can bring a second אשם depends on an argument between רבי מאיר, who starts over, and רבי אלעזר and רבי שמעון, who continue from where they stopped. רב חסדא objects that the פסוק says 'אותו', so no replacement should work for anyone, and the גמרא admits this is a difficulty. A ברייתא saying the מצורע 'needs another אשם' seems to support רבי יוחנן. רב חסדא reads 'needs' as 'he needs one but has no remedy', and the גמרא backs this reading from בית שמאי's ruling about a bald נזיר, as רבי אבינא explains it. רבי פדת disagrees with that reading and compares בית שמאי to רבי אלעזר, who lets the blood go on the spot of a missing thumb. Next, a ברייתא teaches that two כהנים catch the blood of the אשם מצורע: one in his hand for the מצורע, and one in a כלי for the מזבח. The גמרא then returns to יום הכפורים animals whose blood spilled and were replaced, and quotes a dispute over which of them are burned at the בית הדשן. Finally, רבא asks רב נחמן how many goats are sent to עזאזל, and רבא's reply continues on the next amud.
