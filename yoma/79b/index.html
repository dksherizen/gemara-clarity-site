Yoma 79b

יומא ע״ט ע״ב

Learn Yoma 79b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Yoma 79b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Is a כותבת הגסה bigger than a כביצה, and do fruits need a סוכה?

Overview

The גמרא compares two measures: the כותבת הגסה (a large date, the amount that makes one liable for eating on יום כיפור) and the כביצה (an egg's volume, the amount that must be eaten in a סוכה). The סוכה stories suggest that two dates are less than a כביצה, so how can one large date be more? רבי ירמיה answers that date pits are very large, and רב פפא backs this up with a folk saying. רבא answers differently: the dates were allowed outside the סוכה only because fruit never needs a סוכה. The גמרא defends רבא against a ברייתא about figs and grapes. It then rejects an attempted proof for him from a ברייתא about מיני תרגימא. רב זביד argues the opposite: a כותבת הגסה is smaller than a כביצה. His proof from בית שמאי's measures for שאור and חמץ is rejected. The גמרא then proves his point from רבי יהודה's rule about זימון: a כביצה satisfies a person, while a כותבת only settles the mind. The amud ends by opening a ברייתא from רבי, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הָא כְּבֵיצָה — בָּעֵי סוּכָּה.

This implies: an egg-bulk requires a sukkah.

וְאִי סָלְקָא דַעְתָּךְ

And if it should enter your mind

כּוֹתֶבֶת הַגַּסָּה שֶׁאָמְרוּ

that the large date they spoke of

יְתֵירָה מִכְּבֵיצָה,

is larger than an egg-bulk,

The steps (22)

  1. StatementAn egg's volume of food must be eaten in the סוכה
  2. ChallengeHow can one large date be bigger than an egg?
  3. Resolutionרבי ירמיה: Yes, the pit makes the difference
  4. Proofרב פפא: A folk saying proves pits are huge
  5. Resolutionרבא: Those dates were allowed outside because fruit needs no סוכה
  6. ChallengeChallenge: רבי ate figs and grapes outside only as a snack
  7. ResolutionRead it: We ate them like a snack
  8. ResolutionOr: The snack was the bread eaten with the fruit
  9. ProofPossible support for רבא from מיני תרגימא
  10. RejectionRejection: מיני תרגימא means fruit
  11. RejectionOr: It speaks of a place where fruit is rare
  12. Statementרב זביד: The large date is smaller than an egg
  13. QuestionWhy do בית שמאי give two different measures?
  14. ResponseThe Torah wrote both words to show different measures
  15. ProofIf a date were egg-sized or bigger, they would have said 'egg'
  16. RejectionMaybe they meant an ordinary date, or just picked one of two equal measures
  17. ProofA new source: the minimum amount of food for a זימון
  18. QuestionWhat is the basis of their disagreement?
  19. ResponseTwo readings of 'you shall eat and be satisfied'
  20. ProofIf an egg-bulk satisfies, a date must be smaller
  21. ConclusionEgg-bulk satisfies, date-bulk settles the mind
  22. StatementOpening a new teaching from רבי
