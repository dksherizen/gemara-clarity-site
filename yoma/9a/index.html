Yoma 9a

יומא ט׳ ע״א

Topic

The bakers' exemptions from tithing דמאי, the short terms of the מקדש שני's כהנים גדולים, and why שילה was destroyed

Overview

This amud finishes the גמרא's discussion of bakers (נחתומים) who bought דמאי grain from an עם הארץ. They need not separate תרומה גדולה, because יוחנן כהן גדול found that everyone already separated it. They need not give מעשר ראשון or מעשר עני, because a לוי or poor person must prove the grain was untithed (המוציא מחבירו עליו הראיה). The גמרא then challenges: why not require מעשר שני, which goes to no one else? עולא answers that government officials (פרהדרין, explained as פורסי) pressured the bakers all year to sell cheaply, so the רבנן did not burden them further. Next, רבי יוחנן reads a פסוק in משלי as a contrast. The מקדש ראשון stood 410 years with only 18 כהנים גדולים, while the מקדש שני stood 420 years with over 300, so apart from a few צדיקים, none of them finished a year. Finally, רבי יוחנן בן תורתא teaches that שילה was destroyed for גילוי עריות and בזיון קדשים, and the גמרא brings a פסוק about בני עלי. It then notes רבי יוחנן's view that בני עלי did not literally sin, and begins an explanation that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לְפִי שֶׁשָּׁלַח בְּכׇל גְּבוּלֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל,

Because he sent messengers throughout all the borders of Israel,

וְרָאָה שֶׁאֵין מַפְרִישִׁין

and he saw that they do not separate

אֶלָּא תְּרוּמָה גְּדוֹלָה בִּלְבָד.

anything except the great terumah alone.

מַעֲשֵׂר רִאשׁוֹן וּמַעְשַׂר עָנִי נָמֵי לָא,

First tithe and poor man's tithe are also not required,
