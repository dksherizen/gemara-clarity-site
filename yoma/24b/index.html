Yoma 24b

יומא כ״ד ע״ב

Open Yoma 24b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Which services make a זר liable to מיתה (inside, outside, and in the היכל; עבודה תמה), and the details of the daily lotteries

Overview

This amud continues the discussion of which Temple services make a זר (non-כהן) liable to מיתה. From the פסוק "ולמבית לפרוכת ועבדתם", the גמרא learns that the rule 'only עבודת מתנה, not עבודת סילוק' applies only inside. Outside, even a removal service makes a זר liable. The ו of "ועבדתם" shows that the rule of עבודה תמה applies both inside and outside. רבא asks whether the היכל counts as inside or outside, and he answers from the extra letters of "ולמבית" that it counts as inside. The גמרא then tests this rule on the שלחן, the מנורה, and the מערכה on the altar. Each step turns out to be incomplete, except lighting the מנורה, which is not an עבודה at all. רב and רבי יוחנן disagree about the two logs, and a ברייתא is brought supporting each of רב and לוי. Finally, the גמרא returns to the משנה's lotteries. רבי יוחנן explains that they were repeated to stir up the עזרה. רב נחמן and רב ששת then argue about whether the כהנים wore weekday or holy clothes for the lottery.

Opening lines

״וּלְמִבֵּית לַפָּרוֹכֶת וַעֲבַדְתֶּם״,

"And to within the veil; and you shall serve" (Bamidbar 18:7)

״אֶל מִבֵּית לַפָּרוֹכֶת״ הוּא

it is "to within the veil"

דַּעֲבוֹדַת מַתָּנָה

that a giving-service brings liability

וְלֹא עֲבוֹדַת סִילּוּק.

but not a removal-service.
