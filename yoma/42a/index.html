Yoma 42a

יומא מ״ב ע״א

Open Yoma 42a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The weights of the crimson wool strips (לשון של זהורית) used for the פרה אדומה, the שעיר המשתלח, and the מצורע, and רבי יצחק's doubt about which שחיטה may be done by a זר: the פרה אדומה or the פר of the כהן גדול.

Overview

This amud opens by finishing a ruling about the weight of the crimson strip used for the פרה אדומה, the שעיר המשתלח and the מצורע. רבי יוחנן reports a מחלוקת about the פרה's strip. רבי ירמיה מדיפתי says the dispute was really about the שעיר's strip, and the death of רביא בר קיסי that day became its סימן. Next, רבי יצחק remembers that a זר may slaughter either the פרה or the פר of the כהן גדול, but he does not remember which one. רב and שמואל disagree about this, and the גמרא proves that רב is the one who invalidates the פרה, because of the words ״אלעזר״ and ״חוקה״. The גמרא defends רב with several points: שחיטה is not an עבודה, the פרה is קדשי בדק הבית, and מראות נגעים show that a כהן can be required even where there is no עבודה. For שמואל, the verse ״לפניו״ shows that a זר slaughters while אלעזר watches. The גמרא then explains the rule against taking one's attention off the פרה, and why it must be written for both slaughtering and burning. The amud ends as the גמרא begins asking which stage this rule leaves out, and the discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שֶׁל פָּרָה — מִשְׁקַל עֲשָׂרָה זוּז,

The strip of the red heifer: the weight of ten zuz,

וְשֶׁל שָׂעִיר הַמִּשְׁתַּלֵּחַ — מִשְׁקַל שְׁנֵי סְלָעִים,

and that of the scapegoat: the weight of two sela'im (eight zuz),

וְשֶׁל מְצוֹרָע — מִשְׁקַל שֶׁקֶל.

and that of the leper: the weight of a shekel (two zuz).

אָמַר רַבִּי יוֹחָנָן:

Rabbi Yochanan said:
