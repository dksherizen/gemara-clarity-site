Yoma 67a

יומא ס״ז ע״א

Learn Yoma 67a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Yoma 67a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The end of the שעיר המשתלח's journey on יום כפור: the booths and escorts, the split red strip and its history, and whether one may benefit from the goat's limbs.

Overview

This עמוד finishes the משנה about the last stage of the שעיר המשתלח on יום כפור. Booths along the 12-מיל route offered the sender food and water, and people escorted him from booth to booth. At the cliff he tied half of a red strip (לשון של זהורית) to a rock and half between the goat's horns, then pushed the goat backward off the cliff. He waited under the last booth until dark, and the משנה ends with a מחלוקת about when his clothes become טמא. The גמרא brings a ברייתא where רבי מאיר, רבי יהודה and רבי יוסי argue about the number of booths and the distance. רבי יוסי says the escorts relied on an עירוב, and the line about the last escort staying back is shown to follow רבי מאיר. The גמרא then explains several points. The food was offered only for peace of mind. The strip was split so the sender would stay focused on the goat and still be able to see the sign. Two traditions describe how the strip used to hang at the אולם, with the פסוק 'כשנים כשלג ילבינו' as a hint that red turning white means כפרה. Finally, רב and שמואל disagree about whether one may benefit from the goat's limbs, and the explanation of their reasoning continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

תִּשְׁעִים רִיס,

Ninety ris (total distance),

שִׁבְעָה וּמֶחֱצָה לְכׇל מִיל.

seven and a half for each mil (so 12 mil in all).

עַל כׇּל סוּכָּה וְסוּכָּה אוֹמְרִין לוֹ:

At each and every booth they say to him:

הֲרֵי מָזוֹן, וַהֲרֵי מַיִם.

Here is food, and here is water.
