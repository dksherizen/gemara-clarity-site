Topic

The קטורת procedure inside קודש הקדשים, and whether a replacement כהן גדול may use the blood of a פר his predecessor slaughtered, with challenges from פסח and פטר חמור.

Overview

This amud opens with a ברייתא describing how the כהן גדול, inside קודש הקדשים on יום הכיפורים, moves the קטורת from the ladle back into his cupped hands. It then records a dispute over whether he heaps the קטורת on the coals or scatters it. The ברייתא calls this the hardest עבודה in the מקדש. The גמרא objects that מליקה and קמיצה are also very hard, so it rereads the line as 'one of the hardest'. From the ברייתא it proves that the כהן גדול scoops the קטורת a second time inside (חופן וחוזר וחופן). Next comes a new question: if the כהן גדול slaughtered his פר and then died, may a replacement enter with its blood? Two pairs of אמוראים split on how to read 'בפר'. רבי אמי challenges רבי יצחק from the deadline for joining a פסח group, and מר זוטרא challenges from the rule against redeeming a פטר חמור with a slaughtered animal. Both proofs are turned aside as special rules tied to the word 'שה' by פסח, and the גמרא explains how the doubled 'תפדה' widens the donkey law while 'שה' still limits it.
