Yoma 69b

יומא ס״ט ע״ב

Learn Yoma 69b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Yoma 69b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Where the כהן גדול could sit during the Yom Kippur reading (sitting is banned in the עזרה), the source of רב חסדא's answer, and what 'הגדול' means in עזרא's blessing.

Overview

The גמרא asks how the כהן גדול could sit before his יום כיפור reading, since only kings of בית דוד may sit in the עזרה. The answer, based on רב חסדא's statement about the הקהל reading, is that the reading took place in the עזרת נשים. A פסוק about עזרא's public reading leads to the question of what 'the great God' means in his blessing. One view is that עזרא said the שם המפורש. Another explains it as a special wording of praise. Since the שם המפורש is not said outside the מקדש, עזרא's use of it is explained as a הוראת שעה. The גמרא then tells how the people at that gathering prayed to be rid of the יצר הרע for עבודה זרה and sealed it in lead, and how they weakened the יצר for עבירה by blinding it without destroying it. Next comes the version from ארץ ישראל: the אנשי כנסת הגדולה restored 'great, mighty, and awesome' after ירמיה and דניאל had dropped words out of truthfulness. Finally, the גמרא returns to the משנה and asks how the כהן גדול may skip from 'אחרי מות' to 'אך בעשור'. אביי answers that skipping in the Torah is allowed within one topic, and a ברייתא supports him.

Opening lines

אֵין יְשִׁיבָה בָּעֲזָרָה

There is no sitting in the Temple courtyard

אֶלָּא לְמַלְכֵי בֵּית דָּוִד בִּלְבַד.

except for kings of the House of David alone.

שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: ״וַיָּבֹא הַמֶּלֶךְ דָּוִד

As it is stated: 'Then King David came'

וַיֵּשֶׁב לִפְנֵי ה׳״?

and sat before the Lord'? (So how could the High Priest be sitting?)
