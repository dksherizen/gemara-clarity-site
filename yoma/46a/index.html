Topic

The end of the צריכותא about where the fire for the קטורת is taken from, and רבי מאיר's ruling (via בר קפרא) that leftover limbs of a עולה get their own מערכה, even on שבת. The ruling is explained as referring to פסולין, but only when the fire has already caught them.

Overview

This amud opens by finishing an earlier צריכותא: both 'מעל המזבח' and 'מלפני ה'' are needed, so fire for the קטורת may be taken even from the sides of the מזבח. Then רבי אלעזר quotes בר קפרא: רבי מאיר said that leftover limbs of a עולה get their own מערכה, even on שבת. The גמרא asks what this adds to the משנה's four מערכות. רבי אבין answers that it teaches the rule even for פסולין. The גמרא then limits this to limbs that the fire has already caught, and gives a second version that applies this limit to valid limbs as well. Next the גמרא asks why 'even on שבת' was needed, since the משנה already has five מערכות on יום הכפורים. רב אחא בר יעקב gives an answer, but רבא rejects it because the משנה says 'every day', and the difficulty stays as a קשיא. The גמרא notes that this view disagrees with רב הונא, who holds that the end of the תמיד service does not override. רב חסדא and רבה disagree about what רב הונא meant: שבת or טומאה. אביי challenges both of them from the word 'במועדו'. רבה begins his defense with 'סופו כתחילתו', and his reply continues on the next amud.
