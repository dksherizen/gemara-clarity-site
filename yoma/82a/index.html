Yoma 82a

יומא פ״ב ע״א

Learn Yoma 82a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Yoma 82a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Training children to fast on יום הכפורים; feeding the pregnant and the sick; פיקוח נפש and the three sins of יהרג ואל יעבור

Overview

The משנה says children are not made to fast on יום הכפורים, but they are trained one or two years before adulthood. רב חסדא explains that a weak child starts one year early and a healthy child two years early. רב הונא, רב נחמן and רבי יוחנן then give age charts for fasting a few hours, a full fast מדרבנן, and a full fast מדאורייתא. רבי יוחנן says there is no rabbinic full-fast stage at all. The גמרא tests each side against the משנה and a ברייתא, and every view survives. רבא בר עולא concludes that there are two kinds of training: delaying meals for young children, and a full fast for older ones. A new משנה rules that a pregnant woman with a craving, or a sick person, is fed on יום הכפורים. A ברייתא explains that פיקוח נפש overrides every איסור except עבודה זרה, גילוי עריות and שפיכות דמים. The גמרא then gives the source for each of those three exceptions.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳

MISHNAH:

הַתִּינוֹקוֹת אֵין מְעַנִּין אוֹתָן

The children, one does not afflict them (make them fast)

בְּיוֹם הַכִּפּוּרִים,

on Yom Kippur;

אֲבָל מְחַנְּכִין אוֹתָן

but one trains them

  15. StatementFeeding a sick person based on experts or his own word

  17. StatementNothing stands in the way of saving a life except three sins
  18. QuestionWhere do we learn that עבודה זרה is an exception?
  19. Responseרבי אליעזר asks why the verse says both 'soul' and 'might'
  20. ProofEach person is told to give up what he values most
  21. QuestionWhere do we learn the exceptions of גילוי עריות and שפיכות דמים?
  22. Responseרבי quotes the verse comparing the betrothed girl to a murder victim
  23. QuestionWhat does a murderer teach us about the betrothed girl?

  25. ConclusionFrom the murderer to the betrothed girl: one must die rather than sin
