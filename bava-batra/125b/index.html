Topic

A man left his property to his grandmother and then 'to my heirs.' Does his late daughter's husband inherit, and what is the rule of ראוי versus מוחזק for a husband and a בכור?

Overview

A man wrote: 'My property goes to my grandmother, and after her to my heirs.' His married daughter died before the grandmother. When the grandmother died, the daughter's husband claimed the property as his wife's heir. רב הונא says 'my heirs' includes the heirs of my heirs, so the husband wins. רב ענן says it does not, so the husband loses. A ruling from ארץ ישראל follows רב ענן's result but gives a different reason. The property was only ראוי (due to come) to the wife, and a husband does not inherit ראוי the way he inherits מוחזק (what she actually held). רבי אלעזר defends רב הונא: saying 'after you' is like saying 'from now,' so the wife already owned it. רבה backs the ארץ ישראל view, because the grandmother could have sold the property. רב פפא rules that a husband and a בכור take only from מוחזק, and a בכור gets no double share of a loan.
