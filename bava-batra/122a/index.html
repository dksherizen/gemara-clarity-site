Topic

How ארץ ישראל was divided among the שבטים: equal portions per שבט, money payments to even out land values, and the use of a גורל together with the אורים ותומים.

Overview

The גמרא wants to prove that ארץ ישראל was divided by שבטים. It brings the פסוק 'בין רב למעט' and then a long ברייתא. The ברייתא says the first division was among twelve שבטים and the future one will be among thirteen. It says money was used to even out the value of the portions, and that a גורל and the אורים ותומים worked together: אלעזר announced the result, and the lottery boxes confirmed it. It also says that in the future everyone will get every kind of land, and הקדוש ברוך הוא will divide it Himself. From the line 'twelve שבטים' the גמרא concludes that the land was divided by שבטים. The גמרא then explains parts of the ברייתא. The thirteenth share is real land for the נשיא. The money made up for land that was near or far from ירושלים, and תנאים argue whether that was paid in money or in land. Finally, 'אך בגורל' teaches that יהושע and כלב got their land by the word of ה׳ and not by lottery. The proof for יהושע is brought here, and the proof for כלב comes on the next amud.

Opening lines

תָּא שְׁמַע:

Come and hear a proof:

״בֵּין רַב לִמְעָט״.

"Between the more and the fewer."

וְעוֹד, תַּנְיָא:

And also, it was taught in a ברייתא:

עֲתִידָה אֶרֶץ יִשְׂרָאֵל

Eretz Yisrael is destined
