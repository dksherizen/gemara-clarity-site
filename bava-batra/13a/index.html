Topic

Dividing shared property that cannot be split well: the rule of גוד או אגוד, and sharing a slave by days

Overview

This amud first finishes the case of a field with water on two sides, which is split diagonally so each brother touches both. Then it returns to the משנה about a hall (טרקלין) too small to split. רב יהודה says a partner can force a buyout with גוד או אגוד; רב נחמן says he cannot. רבא challenges רב נחמן with brothers who inherit a slave, and רב נחמן answers that they share the slave's days. The גמרא then objects to רב יהודה from a משנה about a half-slave. There the court forces the master to free him only because he could not otherwise marry and have children. The גמרא answers that this case is different: the slave can buy his master's half, but he cannot sell himself, so גוד או אגוד could not work there anyway. Finally the גמרא begins a new proof about two brothers, one rich and one poor, who inherit a bathhouse and an olive press. That proof continues on the next amud.
