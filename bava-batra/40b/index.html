Topic

When a מודעא is valid, the hidden gift (מתנתא טמירתא), and whether a hidden gift cancels another gift

Overview

This amud finishes a rule about the wording of a מודעא: the witnesses must write that they know about the person's duress. The גמרא asks what kind of deal this rule is about. A גט or a מתנה needs no proof of pressure, and רבא said we don't write a מודעא for a sale. The answer is that it is about a sale where the seller was truly forced, like the orchard story. Next, רב יהודה rules that a hidden gift document cannot be used to collect, and רב יוסף defines a hidden gift in two versions that differ when the giver said nothing. רבא is quoted as saying a hidden gift also cancels another gift. רב פפא shows this was only inferred from a story about a woman and an eldest son. In that story the woman's gift really failed because it was given under obvious pressure. The amud ends by opening a new question that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דְּלָא כְּתִיב בָּהּ:

That does not have written in it:

"We know about him

בְּאוּנְסָא דִפְלָנְיָא״

the duress of so-and-so"

– לָאו מוֹדָעָא הִיא.

is not a declaration.
