Topic

Rewriting documents and documents with problem dates: replacement שטרות, a half-paid loan, and a שטר dated on שבת or יום כיפור

Overview

The גמרא asks whether witnesses who already wrote a שטר can write a replacement. A בית דין has the power to take away money, but witnesses seem to finish their job after one document. רב says witnesses may write even ten documents for one field. רב יוסף and רבה answer that רב meant documents that cannot take money from buyers: a gift document, or a document with no אחריות. The גמרא then quotes a full ברייתא about a loan that was half paid back. רבי יהודה says to write a new שטר dated from the first date, while רבי יוסי says to keep the old שטר and write a שובר, for two reasons. A second ברייתא records another argument between them: רבי יהודה says a שטר dated on שבת or יום כיפור is a valid שטר מאוחר, and רבי יוסי says it is invalid. רבי יוסי explains away his own ruling in ציפורי, and רבי פדת begins to explain the difference by describing a case where everyone agrees such a שטר is valid.

Opening lines

בִּשְׁלָמָא בֵּי דִינָא –

Granted, a court

אַלִּימֵי לְאַפְקוֹעֵי מָמוֹנָא.

is powerful enough to take away money.

אֶלָּא עֵדִים שֶׁעָשׂוּ שְׁלִיחוּתָן –

But witnesses who already performed their mission,

חוֹזְרִין וְעוֹשִׂין שְׁלִיחוּתָן?!

can they go back and perform their mission again?!
