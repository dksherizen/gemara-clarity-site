Topic

A gift of all a man's property to his wife: is it real ownership or just management, and when does such a gift cost her the כתובה?

Overview

Earlier, the גמרא taught that when a שכיב מרע writes all his property to his wife, he only makes her an אפוטרופוס, so that people will respect her. רבא asks whether the same is true of a healthy man (בריא). The גמרא tries to answer from a ברייתא. There, a man gave his wife all his property and then an earlier שטר חוב appeared. The חכמים rule that she loses both her כתובה and the gift. The proof is that, without the creditor, the gift would have been hers, so the ברייתא seems to be about a healthy man. The proof is pushed off: the ברייתא can be about a שכיב מרע in cases where the wife really does acquire. רב נחמן then rules like the חכמים. The גמרא asks whether he ignores אומדנא, since he follows רבי שמעון בן מנסיא elsewhere. It answers that a wife wants it known that she was given everything. Finally, the גמרא quotes a משנה: a wife who is written a bit of land while the sons get the property loses her כתובה. רב, שמואל, and רבי יוסי ברבי חנינא each explain what case that means.

Opening lines

בָּעֵי רָבָא:

Rava asked:

בְּבָרִיא הֵיאַךְ?

In the case of a healthy person, what is the law?

בִּשְׁכִיב מְרַע הוּא דְּנִיחָא לֵיהּ –

Is it only a person on his deathbed who prefers

דְּלִישְׁתַּמְעוּן מִלֵּהּ,

that her word be listened to,
