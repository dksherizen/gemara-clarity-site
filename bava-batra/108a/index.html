Topic

The end of the ditches discussion and the close of פרק בית כור. Then the opening of פרק יש נוחלין: its משנה on four inheritance groups and the גמרא's question about the order of the משנה's list.

Overview

First, the גמרא finishes a discussion from the previous page about two ditches dug to keep wild animals from jumping in: a larger חריץ and a smaller בן חריץ. It asks why both are needed. A wide ditch alone lets the animal stand inside it and jump. A narrow ditch alone lets the animal stand on its edge and jump. So both are dug, one טפח apart. Then a הדרן closes פרק בית כור, and a new chapter, פרק יש נוחלין, begins. Its משנה sorts relatives into four groups: those who inherit both ways, those who only receive, those who only pass on, and those who do neither. The גמרא then asks why the משנה lists the father inheriting from his sons before the sons inheriting from the father. It gives a first reason the sons should come first: we do not open with misfortune. The rest of the question continues on the next amud.
