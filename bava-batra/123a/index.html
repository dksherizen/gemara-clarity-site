Topic

The בכור's double portion, why the בכורה went to יוסף, and the count of יעקב's family

Overview

This amud opens in the middle of a ברייתא about how big the בכור's double portion (פי שנים) is. Logic alone could argue either for 'double what one brother gets' or for 'double the whole estate.' A פסוק settles it: the בכור gets double what one brother gets. The ברייתא backs this up with פסוקים about ראובן's בכורה passing to יוסף, who received two full shares through אפרים and מנשה. The גמרא then explains why each extra פסוק was needed. Next, רבי חלבו asks why יעקב gave the בכורה specifically to יוסף. The answer, from רבי יונתן, is that it belonged to רחל's line: לאה won it through tearful prayer, and רחל earned it back through her modesty in giving her secret signs to לאה. The amud ends with a question about why יעקב's family is counted as seventy but the list of names shows only sixty-nine, and that discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

חֶלְקוֹ עִם אֶחָד,

His portion with one brother,

וְחֶלְקוֹ עִם חֲמִשָּׁה;

and his portion with five brothers;

מָה חֶלְקוֹ עִם אֶחָד –

just as his portion with one brother

פִּי שְׁנַיִם כְּאֶחָד,

is double what one brother gets,
