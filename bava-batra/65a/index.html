Topic

How generously property is transferred: rulings on רבי עקיבא and חכמים, brothers dividing an estate, passage between inner and outer houses, and what comes with a sold house

Overview

The משנה just before this argued about a seller who keeps something inside the property he sold. רבי עקיבא holds a seller sells generously, and חכמים hold he sells sparingly. רב rules like חכמים and שמואל rules like רבי עקיבא. רב ירמיה בר אבא's story about רב's silence turns out to fit this, because he had the names in the משנה reversed. רבינא shows that רב and שמואל argue the same way about brothers who divide an estate, and the גמרא explains why both cases had to be stated. רב הונא then admits the הלכה follows שמואל's side, since his students sat near the judges at the ריש גלותא's gate. Next comes a ruling about two houses, one behind the other. The גמרא concludes that when the inner house is given as a gift it comes with a path, because a gift is given generously. Finally a new משנה begins listing what is included when a house is sold: fixed parts yes, movable items no. Its case of a seller who says 'it and everything inside it' continues on the next amud.
