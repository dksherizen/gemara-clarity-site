Topic

Do we follow the רוב (majority) in money cases? The גמרא tests the view that we do, using the case of a slave sold with a hidden flaw and the case of an ox that gored a pregnant cow.

Overview

This amud continues the debate between רב and שמואל over whether we follow the רוב (majority) in money cases. First the גמרא tries to prove רב's view from the case of a slave who turns out to be a thief or a gambler, but it rejects the proof because all slaves are assumed to be like that. Next it challenges רב from a משנה about an ox that gored a pregnant cow. The answer is that the רוב shows the ox's attack caused the miscarriage, but we still don't know whether it was fright or goring, so the money is split. The גמרא then tries to match רב and שמואל to a dispute between תנאים about an animal found dead beside a dangerous animal. Both רב and שמואל escape that link by showing that a רוב and a חזקה are not the same. Finally the גמרא challenges רב from the משנה about flaxseed that did not sprout. It answers that רב follows other תנאים, and it begins quoting a ברייתא whose last opinion, from רבי יוסי, continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַאי טַעְמָא?

What is the reason?

לָאו מִשּׁוּם דְּרוּבָּא הָכִי אִיתַנְהוּ?

Is it not because most of them are like this?

לָא, כּוּלְּהוּ הָכִי אִיתַנְהוּ.

No, all of them are like this.
