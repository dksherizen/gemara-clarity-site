Bava Basra 90b

בבא בתרא צ׳ ע״ב

Topic

Enlarging communal measures, and the rules against hoarding, price-gouging and exporting food from ארץ ישראל.

Overview

The גמרא finishes the פסוק in יחזקאל that seems to make a מנה equal sixty שקלים. It learns three rules from it: the holy מנה was double, a community may enlarge measures by up to a sixth, and that sixth is counted as part of the new total. In a story, רב פפא בר שמואל makes a bigger measure and defends it as a brand-new measure. Some towns accepted it and some did not. A memory aid then opens a new topic: protecting the public's food supply and prices. A ברייתא says the נביא עמוס condemns hoarders, lenders who charge רבית, people who cheat on measures, and price-gougers. The גמרא compares how שמואל and his father sold produce and explains why selling early is better. It then sets the limits of the hoarding ban: one's own produce, spices, and the years around שביעית are allowed, but in a drought nothing may be stored. Finally, a ברייתא forbids taking basic foods out of ארץ ישראל, even to סוריא. רבי's lenient view begins at the end of the amud and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מָנֶה?!

A maneh?!

מָאתָן וְאַרְבְּעִין הָווּ!

They are two hundred and forty!

אֶלָּא שְׁמַע מִינַּהּ תְּלָת:

Rather, learn from it three things:

שְׁמַע מִינַּהּ מָנֶה שֶׁל קֹדֶשׁ – כָּפוּל הָיָה;

Learn from it that the holy maneh was doubled;
