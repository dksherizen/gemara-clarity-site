Topic

אבק לשון הרע; קפידא versus מראה מקום in the dispute about local custom for documents; and whether a written witness and a spoken witness combine.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing רב's teaching about לשון הרע. He says most people fail in גזל, some in עריות, and everyone in לשון הרע, and the גמרא explains that 'everyone' means only אבק לשון הרע, a faint trace of it. The גמרא then returns to the משנה about פשוט and מקושר documents, where רבן שמעון בן גמליאל says everything follows local custom. רב אשי explains that both sides accept local custom. They argue only in a town that uses both kinds of documents, when the person asked for a פשוט and got a מקושר: was he being strict (קפידא), or just pointing out an option (מראה מקום)? אביי adds that רבן שמעון בן גמליאל, רבי שמעון and רבי אלעזר all hold מראה מקום, and the גמרא proves this from משניות in קידושין and גיטין. Next the גמרא asks why the משנה needs to say that a פשוט with one witness is invalid. אביי answers that even one written witness plus one spoken witness do not combine. אמימר disagrees with אביי, and רב אשי points out that the question on the משנה then comes back; the discussion continues on the next amud.

Rather, [he meant] the dust of evil speech.

and a minority in forbidden relations,
