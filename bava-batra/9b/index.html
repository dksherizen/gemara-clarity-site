Topic

The greatness and rewards of צדקה, with a story about the nickname of a Sage and the source that a מצורע in his counting days makes a person impure.

Overview

The גמרא teaches that every small coin of צדקה joins into one big total, like scales in a coat of mail or threads in a garment. It then tells why the Sage who reported this was nicknamed 'the infant who perverted the ways of his mother'. רב אחדבוי בר אמי mockingly took apart רב ששת's proof that a מצורע in his counting days makes a person impure. רב אחדבוי was punished until his mother, who had nursed רב ששת, pleaded for him. The גמרא then gives the real source for that rule: a גזירה שוה from רבי שמעון בן יוחי. Back on צדקה, רבי אלעזר says secret giving is greater than משה רבינו, and רבי יצחק reads the same פסוק differently. רבי יצחק adds that giving a פרוטה earns six blessings and comforting the poor earns eleven, and that הקב״ה gives the pursuer of צדקה money to give, while רב נחמן בר יצחק says He sends him worthy recipients. The עמוד ends with רבי יהושע בן לוי's teaching that regular givers merit wise, wealthy, and learned sons, and the first proof continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

״מְשַׁגֵּשׁ אֹרְחָתַיהּ דְּאִימֵּיהּ״,

"who perverted the ways of his mother,"

מִשְּׁמֵיהּ דְּרַבִּי אֶלְעָזָר –

said in the name of Rabbi Elazar:

מַאי דִּכְתִיב:

What is meant by that which is written:

״וַיִּלְבַּשׁ צְדָקָה כַּשִּׁרְיֹן״?

"And He put on charity like a coat of mail"?
