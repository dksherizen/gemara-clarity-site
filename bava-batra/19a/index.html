Topic

Distancing damaging things from a neighbor's wall: launderers' ponds, plaster with lime, rocks versus sand, and seeds versus plowing

Overview

The עמוד opens with the last line of an earlier discussion about bees and a neighbor's mustard plants. Next comes the משנה's rule about a launderers' pond. רב נחמן says the three טפחים applies only to a soaking pond (מחמצן), while a washing pond (נדיין) needs four אמות. This is first proven from a ברייתא, then retold as a contradiction that his distinction resolves. The גמרא then asks whether the משנה says 'and plasters with lime' or 'or plasters with lime'. Two proofs are tried, and each one is pushed aside. Next the גמרא compares our משנה's list of heat-producing materials with a list in מסכת שבת and asks why one mentions rocks and the other sand. After answers from רב יוסף and אביי are rejected, רבא explains that rocks scratch pots and dry sand does not harm walls, and it is defended against a ברייתא about damp sand. Finally the גמרא explains why the משנה lists seeds, a plow and water separately, and begins a new question about seeds that continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

לָא מַשְׁכַּחַתְּ לֵיהּ,

[the bees] will not find it;

אִי בְּטַרְפָּא – הָדַר פָּארֵי.

if [they eat] a leaf – it grows back.

וְלֹא נִבְרֶכֶת הַכּוֹבְסִין וְכוּ׳.

[The משנה says:] Nor a launderers' pond, etc.

אָמַר רַב נַחְמָן אָמַר רַבָּה בַּר אֲבוּהּ:

Rav Naḥman said in the name of Rabba bar Avuh:
