Topic

יוכבד's renewed youth and wedding, and the בנות צלפחד: the order of their names, whom they could marry, and why the tribe rule lasted only one generation

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a side discussion about עמרם remarrying יוכבד. The גמרא asks how a 130-year-old woman can be called 'בַּת', and why the פסוק says 'וַיִּקַּח' (took) instead of 'took back.' רב יהודה בר זבידא answers that her youth returned and that עמרם made her a full wedding. The גמרא then returns to בנות צלפחד. Their names appear in one order by age and in another by wisdom, which supports רבי אמי's rule: honor wisdom when learning and age at a meal. רב אשי limits this rule to someone who clearly stands out. The school of רבי ישמעאל instead says all five sisters were equal. שמואל rules they could marry into any tribe, and he reads 'only within their father's tribe' as good advice. רבה challenges this from a ברייתא and answers that the tribe rule applied to everyone except בנות צלפחד. Finally, רבא learns from 'זה הדבר' that the tribe rule was only for that generation, and רב אשי explains why שחוטי חוץ is different.

Opening lines

אֶפְשָׁר בַּת מֵאָה וּשְׁלֹשִׁים שָׁנָה,

Is it possible? She was one hundred thirty years old,

וְקֹרֵא לָהּ: ״בַּת״?!

and the verse calls her 'daughter'?!

As Rabbi Ḥama bar Ḥanina said:

זוֹ יוֹכֶבֶד;
