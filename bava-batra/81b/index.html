Bava Basra 81b

בבא בתרא פ״א ע״ב

Topic

Why a person whose land ownership is doubtful brings ביכורים without reading the פרשה, and how the doubtful fruit is handled: הקדש, redemption, תרומה and מעשר, and bringing it without the recitation.

Overview

רבי שמעון בן אליקים asks רבי אלעזר why someone who owns one tree (according to רבי מאיר) or two trees (according to רבנן) brings ביכורים but does not read the פרשה. רבי אלעזר answers that even the earlier sages gave no reason. רבה explains that both opinions are unsure whether the tree-owner also owns the land. So he brings, in case he is obligated, but does not read. The גמרא then works out how to handle such doubtful fruit. He makes it הקדש, it is redeemed so the כהן can eat it, and תרומה and מעשר are separated from it. Every gift goes to a כהן, and מעשר ראשון also goes to a כהן, following רבי אלעזר בן עזריה. The גמרא says the missing recitation is not essential, and רבי זירא's rule challenges this: a step that cannot be done at all does hold things back. The גמרא answers with רבי יוסי בר חנינא's case of ביכורים that are brought without any recitation, and his proof from the פסוק continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַאי טַעְמָא דְּרַבִּי מֵאִיר בְּאִילָן אֶחָד,

What is the reason of Rabbi Meir regarding one tree,

וּמַאי טַעְמָא דְּרַבָּנַן בִּשְׁנֵי אִילָנוֹת?

and what is the reason of the Rabbis regarding two trees?

אָמַר לוֹ:

He said to him:

דָּבָר שֶׁהָרִאשׁוֹנִים לֹא אָמְרוּ בּוֹ טַעַם,

A matter for which the earlier ones did not give a reason,
