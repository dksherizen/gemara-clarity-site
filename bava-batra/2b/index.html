Topic

What מחיצה means in the opening משנה of בבא בתרא, and whether היזק ראיה (harm from being seen) counts as damage.

Overview

The opening משנה says that partners who 'wanted to make a מחיצה' in a shared courtyard build a wall in the middle. The גמרא first finishes a proof that מחיצה means a partition-wall. On that reading, the wall is built only when both partners agree, so היזק ראיה (harm from being seen) is not called damage. The גמרא then suggests that מחיצה means 'division,' which would point the other way. It goes back to the partition reading by looking closely at the משנה's wording. Next it explains why the משנה needs to say the wall goes 'in the middle': it covers a partner who was talked into building. Then the גמרא challenges the idea that היזק ראיה is not damage, using a list of sources: the garden, a fallen wall, a gatehouse, dividing a courtyard, windows, and רב נחמן's rule about a roof. Each proof is pushed aside by showing that its case is different. The answer to the last proof, the roof, continues on the next amud.
