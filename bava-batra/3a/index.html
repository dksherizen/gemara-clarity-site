Topic

Partners dividing a courtyard: היזק ראיה, binding the agreement with a קנין, the wall's building materials and thickness, and the אמה טרקסין

Overview

This amud opens with the last words of the first version of the discussion about the משנה's wall between partners. Then the גמרא gives a second version. It asks whether מחיצה means 'division' or 'wall', and whether היזק ראיה (damage from being seen) counts as real damage. After a back-and-forth about the wording, the 'division' reading stands. רבי יוחנן explains that the משנה is about a courtyard too small to be divided by force, which is split only when both partners agree. Our משנה teaches that a real wall is then required, and a later משנה is needed for its rule that holy books are never divided. The גמרא then asks why an agreement binds the partners at all. It answers that they made a קנין on specific sides of the courtyard, or, according to רב אשי, each took possession of his own part. Next the גמרא defines the building materials גויל, גזית, כפיסין and לבנים, and רב אשי teaches that these meanings come from tradition. אביי learns that the gap between a wall's rows is a טפח, and the גמרא discusses how thick a wall must be, using the אמה טרקסין of the first מקדש. The amud ends with a dispute over whether the second מקדש was 'greater' in its building, and that dispute continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דְּאִיצְטַנַּע מִינָּךְ.

so that I can be hidden from you.

לִישָּׁנָא אַחֲרִינָא אָמְרִי לַהּ:

Another version says it this way:

סַבְרוּהָ, מַאי ״מְחִיצָה״ – פְּלוּגְתָּא,

They assumed: what does "מחיצה" mean? A division,

דִּכְתִיב: ״וַתְּהִי מֶחֱצַת הָעֵדָה״;

as it is written: "And the division of the congregation was";
