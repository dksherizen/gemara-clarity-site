Topic

Forgery concerns with a שטר written on erased paper (מחק), the ברייתא on text and witnesses on erased versus clean paper, and the start of רבי's objection to רבי חנינא בן גמליאל

Overview

The גמרא defends רב's ruling that a שטר whose text and witness signatures are both on erased paper is כשר. A forger can't simply erase the text again, because paper erased twice looks different from paper erased once. To block a forger who pre-erases the witnesses' spot, אביי explains that רב requires the witnesses to see the paper erased before they sign. A ברייתא then says that text on clean paper with signatures on an erasure is כשר. The גמרא explains that this is only when the witnesses write a note saying so, placed between their two signatures. When asked why the reverse case (text on an erasure, signatures on clean paper) is פסול, it answers that a double erasure stands out only next to a single erasure, and here there is nothing on the page to compare it to. Every proposed test fails: erasures on other sheets differ, fresh erasures differ from old ones, and רבי ירמיה worries that courts will make mistakes with complicated procedures. Finally the גמרא turns to רבי חנינא בן גמליאל's view on a tied document, and רבי begins an objection that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְאִם תֹּאמַר:

And if you will say:

מוֹחֵק וְחוֹזֵר וּמוֹחֵק!

He erases, and then goes back and erases again!

אֵינוֹ דּוֹמֶה נִמְחַק פַּעַם אַחַת,

Something erased one time is not similar

לְנִמְחַק שְׁתֵּי פְעָמִים.

to something erased two times.
