Topic

Learning חזקה from שור המועד, its limits, and the search for the חכמים' source for three years

Overview

The גמרא is exploring the idea that the three years of חזקה are learned from שור המועד: three gorings make an ox 'established', and three years of use make someone the established owner of land. It explains why the ox's fourth goring does not spoil the comparison. It then defends the comparison against two challenges: why a חזקה needs a claim, and why a מחאה counts even when the holder is not present. Next it tests the comparison on timing. Following רבי מאיר, three gorings in one day are enough, so the גמרא asks about fig fruits, caper fruits, and alfalfa cut quickly. The answer is that each harvest must be a truly separate, normal crop. When alfalfa is properly harvested three times in three months, the גמרא admits this would be a חזקה, because the ox derivation follows רבי ישמעאל, who counts harvests rather than years. It then asks what the source is for the חכמים, who require three full years. רב יוסף brings a פסוק from ירמיהו about writing deeds, but אביי rejects it as mere good advice.
