Topic

שיור in sales and gifts, and the rules of a שכיב מרע's gifts: dividing, reconsidering, partial retraction, holy gifts, and the wording of a gift.

Overview

The גמרא first asks whether keeping back an upper story (דיוטא) counts as a שיור. רבא quoting רב נחמן rules that selling a house 'except for the upper story' is a שיור, because the seller also keeps the right to stick out beams (זיזין). In the same way, giving a palm tree 'except for its fruit' keeps the space of the fruit. Next, רב נחמן teaches about a שכיב מרע who gives away all his property. If he divided it in one plan, he can take it all back if he recovers. If he reconsidered between gifts, he can take back only the last one. He also cannot take back a gift of all his known property, since he may own land elsewhere. The גמרא then rules that taking back part of a deathbed gift cancels the whole gift, proving this from a ברייתא. Questions about a dying man's הקדש, הפקר and צדקה end in תיקו. Finally, רב ששת lists the words that make a gift, and a ברייתא adds inheritance words for a fitting heir, following רבי יוחנן בן ברוקא.

Opening lines

אִיבַּעְיָא לְהוּ:

A question was asked before them:

בַּיִת לְאֶחָד וּדְיוֹטָא לְאֶחָד,

A house to one person and the upper story to another,

מַהוּ?

what is the law?

מִי הָוֵי שִׁיּוּר, אוֹ לָא?

Is it considered a keeping-back, or not?
