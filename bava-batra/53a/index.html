Topic

Acquiring land through חזקה: the seller's permission ״לך חזק וקני״, whether a gift is different, and which small acts on a fence, gap or stone count as חזקה

Overview

This amud opens by finishing a ruling: if the buyer does the act of חזקה when the seller is not there, the seller must have told him ״לך חזק וקני״. רב asks whether a gift works the same way. שמואל says the answer is obvious by a קל וחומר: if a paying buyer needs permission, someone getting a gift surely does. The גמרא explains that רב was unsure because a giver gives with an עין יפה. Next, the גמרא explains the משנה's כל שהוא using שמואל's two cases: finishing a fence to ten טפחים, or widening a gap until a person can walk through. For each case it shows the small but real change involved: a fence that goes from easy to hard to climb, and a gap that goes from a squeeze to easy passage. רב אסי then quotes רבי יוחנן that placing or removing a stone that helps is a חזקה. The גמרא explains this means the stone brought water to the field, not just protected it from flooding, since saving a field is like chasing a lion off a friend's land. The amud ends with another ruling of רבי יוחנן about two fields with one boundary between them.

Opening lines

שֶׁלֹּא בְּפָנָיו –

Not in his presence –

צָרִיךְ לְמֵימַר לֵיהּ: ״לֵךְ חֲזֵק וּקְנִי״.

he must say to him: "Go, take possession and acquire."

בָּעֵי רַב:

Rav asked:

מַתָּנָה – הֵיאַךְ?

A gift – how does it work?
