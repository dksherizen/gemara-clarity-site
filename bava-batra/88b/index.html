Topic

The seller's extra weight (הכרעה and גירומין), and רבי לוי's teachings on false measures, theft and blessings

Overview

The משנה teaches that a seller must tilt the scale a טפח in the buyer's favor. If he weighed exactly, he must add extra amounts (גירומין). Local custom also decides how goods are measured. The גמרא first brings ריש לקיש's source, ״וצדק״, for the tilting. It then concludes that tilting is only local custom, and that ריש לקיש's פסוק really teaches the גירומין. רב gives the liquid rate as a tenth of a ליטרא per ten ליטרין, and the dry-goods rate stays a תיקו. Then come teachings of רבי לוי. Cheating with measures is harsher than עריות because it cannot be fixed through תשובה. Robbing a person is worse than robbing הקדש. ה׳ blessed ישראל with twenty-two letters and cursed with only eight, while משה רבינו did the opposite.
