Topic

Acquiring a שטר and coins אגב קרקע, and what is included when selling a ship, a wagon or a yoke

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a challenge to רב אשי, who holds that a שטר cannot be handed over through words alone. Two משניות show that a שטר is acquired automatically once the buyer takes possession of land. The גמרא answers that acquiring along with land (אגב) is a special, powerful method. Even coins, which cannot be acquired through חליפין, can be acquired אגב קרקע, as a story about רב פפא sending money through רב שמואל בר אחא shows. The גמרא then explains that the אנתיקי left out of a ship sale is the merchandise on board. A new משנה teaches that a wagon and its mules, or a yoke and its oxen, are sold separately. It also brings a dispute: רבי יהודה says the price can show what was meant, and חכמים say it cannot. The גמרא settles a ברייתא that seems to contradict the משנה by setting it in a case where the mules are harnessed to the wagon. It then explains that the price dispute applies where the word צמדא can mean either the yoke or the oxen. The amud ends with a challenge to חכמים: if the price is not proof, the sale should be cancelled. That challenge continues on the next amud.

כְּאוֹתָהּ שֶׁשָּׁנִינוּ:
