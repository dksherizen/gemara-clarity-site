Topic

What divides one domain or field from another (for שבת and for a dead גר's land), the borders of ארץ ישראל for מעשר, and עדים זוממין who testify to a חזקה.

Overview

The amud opens with the end of רבי יוסי's ruling: two halves of a dried fig carried into one domain on שבת make a person חייב, but halves carried into two domains leave him פטור. רבה, אביי and רבא then argue about what splits one domain into two. רבה requires a רשות היחיד, אביי accepts even a כרמלית, and רבא accepts even a beam. רבא's view follows his rule that a domain for שבת is defined like a domain for גיטין. The גמרא then returns to acquiring a dead גר's field. Where there is no boundary, one act of acquisition covers the whole area called by the owner's name, such as 'so-and-so's well area.' A row of חצובא also divides the land, since יהושע used it to mark the borders of ארץ ישראל. This leads to teachings about borders: יהושע listed only the border towns, all land shown to משה is obligated in מעשר, and the lands of קיני, קניזי and קדמוני are excluded, with three תנאים identifying them differently. Finally a new משנה teaches that עדים זוממין who testified to a three-year חזקה pay the full value of the field. It then begins a case of three separate pairs of witnesses, one pair for each year, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בִּרְשׁוּת אַחַת – חַיָּיב,

In one domain – he is liable,

בִּשְׁתֵּי רְשׁוּיוֹת – פָּטוּר.

in two domains – he is exempt.

וְאָמַר רַבָּה:

And Rabba said:

וְהוּא שֶׁיֵּשׁ חִיּוּב חַטָּאת בֵּינֵיהֶם,

And this is only where there is a sin-offering liability between them,
