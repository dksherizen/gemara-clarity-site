Topic

Erased and torn שטרות, אביי's invalid backup deed, and rewriting lost documents

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה about a creditor whose loan document (שטר) was erased, and the court writes a confirming document (קיום) for him. A ברייתא gives the exact wording of that קיום. It adds that the creditor can collect without more proof only if the judges wrote that they checked the witnesses and found them consistent. The ברייתא also rules that a שטר that is נקרע is invalid, one that is נתקרע is valid, and an erased one is valid if its writing can still be made out. רב יהודה explains that נקרע means a tear made by a בית דין. He and אביי then give two different signs of such a tear. Next comes a story: landowners in פומבדיתא, afraid of land-grabbers, asked אביי for a second deed. He refused at first, citing רב ספרא's rule against two deeds for one field. Then he had an invalid deed written, and he defended this against רב אחא בר מניומי's challenge. Finally, a ברייתא rules that a lost loan document is not rewritten, but a lost sale deed is rewritten without its אחריות guarantee.
