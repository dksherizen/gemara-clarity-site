Bava Basra 116b

בבא בתרא קט״ז ע״ב

Topic

Who inherits first: the grandfather (אבי האב) or the brother of the deceased? Then the opening of the משנה about the three portions taken by בנות צלפחד.

Overview

The amud opens with a short answer explaining why a sharp sage missed a point in the משנה: his quick mind made him skip careful study. Then רמי בר חמא asks a question. If עשו dies, who inherits first: his grandfather אברהם or his brother יעקב? רבא tries to answer from the משנה, 'the father comes before all his descendants.' The גמרא explains that רמי בר חמא read that line as covering only a father's own children, not his grandchildren. It then proves from the משנה's rule, 'whoever comes first in inheritance, his descendants come first,' that יעקב takes the place of יצחק. So the brother inherits before the grandfather. Finally, a new משנה begins: בנות צלפחד received three portions of land. These were their father's own portion as one who left מצרים, his share in his father חפר's estate, and the extra share he got as a בכור.

Opening lines

אַגַּב חוּרְפֵּיהּ

Because of his sharpness

לָא עַיֵּין בָּהּ.

he did not look into it carefully.

בָּעֵי רָמֵי בַּר חָמָא:

Rami bar Ḥama asked:

אֲבִי הָאָב וְאָחִיו –

The father's father and his brother –
