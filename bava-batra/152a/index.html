Bava Basra 152a

בבא בתרא קנ״ב ע״א

Topic

A deathbed gift (מתנת שכיב מרע) that also records a קנין: the dispute between רב and שמואל, the contradictions in each one's rulings and how they are resolved, and רבא's challenge to שמואל

Overview

The גמרא brings a dispute about a שכיב מרע (a person on his deathbed) whose gift document also records a קנין (a formal act of acquisition). רב's study hall says this gift is doubly strong. Like a healthy person's gift, it cannot be taken back if he recovers. Like a deathbed gift, it can even transfer a loan by words alone. שמואל is unsure, because maybe the giver meant the gift to work only through the שטר, and a שטר cannot take effect after death. The גמרא then sets each אמורא against himself. In the 'write and give a מנה' case, רב worries about the document problem and שמואל does not. The answer for רב is that his two rulings differ on whether a קנין was done. The answer for שמואל is that in the 'write and give' case the document only adds strength for the recipient. Finally רבא challenges שמואל with another of his rulings: a dying man who gave away everything, even with a קנין, can take it back if he recovers. The challenge continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אִיתְּמַר: מַתְּנַת שְׁכִיב מְרַע

It was stated: a gift of a person on his deathbed

שֶׁכָּתוּב בָּהּ קִנְיָן –

in which it is written that a קנין was done

בֵּי רַב מִשְּׁמֵיהּ דְּרַב אָמְרִי:

The study hall of רב say in the name of רב:

אַרְכְּבֵיהּ אַתְּרֵי רִיכְשֵׁי.

He mounted him on two horses.
