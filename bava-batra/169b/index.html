Bava Basra 169b

בבא בתרא קס״ט ע״ב

Topic

Receipts, guarantees (אחריות) in land deeds, and whether rights in a document pass by handing it over (אותיות נקנות במסירה)

Overview

The amud opens by defending the general rule that a court writes a שובר (receipt). In the ברייתא's case of stolen land that was sold, a שובר would not protect the later לקוחות (buyers). Either the wrongful collector eats the fruit in the meantime, or a buyer without אחריות never goes back to the seller to find out. Loans are different, because buyers suspect a money debt was already paid and check with the borrower. Next the גמרא asks how a deed is rewritten without its guarantee. רב נחמן gives the wording. From that wording רפרם learns that a missing guarantee is a scribe's mistake, and רב אשי disagrees. A story follows in which רב נחמן makes an agent who bought land without a guarantee cover it himself. Finally the גמרא explains the dispute over a returned gift deed, and then a dispute between רבי and רבן שמעון בן גמליאל about judging by שטר or by חזקה, using the idea of אותיות נקנות במסירה. רב דימי's explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֲמַר לְהוּ: בְּעָלְמָא כּוֹתְבִין שׁוֹבָר,

He said to them: generally, we write a receipt,

וְהָכָא הַיְינוּ טַעְמָא –

and here, this is the reason:

דְּדִלְמָא אָזֵיל בַּעַל חוֹב

that perhaps the creditor will go

וְטָרֵיף מִינֵּיהּ דְּלוֹקֵחַ,

and seize from the purchaser,
