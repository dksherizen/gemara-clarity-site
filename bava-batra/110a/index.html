Topic

פנחס's descent from יוסף and יתרו, checking a bride's brothers, the story of יהונתן the priest of מיכה's idol and his later תשובה, and the source that sons inherit their father before daughters

Overview

The גמרא finishes explaining how פנחס came from both יוסף and יתרו. One of his mother's parents came from each family, and the plural wording 'daughters of פוטיאל' supports this. רבא then learns from the extra words 'sister of נחשון' that a man should check a woman's brothers before marrying her, because most sons take after their mother's brothers. Next the גמרא tells the story of יהונתן, a descendant of משה who served as a priest for מיכה's idol. The men of דן rebuke him with words that echo פסוקים about משה. He answers with a family saying that it is better to work even for עבודה זרה than to depend on others. The גמרא says he misread that saying: it really meant humble work that feels beneath you, as רב told רב כהנא. Later דוד put יהונתן over the treasuries, and the פסוק calls him שבואל because he returned to God with all his heart. Finally the גמרא returns to the משנה and asks for the source that sons inherit their father ahead of daughters. רב פפא challenges the proof, and אביי begins a reply that continues on the next amud.

אֶלָּא אִי אֲבוּהּ דְּאִמֵּיהּ מִיּוֹסֵף –
