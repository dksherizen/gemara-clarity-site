Topic

The meaning of "זֶה הַדָּבָר": the one-generation limit, the גזרה שוה between נדרים and שחוטי חוץ, and how בית שמאי read it

Overview

Earlier, the גמרא learned from the words "זֶה הַדָּבָר" that the rule limiting whom בנות צלפחד could marry applied only to their generation. רבה זוטי asks רב אשי why the same words at the start of the laws of נדרים do not limit vows to one generation as well. רב אשי answers that in נדרים the words are needed for a גזרה שוה with שחוטי חוץ. In the inheritance parsha, the words would be extra unless they teach a one-generation limit. A ברייתא spells out the גזרה שוה in both directions. From the first direction, רב אחא בר יעקב learns that three ordinary people (הדיוטות) can release a vow, and "ראשי המטות" adds that one expert alone can also do it. From the second direction, רב ששת learns that a חכם can undo a הקדש. בית שמאי hold that a הקדש cannot be undone, so they use each "זה הדבר" for its own lesson. The גמרא then asks where they learn the rule of three ordinary people, and begins a ברייתא of רבי יוסי הגלילי on the festivals פסוק that continues on the next amud.
