Bava Basra 174b

בבא בתרא קע״ד ע״ב

Topic

Minor orphans and their father's debts, guarantors (ערב and קבלן) for loans and for a כתובה, and a dying man's claim of debt after giving his property to הקדש.

Overview

Earlier on the daf, רב פפא and רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע both said that minor orphans do not pay their father's debt right away, but for different reasons. The גמרא finds the practical difference: when we know the father never paid, because he admitted the debt or died under a שמתא. The Sages of ארץ ישראל rule like רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע. A ברייתא about a guarantor who collects from minor orphans challenges רב פפא, and the גמרא answers that the words 'התקבלתי' make it a special case. Next come a story about a guarantor for a non-Jewish lender and a debate over the worry about 'bundles' (צררי). Then the גמרא turns to the משנה's rule about a guarantor for a כתובה. It tells how אביי suggested a divorce plan to help the poor scholar רב הונא, a plan that failed because he was a כהן. From that story the גמרא sets out which guarantors are bound and rules that an ordinary guarantor of a כתובה is never bound. Finally, רב הונא rules that a dying man who gave everything to הקדש is believed about a debt, and רב נחמן begins to challenge him. That discussion continues on the next amud.
