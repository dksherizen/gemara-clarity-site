Topic

Overpaying for a yoke, and which of a donkey's equipment is sold with the donkey

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier case: a buyer paid a very high price for a yoke, and the רבנן said the price does not prove the oxen were included. The גמרא asks why the sale is not then cancelled for overcharging. It gives two answers: the sale really is cancelled, or the overpayment was so obvious that it counts as a gift. Then a new משנה asks whether a donkey's equipment is sold with the donkey. The תנא קמא says no, נחום המדי says yes, and רבי יהודה says it depends on how the buyer asked. עולא explains that the argument is only about load gear (sack, saddlebag, כומני), while the saddle and similar gear are sold according to everyone. The גמרא defends עולא from a ברייתא and explains that a כומני is a women's riding seat. Finally, it asks whether the argument is about gear on the donkey or gear off it. It tries proofs from the ברייתא and from רבי יהודה, and the rejection of the second proof continues on the next amud.
