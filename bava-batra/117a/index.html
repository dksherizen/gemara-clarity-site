Topic

How ארץ ישראל was divided: was it given to יוצאי מצרים (those who left Egypt) or to באי הארץ (those who actually entered the land)?

Overview

The גמרא opens by noting that our משנה follows the view that ארץ ישראל was divided among יוצאי מצרים, the people who left Egypt. It then brings a ברייתא that records a debate on this question. רבי יאשיה says the land went to those who left Egypt. He reads the other פסוק, 'לָאֵלֶּה', as 'כָּאֵלֶּה', meaning only adults like those counted, so young children are left out. רבי יונתן says the land went to באי הארץ, the people who entered it. He explains the 'fathers' פסוק as describing an unusual inheritance in which 'the dead inherit from the living.' רבי gives a parable about two brothers who are כהנים: they collect shares by the number of their sons, then bring everything back to their father and split it equally. The ברייתא then introduces a third opinion, from רבי שמעון בן אלעזר, and his words continue on the next amud.
