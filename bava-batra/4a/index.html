Topic

The story of הורדוס rebuilding the בית המקדש, then the משנה's rules on shared walls, partitions in gardens and fields, and the חזית mark

Overview

This amud finishes the story of הורדוס and בבא בן בוטא. הורדוס comes in disguise and tries to get the blind חכם to curse the king, but he refuses every time, backing each refusal with a פסוק. הורדוס reveals himself and asks how to make up for killing the חכמים. בבא בן בוטא tells him to rebuild the בית המקדש and gives him a plan to get past רומי. The גמרא praises the beautiful marble building and asks how בבא בן בוטא could advise a wicked king, when דניאל was punished for advising נבוכדנצר. It answers in two ways and proves that דניאל was punished. Then the גמרא returns to the משנה about a shared wall: local custom decides the material, a fallen wall belongs to both neighbors, and רבא explains when a garden or a field needs a partition. Finally the גמרא discusses the חזית, the mark on a wall built by one neighbor, and begins רבי יוחנן's explanation, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אַהְדַּר לֵיהּ כְּלִילָא דְּיָילֵי,

He put around him a crown of porcupine hide,

נַקְּרִינְהוּ לְעֵינֵיהּ.

and it pierced his eyes.

יוֹמָא חַד אֲתָא וִיתִיב קַמֵּיהּ,

One day he came and sat before him,

אֲמַר: חֲזִי מַר,

he said: See, master,

The steps (53)

  1. Statementהורדוס blinds בבא בן בוטא
  2. Statementהורדוס, in disguise, urges him to curse the king
  3. RejectionRefusal: do not curse a king even in thought
  4. Challengeהורדוס objects: he is not a real king
  5. ResolutionEven a rich man or a leader may not be cursed
  6. Challengeהורדוס objects: he does not act like your people
  7. ResolutionI am afraid of him
  8. Challengeהורדוס objects: no one will tell him
  9. ResolutionA bird of the sky will carry the sound
  10. Questionהורדוס reveals himself and asks how to repent
  11. ResponseYou put out the light of the world, so work on the light of the world
  12. ResponseAnother version: you blinded the eye of the world
  13. Challengeהורדוס objects: I am afraid of the government of רומי
  14. ResolutionSend a messenger and build in the meantime
  15. Conclusionרומי's angry reply to הורדוס
  16. QuestionWhat does the word רכא mean?
  17. Responseרכא means royalty, proven from a פסוק about דוד
  18. ResponseA second source: what they called out before יוסף
  19. StatementNo building was more beautiful than הורדוס's
  20. QuestionWhat did he build it with?
  21. ResponseBuilt from marble stones
  22. StatementHow the stones were laid
  23. StatementThe חכמים tell him not to cover it with gold
  24. ChallengeHow could בבא בן בוטא give advice to הורדוס, when דניאל was punished for advising נבוכדנצר?
  25. ResolutionA slave is obligated in מצוות
  26. ResolutionThe בית המקדש is different
  27. QuestionHow do we know דניאל was punished?
  28. ProofA suggested source: התך is דניאל
  29. ChallengeThe proof only works by one explanation of the name
  30. ResolutionHis punishment was the lions' den
  31. QuestionWhat does the word "everything" add?
  32. ResponseIt adds a place that uses palm and laurel branches
  33. ChallengeIsn't it obvious that a fallen wall belongs to both?

  35. ChallengeThe garden ruling seems to contradict itself

  39. Resolutionרבא: an ordinary garden counts as a fencing place, an ordinary field does not
  40. QuestionWhat is the חזית mentioned in the משנה?
  41. Responseרב הונא: bend the wall's edge outward
  42. ChallengeWhy not bend it inward?

  44. ChallengeThe neighbor could cut off an outer mark
  45. ResolutionA cut can be noticed
  46. ResponseSecond version: רב הונא says bend the edge inward
  47. ChallengeWhy not bend it outward?
  48. ResolutionAn outer mark could be cut off
  49. ChallengeThe neighbor could add a matching mark
  50. ResolutionAn addition can be noticed
  51. ChallengeThe משנה says 'on the outside'
  52. ConclusionThe difficulty remains
  53. StatementIntroducing רבי יוחנן's view
