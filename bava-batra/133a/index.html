Topic

A wife's כתובה after her husband divides his property; רב הונא's rule that a שכיב מרע's transfer to a fit heir counts as ירושה, and why that matters

Overview

First, רב כהנא argues that a wife can collect her כתובה from a deceased daughter's share, since she could collect from any property her husband acquired later. Next, a man split his property between his wife and son but left out one palm tree. רבינא first thinks the wife can collect her כתובה only from that tree. רב יימר replies that if she can collect from the tree at all, she can collect from everything. Then רב הונא rules that when a שכיב מרע writes all his property to someone, a possible heir takes it as ירושה and anyone else takes it as מתנה. רב נחמן objects that this is just רבי יוחנן בן ברוקה's view, and the two agree that רב הונא meant a dying man who only answered 'To whom else?'. The גמרא asks what difference the label makes, and רבא rejects רב אדא בר אהבה's suggestion about the widow's food support. רבא's answer is that if a fit heir is told 'to you, and after you to so-and-so,' the second person gets nothing, because a ירושה can never be cut off.
