Topic

A טומטום found to be male and laws tied to birth; doubtful firstborns and the הרשאה between them

Overview

This amud continues the discussion of a טומטום who is later found to be a זכר. אמימר says he does not reduce the בכור's extra share. רב שיזבי says he is not circumcised on the eighth day. רב שרביא says his mother is not טמאה לידה, because the פסוק requires a known זכר from birth. A משנה says a woman who miscarries a טומטום keeps the rules of both a male and a female birth. This conclusively refutes רב שרביא. The גמרא tries to save רב שיזבי by saying the משנה was ruling strictly out of doubt, but that answer ends with an unanswered difficulty. רבא then brings a ברייתא supporting רבי אמי: a טומטום and a doubtful בכור get no double share. This leads to רבא publicly taking back his ruling about two sons born in hiding, in favor of רבי ינאי's rule. Finally, the people of אקרא דאגמא ask שמואל what happens when a father names a different son as בכור than the one everyone presumed, and his answer begins here and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אַמֵּימָר אָמַר:

Ameimar said:

אַף אֵינוֹ מְמַעֵט חֵלֶק בְּכוֹרָה,

He also does not reduce the firstborn's portion,

שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: ״וְיָלְדָה לוֹ בָּנִים״

as it is said: "And they bore him sons"

– עַד שֶׁיְּהֵא בֵּן בִּשְׁעַת לֵידָה.

until he is a son at the time of birth.
