Bava Basra 36b

Topic

Does plowing count toward a חזקה on land, how do רב and שמואל measure a חזקה, and how does a חזקה work in an orchard?

Overview

The גמרא first shows that רבי ישמעאל and רבי עקיבא do not argue about plowing (ניר). Both agree plowing is not a חזקה. They argue about whether you need enough time for big crops or small crops. A ברייתא then shows that some Sages do count plowing, and רב חסדא identifies that view as רבי אחא. רב אשי reports that the great Sages of his generation agreed. רב נחמן explains both sides: an owner would not stay silent while others plow his land, or he is simply happy to get free plowing. רב נחמן בר רב חסדא answers the people of פום נהרא that plowing counts. רב נחמן בר יצחק objects that counting names proves nothing, since רב, שמואל, רבי ישמעאל and רבי עקיבא all say plowing does not count. The גמרא then shows where רב and שמואל say this, and אביי explains how they differ, using a young date palm that gives three crops a year. Finally, the גמרא turns to רבי ישמעאל's line about a grain field, and אביי learns from it that eating from ten different closely planted trees each year for three years is a חזקה even for the חכמים.

Opening lines

אֲפִילּוּ יוֹם אֶחָד נָמֵי!

Even one day should also be enough!

אֶלָּא דְּכוּלֵּי עָלְמָא –

Rather, according to everyone,

נִיר לָא הָוֵי חֲזָקָה;

plowing is not a חזקה;

וְהָכָא – פֵּירָא רַבָּא

and here, big produce
