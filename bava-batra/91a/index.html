Topic

Food prices and profit in ארץ ישראל, leaving the Land, and stories about רות, בעז and אברהם

Overview

This amud opens with the last words of a ruling from the previous page about taking food out of ארץ ישראל. A ברייתא forbids buying and reselling basic foods in ארץ ישראל for profit, and the גמרא explains why רבי אלעזר בן עזריה could still profit from wine and oil. Another ברייתא forbids profiting 'twice' from eggs, and רב and שמואל disagree about what that means. The גמרא then teaches that a community cries out in prayer, even on שבת, when the price of its main product crashes badly. Next, a ברייתא rules when one may leave ארץ ישראל in a famine, and רבי שמעון limits this to someone who cannot afford food. The punishment of אלימלך and his sons, who left ארץ ישראל, supports this, and the גמרא brings a series of stories about רות, בעז (identified with the judge אבצן) and his sixty children. Finally, רב חנן בר רבא brings a list of teachings in the name of רב: the family of נחשון, the names of the mothers of famous figures, אברהם's ten years in prison, and the world's mourning when אברהם died, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מֵהִיפַּרְכְיָא לְהִיפַּרְכְיָא.

From province to province.

תָּנוּ רַבָּנַן:

The Rabbis taught:

אֵין מִשְׂתַּכְּרִים בְּאֶרֶץ יִשְׂרָאֵל

One may not make a profit in ארץ ישראל

בִּדְבָרִים שֶׁיֵּשׁ בָּהֶן חַיֵּי נֶפֶשׁ,

with things that people need to live,
