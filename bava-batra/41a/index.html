Topic

A חזקה only counts with a טענה, and the cases of walls rebuilt by mistake on a neighbor's land.

Overview

The amud first finishes an earlier topic. If a gift document does not say whether the giver wanted it public, do we worry that it was a concealed gift? רבינא says no, רב אשי says yes, and the הלכה follows רב אשי. Then a new משנה teaches that using land (חזקה) proves nothing unless the user also gives a reason (טענה) for how it became his, such as a sale or a gift. An heir does not need such a reason. The גמרא explains that the משנה is needed because we might have thought the court should suggest a 'lost document' claim for the holder (פתח פיך לאלם), and it teaches that the court does not. Next comes a story: רב ענן rebuilt his flood-damaged wall on his neighbor's land, and רב נחמן told him to move it back. רב נחמן rejected both of רב ענן's arguments. There is no instant חזקה, and the neighbor's help building the wall was a מחילה בטעות, a giving-up based on a mistake. A similar story about רב כהנא begins at the end and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

סְתָמָא מַאי?

An unspecified one, what is its law?

רָבִינָא אָמַר: לָא חָיְישִׁינַן,

Ravina said: We are not concerned,

רַב אָשֵׁי אָמַר: חָיְישִׁינַן.

Rav Ashi said: We are concerned.

וְהִלְכְתָא: חָיְישִׁינַן:

And the law is: We are concerned.
