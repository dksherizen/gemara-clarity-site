Topic

Righteous people who received special gifts, the end of פרק השותפין, and the opening משנה on distances from a neighbor's wall

Overview

The amud opens with a series of lists of very special righteous people. אברהם, יצחק and יעקב got a taste of עולם הבא in this world, and the יצר הרע had no power over them. Some say דוד also belongs on that second list, but the other opinion reads his פסוק as describing his suffering. A ברייתא lists six people who died by a divine kiss and not through the מלאך המות. The גמרא asks how מרים can be included, and רבי אלעזר answers with a גזירה שוה. A second ברייתא lists seven people whose bodies worms never touched, with an argument again about whether דוד is included. A third ברייתא lists four people who died only because of the נחש, with no sin of their own, and the גמרא proves ישי's place on that list from the words 'אביגיל בת נחש'. Then פרק השותפין ends. The new פרק opens with a משנה about how far a person must keep pits, materials, planting, a mill and an oven from a neighbor's wall.

Opening lines

מֵעֵין הָעוֹלָם הַבָּא, אֵלּוּ הֵן:

a taste of the World-to-Come; they are:

אַבְרָהָם, יִצְחָק וְיַעֲקֹב.

Abraham, Isaac and Jacob.

אַבְרָהָם – דִּכְתִיב בֵּיהּ: ״בַּכֹּל״.

Abraham, as it is written about him: 'with everything.'

יִצְחָק – דִּכְתִיב בֵּיהּ: ״מִכֹּל״.

Isaac, as it is written about him: 'from everything.'
