Topic

הקב״ה's precision and איוב's words and friends, איוב's daughters, the meaning of אברהם's blessing 'בכל', עשו's sins and ישמעאל's תשובה

Overview

This amud finishes the גמרא's reading of הקב״ה's answer to איוב: He times the eagle that catches the mountain goat's kid and the snake that helps the hind give birth to the exact moment, so He would never confuse איוב with אויב. רבא settles two פסוקים that seem to disagree about איוב's words: a person is not held responsible for what he says in pain. The גמרא then describes איוב's three friends, who lived far apart yet came in through one gate together, and רבא praises friendship like theirs. רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש argue whether the birth of daughters brought growth or fighting into the world, and איוב's daughters were doubled in beauty, as their names show. A story about רבי, his son and בר קפרא leads to a dispute among תנאים over what 'בכל' means in אברהם's blessing. Among the answers, עשו did not rebel and ישמעאל did תשובה in אברהם's lifetime. Along the way the גמרא explains why mourners eat lentils, lists עשו's five sins on the day אברהם died, and proves ישמעאל's תשובה from the order of names at אברהם's burial. The amud ends by opening a ברייתא about three people who were given a taste of עולם הבא in this world.
