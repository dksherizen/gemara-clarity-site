Topic

Sources for inheritance rules: why a son comes before a daughter, why brothers inherit only through the father's side, and the start of the source for a son inheriting his mother.

Overview

This amud looks for the source that a son comes before a daughter in inheritance. אביי says it comes from the words 'וּבֵן אֵין לוֹ' (and he has no son). He knocks down רב פפא's idea that a son and daughter would split the estate equally, since then the פסוק would be teaching something obvious. Two other sources are tried and rejected: רב אחא בר יעקב's proof from the words of בנות צלפחד, and רבינא's proof from 'הַקָּרֹב אֵלָיו'. Each time the גמרא goes back to the first source. Another source is offered from 'לִבְנֵיכֶם', and it survives a challenge because a בְּרָכָה is read differently from a law. The גמרא then asks how we know that brothers from the same father inherit from each other. It concludes that the word משפחה already teaches that inheritance goes through the father's side, and רבה's comparison to the sons of יעקב is really about יבום. Finally, the גמרא asks for the source that a man inherits his mother and begins a ברייתא that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַאן כּוּ׳ לֵירוֹת?

Who, etc., should inherit?

אַטּוּ בַּר קַשָּׁא דְּמָתָא לֵירוֹת?!

Is that to say the ruler of the city should inherit?!

הָכִי קָא אָמֵינָא:

This is what I meant to say:

אִיכָּא בֵּן וּבַת –

If there is a son and a daughter,
