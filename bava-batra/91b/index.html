Topic

Leaders, leaving ארץ ישראל in famine, the names of אלימלך's sons and רות's legacy, and the blessing of old produce

Overview

This amud opens at the end of a lament over losing a great leader, and then teaches that even the smallest official is appointed by Heaven. רבי יהושע בן קרחה says אלימלך and his sons were not punished for leaving ארץ ישראל. Their sin was failing to pray for their generation. רבי יוחנן rules that if money itself is scarce, one may leave ארץ ישראל even when food prices are fairly low. He backs this with a memory of starvation in טבריה, then adds more memories of hunger, plenty and holiness. The גמרא then discusses the two sets of names for אלימלך's sons, מחלון and כליון or יואש and שרף, where רב and שמואל disagree. Two ברייתות on פסוקים in דברי הימים support one view and teach that רות lived to see the kingdom of שלמה. The פרק ends with the blessing of plenty: old grain that lasts without worms or blight and improves with age, and רב פפא's rule that old things are better except dates, beer and small fish.

Opening lines

מַנְהִיגוֹ,

its leader,

וְאוֹי לָהּ לַסְּפִינָה שֶׁאָבַד קַבַּרְנִיטָהּ.

and woe to the ship that has lost its captain.

״וְהַמִּתְנַשֵּׂא לְכֹל לְרֹאשׁ״ –

"And You are exalted as head above all":
