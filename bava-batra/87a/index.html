Topic

Wage arrangements and ריבית, acquiring flax, and when a sale of wine or oil becomes final

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a ברייתא about hiring a worker cheaply for harvest time. The גמרא uses it to challenge רב and שמואל, but רבא answers that lowering wages is not real ריבית. The only problem is how it looks, and that depends on whether the worker starts working right away. Next, רב ששת explains how cutting a little flax can acquire all of it: the seller told the buyer to clear some land and acquire everything on it. A new משנה then teaches that a sale of wine or oil becomes final when the measuring vessel is filled. It also says a middleman bears the loss of a broken barrel, and the seller must drip three extra drops for the buyer. The גמרא asks whose measuring vessel this is, and רבי אילעא answers that it belongs to a סרסור. Finally, רבי אלעזר seeks out רב's student to raise a difficulty about the drained drops, and his objection continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְלַגּוֹרֶן יָפֶה סֶלַע –

and at the threshing floor [a day's work] is worth a sela —

אָסוּר לֵהָנוֹת הֵימֶנּוּ.

it is forbidden to benefit from him.

אֲבָל אִם שְׂכָרוֹ מֵהַיּוֹם

But if he hired him from today

בְּדִינָר לְיוֹם,

for a dinar a day,
