Topic

Brothers' rights after dividing an estate, the orphans' document and receipt, and the משנה on sharing the cost of a gatehouse and a city wall

Overview

The amud opens with the sons of רב חסדא explaining that רב חמא followed the approach of נהרדעא. They show this from שמואל, who rules that brothers who divide an estate keep no path, window, ladder or water-channel rights over each other. רבא disagrees. רב חמא also rules that when a receipt is produced against an orphans' loan document, the court neither collects with it nor tears it up. רבינא follows רב חמא except in the receipt case, while מר זוטרא follows him there too. A new משנה then rules that neighbors can be forced to share the cost of a courtyard gatehouse and a city wall, and defines when a newcomer counts as a resident. The גמרא reconciles the gatehouse with a story about אליהו, giving four ways to tell a good gatehouse from one that blocks the poor. Next it explains the dispute with רבן שמעון בן גמליאל and brings two versions of how רבי יוחנן said the wall cost is divided. Finally ריש לקיש and רבי יוחנן argue from פסוקים that Torah scholars need no wall, and that explanation continues on the next amud.
