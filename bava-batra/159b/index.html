Bava Basra 159b

בבא בתרא קנ״ט ע״ב

Topic

Inheriting through a grandfather, whether a dead son inherits his mother 'in the grave,' and who must bring proof in two property disputes.

Overview

The גמרא first asks whether 'תחת אבתיך יהיו בניך' gives a grandson a real legal claim to inherit his grandfather directly. It tries to prove this from the משנה of a house that fell on a son and his father, but pushes the proof away by reading 'the father's heirs' as the son's brothers. Next, the חכמים ask רב ששת whether a son who died before his mother inherits from her 'in the grave' and passes it to his brothers from his father. רב ששת proves he does not, from a ברייתא about a captive grandfather and his daughter's son. רב אחא בר מניומי brings the same proof from the משנה of a house that fell on a son and his mother. אביי gives the source: the word 'סיבה' links a son to a husband, and a husband does not inherit his wife in the grave. The amud then tells of a sale of 'בר סיסין's property', where רב נחמן and רבא seem to contradict their own rulings in a case about חזקה on a house. The גמרא shows each one is consistent, and the פרק מי שמת ends.

Opening lines

אֲבָל לְעִנְיַן דִּינָא לָא?!

But with regard to the law, it means nothing?!

וְהָתַנְיָא: נָפַל הַבַּיִת עָלָיו וְעַל אָבִיו,

But isn't it taught: A house fell on him and on his father,

עָלָיו וְעַל מוֹרִישָׁיו;

or on him and on those he would inherit from;

וְהָיְתָה עָלָיו כְּתוּבַּת אִשָּׁה וּבַעַל חוֹב;

and he owed a wife's כתובה and a creditor;

The steps (24)

  3. ChallengeIf the heirs are the son's sons, the argument only works if grandsons inherit directly

  9. ProofIf the son inherited in the grave, his side would always win
  10. ProofA second proof from the house that fell on a son and his mother
