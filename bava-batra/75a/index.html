Topic

אגדות about the future: the לויתן, the glory of ירושלים, and the canopies of the צדיקים

Overview

This amud is a long string of אגדות about the future. It begins with the לויתן: גבריאל will hunt it only with הקדוש ברוך הוא's help, its breath boils the sea, and its thirst leaves furrows that take seventy years to settle. רבה quotes רבי יוחנן that the צדיקים will feast on its meat, and the leftover meat will be sold in ירושלים. Its skin will become סוכות and smaller gifts according to each person's merit, and the rest will shine from the walls of ירושלים. The גמרא then explains ישעיהו 54:12. The walls will be of both onyx and jasper, and the gates will be giant jewels, as רבי יוחנן taught. A student who mocked that teaching saw angels cutting those very jewels and was punished. A ברייתא about how tall people will be in the future challenges the size of the gates, and the גמרא answers that רבי יוחנן meant wind-windows. The amud ends with canopies: seven for each צדיק, the meaning of their smoke and fire, the canopies of אדם הראשון in גן עדן, and the start of a פסוק about חירם מלך צור that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

קְנִיגְיָא עִם לִוְיָתָן,

a hunt with the leviathan,

שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: ״הַתִּמְשֹׁךְ לִוְיָתָן בְּחַכָּה

as it is said: "Can you draw out leviathan with a hook

וּבְחֶבֶל תַּשְׁקִיעַ לְשֹׁנוֹ״,

or press down his tongue with a rope?"

וְאִלְמָלֵא הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא עוֹזְרוֹ –

And if the Holy One, Blessed be He, were not helping him,
