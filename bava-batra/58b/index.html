Topic

The end of רבי בנאה's corrections of the inscriptions, the measures at קפוטקיא, and a new משנה on חזקה for spouts, gutter pipes, ladders and windows, with three explanations of the מרזב rule.

Overview

The amud opens in the middle of the story of רבי בנאה, who corrects badly worded inscriptions written by the wise men of the nations. First he fixes the rule about judges: a judge is disqualified only if the court actually takes money from him, not just because he was sued. Next he fixes the line about blood and wine: blood is the head of all illness, not all death, and wine is the head of all healing, not all life. Each time, the writers add his wording in the name of 'the elders of the Jews.' The גמרא then notes three measure names written at the gate of קפוטקיא and explains that אנטל is the רביעית of the תורה. A new משנה follows. It covers when a person gains a חזקה (a lasting right) for a roof spout (מרזב), a gutter pipe (מזחילה), ladders, and windows, and רבי יהודה adds that a framed window counts even if it is small. The גמרא then gives three explanations of how a מרזב can have no חזקה while its place does: שמואל, רבי חנינא, and רב ירמיה בר אבא.
