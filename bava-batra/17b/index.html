Topic

The 'wall' of a pit in the משנה, and the argument between אביי and רבא about digging next to the boundary (מצר)

Overview

The גמרא asks why the משנה starts by talking about a neighbor's pit but then says to keep away from his 'wall.' אביי (some say רב יהודה) explains that it means the wall of the neighbor's pit, and the word teaches that a pit's wall is three טפחים thick. This matters when someone sells 'a pit and its walls.' Next, אביי and רבא argue about digging right at the boundary when the neighbor has no pit yet. One version says they argue only about a field not meant for pits. Another version says they argue only about a field meant for pits, and it brings support from the rules about planting trees near a pit. The גמרא then tests both versions against the משנה, and each side defends itself: רבא with 'from the wall of his pit,' and אביי by saying the משנה is about two neighbors digging at the same time. A ברייתא about soft, crumbly rock is brought against אביי but is pushed off as a special case. A new proof about olive waste and manure begins, and it continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

גְּמָ׳

GEMARA

פָּתַח בְּבוֹר – וּמְסַיֵּים בְּכוֹתֶל;

He opens with a pit, and ends with a wall;

לִיתְנֵי: אֶלָּא אִם כֵּן הִרְחִיק

let it teach: unless he distanced it

מִבּוֹרוֹ שֶׁל חֲבֵירוֹ שְׁלֹשָׁה טְפָחִים!

from the pit of his fellow three handbreadths!
