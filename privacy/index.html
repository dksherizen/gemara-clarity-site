Privacy

Last updated November 2026

Gemara Clarity has no ads, does not sell or share your information, and does not use tracking cookies. You can use all of it without an account.

Without an account

Your bookmarks, recent amudim, settings, saved words and Ameilus progress are kept only in your browser on this device. Amudim you open are also saved there so they work offline. To erase all of it, open Menu › Settings and choose Clear my bookmarks, history and saved data.

If you sign in (optional)

Signing in with Google keeps your learning in step across your devices. It uses Google Firebase. Your name, email address and profile photo from Google are shown in the app, and the following are stored in Firebase under your account: bookmarks, the last 30 amudim you opened and when, your settings, your saved words and your Ameilus progress. They are used only to sync your learning.

To delete them, open Menu › Account › Delete account. This removes your saved data and your sign-in from Gemara Clarity. You can also write to us and we will delete it for you.

If you use the separate Gemara Clarity course site and sign in there with your Gemara Clarity account, your account ID, name and email address are shared with that site so it can sign you in.

Services the site uses

  • Cloudflare hosts the site. It counts visits and measures page speed with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not track you across sites.
  • Sefaria supplies the Gemara text and the Daf Yomi calendar. Your browser fetches these from Sefaria directly, so Sefaria sees an ordinary web request, as with any website.
  • Google Firebase handles sign-in and synced data, only if you sign in.
