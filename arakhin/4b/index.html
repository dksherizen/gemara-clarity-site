Topic

Who is included in ערכין and דמים, the unspecified ערך, pledging limbs, and whether a vital part is judged as the whole (נידון בכבודו).

Overview

This amud works through a ברייתא on the פסוקים of ערכין in ויקרא 27. The word נפשות teaches that a woman can make a ערך vow and that a מנוול or a מוכה שחין can be valuated. The words והיה ערכך teach that a טומטום and an אנדרוגינוס can be pledged by דמים (market value), but they have no ערך, since only a definite male or female has one. The גמרא then explains ערך סתום: someone who pledges an unnamed ערך pays the smallest amount, three שקלים, because of תפשת מרובה לא תפשת. רב נחמן gives two opposite versions of whether this amount can be lowered for a poor person (הֶשֵּׂג יָד). Next the גמרא learns that a ערך covers a whole person, not a limb. A part that life depends on counts as the whole person, while the dead and the dying are left out. It then shows how one word can teach several lessons. Finally, רבא says a טומטום is נידון בכבודו, and אביי challenges him from the case of the head of a slave, donkey or cow. They settle it by splitting קדשי מזבח from קדשי בדק הבית, and a new challenge about the cow begins that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֵין לִי אֶלָּא אִישׁ שֶׁהֶעֱרִיךְ

I have only a man who valuated

whether a man or a woman;

אִשָּׁה שֶׁהֶעֱרִיכָה אִישׁ,

a woman who valuated a man,

אִשָּׁה שֶׁהֶעֱרִיכָה אִשָּׁה מִנַּיִן?

or a woman who valuated a woman, from where do we know?
