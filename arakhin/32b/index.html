Topic

The cities walled since יהושע, whether the first קדושה of ארץ ישראל lasted forever, and counting יובל in עזרא's time

Overview

The גמרא explains a ברייתא that names גמלא, גדוד, חדיד, אונו and ירושלים as cities walled since the days of יהושע בן נון. אביי reads the names as boundary points, and רבא reads the regions as labels that tell us which city of a shared name is meant. The גמרא asks how ירושלים can be on the list, since a house there never becomes חלוט. רבי יוחנן answers that the list means cities like ירושלים, and רב אשי answers that there were two places called ירושלים. Next, two ברייתות of רבי ישמעאל ברבי יוסי seem to disagree about whether the first קדושה of the land lasted forever. The גמרא answers that two תנאים reported his view differently, or that the 'forever' view really belongs to רבי אלעזר בר יוסי. It then brings the sources for each side: the פסוק about the סוכות in עזרא's time, and the פסוק 'which your fathers inherited.' Finally it asks how יובל could be counted in עזרא's time, and רב נחמן בר יצחק answers that they counted it only to keep the שמיטה years in the right place.

Opening lines

אָמַר אַבָּיֵי: הָכִי קָאָמַר —

up to Gedod in Transjordan,

וְחָדִיד וְאוֹנוֹ וִירוּשָׁלַיִם בִּיהוּדָה.

and Ḥadid and Ono and Jerusalem in Judea.
