Topic

Unwalled settlements, the special redemption rights of לוים and their cities, and whether לוים can own walled-city houses

Overview

The משנה teaches that a tiny place, with just two courtyards of two houses each, counts as unwalled even if it has a wall from יהושע's time. The גמרא learns this from extra words in the פסוק and explains why there must be two houses in each courtyard. A new משנה discusses a grandson who inherits a house from his mother's father when one is a לוי and the other a ישראל. רבי holds that the special לוי redemption rule needs both a לוי seller and עָרֵי הַלְוִיִּם, while חכמים require only the location. Next, the משנה forbids changing the field, open land and city zones of Levite cities, and the גמרא finds the source in the words 'לֹא יִמָּכֵר'. A ברייתא then shows three things that 'גְּאֻלַּת עוֹלָם' teaches for a לוי. When the גמרא asks how לוים could own houses in walled cities, רב כהנא's answer is rejected, and a new answer begins that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי׳ וְאֵלּוּ הֵן בָּתֵּי הַחֲצֵרִים —

MISHNA: And these are the houses of the courtyards

שְׁנֵי חֲצֵרִים שֶׁל שְׁנֵי בָתִּים,

two courtyards, each with two houses,

אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁמּוּקֶּפֶת חוֹמָה

even though it is surrounded by a wall

מִימוֹת יְהוֹשֻׁעַ בִּן נוּן,

from the days of Joshua son of Nun,
