Topic

The return of the tribes in the time of ירמיהו, and the laws of houses in unwalled villages (בתי החצרים): their redemption, the יובל, and הקדש.

Overview

The amud opens with the end of a discussion about whether שמיטות and יובלות were still counted after the tribes of ראובן, גד and half of מנשה were exiled. A verse in ירמיהו about freeing slaves seems to show that יובל was still kept in his time. After the answer that the verse was only a rebuke is rejected, רבי יוחנן explains that ירמיהו brought the exiled tribes back and יאשיהו ruled over them, and the גמרא brings פסוקים to prove both points. A new משנה then teaches that houses in unwalled villages (בתי החצרים) get the advantages of both walled-city houses and fields. They can be redeemed right away, and they go back to the seller in the יובל with גרעון כסף. A ברייתא learns these rules from ויקרא 25:31. רב הונא explains that ״וּבַיּוֹבֵל יֵצֵא״ teaches that such a house, made הקדש and redeemed by someone else, returns to its original owner. אביי adds that הקדש never leaves without פדיון, which the גמרא learns from a קל וחומר from a בן לוי. רבי אושעיא disagrees and holds that whoever redeems from הקדש keeps it, so רב פפא gives the פסוק a different use. A ברייתא then supports רב הונא and refutes רבי אושעיא.

Opening lines

הָנִיחָא לְרַבָּנַן דְּאָמְרִי:

This works well according to the Rabbis, who say:

שְׁנַת חֲמִשִּׁים אֵינָהּ מִן הַמִּנְיָן,

the fiftieth year is not part of the count,

אֶלָּא לְרַבִּי יְהוּדָה דְּאָמַר:

but according to Rabbi Yehuda, who says:

שְׁנַת חֲמִשִּׁים עוֹלָה לְכָאן וּלְכָאן,

the fiftieth year counts for here and for there,

The steps (33)

  4. ProofThe 'seven years' refers to the נרצע, who goes free at יובל
  5. ResolutionThe verse is a rebuke about the past
  6. ChallengeBut the people did listen and free their slaves
  7. Resolutionירמיהו brought the tribes back and יאשיהו ruled over them

  13. ChallengeMaybe these houses also need a two-year wait before redemption
  14. ResolutionThey can be redeemed right away
  15. ChallengeMaybe getting both advantages means they do not return in the יובל

  20. ChallengeWhy only when someone else redeemed it?

  32. ProofA ברייתא that matches רב הונא
  33. ConclusionThe house goes back to the owner in the יובל
