Topic

Consecrating part of an animal, 'for the מזבח' pledges, and ערך vows on a baby or a utensil

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא about someone who says 'the leg of this animal is an עולה.' רבי מאיר says only the leg is holy, so the animal is sold to be brought as an עולה, and only the money for the leg is holy. רבי יהודה, רבי יוסי and רבי שמעון say the whole animal becomes an עולה. The גמרא uses this to challenge רבא. Even רבי מאיר agrees that consecrating a vital part makes the whole animal holy. רבא answers in steps, distinguishing קדושת הגוף from קדושת דמים, a whole animal from one limb, and an unblemished animal from a blemished one. Three questions about pledges 'for the מזבח' follow, from רבה, רבא and רב אשי, and all end in תיקו. Then a new משנה teaches that a baby under one month can be pledged by his value but has no ערך. The גמרא explains that רבי מאיר and the רבנן argue over whether a person ever speaks for nothing. It also shows that רב's ruling about the ערך of a utensil follows only רבי מאיר.

״רַגְלָהּ שֶׁל זוֹ עוֹלָה״ —

יָכוֹל תְּהֵא כּוּלָּהּ עוֹלָה?

one might think the whole animal will be a burnt offering?

תַּלְמוּד לוֹמַר

Therefore the verse states:

״כֹּל אֲשֶׁר יִתֵּן מִמֶּנּוּ לַה׳ יִהְיֶה קֹּדֶשׁ״ —

"Anything of it that one gives to the Lord shall be sacred"
