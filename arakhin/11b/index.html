Topic

The source for the לויים' שירה and the punishment for doing another's job, and whether a voluntary communal עולה needs שירה

Overview

The גמרא brings several sources for the rule that the לויים must sing (שירה) during the קרבנות. A ברייתא learns from a פסוק that a כהן and a לוי who swap jobs get מיתה, while one לוי doing another לוי's job breaks only an אזהרה. אביי says a singer who did a gatekeeper's job gets מיתה, but a ברייתא says it is only an אזהרה. The גמרא tries to show that תנאים argue about this, using a story of two sages at the Temple doors, but the story can also be read another way. Then רבי אבין asks whether a voluntary communal עולה needs שירה. Proofs from חזקיהו's offering and from the day the בית המקדש was destroyed are each pushed away. Finally, a פסוק that puts עולות next to שלמים teaches that only an עולה with a set time gets שירה, so a voluntary עולה does not.

Opening lines

עַל עִסְקֵי קוֹל.

about matters of voice.

רַב אָשֵׁי אָמַר, מֵהָכָא:

Rav Ashi said: from here:

״וַיְהִי כְאֶחָד לַמְחַצְּצרִים

"It came to pass, when the trumpeters were as one

וְלַמְשֹׁרְרִים לְהַשְׁמִיעַ קוֹל אֶחָד״.

with the singers, to make one sound heard."
