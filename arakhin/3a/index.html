Topic

What the word הכל ('everyone') comes to include in a long series of rulings, from מגילה and זימון to טומאה, פרה אדומה, שחיטה and moving to ארץ ישראל

Overview

The גמרא goes through a long list of rulings that begin with the word הכל ('everyone'). For each one it asks לאתויי מאי: whom does 'everyone' come to include? Reading the מגילה includes women, and being obligated in זימון includes women and slaves. Being counted toward a זימון includes a קטן who knows to Whom we bless. For זיבה, even a one-day-old baby becomes impure; רבי יהודה learns this from 'איש איש', and רבי ישמעאל learns it from 'לזכר ולנקבה', holding that דברה תורה כלשון בני אדם. For טמא מת and נגעים a child is included, and the גמרא explains what the word איש teaches in each case: no כרת for a child, and only a man tears his clothes and grows his hair. It goes on to inspecting נגעים, mixing and sprinkling the פרה אדומה water, and שחיטה. The amud ends by asking what הכל adds in the rule about moving to ארץ ישראל, and the answer comes on the next amud.
