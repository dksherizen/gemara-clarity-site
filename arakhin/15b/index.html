Topic

The end of the ten trials in the desert, and the severity, remedy and definition of לשון הרע

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a ברייתא's list of the ten times the Jewish people tested הקב״ה in the desert: two with the מן, two with the quail, the golden calf, and the spies in מדבר פארן. Then the גמרא turns to a long series of teachings on how serious לשון הרע is. It is compared to denying God, it brings נגעים, it raises sins to the heavens, it deserves stoning, and God cannot share the world with the one who speaks it. רבי חמא ברבי חנינא gives a remedy: a תלמיד חכם should learn Torah and an עם הארץ should humble himself. רבי אחא ברבי חנינא says these only prevent the sin and cannot fix it after it is spoken. The school of רבי ישמעאל teaches that לשון הרע equals עבודה זרה, גילוי עריות and שפיכות דמים together. A chain of פסוקים then shows that the tongue kills like a hand, like an arrow, and from heaven to earth. Finally, רבא, אביי and רבה argue about what counts as לישנא בישא, including whether speech said in front of its subject is included. A new statement begins at the end and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

״אַל תֵּצֵאוּ״ וַיֵּצְאוּ,

"Do not go out" and they went out;

״אַל תּוֹתִירוּ״ וַיּוֹתִירוּ.

"Do not leave any over" and they left some over.

שְׁנַיִם בַּשְּׂלָיו

Two with the quail:

[בִּשְׂלָיו] — רִאשׁוֹן וּבִשְׂלָיו שֵׁנִי.

with the first quail and with the second quail.
