Topic

Who sets the size of a vowed מצורע offering, and when a person's wealth is measured in ערכין and in offerings

Overview

The amud opens by explaining that the משנה's two rules about vowing to pay for a מצורע's offering cover two different cases. A poor sponsor of a poor מצורע may bring the poor offering, but a poor sponsor of a rich מצורע must bring the rich one. A ברייתא learns this from "אם דל הוא". According to רבי, the word "הוא" instead excludes a rich sponsor of a poor מצורע. A new משנה then teaches that in ערכין, being rich either when you vow or when you pay means paying the rich amount, and רבי יהודה says even being rich only in between counts. By contrast, a מצורע's offerings follow only what he has right now. The גמרא brings the פסוקים for these rules, and it learns רבי יהודה's view from "ואם מך הוא": a person counts as poor only if he is poor from start to finish. It defends this reading against the parallel פסוק of the מצורע, which has its own special source, "בטהרתו". It then raises a new challenge from the witness פסוק "והוא עד" and begins quoting a ברייתא about witnesses. The answer to that challenge comes on the next amud.
