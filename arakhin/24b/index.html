Topic

Consecrating a field during the יובל year: רב and שמואל, the ברייתא, and the yearly redemption price

Overview

This amud continues a מחלוקת between רב and שמואל: if someone is מקדיש his field during the יובל year itself, does it become holy? רב says yes, and it is redeemed at the full price. שמואל says no. First רב knocks down שמואל's קל וחומר. A field consecrated before יובל never goes back to its owner anyway; it goes to the כהנים through הקדש. Then רב and שמואל argue over the פסוק 'וְאִם מִשְּׁנַת הַיּוֹבֵל': does 'from' include the יובל year or start after it? A ברייתא says a field consecrated in יובל 'is not holy.' רב first explains this as 'not holy with a deduction.' When that runs into trouble, he says the ברייתא follows the רבנן, while he follows רבי, who holds that 'from X' includes X. The גמרא then works through the math of 'a סלע and a פונדיון' per year and shows how it fits both רב and שמואל, depending on how the years of the cycle are counted. Finally, a משנה about not redeeming 'less than a year after the יובל' seems to support שמואל. The גמרא begins to answer for רב, and its explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְרַב סָבַר:

And Rav holds:

סוֹף סוֹף לַבְּעָלִים מִי קָהָדְרָא?

Ultimately, does it return to the owners?

לְכֹהֲנִים הוּא דְּנָפְקָא,

It goes out to the priests,

וְכֹהֲנִים מִשּׁוּלְחַן גָּבוֹהַּ קָא זָכוּ!

and the priests acquire from the table of the Most High!

The steps (17)

  1. Rejectionרב rejects שמואל's קל וחומר
  2. QuestionWhat is רב's source?
  3. Responseרב reads 'from the Jubilee year' as including it
  4. Rejectionשמואל: 'from' means starting after the Jubilee
  5. ChallengeWhat does 'after the Jubilee' mean for שמואל?
  6. ResolutionThe year after the year after the Jubilee
  7. ChallengeA ברייתא says a field consecrated in the יובל year is not holy
  8. Resolutionרב: It is holy, just not redeemed with a deduction
  9. ChallengeThat answer clashes with the rule about the last two years
  10. Resolutionרב: The ברייתא follows the רבנן, but I follow רבי
  11. ChallengeIf רבי is right, why the extra פונדיון?
  12. Challengeרבי clearly requires the extra פונדיון
  13. Resolutionרבי follows רבי יהודה's counting of the fiftieth year
  14. QuestionFor שמואל, must רבי follow the רבנן?
  15. ResponseYes, for שמואל רבי follows the רבנן
  16. ProofThe משנה's 'a year after the יובל' fits שמואל, not רב
  17. Resolution'After the יובל' does not mean the year after
