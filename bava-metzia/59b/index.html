Bava Metzia 59b

בבא מציעא נ״ט ע״ב

Topic

תנור של עכנאי, the power of אונאת דברים, and not hurting a גר

Overview

This amud finishes the story of the תנור של עכנאי. רבי אליעזר brings every argument and then miracles to prove his view: a carob tree, a stream, the walls of the בית המדרש, and finally a בת קול. רבי יהושע answers 'לא בשמים היא': the תורה was given at סיני, and it tells us to follow the majority. אליהו later reports that הקדוש ברוך הוא smiled and said, 'My children have defeated Me.' The חכמים then place רבי אליעזר in נידוי, and his pain brings damage to the world. In the end רבן גמליאל dies, because the gates of Heaven are always open to someone hurt by words. The גמרא then teaches that hurting or oppressing a גר breaks three לאוין, and that we must not insult a גר, since we were strangers in מצרים ourselves. Finally, a new משנה begins about not mixing batches of produce when selling.

Opening lines

וְזֶה הוּא תַּנּוּר שֶׁל עַכְנַאי.

And this is the oven of akhnai.

מַאי עַכְנַאי?

What is akhnai?

אָמַר רַב יְהוּדָה אָמַר שְׁמוּאֵל:

Rav Yehuda said that Shmuel said:

שֶׁהִקִּיפוּ[הוּ] דְּבָרִים כְּעַכְנָא זוֹ,

That they surrounded it with words like this snake,
