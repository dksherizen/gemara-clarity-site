Bava Metzia 32a

בבא מציעא ל״ב ע״א

Learn Bava Metzia 32a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Bava Metzia 32a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Dividing a partnership without a בית דין; returning lost animals; honoring parents versus מצוות; unloading and loading a fallen animal

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the story of רב ספרא, who divided a partnership's property on his own, without his partner and without a בית דין. רבה בר רב הונא says he needs proof that he divided before three. רב ספרא answers that taking your own share only reveals a fact, so two witnesses are enough, and he brings support from a widow who sells without a בית דין. אביי pushes back with רב נחמן's teaching that the widow still needs a בית דין of ordinary people. A new משנה then teaches when a lost animal must be returned, that a כהן may not become טמא to return it, and that a child must not obey a parent who says to break a מצוה. The same משנה also teaches the מצוות of unloading (פריקה) and loading (טעינה) a fallen animal. The גמרא defines the stable in the משנה, brings רבי יצחק's limit about the תחום in two versions, and finds the source for not obeying a parent. It ends by explaining that unloading is done for free and loading for pay, according to רבנן, while רבי שמעון says both are free, and each side argues from the פסוקים. רבא's new statement begins at the very end and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

תְּרֵי מִגּוֹ תְּלָתָא,

Two out of the three,

וְאִי נָמֵי תְּרֵי סָהֲדִי

or alternatively, two witnesses

דִּפְלַגְתְּ בְּאַפֵּי בֵּי תְלָתָא.

that you divided before a group of three.

אֲמַר לֵיהּ: מְנָא לָךְ הָא?

He said to him: From where do you have this?
