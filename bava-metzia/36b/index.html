Topic

שומר שמסר לשומר, and the dispute about negligence followed by a natural death (marsh and cliff cases)

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an answer for רבי יוחנן's view that a שומר who hands a deposit to someone else is liable. His own wife and children are an exception, because the owner expects them to be around. The sages of נהרדעא show that the wording of the משנה supports this. רבא rules as final הלכה that a שומר who hands a deposit to another שומר is always liable, because the owner trusts only the first שומר's oath. Next, אביי and רבא both quote רבה on a careless שומר whose animal wandered into a marsh and died naturally. אביי says he is liable because the marsh killed it, and רבא says he is exempt because death would have come anywhere. Each admits a limit to his view. Then each defends his view against a challenge, including a long back-and-forth about a משנה on animals at the cliffs. Finally, שמואל rules like רבי יוסי that a renter may not profit from the owner's cow, and רב שמואל בר יהודה begins to question how far that ruling reaches, which continues on the next amud.
