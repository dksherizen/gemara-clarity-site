Bava Metzia 76a

בבא מציעא ע״ו ע״א

Topic

Workers misled by a middleman hiring for the בעל הבית: why they have a complaint, and whose words they rely on when they say "as the בעל הבית said."

Overview

The amud opens with the last lines of the משנה: whoever backs out of a work deal, or changes its terms, ends up at a disadvantage. The גמרא then explains the משנה's words "הטעו זה את זה" as a case where a middleman sent by the בעל הבית misled the workers he hired. If he offered less than allowed, the workers agreed, and if he promised more and said "your pay is on me," he owes it himself, as a ברייתא shows. So the case must be that he said the pay comes from the בעל הבית. The גמרא then explains the workers' complaint (תרעומת) in several ways: they lost a chance at a better job, or they were landowners who would not have worked for less, or they worked harder in a way that cannot be checked. Another answer says even a lower offer they agreed to gives them a complaint, because of the פסוק "אל תמנע טוב מבעליו." Next the גמרא asks a new question: if the workers said "as the בעל הבית said," do they rely on the middleman's report or on the בעל הבית's real offer? It tries to prove they rely on the middleman from a case of a גט sent through a שליח. רב אשי begins to respond, and his words continue on the next amud.

Opening lines

אִם בַּעַל הַבַּיִת חוֹזֵר בּוֹ

If the employer backs out,

יָדוֹ עַל הַתַּחְתּוֹנָה.

his hand is on the bottom.

כׇּל הַמְשַׁנֶּה – יָדוֹ עַל הַתַּחְתּוֹנָה,

Whoever changes the terms is at a disadvantage,

וְכׇל הַחוֹזֵר בּוֹ – יָדוֹ עַל הַתַּחְתּוֹנָה.

and whoever backs out is at a disadvantage.
