Bava Metzia 64a

בבא מציעא ס״ד ע״א

Learn Bava Metzia 64a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Bava Metzia 64a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Extra money in a payment that may be a mistake or a hidden repayment of theft, and ריבית in advance purchases of growing produce (gourds, milk, wool) and wine.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a rule about extra money found in a payment. If the extra fits counting in tens or fives, we assume it was a mistake. רב אחא בריה דרבא asks רב אשי about a harsh giver who never gives gifts, and then about a stranger who never dealt with the receiver. Both times רב אשי answers that the extra may be stolen money being quietly returned. He backs this with a ברייתא: a thief who slips the stolen amount into a calculation has fulfilled his duty. Next, רב כהנא tells how he heard רב say "קרי קרי" (gourds). The students explain that paying a gardener now for bigger gourds later is ריבית unless the big gourds already exist, even though gourds grow by themselves. A ברייתא about selling a set amount of milk, wool and honey supports this. In another version רבא permits the sale, and he answers the ברייתא: milk and wool are new produce that replaces what was taken, while a gourd itself grows. Finally אביי permits paying in advance for wine when the buyer takes the price risk, and רב שרביא begins to challenge him on the next amud.

Opening lines

בְּעִישּׂוּרְיָיתָא וְחוּמְשְׁיָיתָא.

In tens and fives.

אֲמַר לֵיהּ רַב אַחָא בְּרֵיהּ דְּרָבָא לְרַב אָשֵׁי:

Rav Acha the son of Rava said to Rav Ashi:

וְאִי אִינִישׁ תַּקִּיפָא הוּא

And if he is a harsh person,

דְּלָא יָהֵיב מַתָּנָה, מַאי?

who doesn't give gifts, what then?
