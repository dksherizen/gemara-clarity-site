Topic

The 'fifth of the fifth' (חומשא דחומשא) in גזל, תרומה, מעשר and הקדש, and רבי יהושע בן לוי's rule that a הקדש שני gets no חומש.

Overview

This amud first closes the previous discussion: the חומש has the same status as the קרן. Then רבא lists where a 'fifth of the fifth' is clearly taught. For גזל and תרומה there is a פסוק and a משנה, but for מעשר there is nothing at all. The גמרא then asks whether הקדש also has a fifth of the fifth. One side says yes, because both פסוקים say 'וְיָסַף'. The other side says no, because a letter trick only makes a plural for תרומה. A shortcut using רבי יהושע בן לוי's rule about הקדש שני is rejected, because the fifth counts as a fresh הקדש. רב טביומי, in the name of אביי, settles it from a הקש: the fifth also gets a fifth. The גמרא then gives רבא's source for רבי יהושע בן לוי's rule ('הַמַּקְדִּישׁ, וְלֹא הַמַּתְפִּיס'). It brings a ברייתא about a בהמה טמאה whose words 'תחילת הקדש' turn out to follow that rule. The amud ends as רב אשי begins a question about the ברייתא, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שְׁמַע מִינַּהּ חוּמְשׁוֹ כְּמוֹתוֹ.

Learn from it: its fifth is like itself.

שְׁמַע מִינַּהּ.

Indeed, learn from it.

אָמַר רָבָא,

Rava said:

Regarding robbery it is written:
