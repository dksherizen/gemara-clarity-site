Topic

Stories of the חכמים: אליהו's rebuke, the size and beauty of the חכמים, and the friendship and loss of רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש

Overview

This amud is all אגדה, stories rather than legal debate. It opens with אליהו הנביא rebuking רבי ישמעאל ברבי יוסי for handing Jews over to the Roman government, telling him to run away as his father did. Next the גמרא describes how large רבי ישמעאל and רבי אלעזר ברבי שמעון were, and how they answered a Roman noblewoman who mocked them. The גמרא explains that they answered her only to protect their children's good name. It then turns to רבי יוחנן's great beauty, and to why he sat by the מקוה without fearing the עין הרע: he came from יוסף. Then comes the famous story of how רבי יוחנן brought ריש לקיש, a bandit, to Torah and made him a great scholar. After a sharp exchange in the בית מדרש, רבי יוחנן was hurt, ריש לקיש fell ill and died, and רבי יוחנן mourned him so deeply that no one could replace him, until he too died.

Opening lines

כִּי הַאי מַעֲשֶׂה לִידֵיהּ.

a case like this came into his hands.

פְּגַע בֵּיהּ אֵלִיָּהוּ,

Elijah met him,

אֲמַר לֵיהּ: עַד מָתַי

and said to him: Until when

אַתָּה מוֹסֵר עַמּוֹ שֶׁל אֱלֹהֵינוּ

will you hand over the nation of our God
