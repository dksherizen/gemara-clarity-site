Topic

When a lender takes land back from a buyer (טורף מן הלקוחות), who gets the שבח (improvement) the buyer added? The גמרא works to fit שמואל's ruling with a ברייתא that seems to disagree.

Overview

This amud begins by finishing an answer about a lender who takes land back from a buyer who improved it. Is the lender allowed to keep the buyer's שבח? The גמרא first fits שמואל together with a ברייתא by looking at how ripe the produce is. When that fails, it splits them by the size of the debt, and it closes with the case of an אפותיקי. Next comes a new dispute about a buyer who knew the field was stolen. רב says he gets his money back because it was a פקדון. שמואל says he does not, because it was a מתנה. The גמרא shows that the same dispute appears with a man who does קידושין with his sister, and explains why both cases are needed. רבא then rules like רב, and he also rules that a missing אחריות clause is a טעות סופר. Finally, שמואל asks רב about a גזלן who sold a field and later bought it from the real owner. The amud ends partway through explaining the practical difference between מר זוטרא and רב אשי, and that explanation continues on the next amud.
