Bava Metzia 58a

בבא מציעא נ״ח ע״א

Topic

Oaths and guarding responsibility for הקדש, and רבי שמעון's rule about קדשים one must replace

Overview

This amud finishes quoting a משנה from מסכת שקלים about agents who lost the שקלים they were carrying and must swear about them. That seems to clash with our rule that no oath is taken about הקדש. שמואל says the agents were paid and swear only to collect their wages. רבה defends this reading against two challenges. רבי יוחנן instead says the משנה follows רבי שמעון, but this answer fails once the Temple collection has been made. So רבי אלעזר says the oath is a rule of the חכמים to protect הקדש. Next, רב יוסף בר חמא raises a ברייתא that seems to make a paid guard of holy items pay. The answer, from רב ששת and רבי יוחנן, is that those workers took on responsibility with a קנין. Finally, a ברייתא gives the source for רבי שמעון's rule about קדשים one must replace, and רבי יצחק בר אבא protests that it looks backwards.

Opening lines

נִשְׁבָּעִין לַגִּזְבָּרִין,

They take an oath to the treasurers;

וְאִם לָאו – נִשְׁבָּעִין לִבְנֵי הָעִיר,

and if not, they take an oath to the people of the town,

וּבְנֵי הָעִיר שׁוֹקְלִין אֲחֵרִים תַּחְתֵּיהֶן.

and the people of the town give other shekels in their place.

נִמְצְאוּ, אוֹ שֶׁהֶחְזִירוּם הַגַּנָּבִים –

If they were found, or the thieves returned them,

The steps (18)

  1. ChallengeEnd of the quoted משנה about lost שקלים
  2. Resolutionשמואל: the agents were paid and swear to get their wages
  3. ChallengeIf it is about wages, why swear to the treasurers?
  4. Resolutionרבה: they swear to the townspeople while the treasurers are present
  5. ChallengeA paid שומר is responsible for theft and loss, so shouldn't they lose their wages?
  6. Resolutionרבה: armed robbers and a sunken ship
  7. Resolutionרבי יוחנן says the משנה follows רבי שמעון
  8. ChallengeAfter the Temple collection, no one is responsible anymore
  9. Resolutionרבי אלעזר: The oath is a rabbinic enactment
  10. ChallengeContradiction: Does a paid guard of holy items pay?
  11. ChallengeThe second half of the ברייתא seems to make the guard pay
  12. ResolutionResponsibility means losing his wages
  13. ChallengeThat reading fails in the first half of the ברייתא
  14. Questionרבה asks if there is a known answer
  15. ResponseThe workers took on responsibility with a קנין
  16. Statementרבי שמעון: holy items have אונאה only if one must replace them
  17. StatementThe ברייתא: holy items one must replace versus holy items one need not replace
  18. Challengeרבי יצחק protests that the matching is backwards
