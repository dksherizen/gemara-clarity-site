Bava Metzia 103b

בבא מציעא ק״ג ע״ב

Learn Bava Metzia 103b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Bava Metzia 103b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Local custom in farming partnerships (who does which work, what is shared), and a new משנה on rent when a field's spring or trees are lost.

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה about a worker who farms another person's field. The owner and worker share the vine branches and poles, and both supply the poles. A ברייתא teaches that the worker must cut or uproot the crop according to local custom, because each side has a real interest and can block a change. The גמרא then explains several lines of the משנה. A worker who weeded on his own must still plow. The word 'הכל' adds that local custom decides whether the trees are part of the deal. In each case, a silent intention does not count, even one backed by a changed share; you have to spell it out at the start. רב יוסף adds the Babylonian custom about straw, and he divides the fencing, tools and irrigation work between the owner and the אריס. A new משנה then rules that a tenant pays full rent when the spring dries up or the trees are cut down, unless he rented the field by name as an irrigated or tree field. רב פפא explains that the case is a small river serving only this field, and his explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בַּזְּמוֹרוֹת וּבַקָּנִים,

in the branches and the poles,

וּשְׁנֵיהֶם מְסַפְּקִין אֶת הַקָּנִים.

and the two of them supply the poles.

גְּמָ׳ תָּנָא: מְקוֹם שֶׁנָּהֲגוּ לִקְצוֹר –

GEMARA: It was taught: In a place where they were accustomed to cut,

אֵינוֹ רַשַּׁאי לַעֲקוֹר,

he is not permitted to uproot;
