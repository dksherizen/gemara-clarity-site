Bava Metzia 50b

בבא מציעא נ׳ ע״ב

Topic

The time limits for overcharging (אונאה) claims, learned from the merchants of לוד and settled by רבא, with רב נחמן limiting the deadline to the buyer.

Overview

The משנה tells that רבי טרפון ruled in לוד that אונאה (overcharging) goes up to one-third, and the merchants were happy. When he added that the buyer can back out all day, they went back to the view of the חכמים. The גמרא first finishes a proof from this story: under the רבנן, a small overcharge is forgiven only after the time it takes to show the item to a merchant or relative. That proof is pushed back. The גמרא then uses the merchants' happiness to ask whether a sale cancelled for an overcharge of more than one-sixth can be undone forever, and that proof is pushed back too. רבא rules the final הלכה: less than one-sixth, the sale stands; exactly one-sixth, the extra money is returned; more than one-sixth, the sale is cancelled. Both claims must be made within the short checking time, and a ברייתא supports him. Finally, רב נחמן says this time limit applies only to the buyer, and the גמרא begins to bring support for him from the merchants of לוד.
