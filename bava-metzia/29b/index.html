Bava Metzia 29b

בבא מציעא כ״ט ע״ב

Topic

Using found money, caring for found scrolls, garments and vessels, borrowed and deposited Torah scrolls, and רבי יוחנן's practical advice

Overview

The גמרא finishes the earlier משנה by quoting רבה: a finder who is allowed to use the money must pay even if it is taken by armed bandits or lost in a shipwreck. It rules like רבי טרפון, but אביי shows that the permission covers only money a finder earned by caring for a lost object, so רחבה may not use orphans' money. A new משנה then explains how a finder looks after scrolls, garments and vessels, and when he need not pick up an item at all. שמואל rules that found תפילין may be valued and the money set aside, because new תפילין are easy to buy while scrolls are not. A ברייתא gives rules for a borrowed or deposited ספר תורה, and the גמרא explains each line, correcting the view of רבי אליעזר בן יעקב. Next come a contradiction about two people reading one scroll and four answers about whether shaking a garment helps it. The amud ends with practical advice from רבי יוחנן about lukewarm water and about habits that waste an inheritance. The explanation of the last habit continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

כִּדְרַבָּה.

In accordance with Rabba.

דְּאָמַר רַבָּה:

As Rabba said:

״נִגְנְבוּ״ בְּלִסְטִים מְזוּיָּין,

'They were stolen' means by armed bandits;

״אָבְדוּ״ שֶׁטָּבְעָה סְפִינָתוֹ בַּיָּם.

'They were lost' means that his ship sank at sea.
