Topic

The liability and the 'owner with him' (בבעלים) exemption of a שואל and a שומר שכר: injury and death, theft and loss, and negligence.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a challenge to רבי נתן, who uses the word 'או' in 'ונשבר או מת' for an extra teaching. The גמרא answers that even רבי יאשיה agrees, because logic alone shows that injury is just a 'partial death.' So 'או' is not needed to divide the two cases. Next the גמרא looks for the source that a שואל pays for גניבה ואבידה. It finds it in a ברייתא's קל וחומר from the שומר שכר, and defends that קל וחומר against a refutation about כפל for an armed bandit. Then it asks why a שואל בבעלים is exempt for theft and loss. After several attempts fail, it answers with the rule of דיו and with the ו׳ of 'וכי ישאל', which links the שואל and שומר שכר sections. Finally, רב אחא and רבינא disagree whether negligence בבעלים is exempt. The disagreement depends on whether a verse reaches back to the שומר חנם section. A proof from our משנה is pushed away, and the גמרא's explanation of the משנה's wording continues on the next amud.
