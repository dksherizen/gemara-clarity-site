Bava Metzia 105b

בבא מציעא ק״ה ע״ב

Learn Bava Metzia 105b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Bava Metzia 105b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Measures for 'weak' things, four קבין for תפלה and תפילין, כדי נפילה, and a tenant's rent after a regional disaster

Overview

The amud opens by defining 'weak' (שכוחו רע) in the laws of טומאה. It covers a weak tree or branch that a זב and a טהור person climb together, and a weak person or animal ridden through a בית הפרס. Because דבי רבי ינאי gave some of those measures, the גמרא brings their rule that four קבין is the key measure for תפלה and תפילין. A four-קבין load must be put down before praying, and it may not be carried on top of head-תפילין. Related teachings from רבי חייא, רבי שילא and אביי add that תפילין must be treated with respect, and nothing at all may rest on top of them. The גמרא then returns to רבי יהודה's measure, כדי נפילה (the amount of seed sown), and explains why the number changed from four to eight סאין as the land grew poorer. A related משנה about wind-scattered sheaves leads to a question that ends in a תיקו. Finally a new משנה rules that a tenant reduces his rent only for a מכת מדינה, a regional disaster, and the גמרא begins defining that term with questions that continue on the next amud.

Opening lines

עָלוּ בְּאִילָן שֶׁכּוֹחוֹ רַע,

If they climbed a tree that is weak,

וּבְסוֹכָה שֶׁכּוֹחָהּ רַע –

or a branch that is weak –

טָמֵא.

he is impure.

הֵיכִי דָּמֵי אִילָן שֶׁכּוֹחוֹ רַע?

What is the case of a tree that is weak?

The steps (39)

  1. StatementClimbing a weak tree or weak branch makes the pure person impure
  2. QuestionWhat counts as a weak tree?
  3. Responseדבי רבי ינאי: a trunk too thin to carve a רובע vessel
  4. QuestionWhat counts as a weak branch?
  5. Responseריש לקיש: a branch that fits inside a fist
  6. StatementA parallel משנה: riding on a weak person or animal in a בית הפרס
  7. QuestionWhat counts as a weak person?
  8. Responseריש לקיש: his knees knock when someone rides him
  9. QuestionWhat counts as a weak animal?
  10. Responseדבי רבי ינאי: it releases dung when ridden
  11. StatementFour קבין is the measure for תפלה and תפילין
  12. QuestionWhat does four קבין mean for prayer?
  13. ResponseA heavy load must be put down before praying
  14. QuestionWhat does four קבין mean for תפילין?
  15. ResponseA four-קבין load on the head crushes תפילין
  16. StatementCarrying garbage while wearing תפילין
  17. StatementEven the תפילין wrapper may not rest on the head
  18. QuestionHow small a load is forbidden?
  19. Responseאביי: even a tiny amount is forbidden
  20. StatementBack to the משנה: רבי יהודה's measure of כדי נפילה
  21. QuestionHow much is כדי נפילה?
  22. Responseרבי יוחנן: four סאין per כור
  23. Responseרבי אמי's own view: eight סאין per כור
  24. ResolutionRich land versus poor land
  25. StatementA related משנה: wind scattered the sheaves
  26. QuestionHow much falls during harvest?
  27. Responseרב דימי: four קבין per כור
  28. Questionרבי ירמיה: which כור, and which kind of sowing?
  29. ResponseThe כור means a field sown with a כור of seed
  30. ConclusionHand sowing or oxen sowing stays unresolved
  31. StatementA new משנה: crop disaster and the tenant's rent
  32. Statementרבי יהודה: a tenant who pays money never reduces his rent
  33. QuestionWhat counts as a regional disaster?
  34. Responseרב יהודה: most of the valley was blasted
  35. Responseעולא: the four fields around it were blasted
  36. QuestionOnly one blasted furrow connects the fields
  37. QuestionOne furrow survived along the whole length
  38. QuestionA fallow field separates the fields
  39. QuestionA field of animal fodder in between
