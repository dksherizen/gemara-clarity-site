Bava Metzia 47a

בבא מציעא מ״ז ע״א

Open Bava Metzia 47a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Coins, חליפין, and symbolic acquisition: "sell me for these," exchanges with set prices, whose vessel is used, and whether produce can be used.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a ruling: if a buyer says "sell me for these" and the seller doesn't care about the exact sum, the buyer acquires at once, like חליפין, but there is still אונאה. In רב אבא's report, רב הונא says there is no אונאה. The גמרא then asks about a real חליפין where the parties do care about the values. A ברייתא about trading a cow for a donkey seems to show such a trade fails, but רבא explains that the ברייתא is about an incomplete משיכה. Next the גמרא asks whether רב הונא lets a coin act as חליפין, and another version has him say plainly that it cannot. רב and לוי then argue whose vessel is used for symbolic חליפין, and the גמרא ties this to a dispute of תנאים about the shoe in רות. רב נחמן and רב ששת argue whether produce can be used, each reading the same פסוק. The amud ends with the wording of documents, whose explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְיֵשׁ לוֹ עָלָיו אוֹנָאָה.

And he has a claim of overcharging against him.

קָנָה – אַף עַל גַּב דְּלָא מָשַׁךְ,

He acquires – even though he did not pull it,

דְּכֵיוָן דְּלָא קָפֵיד – קָנָה,

because since he is not particular – he acquires,

דְּכִי חֲלִיפִין דָּמֵי.

as it is similar to an exchange.
