Bava Metzia 15a

בבא מציעא ט״ו ע״א

Learn Bava Metzia 15a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Bava Metzia 15a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

How a נגזל, a buyer and a creditor collect the קרן, פירות and שבח, and שמואל's rules on שבח for a buyer from a robber and for a creditor.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing רבה בר רב הונא's explanation of the ברייתא about a stolen field: thugs seized it, so the נגזל collects the קרן from sold property and the פירות only from free property. The גמרא shows that רבא and רבה בר רב הונא each read the phrase 'הרי היא יוצאה מתחת ידו' differently. רב אשי offers a third reading: the buyer collects the קרן and the נגזל collects the פירות. The גמרא then asks how a מלוה על פה can be collected from liened property. It answers that the robber stood trial for the קרן before he sold his land. Next the גמרא questions שמואל's view that a לוקח מגזלן gets no שבח. רב יוסף answers in two versions: the robber owns land, or a קנין was made, and a sale is different from a loan like סאה בסאה. The גמרא then returns to שמואל's rule that a בעל חוב collects the שבח. רבא proves it from the wording of a sale document and accepts that a gift is stronger than a sale in this respect. Finally, two ברייתות are brought. The second one is defended for שמואל in two ways, and the second defense continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שֶׁנְּטָלוּהָ מַסִּיקִין,

[for example,] where thugs took it [the field] away,

בָּא נִגְזָל לִגְבּוֹת קֶרֶן –

when the robbery victim comes to collect the principal –

גּוֹבֶה מִנְּכָסִים מְשׁוּעְבָּדִים,

he collects from liened property;

בָּא נִגְזָל לִגְבּוֹת פֵּירוֹת –

when the robbery victim comes to collect for the produce –
