Bava Metzia 30b

בבא מציעא ל׳ ע״ב

Open Bava Metzia 30b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The dignity exemption in returning lost items, acting beyond the letter of the law, and when a found animal counts as lost

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a source: your own loss comes before your friend's, learned from 'לא יהיה בך אביון'. So the word 'והתעלמת' must teach something else: a זקן does not have to return an item when handling it is beneath his dignity. רבה adds that once he starts handling the animal, he must finish, as the story of אביי and the goats shows. The גמרא leaves unresolved (תיקו) whether someone who would return an item in the field must also return it in the city. רבא gives a simple test: whatever you would do for your own property, you must do for your friend's. The story of רבי ישמעאל ברבי יוסי, who bought a man's wood rather than lift it, leads to a question about הפקר and to the idea of acting לפנים משורת הדין. That idea is backed by רב יוסף's ברייתא and by רבי יוחנן's teaching that ירושלים was destroyed because people never went beyond strict law. A new משנה then defines which animals count as lost, requires returning one again and again, and sets the finder's pay. The גמרא explains the משנה's wording and adds רב's three-day rule for animals seen grazing at dawn and dusk. A supporting ברייתא begins at the end of the amud and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לֹא יִהְיֶה בְּךָ אֶבְיוֹן״ –

'There shall be no needy among you'

שֶׁלְּךָ קוֹדֵם לְשֶׁל כׇּל אָדָם,

yours comes before that of any other person.

אֶלָּא לְזָקֵן

Rather, it is needed for an elderly person

וְאֵינָהּ לְפִי כְּבוֹדוֹ.

when it is not in keeping with his dignity.
