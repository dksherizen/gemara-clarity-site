Topic

The חומש for a consecrated בהמה טמאה, and the משנה's list of laws measured by a פרוטה

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a discussion about a consecrated בהמה טמאה (non-kosher animal) and the extra חומש added when redeeming it. רבינא holds that the חומש applies only at the first stage of הקדש, not the middle stage. רב אחא מדיפתי and רב זוטרא challenge him. Logic could compare the middle stage to either the first or the last stage, so the גמרא settles it from the word ״הטמאה״, which means the first stage, just as רבא read ״העולה״. A ברייתא then supports רבי יהושע בן לוי and states that חומש is added only on the first הקדש. Next comes a new משנה giving the amounts for אונאה, a claim, an admission, and a list of five laws measured by a פרוטה. The גמרא explains why the משנה repeats earlier rules. It brings the argument between רב כהנא and לוי about whether אונאה applies to פרוטות, and compares our משנה's list with לוי's list to explain what each one leaves out. Finally the גמרא returns to רב קטינא, who says a בית דין hears even claims under a פרוטה. רבא begins an objection from a פסוק, and that objection continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בְּאֶמְצַע הֶקְדֵּשׁ, לֵיתַהּ?

At the intermediate stage of consecration, it is not found?

אֲמַר לֵיהּ:

He said to him:

לְפִי שֶׁאֵינָהּ בְּסוֹף הֶקְדֵּשׁ.

Because it is not found at the final stage of consecration.

אֲמַר לֵיהּ רַב אַחָא: מִדִּיפְתִּי לְרָבִינָא,

Rav Acha of Difti said to Ravina:
