Topic

Taking collateral: the court agent's rights, what a debtor must be left (סידור), and whether collateral is sold

Overview

This amud deals with how a debt is collected and what must be left for the borrower. First the גמרא shows that תנאים disagree about whether a שליח בית דין must wait outside like the lender or may go into the house. A ברייתא then teaches that food-making tools may not be taken, and that the borrower keeps two beds for himself. Those beds follow the rule of סידור, which is learned from ערכין. The גמרא explains that one bed is for eating and one is for sleeping, based on a health warning from שמואל. Next, רב נחמן asks how there can be סידור if collateral is sold. The גמרא explains that he was speaking according to רבן שמעון בן גמליאל. It proves that רבן שמעון בן גמליאל means a full sale, because he holds that all Jews are children of kings. Finally, רב חגא challenges the whole idea of leaving the borrower anything, and אביי's answer continues on the next amud.
