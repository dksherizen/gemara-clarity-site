Bava Metzia 94a

בבא מציעא צ״ד ע״א

Open Bava Metzia 94a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

How a שומר can use a תנאי to change his Torah obligations, the rules for a valid תנאי, and the start of פרק השואל את הפרה.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the case of a shepherd who bragged to a thief about his men, dogs and slings and was then robbed. Bragging like this counts as leading the flock into a dangerous place, so the loss is his fault. A new משנה then teaches that each kind of שומר may make a condition freeing himself from a שבועה or from paying. It also gives general rules: a condition against the Torah is void, a condition said after the action is void, and a condition said first that can be fulfilled is valid. The גמרא asks how a שומר's condition can work if it goes against the Torah. It first says the משנה follows רבי יהודה. Then it settles on רבי מאיר: the שומר never took on the Torah's duties in the first place. A ברייתא adds that a שומר שכר can take on a שואל's liability, and שמואל and רבי יוחנן argue about whether a קנין is needed for this. Next, the גמרא shows that the rule about conditions that can be fulfilled is רבי יהודה בן תימא's view, and the הלכה follows him: an impossible condition is ignored. The chapter then closes, and a new chapter opens with the rule that a borrower whose owner is working with him does not pay.

Opening lines

כָּךְ וְכָךְ גַּבְרֵי אִיכָּא בַּהֲדַן,

Such and such men are with us,

כָּךְ וְכָךְ כַּלְבֵי אִיכָּא בַּהֲדַן,

such and such dogs are with us,

כָּךְ וְכָךְ זוּקָתָא פְּסִיקָא לַן.

such and such slings are ready for us.

וַאֲזַל וּשְׁקַל מִינֵּיהּ, מַאי?

And the thief went and took from him. What is the law?
