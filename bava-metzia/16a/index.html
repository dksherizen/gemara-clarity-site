Bava Metzia 16a

בבא מציעא ט״ז ע״א

Topic

Why רב lets the buyer keep a stolen field the גזלן later buys back, which cases this applies to, and whether it clashes with the rule that you cannot sell what you do not yet own.

Overview

רב ruled that if a גזלן sold a stolen field and later bought it from the real owner, the buyer gets to keep it. Two reasons were given: the גזלן doesn't want to be called a robber, or he wants to keep his reliability. The גמרא tests two cases where these reasons might differ, a buyer who died and a robber who died, and rejects both. It settles on a gift: by the 'reliability' reason the person who received the field keeps it, but by the 'robber' reason he lost nothing and cannot complain. Next it goes through the ways the field might reach the גזלן: selling it on, inheriting it, collecting it for a debt, or receiving it as a gift. Three אמוראים then argue about how long the גזלן still cares about his good name. רמי בר חמא and רב ששת challenge רב's ruling, saying a person cannot sell what he does not yet own. רבא and אביי defend רב: in his case the buyer truly relies on the seller. Finally רבי יוחנן begins to explain why a sale limited to 'today' is valid, and his explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הָא מִית לֵיהּ.

he has died.

וּמַאן דְּאָמַר:

And the one who says:

נִיחָא לֵיהּ דְּלֵיקוּם בְּהֵמָנוּתֵיהּ –

it is preferable for him to maintain his reliability –

בַּהֲדֵי בְּנֵי נָמֵי

with the children too
