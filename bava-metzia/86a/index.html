Bava Metzia 86a

בבא מציעא פ״ו ע״א

Topic

The end of the book of אדם הראשון list, the death of רבה בר נחמני, the story of רבי שמעון בן חלפתא, and the return to the משנה on feeding workers by local custom

Overview

This amud first finishes a list from the book of אדם הראשון. שמואל ירחינאה will heal רבי. רבי and רבי נתן are the end of the משנה, and רב אשי and רבינא are the end of הוראה, with a פסוק given as a memory aid. Then the גמרא tells the long story of רבה בר נחמני's death. He was slandered to the king and ran from the king's messenger, and miracles saved him. While he hid and learned in a swamp, the heavenly yeshiva needed him to decide a doubt about צרעת. He died saying 'טהור, טהור', and heaven, the חכמים and even nature honored him. A short story about רבי שמעון בן חלפתא and a cooling wind follows. Then the גמרא goes back to the משנה about feeding workers. The word הכל teaches that an employer cannot change the local eating custom. The story of רבן יוחנן בן מתיא seems to contradict the rule, so the גמרא says words are missing from the משנה. It begins the missing line, 'if he promised them food', which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

חַכִּים יִתְקְרֵי,

He will be called 'wise one,'

וְרַבִּי לָא יִתְקְרֵי.

but he will not be called 'Rabbi.'

וְאַסּוּ דְּרַבִּי עַל יָדוֹ תְּהֵא.

And Rabbi's healing will be through him.

רַבִּי וְרַבִּי נָתָן – סוֹף מִשְׁנָה.

Rabbi and Rabbi Natan are the end of the Mishna.
