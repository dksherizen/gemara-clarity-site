Topic

Found loan documents: רב אסי, אביי (עדיו בחתומיו זכין לו), and how שמואל explains our משנה

Overview

The עמוד opens with the end of רב אסי's answer. A scribe may write a loan document for a borrower without the lender present only if it is a שטר הקנאה, where the borrower ties his land to the debt from the written date. The גמרא challenges him from our משנה, which refuses to return a found document with אחריות נכסים because it may be dated before the real loan. רב אסי answers that a lost document has become less trustworthy. אביי gives a different reason: עדיו בחתומיו זכין לו, meaning the witnesses' signatures acquire the lien for the lender even in an ordinary document. The גמרא limits this to documents that actually reached the recipient. It then explains that for אביי our משנה worries about repayment and a scheme (פרעון וקנוניא). For שמואל, who does not share that worry, the משנה is about a borrower who denies the debt. A document without a guarantee is still returned, because following רבי מאיר it collects nothing and is only paper to cover the lender's flask. The גמרא then asks why the paper should not go to the borrower instead, and the answer continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

בִּשְׁטָרֵי הַקְנָאָה,

It is talking about deeds of transfer,

דְּהָא שַׁעְבֵּיד נַפְשֵׁיהּ.

since he has obligated himself.

אִי הָכִי, מַתְנִיתִין דְּקָתָנֵי:

If so, our Mishnah, which teaches:

״אִם יֵשׁ בָּהֶן אַחְרָיוּת נְכָסִים לֹא יַחֲזִיר״,

'If they include a property guarantee, he should not return them,'
