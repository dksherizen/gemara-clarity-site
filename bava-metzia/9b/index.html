Bava Metzia 9b

בבא מציעא ט׳ ע״ב

Learn Bava Metzia 9b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Bava Metzia 9b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The moving courtyard (חצר מהלכת), and acquiring a found item or פאה on behalf of someone else (מגו)

Overview

This amud opens by finishing an earlier discussion: riding an animal acquires it in the public domain, and also for an important person, a woman, or a lowly person. Then רבי אלעזר asks whether pulling an animal can acquire the vessels on its back. רבא answers no. Even if the buyer owns the animal, it is a חצר מהלכת, a moving courtyard, and a moving courtyard does not acquire. Standing still does not help, and the הלכה is that it works only when the animal is tied up. Challenges from fish jumping into a boat and a גט thrown into a walking woman's basket are answered: the boat and the basket themselves are resting, and something else moves them. A new משנה follows about a rider who asks his friend to hand him a found item. The גמרא brings the argument between רבי אליעזר and חכמים about gathering פאה for a poor person. עולא explains that argument through one or two steps of מגו, and רב נחמן begins to challenge him from our משנה. The challenge continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וְאִי רְשׁוּת הָרַבִּים הוּא – קָנֵי,

And if it is the public domain, he acquires;

וְאִי אָדָם חָשׁוּב הוּא – קָנֵי,

and if he is an important person, he acquires;

וְאִי אִשָּׁה הִיא – קָנְיָא,

and if she is a woman, she acquires;

וְאִי אִינִישׁ זִילָא הוּא – קָנֵי.

and if he is a lowly person, he acquires.
