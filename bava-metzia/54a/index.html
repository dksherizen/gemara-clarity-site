Topic

The added fifth (חומש): how it is calculated, whether it holds back redemption, and whether it follows the rules of the main payment

Overview

This amud finishes a משנה (from ערכין) about an owner who redeems his own הקדש while someone else bids against him. Its numbers prove that the added fifth (חומש) is counted 'from outside' (מלבר), a quarter of the principal, which matches רבי יאשיה against רבי יונתן. The גמרא then asks whether an unpaid חומש holds back the redemption of מעשר שני. רבינא proves from the rule about דמאי that it does not. The גמרא suggests that רבי אליעזר and רבי יהושע argue about this, but רב פפא explains that their argument is only about worrying the person will be careless. רבי יוחנן's rule about הקדש is challenged from a ברייתא and narrowed so that it applies only on שבת. Finally, רמי בר חמא asks three questions about whether the חומש follows the rules of the main payment. רבא answers from the word ״עליו״ that it does, and רבינא supports him from a משנה about stolen תרומה.

Opening lines

הַבְּעָלִים נוֹתְנִין עֶשְׂרִים וָשֵׁשׁ.

the owner gives twenty-six.

עֶשְׂרִים וּשְׁנַיִם – הַבְּעָלִים נוֹתְנִין עֶשְׂרִים וָשֶׁבַע.

If twenty-two, the owner gives twenty-seven.

בְּעֶשְׂרִים וְשָׁלֹשׁ – הַבְּעָלִים נוֹתְנִין עֶשְׂרִים וּשְׁמוֹנֶה.

If twenty-three, the owner gives twenty-eight.

בְּעֶשְׂרִים וְאַרְבַּע – הַבְּעָלִים נוֹתְנִין עֶשְׂרִים וָתֵשַׁע.

If twenty-four, the owner gives twenty-nine.
