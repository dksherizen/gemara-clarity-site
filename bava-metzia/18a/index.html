Topic

Why a גט cannot serve as a כתובה document, and when found documents such as גיטין may be returned

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the question of why אביי took back his challenge to רבי יוחנן. רבי יוחנן holds that a woman with only a גט still collects her כתובה. The גמרא first rejects two outside sources, the betrothed-widow משנה and the ברייתא of רב חייא בר אמי, because each could be about a written כתובה. The real reason comes from our משנה itself. A גט never mentions the 100 or 200 דינרים, and even if it counted as written, the husband could claim he already paid. Tearing the גט or writing a note on its back does not fix this, since not every payment happens in a בית דין. Next, a new משנה rules that found documents such as גיטין, release documents of slaves, wills, gift documents and receipts are not returned, because the writer may have changed his mind. The גמרא infers that if the writer says 'give', we return them even after a long time. That seems to contradict a משנה in גיטין, and רבה answers that it depends on whether caravans pass through often. The גמרא then limits the worry about a second person with the same name to cases where such a person is known to live in the city. To prove this, it begins a story about a גט found in רב הונא's בית דין, and that story continues on the next amud.
