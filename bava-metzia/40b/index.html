Bava Metzia 40b

בבא מציעא מ׳ ע״ב

Open Bava Metzia 40b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Oil sales: profit limits, deductions for sediment and absorption, mixing sediment, residue, and a guard who moves a deposited barrel

Overview

The גמרא first works out the math of רב יהודה's oil sale. It then shows that his profit fits שמואל's rule that a seller may profit up to one-sixth: the barrel and the sediment he keeps add to his profit, and his labor and the cost of the tap balance it out. Next it returns to the משנה and explains why an old cask counts as absorbing no oil. רב נחמן says the משנה means casks coated with pitch, and אביי says a cask that is already soaked takes in no more. אביי then ties the argument between רבי יהודה and the חכמים about sediment to whether a seller may mix sediment into oil, and he defends רבי יהודה's view using the משנה about selling a צמד. רב פפא reverses אביי's reading of both sides. A ברייתא then compares a buyer and a depositor regarding residue, and the גמרא settles a contradiction by separating a buyer who paid in תשרי from one who paid in ניסן. Finally, a new משנה rules on a guard who moves a deposited barrel that then breaks. The גמרא says it follows רבי ישמעאל, who holds that an item can be returned without the owner knowing. רבי עקיבא's opposing view continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דַּל תְּלָתִין וְשִׁיתָּא בְּשִׁיתָּא,

Subtract thirty-six sold for six each,

פָּשׁוּ לֵיהּ תְּרֵיסַר.

twelve remain for him.

דַּל תְּמָנְיָא שְׁתוּתֵי,

Subtract eight for the sixths,

פָּשׁוּ לְהוּ אַרְבְּעָה.

four remain for them.
