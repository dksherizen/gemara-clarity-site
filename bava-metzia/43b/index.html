Topic

The dispute over how a שומר who misuses a deposit pays, the הלכה about רבי עקיבא, and a new משנה on planning to misuse a deposit and on tilting versus lifting a barrel

Overview

The גמרא first decides that בית הלל's 'כשעת הוצאה' means the value at the moment the שומר took the deposit for himself. That seems to put רבה on the side of בית שמאי. To avoid this, the גמרא tries three explanations of the dispute. The first is whether שליחות יד needs actual loss. The second is whether a שואל שלא מדעת counts as a thief. Each of these would make רבא follow בית שמאי, so the גמרא settles on a third: the schools argue about שבח גזילה, such as wool and newborn young, the same argument as רבי מאיר and רבי יהודה. The wording of the משנה supports this. Next the גמרא turns to רבי עקיבא, who sets the value at the time of the claim. שמואל rules like him, and there is a debate over whether witnesses change his rule. רבי יוחנן says the הלכה is 'always' like רבי עקיבא, and רב אשי explains what 'always' adds, while רבא rules like בית הלל. A new משנה then asks whether merely planning to misuse a deposit creates liability. It also contrasts tilting a barrel with lifting it.
