Topic

מי שפרע: informing or cursing, and how much a down payment (ערבון) acquires

Overview

This amud opens by answering a difficulty against ריש לקיש: the ברייתא follows רבי שמעון. Then the גמרא turns to the משנה's warning of מי שפרע, said to someone who backs out of a deal after the money was paid. אביי says we only inform him of the punishment, and רבא says we actually curse him, and each brings a פסוק. רבא tries to prove his view from a story where רבי יוחנן told רבי חייא בר יוסף to deliver salt or accept מי שפרע. The גמרא rejects the proof and retells the story as a question about a down payment (ערבון). That leads to a disagreement: רב says a down payment acquires only its own value, while רבי יוחנן says it acquires the whole deal. A ברייתא seems to challenge רב, but the גמרא answers that land, which money truly acquires, is different from movable items. Finally, the גמרא suggests that the dispute matches a תנאים argument about collateral and שמיטה, and it begins testing what רבן שמעון בן גמליאל meant. That discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֶלָּא קַשְׁיָא לְרֵישׁ לָקִישׁ!

But it is difficult for Reish Lakish!

אָמַר לָךְ רֵישׁ לָקִישׁ:

Reish Lakish could say to you:

הָא מַנִּי רַבִּי שִׁמְעוֹן הִיא.

Whose view is this? It is Rabbi Shimon's.

אֲבָל אָמְרוּ מִי שֶׁפָּרַע וְכוּ׳.

But they said: He Who exacted payment, etc.
