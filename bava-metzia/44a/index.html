Topic

End of פרק המפקיד (planning to misuse a פקדון, and the barrel cases) and the opening of פרק הזהב on which side of a coin exchange finalizes the deal

Overview

The גמרא asks for the source of the dispute in the משנה about a שומר who only plans to misuse a פקדון. A ברייתא shows that בית שמאי learn from 'על כל דבר פשע' that a plan counts like an act. בית הלל require a real act, and read that פסוק as making him liable when he tells his slave or agent to misuse it. רבה limits the case of a tilted barrel: he pays only for what he took if the barrel broke, but if the wine soured he pays for all of it, since his own act caused that. שמואל says that lifting the barrel in order to take is already enough to make him liable. The גמרא explains this is because he wants the whole barrel as a base for his wine, not because a loss isn't needed. רב אשי asks whether lifting a purse to take one coin works the same way, and the question stays unresolved (תיקו). Then פרק המפקיד ends, and פרק הזהב opens with a משנה listing which side of a trade finalizes the deal: taking the item does, paying money does not. The גמרא begins with רבי changing his wording from 'silver acquires gold' to 'gold acquires silver' and starts to explain his reasoning, which continues on the next amud.
