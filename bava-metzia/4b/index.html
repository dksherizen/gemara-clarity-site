Bava Metzia 4b

בבא מציעא ד׳ ע״ב

Topic

Testing whether הילך is exempt from a שבועה, using a ברייתא about an unnumbered שטר and a משנה about claims of vessels and land.

Overview

The גמרא continues its question of whether a defendant who says הֵילָךְ ('here you are') is exempt from a שבועה. It brings a ברייתא about a שטר that says only 'סלעים' or 'דינרין', which proves at least two. The lender claims five and the borrower admits three. רבי שמעון בן אלעזר says he swears as a מודה במקצת, and רבי עקיבא says he is like a מֵשִׁיב אֲבֵידָה and is פטור. The גמרא first tries to prove from רבי שמעון בן אלעזר that הילך פטור. It ends up saying that admitting two is פטור only because the שטר backs the borrower up, or because a שטר creates a lien on land and no oath is taken over land. A second version reads רבי עקיבא to prove the opposite, that הילך חייב. The גמרא shows by logic that רבי עקיבא exempts even someone who admits two, and that this still does not refute רבי חייא. Finally מר זוטרא tries to prove הילך חייב from a משנה about claims of vessels and land. The גמרא answers that the land case was chosen to teach זוקקין, an oath on movables pulling in the land, and that שבועות, not קידושין, is that rule's main home.

  13. ResolutionA land-backed debt carries no oath
