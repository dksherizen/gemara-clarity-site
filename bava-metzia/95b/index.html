Topic

The exemption of בעליו עמו: whether it covers a שומר חנם, the refutation of רב המנונא's strict conditions, and how אביי derives from the פסוקים that the time of borrowing is what counts.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a rejected proof. Our משנה lists only the שואל, whose exemption when the owner is with him is written openly in the Torah, so its silence about a שומר חנם proves nothing. A ברייתא about borrowing or renting an animal together with its owner is then tried as a proof. It is pushed away, because it could follow רבי מאיר, or a reversed version of the opinions, where a שוכר counts as a שומר חנם. Next, רב המנונא rules that the borrower is exempt only if the owner does the same work as his animal, the whole time from the borrowing until the mishap. רבא challenges him from the same ברייתא, and its wording shows the owner can be doing different work. Two more ברייתות show that being with the borrower at the time of borrowing, or even for one moment after the handover, is enough. So רב המנונא is refuted. Finally, the גמרא begins explaining how the פסוקים teach this: אביי reads them like רבי יאשיה, and the time of borrowing turns out to be what counts.

Opening lines

דִּכְתִיבָא בְּהֶדְיָא – קָתָנֵי,

What is written explicitly – he teaches,

דְּאָתְיָא מִדְּרָשָׁא – לָא קָתָנֵי.

what comes from interpretation – he does not teach.

תָּא שְׁמַע: שְׁאָלָהּ וְשָׁאַל בְּעָלֶיהָ עִמָּהּ,

Come and hear: If he borrowed it and borrowed its owner with it,

שְׂכָרָהּ וְשָׂכַר בְּעָלֶיהָ עִמָּהּ,

rented it and hired its owner with it,
