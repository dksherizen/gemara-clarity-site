Topic

Hidden forms of רבית: mortgaged fields, רבא's three rejected practices, and half-profit partnerships in the new משנה

Overview

This amud finishes the argument over whether a lender may hold a mortgaged field (a משכנתא) and eat its produce for a fixed yearly deduction from the debt. The side that permits it compares it to redeeming a שדה אחוזה from הקדש at a fixed price. The side that forbids it answers that a loan between people looks like רבית. רב אשי then brings two teachings from the elders of מתא מחסיא: an unspecified משכנתא lasts at least a year, and the field 'resides' with the lender, which matters for the law of בר מצרא. Next, רבא rejects three practices: רב פפא's higher price for delayed payment, the documents of מחוזא that wrote expected profit into the loan, and the leases of נרש where the borrower rented back his own mortgaged field. Along the way, רב אשי shows why even אמימר's careful use of such documents fails once the lender dies, and a newer lease wording is rejected. A new משנה then forbids half-profit deals with a storekeeper or an animal raiser unless the worker is paid, but allows taking calves and foals to raise for half without an appraisal. The גמרא begins by explaining that the wage is that of a פועל בטל and asks what that means.

Opening lines

קָא פָרֵיק לַהּ בְּאַרְבְּעָה זוּזֵי,

he redeems it for four zuz [a year],

הָכָא נָמֵי לָא שְׁנָא.

here too, it is no different.

וּמַאן דְּאָסַר – אָמַר לָךְ:

And the one who forbids will tell you:

שְׂדֵה אֲחוּזָּה הֶקְדֵּשׁ הִיא,

an ancestral field is consecrated property,
