Bava Metzia 108b

בבא מציעא ק״ח ע״ב

Topic

The limits of דינא דבר מצרא, the bordering neighbor's right of first purchase: the cases it does not cover, the order of priority among buyers, and who counts as a bordering neighbor.

Overview

This amud is about דינא דבר מצרא, the right of a bordering neighbor to buy a field before an outsider. It opens by finishing an earlier case: a buyer who buys a plain plot in the middle of someone's land is using a trick to get around this right. Then the גמרא lists cases the right does not cover. These include a gift (unless it comes with an אחריות guarantee, says אמימר), selling all one's property to one buyer, selling back to the original owners, buying from or selling to a גוי, and selling a משכנתא to the lender who holds it. They also include trading a field up for a closer or better one, urgent sales for taxes, food support or burial, and sales to a woman, orphans or partners. A Jew who sells to a גוי is put in שמתא until he accepts responsibility for any harm the גוי causes the neighbor. Next comes an order of priority: city-side neighbors before field-side neighbors, and a תלמיד חכם before a שכן or a קרוב. A שכן comes before a קרוב, based on a פסוק in משלי. The neighbor's right also gives way to better or readier money and to a buyer who will build homes. We wait for a neighbor who must fetch money only if he is known to be well-off. Last, the גמרא covers split ownership of land and houses or trees, barriers between fields, and four neighbors with equal rights.

Opening lines

וְאִי לָא,

And if not,

אִיעָרוֹמֵי קָא מַעֲרֵים.

he is surely using a trick.

מַתָּנָה לֵית בָּהּ

A gift is not subject

מִשּׁוּם דִּינָא דְּבַר מִצְרָא.

to the law of the neighbor.
