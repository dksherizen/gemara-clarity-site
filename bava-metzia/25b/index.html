Bava Metzia 25b

בבא מציעא כ״ה ע״ב

Topic

Coins and their סימנים, items that look deliberately placed (ספק הינוח), a vessel in a garbage heap, and items found in walls

Overview

The גמרא asks what the law is when found coins are stacked like the stones of בית קוליס, and a ברייתא rules that the finder must announce them. A second ברייתא teaches that a coin has no usable סימן. Even the claimant's name on it proves nothing, because he may have spent it and someone else dropped it. A new משנה then rules that tied young birds near a fence or on a field path must not be touched. The גמרא explains that they look deliberately placed and have no סימן. Neither the common wing-knot nor the place works as a סימן, since the birds hop. That makes the case a ספק הינוח, and רב rules that such an item should not be taken, but if it was taken it need not be returned. The משנה's rule that a covered vessel in a garbage heap is left alone seems to clash with a ברייתא. רב זביד resolves this by the size of the item, and רב פפא resolves it by the type of heap. The amud ends with a new משנה about items found in rubble or in walls, and a ברייתא saying the finder can claim they belonged to the אמוריים. The גמרא's answer to the challenge against that ברייתא continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

כְּאַבְנֵי בֵּית קוּלִיס, מַהוּ?

Like the stones of בית קוליס, what is the law?

תָּא שְׁמַע, דְּתַנְיָא:

Come and hear, as it was taught in a ברייתא:

מָצָא מָעוֹת מְפוּזָּרוֹת –

If he found scattered coins –

הֲרֵי אֵלּוּ שֶׁלּוֹ,

these belong to him;
