Bava Metzia 79b

בבא מציעא ע״ט ע״ב

Topic

Rental disputes over boats and donkeys: unfinished trips, added cargo, what may be loaded, and who may ride

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the discussion of רבי נתן's ruling about a rented boat that could not finish its trip. רב פפא explains that the money stays where it is only when both a specific boat and specific wine were agreed on; if neither was specified, the two sides split the fee. Next comes a ברייתא about a renter who 'unloads' the boat halfway: he pays for half the trip, and the owner has only a תרעומת (grievance). After a back-and-forth, the גמרא rereads the case: the renter loaded extra cargo midway. The owner's grievance is about the change from the deal or the extra rope. A second ברייתא sets what the renter and the donkey driver may load on a rented donkey. רב פפא explains the difference by their usual habits: drivers buy food at each stop, renters do not. A third ברייתא teaches which riders are allowed on a donkey rented for a man or for a woman, and רב פפא explains its mention of a pregnant woman as one who is also nursing. אביי learns from this that a fish's weight goes by its belly, which matters when buying and selling.

Opening lines

נֵימָא לֵיהּ:
