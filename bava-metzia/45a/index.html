Topic

Is gold counted as money for redeeming מעשר שני and for loans? The גמרא looks at רבי יוחנן's view and at the argument between בית שמאי and בית הלל about swapping silver for gold.

Overview

The גמרא starts with רבי יוחנן's rule that one may not borrow a gold dinar and repay a gold dinar. It first tries to prove from this that רבי יוחנן holds gold cannot redeem מעשר שני according to בית שמאי. It rejects that proof: loans are different, because in business the Rabbis treat gold like merchandise. רבין then reports that רבי יוחנן himself allowed redeeming with gold. Next comes another version, an argument between רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש. One says בית שמאי and בית הלל argue only about swapping silver סלעים for gold דינרים, based on the idea of 'first money and not second money.' The other says they argue even about redeeming produce onto gold. Two משניות about changing a סלע are used to challenge the first opinion. רבא pushes off the case in ירושלים, but the case outside ירושלים forces the גמרא to agree that everyone allows 'second money.' The גמרא then begins restating the argument with a new reason, a protective rule of בית שמאי, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אָסוּר לִלְווֹת דִּינָר בְּדִינָר.

It is forbidden to borrow a dinar for a dinar.

דִּינָר דְּמַאי?

A dinar of what?

אִילֵּימָא דִּינָר שֶׁל כֶּסֶף בְּדִינָר שֶׁל כֶּסֶף,

If you say a silver dinar for a silver dinar,

לְגַבֵּי נַפְשֵׁיהּ

relative to itself,
