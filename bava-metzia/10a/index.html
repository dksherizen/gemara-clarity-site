Topic

Acquiring a lost item for someone else, and acquiring through a person's four אמות

Overview

This amud finishes the argument between רב נחמן and עולא over whether picking up a lost item for someone else gets it for him. עולא explains the rider משנה as a case where the finder said he took it for himself from the start, and the two argue over the extra word תחילה. Next, רב נחמן and רב חסדא rule that lifting a מציאה for a friend does not acquire it, because it takes the item away from everyone else. רבא challenges this from a hired worker. רב נחמן answers that a worker's hand is like the boss's hand, even though a worker may quit, since Jews are servants of ה׳. רבי יוחנן disagrees and says the friend does acquire. A new משנה teaches that falling on a מציאה does not acquire it. ריש לקיש says a person's four אמות acquire for him, and the גמרא defends this against a challenge from the משנה in פאה: by falling, the poor person showed he did not want to acquire through his four אמות.

Opening lines

מַתְנִיתִין מַנִּי – רַבָּנַן הִיא.

Our משנה, whose view is it? It is the Rabbis.

אֶלָּא אִי אָמְרַתְּ

But if you say

בְּעָשִׁיר וְעָנִי מַחְלוֹקֶת,

that the dispute is about a rich person and a poor person,

אֲבָל מֵעָנִי לְעָנִי

but when a poor person gathers for a poor person
