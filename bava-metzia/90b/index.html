Bava Metzia 90b

בבא מציעא צ׳ ע״ב

Learn Bava Metzia 90b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Bava Metzia 90b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The קנס for castrating through a נכרי, the limits of the ban on muzzling a threshing animal, and whether speech counts as an action for מלקות.

Overview

The amud opens at the end of a question about Jewish oxen that non-Jews 'steal' and castrate. The answer is that the owners used a trick, so they are penalized with a forced sale. רב פפא says this proves nothing about אמירה לנכרי in general, because the askers held like רבי חידקא that בני נח may not castrate either, so the owners broke לפני עור. אביי rejects רבא's idea that the sale must be for slaughter only. The גמרא then discusses whether selling to a son counts. Next, רמי בר חמא asks a list of questions about indirect ways of keeping a threshing animal from eating, like a thorn, a lion, its baby, thirst, or a cover over the grain. An attempted proof from a ברייתא about straw is rejected. רבי סימאי proves from the law of a כהן drinking wine that muzzling before threshing is also forbidden, because what counts is the animal's state while it threshes. Finally, a ברייתא gives מלקות only to the one who threshes or leads, and רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש argue whether muzzling or leading by voice counts as an action. רבי יוחנן's objection to ריש לקיש begins at the end of the amud and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דְּגָנְבִין אַרְמָאֵי

[oxen] that non-Jews steal

וּמְגַנְּחִין יָתְהוֹן, מַהוּ?

and castrate them — what is the law?

שְׁלַח לְהוּ:

He sent to them:

הַעֲרָמָה אִתְעֲבִיד בְּהוּ,

A trick was done with them;

The steps (24)

  1. QuestionThe question about oxen castrated by non-Jews
  2. ResponseThey used a trick, so trick them back and make them sell
  3. Rejectionרב פפא: no proof, because non-Jews are also forbidden to castrate
  4. Statementרבא: they must be sold for slaughter
  5. Rejectionאביי: forcing the sale is penalty enough
  6. QuestionCan he sell the animal to his son?
  7. Responseרב אחי forbids, רב אשי permits
  8. ConclusionPious Sages traded such oxen with each other
  9. QuestionA thorn placed in the animal's mouth
  10. ChallengePlacing it yourself is obviously muzzling
  11. ResolutionReworded: a thorn got stuck by itself
  12. QuestionA lion crouching outside
  13. QuestionMore cases: its baby, thirst, a leather cover
  14. ProofTrying to answer one question from a ברייתא
  15. RejectionThe straw case is different: the animal still eats
  16. RejectionAnother reading: the straw is given before threshing
  17. QuestionMuzzling the animal before threshing starts
  18. ProofA parallel from a כהן drinking wine
  19. RejectionThe next פסוק shows the timing of drinking is not what counts
  20. ResponseWhat counts is the state during the act
  21. StatementWho gets מלקות for muzzling and for mixed animals
  22. StatementMuzzling or leading with the voice: a dispute
  23. StatementThe reasons: is moving the mouth an action?
