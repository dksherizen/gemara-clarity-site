Bava Metzia 8a

בבא מציעא ח׳ ע״א

Learn Bava Metzia 8a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Bava Metzia 8a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Dividing a disputed item's value, and whether lifting a found item for a friend acquires it for him (רמי בר חמא vs. רבא).

Overview

This amud opens by asking whether two people who split a disputed item really cut it in half. A plain טלית can still clothe small children, and a gold-threaded one can still clothe the children of kings. But a non-kosher animal would be ruined if cut, so the גמרא concludes that 'dividing' means splitting the item's value. Next, רמי בר חמא learns from the משנה that if you lift a found item for a friend, the friend acquires it. רבא disagrees: in the משנה each person acquires for himself, and because of that he also acquires for the other (מגו דזכי לנפשיה זכי נמי לחבריה). רבא proves this from partners who steal together. The גמרא then works through רבא's ruling about a חרש and a פקח who lift an item together, and concludes that neither acquires. Then רב אחא asks which line of the משנה רמי בר חמא used. After several lines are tried and rejected, the last clause about dividing without an oath is offered, though רבא explains it through his מגו. The amud ends as רב יוסף begins a teaching from רב יהודה about two riding an animal; the teaching itself continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

דְּחַזְיָא לִקְטַנִּים.

Because it is fit for small children.

וְהָא דְּאָמַר רָבָא:

But that which רבא said:

אִם הָיְתָה טַלִּית מוּזְהֶבֶת – חוֹלְקִין,

If the garment was woven with gold, they divide it,

הָכִי נָמֵי דְּפָלְגִי לַהּ,

does that also mean they cut it up?
