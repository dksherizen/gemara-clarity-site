Topic

Why the משנה of שנים אוחזין needs both cases, and whether it fits בן ננס and סומכוס

Overview

The גמרא explains why the משנה teaches two cases, a found item and a purchase. Each case has its own excuse a person might use to grab dishonestly, so neither case could be learned from the other. It then asks how a purchase dispute could happen at all. The answer is that the seller took money from both people and forgot which payment he truly accepted. Next, the גמרא asks whether our משנה disagrees with בן ננס, who objects to oaths where one must be false. It answers that here both oaths could be true, since the two may have lifted the item together. Finally, it tests our משנה against סומכוס, who splits doubtful money without an oath. It defends the משנה first by limiting his rule to שמא ושמא, then by limiting it to cases of דררא דממונא. A new קל וחומר against that last answer begins and continues on the next amud.
