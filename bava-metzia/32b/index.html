Topic

Is preventing צער בעלי חיים (animal suffering) a Torah law (דאורייתא)?

Overview

This amud asks whether the duty to prevent צער בעלי חיים, animal suffering, comes from the Torah. First the גמרא tries to prove it is דאורייתא from the קל וחומר of loading to unloading, and from רבי יוסי הגלילי's ruling about an overloaded animal. Each proof is pushed back. Then the גמרא tries to prove the opposite from the רישא of the משנה, where the passerby is פטור if the owner sits down. That proof is answered: he may only be exempt from helping for free. A סימן then lists five more sources: בהמת, בהמת, אוהב, שונא, רבצן. Proofs come from a גוי's animal, from יין נסך, from a ברייתא about whose animal and whose load, from choosing between a friend and an enemy, and from limiting 'enemy' to a Jewish enemy. Each one is answered by איבה, by saying the case is about loading, by saying a ברייתא follows only רבי יוסי הגלילי, or by the value of overcoming one's יצר הרע. The amud ends as the גמרא begins the last source, רבצן, which continues on the next amud.
