Bava Metzia 39b

בבא מציעא ל״ט ע״ב

Topic

Managing property of minors and captives, and רב חסדא's ruling against a violent heir

Overview

The גמרא finishes the rule that a relative who manages a קטן's land never gains a חזקה, even after the קטן grows up. It suggests three limits to the rule אין מורידין קרוב לנכסי קטן, and then rejects all of them. Next comes a case: an old woman and one daughter were taken captive, and another daughter died and left a small child. אביי shows that neither the free sister nor the child can simply manage the property. He splits it, giving half to the sister and putting a steward over the other half. רבא says one steward should manage it all. When news comes that the grandmother died, they disagree the same way about the captive daughter's third. Finally, רב חסדא judges a man who claims to be the brother of the powerful מרי בר איסק. He puts the burden of proof on מרי, because frightened witnesses may stay silent but will not lie. Once the brother is confirmed, he shares even in the orchards מרי planted.

Opening lines

וַאֲפִילּוּ הִגְדִּיל.

And even if he grew up.

וְלָא אֲמַרַן אֶלָּא בְּאַחֵי דְאַבָּא,

And we only said this about brothers from the father's side,

אֲבָל בְּאַחֵי דְאִמָּא לֵית לַן בַּהּ.

but with brothers from the mother's side, we have no problem with it.

וַאֲחֵי דְאַבָּא נָמֵי לָא אֲמַרַן אֶלָּא בְּאַרְעָתָא,

And even with brothers from the father's side, we only said it about fields,
