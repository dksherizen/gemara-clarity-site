Topic

Misleading buyers by dressing up goods (end of פרק הזהב), and the start of the laws of interest: נשך and תרבית

Overview

This amud first finishes the laws of misleading a buyer. The חכמים who allow sifting beans are identified as רבי אחא, who permits a change the buyer can plainly see. A ברייתא lists forbidden tricks that make animals and meat look better than they are. Several אמוראים allowed decorating goods, and the גמרא reconciles this with the משנה: new goods may be decorated, but old goods may not. A story about an old slave who dyed his hair shows what adorning a person means. After the Hadran for פרק הזהב, a new chapter opens with a משנה defining נשך and תרבית, the two Torah words for interest. The גמרא tries to find a case of one without the other, and רבא concludes that they always come together, so every loan with interest breaks two לאוין. A ברייתא then shows from the פסוקים that both נשך and ריבית apply to money and to food.

Opening lines

מִשּׁוּם דְּקָא מַרְוַוח לְתַרְעָא:

Because he eases the market rate.

וְלֹא יָבוֹר אֶת הַגְּרִיסִין,

And he may not sift the ground beans,

דִּבְרֵי אַבָּא שָׁאוּל.

these are the words of Abba Shaul.

וַחֲכָמִים מַתִּירִין וְכוּ׳.

And the Rabbis permit it, etc.
