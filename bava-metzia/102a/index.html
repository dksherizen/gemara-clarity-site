Bava Metzia 102a

בבא מציעא ק״ב ע״א

Open Bava Metzia 102a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The renter's מזוזה, who owns manure in a rented courtyard, and whether a חצר acquires for its owner without his knowledge

Overview

The amud opens with the end of an answer about putting a מזוזה inside a hollow reed. A ברייתא then teaches that a renter must put up a מזוזה and may not take it when he leaves a Jewish landlord's house, and רב ששת explains that the story of the punished man goes with that rule. Next the גמרא explains the משנה's ruling that manure belongs to the landlord: it is about strangers' oxen in the landlord's courtyard. This supports רבי יוסי ברבי חנינא, who says a person's חצר acquires for him even without his knowledge. The גמרא defends his rule against several challenges: lost items in an unguarded courtyard, manure caught from the air (answered by אביי and by רבא), and doves' eggs that are still subject to שילוח הקן. Along the way, רב אשי learns that renting out a courtyard without details does not include its barn. The eggs discussion ends with a minor who took them, whose father must return them because of דרכי שלום. Finally a new משנה teaches who gains when a rented year becomes a leap year, and it begins a story from ציפורי that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בְּגוּבְתָּא דְּקַנְיָא.

Inside a hollow reed.

תָּנוּ רַבָּנַן:

The Rabbis taught:

הַמַּשְׂכִּיר בַּיִת לַחֲבֵירוֹ –

One who rents out a house to his friend,

עַל הַשּׂוֹכֵר לַעֲשׂוֹת לוֹ מְזוּזָה,

it is upon the renter to make a mezuza for it.
