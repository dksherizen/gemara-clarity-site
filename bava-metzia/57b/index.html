Topic

אונאה and רבית with הקדש, and why land, slaves, documents and הקדש are excluded from double payment and the watchman laws.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a statement: with הקדש, even an overcharge smaller than the usual measure of אונאה lets the sale be undone. A ברייתא saying 'no רבית and no אונאה for הקדש' seems to contradict this. The גמרא reads it as meaning that only the ordinary one-sixth rules of אונאה do not apply. It explains that the ברייתא's 'stringency of the הדיוט' refers only to רבית, and that הקדש has too many stringencies to name just one. The גמרא then asks what a case of רבית with הקדש would look like. רב הושעיא answers with a flour-supply deal where הקדש always gets the better price, and רב פפא answers with building stones that are still חולין. Next, ברייתות use the pattern of כלל ופרט וכלל to show that land, slaves and documents have no double payment and are outside the laws of the unpaid and paid watchman. הקדש is left out of these laws by the word 'רעהו'. Finally, the גמרא begins a contradiction to the rule about הקדש from a משנה about shekels sent to the Temple that were stolen or lost.

Opening lines

דַּאֲפִילּוּ פָּחוֹת מִכְּדֵי אוֹנָאָה חוֹזֵר.

that even if it is less than the measure of exploitation, he may go back on the sale.

מֵיתִיבִי: רִבִּית וְאוֹנָאָה לַהֶדְיוֹט,

They raised an objection: Interest and exploitation apply to a layman,

וְאֵין רִבִּית וְאוֹנָאָה לַהֶקְדֵּשׁ!

but interest and exploitation do not apply to consecrated property!

מִי אַלִּימָא מִמַּתְנִיתִין
