Topic

Why a house smaller than four by four אמות is not treated as a real house, and lowering a too-tall סוכה

Overview

This amud explains a ברייתא that lists the הלכות that do not apply to a house smaller than four by four אמות. The גמרא first asks whether the ברייתא follows only רבי, who requires four by four for a סוכה. It answers that even the רבנן agree: a סוכה is a temporary home and can be small, but a permanent house must be four by four or no one would live in it. Torah laws such as מזוזה and מעקה do not apply to it, because they say בית and this is not a בית. Rabbinic עירוב rules leave it out because it is not fit to live in, though the food of a שיתוף may still be placed there, since it is no worse than a courtyard. A tiny house also cannot join two cities, because unlike a watchman's hut it does not serve its purpose. It gets no share when a courtyard is divided, because it is expected to be torn down. Finally, the גמרא returns to the משנה and rules that cushions and blankets on the floor do not lower a סוכה that is taller than twenty אמות.

Opening lines

וְאֵין עוֹשִׂין אוֹתוֹ עִיבּוּר

And one does not make it an extension

בֵּין שְׁתֵּי עֲיָירוֹת,

between two towns,

וְאֵין הָאַחִין וְהַשּׁוּתָּפִין חוֹלְקִין בּוֹ.

and brothers and partners do not divide it.

לֵימָא רַבִּי הִיא וְלָא רַבָּנַן?

Shall we say it is Rabbi and not the Rabbis?

The steps (24)

  1. StatementThe last two rules of the ברייתא about a tiny house
  2. QuestionDoes this ברייתא only follow רבי?
  3. RejectionEven the רבנן agree a real house needs four by four
  4. StatementQuoting the Torah-based rules from the ברייתא
  5. ResponseReason: the Torah uses the word בית
  6. StatementQuoting the rabbinic עירוב rules from the ברייתא
  7. ResponseReason: it is not fit to live in
  8. ConclusionOnly עירובי חצירות is ruled out; a שיתוף may be placed there
  9. QuestionWhy can a שיתוף be placed in a tiny house?
  10. ResponseA tiny house is no worse than a courtyard in the alley
  11. ProofSource: each עירוב goes in its own area
  12. ChallengeAn עירוב חצירות in a courtyard? Another source says no
  13. ResolutionCorrected reading: a house in the courtyard, a courtyard in the alley
  14. ConclusionSo a tiny house can hold the שיתוף
  15. StatementA tiny house does not extend a city's boundary
  16. ResponseNot even counted like a watchman's hut
  17. QuestionWhy is it worse than a hut?
  18. ResponseHuts fit their purpose; the tiny house does not
  19. StatementBrothers and partners do not divide a tiny house
  20. ChallengeA משנה requires 4 אמות for each partner
  21. ResolutionReading the ברייתא as being about the courtyard
  22. StatementHow a courtyard is divided: by its doorways
  23. ConclusionA house meant to be torn down gets no courtyard share
  24. StatementCushions and blankets do not lower a too-tall סוכה
