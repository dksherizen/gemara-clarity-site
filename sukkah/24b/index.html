Topic

Writing a גט on an animal (רבי יוסי הגלילי vs. רבנן), and trees as סוכה walls that must withstand a normal wind

Overview

This amud opens with the closing line of an earlier discussion: a living animal used as a סוכה wall counts as a מחיצה made by a person. The גמרא then quotes רבי יוסי הגלילי, who says a גט may not be written on an animal. His source is a ברייתא: "וכתב לה" allows any material, but "ספר" limits it to things that, like a scroll, are not alive and are not food. רבנן read "ספר" as ספירת דברים, a written account, so for them a גט may be written on anything. The גמרא then works out how each side uses "וכתב", "ספר כריתות" and the longer form "כריתות". These sources teach that money cannot divorce and that a גט must fully cut the bond. A new משנה rules that trees can serve as the walls of a סוכה. רב אחא בר יעקב says a wall that cannot stand in a normal wind is not a wall. The גמרא reconciles this rule with the משנה and with two other sources by saying the trees were hard and their branches tied down. It ends with a challenge from רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

הֲרֵי עֲשׂוּיָה בִּידֵי אָדָם.

Behold, it is made by human hands.

אָמַר מָר.

The Master said:

מִשּׁוּם רַבִּי יוֹסֵי הַגְּלִילִי אָמְרוּ:

In the name of Rabbi Yosei HaGelili they said:

אַף אֵין כּוֹתְבִין עָלָיו גִּיטֵּי נָשִׁים.

Also, one may not write bills of divorce for women on it (the animal).
