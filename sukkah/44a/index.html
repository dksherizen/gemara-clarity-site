Topic

לולב on שבת today, why לולב but not ערבה is done seven days זכר למקדש, and the source and status of the ערבה מצוה

Overview

The גמרא first rules that today, even in ארץ ישראל, the לולב does not override שבת. It reinterprets two sources about bringing לולבים on ערב שבת so that both describe the time of the מקדש. Then אביי asks רבא why we take the לולב all seven days זכר למקדש but not the ערבה. רבא's first answer is rejected. רב זביד then says in רבא's name that לולב is דאורייתא and ערבה is דרבנן. That wording is attacked, because for both אבא שאול and the רבנן the ערבה of the מקדש is more than rabbinic. So it is re-worded: the לולב has a written Torah root even in the גבולין, while the ערבה does not. Next, ריש לקיש rules that blemished כהנים may enter for the ערבה, and רבי יוחנן questions how and by whom the מצוה is done. Finally, the גמרא sorts out רבי יוחנן's conflicting statements on whether the ערבה is from סיני, a יסוד נביאים, or the חכמים' own enactment. The last answer begins with לא קשיא and continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אָמְרִי:
