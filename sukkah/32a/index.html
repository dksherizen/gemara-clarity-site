Topic

Defects that make a לולב פסול, the source for binding the לולב in the word כפות, and the palms of הר הברזל

Overview

This עמוד goes through the physical defects that can make a לולב פסול. It starts at the end of a ברייתא that lists the flaws: thorny, split, curved like a sickle, or hardened. רב פפא explains that 'split' means split like a fork. רבא says a forward curve disqualifies the לולב, but a backward curve is natural and is fine. רב נחמן's ruling on a sideways curve is reported in two versions. רבא adds that a לולב with leaves on only one side is a בעל מום. Next, רב פפא defines the משנה's words נפרצו and נפרדו. He then asks about a split תיומת, which one version of רבי יהושע בן לוי rules is פסול. From the word כפות, the גמרא learns that a לולב should be bound, and it uses this to show that the מצוה is one young לולב, not a hardened branch, the trunk, a thorny branch, two branches, or a single leaf. Finally, אביי rules that palms of הר הברזל are כשר only when their leaves reach one another, and a ברייתא confirms this.

קָווּץ, סָדוּק,

curved like a sickle — unfit.

Hardened like wood — unfit.

דּוֹמֶה לְחָרוּת — כָּשֵׁר.

Merely resembling hardened — fit.
