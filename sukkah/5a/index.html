Topic

Ten טפחים as a separate domain: רבי יוסי's ברייתא, and the source for the כפורת being one טפח

Overview

The גמרא wants to show that ten טפחים off the ground is a real boundary, which is why a סוכה must be at least ten טפחים tall. It brings a ברייתא where רבי יוסי says the שכינה never came down to earth and משה and אליהו never went up to heaven. Five פסוקים seem to say otherwise. Each time the answer is the same: the שכינה stayed above ten טפחים, and משה and אליהו stayed below ten. Next the גמרא checks the calculation that the ארון (nine טפחים) plus the כפורת (one טפח) equals ten. The ארון's height is written in the תורה, but the כפורת's thickness is not. רבי חנינא teaches that we learn it from the smallest measurement among the vessels, the one-טפח frame (מסגרת) of the שלחן. The גמרא asks why we don't use the larger vessels, the smaller ציץ, or the tiny זר. In the end it rules that we compare only items whose measurements are written in the תורה. רב הונא then offers a different source: the 'face' of the כפורת, since no face is less than a טפח. The גמרא begins to question that proof, and the question continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

וְתַנְיָא, רַבִּי יוֹסֵי אוֹמֵר:

And it was taught: Rabbi Yosei says:

מֵעוֹלָם לֹא יָרְדָה שְׁכִינָה לְמַטָּה,

The Divine Presence never came down below,

וְלֹא עָלוּ מֹשֶׁה וְאֵלִיָּהוּ לַמָּרוֹם,

and Moses and Elijah never went up on high,

שֶׁנֶּאֱמַר: ״הַשָּׁמַיִם שָׁמַיִם לַה׳

as it says: "The heavens are the heavens of God,
