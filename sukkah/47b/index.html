Succah 47b

Topic

Saying זמן (שהחיינו) on שמיני עצרת, whether it needs a כוס, and whether רבי יהודה requires לינה on שמיני; the final ruling follows רב נחמן.

Overview

The גמרא tries to use a ברייתא to support רבי יוחנן, who says we recite זמן (שהחיינו) on שמיני עצרת. The proof is pushed off: the ברייתא's 'blessing' may just mean mentioning the day in ברכת המזון and תפלה. Along the way the גמרא asks whether זמן needs a cup of wine. It tries to support רב נחמן, who says זמן can be said even in the marketplace, but that proof is also pushed off. Next the גמרא asks whether רבי יהודה really holds that שמיני requires לינה, since his own six-day rule seems to exclude it. It answers that the rule only excludes פסח שני. It tries to back this up from a משנה about ביכורים, but that משנה might follow רבי אליעזר בן יעקב, who learns waving through a גזירה שוה from שלמים. Finally, רב נחמן and רב ששת argue about זמן on שמיני. The הלכה follows רב נחמן: we say זמן. A supporting ברייתא begins at the end and continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

מַאי לָאו, זְמַן?

What, is it not the blessing of 'time'?

לֹא, בִּרְכַּת הַמָּזוֹן וּתְפִלָּה.

No, it means Grace after Meals and prayer.

הָכִי נָמֵי מִסְתַּבְּרָא,

So too it is reasonable,

דְּאִי סָלְקָא דַעְתָּךְ זְמַן —

for if it entered your mind that it means 'time',
