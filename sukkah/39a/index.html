Topic

Pausing within phrases (ברוך הבא, יהא שמיה רבא), customs and the ברכה for הלל with the rule of עובר לעשייתן, and buying a לולב and אתרוג during שביעית without giving holy money to an עם הארץ.

Overview

This amud finishes the discussion about pausing in the middle of a phrase. רבא says not to split 'יהא שמיה רבא' from 'מברך' in קדיש, and רב ספרא rejects him: the completing word makes the meaning clear either way. The גמרא then explains two lines of the משנה about הלל. For repeating verses, a ברייתא says רבי repeated verses and רבי אלעזר בן פרטא added more, which אביי explains as repeating from 'אודך' onward. For the ברכה, אביי says custom decides only the ברכה after הלל. The ברכה before it is a real מצוה, based on שמואל's rule that every מצוה is blessed 'עובר לעשייתן', and three פסוקים prove that 'עובר' means 'before'. A new משנה then teaches that during שביעית a buyer pays for the לולב and gets the אתרוג as a gift. The גמרא explains that if the seller refuses, the אתרוג's price is hidden in the לולב's price. The reason is that holy שביעית money may not be handed to an עם הארץ beyond three meals' worth. The ברייתא's fix for money already handed over continues on the next amud.

מֹשֶׁה, שַׁפִּיר קָאָמְרַתְּ?!
