Topic

Canopies inside a סוכה, and whether cutting attached vines (or ציצית strings) counts as 'making' them

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an earlier point: a בית counts as its own tent even under ten טפחים, because it is fixed. The גמרא then brings another version of שמואל's ruling, that a bridal canopy is allowed in the סוכה because it has no roof, and defends it against two ברייתות. One ברייתא is limited to a canopy with a roof. The other is answered by noting that two bed posts (נקליטין) are fixed, so at ten טפחים they make a tent even without a roof. Next, רבה בר רב הונא permits sleeping under any canopy, even one with a roof that is ten טפחים high. He follows רבי יהודה, who holds that a temporary tent does not cancel the סוכה. A new משנה then teaches that attached vines under the סכך make a סוכה unfit unless the vines are cut, and it gives the general rule for valid סכך. The גמרא debates whether cutting the vines is enough. שמואל requires shaking them as well, while רב holds that cutting itself counts as 'making', as he ruled for uncut ציצית strings. The amud ends with a challenge from a ברייתא about ציצית, whose answer continues on the next amud.

וּבַיִת נָמֵי,

And a house too,

since it is fixed,
