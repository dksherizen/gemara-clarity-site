Topic

Why the ערבה in the מקדש overrides שבת on the seventh day, and why it does not override שבת today

Overview

This amud opens by finishing the source that a person must sit in the סוכה at night as well as by day, through a גזירה שוה from the word "תשבו" in the מילואים. Then the גמרא turns to the משנה's rule that the ערבה in the מקדש overrides שבת on the seventh day. רבי יוחנן explains that this publicizes that the ערבה is a Torah-level מצוה. A chain of challenges then explains why the לולב does not do the same, why the ערבה is not blocked by a decree, and why it overrides on the seventh day and not every day, the first day, or a middle day. Next the גמרא asks why the ערבה does not override שבת today. In בבל the reason is doubt about the calendar, and travelers from ארץ ישראל disagree about whether the seventh day even falls on שבת there. רב יוסף suggests the ערבה מצוה was only standing the branches by the מזבח, but אביי challenges him. A ברייתא about the בייתוסין then refutes רב יוסף and shows the ערבה was taken in hand. The amud ends with a new answer, that ארץ ישראל matches the practice of חוץ לארץ. That answer is challenged from the first day of סוכות, and the discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

״תֵּשְׁבוּ״ ״תֵּשְׁבוּ״ — לִגְזֵרָה שָׁוָה.

"You shall sit," "you shall sit": for a verbal analogy.

נֶאֱמַר כָּאן ״תֵּשְׁבוּ״,

It is said here "you shall sit,"

וְנֶאֱמַר בְּמִלּוּאִים ״תֵּשְׁבוּ״.

and it is said about the inauguration "you shall sit."

מָה לְהַלָּן — ״יָמִים״ וַאֲפִילּוּ לֵילוֹת,

Just as there, "days" means even nights,
