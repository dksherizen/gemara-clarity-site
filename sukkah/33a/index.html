Topic

Disqualifications of the הדס: the Egyptian myrtle, dried leaves, a severed top, דחוי אצל מצוות, binding the לולב, and too many berries

Overview

This עמוד deals with problems that can make a הדס invalid. It opens by explaining that an אסא מצראה, which has seven leaves per cluster, stays valid even after most leaves fall off, and אביי rules that it is valid because the Torah only requires an עץ עבות. A ברייתא allows a הדס with mostly dried leaves if three moist leaf-branches remain, and רב חסדא adds that they must be at the top. עולא בר חיננא teaches that a severed top is fixed if a berry grows there. רבי ירמיה then asks whether a הדס that was invalid when יום טוב began can become valid, which turns on whether יש דחוי אצל מצוות. רב פפא's rule from כיסוי הדם seems to answer this, but the גמרא explains that the question is whether that rule applies to make things easier, and it ends in תיקו. An attempt to tie דחוי to a dispute of תנאים is rejected in two ways, which leads to the dispute over binding the לולב: רבי יהודה requires it from אגודת אזוב, while the חכמים call it only a beautification (זה אלי ואנוהו). Finally, רב חסדא, quoting רב, rules that too many berries invalidate a הדס only when they are bunched in one place, and רבא begins to respond on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

בְּאַסָּא מִצְרָאָה

With an Egyptian myrtle

דְּקָיְימִי שִׁבְעָה שִׁבְעָה בְּחַד קִינָּא,

which has seven and seven [leaves] standing in one base,

דְּכִי נָתְרִי אַרְבְּעָה,

so that when four fall off,

פָּשׁוּ לְהוּ תְּלָתָא.

three remain.
