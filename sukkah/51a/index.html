Topic

The Temple song (voice or instruments), the dispute over the Water Drawing music on שבת, and the משנה describing שמחת בית השואבה

Overview

The גמרא first tries to find the argument over whether the Temple song is mainly the voice (בפה) or the instruments (בכלי) in a dispute of תנאים. That dispute is about who played the instruments: slaves of כהנים, distinguished Israelite families, or לוים. רבי יוסי's middle view fits neither side, so the גמרא concludes that everyone holds עיקר שירה בפה. The תנאים only disagree about what actually happened. That historical fact matters for whether playing on the דוכן proves pure lineage and the right to מעשר. Next, רבי ירמיה בר אבא says that רבי יוסי בר יהודה and the רבנן argue only about the extra-rejoicing music of the Water Drawing. A ברייתא and then the משנה refute רב יוסף on both counts: the dispute is about the Water Drawing, and everyone agrees the music of the קרבן overrides שבת. The גמרא then gives each side's verse for בפה or בכלי, and each side explains away the other's verse. Finally, a new משנה describes the glorious שמחת בית השואבה: giant golden מנורות, wicks from the כהנים' old clothes, light filling all of ירושלים, and חסידים dancing before the crowd.

Opening lines

כְּתַנָּאֵי, (דְּתַנְיָא):

It is like a dispute of tanna'im, as it was taught:

עַבְדֵי כֹהֲנִים הָיוּ,

They (the instrument players) were slaves of priests,

דִּבְרֵי רַבִּי מֵאִיר.

these are the words of Rabbi Meir.
