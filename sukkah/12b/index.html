Topic

Which materials are valid סכך: the wording of the bundles and haystack משניות, arrow shafts (זכרים and נקבות), stages of flax, and licorice and wormwood leaves.

Overview

This amud opens in the middle of an answer. The גמרא explains that the bundles משנה says 'אין מסככין' because the problem there is only a rabbinic decree, גזירת אוצר. The haystack משנה says 'אינה סוכה' because that סוכה is invalid even מדאורייתא. Next, רב rules that זכרים arrow shafts are kosher סכך but נקבות arrow shafts are פסול. The גמרא asks 'פשיטא!' on each half and shows that each half teaches something new. Then רבי יוחנן rules on three stages of flax: combed flax is פסול, raw stalks are כשר, and he does not know the law for the middle stage, הושני. רבה בר בר חנה adds that he is not even sure what the word הושני means. Finally, רב יהודה permits licorice and wormwood leaves as סכך, while אביי forbids wormwood. The גמרא begins to give אביי's reason, and the answer continues on the next amud.
