Topic

The third wall of a סוכה (corner and מבוי shapes, צורת הפתח) and whether a סוכה wall counts for שבת (מגו)

Overview

The גמרא continues discussing a סוכה with two full walls and a third wall of only one טפח. רב's ruling that the טפח is placed 'כנגד היוצא' is supported by שמואל in the name of לוי and by the ruling of the בית מדרש. רבי סימון (or רבי יהושע בן לוי) offers another method: a slightly wide טפח placed less than three טפחים from a wall, so that through לבוד it counts as a bigger wall. For a סוכה shaped like an alleyway (two parallel walls), רב יהודה allows a single טפח on any side, but רבי סימון requires a board of four טפחים and a bit. The גמרא explains the difference: walls that meet at a corner are a real structure, while parallel walls are not. רבא is quoted in three versions about using a צורת הפתח, and רב כהנא follows the version that requires both the טפח and the צורת הפתח. רבא then rules by מגו that a valid סוכה wall also counts as a wall for שבת during סוכות. אביי challenges this from a ברייתא, and רבא defends it by rereading the ברייתא and by a כל שכן argument. The amud ends by starting a fresh look at רבא's ruling about סכך over a מבוי, which continues on the next amud.
