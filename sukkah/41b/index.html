Topic

The end of the יום הנף discussion, and the new משנה on the first day of סוכות falling on שבת: the need to own your לולב, the gift rule, and love of מצוות.

Overview

The עמוד opens by finishing a discussion about new grain (חדש). רבן יוחנן בן זכאי forbade eating it all of יום הנף, and the גמרא explains that this follows רבי יהודה's Torah-level reading of 'עד עצם היום הזה.' A ברייתא seems to show רבי יהודה arguing with him. The גמרא answers that רבי יהודה misunderstood him, and that 'הִתְקִין' means he expounded the פסוק and made it public practice. Then a new משנה begins. When the first day of סוכות falls on שבת, everyone brings his לולב to the בית הכנסת beforehand, because on the first day a person must use his own לולב. רבי יוסי adds that someone who forgot it was שבת and carried his לולב out is פטור. The גמרא learns the ownership rule from 'וּלְקַחְתֶּם' and 'לָכֶם', and shows it in action with four חכמים on a ship passing one לולב to each other as a gift. From that story it learns that a gift given on condition of return is a real gift, and רבא adds that the gift counts only if the item is actually returned. The גמרא then praises love of מצוות: אמימר prayed holding his לולב, a מצוה object does not distract a person during תפילה, and the people of ירושלים carried their לולב almost everywhere. At the end the גמרא returns to רבי יוסי's ruling, and אביי's comment on it continues on the next עמוד.

Opening lines

״עַד עֶצֶם הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה״

"Until this very day"

— עַד עִיצּוּמוֹ שֶׁל יוֹם,

means until the essence (full body) of the day,

וְקָסָבַר ״עַד״ וְעַד בַּכְּלָל.

and he holds that "until" means until and including.

וּמִי סָבַר לֵיהּ כְּווֹתֵיהּ?

And does he really hold like him?
