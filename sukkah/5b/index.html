Topic

The one-טפח כפורת, and the source for a סוכה's ten-טפח minimum height

Overview

The amud opens by testing רב הונא's teaching that the כפורת was one טפח thick, based on the word ״פני״ (face). Using the rule תפשת מרובה לא תפשת and a גזירה שוה to the face of יצחק, the גמרא confirms the one-טפח size. A tradition that the כרובים' faces were at least a טפח supports it too. רבי אבהו explains that a כרוב has a child's face, and he defends this against אביי's challenge from יחזקאל. The גמרא then asks how we know a סוכה needs ten טפחים of empty space below the סכך. It answers with a calculation from the בית עולמים and the משכן: the כרובים filled one-third of the height, and their wings, called 'סוככים', were spread just above ten טפחים. This math only works for רבי מאיר, so for רבי יהודה the גמרא says the ten טפחים are a הלכה למשה מסיני, as רב taught about שיעורין, חציצין, and מחיצין. The גמרא challenges that שיעורין come from the פסוק of the seven species and begins explaining it with wheat. That explanation continues on the next amud.
