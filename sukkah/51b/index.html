Topic

End of the משנה on שמחת בית השואבה; the glory of הורדוס's בית המקדש and the synagogue of אלכסנדריא; the balcony separating men and women

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה about שמחת בית השואבה on סוכות. Pious men danced with torches, and the לוים played music on the fifteen steps. At dawn, כהנים blew trumpets as the procession went down to draw water. At the eastern gate they turned to face the היכל and declared that their eyes are turned to ה', unlike their ancestors who worshipped the sun. The גמרא praises the celebration, ירושלים and the בית המקדש as rebuilt by הורדוס, whose marble walls looked like the waves of the sea. It then describes the huge synagogue of אלכסנדריא and tells how its community was destroyed by אלכסנדר מוקדון for settling in מצרים. Finally it explains the משנה's 'תיקון גדול': a balcony so the women sat above and the men below. It asks how anyone could change the בית המקדש's divine plan, and רב begins to answer that they learned it from a פסוק, which the next amud quotes.

Opening lines

בַּאֲבוּקוֹת שֶׁל אוֹר שֶׁבִּידֵיהֶן,

with torches of fire that were in their hands,

וְאוֹמְרִים לִפְנֵיהֶם

and they would say before them

דִּבְרֵי שִׁירוֹת וְתוּשְׁבָּחוֹת.

words of songs and praises.

וְהַלְוִיִּם בְּכִנּוֹרוֹת וּבִנְבָלִים

And the Levites with lyres and with harps
