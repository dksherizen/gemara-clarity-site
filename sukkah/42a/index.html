Topic

רבי יוסי's exemption for one who errs while busy with a מצוה, then handling the לולב on שבת and the stages of a child's חינוך

Overview

The amud opens with רבי יוסי's ruling from the משנה: someone who forgot it was שבת and carried his לולב into the public domain is exempt, because he was busy with a מצוה. The גמרא limits this to a case where he had not yet fulfilled the מצוה. It explains how that can happen: אביי says he held the לולב upside down, and רבא says he carried it in a כלי, which is a degrading way of holding it and does not count as taking. Next, רב הונא reports that רבי יוסי also exempts a כהן who ate an עולת העוף thinking it was a חטאת העוף. This teaches that the exemption applies even when no מצוה was actually done. A ברייתא seems to show רבי יוסי holding liable someone who slaughtered an uninspected תמיד on שבת. The answer is that this was a careless mistake, since he took the animal from the chamber of uninspected animals. A new משנה then teaches that a woman may take the לולב and return it to water on שבת, and that a קטן who knows how to shake it is obligated. The גמרא explains why the woman's case needed to be said. It then brings a ברייתא listing the stages at which a child begins each מצוה and status: לולב, ציצית, תפילין, learning תורה and קריאת שמע, טהרה, ספק טומאה, and receiving תרומה.

Opening lines

לֹא שָׁנוּ אֶלָּא שֶׁלֹּא יָצָא בּוֹ,

They only taught this where he had not yet fulfilled his obligation with it,

אֲבָל יָצָא בּוֹ — חַיָּיב.

but if he had fulfilled his obligation with it, he is liable.

הָא מִדְּאַגְבְּהֵיהּ נְפַק בֵּיהּ!

But from the moment he lifted it, he fulfilled his obligation with it!

אָמַר אַבָּיֵי: כְּשֶׁהֲפָכוֹ.

Abaye said: When he turned it over.
