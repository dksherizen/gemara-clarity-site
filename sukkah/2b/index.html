Topic

The sources for the twenty-אמה limit of a סוכה, רב's rulings that narrow the מחלוקת, and the story of הילני המלכה's tall סוכה

Overview

Earlier on the דף, רבה, רבי זירא and רבא each gave a different source and reason for why a סוכה taller than twenty אמות is פסול. The גמרא first explains why each one rejects the others' sources: every source has a weak point. Next it brings three reports of a ruling by רב that narrow the מחלוקת between רבי יהודה and the חכמים. רבי יאשיה's report (walls that reach the סכך) fits רבה's reason. רב הונא's report (a סוכה bigger than four by four אמות) fits רבי זירא's reason. רב חנן בר רבה's report (anything bigger than head, most of body and table) fits none of them. The גמרא shows that רב הונא and רב חנן בר רבה agree on the minimum size of a סוכה and differ only on how far the מחלוקת reaches. Then a ברייתא tells of הילני המלכה's tall סוכה, and the גמרא explains why רבי יהודה needed both of his answers to the חכמים. Finally the גמרא starts testing that story against the reports of רב, and the test continues on the next amud.

that is knowledge for the generations.

They also do not say like Rabbi Zeira,

הָהוּא לִימוֹת הַמָּשִׁיחַ הוּא דִּכְתִיב.

that verse is written about the days of Mashiach.
