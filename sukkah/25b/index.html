Topic

The source for עוסק במצוה פטור מן המצוה, and רב's rulings on an אבל (תפילין, סוכה) and the wedding party's exemptions

Overview

This amud finishes the search for a source for עוסק במצוה פטור מן המצוה (someone busy with one מצוה is exempt from another). The question is who the טמאים were who could not bring the קרבן פסח. רבי עקיבא says they were מישאל and אלצפן. רבי יצחק rejects this, and he concludes they were people burying a מת מצוה whose seventh day fell on ערב פסח. A צריכא then explains why both sources (the חתן with קריאת שמע and the טמאים with פסח) are needed. Next, with גופא, the גמרא turns to rulings of רבי אבא בר זבדא in the name of רב. An אבל is exempt from תפילין only on the first day of mourning. He is still obligated in סוכה, because his sadness does not come from the סוכה. The wedding party is exempt from סוכה for all seven days, because their joy and their meal belong in the חופה. אביי and רבא give different reasons why the חופה cannot simply be put in the סוכה. רבי זירא tells how he chose to eat in the סוכה and rejoice in the חופה anyway. The amud ends with a ברייתא: the wedding party is exempt from תפלה and תפילין but must say קריאת שמע.
