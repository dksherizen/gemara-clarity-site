Topic

שמחת בית השואבה: songs, sayings, and feats of the Sages, the near-sleepless festival, and the fifteen שיר המעלות

Overview

This amud continues describing שמחת בית השואבה from the משנה. The light was so bright that a woman could sort wheat by it. A ברייתא gives the songs of the חסידים and the בעלי תשובה, and the message both groups shared about תשובה. Next come sayings of הלל הזקן at the celebration, and his words to a floating skull about מדה כנגד מדה. רבי יוחנן teaches that a person's feet bring him where he is needed, and the story of שלמה's two scribes illustrates it. The גמרא then describes רבן שמעון בן גמליאל juggling torches and bowing in קידה. It discusses why לוי became lame, and lists other Sages who juggled. רבי יהושע בן חנניה says they hardly slept during the celebration, and the גמרא explains that they only dozed. Finally, רב חסדא discusses why דוד said the fifteen שיר המעלות, and the story of the rising תהום continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אִשָּׁה הָיְתָה בּוֹרֶרֶת חִטִּים

A woman would sort wheat

לָאוֹר שֶׁל בֵּית הַשּׁוֹאֵבָה.

by the light of the Place of the Water-Drawing.

חֲסִידִים וְאַנְשֵׁי מַעֲשֶׂה כּוּ׳.

[The Mishnah said:] The pious and the men of action, etc.

תָּנוּ רַבָּנַן, יֵשׁ מֵהֶן אוֹמְרִים:

The Rabbis taught: Some of them would say:
