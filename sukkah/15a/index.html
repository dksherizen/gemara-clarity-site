Topic

Validating a board ceiling as סכך (גזירת תקרה), and roofing with skewers or bed boards

Overview

The משנה deals with a ceiling of boards that has no plaster. It was not put up as סכך, and it gives three views on how to make it valid: moving the boards, removing one board, or both. The גמרא explains that בית שמאי's reason is גזירת תקרה, so moving the boards does nothing. It then explains that רבי מאיר is saying the two houses never argued about this. The גמרא asks why the dispute between רבי מאיר and רבי יהודה over גזירת תקרה would appear twice, since they already argue about roofing with boards in an earlier משנה. רבי יוחנן's answer, that the earlier משנה is about planed boards and גזירת כלים, is pushed away using רב's ruling on arrow shafts. The גמרא concludes that our משנה is רבי יהודה's reply to רבי מאיר in the same debate. According to שמואל, who holds that everyone forbids wide boards, the two argue only about how to cancel an existing board roof. A new משנה then rules on roofing with skewers or bed boards with gaps 'like them,' and on hollowing out a grain stack. The גמרא defends רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע by reading 'like them' as gaps slightly wider than the boards.
