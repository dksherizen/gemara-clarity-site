Succah 8b

סוכה ח׳ ע״ב

Topic

The end of the math for רבי יוחנן's round סוכה, the potters' booths and מזוזה, and the two ברייתות (גנב״ך and רקב״ש) that a סוכה is כשרה no matter who built it, if its roof gives shade

Overview

This amud opens by finishing a calculation from the previous amud about how big a round סוכה must be, according to רבי יוחנן. The גמרא then notes that the math overestimates the real size. Next, רבי לוי in the name of רבי מאיר teaches about two potters' booths, one inside the other. The inner one is a year-round home, so it needs a מזוזה but is not a valid סוכה. The outer one is a valid סוכה and needs no מזוזה. The outer booth is not treated as a gatehouse because the booths are not permanent. Two ברייתות, remembered as גנב״ך and רקב״ש, teach that booths built by or for גוים, women, animals, כותים, shepherds, fig-driers, and guards are all כשרה. רב חסדא explains that the roof must be made for shade, and each ברייתא's 'any kind' includes the other list. The גמרא explains that one תנא names the permanent booths outright, while the other names outright the booths whose builders are obligated in the מצוה.
