Succah 39b

סוכה ל״ט ע״ב

Topic

Buying שביעית produce from an עם הארץ: the three-meal limit, ownerless versus guarded produce, and whether the לולב and אתרוג count as שביעית produce

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא about paying an עם הארץ with money that carries קדושת שביעית. You may give him only enough for three meals, and only for produce from an ownerless (מופקר) field. Produce from a guarded (משומר) field may not be bought from him at all. רב ששת challenges this from a משנה that allows buying wild plants 'from any person'. He answers that the משנה also means only a food-amount (כדי מן), and רבי יוחנן says the same. The גמרא proves from a פסוק in דניאל that 'מן' means food. The גמרא then asks why the same rule does not forbid buying a לולב, and answers that the לולב grew in the sixth year. It asks the same about the אתרוג and answers that an אתרוג follows its picking. The גמרא challenges this from a משנה in ביכורים, which says that for שביעית an אתרוג follows tree rules (budding). The discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וּבָא וְאוֹכְלָן

And he comes and eats them

בִּקְדוּשַּׁת שְׁבִיעִית.

with the sanctity of the Seventh Year.

בַּמֶּה דְּבָרִים אֲמוּרִים —

In what case are these words said?

בְּלוֹקֵחַ מִן הַמּוּפְקָר.

When one buys from ownerless produce.

The steps (14)

  1. StatementEnd of the instruction: he eats his own produce with שביעית sanctity
  2. StatementThe three-meal permission applies only to ownerless produce
  3. Challengeרב ששת challenges the three-meal limit from a משנה about wild plants
  4. Resolutionרב ששת answers himself: the משנה also means a food-amount
  5. Proofרבי יוחנן says the same answer
  6. QuestionHow do we know 'מן' means food?
  7. ResponseProof from the פסוק in דניאל: 'וימן להם המלך'
  8. ChallengeThen why not forbid buying the לולב too?
  9. ResolutionThe לולב grew in the sixth year
  10. ChallengeThe אתרוג also grew in the sixth year
  11. ResolutionFor an אתרוג we follow the time of picking
  12. ChallengeBoth תנאים agree the אתרוג follows budding for שביעית
  13. Proofמשנה: the אתרוג is like a tree in three ways
  14. ProofThe three tree ways include שביעית
