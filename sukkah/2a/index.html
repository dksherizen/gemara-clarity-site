Topic

Why a סוכה above twenty אמה is פסולה: the wording of the משנה compared with מבוי, and three sources for the height limit

Overview

The משנה lists four ways a סוכה can be פסולה: higher than twenty אמה (רבי יהודה disagrees), lower than ten טפחים, missing walls, or more sun than shade. The גמרא compares this to a משנה in מסכת עירובין about a מבוי higher than twenty אמה, where the משנה gives a fix: lower it. It asks why our משנה just says 'פסולה', and gives two answers: סוכה is a Torah law while מבוי is rabbinic, or a סוכה has too many rules to list all the fixes. Then the גמרא asks for the source of the twenty-אמה limit and brings three answers. רבה says a person must know he is in a סוכה, and above twenty אמה the eye does not notice the roof. רבי זירא says the roof must give the shade, and above twenty אמה the walls give the shade instead. רבא says a סוכה must be a temporary home, and above twenty אמה a building must be permanent. אביי challenges רבי זירא and רבא, and each one defends his reason.
