Succah 11b

סוכה י״א ע״ב

Sukkah 11b

Learn Succah 11b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Succah 11b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Whether a later act like cutting counts as 'making' a מצוה object (ציצית, סוכה, לולב), binding the לולב, and the source for valid סכך

Overview

The amud opens by finishing שמואל's point: ציצית may be threaded through two corners at once. רב holds that ציצית tied while their strands were still joined become כשר once the strands are cut apart, because 'cutting is making.' שמואל, לוי, and רב מתנה say such ציצית are פסול forever. A ברייתא about ציצית, together with the rule of תעשה ולא מן העשוי from סוכה, is raised against רב. רב answers the סוכה case, but the ציצית ברייתא stays a קשיא for him. The גמרא then asks whether a תנאים dispute about picking extra berries off a הדס on יום טוב matches רב and שמואל. It rejects that comparison in two ways: perhaps the argument is whether we learn לולב from סוכה, or whether a לולב needs אגד at all. Along the way it explains that binding the לולב is a הידור מצוה based on 'זה אלי ואנוהו.' Finally, the גמרא looks for the Torah source of the rules for סכך: ריש לקיש learns them from the mist (אד), and רבי יוחנן learns them from the חגיגה offering, a source that works even for רבי עקיבא.

Opening lines

בָּעֵינַן כָּנָף בִּשְׁעַת פְּתִיל —

we require a single corner at the time of threading the string —

וְלֵיכָּא, קָא מַשְׁמַע לַן.

and there is none here; so he teaches us (that it is fine).

מֵיתִיבִי: תְּלָאָן וְלֹא פָּסַק

They challenged: If one attached them and did not cut

רָאשֵׁי חוּטִין שֶׁלָּהֶן — פְּסוּלִין.

the ends of their strands — they are invalid.
