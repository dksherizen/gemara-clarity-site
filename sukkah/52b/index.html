Topic

The battle with the יצר הרע, end-of-days figures in the נביאים, and the lighting at שמחת בית השואבה.

Overview

This amud continues the אגדה about the יצר הרע. ריש לקיש teaches that it attacks a person every day, and only הקדוש ברוך הוא's help lets him win. דבי רבי ישמעאל says to drag it into the בית המדרש, because תורה wears it down like water on stone or smashes it like a hammer. Other teachings show how it grows: it tempts a person and later testifies against him, it moves from outside him to inside him, and it changes from a passing traveler to a guest to the master of the house. Feeding a desire only makes it hungrier. The יצר הרע is listed among four creations הקדוש ברוך הוא 'regrets,' and רבי יוחנן and רב פפא bring verses showing that ה׳ takes part in fixing the human heart. Next the גמרא identifies end-of-days figures: the four craftsmen in זכריה (after a challenge from רב ששת), and the seven shepherds and eight princes in מיכה. Finally it returns to the משנה of שמחת בית השואבה. It explains that the young כהנים carried 30 לוגין each up steep ladders, a feat greater than that of the strong son of מרתא בת בייתוס. It then begins a ברייתא about the light reaching every courtyard in ירושלים, which continues on the next amud.
