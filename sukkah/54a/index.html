Topic

רב כהנא's 'no pause at all' between שופר blasts, and the משנה's count of 48 trumpet blasts in the מקדש: whose view it follows, and whether each מוסף gets its own blasts (רבי אחא בר חנינא).

Overview

This amud opens by finishing an earlier discussion. The words 'ולא כלום' (not at all) prove that רב כהנא follows רבי יהודה and allows no pause at all between the תקיעה and the תרועה. Next the גמרא turns to the משנה's count of 48 trumpet blasts on ערב שבת during סוכות. That list has blasts on the altar but none at the tenth step, so it follows רבי אליעזר בן יעקב, and the גמרא explains the reasoning of each side. Then רבי אחא בר חנינא brings a ברייתא teaching that each מוסף gets its own set of blasts. The גמרא challenges him: then שבת of סוכות should have 51 blasts, and the משנה should have listed it. רבי זירא's answer, that there are no gate blasts on שבת, is rejected by רבא. רבא answers instead that the water-drawing blasts are not sounded on שבת, so the total drops. Finally, ראש השנה on שבת would also reach 48, but the תנא simply left cases out (תנא ושייר), including ערב פסח. So רבי אחא בר חנינא's rule stands.

Opening lines

מַהוּ דְּתֵימָא:

Lest you say:

אֲפִילּוּ כְּרַבָּנַן,

(he holds) even like the Rabbis,

וּלְאַפּוֹקֵי מִדְּרַבִּי יוֹחָנָן דְּאָמַר:

and (he comes) to exclude Rabbi Yochanan, who said:

שָׁמַע תֵּשַׁע תְּקִיעוֹת בְּתֵשַׁע שָׁעוֹת בַּיּוֹם —
