Topic

ניסוך המים on סוכות: the משנה's procedure, the source for drawing the water, the stories of ששון, and the גמרא's explanations of the משנה

Overview

This עמוד finishes the משנה about the ניסוך המים on סוכות. The two bowls on the מזבח had spouts of different widths, the water bowl was on the west and the wine bowl on the east, and switching them is still valid after the fact. רבי יהודה says the water was poured with a one-לוג vessel on all eight days. The pourer was told to raise his hand because a צדוקי once poured the water on his feet. On שבת the water was drawn on ערב שבת, and spilled or uncovered water was replaced from the כיור. The גמרא asks for the source of drawing the water, and רב עינא quotes "ושאבתם מים בששון". Two stories follow in which מינים named ששון, and then רבי אבהו, play on that פסוק. Next a ברייתא explains why the כהן turned left on the ramp, and the גמרא explains why even the water bowl turned black. It then proves that the משנה about the spouts follows the רבנן, not רבי יהודה. The עמוד ends with a ברייתא about the צדוקי who was pelted with אתרוגים and the damaged corner of the מזבח, which was sealed with salt.
