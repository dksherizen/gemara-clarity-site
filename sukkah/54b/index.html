Topic

Trumpet blasts in the מקדש: the 48-blast limit, the fixed calendar, and the songs of ראש חודש on שבת

Overview

The גמרא first looks for a second 48-blast day that the משנה left out. It rejects weekday ערב הפסח, which comes to only 42 by רבי יהודה's view, and settles on ערב הפסח that falls on Friday. It then quotes the משנה's rule that no day has more than 48 blasts. It challenges that rule with ערב הפסח on שבת, which has 51 or 57 blasts, and answers that the תנא lists only cases that happen every year. A Friday inside סוכות is shown to happen every year, because the calendar is pushed off so that יום הכפורים never falls next to שבת. Sources that let יום הכפורים fall next to שבת are explained as following אחרים, who hold a fixed calendar, while our משנה follows רבנן. Finally the גמרא tests רבי אחא בר חנינא's view of separate blasts for each מוסף, using the songs for ראש חודש that falls on שבת. That leads to רב ספרא's reading and רבי יוחנן's reason, and ends with a challenge that another sign for ראש חודש already exists. That challenge continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אִי מִשּׁוּם עֶרֶב הַפֶּסַח —

If it is because of Passover eve —

לָאו שִׁיּוּרָא הוּא,

that is not an omission,

דְּהָא מַנִּי רַבִּי יְהוּדָה הִיא,

for who is the author? It is Rabbi Yehuda,

דְּאָמַר: מִימֵיהֶם שֶׁל כַּת שְׁלִישִׁית

who said: never in all the days of the third group
