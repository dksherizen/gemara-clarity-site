Niddah 57b

נדה נ״ז ע״ב

Learn Niddah 57b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Niddah 57b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Where a כתם makes a woman טמאה, and שמואל's rule that she must feel the blood (הרגשה)

Overview

The משנה gives a simple test for a כתם (blood stain): could blood from בית התורפה have reached the spot where it was found? It applies this test to her body, her legs and feet, her robe and sleeve, and finally to a robe she sleeps under and a kerchief, where a stain anywhere counts. The גמרא opens with שמואל's rule: a woman is טמאה only if she feels the blood leave her body. He learns this from the word ״בִּבְשָׂרָהּ״, and the גמרא shows that this one word can teach several laws at once. Next the גמרא tests שמואל against three sources: blood while urinating, a בדיקה cloth under the pillow, and blood found after relations. Each time it answers that she did feel something, but the feeling could have had another cause. A ברייתא about three doubtful cases is answered the same way: 'follow the majority' means she usually feels it when she bleeds. Along the way, the גמרא explains how a doubtful stain can be טמא on her body but טהור on her garment, by setting the case in a butchers' market or after she jumped. Finally, stain cases themselves seem to make her טמאה without any feeling. רב ירמיה מדפתי answers that שמואל agrees she is טמאה, and his explanation continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי' הָרוֹאָה כֶּתֶם עַל בְּשָׂרָה

MISHNA: A woman who sees a stain on her flesh

כְּנֶגֶד בֵּית הַתּוּרְפָּה — טְמֵאָה,

across from her vagina is impure,

וְשֶׁלֹּא כְּנֶגֶד בֵּית הַתּוּרְפָּה — טְהוֹרָה.

and if not across from her vagina, she is pure.

עַל עֲקֵבָהּ וְעַל רֹאשׁ גּוּדָלָהּ — טְמֵאָה.

On her heel or on the tip of her big toe, she is impure.
