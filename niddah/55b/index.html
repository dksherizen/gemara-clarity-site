Topic

The impurity of a זב's fluids: his זוב, saliva, nasal fluid, tears, blood, milk and urine, and the verses they are learned from.

Overview

The גמרא finishes showing that the זוב of a זב makes a person and his clothes impure through both touching and carrying, because the verse "הזב את זובו" compares the זוב to the זב himself. רב יהודה מדסקרתא explains that "זובו טמא" is still needed: otherwise we might compare זוב to the שעיר המשתלח, since the other verse is busy counting sightings. Next comes the זב's saliva. The verse "וכי ירוק בטהור" teaches that it must actually reach the person, and it adds phlegm, mucus and nasal fluid. ריש לקיש reads "בטהור" to teach two things: carrying saliva makes a person impure (unlike a שרץ), and touching it makes his clothes impure too (unlike a נבלה). רב and רבי יוחנן argue about nasal fluid: is it impure only because it mixes with saliva in the mouth, or as a flow of its own? A ברייתא of nine fluids of a זב is used to challenge רבי יוחנן, and the גמרא defends him. Then פסוקים show that tears, blood from a wound and a woman's milk count as liquids. The amud ends as a ברייתא asks whether a קל וחומר from saliva could prove that urine is impure. That argument continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

כִּי אִיצְטְרִיךְ קְרָא לְמַשָּׂא.

When the verse was needed, it was for carrying.

וְאֵימָא: בְּמַשָּׂא מְטַמֵּא אָדָם וּבְגָדִים,

But say: by carrying, it makes a person and his clothes impure,

וּבְמַגָּע אָדָם מְטַמֵּא

and by touching, it makes the person impure,

בְּגָדִים לָא לְטַמֵּא,

but it does not make the clothes impure,
