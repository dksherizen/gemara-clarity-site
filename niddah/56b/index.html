Topic

How far back stains and a שרץ make things impure, which found stains are pure, and when the כותים are believed

Overview

The amud first finishes a question about how far back impurity reaches. A שרץ found in a מבוי, or a כתם found on a garment, makes things impure only back to the last sweeping or laundering. The גמרא asks whether that is because the person surely checked at that time, or because the cleaning itself would have removed the problem. From רבי מאיר's ברייתא it concludes that sweeping and laundering count because a check is presumed (חזקתו בדוק). רבי אחא and רבי then give ways to tell whether a stain came before or after a laundering. רבי אלעזר explains that רבי שמעון's leniency for a moist item applies only to a שרץ, because a moist כתם might be an old stain that got wet. A new משנה then rules on stains coming from רקם, from גוים, and from places with ישראל and כותים. The גמרא uses that משנה to discuss accepting converts from תרמוד and rereads the dispute about the כותים. A final משנה teaches that stains found lying anywhere are pure, with two exceptions. It also covers the כותים's בית הטמאות and lists what the כותים are and are not believed about. It closes with the rule that they are not believed about any matter they are suspected of not keeping.
