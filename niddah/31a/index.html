Niddah 31a

נדה ל״א ע״א

Open Niddah 31a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

How a baby forms and is born, ה׳'s role in creating a person, and what decides a baby's gender

Overview

This amud is full of אגדה about how a baby is formed and born. It opens with the פסוק for the oath a baby takes before birth, and רבי אלעזר's picture of a baby floating in the womb like a nut in water. A ברייתא then describes where the baby lies during each three months of pregnancy, and says that labor pains come from the baby turning over. That is why labor is harder with a girl, who must also turn her face. Next come teachings about marital relations during pregnancy, including the danger of the ninetieth day and אביי's answer, 'שומר פתאים ה׳'. The גמרא then teaches that every person has three partners: father, mother, and הקדוש ברוך הוא. It follows with several פסוקים that praise ה׳'s wonders in the womb, the story of the thorn that saved a traveler, and teachings that ה׳ is involved in conception. The amud ends with what decides whether a baby is a boy or a girl, and a question about the sons of אולם whose answer continues on the next amud.
