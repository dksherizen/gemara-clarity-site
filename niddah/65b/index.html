Niddah 65b

נדה ס״ה ע״ב

Learn Niddah 65b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof color-coded.

Open Niddah 65b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Purifying pressing baskets and the length of an עונה; a bride's bleeding after the בעילת מצוה; telling נדה blood from בתולים blood

Overview

This amud finishes a ברייתא about getting olive and grape pressing baskets ready for use in purity. Less absorbent baskets are washed, while absorbent ones must sit unused for twelve months, or from one pressing season to the next. רבי יוסי gives faster methods: boiling water, hot olive water, or leaving them in running water for an עונה. The גמרא fixes the wording of the comparison to יין נסך, and then shows that an עונה is twelve hours in every season. Next comes the practical ruling of רב and שמואל: a groom completes the בעילת מצוה and then separates. This ruling is challenged from the משנה and from a story about רבי. The answer is that it follows a later vote of רבותינו, and רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש ruled the same way. A ברייתא then explains that bleeding which continues past the bride's allowed nights must be checked. רבי מאיר says the two kinds of blood can be told apart by their appearance, while the חכמים say all blood looks the same. The amud ends with a new ברייתא about a woman who bleeds from marital relations three times.

Opening lines

הָעֲקָלִים שֶׁל נְצָרִים וְשֶׁל בִּצְבּוּץ — מְנַגְּבָן,

The baskets of palm leaves or of hemp: he cleans them.

שֶׁל שִׁיפָא וְשֶׁל גֶּמִי — מְיַישְּׁנָן.

Those of bast or of reeds: he leaves them unused.

וְכַמָּה מְיַישְּׁנָן? שְׁנֵים עָשָׂר חֹדֶשׁ.

And how long does he leave them? Twelve months.

רַבָּן שִׁמְעוֹן בֶּן גַּמְלִיאֵל אוֹמֵר:

Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says:
