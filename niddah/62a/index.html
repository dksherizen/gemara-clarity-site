Niddah 62a

נדה ס״ב ע״א

Topic

The seven-substance stain test (שבעה סמנין): the משנה's rules, the גמרא's identification of the substances, and follow-up cases with soap and repeated washing

Overview

The משנה finishes its list of seven cleaning substances used to test a stain on a garment. If the stain survives all seven, it is dye and the pure items handled on the garment stay pure. If it disappears or even fades, it is blood (כתם), so those items are impure and the garment must be immersed again. The משנה defines tasteless saliva, bean liquid and urine. It then requires each substance to be rubbed three times, in order and one at a time. The גמרא identifies the substances: Alexandrian נתר, בורית as a kind of אהלא (after testing and rejecting sulfur because of the laws of שביעית), קמוניא, and אשלג, which is found in pearls. Two ברייתות then add cases. If soap removes a stain after only six substances, the items are impure out of doubt. If repeating all seven removes it, items handled before the second washing stay pure, but רבי זירא rules that items handled after it are impure.

Opening lines

קִמוֹנְיָא, וְאַשְׁלָג.

Cimolian earth, and potash.

הִטְבִּילוֹ וְעָשָׂה עַל גַּבָּיו טְהָרוֹת,

If he immersed it and handled pure items on it,

הֶעֱבִיר עָלָיו שִׁבְעָה סַמָּנִין

then applied the seven substances to it

וְלֹא עָבַר —

and it did not go away —
