Niddah 60a

נדה ס׳ ע״א

Open Niddah 60a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

When may a woman who finds a stain on a garment she lent blame it on the borrower: a נׇכְרִית, a woman in a period of impurity, or one impure from an earlier stain?

Overview

The amud opens by finishing רב's ruling: a woman who lent her garment to a נׇכְרִית may blame a stain on her only if that woman already sees blood. רב learns this by comparing the נׇכְרִית to a נדה. רב ששת calls this a mistake, citing a ברייתא where even רבי מאיר only requires that she be fit to see. רבא shows from another ברייתא that רבי מאיר is actually the lenient view, so the תנא קמא requires one who actually sees, just like רב. Next comes a ברייתא about lending a garment to a שומרת יום כנגד יום or a סופרת שבעה. רבן שמעון בן גמליאל lets the lender blame the stain on the borrower, while רבי says both women are ruined, though both agree in cases where the borrower loses nothing. רב חסדא links this disagreement to two people on two paths, רב אדא disputes the comparison, and רבי יוסי ברבי חנינא rules that everyone agrees in the paths case. Finally, רבי יוחנן asks whether a stain may be blamed on a woman already impure from a stain. The answer is no, and a ברייתא that seems to contradict itself on this point gets three answers; רב אשי's answer continues on the next amud.
