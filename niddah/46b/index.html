Niddah 46b

נדה מ״ו ע״ב

Open Niddah 46b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Is a מופלא סמוך לאיש an adult by Torah law or only by rabbinic law, for vows, הקדש and תרומה?

Overview

The amud starts with a ברייתא. It says the Torah treats a מופלא סמוך לאיש like an adult for oaths and vows, but he does not bring a קרבן for misusing his own הקדש. The גמרא tries to prove from it that such a minor gets lashes, but rereads the wording. After a few attempts, it concludes that his vow binds only מדרבנן, and the פסוק is just an אסמכתא. Then it examines the dispute between רב כהנא and רבי יוחנן with ריש לקיש about others eating a minor's הקדש. It traces the dispute to whether a מופלא סמוך לאיש is an adult by Torah law or only by rabbinic law. רב ירמיה challenges the Torah-law view from an orphan girl whose rabbinic-level husband cancels her vow. The גמרא defends it with שמואל's 'either way' answer, רבה בר ליואי's ongoing cancellation, and רבא's rule that a wife vows with her husband's consent in mind. Finally, אביי tries a proof from רבי יוסי's ruling on a minor's תרומה. That leads to a debate over whether רבי יוסי holds that תרומה nowadays is Torah law. The amud ends just as the גמרא begins a supporting ברייתא about dough mixed with תרומה, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

לְפִי שֶׁמָּצִינוּ שֶׁהִשְׁוָה הַכָּתוּב

Since we find that the verse equated

הַקָּטָן כַּגָּדוֹל לִזְדוֹן שְׁבוּעָה

the minor to the adult regarding an intentional oath

וּלְאִיסָּר וּלְבַל יַחֵל,

and regarding a vow of prohibition, and regarding 'he shall not break [his word],'

יָכוֹל יְהֵא חַיָּיב

one might think he should be liable
