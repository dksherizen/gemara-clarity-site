Topic

רבי יצחק's teaching about a זב and רב הונא; the unimmersed יולדת; and the dispute between רב and לוי over one source of blood or two.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing רבי יצחק's teaching: the separate section on a זב makes him easier in one way (he is not made impure through אונס) and stricter in another (he makes משכב ומושב impure). The גמרא first reads this as being about the first sighting, which would contradict רב הונא. It then rereads it as being about the second sighting, so רב הונא's rule stands. רב הונא also describes how זוב looks different from שכבת זרע. Next the גמרא turns to the משנה about a יולדת who did not immerse. A ברייתא shows בית הלל and בית שמאי trading proofs, and the גמרא settles which case they argue about: a יולדת בזוב who counted her clean days. Then רב (one source of blood) and לוי (two sources that take turns) argue about the blood after childbirth, and they rule differently when the flow keeps going past a boundary day. The גמרא tests both views against the משנה and finds a way to explain it under each one. It finally brings a ברייתא on דותה תטמא that fits רב, and the challenge against לוי continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

שֶׁהוּא עוֹשֶׂה מִשְׁכָּב וּמוֹשָׁב.

that he makes bedding and seats impure.

אֵימַת? אִילֵּימָא בִּרְאִיָּיה שְׁנִיָּה —

When? If we say at the second sighting —

הֵיכָא הֲוָה בִּכְלַל בַּעַל קֶרִי?

where was he ever included in the category of one with a seminal emission?

אֶלָּא פְּשִׁיטָא בִּרְאִיָּיה רִאשׁוֹנָה,

Rather, it is obvious he means the first sighting,
