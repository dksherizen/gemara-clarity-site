Niddah 7b

נדה ז׳ ע״ב

Topic

The four women who are דיין שעתן, and שמואל's list of cases where the הלכה follows רבי אליעזר

Overview

This amud finishes the משנה about four women (בתולה, מעוברת, מניקה, זקנה) who are טמא only from the moment they see blood (דיה שעתה). The משנה rules like רבי אליעזר and defines each of the four women. It also brings a מחלוקת about a mother who stopped nursing and wider views from רבי אליעזר and רבי יוסי. It then limits the leniency to the first sighting, unless that first sighting came from an אונס. The גמרא brings a ברייתא where רבי אליעזר tells רבי יהושע that he heard the tradition about many women. It explains why people followed רבי יהושע while רבי אליעזר was alive: רבי אליעזר was a שמותי, and out of respect for him no one could protest if people followed his other rulings. Then שמואל lists four cases where the הלכה follows רבי אליעזר, including the impure outsides of vessels, along with a debate about a קל וחומר from a טבול יום. The גמרא explains that שמואל teaches that we do not decide הלכה only from a ruling written in a משנה or ברייתא. Finally it asks whether his list is complete, and that question continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

אֲבָל הֲלָכָה כְּרַבִּי אֱלִיעֶזֶר.

But the halacha is in accordance with Rabbi Eliezer.

אֵיזוֹ הִיא בְּתוּלָה —

Who is a 'virgin'?

כֹּל שֶׁלֹּא רָאֲתָה דָּם מִיָּמֶיהָ,

Any woman who has not seen blood in all her days,

אַף עַל פִּי שֶׁנְּשׂוּאָה.

even though she is married.
