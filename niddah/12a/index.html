Topic

When must a woman check herself for blood around relations with her husband: the limits of שיעור וסת, the cloth of צנועות, and שמואל's rule for a woman with or without a fixed וסת.

Overview

This amud deals with when a woman checks herself for blood around the time she is with her husband. First, רב יהודה and then רב הונא rule that she should not do extra checks that would make a careful husband worry and stay away from her. Along the way, the גמרא shows that the tiny time window called שיעור וסת only leaves room for a quick wipe (קינוח), not a full בדיקה. Next, the גמרא explains the משנה's check before relations. רבי אמי calls it the cloth of צנועות. After pushback, רבא explains that every woman must check, and 'צנועות' means women who use a fresh cloth each time. Then the גמרא examines שמואל's rule that a woman with no fixed וסת must check before relations. רבי אבא בר ירמיה explains that a woman with a fixed וסת checks only when awake. רבא learns from this that שמואל holds no check is needed just for the husband. A ברייתא about husbands returning home seems to clash with this. The גמרא answers that the husband's request is itself the best check. The amud ends with רב פפא asking רבא whether one may rely on that ברייתא in practice.

Opening lines

בְּעָא מִינֵּיהּ רַבִּי זֵירָא מֵרַב יְהוּדָה:

Rabbi Zeira asked Rav Yehuda:

אִשָּׁה מַהוּ שֶׁתִּבְדּוֹק עַצְמָהּ לְבַעְלָהּ?

A woman, what is the law: should she check herself for her husband?

אֲמַר לֵיהּ: לֹא תִּבְדּוֹק.

He said to him: She should not check.

וְתִבְדּוֹק — וּמָה בְּכָךְ?

But let her check. What is wrong with that?
