Topic

The limits on comparing a נדה's blood to her, the sources and limits of טומאה from a dead body's parts, and the source that זוב is טמא

Overview

The גמרא first asks whether a נדה's blood shares her special power of אבן מסמא. רב אשי answers that the word ״אותם״ limits that rule. Next, ריש לקיש and רבי יוחנן give two different פסוקים for why dry בשר המת is טמא. Their sources lead to different rulings about flesh that has crumbled. A משנה says the teeth, hair and nails of a מת are טהור, and רב אדא בר אהבה explains this with a two-part test: only parts that were created with the person and do not grow back are like a bone. The גמרא tests his rule against skin, using עולא's statement about a rabbinic גזרה, and against flesh, which leaves a scar. Finally, a ברייתא learns from ״זובו טמא״ that זוב is טמא, since a קל וחומר fails because of the שעיר המשתלח. רב ביבי בר אביי begins to explain why the פסוק must be teaching more than טומאה by touching, and his answer continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

so too her menstrual flow should also transmit impurity

בְּאֶבֶן מְסָמָא?

through a very heavy stone?
