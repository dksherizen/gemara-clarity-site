Niddah 71a

נדה ע״א ע״א

Learn Niddah 71a step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Niddah 71a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Having sons through conduct and תפילה; why בית שמאי treat all who die as נדות and זבין; the impurity of blood from a woman after death, and דם תבוסה

Overview

The amud opens at the end of רבי יהושע's advice on how to have sons. The advice is to marry a fitting wife and act with holiness during תשמיש. When the questioners say this often does not work, he adds that one must pray to הקב״ה, to Whom children belong. The גמרא concludes that both are needed. רבי חמא ברבי חנינא then explains 'שכר פרי הבטן' as a reward for a husband who puts his wife first. Next, the גמרא asks why בית שמאי say every woman who dies is a נדה. It rejects the idea that fear of the מלאך המות brings on blood. It concludes instead that this is a תקנה to protect the dignity of living נדות, and likewise of living זבין. A new משנה then discusses blood that comes out of a woman after death, with disputes about כתם impurity and אהל impurity. The גמרא explains these disputes through the impurity of the מקור and through דם תבוסה, which a ברייתא then defines. The ברייתא's final case, blood dripping into a hole, continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וַיְקַדֵּשׁ עַצְמוֹ

and he should sanctify himself

בִּשְׁעַת תַּשְׁמִישׁ.

at the time of intimacy.

אָמְרוּ: הַרְבֵּה עָשׂוּ כֵּן,

They said: Many have done so,

וְלֹא הוֹעִילוּ!

and it did not help!
