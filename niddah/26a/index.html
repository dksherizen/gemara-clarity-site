Niddah 26a

נדה כ״ו ע״א

Open Niddah 26a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

The סנדל's twin and birth order, the שליא that makes a house טמא, and the list of five things measured by a טפח

Overview

Just before this amud, the גמרא taught that a סנדל (a flattened fetus) is always born with a full twin. So why do the משניות in בכורות and כריתות teach laws about the סנדל itself? The גמרא answers with cases where the twin cannot decide the law: the סנדל is born first (בכורות), the twin is born by caesarean section, or the twin is born before the mother converts (כריתות). The חכמים ask whether the two can even be born at different times, since a ברייתא says they come out wrapped together. רב פפא and רבא each explain how one can still come out first, depending on whether the babies come head first or feet first. Then a new משנה teaches that a שליא (afterbirth) in a house makes the house טמא, because there is no שליא without a baby, while רבי שמעון says the baby dissolved before it came out. The גמרא describes what a שליא looks like and brings a list of five things whose minimum size is a טפח. It then defends that list against other טפח rules, and the amud ends in the middle of another such challenge, which continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

גַּבֵּי בְּכוֹרוֹת,

[The sandal that we learned] regarding firstborns,

לְמַאי הִלְכְתָא?

for what law is it needed?

לַבָּא אַחֲרָיו —

For the one that comes after it:

בְּכוֹר לְנַחֲלָה,

he is a firstborn for inheritance,
