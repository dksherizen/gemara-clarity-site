Niddah 40a

נדה מ׳ ע״א

Open Niddah 40a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

יוצא דופן (caesarean birth): the מחלוקת between the רבנן and רבי שמעון about the laws of a יולדת, the פסוקים each side uses, and רבי יוחנן's rule that a יוצא דופן animal cannot become קדשים

Overview

The משנה teaches three rules. First, the רבנן say a caesarean birth (יוצא דופן) does not give the mother the days of טומאה and טהרה or the קרבן of a יולדת, while רבי שמעון says it counts like a normal birth. Second, a woman becomes טמאה once blood reaches the בית החיצון, but a זב and a בעל קרי become טמא only when the discharge leaves the body, in any amount. The גמרא then traces each side's sources. The רבנן use ״תזריע״ to exclude a caesarean birth and ״תלד״ to include a טומטום and an אנדרוגינוס. רבי שמעון uses ״תלד״ for a caesarean birth and ״לבן או לבת״ for the טומטום. The two sides also differ on which words teach that separate births need separate קרבנות: ״זאת״ for רבי שמעון, ״לבן או לבת״ for the רבנן. רבי יוחנן adds that even רבי שמעון agrees an animal born by caesarean cannot become קדשים, learning it from בכור rather than אדם because בכור shares more features with קדשים. Finally, a ברייתא of רבי יהודה is brought to support רבי יוחנן, and it continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מַתְנִי' יוֹצֵא דּוֹפֶן —

MISHNAH: One born by caesarean section —

אֵין יוֹשְׁבִין עָלָיו

they do not observe for it

יְמֵי טוּמְאָה וִימֵי טׇהֳרָה,

days of impurity and days of purity,

וְאֵין חַיָּיבִין עָלָיו קׇרְבָּן.

and they are not obligated to bring an offering for it.

  5. ResponseThe source: she must give birth from the place where she conceives

  7. Responseתזריע teaches about a fetus that dissolved before birth
  8. QuestionWhat is רבי שמעון's source for counting a caesarean birth?
  9. ResponseThe extra word תלד includes a caesarean birth
  10. QuestionWhat do the רבנן learn from תלד?
  11. ResponseThe רבנן use תלד to include a טומטום and an אנדרוגינוס

  13. Responseרבי שמעון learns it from לבן and לבת
  14. QuestionWhat do the רבנן do with ״לבן או לבת״?

  16. QuestionWhere does רבי שמעון learn this rule?

  20. Responseרבנן still need ״לבן או לבת״ even with ״זאת״
  21. ResponseWhy ״לבן או לבת״ is still needed: twins from one pregnancy

  27. ResolutionFive features shared by בכור and קדשים

  29. ConclusionThe side with more shared features wins
