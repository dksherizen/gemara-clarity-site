Niddah 43b

נדה מ״ג ע״ב

Learn Niddah 43b step by step: Aramaic beside English, every question, answer and proof colour-coded.

Open Niddah 43b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

How much discharge or semen causes טומאה (for a זב, the man who emits semen, and one who touches it), and a new משנה on newborns' laws.

Overview

This amud starts by finishing a question about a non-Jewish זבה who converted after her urine had started to move inside her. The question ends in תיקו. The גמרא then turns to the משנה's rule that זוב and שכבת זרע make impure 'in any amount.' שמואל says a זב needs enough discharge to seal the opening, following רבי נתן in the name of רבי ישמעאל, a view the חכמים rejected. The גמרא shows how each side uses every phrase of the פסוקים in ויקרא 15. Together they teach that two sightings bring טומאה, three bring a קרבן, the discharge must leave the body, and the discharge itself is impure. Next, רב חנילאי rules that semen makes the man who emits it impure in any amount, but someone who touches it needs a lentil-size. The גמרא defends this against a משנה and a ברייתא, and explains the ברייתא's comparison of semen with a שרץ. רב פפא then shows that the toucher's amount depends on a תנאים dispute about how much of a comparison carries over. רב הונא בריה דרב נתן suggests a different source verse, and both versions turn out to have been taught. Finally, a new משנה begins, listing laws that apply to a baby girl and a baby boy from their very first days.

Opening lines

וְיָרְדָה וְטָבְלָה — מַהוּ?

And she went down and immersed — what is the law?

אִם תִּמְצֵי לוֹמַר

If you say

בָּתַר עֲקִירָה אָזְלִינַן,

that we go by the uprooting,

אַף עַל גַּב דְּמָצֵי נָקֵיט לְהוּ —

even though she is able to hold it in —

  34. StatementA newborn girl can become נדה and זבה
