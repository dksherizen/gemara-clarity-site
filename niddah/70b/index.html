Topic

The end of רבי שמעון's conditional offering for a מצורע, and the questions of אגדה, בורות and דרך ארץ that the people of Alexandria asked רבי יהושע

Overview

This amud first finishes a ברייתא about רבי שמעון's idea that a מצורע in doubt can bring an animal with a condition: an אשם if he needs one, and a שלמים if not. Because of the doubt, the animal follows the stricter rules of both offerings. The חכמים reject this, because it shortens the eating time of what may be a שלמים. The גמרא answers that שמואל agrees with רבי שמעון on one point only. Then come the questions the people of Alexandria asked רבי יהושע. In three questions of אגדה, he settles contradictions between פסוקים: it depends on תשובה, on whether the גזר דין has already been given, and on whether it was before or after שלמה married בת פרעה. In three questions of בורות, he rules that a pillar of salt and a living person do not make things טמא, and he puts off the question about the revived dead for the future. In the questions of דרך ארץ, he teaches that wisdom and wealth need both effort and תפילה, and the question about having sons continues on the next amud.
