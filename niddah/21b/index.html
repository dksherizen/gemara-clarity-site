Topic

A woman who discharges a חתיכה: whether the womb can open without blood, tests for blood or bone inside the tissue, and blood that comes out through a tube (שפופרת)

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a ברייתא about a woman who miscarried something she cannot identify. The תנא קמא treats her as a ספק, so her קרבן is not eaten. רבי יהושע says it is eaten, because the womb cannot open without blood. In a second version of the discussion, שמואל limits רבי יהודה's ruling to a חתיכה with an impure blood color. A ברייתא from רב הושעיא disproves this, so רב יהודה explains that the argument is whether the womb can open without blood. The גמרא then lists views on tearing open a חתיכה: סומכוס counts blood inside it, רבי אחא counts even a red inside, and רבי שמעון בן יוחאי needs collected blood. רבי בנימין says a bone inside a white piece makes it a birth. רבי זירא learns from בבשרה that blood coming out through a tube (שפופרת) does not make her טמאה, and he explains why tissue is different. The גמרא suggests the tube question is a dispute between תנאים. אביי rejects that suggestion, saying everyone agrees she is טהורה, and the explanation continues on the next amud.
