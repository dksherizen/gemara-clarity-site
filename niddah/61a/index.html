Topic

Three women and blood in one bed, searching for hidden טומאה, and the stories and lessons that follow (נדה 61a)

Overview

The amud opens with the last words of the previous discussion. Then a new משנה begins: three women sleep in one bed by a wall, and blood is found under one of them. Where the blood was found, how the women got into the bed, and which women examined themselves decide who is טמאה. The משנה compares this to a טמא pile of stones mixed up with two טהור piles. רבי מאיר says that if all the piles are searched and nothing is found, they all stay טמא. The חכמים say a search down to bedrock or untouched soil clears them. The גמרא explains why the רבנן still keep all three women טמאות: blood had to come from someone, while a raven might have carried off the טומאה from a pile. Stories follow about searches that missed hidden bones, and one clever search with wet sheets that found a pit full of bones. That pit leads to גדליה, who ignored a warning, and to רבא's rule that you must not believe לשון הרע but must still take precautions because of it. רבי טרפון applies this rule. The גמרא also explains why משה feared עוג but not סיחון. The amud ends with a ברייתא about a garment whose stain can no longer be found, which continues on the next amud.
