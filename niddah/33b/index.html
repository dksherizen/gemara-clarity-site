Topic

A זבה's discharge of semen, תרומה and garments touched by a כותי, and the status of צדוקי women

Overview

The עמוד opens by finishing רמי בר חמא's question: does a זבה who discharges semen during her seven clean days lose her count? רבא tries to show the question cannot work, but the גמרא answers him using the זב, whose 'unbroken days' rule only blocks זיבה. So the question stands. Next, the גמרא tells how רב פפא visited רב שמואל, who served him a bull and pointed out a clash between two משניות. One משנה does not burn תרומה over the garments of a כותי, while the other burns תרומה over the uncertain garments of an עם הארץ. רב פפא's answer (a כותי חבר) is rejected. רב שימי בר אשי then explains the case as a naked כותי who went to the מקוה, which leaves only a ספק ספיקא. A new משנה discusses צדוקי women. The גמרא proves from רבי יוסי's words that for the תנא קמא, a צדוקי woman whose practice is unknown is like a כותית. Finally, a ברייתא tells of a צדוקי's spit landing on the כהן גדול. The גמרא asks why the spit was not impure simply as an עם הארץ's. אביי answers, רבא rejects his answer, and רבא's own answer continues on the next עמוד.
