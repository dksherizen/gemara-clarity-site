Niddah 57a

נדה נ״ז ע״א

Open Niddah 57a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

When the כותים can be trusted about burial, בכור, graves and other matters they are careless about

Overview

This amud finishes this פרק's discussion of the כותים and closes the פרק of דם הנדה. First the גמרא explains why the כותים don't bury נפלים: they read 'לא תסיג גבול' as teaching that only someone with a נחלה gets a burial plot. Then it asks how we can trust the כותים at all, since they don't accept לפני עור. The answer is that we rely on their own actions: a כותי כהן eating pure תרומה on the spot, or a כותי shearing and working an animal that would be forbidden if it were a בכור. Each time the גמרא adds that the כותים are knowledgeable enough to be right. They are also trusted about grave-markings, because that practice is written in a פסוק. They are not trusted about doubtful places like סככות, פרעות and a בית הפרס, unless the כותי walked over the whole area himself. The משנה's rule 'זה הכלל' adds that they are not believed about תחומין or יין נסך, and the פרק of דם הנדה closes with הדרן.

Opening lines

גְּמָ' מַאי דְּרוּשׁ?

GEMARA: What did they expound?

״לֹא תַסִּיג גְּבוּל רֵעֲךָ

"You shall not move your fellow's boundary

אֲשֶׁר גָּבְלוּ רִאשֹׁנִים בְּנַחֲלָתְךָ״.

which the earlier ones set, in your inheritance."

כֹּל שֶׁיֵּשׁ לוֹ נַחֲלָה —

Anyone who has an inheritance

The steps (27)

  1. QuestionWhich verse did the כותים expound?
  2. ResponseThe boundary verse: no inheritance means no burial plot
  3. ChallengeHow can we trust the כותים if they ignore לפני עור?
  4. Resolutionרבי אבהו: a כותי כהן is standing on the spot
  5. ChallengeMaybe the כהן is already impure
  6. ResolutionHe is holding תרומה in his hand
  7. ChallengeMaybe the תרומה itself is impure
  8. ResolutionHe is eating the תרומה
  9. ChallengeIf the case is that obvious, why teach it?
  10. ResolutionWe might have thought they don't know how a fetus forms
  11. ChallengeHow can we trust them about a firstborn animal?
  12. ResolutionThe כותי himself is shearing and working the offspring
  13. ChallengeAgain: if so, why teach it?
  14. ResolutionWe might have thought they don't recognize a murky discharge
  15. StatementTrusted on grave-markings because it appears in a פסוק
  16. StatementDefining overhanging boughs and protruding stones
  17. StatementYou may blow on a בית הפרס and walk through
  18. StatementA trampled בית הפרס is pure
  19. StatementHow large an area becomes a בית הפרס
  20. ChallengeBut a ברייתא trusts a כותי about a lost grave
  21. ChallengeThe ברייתא's reason, and the overhanging tree
  22. Resolutionרבי יוחנן: the כותי walked over the whole area
  23. ChallengeIf so, why say it at all?
  24. ResolutionWe might have worried about a narrow strip
  25. QuestionWhat does 'this is the rule' add?
  26. ResponseIt includes תחומין and יין נסך
