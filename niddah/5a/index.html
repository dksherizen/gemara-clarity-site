Topic

How a כתם works for women who are דיין שעתן, and when a checking cloth used during תשמיש counts as a פקידה

Overview

This amud opens by finishing an earlier answer: the משנה follows רבי דוסא, and the ברייתא is a ruling everyone agrees with. The גמרא asks why we don't flip this, and answers that when a text can be read leniently or strictly, we read it לחומרא. Next, the גמרא infers from the ברייתא that for women who are דיין שעתן, a כתם is treated like a ראייה. It identifies this as the view of רבי חנינא בן אנטיגנוס, who also says a young girl before the age of seeing has no כתם at all. A second ברייתא seems to say he holds these women have no כתם whatsoever. The גמרא explains that he means only that it is not retroactive, and that the opposing view is רבי מאיר, who is strict about כתמים. Then the גמרא turns to checking cloths during תשמיש: שמואל rules that a cloth used before תשמיש does not count as a פקידה, because in her rush she does not check thoroughly. The גמרא defends this against the plural 'עדים' in the משנה with two different answers. It ends by starting a new question about the משנה's words 'it reduces from מעת לעת', which continues on the next amud.
