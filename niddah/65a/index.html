Niddah 65a

נדה ס״ה ע״א

Open Niddah 65a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

How רבי's four nights of דם בתולים are counted, a story about misapplying רב's ruling, and how long an עונה שלמה is for a girl who already saw blood.

Overview

The גמרא tries to explain an earlier case where רבי gave a newly married girl four nights, spread over twelve months, in which her blood was blamed on the torn hymen and not treated as נדה blood. It tests and rejects several timings: all four nights as a minor, all four as a נערה (שמואל says נערות lasts exactly six months), two and two (רב counts all relations during minority as one night), and one-two-one. The final answer is one night for her minor years and three during נערות, with each period counted as two months. Next comes a story: מנימין סקסנאה planned to apply רב's lenient first-night ruling even to a woman who had already seen blood, but he died on the road, and שמואל said about רב, 'No mishap shall befall the righteous.' After a side teaching from רב about losing one's teeth, the גמרא returns to the משנה. A ברייתא says that a girl who saw blood in her father's house gets an עונה שלמה, which רבן שמעון בן גמליאל defines as a night and half a day. The גמרא then asks whether that much time is really needed, and begins a contrasting ברייתא about purifying winepresses that continues on the next amud.

״עַד שֶׁתִּחְיֶה הַמַּכָּה״ תְּנַן!
