Topic

Actions that can lead to קרי; examination cloths and when blood found on them makes a woman definitely or doubtfully a נדה

Overview

The amud begins by finishing אביי's ruling that camel riders may not eat תרומה, because riding can lead to קרי. A ברייתא supports this, and the גמרא gives two explanations of why some donkey drivers are צדיקים and some are רשעים. רבי יהושע בן לוי curses anyone who sleeps flat on his back. The גמרא explains that tilting slightly is enough for sleeping but not for קריאת שמע, except for a heavy person like רבי יוחנן. A new משנה then describes how women check with cloths at relations, and when blood found on them brings definite impurity and a קרבן, or only impurity because of doubt. רבי עקיבא and the חכמים argue whether the husband becomes impure in the doubtful case. The גמרא asks why we don't worry that the blood came from a louse, and gives two versions of רבי זירא's answer, each with a different practical result. Finally it discusses a woman who checked herself and found blood the next day. With a cloth known to be clean, רב, שמואל and the בית מדרש rule she is definitely a נדה. With an unchecked cloth, רבי חייא changed his ruling, and the גמרא begins to clarify what that change was. That clarification continues on the next amud.
