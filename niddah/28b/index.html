Topic

רב's derivation that a טמא טומטום or אנדרוגינוס is not liable for entering the מקדש, and the challenges to it

Overview

This עמוד deals with רב's teaching that a טומטום or אנדרוגינוס who is טמא is not liable for entering the מקדש. He learns this from the פסוק 'מזכר ועד נקבה תשלחו', which speaks only of a definite male or a definite female. The גמרא first tries to prove רב from a ברייתא about a טומטום who saw both white and red discharge. עולא rejects the proof: the ברייתא follows רבי אליעזר, who requires a person to know the exact source of his טומאה. A טומטום can never know whether the white or the red made him טמא. The גמרא then defends רב's source. The 'male and female' words in the זב פסוק are needed for רבי יצחק's lesson, so תרומה is still burned. The extra wording 'from... until' in רב's פסוק teaches רבי יוסי's lesson about things that can become pure in a מקוה, and 'מזכר' limits the exclusion to טומאה that comes out of a male body. Finally, the גמרא challenges רב from the laws of ערכין and begins an answer that continues on the next עמוד.
