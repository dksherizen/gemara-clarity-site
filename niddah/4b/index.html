Topic

The loaf case in the תוספתא, and the חכמים' 24-hour rule for a woman who finds blood: its reason, its measure, and how it fits רבי דוסא's view

Overview

The amud opens by finishing the loaf case from the תוספתא. The loaf is טהור because we assume a person moved it, and we do not make the same assumption about a raven, since a raven does not act on purpose. The גמרא then defends the ruling against the rule that a ספק טומאה ברשות היחיד is טמא. It gives two answers: a loaf is a דבר שאין בו דעת לישאל, or the טומאה there is only דרבנן, which the word מדף supports. Next the גמרא returns to the משנה. A ברייתא explains that the חכמים rejected both שמאי and הלל. Their rule is that a woman who finds blood is impure backward for the shorter of 24 hours or the time since her last check. After רבה's reason ('she would feel it') is set aside, the real reason turns out to be a rabbinic enactment: women check every morning and evening, so a woman who skipped her checks loses an extra עונה. רב פפא challenges this, and the גמרא explains the fixed 24 hours in two ways: a uniform measure, or so that the sinner should not gain. Finally, the גמרא asks whether the משנה about a woman with a וסת follows only רבי דוסא, and it narrows the dispute with the רבנן in two different ways.

Opening lines

שֶׁאֲנִי אוֹמֵר:

For I say:

אָדָם טָהוֹר נִכְנַס לְשָׁם וּנְטָלָהּ.

a pure person entered there and took it.

הָכָא נָמֵי נֵימָא:

Here too, let us say:

עוֹרֵב אֲתָא וּשְׁדָא!

a raven came and threw it in!
