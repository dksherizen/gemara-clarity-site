Topic

How בית שמאי treat a שומרת יום כנגד יום, the argument over watching after bleeding on the tenth day, and whether the eleven days of זיבה are a הלכה למשה מסיני or come from פסוקים.

Overview

The amud first finishes a challenge to רב הונא. He said that for בית שמאי, a שומרת יום כנגד יום who immersed and had relations is definitely טמאה by rabbinic law. A ברייתא seems to show that בית שמאי treat her only as תולין (in suspense). The גמרא rereads the ברייתא as talking about the man who was with her, not the woman herself. It then explains that the רבנן decreed only for common cases. A second challenge comes from the משנה, which says her contact is 'suspended.' The גמרא answers that this clause follows only בית הלל, using a ברייתא of רבי יהודה whose wording it corrects. Next, רבי יוחנן and ריש לקיש argue whether bleeding on the tenth day of the eleven זיבה days needs watching. Each one ties his view to how he reads the הלכה למשה מסיני about the eleven days. Finally the גמרא asks whether the eleven days really come from פסוקים, and begins quoting a ברייתא to show this. The ברייתא continues on the next amud.
