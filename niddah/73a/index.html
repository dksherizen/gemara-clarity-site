Topic

The ברייתא's derivation of the laws of זבה from the פסוקים, the question of פסוקים versus הלכה למשה מסיני, and the closing of מסכת נדה.

Overview

A ברייתא works through the פסוק about a זבה (ויקרא 15:25-26), word by word. First it shows which days after her נדה days count as days of זיבה. Some days come from extra words in the פסוק, and days up to the tenth come from a comparison with the fourth day. The eleventh day comes from 'בלא עת נדתה', but the twelfth day is left out because it is not fit for the counting of 'או כי תזוב'. The ברייתא then teaches that bleeding for three, two, or even one day counts. It also teaches that a זבה makes the man who is with her טמא, but a זב does not, even though a קל וחומר seemed to say he should. She also makes משכב ומושב טמא, and after one or two days of bleeding she counts only one clean day. The גמרא asks how these laws can come from פסוקים if they are a הלכה למשה מסיני. It answers that רבי עקיבא learns them from פסוקים, while רבי אלעזר בן עזריה holds they are a הלכה. רבי אבא then proves from 'על נדתה' that bleeding at night also makes her a זבה. The מסכת closes with דבי אליהו's teaching that whoever learns הלכות every day is promised עולם הבא, followed by the הדרן.

Opening lines

״בְּלֹא עֶת נִדָּתָהּ״

"Not in the time of her menstruation"

(עַל נִדָּתָהּ) — סָמוּךְ לְנִדָּתָהּ.

(Beyond her menstruation) means close to her menstruation.

וְאֵין לִי אֶלָּא סָמוּךְ לְנִדָּתָהּ,

And I have only bleeding close to her menstruation;

מוּפְלָג לְנִדָּתָהּ יוֹם אֶחָד מִנַּיִן?

from where do we learn bleeding one day separated from her menstruation?
