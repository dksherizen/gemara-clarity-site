Niddah 13b

נדה י״ג ע״ב

Open Niddah 13b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Wasting seed and arousal; women and a כהן שוטה who cannot check their own purity (נדה 13b)

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a rule about where a man may hold himself, then speaks strongly about deliberately causing arousal. רב says such a man deserves נידוי, because he stirs up his own יצר הרע. רבי אמי adds that he is called an עבריין, since the יצר הרע leads a person step by step all the way to עבודה זרה. רבי אלעזר and the school of רבי ישמעאל use פסוקים to show how serious wasting seed is. A ברייתא says converts and 'those who play with children' delay the משיח, and the גמרא explains the second group as men who marry girls too young to have children. The גמרא then asks whether the משנה's 'the hand should be cut off' is a real punishment or only a curse. It tries to prove the answer from רבי טרפון, but the proof falls away. A new משנה teaches that capable women may check women who cannot check themselves, and the גמרא clarifies who is on that list. Finally, a ברייתא discusses how a כהן שוטה can stay טהור to eat תרומה, which leads to a discussion of pouches and of trousers, and a new statement of אביי that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

מֵעֲטָרָה וּלְמַעְלָה — אָסוּר.

From the corona and above — it is forbidden.

אָמַר רַב:

Rav says:

הַמְקַשֶּׁה עַצְמוֹ לְדַעַת —

One who intentionally causes himself an erection —

יְהֵא בְּנִדּוּי.

shall be ostracized.
