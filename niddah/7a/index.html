Topic

ספק חלה as a weak doubt like נשען, whether dough before חלה is treated like חלה (אביי's rule and the מעת לעת challenge), and a new משנה on women whose time is enough

Overview

The גמרא finishes quoting a ruling that the חלה from a doubtful dough is 'suspended': not eaten and not burned, because it is a 'ספק חלה'. אביי and רבא explain that this is not an even doubt like the 'two paths' case, which would make even plain חולין impure. It is a weaker doubt like נשען, where a זב may have leaned on a pure person, and that kind of doubt counts only for holy food. The גמרא challenges this: is dough that still needs חלה really treated like חלה? A ברייתא lets a טבולת יום knead such dough, because her touch only makes it שלישי, which is pure for חולין. אביי answers that the חכמים made a decree about doubts only where the definite impurity already affects חולין, and a טבולת יום never does. The גמרא then challenges אביי from the backward 24-hour impurity (מעת לעת) of a נדה. It gives two answers: dough has the future חלה mixed inside it, and the מעת לעת is only rabbinic anyway. A new משנה then begins. רבי אליעזר says four women are impure only from the moment they see blood, and רבי יהושע says he received this only about a בתולה. The משנה continues on the next amud.
