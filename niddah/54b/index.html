Niddah 54b

נדה נ״ד ע״ב

Open Niddah 54b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Birth days counting toward a זבה's clean days, and the impurity of דם הנדה whether moist or dry

Overview

The amud opens with a question: a זבה gives birth, and she sees no blood during the days of impurity after the birth. Do those days count toward her seven clean days for זיבה? רב כהנא brings a ברייתא about a woman who miscarried something unknown, which says those days do count. רב פפא tries to reject the proof, but רב הונא בריה דרב יהושע shows the rejection fails, and the גמרא concludes that the days count. The ברייתא then works out how many days a woman with a fixed cycle of bleeding and clean days may be with her husband. Then the chapter בא סימן ends, and a new chapter opens with a משנה. The משנה teaches that דם הנדה and בשר המת make things impure even when dry, while other substances stay impure only while moist, unless they can be soaked back to their original form. The גמרא finds verses for the impurity of דם הנדה, including dry blood. It then explains, from the word ״היא״, that her blood does not make a מִשְׁכָּב וּמוֹשָׁב impure.

Opening lines

יְמֵי לֵידָה שֶׁאֵינָהּ רוֹאָה בָּהֶן

The days of birth on which she does not see blood

מַהוּ שֶׁיַּעֲלוּ לִסְפִירַת זִיבָתָהּ?

what is the law: do they count toward the counting of her זיבה?

אָמַר רַב כָּהֲנָא: תָּא שְׁמַע,

Rav Kahana said: Come and hear,

רָאֲתָה שְׁנַיִם, וְלַשְּׁלִישִׁי הִפִּילָה,

she saw blood two days, and on the third she miscarried,

The steps (29)

  1. QuestionDo blood-free birth days count toward a זבה's clean days?
  2. ProofA woman saw blood two days and miscarried on the third
  3. ProofHer blood-free birth days count toward her זיבה
  4. Rejectionרב פפא rejects the proof: the extra days may not be birth days at all
  5. Challengeרב הונא challenges רב פפא: she might also have had a girl
  6. ConclusionConclusion: birth days do count toward the זיבה count
  7. StatementNine days of bleeding and nine clean days: eight permitted days
  8. StatementTen and ten: her permitted days equal her זיבה days
  9. ConclusionHadran — Closing פרק בא סימן
  10. StatementMenstrual blood and corpse flesh are impure whether moist or dry
  11. StatementOther impure substances make things impure only while moist
  12. StatementIf they can be soaked back to their original state, they stay impure even when dry
  13. QuestionHow long must the soaking last?
  14. ResponseIn lukewarm water for a full day
  15. Statementרבי יוסי: dry corpse flesh that cannot be restored is pure
  16. QuestionWhat is the source?
  17. Responseחזקיה: the flow is compared to the woman herself
  18. QuestionWe have a source for moist blood, but what about dry blood?
  19. Responseרבי יצחק learns from ״יהיה״ that the blood keeps its status
  20. ChallengeThat only covers blood that dried later, not blood dry from the start or the dissolving test
  21. Resolution״יהיה״ is an inclusion that covers every form of the blood
  22. ChallengeIf the blood is like her, it should also make a bed and seat impure
  23. ResolutionBlood cannot lie or sit
  24. ChallengeA צרעת stone cannot lie or sit either, yet a verse was needed to exclude it
  25. ProofThe ברייתא that considered making a צרעת stone cause מִשְׁכָּב וּמוֹשָׁב
  26. ProofThe ברייתא's קל וחומר: a צרעת stone should make a bed and seat impure
  27. ProofThe ברייתא's conclusion: the word ״הזב״ excludes the stone
  28. ChallengeThe inference: without the verse, the stone would make a bed and seat impure
  29. ResolutionThe answer: just as ״הזב״ excludes the stone, ״היא״ excludes her blood
