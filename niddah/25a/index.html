Niddah 25a

נדה כ״ה ע״א

Open Niddah 25a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

A woman who miscarries a שפיר: when does it count as a birth, the argument about cloudy and clear fluid, and the signs of a formed embryo.

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an אגדה about how tall certain people were. Then students ask רבי about a woman who miscarries a שפיר full of flesh. רבי ישמעאל בן רבי יוסי quotes his father that she is טמאה לידה, but רבי does not accept it as הלכה, because it may follow רבי יהושע's lone view. That view comes from a ברייתא: רבי יהושע calls even an unformed שפיר a baby, and the חכמים disagree. ריש לקיש, quoting רבי אושעיא, says they argue only about cloudy fluid, while רבי יהושע בן לוי says they argue about clear fluid. The גמרא proves the clear-fluid reading from the פסוק about garments of skin. When רבא challenges רב נחמן, who agrees with רבי אושעיא, the גמרא answers that רבי יהושע is only in doubt and the פסוק is just an אסמכתא. רב rules the woman need not worry in either case, and שמואל says she must, which fits a story from נהרדעא. Finally a ברייתא describes what a formed שפיר looks like and begins to say it is not checked in water, a point that continues on the next amud.
