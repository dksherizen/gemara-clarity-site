Topic

Whether a caesarean-born (יוצא דופן) animal can become קדשים, and a ברייתא on a woman who gives birth by caesarean after three days of labor.

Overview

The amud opens with the end of a ברייתא where רבי שמעון explains which disqualified offerings stay on the מזבח: those whose פסול happened בקודש stay, and the others come down. The גמרא uses this ברייתא as a proof that an animal born יוצא דופן cannot become valid קדשים, which supports רבי יוחנן. רב הונא בריה דרב נתן suggests the ברייתא might mean a בכור instead. The back-and-forth that follows shows that the קדשים reading makes better sense. A verse ('אמו' 'אמו') already teaches about a חולין animal consecrated after birth, so the ברייתא's verse is needed for the offspring of a holy animal. With a בכור, by contrast, 'פטר רחם' already covers everything. The גמרא supports this from the other animals on the ברייתא's list, which are already excluded elsewhere, so two verses must exist there too: one for חולין and one for קדשים. The גמרא then turns to a new ברייתא about a woman in hard labor for three days whose baby is born by caesarean. There the תנא קמא and רבי שמעון disagree about her status as a זבה and about blood that comes out 'from there'. רבינא begins to define the case of the second disagreement, and his explanation continues on the next amud.
