Topic

Testing strange discharges, רבי יהודה and the fish-shaped case, and רבי מאיר's animal-shaped birth learned through a גזירה שוה

Overview

The גמרא first explains the משנה's water test for discharges that look like shells or hairs. If they dissolve, they are blood and she is טמאה; if they don't, רבה says they are a separate thing, not blood. A ברייתא tells of two real cases brought to the חכמים in יבנה, and the גמרא adds that the water must be lukewarm. It also brings רבן שמעון בן גמליאל's test of mashing with saliva, and it leaves unresolved (תיקו) whether the soak must last twenty-four hours. Next, the גמרא asks why רבי יהודה doesn't argue about a fish-shaped discharge. ריש לקיש and רבי יוחנן answer in different ways, and the גמרא ties each answer to a version of רבי יוחנן's view. Finally, שמואל explains that רבי מאיר treats an animal-shaped discharge like a birth because the Torah says both man and animals were 'formed' (יצירה). Sea monsters, which were 'created' (בריאה), are not included, because the word 'formed' is free on both sides. This leads to the rules for how free a גזירה שוה must be, and that discussion continues on the next amud.
