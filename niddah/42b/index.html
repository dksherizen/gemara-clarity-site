Topic

Impurity inside the body: a יולדת whose baby's head has left the פרוזדור, and whether hidden places count as בלוע or בית הסתרים.

Overview

This amud first finishes a discussion of רבי זירא's ruling about a woman who went to the מקוה before blood left her body. רבי ירמיה shows that her case is not like someone who swallowed a נבלת עוף טהור. So רבי זירא must mean the blood actually came out after her טבילה. Her טבילה does not protect her from that blood. The גמרא then explains why a ברייתא lists a יולדת separately: the baby's head has left the פרוזדור, so it counts as born even while still inside. A story with רבא shows the practical result for a ברית on שבת. Next, אביי and רבא argue whether a woman's inner place, and the throat where a bird נבלה makes one טמא, count as בלוע (fully swallowed) or בית הסתרים (a hidden part of the body). Each side brings a proof, and the proofs are discussed. Finally רבא rules on a שרץ or a נבלה caught in a body fold, and on bringing such a שרץ into an oven. That discussion continues on the next amud.
