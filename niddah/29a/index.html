Niddah 29a

נדה כ״ט ע״א

Open Niddah 29a in Gemara Clarity

Topic

When a fetus counts as born (head versus most of the body), and relying on רוב for an unknown miscarriage

Overview

The amud opens with the closing line of a discussion about ערכין. It then turns to a fetus that comes out in pieces or feet-first. רבי אלעזר says the head does not count as birth here until most of the body is out, while רבי יוחנן says the head alone counts. The גמרא asks whether this depends on שמואל's rule about נפלים, gives other versions of the dispute, and defends רבי יוחנן from the wording of the משנה. רב פפא and רב זביד link the dispute to a ברייתא, and the ברייתא defines 'most of the head' in three ways. A new משנה then covers a miscarriage of unknown kind. The גמרא tests רבי יהושע בן לוי's rule that we follow the majority of women, who give birth to a real baby, both against the משנה and against a ברייתא about an animal's בכור. The amud ends as רבין reports an objection from the law of a טועה, which is explained on the next amud.

Opening lines

לְחַלֵּק בֵּין עֵרֶךְ אִישׁ
