Topic

The חכמים's reply to רבי יוחנן בן נורי about נבלת עוף טהור, and רבא on plans made while produce is attached and 'type' rules for מעשר

Overview

The amud opens by explaining how the חכמים answered רבי יוחנן בן נורי. He used a קל וחומר to argue that the food-impurity of נבלת עוף טהור should not need מחשבה. The first explanation is that food-impurity spreads to similar items, but אביי shows this only makes the קל וחומר stronger. רב ששת then says the difference is הכשר. A משנה seems to say this carcass needs no הכשר, and the teaching of the school of רבי ישמעאל backs that up. Finally רבא, or some say רב פפא, explains that the חכמים compared whole categories of impurity. Next רבא says that for מעשר, even רבי יוחנן agrees that a plan made while produce is still attached counts. רב אשי rejects his proof from herbs in a courtyard and challenges him with the rule that whatever owes מעשר becomes impure as food. רבא answers that such rules speak about types (מין). A proof from a טרפה is pushed away by רבינא, but a ברייתא about an ownerless vineyard proves רבא right. The amud ends by quoting a משנה about פאה for dill or mustard sown in several spots, and the discussion continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

בְּטוּמְאָה חֲמוּרָה

[You said this] about severe impurity

שֶׁכֵּן אֵינָהּ עוֹשָׂה כַּיּוֹצֵא בָּהּ,

since it does not make something like itself impure,

תֹּאמַר בְּטוּמְאָה קַלָּה

will you say the same about lenient impurity,

שֶׁעוֹשָׂה כַּיּוֹצֵא בָּהּ.

which does make something like itself impure?
