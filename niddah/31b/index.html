Topic

Conception and birth אגדות, the end of פרק המפלת חתיכה, and the new משנה about כותים (נדה ל״א ע״ב)

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a teaching about having sons: men who hold back so their wives 'give seed' first are credited with many sons, and רבא adds another method. Next, רבי אמי and רבי יוחנן disagree about when a woman conceives, close to her period or close to her טבילה, each reading half of the same פסוק in תהלים. A string of אגדות follows: a boy brings peace and his own livelihood, while a girl comes empty-handed. The students of רבי שמעון בן יוחי ask why a יולדת brings a קרבן, why she is טמאה seven or fourteen days, and why מילה is on the eighth day. He explains all three through the oath she makes in labor, though רב יוסף challenges it. רבי מאיר explains the seven days of נדה as keeping a wife dear to her husband, and רבי דוסתאי explains differences between men and women from how each was created. Then פרק המפלת חתיכה ends, and a new משנה rules that daughters of כותים are נדות from the cradle and their men are בועלי נדות, though their impurity is only a ספק. The גמרא asks what case this means, רב ששת answers that it is an unknown case where we worry about the minority, and the גמרא begins asking which תנא holds that view. The answer comes on the next amud.
