Niddah 53b

נדה נ״ג ע״ב

Open Niddah 53b in Gemara Clarity

Topic

Twilight views, the laws of כתמים (stains), and the משנה on blood seen at בין השמשות on borderline days

Overview

The amud opens by finishing an answer: two ברייתות disagree about whether the בין השמשות of רבי יוסי comes after the בין השמשות of רבי יהודה or falls inside its end. Next comes a ברייתא about a כתם. רבי says it makes the woman and קדשים impure retroactively. רבי שמעון בן אלעזר holds that a stain should never be stricter than actually seeing blood, and the גמרא rewords his view so that this holds even for קדשים. A second ברייתא asks when a stain can be connected to a later sighting of blood: within 24 hours according to רבי, or only on the same day according to רבי שמעון בן אלעזר. רבי's comment that the other view 'fixes' her situation is explained two ways. רבינא reverses it, and רב נחמן says it refers to the laws of זיבה. Then רבי זירא learns from רבי אסי that כתמים need a הפסק טהרה. Finally, a new משנה discusses a woman who sees blood at בין השמשות on borderline days. רב חסדא explains its labels, and the גמרא begins asking about רבי יהושע's comment on the 'misguided' women, a question that continues on the next amud.

Opening lines

וַהֲדַר חָיֵיל בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת דְּרַבִּי יוֹסֵי,

and afterward the twilight of Rabbi Yosei begins,

וְהַאי תַּנָּא סָבַר,

but this tanna holds,

בֵּין הַשְּׁמָשׁוֹת דְּרַבִּי יוֹסֵי

the twilight of Rabbi Yosei

מֵישָׁךְ שָׁיֵיךְ בִּדְרַבִּי יְהוּדָה.

stretches into and belongs within that of Rabbi Yehuda.
