Topic

נדה מה. – The age of three for a girl and nine for a boy: בתולין under three, two stories, and a new משנה about a nine-year-old and his יבמה

Overview

The amud opens by finishing a debate: a ברייתא fits רבי יוחנן's reading of רבי מאיר, but the גמרא admits it is a difficulty for רבי ינאי. Next the גמרא asks about the משנה's line that under age three it is 'like a finger in the eye.' The question is whether the בתולין go away and grow back, or are never removed before three. A first practical difference is rejected by רב חייא בריה דרב איקא, and a second one about דם בתולין versus דם נדה is offered. רב חסדא then proves from the eye comparison that the בתולין go away and come back. Two stories follow. In the first, יוסטני claims she gave birth at seven, and the גמרא challenges this from the ברייתא about the three women who use a מוך. In the second, רבי עקיבא tests his students about a girl under three marrying a כהן, and the answer is that she is כשרה as a הלכה למשה מסיני. A new משנה then teaches that the relations of a boy of nine years and a day count legally, for יבום, טומאה, תרומה and עריות. The גמרא challenges whether a גט alone is enough for his יבמה, and רב's answer begins at the end of the amud.
